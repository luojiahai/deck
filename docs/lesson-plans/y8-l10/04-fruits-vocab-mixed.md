# Fruits 水果

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name fruits in Chinese and to say we like a whole group of things using 都.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write four fruits in Chinese (水果、苹果、香蕉、橘子)
- I can ask what fruit someone likes (你喜欢吃什么水果？)
- I can use 都 to cover a list (苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃)
- I can sort food words into vegetables and fruits

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l4-fruits-vocab`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- 1:1 devices for Quizlet Live
- Printed sorting sheet (WB Ex.10 format) — three columns 蔬菜 / 水果 / 运动, one per pair
- Quizlet set prepared in advance: the ten taught food words

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 可是, 非常, 应该 and the vegetables — met again inside a reading passage rather than as a word list.

**How:** WB Ex.6 reading comprehension, on the projector. Read it together, then true or false on whiteboards.

> 在天喜家里，他妈妈做饭。我非常喜欢吃她做的饭。她有时候做西餐，有时候做中餐。
> 她在花园里种了黄瓜、土豆和西红柿。她用这些蔬菜做沙拉，非常好吃。我最爱吃她做的蔬菜沙拉了。
> 我以前不喜欢吃蔬菜，但是现在我喜欢吃了。

1. 天喜家里妈妈做饭。 (对)
2. 他妈妈每天做西餐，她不会做中餐。 (错 — 有时候做西餐，有时候做中餐)
3. 她在花园里种了黄瓜和菜花儿。 (错 — 黄瓜、土豆和西红柿)
4. 我现在还是不喜欢吃蔬菜。 (错 — 现在我喜欢吃了)

Point out 但是 in the last line: same job as 可是, and the test will use either.

**Connection:** "Three lessons on vegetables. Same sentences, new food: today it's fruit 水果 — and a word that lets you say you like *all* of something."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, visible all lesson. Read aloud together. Show the four fruit pictures with no Chinese and ask for the English chorally — then reveal 水果 as the heading and ask what it must mean.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 水果 | shuǐguǒ | fruit | plate of mixed fruit |
| 1 | 苹果 | píngguǒ | apple | apple |
| 2 | 香蕉 | xiāngjiāo | banana | bananas |
| 2 | 橘子 | júzi | tangerine | tangerines |

Point at 果 in both 水果 and 苹果 — they wrote it as a simple character last lesson. It is the same character doing the same job. Traditional form of 苹 shown in brackets: 苹（蘋）果.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我每天都吃**水果**。 — I eat fruit every day.
2. 我非常喜欢吃**苹果**。 — I really like eating apples.
3. **苹果**是我最喜欢的**水果**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃苹果。 · 我每天 ______ 吃水果。
**Extension —** Two clauses joined with 可是: 我每天都吃水果，可是我不太喜欢吃苹果。 Then say which fruit you *do* like, in the same sentence.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **香蕉**是黄色的。 — Bananas are yellow.
2. 我妈妈喜欢吃**橘子**。 — My mum likes eating tangerines.
3. **香蕉**和**橘子**我都喜欢吃。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ______ 和 ______ 我都喜欢吃。
**Extension —** Write about three people — you, someone at home, and a friend — one sentence each, and make the three sentences disagree with each other: 我喜欢吃香蕉，可是我妹妹不喜欢吃。

**Recall slide:** 水果 苹果 香蕉 橘子 — characters only. Then the previous six vegetables underneath, also characters only, and read all ten back.

#### Character copying — 苹 香 蕉 橘 (3 min, WB Ex.9 format)
Stroke order modelled on the board, students trace each three times in their books. 蕉 is the long one — fifteen strokes, built from 艹 + 隹 + 灬. 橘 has the 木 radical because tangerines grow on trees; 苹 has 艹 because it is a plant. Name the radical logic as you write.

*Extra Words for recognition only, from the p.96 box — not tested:* 葡萄 pútao (grapes) · 李子 lǐzi (plum) · 桃子 táozi (peach) · 草莓 cǎoméi (strawberry).

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**都 covering a list** — the list comes first, then 都, then the verb.

1. 苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃。 — Apples, bananas, tangerines — I like them all.
2. 这些水果我都不喜欢吃。 — I don't like any of these fruits.
3. 我每天都吃水果。 — I eat fruit every day. *(都 with a time word, not a list.)*
4. 苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃，可是我最喜欢吃苹果。 ← hardest: the list, 都, 可是, and a superlative.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Does 都 go before or after 喜欢?" (before the verb)
- "Write on your whiteboard: 'I like eating both bananas and tangerines.'" — 香蕉和橘子我都喜欢吃。
- "What's wrong with this? 我都喜欢吃苹果。" (都 needs a plural set, not one item)
- "Which character do 水果 and 苹果 share, and what does it mean?" (果, fruit)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — Activity 1: Sort all ten — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** all four fruits and all six vegetables, characters and pictures only. Stays up throughout.

Pairs get the three-column sheet — 蔬菜 / 水果 / 运动 — and sort eighteen words drawn from WB Ex.10: 黄瓜 青菜 苹果 游泳 香蕉 西红柿 橘子 南瓜 冬瓜 土豆 生菜 菜花儿 西瓜 梨 篮球 跑步 网球 足球. Every word placed, written in characters. The two fruits they haven't been taught yet — 西瓜 and 梨 — are in the list deliberately; they can guess from the 瓜 in 西瓜 and place them by elimination.

**Share:** Two pairs justify one placement each aloud: 西瓜是水果，因为…… — or simply 西瓜是水果，苹果也是水果。

**Extension —** Sort them, then write a sentence for each of the three columns that uses at least three words from that column and closes with 等等: 我喜欢吃黄瓜、青菜、土豆等等。 Then a fourth sentence using 都 across two columns.

#### Activity 2: Fruit interview — You Do (8 min)
Pairs, then report. A asks 你喜欢吃什么水果？ and B must answer with **at least two** fruits and 都: 苹果和香蕉我都喜欢吃。 Then swap. Then each student writes one sentence about their partner in their book: 他喜欢吃香蕉和橘子。

Pair deliberately — the fluent speaker asks first and reports back second, which is the harder half.

**Extension —** Interview three people, ask a follow-up each time (你每天都吃水果吗？ 你最喜欢吃什么？), and then report the *whole group* to the class in Chinese without notes: 他们都喜欢吃苹果，可是只有一个人喜欢吃橘子。

#### Game: Quizlet Live (9 min)
Devices out. The prepared set: 水果 苹果 香蕉 橘子 蔬菜 生菜 黄瓜 土豆 菜花儿 西红柿 — characters prompting English, then run a second round the other way, English prompting characters.

- **Setup needed:** Quizlet set built in advance; students on devices; the class join code on the projector.
- **Harder version:** For the second round, run it with pinyin hidden entirely and add the four Extra Words (葡萄, 李子, 桃子, 草莓) to the set. If a fluent student's team finishes first, they write two extra terms for the class set on the board before the next round starts.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip — 苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都 ________ 。 Complete it, and add one more sentence naming your favourite.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: two more fruits, the word 这些, and the whole Text 2 dialogue."
