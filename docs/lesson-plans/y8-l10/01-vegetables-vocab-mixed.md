# Vegetables 蔬菜

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name common vegetables in Chinese and say which ones we like eating.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and read six vegetables in Chinese (蔬菜、生菜、黄瓜、土豆、菜花儿、西红柿)
- I can ask someone what vegetables they like (你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？)
- I can say how much I like a vegetable using 很 / 不太 (我很喜欢吃黄瓜)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l1-vegetables-vocab`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed sorting sheet — two columns 我喜欢吃 / 我不太喜欢吃, one per pair
- Printed character cards for Slap the Character: 蔬菜 生菜 黄瓜 土豆 菜花儿 西红柿 (two sets, A3)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The 喜欢 degree scale from Unit 3 — 最喜欢 / 很喜欢 / 不太喜欢 / 不喜欢 — carried on the hobby verbs 游泳、跑步、弹钢琴、唱歌、打篮球、画画儿.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the projector: six hobby verbs in characters only, class says pinyin + English chorally. Then cold call four students with 你喜欢做什么运动？ — they must answer with a degree word, not a bare 喜欢.

**Connection:** "You already know how to say how much you like doing something. Today we keep exactly that language and change what comes after 吃 — we start Unit 4, food. Today: vegetables 蔬菜."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, left on screen all lesson. Read the intention aloud together. Then hold up the six vegetable pictures one at a time — "shout the English" — before any Chinese appears. Gets the pictures in the room first so the characters land on something.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 蔬菜 | shūcài | vegetables | mixed basket of vegetables |
| 1 | 生菜 | shēngcài | lettuce | lettuce head |
| 2 | 黄瓜 | huángguā | cucumber | cucumber |
| 2 | 土豆 | tǔdòu | potato | two potatoes |
| 3 | 菜花儿 | càihuār | cauliflower | cauliflower head |
| 3 | 西红柿 | xīhóngshì | tomato | two tomatoes |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢吃**蔬菜**。 — I like eating vegetables.
2. 我很喜欢吃**生菜**。 — I really like eating lettuce.
3. 我喜欢吃**蔬菜**，我每天都吃**生菜**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃 ______ 。
**Extension —** Write about someone else, two clauses: 我妈妈很喜欢吃生菜，我不太喜欢吃。 Then say it aloud without reading it.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢吃**黄瓜**。 — I like eating cucumber.
2. **土豆**我也喜欢吃。 — I like potatoes too.
3. **黄瓜**和**土豆**我都喜欢吃。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ______ 和 ______ 我都喜欢吃。
**Extension —** 除了黄瓜以外，我还喜欢吃土豆。 Add a third vegetable and keep the structure working.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我不太喜欢吃**菜花儿**。 — I don't much like eating cauliflower.
2. **西红柿**是红色的。 — Tomatoes are red.
3. 我喜欢吃**西红柿**，不喜欢吃**菜花儿**。 — both words.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃菜花儿。我 ______ 喜欢吃西红柿。
**Extension —** Ask your partner about both, then report what *they* said in the third person: 他很喜欢吃西红柿，不太喜欢吃菜花儿。

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 蔬菜 生菜 黄瓜 土豆 菜花儿 西红柿 — characters only, no pinyin, no English. Class reads them back. If it stalls, reteach the pair that stalled before moving on.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？ → 我喜欢吃……。**

1. 你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？ — 我喜欢吃黄瓜。
2. 你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？ — 我喜欢吃生菜和土豆。
3. 我最喜欢吃黄瓜，不太喜欢吃菜花儿。
4. 除了生菜以外，我还喜欢吃西红柿和土豆。 ← hardest: two structures, three vegetables.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Thumbs up if 生菜 means lettuce." (It does — watch who follows the room.)
- "Write the characters for huángguā." (whiteboards, hold up)
- "What's wrong with this? 我吃喜欢黄瓜。" (verb order)
- "How do you say 'I don't much like tomatoes'?" (cold call, then choral)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — Activity 1: Sort them — We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters and pictures only, stays on screen for the whole activity.

Pairs get the two-column sheet — 我喜欢吃 / 我不太喜欢吃 — and must place **all six** words, writing them in characters, not pinyin. Every word goes in a column; nothing is left out. Then each pair says two of their placements aloud in a full sentence: 我很喜欢吃生菜。我不太喜欢吃菜花儿。

**Share:** Three pairs report one placement each while the summary board is still up.

**Extension —** Sort into three columns instead of two — 我每天吃 / 我有时候吃 / 我不吃 — and join two of the placements into one sentence with 除了…以外: 除了黄瓜以外，我还每天吃西红柿。 Then justify one placement aloud with no notes: why is that vegetable in that column?

#### Activity 2: Vegetable interview — You Do (8 min)
Students stand and find three partners. To each: 你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？ Note the answer in characters on their whiteboard. Back at their seat, they write one sentence about one person they asked: 小明很喜欢吃土豆。

Pair deliberately — put a fluent speaker with a partner who needs the model, and give the fluent speaker the interviewer role each time.

**Extension —** Ask five people, and ask a follow-up each time — 你每天都吃吗？ Then report back to the class in Chinese about the whole set, using 都: 他们都喜欢吃黄瓜，可是没有人喜欢吃菜花儿。 No notes on screen when reporting.

#### Game: Slap the Character (7 min)
Two teams. One player from each team at the board, facing the six A3 character cards. Teacher says a vegetable in Chinese; first to slap the right card wins the point. Rotate every round so everyone plays.

- **Setup needed:** Two sets of six A3 character cards, printed. No pinyin on them.
- **Harder version:** The teacher says an English gloss *or* a sentence containing the word (我妈妈每天都吃这个) instead of the bare Chinese word — players have to hear it inside a sentence. For the last three rounds, the slapping player must also say the word aloud correctly to keep the point.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads the three criteria off the board.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍 / 😐 / 👎 against each criterion, one at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, WB Ex.4 format — complete this in characters: 我喜欢吃蔬菜，我 ________ 。 The second half must name at least two specific vegetables from today.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: how to tell someone what they *should* eat — 应该."
