# Lesson 10.1: Vegetables Vocabulary 蔬菜

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 1 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name common vegetables in Chinese and say how much we like (or don't like) eating them.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 蔬菜, 生菜, 黄瓜, 菜花儿, 土豆, 西红柿 in Chinese (用拼音说出新词汇)
- I can use 非常 to say I like/dislike something a lot (我非常喜欢吃黄瓜)
- I can name at least 3 extra vegetables from the Extra Words list
- I can draw and label vegetables I know in Chinese

## Materials Needed
- Projector (vocab slides: 汉字 + pinyin + English + vegetable images)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed vegetable-tracing worksheet (new words below)
- Blank paper / "supermarket" drawing template (one per student, for Act.8-style project)
- Flashcard set: 8 vegetable pictures (from textbook Act.1) + 5 Extra Words cards (青菜/冬瓜/南瓜/四季豆/大白菜)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Unit 3 hobby vocabulary and 非常/很/不太 intensifiers implicitly used across L7–L9 (e.g. 非常喜欢, 不太喜欢)
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the projector — show 6–8 Unit 3 hobby words (弹钢琴, 唱歌, 打网球, 跳舞, 画画儿, 游泳), class says pinyin + English chorally. Then teacher asks "你喜欢做什么？" to 3–4 students for quick oral answers using 非常喜欢/不太喜欢.
**Connection:** "You already know how to say how much you like an activity — 非常喜欢, 不太喜欢. Today we start a brand-new unit: food! First topic — vegetables 蔬菜. We'll use the same liking-language to talk about what we eat."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read the learning intention aloud together. Ask: "Which of these vegetables do you already know in English? Put your hand up." Hold up the 8 vegetable flashcards one at a time before revealing the Chinese.

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 蔬 | shū | vegetables |
| 菜 | cài | vegetable; dish |
| 蔬菜 | shūcài | vegetables |
| 生菜 | shēngcài | lettuce |
| 非 | fēi | wrong; not |
| 非常 | fēicháng | very; extremely |
| 黄瓜 | huángguā | cucumber |
| 花 | huā | flower |
| 菜花儿 | càihuār | cauliflower |
| 土豆 | tǔdòu | potato |
| 柿 | shì | persimmon |
| 西红柿 | xīhóngshì | tomato |

**Extra Words** (Activity 1, p. 91): 青菜 qīngcài (greens), 冬瓜 dōngguā (winter melon), 南瓜 nánguā (pumpkin), 四季豆 sìjìdòu (green beans), 大白菜 dàbáicài (Chinese cabbage)

Introduce each word: 汉字 → pinyin → English, class repeats twice (choral). Point out that 菜 appears in 蔬菜, 生菜, and 菜花儿 — it's the "vegetable/dish" building block, much like 球 was the building block for sports in Unit 3. Point out 花 in 菜花儿 literally means "flower" — cauliflower looks like a vegetable flower.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 你喜欢吃蔬菜吗？我非常喜欢吃。(Do you like eating vegetables? I like it very much.)
> 你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？我喜欢吃黄瓜、菜花儿、土豆……(What vegetables do you like? I like cucumber, cauliflower, potato…)

Model 2–3 examples, thinking aloud: "To say 'I really like eating tomatoes', I need 我 + 非常喜欢吃 + 西红柿 → 我非常喜欢吃西红柿。" Second example: 我不太喜欢吃生菜。 Insert a silly one: 我非常喜欢吃土豆、土豆、土豆！(wait for reactions — repeating 土豆 three times for comic emphasis).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 黄瓜 mean? Thumbs up if you know."
- "How would I say 'I like eating cauliflower very much'? Write it on your whiteboard."
- "Error check: 我喜欢非常吃西红柿。What's wrong?" (check: 非常 must come before 喜欢, not between 喜欢 and 吃)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Vegetable Vocab Tracing Worksheet — We Do (8 min)
Students trace each of the 12 new words/phrases three times on the printed worksheet, saying the word aloud as they write.
- **Scaffold:** Pinyin printed above every character on the tracing lines.
- **Extension:** After tracing, students cover the pinyin/English columns and self-quiz by writing meanings from memory; then write one sentence using 非常喜欢吃 + a vegetable of their choice.
- **OTR:** Every student writes every word — 100% response rate by design.

#### Activity 2: Name the Vegetables (Textbook Act.1) — We Do → You Do (7 min)
Project the 8 numbered vegetable pictures from p. 91. Students write the Chinese name for each on mini whiteboards, holding up on "three." Reveal the Extra Words box (青菜/冬瓜/南瓜/四季豆/大白菜) and repeat with 3 more pictures.
- **Scaffold:** Word bank of all 13 vegetable names displayed on the projector throughout.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers must also write one sentence per vegetable using 我(不太)喜欢吃……
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards — every student responds every round.

#### Activity 3: Supermarket Vegetable Project (Textbook Act.8, adapted) — You Do (8 min)
Students draw 3–4 vegetables they'd find in a local supermarket (from memory or the flashcard set) and label each in Chinese on their drawing template.
- **Scaffold:** Students may copy from the vocabulary sheet if needed; word bank stays visible.
- **Extension:** Students add a sentence under their drawing: 我在超市买了……(I bought … at the supermarket), using 都 or 等等 if they know 2+ items.
- **OTR:** Turn and talk — partners show and describe their drawing to each other in Chinese, 30 seconds each.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads each criterion aloud; class responds with the target word/sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 4 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one sentence about a vegetable you don't like, using 不太喜欢吃."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we'll learn a new word, 应该 (should) — and use it to give advice about eating vegetables!"
