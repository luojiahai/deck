# Lesson 10.7: 种 (Kind/Type) + Number Ranges

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use 种 as a measure word for "kinds/types" of things, and to say approximate number ranges like "two or three kinds."

## Success Criteria
- I can use 种 correctly as a measure word (三种水果, 两种运动)
- I can say a number range using 两三种/三四个小时 (two-to-three / three-to-four)
- I can ask and answer "how many kinds of X do you like?" (你喜欢几种蔬菜？)
- I can match spoken sentences with number ranges to the correct picture/person

## Materials Needed
- Projector (Activity 14 listening audio, CD Track 50; Activity 13 matching slide)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed partner-dialogue question cards (Textbook Act.11, 6 questions)
- Printed matching worksheet (Textbook Act.13 — 9 sentence-halves)
- Word-strip set of number ranges (两三, 三四, 四五, 五六, 七八, etc.) for sentence building

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 这些/那些/一些 and 都 from Lesson 6
**How:** Pair dialogue — students redo the near/far object-pointing activity from memory with a new partner, then a quick whiteboard check: "Write one sentence with 都."
**Connection:** "You can now talk about groups of things with 这些/那些/一些. Today we get more precise — using 种 to count *kinds* of things, and learning how to say ranges like 'two or three' instead of an exact number."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Ask: "If I said 'I speak two or three languages' in English, how would you say the exact number without knowing it precisely? We're learning the Chinese way to do this today."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### Grammar Focus
> 种 (zhǒng) — measure word for "kind/type": [number] + 种 + noun
> Number range pattern: two adjacent numbers said together = "about X to Y" (两三种 = two or three kinds; 三四个小时 = three or four hours)

Present examples building up: 一种水果 (one kind of fruit) → 两种水果 (two kinds) → 两三种水果 (two or three kinds). Show the parallel with duration from Unit 3: 三四个小时 (three or four hours), 五六天 (five or six days).

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 你每天吃几种水果？我每天都吃两三种水果。(How many kinds of fruit do you eat every day? I eat two or three kinds every day.)
> 他会说两三种语言。(He can speak two or three languages.)

Model 2–3 examples, thinking aloud: "To say 'I eat three or four kinds of vegetables', I need 我吃 + 三四种 + 蔬菜 → 我吃三四种蔬菜。" Second example: 她每个周末画两三个小时的画儿。 Insert a silly one: 我家有八九种水果，可是我只吃一种！(wait for reactions).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 种 measure — people, kinds, or time?" (kinds/types)
- "How would I say 'four or five kinds'? Write it on your whiteboard."
- "Error check: 我喜欢两三蔬菜。What's missing?" (missing 种 after the number range)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Partner Dialogue — Kinds of Things (Textbook Act.11) — We Do (7 min)
In pairs, students ask and answer the 6 sample questions from p. 96 (几种语言/几种运动/几种杂志/几种画儿/几种蔬菜/几种水果 — what kind, and how many).
- **Scaffold:** Question cards printed with sentence frames; number-range word strips available to slot in.
- **Extension:** Students extend answers with a full list using 等等 (e.g. 我会说两三种语言：英语、汉语等等), then write full-sentence answers to Workbook Ex.12's 4 questions (你今天吃了几种蔬菜？什么蔬菜？etc.).
- **OTR:** Every student both asks and answers all 6 questions.

#### Activity 2: Match the Sentence Halves (Textbook Act.13) — We Do → You Do (8 min)
Students match 9 sentence beginnings (他会说 / 她今年学了 / 妈妈打了 / 爸爸开了 / 弟弟在家休息了 / 他学了 / 姐姐每天吃 / 哥哥每天看 / 妹妹每天弹) to the correct number-range ending (八九门课 / 三四个小时的车 / 两三种语言 / 四五天 / 一两个小时的电话 / 一两个小时的钢琴 / 三四种水果 / 五六年的油画儿 / 一两个小时的电视).
- **Scaffold:** Work through item 1 together as a class example.
- **Extension:** Students write 2 original sentences of their own using a different number range, then complete Workbook Ex.13 (rearrange 6 word sets into sentences, e.g. 非常/我/打篮球/喜欢 → 我非常喜欢打篮球).
- **OTR:** Whiteboard/worksheet check with a partner before whole-class reveal.

#### Activity 3: Listening — Match People to Activities (Textbook Act.14, CD Track 50) — You Do (8 min)
Play the audio; students match 6 people (大生/小明/美美/冬冬/王星/小英) to their activity descriptions, most of which use number ranges (去过七八个国家 / 三四天没去游泳了 / 每天吃四五种蔬菜、水果 / etc.).
- **Scaffold:** Pre-read the 6 answer options as a class before playing; pause and replay each item once.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers write out the full sentence for 2 people, not just match the letter.
- **OTR:** Whiteboard hold-up after each item.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; class demonstrates each — a 种 sentence, a number range, a Q&A exchange.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 4 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one sentence saying how many kinds of fruit or vegetables you eat, using 种 and a number range."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson is our final Lesson 10 session — we'll pull everything together: vegetables, fruits, 应该, 可是, 这些/那些, and 种 — with a big review game and a short piece of writing."
