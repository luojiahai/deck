# 种 and Number Ranges 两三、三四

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say roughly how many kinds of something there are, using 种 and number ranges like 两三、三四.

## Success Criteria
- I can use 种 as the measure word for kinds (我每天吃两三种水果)
- I can make an approximate number with two consecutive numbers (三四天、四五种、七八个)
- I can ask 你每天吃几种水果？ and answer it
- I can recognise the food vocabulary from the whole of Lesson 10

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l6-zhong-ranges`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Printed matching sheet (textbook p.97 Ex.13, nine pairs)
- Printed listen-and-match sheet (textbook p.98 Ex.14, six names and six statements)
- Teacher copy of the track 50 listening script (in this plan, below)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 这些 and Text 2; plus a cumulative sweep of the simple characters, ready for the unit test.

**How:** Two halves.

*Text 2 from memory (4 min).* Text 2 is **not** on the screen. Pairs reconstruct as much of it as they can, A and B, then swap. Then reveal the text and let them check what they missed — ask who got the last line, which contains 两三种.

*Character dictation race (4 min), textbook p.97 Ex.12.* Class in small groups. Two minutes to memorise the last two rows of the p.97 grid — 夕 心 目 方 平 土 · 角 页 食 果 欠 石 — then books closed and the teacher dictates eight of the twelve in random order. Group with the most correct characters wins. These are the characters the unit test's "find the simple character" question is built from.

**Connection:** "That last line is the one thing in Text 2 we skipped: 我每天都吃两三种水果. Today it's the whole point of the lesson."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, visible all lesson. Read aloud together. Write 两三 on the board, say it, and ask the class in English what they think it means before telling them. Someone will get it.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 1 word in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 种（種） | zhǒng | kind; type | — (abstract, measure word) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我每天吃两**种**水果。 — I eat two kinds of fruit every day.
2. 你喜欢几**种**蔬菜？ — How many kinds of vegetable do you like?
3. 我每天都吃两三**种**水果。 — straight from Text 2.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我每天吃 ______ 种水果。 · 我喜欢 ______ 种蔬菜。
**Extension —** Use 种 with something that isn't food, reaching back to earlier units: 我会说两种语言。 我喜欢三四种运动。 Write one of each, then join them with 除了…以外.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**(a) Number ranges** — two *consecutive* numbers side by side make an approximation. Never skip a number.

| Chinese | English |
|---|---|
| 两三种 | two or three kinds |
| 三四天 | three or four days |
| 四五种 | four or five kinds |
| 七八个 | seven or eight |
| 一两个小时 | an hour or two |

**(b) The pattern in use** — modelled examples, last one hardest:

1. 我每天吃两三种水果。
2. 我三四天没去游泳了。
3. 我们家每天都吃四五种蔬菜、水果。
4. 我每天都吃两三种水果，可是我妈妈说我应该吃四五种。 ← the range twice, plus 可是 and 应该.

**(c) 种 vs 个** — 两三**种**水果 is two or three *kinds*; 两三**个**苹果 is two or three *apples*. Ask for both on whiteboards.

#### Summary Board — everything from Lesson 10
All eighteen words from the six lessons on one board, characters and pictures only, no pinyin, no English:

蔬菜 生菜 黄瓜 土豆 菜花儿 西红柿 青菜 南瓜 · 水果 苹果 香蕉 橘子 西瓜 梨 · 应该 非常 等等 可是 这些 种

Class reads it back, top row first. This board stays on screen for the whole of the practice phase — it is the students' reference while they work.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Is 两四种 correct?" (No — the numbers must be consecutive)
- "Write on your whiteboard: 'three or four kinds of vegetable'." — 三四种蔬菜
- "种 or 个 — 我买了两三 ____ 苹果?" (个)
- "How do you ask 'how many kinds'?" (几种)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Match the halves — We Do (6 min)
Textbook p.97 Ex.13, printed. Nine sentence openings, nine endings, every ending containing a number range. Pairs match all nine and write the completed sentences in their books.

> 他会说 ______ / 她今年学了 ______ / 妈妈打了 ______ / 爸爸开了 ______ / 弟弟在家休息了 ______ / 他学了 ______ / 姐姐每天吃 ______ / 哥哥每天看 ______ / 妹妹每天弹 ______
>
> a) 八九门课 · b) 三四个小时的车 · c) 两三种语言 · d) 四五天 · e) 一两个小时的电话 · f) 三四种水果 · g) 一两个小时的钢琴 · h) 五六年的油画儿 · i) 一两个小时的电视

**Share:** Read the nine completed sentences round the class, one student each.

**Extension —** Match them, then rewrite three of them about yourself and your own family, changing both the range and the noun: 我姐姐每天吃四五种水果。 Then one sentence that uses two ranges at once: 我每天看一两个小时的电视，做三四个小时的作业。

#### Activity 2: Listen and match — You Do (7 min)
Textbook p.98 Ex.14. Printed sheet with six names and six statements. **Teacher reads the script aloud** (below) — twice at natural pace, then once more slowly. Students draw the lines.

Names: 大生 · 小明 · 美美 · 冬冬 · 王星 · 小英
Statements: a) 每天看一两个小时的电视。 b) 去过七八个国家。 c) 三四天没去游泳了。 d) 每个周末画两三个小时画儿。 e) 每天吃四五种蔬菜、水果。 f) 病了两三天。

Answers: 大生 b · 小明 c · 美美 f · 冬冬 e · 王星 d · 小英 a

**Extension —** Write down the whole sentence you hear, not just the letter — full characters, first listen. Then, after the answers are up, write two new items in the same format for a partner to solve, read aloud by the writer.

#### Activity 3: Answer in Chinese — You Do (4 min)
WB Ex.12, in books, full sentences:

1. 你会说几种语言？什么语言？
2. 你喜欢做几种体育运动？什么运动？
3. 你今天吃了几种蔬菜？什么蔬菜？
4. 你今天吃了几种水果？什么水果？

**Extension —** Answer all four with a range rather than an exact number, and add a second clause to each with 可是 or 除了…以外: 我今天吃了两三种水果，可是我没吃蔬菜。

#### Game: Build a phrase (6 min)
Textbook p.99 Ex.17. Class in groups of four. Twenty single characters on the projector; each group adds one character to each to form a real phrase, writing characters if they can and pinyin if they can't. Most correct phrases in five minutes wins.

年__ 电__ 号__ 多__ 学__ 中__ 秘__ 下__ 上__ 走__ 地__ 蓝__ 校__ 长__ 汗__ 毛__ 喜__ 起__ 早__ 火__

- **Setup needed:** Nothing printed — the twenty characters are on the slide.
- **Harder version:** Two phrases per character, both in characters, no pinyin allowed. And for any five of them, the group also writes a full sentence using the phrase — those five sentences are worth double.
- **If there is time for a second round**, switch to WB Ex.17 on the slide: fill the boxed characters to build 黄__、__红__、__菜__、__果、香__、__常 from the bank 瓜 花 西 色 水 生 柿 苹 港 常 蔬 非 蕉. Same rules, same scoring.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read — today's four, then the whole sequence. Point back at the summary board: eighteen words, six lessons, one topic.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards, held up together, for four questions covering the whole of Lesson 10 — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 each:
  1. Can I name six vegetables and six fruits in characters?
  2. Can I use 应该 to give advice?
  3. Can I join two clauses with 可是?
  4. Can I use 都、这些 and 种 correctly?
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip — 你每天吃几种水果？ Answer in one full sentence using a number range.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we start Lesson 11 — 一日三餐, three meals a day. You already have the food words; now you get the meals."
