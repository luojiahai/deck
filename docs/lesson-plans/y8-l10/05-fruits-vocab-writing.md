# Lesson 10.5: Fruits Vocabulary 水果

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name common fruits in Chinese and say what fruit we like to eat.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 水果, 苹果, 香蕉, 橘子, 西瓜, 梨 in Chinese (用拼音说出新词汇)
- I can name at least 2 extra fruits from the Extra Words list
- I can write a sentence saying what fruit is in my fridge at home

## Materials Needed
- Projector (vocab slides: 汉字 + pinyin + English + fruit images)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed fruit-tracing worksheet (new words below)
- Flashcard set: 8 fruit pictures (from textbook Act.10) + 4 Extra Words cards (葡萄/李子/桃子/草莓)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Simple characters 食/果/欠/石 and the ie/üe pinyin distinction from Lesson 4
**How:** Slap the Board — write 食, 果, 欠, 石 (plus a few distractor characters) on the board; teacher says the meaning or pinyin, two students race to slap the correct character.
**Connection:** "Last lesson you learned the character 果, which means fruit. Today we put it to use — learning the Chinese words for lots of fruits, starting with 水果 itself, which literally means 'water-fruit'!"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Hold up (or project) images of 6–8 fruits and ask: "Which of these can you already name in Chinese from other contexts, or even guess?"

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 水果 | shuǐguǒ | fruit |
| 苹(蘋)果 | píngguǒ | apple |
| 香 | xiāng | fragrant |
| 蕉 | jiāo | broadleaf plants |
| 香蕉 | xiāngjiāo | banana |
| 橘 | jú | tangerine |
| 橘子 | júzi | tangerine |
| 这些 | zhèxiē | these |
| 些 | xiē | some |
| 西瓜 | xīguā | watermelon |
| 梨 | lí | pear |
| 种(種) | zhǒng | kind; type |

**Extra Words** (Activity 10, p. 96): 葡萄 pútáo (grapes), 李子 lǐzi (plum), 桃子 táozi (peach), 草莓 cǎoméi (strawberry)

Introduce each word: 汉字 → pinyin → English, class repeats twice (choral). Point out 水果 = "water" + "fruit" (the 果 from last lesson), and that 苹果 also contains 果. Briefly flag that 这些/些/种 are grammar words we'll dig into properly in Lessons 6–7 — for now, just learn them as vocabulary.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 你喜欢吃什么水果？苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃。(What fruit do you like? Apples, bananas, tangerines — I like them all.)

Model 2–3 examples, thinking aloud: "To say 'I like eating watermelon and pears', I need 我喜欢吃 + 西瓜 + 和 + 梨 → 我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。" Second example: 我不喜欢吃香蕉。 Insert a silly one: 我每天吃十个苹果！(wait for reactions).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 橘子 mean? Thumbs up if you know."
- "How would I say 'I like eating grapes'? Write it on your whiteboard using the Extra Word 葡萄."
- "What character do 水果 and 苹果 share?" (答案: 果)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Fruit Vocab Tracing Worksheet — We Do (8 min)
Students trace each of the 12 new words/phrases three times on the printed worksheet, saying the word aloud as they write.
- **Scaffold:** Pinyin printed above every character on the tracing lines.
- **Extension:** After tracing, students cover the pinyin/English columns and self-quiz by writing meanings from memory; then write one sentence using 我喜欢吃 + a fruit of their choice.
- **OTR:** Every student writes every word — 100% response rate by design.

#### Activity 2: Name the Fruits (Textbook Act.10) — We Do → You Do (7 min)
Project the 8 numbered fruit pictures from p. 96. Students write the Chinese name for each on mini whiteboards, holding up on "three." Reveal the Extra Words box (葡萄/李子/桃子/草莓) and repeat with the 4 extra pictures.
- **Scaffold:** Word bank of all 12 fruit names displayed on the projector throughout.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers write one sentence per fruit using 我(不)喜欢吃……
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards — every student responds every round.

#### Activity 3: "What's in My Fridge" Sentence Writing (Workbook Ex.14) — You Do (8 min)
Students complete two sentence starters: 我家冰箱里有苹果、___ (What's in my fridge — list 2–3 more fruits) and 周末我想去买___ (What I want to buy this weekend).
- **Scaffold:** Fruit word bank stays visible; sentence frame given.
- **Extension:** Students extend with a reason, using 因为 if known, or simply add 非常/很/不太 to describe how much they like each item.
- **OTR:** Turn and talk — partners read their "fridge" sentence to each other.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads each criterion aloud; class responds with the target word/sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one sentence about your favourite fruit using 我喜欢吃."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we look closely at 这些/那些/一些 — how to say 'these', 'those', and 'some' in Chinese, plus a radical-review game!"
