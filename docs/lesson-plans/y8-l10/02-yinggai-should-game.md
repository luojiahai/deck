# Lesson 10.2: 应该 (Should) — Giving Advice

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to give advice and say what someone should do, using 应该.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 应该, 等等 in Chinese (用拼音说出新词汇)
- I can make a sentence with 应该 giving advice (你应该每天吃蔬菜)
- I can use 等等 to end a list of items (我喜欢吃黄瓜、土豆等等)
- I can understand short listening clips that use 应该

## Materials Needed
- Projector (vocab slides + Activity 4 listening audio, CD Track 47)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- 16 action/hobby phrase cards for the acting game (跑步/游泳/听音乐/打篮球/吃饭/唱歌/弹钢琴/看电视/洗澡/睡觉/打网球/画画儿/跳舞/跳高/穿衣服/拉小提琴)
- Printed "give advice" sentence-starter strips (今天很冷，你应该…… / 妈妈说我应该…… / 不要看电视了，你应该……)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's vegetable vocabulary — 蔬菜, 生菜, 黄瓜, 菜花儿, 土豆, 西红柿 + 非常
**How:** Bingo grid with the 12 vegetable words from Lesson 1 (pre-made 3×3 or 4×4 grids); teacher calls out English or pinyin, students cross off the matching Chinese word.
**Connection:** "You can now name lots of vegetables and say how much you like them. Today we learn a really useful word — 应该 (should) — so you can tell someone what they *should* do, like eating more vegetables!"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Ask: "In English, when does your mum or dad say 'you should…' to you? Turn and tell your partner one example." Then introduce today's target: saying the same thing in Chinese.

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 等 | děng | etc. |
| 等等 | děngděng | etc. |
| 应(應) | yīng | should |
| 该(該) | gāi | should |
| 应该 | yīnggāi | should |

Introduce each word: 汉字 → pinyin → English, class repeats twice (choral). Point out 应该 is made of two characters that both independently mean "should" — like a double-strength word.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 你应该每天吃蔬菜。(You should eat vegetables every day.)
> 我喜欢吃黄瓜、菜花儿、土豆、生菜、西红柿等等。(I like eating cucumber, cauliflower, potato, lettuce, tomato, etc.)

Model 2–3 examples, thinking aloud: "To say 'today is cold, you should wear a coat', I need 今天很冷 + 你应该 + 穿大衣 → 今天很冷，你应该穿大衣。" Second example: 你应该少看电视，多做作业。Insert a silly one: 你应该每天吃一百个土豆！(wait for reactions).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 应该 mean? Thumbs up if you know."
- "How would I say 'you should eat vegetables'? Write it on your whiteboard."
- "Where does 等等 go in a sentence — beginning, middle, or end of a list?" (answer: end)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Complete the Advice Sentences (Textbook Act.2) — We Do (7 min)
Project the 5 sentence starters from p. 91 (今天很冷，你应该___ / 北京现在是冬天，你应该___ / 妈妈说我应该___ / 不要看电视了，你应该___ / 周末你应该___). Students complete each on mini whiteboards; teacher cold-calls for answers after each one.
- **Scaffold:** Word bank of possible endings (穿大衣, 吃蔬菜, 做作业, 运动, 睡觉) displayed for reference.
- **Extension:** Students complete the "It is your turn!" task — 4 original sentences with 应该, not using the word bank — then translate 2 sentences from Workbook Ex.7 (e.g. 妈妈说我应该每天吃水果和蔬菜。/ 老师说我应该多看中文小说。).
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards, every student responds each round.

#### Activity 2: Listening — Choose the Right Answer (Textbook Act.4, CD Track 47) — We Do (6 min)
Play the audio; students choose a/b/c for each of the 6 应该-sentences (美文应该每天___ / 小明应该每天___ / 大生应该每天___ / etc.), writing answers on whiteboards.
- **Scaffold:** Pause after each item and replay once if needed; pre-teach the 3 answer choices per item before playing.
- **Extension:** After checking answers, fluent speakers write a full sentence for 2 of the items from memory (not just the letter).
- **OTR:** Whiteboard hold-up after every item.

#### Game: Act It Out (Textbook Act.5) — You Do (8 min)
Class splits into two teams. Teacher calls out a phrase from the 16-card set (e.g. 跑步, 弹钢琴, 洗澡) — sometimes framed as advice, "你应该……" — and one member from each team acts it out. First to act correctly wins a point. After a few rounds, teacher inserts vegetable-eating mimes (e.g. "你应该吃蔬菜") to loop in today's theme.
- **Setup needed:** 16 phrase cards, two teams lined up at the front.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read of criteria; class responds with the target structure each time.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 4 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one piece of advice using 应该 for a friend who never eats vegetables."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: 可是 (but) — so you can say 'I don't really like it, BUT I should still eat it!'"
