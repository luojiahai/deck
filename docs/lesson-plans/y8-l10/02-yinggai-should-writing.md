# 应该 (Should) and Listing with 等等

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what someone should do, and to list several things in one sentence.

## Success Criteria
- I can use 应该 to give advice (你应该每天吃蔬菜)
- I can report someone else's advice (妈妈说我应该多吃青菜)
- I can list three or more things and close the list with 等等
- I can use 非常 to say I like something a lot (我非常喜欢吃黄瓜)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l2-yinggai-should`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Printed 应该 translation sheet (WB Ex.7 format, six sentences)
- Printed Sentence Jumble strips — six sentences, one envelope per pair

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Yesterday's six vegetables, plus the Unit 3 action verbs.

**How:** Textbook p.93 Ex.5 as a fast warm-up. Class splits into two teams. Teacher says a phrase from the board set — 跑步、游泳、听音乐、打篮球、吃饭、唱歌、弹钢琴、看电视、洗澡、睡觉、打网球、画画儿、跳舞、跳高、穿衣服、拉小提琴 — and one member of each team in turn acts it out. Correct action = one point. Run it for three minutes, then switch to the six vegetables in characters on the board: class chorally reads them back with no pinyin.

**Connection:** "You can name the vegetables and say whether you like them. Today you learn to tell someone what they *should* eat — which is what everyone's mother says to them anyway."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, left visible all lesson. Read aloud together. Ask in English: "Who in this room gets told they should eat more vegetables?" — hands up. "By the end of today you can say that sentence in Chinese."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles, plus 等等 in the pattern block

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 青菜 | qīngcài | green vegetable | bunch of green bok choy |
| 1 | 南瓜 | nánguā | pumpkin | pumpkin |
| 2 | 应该 | yīnggāi | should | — (abstract) |
| 2 | 非常 | fēicháng | very; extremely | — (abstract) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我妈妈每天都吃**青菜**。 — My mum eats green vegetables every day.
2. 我不太喜欢吃**南瓜**。 — I don't much like eating pumpkin.
3. **青菜**和**南瓜**我都喜欢吃。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃青菜。 · 我 ______ 喜欢吃南瓜。
**Extension —** Use both in one sentence with 除了…以外: 除了青菜以外，我还喜欢吃南瓜。

*Also on screen for recognition only, from the p.91 Extra Words box — not tested:* 冬瓜 dōngguā (winter melon) · 四季豆 sìjìdòu (green beans) · 大白菜 dàbáicài (Chinese cabbage).

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我**非常**喜欢吃西红柿。 — I really like eating tomatoes.
2. 你**应该**每天吃蔬菜。 — You should eat vegetables every day.
3. 我**非常**喜欢吃土豆，可是妈妈说我**应该**多吃青菜。 — both words. *(可是 is previewed here in the teacher's reading only; it is taught properly next lesson.)*

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢 ______ 。 · 你应该 ______ 。
**Extension —** Write the reported version — not advice to the listener but advice you were given: 我的老师说我应该 ______ 。 Two sentences, different advice-givers.

**Recall slide:** 青菜 南瓜 应该 非常 等等 — characters only. Class reads them back.

#### Character copying — Text 1 new words (3 min, WB Ex.1)
Stroke order modelled on the board; students trace each three times in their books:
蔬 (shū, 15 strokes) · 菜 (cài, 11) · 非 (fēi, 8) · 花 (huā, 7) · 柿 (shì, 9) · 应 (yīng, 7) · 该 (gāi, 8).

Name the radical logic as you write: 蔬、菜、花 all take 艹 because they are plants; 柿 takes 木 because persimmons grow on trees; 该 takes 讠 because it is something said. That logic is exactly what the unit test asks for — "write the radical and its meaning."

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**(a) 应该 + verb** — 应该 goes *before* the verb, never after it.

1. 你应该每天吃蔬菜。 — You should eat vegetables every day.
2. 今天很冷，你应该穿大衣。 — It's cold today, you should wear a coat.
3. 妈妈说我应该每天吃青菜。 — Mum says I should eat green vegetables every day.
4. 老师说我们不应该一边做作业一边听音乐。 ← hardest: negative 应该, reported, carrying 一边…一边… from Unit 3.

**(b) Listing with 等等** — items separated by 、 and the list closed with 等等.

- 我喜欢吃黄瓜、土豆、生菜等等。
- 老师说我们应该多吃青菜、菜花儿、西红柿等等。 ← both structures in one sentence.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What's wrong with this? 我吃应该蔬菜。" (应该 before the verb)
- "Write on your whiteboard: 'You should eat fruit every day.'" — 你应该每天吃水果。
- "Thumbs up if 非常喜欢 is stronger than 很喜欢." (It is.)
- "Give me a list of three vegetables, finished with 等等." (cold call, then choral repeat)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — Activity 1: Finish the advice — We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** 青菜 南瓜 应该 非常 等等, plus yesterday's six vegetables, characters only. Stays up.

Textbook p.91 Ex.2 on the projector. Five stems, students complete each in characters on their whiteboards, holding up after each one:

1. 今天很冷，你应该 ________ 。
2. 北京现在是冬天，你应该 ________ 。
3. 妈妈说我应该 ________ 。
4. 不要看电视了，你应该 ________ 。
5. 周末你应该 ________ 。

Take two answers aloud per stem before moving on. Items 3 and 5 must use food vocabulary from this unit.

**Share:** Two students read their item 3 and item 5 aloud.

**Extension —** Write all five in the book, not on the whiteboard, and make each one a **two-clause** sentence giving the reason as well as the advice: 今天很冷，你应该穿大衣，可是不应该穿短裤。 Then write one more stem of your own for a partner to complete.

#### Activity 2: Translation both ways — You Do (8 min)
Printed sheet, WB Ex.7 format. Students translate six sentences from Chinese to English in their books:

1. 今天很冷，你应该多穿点儿衣服。
2. 妈妈说我应该每天吃水果和蔬菜。
3. 爸爸说我应该每天做两个小时作业。
4. 老师说我应该多看中文小说。
5. 老师说我应该多学一门外语。
6. 我感冒了，医生说我应该多休息、多睡觉。

Then "It is your turn!" — two sentences of their own using 应该.

Hand the word bank to the pairs who need it rather than putting it on the slide; take it away for the second half of the sheet.

**Extension —** Translate the other way as well — English to Chinese, from memory, with the sheet turned over: "The doctor said I should eat green vegetables, cauliflower, tomatoes and so on." Every item on the list, closed with 等等. Then write two sentences of advice *you* would give, each with a reason clause.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (8 min)
Pairs get an envelope of six cut-up sentences, word/phrase strips shuffled. They rebuild each one and write it down. All six contain 应该 or a list with 等等. Source strips from WB Ex.13 format:

1. 我们 / 吃 / 每天 / 水果 / 应该。
2. 说 / 妈妈 / 应该 / 我 / 每天 / 吃 / 蔬菜。
3. 非常 / 我 / 吃 / 喜欢 / 青菜。
4. 等等 / 我 / 喜欢 / 吃 / 黄瓜、土豆、生菜。
5. 不 / 一边 / 应该 / 听音乐 / 你 / 一边 / 做作业。
6. 多 / 老师 / 说 / 我们 / 应该 / 吃 / 青菜。

First pair with all six correct wins.

- **Setup needed:** Six sentences printed, cut into strips, one envelope per pair.
- **Harder version:** The strips are given face down and pairs may only turn over three at a time — they have to hold the rest in memory. Once finished, they cut up two sentences of their own and swap with the next pair.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read from the board.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip — translate into Chinese: *"Mum says I should eat vegetables every day."*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we put a 'but' in the middle — 可是 — and read the whole Text 1 dialogue."
