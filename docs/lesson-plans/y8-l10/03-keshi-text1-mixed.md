# 可是 (But), Text 1, and the Supermarket Project

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to join two contrasting ideas with 可是, and to read the whole Text 1 dialogue about vegetables.

## Success Criteria
- I can use 可是 to join two clauses that disagree (我不太喜欢吃菜花儿，可是妈妈说我应该吃)
- I can read Text 1 aloud with a partner and answer questions about it
- I can write the simple characters 食、果、欠、石
- I can hear and mark the tones on ie and üe syllables

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l3-keshi-text1`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- A4 blank paper, one per student, for the supermarket project
- Coloured pencils
- Printed Text 1 handout, pinyin above characters, one per pair
- Teacher copy of the track 47 listening script (at the end of this plan)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 应该 and listing with 等等, from last lesson.

**How:** Two halves.

*Listen and choose (5 min), textbook p.92 Ex.4.* The six options are on the projector; the **teacher reads the dialogues aloud** from the script at the end of this plan, twice each at natural pace. Students write a), b) or c) on whiteboards and hold up after each item. Every item turns on 应该.

*Correct the Teacher (3 min).* Two sentences on the board, each wrong in one way. Students fix them on whiteboards:

1. 我吃应该蔬菜。 → 我应该吃蔬菜。 (应该 before the verb)
2. 妈妈说我每天应该吃青菜等等。 → 妈妈说我应该每天吃青菜、生菜、西红柿等等。 (等等 needs a list in front of it)

**Connection:** "Yesterday: 妈妈说我应该每天吃蔬菜. But what if you don't want to? Today you get the word that lets you push back — 可是."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, visible all lesson. Read aloud together. Say the English version of today's target sentence first — "I don't really like cauliflower, *but* my mum says I should eat it every day" — then tell them that by the end of the I Do they will say the whole thing in Chinese.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 1 word in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 可是 | kěshì | but | — (abstract) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我不太喜欢吃菜花儿，**可是**我每天都吃。 — I don't much like cauliflower, but I eat it every day.
2. 我非常喜欢吃土豆，**可是**妈妈说我应该多吃青菜。 — I really like potatoes, but Mum says I should eat more green vegetables.
3. 我不太喜欢吃蔬菜，**可是**我妈妈说我应该每天吃。 — straight from Text 1.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我不太喜欢 ________ ，可是 ________ 。
**Extension —** Three clauses, not two: 我非常喜欢吃西红柿，可是我不喜欢吃菜花儿，妈妈说我应该都吃。 Then write a second one where the *second* clause is the positive: 我不喜欢吃青菜，可是青菜很好。

#### Simple Characters — 食 果 欠 石 (textbook p.94 Ex.9)

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 食 | shí | food |
| 果 | guǒ | fruit |
| 欠 | qiàn | owe |
| 石 | shí | stone |

Model the stroke order for each on the board — 食 nine strokes, 果 eight, 欠 four, 石 five. Students trace each three times in their books (WB Ex.8 format). Point out that 果 will turn up inside 苹果 and 水果 in two lessons' time, and that 食 is the source of the 饣 radical they will meet in Lesson 11 — this is why the book teaches them now.

#### Text 1 (textbook p.90)

Pinyin shown above the characters for this reading, then hidden for the pair reading.

> A: 你喜欢吃蔬菜吗？
> B: 我非常喜欢吃。
> A: 你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？
> B: 我喜欢吃黄瓜、菜花儿、土豆、生菜、西红柿等等。你呢？
> A: 我不太喜欢吃。可是我妈妈说我应该每天吃蔬菜。

Teacher reads once at pace, students follow. Read together chorally. Then three comprehension questions — push for Chinese answers, accept English from anyone who stalls:

1. B 喜欢吃蔬菜吗？ (非常喜欢)
2. B 喜欢吃什么蔬菜？ (name at least three)
3. A 为什么每天吃蔬菜？ (妈妈说她应该吃)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Where does 可是 go — start of the second clause or end of the first?" (start of the second)
- "Write 食 on your whiteboard. How many strokes?" (nine)
- "Which word in Text 1 tells you the list isn't finished?" (等等)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Text 1 in pairs — We Do (5 min)
Printed handout, pinyin visible. Pairs read the dialogue aloud, A and B. Then swap roles and read it again with the handout turned over and only the characters on the projector. Teacher circulates listening for 蔬 (shū, not shù) and the neutral tone on 什么.

**Extension —** Perform it from memory with one substitution: B names three *different* vegetables from the ones in the book, and A's last line changes to name who gave the advice (爸爸说 / 老师说 / 医生说). No handout at all.

#### Activity 2: 可是 sentence completion — You Do (5 min)
Textbook p.93 Ex.7 on the projector. Students complete four of the seven in their books, in characters:

1. 我不太喜欢运动，可是 ________ 。
2. 我不太喜欢看电视，可是 ________ 。
3. 我不喜欢做作业，可是 ________ 。
4. 我不太喜欢吃蔬菜，可是 ________ 。

Take two answers aloud for each.

**Extension —** Complete all seven, and make the second clause of each one *longer than the first* — it must contain either a time expression (每天, 周末, 有时候) or a second verb. Then flip two of them so the negative is in the second clause: 我很喜欢吃土豆，可是妈妈说我不应该每天吃。

#### Activity 3: Supermarket project — You Do (10 min)
Textbook p.94 Ex.8. Each student draws the vegetable section of the supermarket near their home and labels every vegetable in Chinese characters. Minimum six labels; at least four must come from this unit's taught words, and they may use the Extra Words (青菜、冬瓜、南瓜、四季豆、大白菜) for the rest.

Last two minutes: gallery walk — leave the drawing on the desk, everyone circulates, and each student finds one vegetable on someone else's drawing that isn't on their own.

**Extension —** Label ten, and add a caption line under the drawing using both of today's structures: 我们家附近的超市有青菜、南瓜、生菜、西红柿等等。我非常喜欢吃西红柿，可是我不喜欢吃南瓜。 Written straight onto the drawing, no draft.

#### Game: Tone-mark race — ie / üe (3 min)
Textbook p.93 Ex.6, run as a whiteboard race. Teacher reads each syllable twice; students write it with the tone marks and hold up. Point per correct board.

1. jiějué 2. xièjué 3. quèqiè 4. jiélüè 5. juéliè 6. jiéyuē

- **Setup needed:** Nothing. Teacher reads from this list.
- **Harder version:** Students write the syllable *and* one character they know containing that sound (解, 谢, 月…). Or they take the teacher's job for two rounds and read the syllables to the class themselves.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads them aloud.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍 / 😐 / 👎 per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip — 我不太喜欢吃 ________ ，可是 ________ 。 Both blanks filled, in characters.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we leave the vegetables and start on fruit — 水果."

## Teacher reference — track 47 script (read aloud, textbook p.92 Ex.4)

Read each dialogue twice at natural pace. Students choose a), b) or c) from the projector.

1. A: 美文，你每天吃蔬菜吗？ B: 我不太喜欢吃蔬菜，可是我妈妈说我应该每天吃。 → **a) 吃蔬菜**
2. A: 小明，你每天弹钢琴吗？ B: 不是每天都弹，我的钢琴老师说我应该每天弹。 → **c) 弹钢琴**
3. A: 大生，你每天读书吗？ B: 我不是每天都读，我的英文老师说我应该每天读。 → **b) 读书**
4. A: 京京，你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？ B: 我最喜欢吃生菜，我差不多每天都吃。 → **b) 生菜**
5. A: 冬冬，你喜欢听音乐吗？ B: 非常喜欢听，可是妈妈说我不应该一边听音乐一边做作业。 → **c) 听音乐**
6. A: 妈妈，我的老师说您不应该一边开车一边打电话。 B: 好，我不打了。 → **b) 打电话**

Items 1 and 5 also contain 可是 — worth flagging as you go over the answers, since that is today's new word.
