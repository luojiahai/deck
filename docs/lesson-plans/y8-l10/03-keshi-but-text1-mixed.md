# Lesson 10.3: 可是 (But) + Full Text 1 Dialogue

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 3 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use 可是 to contrast two ideas, and put together everything we've learned so far into the full Text 1 dialogue about vegetables.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and use 可是 to contrast a like/dislike with an obligation (我不太喜欢吃，可是我应该每天吃)
- I can read and understand the full Text 1 dialogue about vegetables
- I can answer comprehension questions about a short passage on vegetables
- I can interview a partner using cumulative questions about hobbies and habits

## Materials Needed
- Projector (Text 1 dialogue slide, reading comprehension passage)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed copy of Text 1 dialogue (p. 90) for choral/paired reading
- Printed reading comprehension passage + True/False worksheet (Workbook Ex.6)
- Printed picture set (8 pictures: 菜花儿/钢琴/电视/西红柿/小提琴/油画儿/篮球/跳舞) for sentence-writing

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 应该 (should) and vegetable vocabulary from Lessons 1–2
**How:** Correct the Teacher — teacher writes 3 sentences with deliberate errors (e.g. 我喜欢非常吃黄瓜。/ 你应该穿大衣今天很冷。/ 我喜欢吃菜花、土豆、生菜等等等。) and students find and fix each one on whiteboards.
**Connection:** "You now know how to name vegetables, say how much you like them, and give advice with 应该. Today we add one more piece — 可是 (but) — so you can say things like 'I don't really like vegetables, BUT I should eat them.' Then we'll read the whole vegetable conversation from the textbook."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Ask: "Can anyone guess what 可是 might mean, based on 但是 from Unit 3?" (可是 and 但是 are near-synonyms — both mean "but".)

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 可是 | kěshì | but |

Introduce the word: 汉字 → pinyin → English, class repeats twice (choral). Briefly compare with 但是 (Unit 3, Lesson 8): both mean "but"; 可是 is slightly more conversational but interchangeable in this context.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 我不太喜欢吃。可是我妈妈说我应该每天吃蔬菜。(I don't really like eating it. But my mum says I should eat vegetables every day.)

Model 2–3 examples, thinking aloud: "To say 'I don't like doing homework, but I should do it', I need 我不喜欢做作业 + 可是 + 我应该做作业 → 我不喜欢做作业，可是我应该做作业。" Second example: 我不太喜欢运动，可是我每天跑步。 Insert a silly one: 我非常喜欢吃土豆，可是妈妈只给我吃一个！(wait for reactions).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 可是 mean? Thumbs up if you know."
- "How would I say 'I like watching TV, but I should do my homework'? Write it on your whiteboard."

#### Text / Dialogue
**Text 1 (p. 90, CD Track 46)**
> 你喜欢吃蔬菜吗？我非常喜欢吃。你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？我喜欢吃黄瓜、菜花儿、土豆、生菜、西红柿等等。你呢？我不太喜欢吃。可是我妈妈说我应该每天吃蔬菜。

Read aloud, students follow with the printed copy. Read together (choral), then in pairs taking A/B roles. Comprehension questions: (1) What vegetables does the first speaker like? (2) Does the second speaker like vegetables? (3) What does the second speaker's mum say?

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Reading Comprehension (Workbook Ex.6) — We Do (8 min)
Students read the short passage about 天喜's mum making 蔬菜沙拉 (vegetable salad) and answer 4 True/False questions.
- **Scaffold:** Pre-teach 沙拉 (salad) and 花园 (garden) before reading; allow English annotations in the margin.
- **Extension:** After finishing, students write one sentence in Chinese summarising why the narrator now likes vegetables, using 可是.
- **OTR:** Whiteboard hold-up (T/F) for each of the 4 items, checked together.

#### Activity 2: Complete the 可是 Sentences (Textbook Act.7 + Workbook Ex.2) — We Do → You Do (7 min)
Students complete the 7 sentence starters from p. 93 (我不太喜欢运动，可是___ / 我不太喜欢看电视，可是___ / 我不喜欢做作业，可是___ / 我不太喜欢吃蔬菜，可是___ / 我不喜欢坐车，可是___ / 我不太喜欢上学，可是___ / 我不太喜欢音乐，可是___). Then, using the 8 pictures from Workbook Ex.2 (菜花儿/钢琴/电视/西红柿/小提琴/油画儿/篮球/跳舞), students write one sentence per picture expressing degree of liking, drawing from the reference word bank (非常喜欢/很喜欢/不太喜欢/不喜欢/很会/不太会/不会).
- **Scaffold:** First 可是 sentence done together as a class example; sentence frame for the picture task: 我(非常/很/不太)喜欢……
- **Extension:** For at least 3 pictures, students extend with 可是 (e.g. 我不太喜欢吃西红柿，可是我应该吃).
- **OTR:** Cold call 4–5 students to read one sentence aloud.

#### Activity 3: Cumulative Partner Interview (Textbook Act.3) — You Do (8 min)
In pairs, students ask and answer the 6 sample questions from p. 92 (name, nationality, age/grade, hobbies, sports/duration, art, homework/duration), using the Words for Reference box (最喜欢/非常喜欢/很喜欢/不太喜欢/不喜欢/很会/会/不太会/不会) to vary their answers.
- **Scaffold:** Questions displayed on the projector throughout; sentence frames for answers provided.
- **Extension:** Students report back one interesting fact about their partner to the class in a full Chinese sentence.
- **OTR:** Turn and talk — every student both asks and answers.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read chorally; class responds with target language for each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 4 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one sentence using 可是 about something you don't like but do anyway."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: a listening and character lesson — we'll practise the ie/üe pinyin sounds and learn 4 new simple characters that will help us with fruit words!"
