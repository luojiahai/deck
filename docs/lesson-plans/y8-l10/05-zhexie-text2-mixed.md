# 西瓜、梨 and 这些 — Text 2

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use 这些 / 那些 / 一些 to talk about groups of things, and to read the Text 2 dialogue about fruit.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write 西瓜 and 梨
- I can use 这些、那些、一些 with a noun (我不喜欢吃这些水果)
- I can read Text 2 aloud with a partner and answer questions about it
- I can ask who something belongs to (这些是谁的？)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l5-zhexie-text2`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Printed Text 2 handout, pinyin above characters, one per pair
- Printed 这些…是谁的？ role-play cards — eight objects, one set per group of four
- Printed translation sheet (WB Ex.15 format, four sentences)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The four fruits and 都 covering a list.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire — the ten food words in characters only, class says pinyin + English chorally, then a second pass at double speed. Then a whiteboard question: 苹果、香蕉、橘子，你都喜欢吃吗？ — everyone answers in a full sentence with 都 and holds it up. Teacher reads three aloud.

**Connection:** "You can say you like *all* of a list. Today: how to point at a list and say *these* — 这些."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, visible all lesson. Read aloud together. Hold up two fruits (or two pictures) in one hand and two in the other: "这些…… 那些……" — say it before explaining it, then ask the class what the difference must be.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 西瓜 | xīguā | watermelon | watermelon, whole and cut |
| 1 | 梨 | lí | pear | two pears |
| 2 | 这些 | zhèxiē | these | — (abstract) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我非常喜欢吃**西瓜**。 — I really like eating watermelon.
2. **梨**和苹果我都喜欢吃。 — I like both pears and apples.
3. 我喜欢吃**西瓜**和**梨**。 — straight from Text 2.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃西瓜。 · ______ 和 ______ 我都喜欢吃。
**Extension —** Two clauses with 可是, and both new words in the same sentence: 我非常喜欢吃西瓜，可是我不太喜欢吃梨。 Then add why, or when you eat it: 夏天我每天都吃西瓜。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **这些**水果很好。 — These fruits are good.
2. 我不喜欢吃**这些**水果。 — I don't like eating these fruits.
3. **这些**水果我都不喜欢吃，可是我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。 — from Text 2, carrying both cycles.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 这些 ________ 我 ________ 喜欢。
**Extension —** Write three sentences, one each with 这些 / 那些 / 一些, and make them about three different categories — fruit, vegetables, and something from an earlier unit (衣服, 小说, 画儿). No word bank.

**Recall slide:** 西瓜 梨 这些 苹果 香蕉 橘子 — characters only, all six of the lesson's fruit-side words. Class reads them back.

#### Word family — the 瓜 group (2 min, WB Ex.11 format)
On one board, three words sharing a character. Students copy them into their books and write the English beside each:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 西瓜 xīguā | watermelon | already known from today |
| 冬瓜 dōngguā | winter melon | Extra Word |
| 南瓜 nánguā | pumpkin | taught in Lesson 2 |

And the 菜 group beside it: 青菜 qīngcài · 生菜 shēngcài · 菜花儿 càihuār. Name the pattern out loud — a shared character is a shared category.

#### Text 2 (textbook p.95)

Pinyin shown above characters for this reading, hidden for the pair reading.

> A: 你喜欢吃什么水果？
> B: 苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃。
> A: 我不喜欢吃这些水果。我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。
> B: 你每天都吃水果吗？
> A: 我每天都吃两三种水果。

Teacher reads once at pace, students follow. Choral read. Three comprehension questions, Chinese answers pushed:

1. B 喜欢吃什么水果？ (苹果、香蕉、橘子，都喜欢)
2. A 喜欢吃什么水果？ (西瓜和梨)
3. A 每天吃水果吗？ (每天都吃两三种) — flag 两三种: it is next lesson's business, so just read it for now.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What's wrong with this? 这些个水果。" (些 is already the measure word)
- "Write the characters for xīguā." (whiteboards)
- "这些 or 那些 — which one for the fruit in my hand?" (这些)
- "In Text 2, does A like apples?" (No — 我不喜欢吃这些水果)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Text 2 in pairs — We Do (6 min)
Printed handout, pinyin visible. Pairs read A and B aloud, then swap, then read it again with the handout face down and only the characters on the projector. Teacher circulates listening for 橘 (jú, not jū) and the 些 neutral tone.

**Extension —** Perform it from memory with the fruits swapped — B names three fruits that are *not* in the book, A rejects them with 这些 and names two others. The last line stays as it is.

#### Activity 2: Translation with 这些 — You Do (8 min)
Printed sheet, WB Ex.15 format. Translate four sentences from Chinese to English in the book:

1. 我不喜欢吃这些水果。你们有西瓜吗？
2. 那些画儿我都不喜欢。你们有国画儿吗？
3. 这些电视节目我都不喜欢看。我们看动画片吧！
4. 我有一些衣服太小了，但是很好看。你要吗？

Then two sentences of their own, one with 这些 and one with 那些.

Give the 这些 / 那些 / 一些 word bank on paper to the pairs who need it; it is not on the slide.

**Extension —** Translate the other way as well, book closed, from the English back into Chinese — then write a fifth sentence that uses 这些 *and* 都 *and* 可是 together: 这些水果我都不喜欢吃，可是我妈妈说我应该每天吃。

#### Game: 这些是谁的？ (9 min)
Textbook p.99 Ex.16, run in groups of four with the object cards face down in the middle. A student takes two cards and asks the group: 这些 ______ 是你的吗？ The dialogue runs:

> A: 这些小说是你的吗？
> B: 不是我的。
> A: 那是谁的？
> B: 是小明的。

Object cards: 小说 · 杂志 · 小人书 · 画儿 · 网球 · 短裤 · 汗衫 · 衣服. Every group member must ask at least twice.

- **Setup needed:** One set of eight object cards per group of four, printed in characters only.
- **Harder version:** The asker may not use the card's word — they must describe the object in Chinese until someone names it, *then* ask the 这些…是谁的？ question. And the answer has to give a reason: 是小明的，因为他每天都看。

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads them aloud.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍 / 😐 / 👎 per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, WB Ex.14 format — 我家冰箱里有苹果、 ________ 。 Name at least three more fruits, in characters.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson, the last one on this topic: how to say 'two or three kinds' — 两三种."
