# Lesson 10.8: Consolidation and Review

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 8 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to bring together everything from this topic — vegetables, fruits, 应该, 可是, 这些/那些/一些, and 种 — in extended speaking and writing.

## Success Criteria
- I can hold a conversation about food likes/dislikes using 应该 and 可是
- I can ask "whose is this?" and answer using 是……的 (是小明的)
- I can build new words by combining known characters
- I can write a short paragraph describing someone's food habits and hobbies, in the style of the model essay

## Materials Needed
- Projector (essay model, word-building grid)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed possession-dialogue word list (小说/杂志/小人书/画儿/网球/短裤/汗衫/衣服)
- Printed word-building worksheet (Textbook Act.17, 20 items)
- Lined paper for the final short essay
- Self-assessment traffic-light cards (or thumbs) for the plenary

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Everything from Lessons 1–7 — vegetables, fruits, 应该, 可是, 这些/那些, 种
**How:** Chain response — first student says a food item and how they feel about it (我喜欢吃苹果), next student repeats and adds their own (我喜欢吃苹果，我不太喜欢吃西红柿), building a class-long chain around the circle/rows.
**Connection:** "Over the last 7 lessons you've built up a whole toolkit for talking about food and giving opinions and advice. Today we put it all together — speaking, a translation challenge, a word-building game, and a short piece of writing."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read the learning intention aloud together. Ask: "By the end of today, you should be able to hold a real conversation about food — let's see how far we've come."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### Review Vocabulary Recap
Quick-fire projector slide cycling through all Lesson 10 vocabulary (vegetables, fruits, 应该, 可是, 这些/那些/一些, 种) — class chorally says pinyin + English for each as it appears, at a brisk pace (roughly 1–2 seconds per word).

#### Key Sentence Pattern — Possession Review
> 这些小说是你的吗？不是我的。那是谁的？是小明的。(Are these novels yours? Not mine. Whose are they, then? They're Xiaoming's.)

This reviews the 是……的 possession pattern using 这些 from Lesson 6. Model with 2 examples: A: 这些杂志是你的吗？B: 不是，是老师的。

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Give me a sentence with 应该. Write it on your whiteboard."
- "Give me a sentence with 可是."
- "How do you say 'whose is this' in Chinese?" (这是谁的？)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Whose Is This? Partner Dialogues (Textbook Act.16) — We Do (7 min)
Using the word list (小说/杂志/小人书/画儿/网球/短裤/汗衫/衣服), pairs build dialogues following the model: A: 这些___是你的吗？ B: 不是我的。 A: 那是谁的？ B: 是___的。
- **Scaffold:** Model dialogue displayed throughout; word list provided.
- **Extension:** Students extend with a follow-up using 可是 (e.g. B: 是小明的，可是他不喜欢看).
- **OTR:** Every student performs at least one full dialogue exchange.

#### Activity 2: Translation Challenge (Textbook Act.15) — We Do → You Do (7 min)
Students translate the 4 given Chinese-to-English sentences (妈妈说我不应该看太多电视 / 老师说我们应该多说汉语 / 医生说我们应该每天吃蔬菜、水果，还要做运动 / 爸爸说他今天不回家吃晚饭), then write 4 more of their own C→E or E→C sentences using this lesson's grammar.
- **Scaffold:** Key vocabulary (应该, 医生, 还要, 做运动) pre-highlighted.
- **Extension:** The 4 original sentences must each use a different one of: 应该, 可是, 这些/那些, 种.
- **OTR:** Cold call 4–5 students to share one original sentence.

#### Game: Word-Building Grid (Textbook Act.17) — You Do (9 min)
Teams work through the 20-item word-building grid (黄___, 电___, 号___, 多___, 学___, etc.), adding a character to each base to form a valid two-character word — characters may be written if known, or pinyin if not. Most correctly completed items wins.
- **Setup needed:** Grid projected or printed per team; whiteboard for teams to write answers.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read chorally; class demonstrates each success criterion live — a 应该 sentence, a 可是 sentence, a 是……的 possession exchange, one word-building example.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light self-assessment against all 4 success criteria for today, plus a quick show of hands on the whole Lesson 10 topic (蔬菜、水果) overall confidence.
- **Exit ticket / Final task:** Students write a short paragraph (4–6 sentences) about a family member's food habits and hobbies, following the model essay structure from Workbook Ex.18 (nationality, appearance, job/role, food likes, hobbies) — collected as the wrap-up assessment for the whole Lesson 10 sequence.
- **Preview:** "Next, we move on to Lesson 11 — 一日三餐 (Three Meals a Day) — where we'll build on today's food vocabulary to talk about breakfast, lunch, and dinner."
