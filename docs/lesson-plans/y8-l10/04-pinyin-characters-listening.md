# Lesson 10.4: Pinyin Focus (ie/üe) + Simple Characters

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — 蔬菜、水果 (Vegetables and Fruits)
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Vegetables and Fruits 蔬菜、水果 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to distinguish the pinyin sounds ie and üe by ear, and to recognise 4 new simple characters that will help us read fruit-related words next lesson.

## Success Criteria
- I can hear the difference between ie and üe and add the correct tonal marks
- I can write and recognise the simple characters 食, 果, 欠, 石
- I can answer question-word questions (什么时候/怎么样/几岁 etc.) as a cumulative review

## Materials Needed
- Projector + audio (CD Track 48 — Activity 6 pinyin listening)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed simple-character worksheet (食 / 果 / 欠 / 石), with stroke-order guides
- Printed question-word review strips (Workbook Ex.16 style)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 应该 and 可是 from Lessons 2–3
**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards — teacher reads 4 English prompts ("You should eat vegetables"; "I don't like it, but I should do it"; etc.), students write the Chinese sentence and hold up.
**Connection:** "You've built up a lot of vegetable vocabulary and two useful grammar words. Today's a slightly different kind of lesson — we're going to sharpen our ears for tricky pinyin sounds, and learn 4 characters that will make next lesson's fruit vocabulary much easier to read."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Say two similar-sounding syllables aloud (e.g. "jie" vs "jue") and ask: "Could you hear the difference? This is what we're training today."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### Pinyin Focus: ie vs üe
Model the two sounds clearly, side by side, several times: jie / jue, xie / xue, que / qie. Explain the visual/spelling difference: "ie" keeps the "i", "üe" uses "ü" (the u with two dots — remember this from earlier units, e.g. 女). Class repeats each pair chorally after the teacher, exaggerating the vowel shape.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Which one did I say — jie or jue?" (teacher says one, class writes on whiteboards)
- "Show me the ü symbol on your whiteboard — two dots over the u."

#### Simple Characters (p. 94, Activity 9)
| 字 | 拼音 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 食 | shí | food |
| 果 | guǒ | fruit |
| 欠 | qiàn | owe |
| 石 | shí | stone |

Introduce each character with its picture-mnemonic from the textbook (食 looks like a covered food bowl; 果 shows fruit on a tree; 欠 shows a figure reaching/yawning; 石 shows a rock shape). Class repeats twice (choral). Point out that 果 is the character inside 苹果 (apple) and 水果 (fruit) — next lesson's topic.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Listen and Add Tonal Marks (Textbook Act.6, CD Track 48) — We Do (8 min)
Play the audio; students listen to 6 syllables (jiejue, xiejue, quejie, jielüe, juelie, jieyue — as printed on p. 93) and add the correct tone marks on their whiteboards.
- **Scaffold:** Play each item twice; write the "Practice Focus: ie üe" reminder on the board throughout.
- **Extension:** After checking answers, fluent speakers create 2 of their own ie/üe syllable pairs and quiz a partner.
- **OTR:** Whiteboard hold-up after every item.

#### Activity 2: Simple Character Tracing + Writing Relay — We Do → You Do (8 min)
Students trace 食/果/欠/石 three times each on the worksheet, then form small teams for a Writing Relay: one student per team runs to the board, writes one character from memory, then tags the next teammate.
- **Scaffold:** Stroke-order arrows shown on the worksheet and projected during the relay.
- **Extension:** Fastest-finishing team members write one word that contains each character (e.g. 苹果 for 果, if known) on the side of the board.
- **OTR:** 100% write, and every team member gets one relay turn.

#### Activity 3: Cumulative Question-Word Review (Workbook Ex.16 style) — You Do (7 min)
Project 8 fill-in-the-blank prompts using question words from across the year (怎么样/干什么/什么时候/多大/几岁/哪儿/什么样儿/怎么). Students fill blanks on whiteboards, then in pairs ask each other 3 of the questions aloud.
- **Scaffold:** Word bank of the 8 question words displayed throughout.
- **Extension:** Students write one original question using a 9th question word not in the box (e.g. 几点, 什么).
- **OTR:** Whiteboard hold-up, then paired oral practice.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; class demonstrates each — say an ie/üe pair, write a simple character, answer a question-word prompt.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Listen: teacher says one syllable. Write it with the correct tone mark." (one live example)
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we move from vegetables to fruit — 水果! You'll already recognise 果 from today's characters."
