# Parts of the Body (4): 厂车立革止虫 — radicals and how characters are built

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l4-radicals-structure`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l4-radicals-structure/) — 17 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning six new radicals and how to break a character into the parts it is built from.

## Success Criteria
- I can name the meaning of 厂, 车, 立, 革, 止 and 虫
- I can write one character that uses a given radical
- I can split a character into its parts and say which part is the radical

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l4-radicals-structure`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Printed radical cards for the sorting round: the six new radicals as headers, plus 12 character cards
- Two board pens for the Writing Relay
- Textbook p.117 (Ex.5, Ex.6)
- Workbook p.158 (Ex.1), p.168 (Ex.16), p.169 (Ex.18)

> **No new vocabulary this lesson.** The whole 50 minutes is characters, which is why it sits here — the words from Lessons 1–3 need a lesson of settling before the Text 2 grammar arrives. It is also direct rehearsal for Test Parts 2 and 3.

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's five words, and the twelve radicals from Workbook Ex.4.

**How:** **Correct the teacher (4 min)** — three sentences on screen, each with one deliberate error, students fix them on whiteboards:
- 她的牙齿很白。→ correct, say so (one of the three has to be right or they stop reading)
- 我有脸很小。→ 我的脸很小。／我有小脸。
- 他的皮肤很白，他有黑色头发。→ missing 的 before 头发

Then **flashcard rapid fire (3 min)** on the radicals from Ex.4: you show 目 氵 月 口 衤 艹, class calls the meaning.

**Connection:** "You already know twelve radicals. Today's six finish the set for this book — and then we take characters apart to see how they were made."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Write 鼻 very large on the board, in parts, as you read criterion 3 — 自 above 田 above 廾 — so the criterion is demonstrated before it's explained.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### The six radicals — three pairs, three cycles
Same rhythm as a vocabulary lesson: two at a time, then an example character, then students write.

| Cycle | Radical | Meaning | Example character | What to say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 厂 | cliff | 厅 tīng hall | A cliff face with a flat top — the shape *is* the meaning |
| 1 | 车（車） | vehicle | 辆 liàng (measure word for vehicles) | They know 车 as a word already; here it does a job inside another character |
| 2 | 立 | stand | 站 zhàn to stand | 站 is 立 (stand) + 占 — the meaning sits on the left |
| 2 | 革 | leather | 鞋 xié shoe | Shoes are made of leather; the radical tells you the material |
| 3 | 止 | stop | 步 bù step | Two feet, one after the other |
| 3 | 虫 | insect | 虾 xiā shrimp | Small creature — the radical covers insects and small animals both |

**After each pair:** students write both radicals **and** the example character on whiteboards, with the radical in a different colour so the split is visible. Hold up. 60 seconds per pair.

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** all six radicals, no meanings. Class calls the meanings back in order, then out of order.

#### Key idea: characters have parts, and one part is the radical
1. 鞋 = 革 (leather) + 圭 → radical on the **left**
2. 步 = 止 (stop/foot) + 少 → radical on **top**
3. 辆 = 车 (vehicle) + 两 → radical on the **left**
4. 鼻 = 自 + 田 + 廾 → **three** parts stacked, and this is a word they learnt in Lesson 1. ← the hardest one, and the point: the words they already know are built this way too.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "鞋 — what is it made of? How do you know?" (the 革 radical)
- "Write a character with 虫 in it." (whiteboards)
- "Is the radical in 站 on the left or the right?"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Radical sort with cards — We Do (6 min)
The six new radicals as headers on a desk. Twelve character cards to sort under them — 厅 厂 辆 车 站 位 鞋 革 步 止 虾 虫 — in pairs, 3 minutes. Then each pair must **justify two** of their placements out loud: "辆 goes under 车 because the vehicle radical is on the left."

**Extension —** Add the six radicals from Workbook Ex.16 (忄 口 衤 亻 夕 女) as extra headers and eight unlabelled characters from Lessons 13–14 to place under them. Then write, for each of the six, one character that is **not** on any card.

#### Activity 2: Draw the structure — You Do (8 min)
**Textbook p.117 Ex.6.** Nine characters to break into their parts: 班 鼻 起 嘴 灰 橙 差 蓝 套. Draw the structure box for each — left/right, top/bottom, or three-part — and mark which part is the radical. Solo, in the workbook, 6 minutes. Then answers on screen and students mark their own.

Note two that catch people out: **差** is not left-right, and **套** has 大 on top, which surprises them.

Then **Workbook p.158 Ex.1** as the writing follow-up: copy the six new radicals with correct stroke order. Stroke order matters here because 革 and 虫 both go wrong in the same place — the vertical goes in before the bottom horizontal.

**Extension —** **Workbook p.168 Ex.16**: write **two** characters for each of the six radicals 忄 口 衤 亻 夕 女 — twelve characters, none of them allowed to be one that was on screen today. Then use three of them in phrases.

#### Activity 3: Writing Relay (6 min)
Two teams, two board pens. You call a **radical meaning** in English — "leather", "vehicle", "stop" — and the first student runs up, writes a character containing that radical, and passes the pen. A character already on the board doesn't count. Two minutes per round, highest count wins.
- **Setup needed:** board space split in two, two pens
- **Harder version:** the character must be written **and** used in a spoken phrase before the pen passes; and for fluent students, you call the radical in Chinese by its shape rather than its meaning.

#### Activity 4: Add a word to make a phrase (3 min)
**Workbook p.169 Ex.18**, quick-fire on whiteboards, first five only: 下午→午__ · 长裤→裤__ · 中学→学__ · 大火→火__ · 毛衣→衣__. This is the character-chain format the test likes, and it needs no new teaching — just speed.

**Extension —** Finish all nine, then build a three-link chain from any of them: 下午 → 午饭 → 饭馆.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** six radicals called back chorally; a student demonstrates criterion 3 on 嘴 at the board.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs per criterion. Expect 😐 on criterion 3 — that's fine, it comes back in Lesson 8.
- **Exit ticket:** *Write one character for the radical 虫, and circle the radical in it.*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson the big question of the whole unit: 你长什么样？— what do you look like?"
