# Parts of the Body (1): 眼睛、耳朵、鼻子、嘴巴 — Text 1

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 1 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l1-face-parts`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l1-face-parts/) — 20 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the parts of the face in Chinese and say whether they are big or small.

## Success Criteria
- I can name 眼睛, 耳朵, 鼻子 and 嘴巴 and point to each one
- I can build a phrase with 大 or 小 — 大眼睛, 小嘴巴
- I can say one sentence about my own face using 我有……

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l1-face-parts`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Printed Slap the Character cards: 眼睛 耳朵 鼻子 嘴巴 大 小 长 短 (two sets, one per team)
- Devices for Kahoot (4 new words + the eight L14 garments as distractors)
- Coloured pencils for the Workbook p.163 colouring task

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The ten garments and ten colours from Lessons 13–14, plus the Unit 4 date questions the workbook drops into this lesson.

**How:** Two rounds, both on whiteboards.

1. **Dates (3 min)** — **Workbook p.162 Ex.6** on screen. Ask the four questions aloud, students write: 今天几月几号？· 今天星期几？· 六月十日是星期几？· 七月一日是星期几？ Hold up. This is Unit 4 material and it is on the workbook page for this lesson, so it gets two minutes here rather than a lesson of its own.
2. **Clothing (4 min)** — **Workbook p.163 Ex.8**, the first three figures only: colour them to match and write one sentence about each, on the model 他穿灰色的校服。 Three figures is all there is time for; the other three come back in next lesson's review. Watch for the dropped 的.

**Connection:** "You can describe what someone is wearing. Today you start describing the person inside the clothes — and we begin with the face."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Draw a large blank face outline on the whiteboard beside the screen as you read the criteria — it stays there all lesson and gets labelled during the I Do.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 眼睛 | yǎnjing | eye | Single eye, line drawing with lashes (SVG) |
| 1 | 耳朵 | ěrduo | ear | Ear in profile (SVG) |
| 2 | 鼻子 | bízi | nose | Nose in profile (SVG) |
| 2 | 嘴巴 | zuǐba | mouth | Smiling mouth (SVG) |

Point out the radical clue on the first slide: **眼** and **睛** both carry 目 (eye) — the character tells you what part of the body it is before you know the word. Model each word twice, class repeats twice, then everyone points to their own.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我的**眼睛**很大。 — My eyes are big.
2. 他的**耳朵**不大。 — His ears aren't big.
3. 她有大**眼睛**和小**耳朵**。 — She has big eyes and small ears.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的 ________ 很 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two clauses about two different people: 我的眼睛很大，我妹妹的眼睛不大。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我的**鼻子**不大。 — My nose isn't big.
2. 她的**嘴巴**很小。 — Her mouth is small.
3. 他有大**鼻子**和大**嘴巴**。 — He has a big nose and a big mouth.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 他有 ________ 鼻子和 ________ 嘴巴。
**Extension —** All four words in one sentence, joined with 、and 和: 她有大大的眼睛、小鼻子和小嘴巴。 (You'll meet 大大的 properly in Lesson 6 — take it if they reach for it.)

#### Vocabulary summary board
All four words, characters and pictures only, no pinyin:
**眼睛 · 耳朵 · 鼻子 · 嘴巴**
Stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**有 + 大/小 + body part**, and the alternative **的 + body part + 很大/很小**.

1. 我有大眼睛。
2. 我的眼睛很大。 ← same meaning, different frame
3. 他有小嘴巴。
4. 他有大耳朵和小眼睛，他的鼻子不大也不小。 ← two frames plus a negative, the hardest one.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Point to your 鼻子." (whole class, gesture response)
- "Which character means eye — 目 or 口? Thumbs up for the first."
- "What's wrong with 我有眼睛大？" (word order: the adjective comes before the noun)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Label the four faces — We Do (7 min)
**Textbook p.115 Ex.1** on screen with the summary board beside it. Take the first cartoon face together as a class: 他有大眼睛和小嘴巴。 Then pairs work through the remaining six, one sentence each, out loud before writing two of them down. Cold call four pairs.

Then **Workbook p.160 Ex.3** as a 90-second race: match the seven pictures to a)–g). Answers by show of hands.

**Extension —** Pairs describe a face that is *not* on the page — one partner describes any face in the room or any teacher, using at least three of the four words and one negative (他的耳朵不大), the other guesses who. No summary board: turn away from the screen.

#### Activity 2: Slap the Character — You Do (7 min)
Two teams, printed cards spread face-up on a desk at the front: 眼睛 耳朵 鼻子 嘴巴 大 小 长 短. One player per team. You say a **phrase**, not a word — 大眼睛, 小嘴巴, 大耳朵 — and the player must slap **both** cards in the right order. First to get both, in order, wins the point.
- **Setup needed:** cards printed, two sets
- **Harder version:** for fluent students, say the phrase in English and require the two cards plus a spoken full sentence using it (大眼睛 → 她有大眼睛。) before the point counts.

#### Activity 3: 老师说 — point to your face (4 min)
Simon Says with the four new words plus 手 and 头 in English so they hear tomorrow's words coming. Fast, standing. Out if you point to the wrong part or if you move when the instruction wasn't 老师说.
- **Harder version:** the caller is a student, and they must call in Chinese only.

#### Game: Kahoot — 眼睛/耳朵/鼻子/嘴巴 (5 min)
Twelve questions on devices: character → English (4), picture → character (4), and four mixed in from Lesson 14 (灰色的毛衣, 校服) so the quiz is cumulative, not just today.
- **Setup needed:** devices, quiz prepared in advance
- **Harder version:** the last two questions give a full sentence with one error in it (我有眼睛大。) and ask which is correct.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student reads each one aloud; class points to the part of their own face for criterion 1.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs for each criterion — 👍 / 😐 / 👎.
- **Exit ticket:** on a slip — *Write one sentence about your own face using 我有.* Collected at the door.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we go below the neck — 手, 脚, 腿, 头 — and label a whole person."
