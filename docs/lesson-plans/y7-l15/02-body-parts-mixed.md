# Parts of the Body (2): 手、脚、腿、头 — labelling a whole person

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l2-body-parts`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l2-body-parts/) — 18 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the parts of the body below the neck and describe them as long, short, big or small.

## Success Criteria
- I can name 手, 脚, 腿 and 头 and point to each one
- I can label all eight body parts on a drawing of a person
- I can say and write phrases like 长腿, 大手, 小脚

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l2-body-parts`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Printed A4 figure outline, one per pair, for the labelling task
- Coloured pencils to finish Workbook p.163 Ex.8
- Textbook p.116 (Ex.3, Extra Words box), p.121 (Ex.10)
- Workbook pp.159–160 (Ex.2), p.161 (Ex.5), p.162 (Ex.7), p.163 (Ex.8, figures 4–6)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's four face words and the 有 + 大/小 + body part frame.

**How:** **Flashcard rapid fire (3 min)** — 眼睛 耳朵 鼻子 嘴巴 in characters only on screen, class says pinyin then English chorally. Then reverse: you say English, they write the characters on whiteboards. Watch the 睛/晴 confusion and the 朵 stroke order.

**Workbook p.163 Ex.8, figures 4–6 (4 min)** — finish the colouring task started last lesson: colour each figure and write one sentence about what they're wearing (他穿灰色的校服。). Then, if there's a minute left, pair dialogue: A: 你的眼睛大吗？B: 大。/ 不大。 — the answer repeats the verb, never 是。

**Connection:** "Four words gets you a face. Today's four gets you a whole person — which is what the test asks you to describe."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Stand up and point to each of the four new parts on yourself as you read criterion 1, and have the class mirror you — they've now heard and done all four before the teaching starts.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 手 | shǒu | hand | Open hand, five fingers (SVG) |
| 1 | 头（頭） | tóu | head | Head and shoulders in outline (SVG) |
| 2 | 脚 | jiǎo | foot | Foot in profile (SVG) |
| 2 | 腿 | tuǐ | leg | Single leg, hip to ankle (SVG) |

Radical note worth making: **脚** and **腿** both carry 月 — the flesh radical, not the moon. Any character with 月 on the left is very often a part of the body. That is the pattern behind Lesson 4's radical work, so plant it now.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我的**手**很小。 — My hands are small.
2. 他的**头**很大。 — His head is big.
3. 她有小**手**和小**头**。 — She has small hands and a small head.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的 ________ 很 ________ 。
**Extension —** A sentence comparing two people, no frame on screen: 我的手很小，我爸爸的手很大。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我的**脚**不大。 — My feet aren't big.
2. 她有长**腿**。 — She has long legs.
3. 他的**腿**很长，他的**脚**很大。 — His legs are long and his feet are big.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 他有 ________ 腿和 ________ 脚。
**Extension —** Use 长/短 with 腿 and 大/小 with 手/脚 in one sentence and add why it matters: 他有长腿，所以他跑得很快。(Take 所以 if they have it — don't teach it.)

#### Vocabulary summary board
All eight body words now known, characters and pictures only:
**眼睛 · 耳朵 · 鼻子 · 嘴巴 · 手 · 头 · 脚 · 腿**
Stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
Same two frames as last lesson, now carrying the new words and the 长/短 pair:
1. 我有大手。
2. 我的腿很长。
3. 她有小脚和长腿。
4. 他的头不大，他有大手和大脚。 ← negative plus two positives in one breath.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "手 or 脚 — which one is at the bottom? Point."
- "Write the character with the flesh radical in it: 腿 or 眼?" (whiteboards)
- "Correct me: 她有腿长。"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Label the figure — We Do (7 min)
**Textbook p.116 Ex.3.** Each pair gets the printed figure outline. Label all eight parts in characters from memory first — 3 minutes, summary board **off** the screen. Then the board comes back on and pairs correct their own in a different colour pen. Cold call: which one did most pairs miss?

Then add the **Extra Words box (p.116)** as a listening-and-adding round — you say 脸, 牙齿, 舌头, 皮肤, 手指头 and show the picture; pairs add the labels they can. These five are properly taught next lesson, so this is exposure, not assessment.

**Extension —** Pairs write four sentences about their labelled figure, each using a different body part and at least one negative. Then one pair's four sentences are read aloud while the class draws what they hear.

#### Activity 2: Stroke order, then draw the phrase — You Do (9 min)
**Workbook pp.159–160 Ex.2** first — copy all ten Text 1 characters with correct stroke order: 眼 睛 耳 朵 鼻 嘴 巴 手 脚 腿. All ten are known now, which is why this waits until today rather than being split across two lessons. The two to watch at the board: 朵 (the 几 sits under 乃, and the vertical goes last) and 鼻, which is three stacked parts and the character most often written in the wrong order.

Then **Workbook p.161 Ex.5** — six phrases to draw: 大鼻子 · 小嘴巴 · 大眼睛 · 长腿 · 大耳朵 · 小手. Quick sketches, 30 seconds each. Then **p.162 Ex.7** in writing: answer the four questions about your own body — 你的眼睛大吗？你的嘴巴小吗？你的腿长吗？你的耳朵大吗？ Full-sentence answers, not 大。/ 不大。

**Extension —** Instead of the four given questions, write six sentences describing a person in the room part by part, without naming them. Swap with a partner, who reads it and says who it is. Every sentence must use a different body part.

#### Game: 老师说 — Simon Says (7 min)
**Textbook p.121 Ex.10.** You call a body part in Chinese, students point to it on themselves. Wrong part, or moving when the call didn't begin 老师说, and you're out. Standing, whole class, runs down to a winner.
- **Setup needed:** nothing
- **Harder version:** the last three rounds call a **phrase** rather than a word — 大眼睛, 长腿 — and the caller becomes the last student standing, calling in Chinese with no English.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** class points to all eight parts in sequence as you name them; a student reads criterion 3.
- **Self-assessment:** whiteboards — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** *Write two phrases: one with 长 and one with 大, each with a different body part.*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: five more body words — 脸, 牙齿, 舌头 — and a pinyin listening test where I read and you write the tone marks."
