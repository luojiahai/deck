# Parts of the Body (6): 大大的眼睛 — reduplication, 头发, 不……也不……

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 6 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l6-reduplication-hair`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l6-reduplication-hair/) — 15 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe someone's features vividly by doubling the adjective, and to describe hair.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 头发 and describe it with a colour and a length
- I can double an adjective for emphasis — 大大的眼睛, 高高的鼻子, 小小的嘴巴
- I can use 不……也不…… to say something is neither one thing nor the other

- Printed Slap the Board cards: 15 characters — 眼睛 耳朵 鼻子 嘴巴 手 脚 腿 头 头发 高 矮 长 短 大 小

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 长得, 高, 矮, 样 from last lesson, and the two readings of 长.

**How:** **Quick quiz on whiteboards (5 min)**, five items:
1. Translate: "My dad is tall." → 我爸爸很高。／我爸爸长得很高。
2. Translate: "She is neither tall nor short." → 她不高也不矮。
3. Read aloud: 他长得很高，他穿长裤。 (cold call two students — checking zhǎng then cháng)
4. Fix it: 他长得大眼睛。→ 他有大眼睛。
5. Ask me what I look like, in Chinese. → 你长什么样？

Then **3 minutes pair dialogue**: A: 你长什么样？B answers in two sentences. Swap.

**Connection:** "Last lesson you said 她长得矮. The book actually says 她长得矮矮的 — it says it twice. Today you find out why, and it makes every description you write sound like a person instead of a form."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Write 大 and then 大大的 underneath it on the board, and say the pair aloud with the second one stretched — the difference is audible before it's explained.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 头发（頭髮） | tóufa | hair | Head in outline with long hair (SVG) |
| 1 | 得 | de | (particle after a verb) | — (abstract: no picture) |

Only two words today, and one of them is a particle they've been using since last lesson without naming it. That's deliberate — the grammar is the content of this lesson, not the vocabulary. **发** is the character to flag: the traditional form 髮 has the hair radical on top, which is why the simplified 发 looks unrelated to hair.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 她的**头发**很长。 — Her hair is long.
2. 我的**头发**是黑色的。 — My hair is black.
3. 她的**头发**不长也不短。 — Her hair is neither long nor short.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的头发 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two clauses, colour **and** length, about two different people: 我的头发是黑色的，很短；我妈妈的头发很长。

#### Key Sentence Pattern 1 — doubling the adjective (p.119 NOTE)
**adjective + adjective + 的 + noun.** Doubling makes the description warmer and more vivid — it is what a person says, not what a form says.

| Plain | Doubled |
|---|---|
| 大眼睛 | 大大的眼睛 |
| 高鼻子 | 高高的鼻子 |
| 小嘴巴 | 小小的嘴巴 |
| 黑头发 | 黑黑的头发 |

1. 她有大大的眼睛。
2. 他有高高的鼻子和小小的嘴巴。
3. 她长得矮矮的。 ← the doubled adjective after 长得, straight from Text 2
4. 他长得高高的，他有黑黑的短头发和大大的眼睛。 ← 长得 + doubled, then two doubled features. This is the target sentence of the whole unit.

**The 的 is not optional when the adjective is doubled.** 大大的眼睛 ✓ · 大大眼睛 ✗

#### Key Sentence Pattern 2 — 不……也不……
**不 + adj + 也不 + adj** = neither … nor …, and the two adjectives are opposites.
- 她的头发不长也不短。
- 他不高也不矮。
- 我的手不大也不小。

#### Text 2 in full (p.118)
Read aloud twice, then choral, then pairs read it to each other:

> 她长得矮矮的。她有大大的眼睛、高高的鼻子和小小的嘴巴。她的头发不长也不短。你长什么样？

**Comprehension questions (in Chinese):** 她的眼睛大吗？· 她的头发长吗？· 她长得高吗？

Point at the 、and the 和 in sentence two — three features listed, 顿号 between, 和 before the last. That's the Lesson 14 listing rule doing a new job.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Plain → doubled — We Do (6 min)
Eight plain phrases on screen, students rewrite each as a doubled phrase on whiteboards, then say it in a full sentence with 有: 大眼睛 → 大大的眼睛 → 她有大大的眼睛。 Fast, choral for the last three.

Then **Textbook p.119 Ex.7** with **both** frames for all eight pictures, in pairs: 他有大大的眼睛。／他的眼睛大大的。

**Extension —** For four of the eight pictures, produce a three-part sentence: height with 长得 + doubled, two doubled features, and one 不……也不…… clause about the hair. Spoken first, then written.

#### Activity 2: Write a few sentences about each picture — You Do (9 min)
**Workbook p.167 Ex.13** — the most valuable writing exercise in the lesson, because its worked example is exactly the paragraph the test asks for. Model it on screen:

> 她长得矮矮的。她有大大的眼睛、小小的鼻子和嘴巴。她的头发不长。

Students then write three or four sentences for each of the pictures. Circulate; the two errors to catch are the dropped 的 after a doubled adjective, and 长得 followed by a noun.

Then finish **Workbook p.165 Ex.10** (figures 5–8 from last lesson) using doubled adjectives this time.

**Extension —** Write the paragraph for one picture, then rewrite it as a **question-and-answer dialogue** between two people about that person — A asks four questions, B answers with the paragraph's content broken across the answers.

#### Game: Slap the Board (6 min)
**Textbook p.119 Ex.8.** Two teams, fifteen character cards face up. You call a **doubled phrase** — 大大的眼睛, 小小的嘴巴, 黑黑的头发 — and the player slaps the adjective card and the noun card, in that order. Both, in order, or no point.
- **Setup needed:** 15 cards printed
- **Harder version:** you call the phrase in English, and the player must slap both cards and then use the phrase in a sentence with 长得 in it before the point counts.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** choral read; for criterion 2, call four plain phrases and have the class answer with the doubled version.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** *Write one sentence with a doubled adjective and one with 不……也不……* — two sentences, on a slip.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we put it all together out loud — Guess Who, and describing a celebrity for the class to identify."
