# Parts of the Body (7): 她长什么样？— Guess Who and the unit Q&A

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l7-describing-people`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l7-describing-people/) — 13 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe a whole person out loud so that someone else can work out who it is.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 她长什么样？and answer with height, features and hair
- I can answer 你穿什么校服？and 你喜欢你的校服吗？
- I can understand a spoken description well enough to say if it's true or false

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Doubled adjectives, 头发, 不……也不…… — everything needed to describe a person in one breath.

**How:** **Listening true/false — Textbook p.121 Ex.11 (T73).** You read each exchange twice; students tick or cross against the six pictures in the book. This is the CD track, read by you:

> 1) A：你爸爸长什么样？B：他长得高高的。
> 2) A：你妈妈长什么样？B：她长得不高也不矮。
> 3) A：你哥哥长什么样？B：他有大眼睛，头发短短的。
> 4) A：你妹妹长什么样？B：她有小小的眼睛和小小的嘴巴。
> 5) A：你弟弟长什么样？B：他有大大的眼睛和小小的嘴巴。
> 6) A：你姐姐长什么样？B：她长得矮矮的，头发长长的。

Mark it together. Note for the class that every single answer uses either 长得 or 有 — those two verbs carry the whole topic.

**Connection:** "You've heard six people described. Today you do the describing, and the class has to guess who you mean."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Describe a member of staff everyone knows without naming them, in three sentences, and let the class shout the answer — then read the criteria. That's the lesson in thirty seconds.

### I Do (10–20 min)

> **No new vocabulary this lesson.** Everything is recycled; the new material is two questions from the Revision list and the structure of a spoken description.

#### The two Revision questions the textbook never teaches
**Workbook pp.170–171** expects both of these on the test, and neither appears anywhere in the textbook lesson. They get taught explicitly, here:

1. **你穿什么校服？** → 我穿白色的衬衫和蓝色的长裤。
2. **你喜欢你的校服吗？** → 喜欢。／不喜欢。

Model each twice, class repeats twice, then whiteboards: everyone writes their own answer to Q1 with their real uniform colours, and their honest answer to Q2. Take five aloud.

#### The full Revision Q&A block (p.171)
All seven questions on screen, drilled as a chain round the room — one student asks, the next answers and then asks the following question:

1. 你喜欢什么颜色？→ 红色、橙色和蓝色。
2. 你喜欢黑色吗？→ 不喜欢。
3. 你喜欢穿什么衣服？→ 汗衫和牛仔裤。
4. 你上学穿校服吗？→ 穿。
5. 你穿什么校服？→ 白色的衬衫和蓝色的长裤。
6. 你喜欢你的校服吗？→ 不喜欢。
7. 你哥哥长什么样？→ 他长得高高的。他有大眼睛和黑色的短发。

**Point out Q4 and Q6:** the answer to a 吗 question repeats the verb — 穿。/ 喜欢。 — never 是。That's Test Part 9.

#### How a description is built — the three-beat shape
Every good answer to 长什么样 has the same three beats, and the model is Q7 above:
1. **Height** — 他长得高高的。
2. **Features** — 他有大大的眼睛和高高的鼻子。
3. **Hair** — 他的头发不长也不短。／她有黑色的长发。

Add clothing as a fourth beat and you have Test Part 11 complete.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Make a dialogue — We Do (6 min)
**Textbook p.120 Ex.9.** Pairs build the dialogue from the frame: A：她长什么样？B：她长得不高。她有…… Then the second half of the exercise — each pair picks a **celebrity** and B describes them in three beats while A, and then the class, guesses.

**Extension —** The describing partner must not use 高 or 矮 at all, so the description has to run on features, hair and clothing alone — and must include one 不……也不…… clause. Then swap and do it again about a person the guesser chooses, with no preparation time.

#### Activity 2: Guess Who — tournament (8 min)
**Textbook p.123 Ex.13**, run as a knockout. A student describes a member of the class or a teacher, in Chinese only, minimum three sentences, no names and no pointing. The class guesses; a correct guess in under three sentences wins the *describer* nothing, so vague descriptions cost you. Model the book's example first:

> 他长得高高的。他有黑色的短发。他是中国人。→ 王老师。

Run it in two halves of the room simultaneously for the first four minutes so everyone describes, then bring the best four describers to the front for the final rounds.
- **Setup needed:** nothing, though the celebrity cards are a fallback for anyone stuck
- **Harder version:** the describer takes questions from the class before the guess — 他的头发长吗？ — and must answer in full sentences, not 对/不对.

#### Activity 3: Group interview — 介绍一个同学的妈妈 (6 min)
**Textbook p.122 Ex.12.** Groups of three. Each student is interviewed about their mother using the book's twelve questions, and the group then introduces one of the three mothers to the class. The questions run from 她叫什么名字？and 她多大？through to the three that make this the cumulative task of the whole unit:

> 10) 她喜欢什么颜色？ 11) 她喜欢穿什么衣服？ 12) 她长什么样？

Any adult in their life substitutes fine if a mother isn't the right person to describe — say so when you set it up, don't wait to be asked.

**Extension —** The reporting student presents without notes, and must include one 不……也不…… clause and one doubled adjective. The rest of the group then takes two questions from the class about the person they just described.

#### Activity 4: Listening — 小英 (3 min)
**Textbook p.123 Ex.14 (T74).** Read the passage twice at normal speed; students tick or cross the eight statements in the book.

> 小英今年十一岁，上小学五年级。她长得高高的。她有大大的眼睛，高高的鼻子和大大的嘴巴。她有黑色的长发。她穿衬衫和裙子。

Mark together. Flag **长发** — the book uses 长发 here rather than 头发 for "long hair"; both are correct and they should recognise it in reading.

**Extension —** After the second reading, reproduce the whole passage from memory in writing, then compare with a partner and count what each of you missed.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** the seven Revision questions read round the room, one student each.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs per criterion; ask specifically who still hesitates on criterion 2, since it's new today.
- **Exit ticket:** *Answer in Chinese: 你穿什么校服？你喜欢你的校服吗？* Two answers, one slip.
- **Preview:** "Last lesson of the book: you read about 毛小红, and then you write the same thing about yourself."
