# Parts of the Body (5): 你长什么样？— 长得、高、矮

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l5-changshenmeyang`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l5-changshenmeyang/) — 18 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask what someone looks like and to answer by describing their height.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 你长什么样？and 他长什么样？
- I can answer with 他长得很高。／她长得不高。
- I can read 长 correctly as both cháng (long) and zhǎng (grow)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The six new radicals, and the thirteen body words — because from today the lesson stops being about single words and starts being about whole descriptions.

**How:**

1. **Radicals (2 min)** — six on screen, class calls the meanings, then two students write one character each at the board.
2. **Slap the board with phrases (5 min)** — thirteen body-word cards face up, you call a **phrase** including 大/小/长/短 (长腿, 小嘴巴, 大手), two students race to slap the noun. Ten calls, then swap the players.

**Connection:** "You can describe a nose and a leg. But if I ask you 你长什么样？— what do you look like? — you need one sentence that covers the whole person. That starts today."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Stand two volunteers of clearly different heights at the front and say 他长得很高。她长得不高。 pointing at each — then read the criteria. The pattern is heard before it is taught.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 高 | gāo | tall | Tall figure beside a short one, the tall one marked (SVG) |
| 1 | 矮 | ǎi | short (in height) | The same pair, the short one marked (SVG) |
| 2 | 长（長） | zhǎng | to grow | — (abstract: no picture) |
| 2 | 样（樣） | yàng | appearance | — (abstract: no picture) |

🔴 **The 长 problem, and say it out loud.** They have known 长 as **cháng** (long) since Lesson 14 — 长裤, 长腿. The same character is **zhǎng** here and means *grow*. One character, two readings, both in this unit. Put the pair on screen side by side: 长裤 chángkù · 长得 zhǎng de. This is the single most likely error in the next three lessons.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我爸爸很**高**。 — My dad is tall.
2. 我妹妹很**矮**。 — My little sister is short.
3. 我不**高**也不**矮**。 — I'm neither tall nor short.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ________ ，我 ________ 很 ________ 。
**Extension —** Compare three people in your family by height in two clauses, using 也: 我爸爸很高，我妈妈不高也不矮，我很矮。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 你**长**什么**样**？ — What do you look like?
2. 他**长**得很高。 — He's tall. (literally: he grows tall)
3. 她**长**得不高，她的头发很**长**。 ← both readings of 长 in one sentence: zhǎng then cháng.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我长得 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write the question and your answer, then the same question and answer about a family member, using a different height word each time.

#### Vocabulary summary board
**高 · 矮 · 长得 · 样** with the thirteen body words underneath in smaller characters. Stays up through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**你长什么样？→ 我长得 + 高/矮/不高也不矮。**

1. 你长什么样？
2. 我长得很高。
3. 她长得不高。
4. 他长得不高也不矮，他有大眼睛和长腿。 ← the double negative plus a body-part clause. This is the shape of the answer the test wants.

**Note for the class:** 长得 is followed by how someone *is*, never by a noun. 他长得很高 ✓ · 他长得大眼睛 ✗ — for body parts you still need 有 or 的.

#### Text 2 — first half (p.118)
Read aloud twice, class follows, then choral read:

> 她长得矮矮的。她的头发不长也不短。你长什么样？

Pinyin on the slide for Y7. 矮矮的 is deliberately left unexplained beyond "saying it twice makes it stronger — that's next lesson"; today they only need to read it.

**Comprehension questions:** 她高吗？· 她的头发长吗？· What is the last sentence asking you?

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 他长什么样？— pair Q&A on the pictures — We Do (8 min)
**Textbook p.119 Ex.7**, using the plain frames for now: 他有大眼睛。／他的眼睛很大。 Work the first picture as a class, adding a height sentence in front: 他长得很高。他有大眼睛。 Then pairs take the remaining seven, A asks 他长什么样？and B gives **two** sentences — one height, one body part. Swap every picture.

**Extension —** B gives four sentences per picture with no frame on screen — height, two body parts, and hair length — and A then repeats back what B said in the third person as a report to the class: 他说他长得很高……

#### Activity 2: Stroke order and sentence writing — You Do (7 min)
**Workbook p.164 Ex.9** — copy 得 矮 高 头 发 样 with stroke order. 矮 is the one to watch: 矢 on the left, and the bottom right is 女, not 攵.

Then **Workbook p.165 Ex.10** first four figures: write one sentence about each. Model the first: 他长得很高。

**Extension —** For all eight figures, write two sentences each — one with 长得, one with 有 — and make two of the eight negative (他长得不高).

#### Activity 3: 20 Questions — guess the person (6 min)
A student holds a card with a well-known figure on it. The class asks yes/no questions in Chinese only — 他长得很高吗？他有黑色的头发吗？他是中国人吗？ — up to twenty, then guesses. Two or three rounds.
- **Setup needed:** eight printed cards
- **Harder version:** the holder may only answer 对 or 不对, and the class must include at least one question using 长什么样 answered in a full sentence before they are allowed to guess.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** ask criterion 3 as a question — "Read this aloud: 他长得很高，他穿长裤。" Two readings of 长, one sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** whiteboards, 👍 / 😐 / 👎 per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** *Answer in Chinese: 你长什么样？* One sentence.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: why 她长得矮矮的 says 矮 twice — and how to talk about hair."
