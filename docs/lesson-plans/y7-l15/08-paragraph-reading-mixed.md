# Parts of the Body (8): 毛小红 — reading a description and writing your own

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 8 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l8-paragraph-reading`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l8-paragraph-reading/) — 14 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning to read a full description of a person, and to write the same kind of description about ourselves.

## Success Criteria
- I can read a short passage about a person and answer true/false questions on it
- I can write four or more sentences describing myself — height, features, hair, clothes
- I can put jumbled words back into the right order

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l8-paragraph-reading`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Devices for the closing Kahoot (whole-unit: colours, clothing, body parts, radicals)
- Workbook p.166 (Ex.11), p.167 (Ex.13, Ex.14), p.168 (Ex.15, Ex.17), p.169 (Ex.18, Ex.19)

> **Last lesson of the book.** It teaches and consolidates like the other seven — it is not a 复习 lesson — but the plenary looks back over the whole sequence, and the Kahoot spans the unit.

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The three-beat description shape from last lesson, plus the seven Revision questions.

**How:** **Workbook p.166 Ex.11 (4 min)** — the 6×6 character grid: circle twelve phrases and write them out. It mixes this lesson's body words (眼睛 耳朵 嘴巴 腿 鼻子 头发) with Lesson 13–14 clothing and colours (校服 外套 衬衫 粉红色 牛仔裤) and the bare adjectives 长 and 短, so it is a recall test of the entire unit in four minutes. Solo, then answers on screen.

**Correct the teacher (4 min)** — four sentences on screen, students fix them on whiteboards:
- 她长得大大的眼睛。→ 她有大大的眼睛。
- 我的头发很长也很短。→ 我的头发不长也不短。
- 他有大大眼睛。→ 他有大大的眼睛。
- 你穿什么校服？→ 是白色的衬衫。→ 我穿白色的衬衫。

**Connection:** "Every one of those four errors appears in the test. Today you write a whole paragraph, so let's make sure none of them are in it."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Read out last lesson's 小英 passage once more, then say: by the end of today, every one of you will have written one of these about yourself.

### I Do (10–20 min)

> **No new vocabulary.** The lesson's content is reading a longer text than any so far, and building a paragraph.

#### Reading — 毛小红 (Workbook p.169 Ex.19)
The best reading in the unit, because it is the only text that combines everything: age, nationality, body description, hair, uniform, today's outfit, and an opinion about a colour. That is exactly the span of Test Part 11.

Read it through three times: you read aloud while they follow, then choral, then silently on their own with a pen in hand.

**What the passage contains** — 毛小红 is twelve, Chinese, has big eyes, a small nose, big ears and short black hair; she wears uniform to school, today she's in a white shirt and a blue short skirt, and she doesn't like her uniform or its colour.

Then the **five true/false questions** in the book. Students answer solo, then compare with a partner and have to agree before you take answers — arguing about a true/false is where the reading actually happens.

**Comprehension push (in Chinese, for those who can):** 她几岁？· 她的头发长吗？· 她喜欢她的校服吗？· 她今天穿什么？

#### The model paragraph (Workbook p.167 Ex.13)
Back on screen, the worked example that has been the target all sequence:

> 她长得矮矮的。她有大大的眼睛、小小的鼻子和嘴巴。她的头发不长。

Annotate it live in a second colour: beat 1 height · beat 2 features with 、and 和 · beat 3 hair. Then add the two beats 毛小红's passage has and this one doesn't — age/nationality at the front, clothing at the end. That five-beat outline stays on screen for the writing.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 你长什么样？— write about yourself — You Do (10 min)
**Workbook p.169 Ex.19 "It is your turn!"** plus **p.167 Ex.14**. Write a passage about yourself following the five-beat outline: age and nationality, height, features, hair, what you wear to school and whether you like it. Minimum five sentences, 7 minutes, in the workbook.

Circulate with the four Review errors in mind. The three to catch: the missing 的 after a doubled adjective, 长得 followed by a noun, and 是 creeping into an answer that should repeat the verb.

Then three students read theirs aloud while the class listens for the five beats and says which one was missing.

**Extension —** Write it about **someone else** in the third person — 我爸爸长什么样 (Ex.14's second half) — and join at least two sentences into one with 也 or 和 rather than leaving five short ones. Then write two sentences comparing the two people: 我长得很高，我爸爸长得不高。

#### Activity 2: Word-order repair — You Do (5 min)
**Workbook p.168 Ex.15.** Five jumbles to rearrange — this is Test Parts 4 and 8, and it's the format most often lost through carelessness rather than ignorance:
- 他/上学/每天/穿/校服 → 他每天穿校服上学。
- 喜欢/橙色/她/白色/和
- 爸爸/开车/每天/上班
- 王星/九点/晚上/睡觉
- 有/你/姐姐/几个

Whiteboards, one at a time, hold up together. The rule worth stating: time comes before the verb, and 每天 comes before the time.

**Extension —** **Workbook p.168 Ex.17** — write a question using each of the six question words 几 谁 吗 什么 怎么 哪儿, and make two of them questions about someone's appearance.

#### Activity 3: Phrase chains (3 min)
**Workbook p.169 Ex.18**, all nine, fast: 下午→午__ · 长裤→裤__ · 中学→学__ · 大火→火__ · 毛衣→衣__ · 早上→上__ · 明年→年__ · 学校→校__ · 出汗→汗__. Answers by cold call.

#### Game: Kahoot — the whole of Unit 5 (5 min)

Twenty questions across the three lessons of the unit: colours (4), clothing (4), body parts (5), 长得/有 sentence choice (3), radicals (4). Devices, individual scoring, leaderboard up.
- **Setup needed:** quiz prepared in advance; devices
- **Harder version:** the final three questions show a full sentence with one error and ask what is wrong, rather than asking for a translation.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** all three read aloud; for criterion 2, every student holds up their written paragraph — a visible check that eight lessons produced a piece of writing.
- **Self-assessment:** whiteboards, 👍 / 😐 / 👎 — **against the whole sequence, not just today**: can you name the thirteen body parts? can you describe a person in three beats? can you name the six radicals?
- **Exit ticket:** *One sentence, your choice of anything from the eight lessons, that you are confident is correct.* Collected — and it makes a good opener for the revision lesson if you run one before the test.
- **Preview:** "That's Book 1 finished. Before the unit test we can spend a lesson purely on revision if you want one — ask me."
