# Parts of the Body (3): 脸、牙齿、舌头、皮肤、手指头 + tone-mark listening

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Parts of the Body 人体部位 | Lesson 3 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l3-extra-body-words`](../../../index/designs/y7-l15/l3-extra-body-words/) — 20 slides

## Learning Intention
We are learning five more words for parts of the body, and to write down pinyin with the right tone marks from listening alone.

## Success Criteria
- I can name 脸, 牙齿, 舌头, 皮肤 and 手指头
- I can write pinyin with the correct tone mark for a word I only hear
- I can name the radical that goes with a meaning — eye 目, mouth 口, flesh 月

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The eight body words from Lessons 1–2, and the Unit 4 time expressions the workbook asks for on this lesson's pages.

**How:**

1. **Time (3 min)** — **Workbook p.166 Ex.12**: write six times in Chinese. Project a clock face for each, students write 七点零五分 style on whiteboards. This is Unit 4 revision sitting on a Lesson 15 page; two or three minutes and move on. The one to check is 差 and 刻, which fade fastest.
2. **Body parts (4 min)** — **Bingo**, 3×3 grids, students fill their own nine squares from the eight known words plus one free choice. You call in English, they cross off the Chinese. First line wins.

**Connection:** "Eight words gets you most of a person. Today's five are the ones you need when something hurts or when you're describing a face closely — and then we test your ear on tones."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Write the four tone marks large on the board beside the screen — ā á ǎ à — and leave them there for the listening task.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 脸（臉） | liǎn | face | Oval face outline, no features (SVG) |
| 1 | 牙齿（齒） | yáchǐ | teeth | Row of teeth, smiling (SVG) |
| 2 | 舌头（頭） | shétou | tongue | Open mouth with tongue out (SVG) |
| 2 | 皮肤（膚） | pífū | skin | Forearm with a small shaded patch (SVG) |
| 3 | 手指头（頭） | shǒuzhǐtou | finger | Hand with one finger raised (SVG) |

Two things to point out, both of which pay off next lesson: **脸** and **皮肤** carry 月 (flesh), like 脚 and 腿 did; and **手指头** is built from 手 — a word they already know — plus 指 plus 头, another word they already know. Chinese builds words out of words.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 她的**脸**很小。 — Her face is small.
2. 我的**牙齿**很白。 — My teeth are white.
3. 她的**脸**上有大眼睛和白**牙齿**。 — Her face has big eyes and white teeth.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的 ________ 很 ________ 。
**Extension —** Describe someone's face in two clauses using a colour word from Lesson 13: 他的脸不大，他有白白的牙齿。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我的**舌头**很长。 — My tongue is long.
2. 他的**皮肤**很白。 — His skin is pale.
3. 我的**皮肤**不白，我的**舌头**很长。 — My skin isn't pale, my tongue is long.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 他的皮肤很 ________ 。
**Extension —** Use two colour words from Lesson 13 in one sentence about a person: 她的皮肤很白，她有黑色的头发。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我有十个**手指头**。 — I have ten fingers.
2. 他的**手指头**很长。 — His fingers are long.
3. 她有小手和长**手指头**。 — She has small hands and long fingers.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我有 ________ 个手指头。
**Extension —** One sentence using 手, 手指头 and one more body part, with a negative in it.

#### Recall slide
All five new words, characters only, no pinyin and no English. Class reads them back. If it stalls on 皮肤 or 手指头, go back to the picture before moving on — these two are the ones that don't stick.

#### Vocabulary summary board
All thirteen body words now known, characters and pictures only:
**眼睛 · 耳朵 · 鼻子 · 嘴巴 · 手 · 头 · 脚 · 腿 · 脸 · 牙齿 · 舌头 · 皮肤 · 手指头**
Stays on screen through Activity 1.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Thirteen words, one face — We Do (7 min)
Summary board up. Pairs get one blank figure and must write a sentence for **every** one of the thirteen words — thirteen short sentences, any frame, 5 minutes. The instruction is what makes it work: a pair that writes four long sentences hasn't done it.

Then three pairs read two sentences each while the board is still up.

**Extension —** The same thirteen words, but as **one connected paragraph** of five or six sentences rather than thirteen separate ones — joined with 和, 、and 也. Written with the summary board turned off.

#### Activity 2: Listening — write the pinyin with tone marks (8 min)
**Textbook p.116 Ex.4.** You read each word twice, at normal speed, students write the pinyin **with the tone mark**. Nine words:

> shénme · kāishǐ · yīnyuè · xǐhuan · liúxíng · yáogǔn · xuéxiào · xiōngdì · lǚxíng

Meaning is not the point and several of these aren't words they know — this is purely an ear-to-tone-mark exercise, so say so before you start or you'll lose the ones who panic about vocabulary. Mark it together off the screen; a mark in the wrong place counts as wrong.

Then **p.115 Ex.2** as the paired version: in pairs, one says a phrase from the summary board, the other writes it in pinyin with tone marks. Most correct in two minutes wins.

**Extension —** Instead of nine single words, take dictation of three full sentences in pinyin with tone marks, then write the same sentences in characters underneath.

#### Activity 3: Radical for the meaning — You Do (6 min)
**Workbook p.161 Ex.4**, run as a race against the clock. Twelve meanings, write the radical for each: eye 目 · water 氵 · flesh 月 · sunset 夕 · clothing 衤 · mouth 口 · wood 木 · cave 穴 · food 饣 · metal 钅 · grass 艹 · page 页. Three minutes solo, then answers on screen and students mark their own out of twelve.

Flag 衤 explicitly: **clothing is 衤, not 礻**. That extra dot was the most common error in Lesson 14 and it is worth two marks on the test.

**Extension —** For each of the twelve radicals, write one character that uses it — and for three of them, write the character in a phrase rather than alone.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; ask for a show of hands on criterion 2 specifically, since that's the one that was tested today.
- **Self-assessment:** traffic light on whiteboards per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** *I say one word, you write it in pinyin with the tone mark and in characters.* (Use 脸.)
- **Preview:** "Next lesson is all characters — six new radicals and how the characters you've learnt are actually built."
