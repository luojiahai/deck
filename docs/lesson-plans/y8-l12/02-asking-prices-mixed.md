# 怎么卖？— Asking the Price

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Eating Out 外出就餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 — Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask how much something costs and to ask for a total.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write 卖、买、斤、一共
- I can ask a price two ways: 这些苹果怎么卖？and 西瓜多少钱一斤？
- I can ask for the total with 一共多少钱？and answer it

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l2-asking-prices`), speakers for CD track 56
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed stall cards: four priced item cards per group of 3–4 (西红柿 ¥3.00/斤, 菜花儿 ¥2.50/斤, 苹果 ¥10.00/五个, 比萨饼 ¥60.00/个), plus play money if you have it
- Textbook p.112 Ex.3 and Ex.4
- Workbook p.130 Ex.5 and Ex.6 projected
- Printed half-slips for the exit ticket

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's six money words, plus a verb-and-object warm-up that reaches back across the whole unit.

**How:** Two halves.

1. *(4 min)* **Money rapid fire.** Teacher says an amount, class writes the figure; then teacher shows a figure, class says it chorally. Six of each. Finish with the 块/元 check: 「二十块」用「元」怎么说？

2. *(4 min)* **Verb + object race** (Workbook p.130 Ex.6, projected). Sixteen verbs, a noun bank on screen: 儿歌 汽车 外套 音乐 西餐 历史 钢琴 可乐 作业 衣服 小提琴 青菜 汉语 电影 篮球 水彩画儿. Pairs match as many as they can in three minutes on one whiteboard — 吃西餐、喝可乐、做作业、打篮球、画水彩画儿、弹钢琴、穿外套、拉小提琴、说汉语、开汽车、买衣服、炒青菜、唱儿歌、听音乐、看电影、学历史. Highest count reads theirs out.

**Connection:** "买衣服、买青菜 — today we find out how to ask what those cost before we buy them."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On screen and left up. Write 买 and 卖 side by side on the board as you introduce the criteria and say nothing about them yet — students will spot the difference themselves in the I Do.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 买（買） | mǎi | buy | a shopper handing over money |
| 1 | 卖（賣） | mài | sell | a stallholder handing over fruit |
| 2 | 斤 | jīn | half a kilogram | a set of scales with vegetables on it |
| 2 | 一共 | yígòng | altogether | three items bracketed together with a total |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我想**买**一个西瓜。 — I want to buy a watermelon.
2. 他们**卖**水果。 — They sell fruit.
3. 我**买**苹果，他**卖**苹果。 — I buy apples, he sells apples.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我想买 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「买」和「卖」写一句话，两个词都要用：这家店卖比萨饼，我想买一个。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 一**斤**苹果十块钱。 — A jin of apples is ten yuan.
2. 我买两**斤**橘子。 — I'll buy two jin of tangerines.
3. 两斤橘子和一个西瓜，**一共**二十块。 — Two jin of tangerines and a watermelon, twenty yuan altogether.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我买 ________ 和 ________ ，一共 ________ 块。
**Extension —** 写三样东西和三个价钱，再写一共多少钱 — 把钱加起来。

**Recall slide:** 买 卖 斤 一共 — characters only. Then the pair trap: teacher writes 买 and 卖, class calls out which is which.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
Two ways to ask a price, and one to ask a total:

> **这些苹果怎么卖？** — How are these apples sold? (loose goods, price per unit unknown)
> **西瓜多少钱一斤？** — How much per jin is watermelon?
> **一共多少钱？** — How much altogether?

Four modelled examples, last one hardest:
1. 这些橘子怎么卖？— 十块钱四个。
2. 苹果多少钱一斤？— 五块五一斤。
3. 菜花儿怎么卖？— 三块一个。
4. 我买一个菜花儿和两斤土豆，一共多少钱？— 一共六块五。

Think aloud on the fourth: "I list what I want — 我买 + item + 和 + item — then ask 一共多少钱？The answer starts with 一共 too."

#### CFU Checkpoints
Straight after the pattern, on whiteboards:
- 「多少钱一斤」and「一斤多少钱」— are both OK? (Yes, both are used.)
- Which do you use for a whole watermelon: 一个 or 一斤？(Either — 一个西瓜 or a price per 斤.)
- What's wrong with 我卖一个西瓜 if you're the customer? (卖 is sell — you want 买.)
- Translate: "How much altogether?" (一共多少钱？)
- 十块钱四个 — 一个多少钱？(两块五)

#### Text / Dialogue — Text 1, first half (p.110, track 56)
Play track 56, students follow with the book closed and the text on screen:

> A: 这些苹果怎么卖？
> B: 十块(元)钱四个。
> A: 西瓜多少钱一斤？
> B: 两块五一斤。
> A: 我买四个苹果、一个西瓜，再来两斤橘子。一共多少钱？
> B: 一共三十块五毛(角)五(分)。

Read together chorally, then half the class takes A and half takes B, then swap.

Comprehension, in Chinese: ① 苹果多少钱一个？(两块五) ② 她买了几个苹果？(四个) ③ 「再来两斤橘子」是什么意思？(also give me two jin of tangerines — 来 used for "give me")

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Measure words — We Do (6 min)
Textbook p.112 Ex.3, projected. Twelve blanks, a measure-word box on screen (口 家 头 朵 个 位 台 条). Pairs fill them on a whiteboard, then the class checks together:

三**口**人 · 一**朵**花 · 一**台**电脑 · 一**家**学校 · 一**位**老师 · 一**条**裙子 · 一**头**白发 · 一**条**裤子 · 一**个**热狗 · 一**个**苹果 · 一**家**饭店 · 一**台**电视

Then add today's: 一**斤**土豆、两**斤**橘子 — 斤 is a measure word too, the one you use when things are weighed.

**Extension —** Workbook p.134 Ex.14, the eighteen-item version, done solo and against the clock. Then write two sentences using a measure word each that *wasn't* in the box: 一包土豆、一门课.

#### Activity 2: Food-stall role play — You Do (12 min)
Textbook p.112 Ex.4. Groups of 3–4 set up a stall with four priced cards:

> 西红柿 ¥3.00/斤 · 菜花儿 ¥2.50/斤 · 苹果 ¥10.00/五个 · 比萨饼 ¥60.00/个

Half the groups keep their stall open; the other half go shopping, then swap after five minutes. Each shopper must visit two stalls and at each one: ask the price of two items with **both** question forms, buy at least two things, and ask 一共多少钱？The stallholder has to work out the total out loud.

Model on screen before they start:
> A: 西红柿怎么卖？ B: 三块一斤。
> A: 比萨饼多少钱一个？ B: 六十块。
> A: 我买两斤西红柿和一个比萨饼。一共多少钱？ B: 一共六十六块。

Teacher circulates with the pinyin still on screen for the first round; take it down for the second.

**Extension —** The shopper has a budget of 50 块 and must leave the stall having spent between 45 and 50. They must say aloud what they are *not* buying and why, using 可是 and 贵 or 便宜 from earlier lessons: 我想买比萨饼，可是六十块，太贵了。我买两斤西红柿和一斤菜花儿。 Then they report their total and their leftover change to the class in Chinese. If they finish, they swap into the stallholder role and must talk the next customer *up* to a more expensive item.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble — price questions (5 min)
Six jumbled sentences on screen, one at a time; pairs reorder on a whiteboard and hold up. First correct board scores.

1. 怎么 / 这些 / 卖 / 苹果 → 这些苹果怎么卖？
2. 一斤 / 多少 / 西瓜 / 钱 → 西瓜多少钱一斤？
3. 多少 / 一共 / 钱 → 一共多少钱？
4. 两斤 / 我 / 橘子 / 买 → 我买两斤橘子。
5. 四个 / 十块钱 → 十块钱四个。
6. 钱 / 一共 / 三十块 / 五毛 → 一共三十块五毛。

- **Setup needed:** Projector only.
- **Harder version:** After reordering, the pair must add a second sentence that answers or follows on from theirs, out loud, with no writing.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; then ask two students to demonstrate criterion 2 live with any classroom object.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards — students write 1, 2, 3 and a 👍 / 😐 / 👎 beside each.
- **Exit ticket (half-slip):** Write the two ways of asking a price, using 香蕉 in one and 土豆 in the other.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we finish the market dialogue and learn how to ask permission — 我可以用人民币吗？"
