# 我可以用人民币吗？— Asking Permission

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Eating Out 外出就餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 — Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask whether we are allowed to do something, using 可以.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write 用、人民币、可以
- I can ask permission with 我可以…吗？and answer 可以 or 不可以
- I can read the whole of Text 1 aloud and act it out with a partner
- I can write the four simple characters 尸、户、革、丁

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l3-keyi-text1`), speakers for CD tracks 56 and 58
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Squared paper or mini whiteboards for the character relay
- Textbook p.113–114 (Ex.6, Ex.7); Workbook p.132 Ex.9 and Ex.10
- Printed Text 1 role cards (A customer / B stallholder), one per pair
- Printed half-slips for the exit ticket

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 2's price questions and Lesson 1's money words, now used together at speed.

**How:** **Pair dialogue from memory** (5 min). Pairs reconstruct the first half of Text 1 with only the four English prompts on screen — "how are these apples sold / how much per jin for watermelon / I'll buy four apples, a watermelon and two jin of tangerines / how much altogether". They rehearse twice, swapping roles, then two pairs perform for the class.

Then (3 min) **Correct the teacher** — three wrong sentences on the board:
- ✗ 这些苹果卖怎么？ → 这些苹果怎么卖？
- ✗ 西瓜一斤多少？ → 西瓜多少钱一斤？
- ✗ 我卖两斤橘子。(said by the customer) → 我买两斤橘子。

**Connection:** "You've bought the fruit and you know the total. There's one thing left to settle at the till — what you're allowed to pay with."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On screen. Introduce by acting it out: hold up your phone and ask the class 我可以用电话吗？— say it twice, let them guess the meaning, then reveal the criteria.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 用 | yòng | use | a hand using a pen |
| 1 | 可以 | kěyǐ | can; may | a green tick beside a raised hand |
| 2 | 人民 | rénmín | the people | — |
| 2 | 币（幣） | bì | currency | a coin |
| 3 | 人民币 | rénmínbì | RMB, Chinese currency | the ¥100 note |

*(Cycle 3 is a single compound made from cycle 2's two parts — run it as a short cycle with one example set and one writing prompt.)*

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我**用**汉语说。 — I'll say it in Chinese.
2. 我**可以**吃吗？ — May I eat?
3. 我**可以用**英语说吗？ — May I say it in English?

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我可以 ________ 吗？
**Extension —** 写两句：一句问老师，一句问妈妈，两句都要用「可以」和「用」— 我可以用你的电脑吗？

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **人民**很多。 — There are a lot of people.
2. 这是中国的钱**币**。 — This is Chinese currency.
3. 「**人民币**」是中国的钱。 — Renminbi is China's money.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 中国的钱是 ________ 。
**Extension —** 中国用人民币，你的国家用什么？写一句。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我有一百块**人民币**。 — I have a hundred yuan of RMB.
2. 我可以用**人民币**吗？ — May I use RMB?
3. 在中国买东西要用**人民币**。 — In China you use RMB to buy things.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我可以用 ________ 吗？
**Extension —** 写一个两句的小对话：一句问，一句回答（可以／不可以，因为…）。

**Recall slide:** 用 可以 人民 币 人民币 — characters only, class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
Asking permission. 可以 goes **before** the verb, and the question is closed with 吗:

> 我 **可以** + V + 吗？ → 可以。／不可以。

Four modelled examples, last one hardest:
1. 我可以吃吗？
2. 我可以用人民币吗？
3. 我现在可以听音乐吗？
4. 我一边做作业一边听音乐，可以吗？ — the tag form: put the whole sentence first, then 可以吗？on the end.

Point out the two shapes: **我可以…吗？** and **…，可以吗？** Both are in the book; the second is the one on Workbook p.132.

#### CFU Checkpoints
On whiteboards, straight after the pattern:
- Where does 可以 go — before or after the verb? (Before. ✗ 我用可以人民币吗)
- Translate: "May I use Chinese?" (我可以用汉语吗？)
- Answer this: 我可以吃你的苹果吗？(可以。／不可以。)
- Turn into the tag form: 我可以现在回家吗？ → 我现在想回家，可以吗？
- Thumbs up if 币 means "currency", thumbs down if it means "money in general". (Up — 钱 is the general word.)

#### Text / Dialogue — Text 1 in full (p.110, track 56)
Play track 56 right through. Students follow on screen, then choral read, then pairs with role cards — A twice, then swap.

> A: 这些苹果怎么卖？ B: 十块(元)钱四个。
> A: 西瓜多少钱一斤？ B: 两块五一斤。
> A: 我买四个苹果、一个西瓜，再来两斤橘子。一共多少钱？
> B: 一共三十块五毛(角)五(分)。
> A: 我可以用人民币吗？ B: 可以。

Comprehension in Chinese: ① 她买了什么？ ② 一共多少钱？ ③ 她用什么钱？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 可以吗？— We Do (7 min)
Workbook p.132 Ex.9, projected. Seven items, each completed into a full 可以吗？question. The first is given: 我用英语说，可以吗？

Students complete the rest on whiteboards, one at a time, held up together:
- 我用 ________ 买这些衣服，可以吗？(人民币)
- 我一边做作业 ________ ，可以吗？(一边听音乐)
- 我今天晚上想 ________ ，可以吗？
- 我再打一会儿 ________ ，可以吗？(篮球／电话)
- 我今天不想 ________ ，可以吗？
- ________ ，可以吗？ — entirely their own

Take three answers aloud on the last one.

**Extension —** For each of your last three, write the *answer* as well, and make one of the answers a refusal with a reason: 不可以，因为你还没有做作业。Then take Workbook p.131 Ex.7 and write one sentence of your own for each of the six dotted words — 要、不要、条、应该、可以、会 — modelled on 今天下雨，你要穿雨衣／不要看电视了，快去睡觉／我想买一条牛仔裤／我应该每天吃三种蔬菜／我现在可以听音乐吗？／今天会下雨吗？

#### Activity 2: Listen and tick (p.114 Ex.6, track 58) — You Do (6 min)
Six three-option items. Play twice. Students tick in the book, then check in pairs before the class check.

Script: ① 我今年学十门课，数学、地理、历史等。② 今天是晴天。气温在零下五度左右。③ 我今天生病了，我下午去看医生了。④ 除了油画儿以外，我还喜欢画国画儿。⑤ 我喜欢吃中餐。我喜欢吃炒面、包子、面条等。⑥ 对不起，小文不在家，她一会儿回来。

This item set is cumulative — weather, illness, 除了…以外, food and phone language all come from earlier units. Don't pre-teach; the point is that they can still hear it.

**Extension —** Instead of ticking, write down the *whole sentence* you hear for items 3, 4 and 5. Then rewrite item 5 about yourself.

#### Activity 3: Simple characters 尸 户 革 丁 (4 min)
Textbook p.114 Ex.7, then Workbook p.132 Ex.10. Teacher writes each with stroke order on the board; class air-writes, then copies three of each.

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Stroke count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 尸 | shī | corpse | 3 |
| 户 | hù | household | 4 |
| 革 | gé | leather | 9 |
| 丁 | dīng | man | 2 |

Point out that 户 is 尸 with a dot on top — the pair is the whole difficulty.

**Extension —** Find a character you already know that contains each of these four as a part (房 contains 户, 鞋 contains 革, 顶／打 for 丁). Write the character and its pinyin.

#### Game: Writing Relay — 尸户革丁 (5 min)
Teams line up. Teacher calls a character by its *meaning* in English ("household!"); the first student runs up, writes it on the board, runs back, next student. A character with a wrong stroke order can be challenged by the other team for a bonus point.

- **Setup needed:** Board space divided into team columns, markers.
- **Harder version:** Call the character by pinyin only, and require the runner to write a **word** containing it (户 → 门户／住户) rather than the bare character.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads; class 对/不对 after each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each of the four criteria.
- **Exit ticket (half-slip):** Write one 可以 question you'd actually ask a teacher, in Chinese. One sentence.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we move from the market to the restaurant, and learn how to say the food is *especially* good — 做得特别好吃。"
