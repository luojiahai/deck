# 人民币 — Talking About Money

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Eating Out 外出就餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 — Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to read and say amounts of money in Chinese renminbi.

## Success Criteria
- I can say the six money words 钱、块、元、毛、角、分
- I can read an amount aloud (三十块五毛五分) and write it down
- I can explain when Chinese uses 块/毛 and when it uses 元/角

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l1-money-rmb`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed RMB note/coin cards (1角, 5角, 1元 coin, 1元, 5元, 10元, 20元, 50元, 100元) — one set per pair, plus one large set for the board
- Textbook p.111 Ex.1
- Workbook p.129 Ex.2 projected
- Printed half-slips for the exit ticket

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 11's meal and restaurant language — 中餐/西餐/快餐, 饭店, 吃饭, 一般, 还是 vs 或者, and 会 for likelihood. This is the first lesson of a new textbook lesson, so the review reaches back to the last one.

**How:** Rapid whiteboard round, five items, all held up together:
1. 你午饭一般吃什么？(any answer in Chinese)
2. Fill the gap: 我晚饭吃米饭 ____ 面条。(或者)
3. Fill the gap: 你想吃中餐 ____ 西餐？(还是)
4. Translate: "It is likely to rain this afternoon." (今天下午会下雨。)
5. 你们家常去饭店吃饭吗？— answer in a full sentence.

Take two answers aloud for item 1 and item 5; the rest are a show-and-scan.

**Connection:** "You can already say where you eat and what you eat. Today we add the part everybody actually needs at a restaurant or a market — the money."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Displayed on the projector, left up all lesson. Read the intention aloud yourself, then have a student read the three criteria. Point at 块/元 and 毛/角 in criterion 3 and say they'll find out the difference in ten minutes — don't explain it yet.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 钱（錢） | qián | money | a small pile of notes and coins |
| 1 | 块（塊） | kuài | yuan (spoken) | the ¥1 note |
| 2 | 元 | yuán | yuan (written) | the ¥1 note with 壹圆 circled |
| 2 | 毛 | máo | 1/10 yuan (spoken) | the 5角 coin |
| 3 | 角 | jiǎo | 1/10 yuan (written) | the 1角 note with 壹角 circled |
| 3 | 分 | fēn | 1/100 yuan | a 1分 coin |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 这是十**块钱**。 — This is ten yuan.
2. 我有五**块钱**。 — I have five yuan.
3. 一个汉堡包二十**块钱**。 — A hamburger is twenty yuan.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我有 ________ 块钱。
**Extension —** 写两句：一个热狗多少块钱？一个比萨饼呢？Use two different foods from Lesson 11 and give each a price.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 五块 = 五**元**。 — five kuai = five yuan.
2. 一个苹果三**毛**钱。 — An apple is three mao.
3. 十**元**五**毛**。 — Ten yuan fifty.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 元 ________ 毛。(write any amount, then read it aloud the spoken way)
**Extension —** 用中文写三个价钱，一个用「块毛」，一个用「元角」，一个两个都写。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 三毛 = 三**角**。 — three mao = three jiao.
2. 一**分**钱。 — one fen.
3. 一块五**角**五**分**。 — one yuan fifty-five.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** ________ 块 ________ 毛 ________ 分。
**Extension —** 这个价钱用「元角分」怎么写？再用「块毛分」说一次。

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 钱 块 元 毛 角 分 — characters only, class reads back in order, then backwards.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
Reading an amount, largest unit first, no word for "and":

> 三十 **块** 五 **毛** 五 **分**
> 二 **块** 五
> 一百 **块**
> 十五 **块** 二 **毛**

Four modelled examples, last one hardest:
1. 十块。
2. 十块五毛。
3. 三十块五毛五分。
4. 一百二十块五毛 — 用「元角」怎么说？(一百二十元五角)

**NOTE (textbook p.111):** 块 and 毛 are what people *say*; 元 and 角 are what is *written* — on price tags, receipts and the notes themselves. 分 is the same in both.

#### CFU Checkpoints
Run these on mini whiteboards, straight after the pattern:
- 「三毛」= 几角？ (三角)
- Write in characters: ¥25.60 (二十五块六毛 / 二十五元六角)
- Read this aloud: 一百零五块 — thumbs up if you heard 零 (yes, 105 keeps 零)
- What's wrong with 五钱块? (钱 goes last: 五块钱)
- 一块 = 几毛？ (十毛)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Money board — We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters only, plus the nine notes and coins from textbook p.111 Ex.1. Stays on screen for the whole activity.

Whole class, then pairs. Teacher points at a note or coin, class says it chorally: 一毛(角)、五毛(角)、一块(元)、五块、十块、二十块、五十块、一百块. Then pairs take their card set, shuffle it, and one holds up a card while the other says it — 30 seconds each way.

Second round, harder: the holder puts **two or three cards** down together and the partner says the total (五十块 + 二十块 + 五毛 → 七十块五毛).

**Extension —** Instead of the total, the partner must say the amount *twice* — once as 块/毛 and once as 元/角 — and then say what you could buy for that in Lesson 11 food: 七十块五毛。七十元五角。这些钱可以吃七个汉堡包。

#### Activity 2: Listen and write the amount — You Do (10 min)
Teacher reads twelve amounts in Chinese, students write the figure (¥) on whiteboards, hold up. Read at natural speed, twice each for the first four, once thereafter.

Suggested list: 五块 · 十二块 · 三毛 · 一块五毛 · 二十块八毛 · 九十九块 · 一百块 · 三十块五毛五分 · 六块二毛 · 四十五块 · 二百块 · 一百零八块五毛.

Then flip it: project Workbook p.129 Ex.2 (the seven note-combination pictures) and students write the amount **in characters** in their books. Take three answers aloud.

**Extension —** Write each of the seven amounts in characters *both ways* (块毛 and 元角), then write one sentence for each of two of them saying what it could buy: 一百块可以买十个热狗。

#### Game: Slap the Board — money (6 min)
Nine amounts written large on the board in characters (五毛 · 一块 · 五块 · 十块 · 二十块 · 五十块 · 一百块 · 三块五 · 十二块八毛). Two students at the board, teacher calls an amount, first to slap it wins the point. Winner stays on.

- **Setup needed:** Board space and a marker. Nothing printed.
- **Harder version:** The teacher calls a *sum* rather than the amount — 五块加五块 → slap 十块 — or calls it in 元/角 and the student must slap the 块/毛 version.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads the three criteria aloud; class answers 对 or 不对 after each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on each criterion. Note who shows 👎 on criterion 3 — the 块/元 split is the one that fades by next lesson.
- **Exit ticket (half-slip):** Write ¥32.50 in Chinese characters, then write it again the other way (块毛 and 元角).
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we learn how to *ask* the price — 这些苹果怎么卖？"
