# 几次？大概多少钱？— How Often and Roughly How Much

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Eating Out 外出就餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 — Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say how many times we do something and to give an approximate amount.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write 次、一次、大概
- I can ask and answer 你去过…几次？and 你一星期吃几次快餐？
- I can use 大概 to give a rough amount (大概花五十块)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l5-ci-guo-dagai`), speakers for CD track 59
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed "Find Someone Who" survey grids, one per student (six 几次 questions, space for names and answers)
- Textbook p.116 Ex.9, p.117 Ex.11, p.118 Ex.13; Workbook p.130 Ex.4, p.134 Ex.13, p.135 Ex.16
- Printed half-slips for the exit ticket

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4's 得 and the 好吃 scale.

**How:** **Chain response round the class** (4 min). Each student adds one 得 sentence about the person to their left: 他画画儿画得很好 → 她跳舞跳得很美 → … No repeats of the same verb. If a student stalls, the class supplies the verb and they build the sentence.

Then (4 min) **quick quiz on whiteboards**:
- Translate: "The food at this restaurant is especially delicious." (这家饭店的饭菜做得特别好吃。)
- Fill: 姐姐的小提琴拉 ________ 不太好听。(得)
- Put in order, strongest first: 很好吃／不好吃／特别好吃／不太好吃
- What's wrong with 他得跑很快？(得 goes after the verb: 他跑得很快)

**Connection:** "You can say the food is especially good. Now — how often do you actually go, and what does it cost you?"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On screen. Open with a live question to the class: 你一星期吃几次快餐？Take a show of fingers before anyone knows the words, then reveal the criteria.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 次 | cì | measure word for actions | three tally marks |
| 1 | 一次 | yícì | once | one tally mark |
| 2 | 概 | gài | general | — |
| 2 | 大概 | dàgài | probably; roughly | a wavy "approximately" sign over a price tag |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我一星期去**一次**。 — I go once a week.
2. 我一个月看两**次**电影。 — I see two films a month.
3. 我去过北京三**次**，去过上海**一次**。 — I've been to Beijing three times and to Shanghai once.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我一星期 ________ 几次？— 我一星期 ________ ________ 次。
**Extension —** 写三句，一句用「一星期」，一句用「一个月」，一句用「一年」，每句都要有「次」。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 这个**大概**十块钱。 — This is roughly ten yuan.
2. 我**大概**每天吃两种水果。 — I probably eat two kinds of fruit a day.
3. 我们每次吃饭**大概**花一百块。 — Each time we eat out we spend roughly a hundred yuan.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我每个月大概花 ________ 块钱买 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「大概」和「差不多」各写一句，说说有什么不一样（差不多 = nearly, 大概 = roughly）。

**Recall slide:** 次 一次 概 大概 — characters only.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — two patterns today

**1. Frequency with 次.** The time period comes first, 几次 sits before the object:
> 你 + 一星期/一个月 + 吃 **几次** + 快餐？
> 我一星期吃两**次**快餐。

**2. Experience with 过 + 几次** (revision from Unit 3, now carrying 次):
> 你去**过**北京**几次**？ — 我去过两次。／一次也没有去过。

Four modelled examples, last one hardest:
1. 你一星期看几次电视？
2. 你一个月吃几次中餐？
3. 你去过美国几次？— 一次也没有去过。
4. 我们家差不多每两星期去一次饭店，每次大概花两百块。 — frequency + 次 + 大概, one sentence.

Flag the negative answer explicitly: **一次也没有去过** — "not even once". It's in the book (p.116) and students reach for ✗ 没有几次 without it.

#### CFU Checkpoints
On whiteboards, straight after both patterns:
- Translate: "I eat fast food twice a week." (我一星期吃两次快餐。)
- Where does 几次 go in 你一个月看___电影？(你一个月看几次电影？)
- Answer: 你去过中国几次？— and use the negative form if the answer is none. (一次也没有去过。)
- 次 or 种 — 两 ___ 水果? 两 ___ 电影? (种 / 次)
- 大概五十块 and 五十块左右 — same or different? (Same meaning; 大概 goes before, 左右 goes after.)

#### Text / Dialogue — Text 2, the frequency lines (p.115, track 59)
Replay the first two exchanges from last lesson, now for the frequency:

> A: 你们家常去饭店吃饭吗？
> B: 差不多每两星期去**一次**。
> A: 你们每次吃饭**大概**要花多少钱？

Choral read. Comprehension in Chinese: ① 他们多长时间去一次饭店？ ② 「差不多」是什么意思？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 你去过…几次？— We Do (5 min)
Textbook p.116 Ex.9. Pairs work through the five cities — 北京、纽约、巴黎、伦敦、东京 — asking and answering. The model answer 一次也没有去过 is on screen and most students will need it for most cities, which is the point: the negative gets twenty repetitions.

Then the same shape with Workbook p.130 Ex.4's six 过 questions: 你去过北京吗？／画过国画儿吗？／吃过青菜吗？／学过法语吗？／看过中医吗？／用过人民币吗？

**Extension —** For every "yes", add when and how it went, using 了 and 得: 我去过北京两次，去年去的，玩得特别好。For every "no", say what you'd like instead: 我没有去过巴黎，可是我很想去。Then write two more 过 questions of your own and ask a second pair.

#### Activity 2: Find Someone Who — You Do (10 min)
Printed grid, one per student, six questions, students circulate and must get a **different name** for each:

1. 你一星期吃几次快餐？
2. 你一个月看几次电影？
3. 你一星期做几次体育运动？
4. 你一个月吃几次中餐？
5. 你一星期看几次电视？
6. 你每个月大概花多少钱买衣服？

Rule: the question must be asked in Chinese and the answer written in Chinese characters or pinyin, not English. Eight minutes moving, then two minutes reporting: three students report one classmate each — 美文一星期吃三次快餐。

**Extension —** Report back on **four** classmates as a connected paragraph rather than separate sentences, comparing them with 比 and grouping them with 都: 美文和大生都一星期吃三次快餐，可是冬冬比他们吃得少，他一个月只吃一次。Deliver it standing, with nothing written in front of you.

#### Activity 3: 大概花多少钱 (4 min)
Textbook p.117 Ex.11 and Workbook p.134 Ex.13 together. Pairs ask and answer five questions:
你每个月大概花多少钱买杂志／午饭／CD／电脑游戏／衣服？and 打电话？

Answers must include 大概 and a real amount in Chinese money words. Take three aloud.

**Extension —** Add up your own five answers and report the total in Chinese with 一共, then say whether it's 太贵 or 不算太贵 and why.

#### Activity 4: The four tenses (p.118 Ex.13) — You Do (4 min)
The unit-test revision item, run tight. Four model sentences on screen with their labels:

> 我上个周末去打网球**了**。 (past) · 我**每个**周末**都**去看电影。 (present)
> 我**正在**看电视**呢**。 (present continuous) · 我下个周末**会**/**要**去北京。 (future)

Time-word bank (昨天 今天 明天 · 去年 今年 明年 · 上个月 这个月 下个月 · 上个星期 这个星期 下个星期) and activity bank (去朋友家 · 在家看书 · 看电视 · 看电影 · 游泳 · 跑步 · 打篮球 · 画画儿 · 去饭店吃饭 · 去上海 · 去美国 · 吃中餐) both on screen.

Students write **one sentence per tense**, four in total, each using a different activity. Whiteboards up together so you can scan for the two usual errors: 了 on a future sentence, and 正在 without 呢.

**Extension —** Write all four about the *same* activity, so the set reads as a story: 我上个月去过一次上海饭店，我每个星期都想去，我正在看他们的菜单呢，我下个星期会再去一次。Every sentence must carry a 次 or a 大概.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student reads them; class answers 对/不对.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each criterion. Check criterion 1 specifically — 大概 fades fastest.
- **Exit ticket (half-slip):** 你一个月去几次饭店？每次大概花多少钱？— answer both in one sentence.
- **Preview:** "Last lesson of this unit: the whole restaurant conversation, including the bill — 五百块左右，不算太贵。"
