# 花了两百多块，不算贵 — The Whole Meal Out

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Eating Out 外出就餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 — Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say how much a meal cost and whether it was worth it.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write 花、百、算、贵
- I can say how much I spent (我们一共花了两百块)
- I can hedge an opinion with 不算太贵 and 左右
- I can describe an eating-out experience in four or more sentences

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l6-huaqian-text2`), speakers for CD tracks 59 and 60
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed stall price lists for Ex.16, one per group of 3–4 (market set and clothing set), plus play money if available
- Board space divided into team columns for the dictation race
- Textbook p.119 Ex.14, Ex.15; p.120 Ex.16; p.121 Ex.17, Ex.18; Workbook p.136 Ex.19, Ex.20; p.137 Ex.22
- Printed half-slips for the exit ticket

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Cumulative — the whole of Lesson 12. This is the last lesson before the unit test, so the review reaches back over all five previous lessons rather than just Lesson 5.

**How:** **Six-question whiteboard sweep** (5 min), one from each lesson:
1. Write ¥45.60 in characters. (四十五块六毛)
2. 这些苹果怎么卖？— answer it.
3. Translate: "May I use RMB?" (我可以用人民币吗？)
4. Fill: 他们的饭菜做 ________ 特别好吃。(得)
5. 你一星期吃几次快餐？— answer in a full sentence.
6. Write the four simple characters: corpse, household, leather, man. (尸 户 革 丁)

Then (3 min) **listening, track 60** (textbook p.119 Ex.14) as a second review item — six three-option items, played twice, ticked in the book, checked chorally.

Script: ① A: 苹果多少钱一斤？B: 五块五一斤。② A: 今天的比萨饼非常好吃。B: 我喜欢吃汉堡包。③ A: 橘子怎么卖？B: 十块钱四个，二十块十个。④ A: 你上个周末去看电影了吗？B: 去了，可是电影不好看。⑤ A: 我差不多每天游泳。你喜欢运动吗？B: 喜欢。我喜欢跑步。⑥ A: 你妈妈今天做的包子特别好吃。我大概吃了六七个。B: 我也吃了四五个。

**Connection:** "Everything you've learned in this lesson happens in one conversation — and it ends with the bill. That's the last piece."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On screen and left up. Open by putting ¥216.00 on the board (the till from textbook p.119) and asking 贵不贵？— collect gut reactions, then reveal the criteria.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 花 | huā | spend | a hand paying at a till |
| 1 | 百 | bǎi | hundred | the ¥100 note |
| 2 | 算 | suàn | regard as; count as | a pair of scales, balanced |
| 2 | 贵（貴） | guì | expensive | a price tag with an upward arrow |

Point out that 花 is the same character they met in Lesson 10 as "flower" (菜花儿) — same character, second meaning.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我**花**了五十块钱。 — I spent fifty yuan.
2. 一个比萨饼六十块，两**百**块可以买三个。 — A pizza is sixty; two hundred buys three.
3. 我们一共**花**了两**百**块。 — We spent two hundred altogether.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 上个星期我花了 ________ 块钱买 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写两句：一句用「大概」，一句用「一共」，两句都要有「花」和一个三位数的价钱。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 这件衣服很**贵**。 — These clothes are expensive.
2. 三百块不**算**贵。 — Three hundred isn't what you'd call expensive.
3. 我们每次大概花两百块，不**算**太**贵**。 — We spend roughly two hundred each time — not too expensive.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 块钱，不算太贵。／太贵了。
**Extension —** 用「可是」写一句，一边贵一边好吃：这家饭店特别贵，可是他们的炒面做得特别好吃。

**Recall slide:** 花 百 算 贵 — characters only, then all 23 words of Lesson 12 as a scan-back board.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
Hedging an amount and an opinion — three devices, all in one sentence in the text:

> 五百块 **左右** ，**不算太** 贵。
> 我们每次 **大概** 花 五百块 **左右** 。

- **大概** goes *before* the amount — 大概五百块
- **左右** goes *after* it — 五百块左右
- **不算太 + adj** softens a judgement — 不算太贵、不算太好吃

Four modelled examples, last one hardest:
1. 大概一百块。
2. 一百块左右。
3. 不算太贵。
4. 我们每次吃饭大概要花五百块左右，不算太贵，因为他们的饭菜做得特别好吃。 — three devices, a reason clause, and a 得 complement.

#### CFU Checkpoints
On whiteboards, straight after the pattern:
- Put 大概 in the right place: ___ 五十块 ___ 。(大概五十块 / 五十块左右)
- Translate: "It isn't too expensive." (不算太贵。)
- 花 — which meaning here: 我花了一百块？(spend)
- Write in characters: 360 (三百六十)
- What's wrong with 我花了一百块钱买了？(the object is missing — 买了一件衣服)

#### Text / Dialogue — Text 2 in full (p.115, track 59)
Play track 59 right through. Choral read, then pairs A/B, then swap.

> A: 你们家常去饭店吃饭吗？ B: 差不多每两星期去一次。
> A: 你们经常去哪家饭店吃饭？ B: 我们经常去一家上海饭店吃饭。他们的饭菜做得特别好吃。
> A: 你们上个周末去了吗？ B: 去了。
> A: 你们每次吃饭大概要花多少钱？ B: 五百块左右，不算太贵。

Comprehension in Chinese: ① 他们多长时间去一次饭店？ ② 那家饭店的饭菜怎么样？ ③ 五百块贵不贵？为什么？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Market and shop role play — We Do (10 min)
Textbook p.120 Ex.16. Groups of 3–4 open **either** a fresh market **or** a clothing shop. Price lists printed, one per group:

> **Market** — 黄瓜 ¥1.20/斤 · 生菜 ¥1.00/斤 · 西红柿 ¥2.00/斤 · 苹果 ¥10.00/四个 · 西瓜 ¥1.20/斤 · 橘子 ¥10.00/五个
> **Shop** — 牛仔裤 ¥260.00/条 · 衬衫 ¥150.00/件 · 毛衣 ¥220.00/件 · 外套 ¥200.00/件

Model on screen:
> 菜花儿怎么卖？— 三块一个。／土豆多少钱一斤？— 一块五。
> 买一个菜花儿和一斤土豆。— 一共四块五。

Half the groups trade first, then swap. Every shopper must, at each stall: ask two prices, buy at least two items, ask 一共多少钱？, and finish with a verdict — 贵 or 不算太贵. Stallholders must give the total out loud.

**Extension —** The shopper has 500 块 and must come back with a full outfit *and* enough food for a family dinner, visiting both a shop and a market. They must compare two stalls out loud with 比 (这家的衬衫比那家的便宜), justify each purchase with 因为…所以, and report to the class at the end: what they bought, 一共花了多少钱, what they left behind and why. No frames, no board. If they finish, they take over a stall and must talk the next customer up to a dearer item.

#### Activity 2: Describe an eating-out experience — You Do (8 min)
Textbook p.119 Ex.15. The five captioned pictures are the model; leave them on screen for the first three minutes, then take them down.

> 上个星期六我们全家人去饭店吃饭了。／我们去了一家上海饭店。／我们吃了炒菜、炒面、粥、饺子等等。／我们还喝了可乐和汽水。／我们一共花了两百多块，不算贵。

Students write five sentences about a real meal out — when, where, what they ate, what they drank, what it cost and whether it was worth it. Every sentence must carry something from this lesson: a 了, a 等等, a price, 一共, 不算太贵.

Three students read theirs aloud at the end.

**Extension —** Write it as the Workbook p.137 Ex.22 essay instead: your **favourite restaurant** — how often you go (次), what food you like, what you normally eat there (一般), how much one meal usually costs (大概…左右), and one sentence on why using 得. Six to eight sentences, joined so it reads as one text, written with nothing on screen.

#### Game: Character dictation race (p.121 Ex.17) (5 min)
The 25 simple characters from the whole book so far, on screen for 90 seconds to memorise:

> 尸 户 革 丁 贝 · 虫 叉 食 果 欠 · 平 石 角 页 舌 · 瓜 旦 矢 皿 斗 · 青 立 寸 巾 足

Screen off. Teacher dictates twelve of them by **meaning in English**; teams write on whiteboards in their groups. Most correct characters wins. Then the screen goes back up for a 30-second self-check.

- **Setup needed:** Projector and one whiteboard per group.
- **Harder version:** Dictate by pinyin alone, and require a **word** containing the character rather than the bare character (革 → 皮革, 户 → 门户, 足 → 足球).

Finish with Workbook p.135 Ex.17 as the written record — write the pinyin **and** the character for: 平 flat (given), 土 soil, 角 horn, 户 household, 尸 corpse, 页 page, 革 leather, 自己 oneself. Two minutes, books open for checking afterwards.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** All four read aloud by a student — and because this is the last lesson of the sequence, also put Lessons 1–5's criteria back on screen and run the class through them in one sweep: money words, price questions, 可以, 得, 次 and 大概.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on all four of today's criteria, then a single show of hands on the whole lesson: "Which of the six lessons do you most want another go at before the test?" Count the hands — that's your data for whether a revision lesson is worth booking.
- **Exit ticket (half-slip):** 上次你去饭店吃饭，花了多少钱？贵不贵？— two sentences, must include 一共 and 不算太贵 or 太贵了.
- **Preview:** "That's the whole of Unit 4. Next we start Unit 5 — 房子, describing where you live."
