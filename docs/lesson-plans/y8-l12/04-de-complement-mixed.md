# 做得特别好吃 — Saying How Well Something Is Done

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Eating Out 外出就餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 — Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say how well something is done, using 得 after the verb.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write 好吃 and 特别
- I can use the 好吃 scale from 特别好吃 down to 不好吃
- I can build a sentence with 得 (他钢琴弹得很好听)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l4-de-complement`), speakers for CD track 59
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed picture cards for the 得 drill (swimming, tennis, piano, getting dressed, painting, dancing) — one set per pair
- Textbook p.115–117 (Ex.8, Ex.10); Workbook p.134 Ex.12, p.135 Ex.15, p.137 Ex.21
- Printed half-slips for the exit ticket

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 3's 可以, plus the market dialogue as a whole.

**How:** Two halves.

1. *(4 min)* **Quick quiz on whiteboards** — five items:
   - Translate: "May I use RMB?" (我可以用人民币吗？)
   - Fill: 我 ________ 用英语说吗？(可以)
   - What's wrong with 我用可以人民币吗？(可以 goes before the verb)
   - Write the character for hù, household. (户)
   - 一共多少钱？— answer with any amount in Chinese.

2. *(4 min)* **Reading comprehension** — Workbook p.131 Ex.8, projected. Students read silently, then answer the four true/false on whiteboards:

> 上个星期天我跟妈妈去市场买菜了。市场上人很多。妈妈先买了两条鱼和一斤猪肉。那天的苹果特别便宜，十块钱九个。妈妈买了二十块钱的苹果。我们还买了八个橙子，花了二十块钱。最后，我们又买了一些青菜。市场离我家很近，我们走路回家了。

① 昨天她跟妈妈一起去市场买菜了。(F — 上个星期天) ② 她妈妈买了十八个苹果。(T — 二十块买十块钱九个) ③ 她妈妈没有买蔬菜。(F — 买了青菜) ④ 她们走路回家了。(T)

鱼、猪肉、橙子、便宜 are unknowns — let them work around them rather than pre-teaching. That is exactly what the test's reading section asks for.

**Connection:** "At the market you asked the price. At a restaurant there's a different question — not how much, but how good. That's today."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On screen. Introduce with a taste test in words: put 炒面 on screen and ask 这炒面怎么样？Accept English answers for ten seconds, then say you'll have six Chinese ways to answer it by the end of the I Do.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 好吃 | hǎochī | delicious | a bowl of noodles with steam lines |
| 1 | 特 | tè | special | — |
| 2 | 别 | bié | other | — |
| 2 | 特别 | tèbié | special; unusually | a star beside the bowl of noodles |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 这个包子很**好吃**。 — This bun is delicious.
2. 我妈妈做的炒面非常**好吃**。 — The stir-fried noodles my mum makes are delicious.
3. 这家饭店的炒菜不太**好吃**。 — This restaurant's fried dishes aren't very nice.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 很好吃。
**Extension —** 写三句，用三个不同的程度：非常好吃／很好吃／不太好吃，每句写一个别的菜。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 这个西瓜**特别**甜。 — This watermelon is unusually sweet.
2. 他们的包子**特别**好吃。 — Their buns are especially delicious.
3. 这家饭店**特别**好，可是**特别**贵。 — This restaurant is especially good, but especially expensive.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 特别好吃。
**Extension —** 用「特别」和「可是」写一句：这家的比萨饼特别好吃，可是特别贵。

**Recall slide:** 好吃 特 别 特别 — characters only.

#### The 好吃 scale (textbook p.116, Words for Reference)
Put it up as a ladder, strongest at the top. This is a parallel to the 喜欢 scale they already know from Unit 3:

> 特别好吃 > 非常好吃 > 很好吃 > 好吃 > 不错 > 不太好吃 > 不好吃

Class reads it down and then up. Then teacher names a food and points at a rung; the class produces the whole sentence.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
得 after the verb, to say **how** the action is done:

> S + V + **得** + 很/特别/不太 + adjective

**NOTE (textbook p.117):** 得 is placed after a verb to form a complement, and there are two shapes for the same meaning:
> 他**说**汉语**说得**很好。 or 他汉语**说得**很好。

Four modelled examples, last one hardest:
1. 他跑得很快。
2. 他跑步跑得很快。
3. 他们的饭菜做得特别好吃。
4. 姐姐的小提琴拉得不太好听，可是她钢琴弹得特别好。 — two clauses, two 得, and a contrast.

#### CFU Checkpoints
On whiteboards, straight after the pattern:
- Where does 得 go — before or after the verb? (After. ✗ 他得跑很快)
- Translate: "My dad plays basketball very well." (爸爸篮球打得很好。／爸爸打篮球打得很好。)
- What's wrong with 他跑得快很？(很 goes before the adjective: 跑得很快)
- Give me the strongest way to say something is delicious. (特别好吃)
- 这家饭店的饭菜做得怎么样？— answer in a full sentence.

#### Text / Dialogue — Text 2, first half (p.115, track 59)
Play track 59 to the end of the second exchange. On screen:

> A: 你们家常去饭店吃饭吗？
> B: 差不多每两星期去一次。
> A: 你们经常去哪家饭店吃饭？
> B: 我们经常去一家上海饭店吃饭。他们的饭菜做得特别好吃。

Choral read, then pairs. Comprehension in Chinese: ① 他们去哪家饭店？ ② 那家饭店的饭菜怎么样？

(次 and 差不多每两星期 are next lesson's business — read them, don't teach them today.)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 这炒面怎么样？— We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** today's four words plus the 好吃 ladder, up for the whole activity.

Textbook p.116 Ex.8. Eight pictured dishes on screen: 炒面 · 比萨饼 · 香蕉 · 炒菜 · 汉堡包 · 西瓜 · 苹果 · 青菜. Pairs work through all eight:

> A: 这炒面怎么样？ B: 非常好吃。

Rule: no rung of the ladder may be used twice by the same student — that forces all seven degrees into play. Teacher circulates; take three pairs aloud at the end.

**Extension —** B must justify every answer with a second sentence using 因为 and something from an earlier lesson: 不太好吃，因为我不喜欢吃青菜，我一般吃水果。Then A asks a follow-up: 那你喜欢吃什么？ — and B answers without looking at the board.

#### Activity 2: 得 sentence building — You Do (8 min)
Textbook p.117 Ex.10, six pictures: 游泳 · 打网球 · 弹钢琴 · 穿衣服 · 画画儿 · 跳舞. Students write one sentence per picture in their books, using 得 and a different degree word each time. Pinyin stays on screen for the first two minutes, then comes down.

Then Workbook p.137 Ex.21 as a fast check — match the six sentence halves:
妈妈的菜…做得不太好吃 · 王天一的油画儿…画得特别好看 · 美文的钢琴…弹得特别好听 · 冬冬的水彩画儿…画得很好看 · 这家饭店的饭菜…炒得不算好吃 · 姐姐的小提琴…拉得不太好听

**Extension —** Write each of the six picture sentences in **both** shapes (他游泳游得很快 / 他游得很快), then write a four-sentence paragraph about one real person in your family: what they do well, what they don't do well, and what they cook — every sentence using 得, joined with 可是 and 除了…以外. No word bank.

#### Activity 3: Character copying — Text 2 new words (3 min)
Workbook p.133 Ex.11. Teacher models stroke order on the board for 特 and 概 (the two hard ones); students copy 次、特、别、概、百、算、贵 three times each. Run it as quiet writing while you circulate and check grip and stroke direction — 别 is written 口 first, not 力 first.

**Extension —** Write each of the seven characters once more from memory with the book closed, then use three of them in one sentence.

#### Activity 4: 很好吃／很好喝／很好看 (3 min)
Workbook p.134 Ex.12, projected. Six frames; students supply the item that fits:
这条裙子很好看 (given) · ________ 很好喝 · ________ 不好看 · ________ 很好吃 · ________ 不好吃 · ________ 很好听

Whiteboards, all held up at once. Point out the family: 好 + verb = "good to V" — 好吃、好喝、好看、好听.

**Extension —** Add 好玩 and 好学 to the family and write a sentence for each; then write one sentence that uses three members of the family at once.

#### Game: Odd One Out (3 min)
Five sets on screen, one at a time. Pairs decide which word doesn't belong **and say why in Chinese**. The reason is the point, not the answer.

1. 特别好吃 / 非常好吃 / 不好吃 / 很好吃 → 不好吃 (the only negative)
2. 炒面 / 包子 / 米饭 / 可乐 → 可乐 (a drink, 不是菜)
3. 得 / 很 / 特别 / 非常 → 得 (not a degree word)
4. 好吃 / 好喝 / 好看 / 好人 → 好人 (好 + noun, not 好 + verb)
5. 苹果 / 西瓜 / 土豆 / 香蕉 → 土豆 (蔬菜，不是水果)

- **Setup needed:** Projector only.
- **Harder version:** The pair must give a reason that works for a *different* word in the set as well, so there are two defensible answers — and argue for theirs in Chinese.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; then one student demonstrates criterion 3 with a sentence about a classmate.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic light on whiteboards — one colour word per criterion.
- **Exit ticket (half-slip):** Write one sentence with 得 about someone in your family.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: how *often* you eat out, and roughly how much it costs — 差不多每两星期去一次。"
