# Wardrobe, Bookshelf, and Questions Without 吗 有没有

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Furniture 家具 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask questions using the verb-not-verb form, and to finish describing a bedroom.

## Success Criteria
- I can name a wardrobe and a bookshelf in Chinese (衣柜、书架)
- I can turn a 吗 question into a verb-not-verb question (你有弟弟吗？→ 你有没有弟弟？)
- I can interview a classmate about their room using at least three verb-not-verb questions

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.138–139, 141 — Text 2, the p.139 NOTE, Ex.14 and Ex.16 displayed
- Audio: CD track 69 (p.138 Text 2), CD track 70 (p.141 Ex.16)
- Printed p.141 Ex.16 answer sheet (six a/b choices) — one per student
- Printed Find Someone Who grids — ten boxes, one per student
- Workbook pp.161–162 (character copying 柜 架, word chains)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4's bedroom — 书桌、椅子、把 — and position with 在.

**How:** **Pair dialogue from memory.** Text 2's trimmed version is on the board for thirty seconds, then taken away. Pairs reconstruct it, both roles, then swap. Two pairs perform.

Then four whiteboard items: *one chair · two desks · The computer is on the desk. · The chair is in front of the desk.*

**Connection:** "Two pieces of furniture are missing from that room, and there's a whole question form hiding in the first line of Text 2 that we skipped. Both today."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. On criterion two, be direct: "This is the biggest idea in the lesson and it costs nothing — you already know every verb involved. 有没有 is just 有 and 没有 pushed together."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 衣柜（衣櫃） | yīguì | wardrobe | Line drawing: tall two-door wardrobe, handles in the middle, a shirt on a hanger visible |
| 1 | 书架（書架） | shūjià | bookshelf | Line drawing: open four-shelf unit with books leaning on the top two shelves |

Both carry a character the class already has: 衣 from 洗**衣**机, 书 from **书**桌 and **书**房. Point at both on the summary board — the new part of each word is only one character.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我的房间里有一个**衣柜**。 — There's a wardrobe in my room.
2. **书架**上有很多书。 — There are lots of books on the bookshelf.
3. **衣柜**在**书架**左面。 — The wardrobe is to the left of the bookshelf.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的房间里有一个 ________ 和一个 ________ 。
**Extension —** Say what is *in* each of them and whether it's full, in two joined clauses: 我的衣柜里面有很多衣服，可是书架上没有很多书，因为我的书都在书桌上。

**Character writing (90 sec, inside the cycles):** after each cycle's *write your own*, before students put the pens down — teacher writes 柜 · 架 once on the board, calling the stroke order aloud; students copy each one three times on whiteboards, then hold up. Workbook pp.161–162 is the same characters with stroke-order boxes; hand it out for whoever wants the grid. This is the only writing practice these characters get, so don't let it slide when the cycle overruns.

**Recall + summary board:** the complete fifteen — 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 · 空调 · 洗衣机 · 里面 · 冰箱 · 烤箱 · 电炉 · 椅子 · 把 · 书桌 · 衣柜 · 书架 — characters and pictures only. This is the full Lesson 14 vocabulary set and it stays up for the rest of the lesson and all of Lesson 6.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**V-not-V questions** — p.139 NOTE: 你有弟弟吗？＝ 你有没有弟弟？

Same meaning, no 吗. Take the verb, say it, say 不 (or 没) plus it again.

| 吗 question | V-not-V |
|---|---|
| 你有自己的房间吗？ | 你**有没有**自己的房间？ |
| 你喜欢你的房间吗？ | 你**喜不喜欢**你的房间？ |
| 你想买一个书架吗？ | 你**想不想**买一个书架？ |
| 这是你的衣柜吗？ | 这**是不是**你的衣柜？ |
| 你要买椅子吗？ | 你**要不要**买椅子？ |
| 你买书架吗？ | 你**买不买**书架？ |

1. 你有没有自己的房间？
2. 你喜不喜欢你的房间？
3. 你的房间里有没有书架？
4. 你有没有自己的房间？你喜不喜欢？为什么？ *(hardest — two V-not-V questions chained, then a reason demanded)*

The trap worth naming: **有** takes 没 (有没有), not 不. Every other verb here takes 不.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Turn this into V-not-V: 你有空调吗？" Whole class, chorally.
- Whiteboards: "Write *Do you like your room?* without using 吗."
- "你有不有书架？ — thumbs up or down?" *(Down. 有没有.)*
- Cold call two students with a V-not-V question and require a full-sentence answer, not 有 on its own.

#### Text 2 in full (p.138, CD track 69)
> A: 你有没有自己的房间？ B: 有。
> A: 你的房间里有什么？ B: 有一张床、一张书桌、一把椅子、一个衣柜和一个书架。
> A: 你的房间里有电脑吗？ B: 有，在我的书桌上。

Now complete — the opening question and the last two nouns are all today's content. Play CD 69 in full, choral read, then pairs both ways. Note that A uses V-not-V in line 1 and 吗 in line 3, both correct.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Your complete room — summary-board task (7 min)
Fifteen-word board up. Students write their own version of B's long answer from Text 2 — everything in their bedroom, in one list-sentence with correct measure words, then two more sentences saying where two of the items are with 在.

Then the constraint that makes it use the whole board: add a second short paragraph about the rest of the house, so that **every one of the fifteen words appears somewhere**. Anything you don't own goes in with 没有.

**Extension —** Write it as the answer to a question you haven't been asked — someone has asked whether you'd swap rooms with your sibling. Use all fifteen words across a comparison of two rooms, join with 可是 and 因为…所以…, and end with a decision. No board.

#### Activity 2: CD 70 — two brothers sharing a room (6 min)
Answer sheet out. Play **CD 70** (p.141 Ex.16) twice; students do the six a/b choices.

Gloss 上下床 (bunk bed) on the board before playing — it's an extra word, recognition only. 我睡上床，弟弟睡下床 is worth putting up afterwards, because 上 and 下 are doing the same job they do in 楼上／楼下.

Mark together, then mine the script for grammar: the first line is 你有没有自己的房间？ Play it once more and have the class write down every V-not-V question they hear.

**Extension —** After marking, rewrite the dialogue from the younger brother's point of view — same room, same furniture, different speaker, and every question the older brother asked has to come back as a V-not-V question with a different verb.

#### Activity 3: p.139 Ex.14 — write five, interview three — You Do (6 min)
Each student writes **five** questions, one for each of 有没有 · 要不要 · 是不是 · 想不想 · 喜不喜欢, all about rooms or furniture. Two minutes writing.

Then out of seats: interview three classmates, note the answers in short form. Two minutes.

Back in seats: two students report one thing they found out, in the third person — 他有自己的房间，可是他不喜欢，因为房间不大。

**Extension —** The five questions must all be about the *same* classmate's room and build on each other, so answer three determines question four. Then report back as a connected paragraph of four sentences rather than one fact, without notes.

#### Game: Find Someone Who (4 min)
Ten-box grid, every prompt a V-not-V question. Students circulate; a signature goes in a box only if the answer is yes, and no one may sign twice.

有没有自己的房间 · 有没有书架 · 喜不喜欢自己的房间 · 房间里有没有电脑 · 家里有没有烤箱 · 想不想买新书桌 · 家里有没有空调 · 有没有两个浴室 · 家里有没有车库 · 房间大不大

- **Setup needed:** Grids printed, one per student.
- **Harder version:** A signature only counts if the signer also gives a reason — and the asker has to write that reason into the box in Chinese. Full grid wins; five boxes with reasons beats ten without.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each criterion. Criterion two is the one to watch — if it's soft, open Lesson 6 with two minutes of transformation drill.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, one question and one answer: ask a V-not-V question about a bedroom, then answer it in a full sentence.
- **Preview:** "Last lesson of this unit: we go furniture shopping, find out which floor sells wardrobes, and then you describe a whole house to the class."
