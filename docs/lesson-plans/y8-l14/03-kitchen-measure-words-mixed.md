# The Kitchen, and Every Measure Word 冰箱、烤箱、电炉

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Furniture 家具 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what is in a kitchen, and to choose the right measure word for anything in the house.

## Success Criteria
- I can name three kitchen appliances in Chinese (冰箱、烤箱、电炉)
- I can pick the right measure word from 个、间、张、辆、台、口 for a given noun
- I can tell 有 and 在 apart and use each correctly (厨房里有冰箱／冰箱在厨房里)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.132, 134, 135, 137 — Ex.3, Ex.4, Ex.6 and Ex.9 displayed
- Audio: CD track 68 (p.137 Ex.9)
- Printed p.137 Ex.9 tick-grid — 客厅 (沙发 电视 电视柜 茶几 空调) and 厨房 (冰箱 电话 电炉 烤箱 洗衣机), one per student
- Printed p.134 Ex.4 translation sheet ×9 — one per student
- Workbook pp.156–157 (measure-word fill-in with the 18-item bank)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The 七 words so far — 沙发、茶几、电视柜、张、空调、洗衣机、里面 — plus 不算大／不大也不小.

**How:** **Correct the Teacher.** Five sentences on the board, three of them wrong. Students work in pairs, then one pair at a time comes up and fixes one.

- 客厅里有一个沙发。 ✓ *(also fine — but ask which measure word Text 1 used)*
- ✗ 洗衣机有厨房里面。 → 洗衣机在厨房里面。
- ✗ 我们家的客厅不算大也不小。 → 不大也不小 *(or 不算大 — not both)*
- 车库里面可以停两辆车。 ✓
- ✗ 我家有一个空调在客厅。 → 客厅里有一个空调。

The 有/在 pair is the one to dwell on — it is coming back in twenty minutes as a whole activity, and the unit test asks about it twice.

**Connection:** "Text 1 had three blanks in it last lesson. Today you get the three words, and then the measure words that go with them — and with everything else in this book."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Flag the second criterion honestly: "Six measure words is a lot. You've met all of them before — 间 and 辆 last lesson, 个 since Year 7. Today is about choosing, not learning."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 冰箱 | bīngxiāng | refrigerator | Line drawing: upright fridge-freezer, two doors, handle on the right |
| 1 | 烤箱 | kǎoxiāng | oven | Line drawing: built-in oven, glass door, two dials above |
| 2 | 电炉（電爐） | diànlú | electric stove | Line drawing: flat cooktop with four rings and a pot on one |

Point out the shared character: 冰**箱** and 烤**箱** both end in 箱 (box). 冰 is ice, 烤 is to roast — an ice-box and a roasting-box. Worth thirty seconds; it makes both words stick.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 厨房里面有一个**冰箱**。 — Inside the kitchen there's a fridge.
2. 我家的**烤箱**很大。 — Our oven is big.
3. **冰箱**在**烤箱**左面。 — The fridge is to the left of the oven.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家的厨房里面有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Say where both are, relative to each other and to something else in the kitchen: 冰箱在洗衣机右面，烤箱在冰箱下面。Then add whether your family actually has one, using 可是.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 厨房里有一个**电炉**。 — There's an electric stove in the kitchen.
2. 我们家的**电炉**不大也不小。 — Our stove is neither big nor small.
3. 厨房里面有冰箱、烤箱和一个**电炉**。 — Inside the kitchen there's a fridge, an oven and a stove.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 厨房里面有 ________ 和一个**电炉**。
**Extension —** Write the whole kitchen sentence from Text 1 from memory, then change it so it's true of *your* kitchen, using 可是 or 没有 for anything you don't have.

**Character writing (90 sec, inside the cycles):** after each cycle's *write your own*, before students put the pens down — teacher writes 冰 · 箱 · 烤 · 炉 once on the board, calling the stroke order aloud; students copy each one three times on whiteboards, then hold up. Workbook pp.157–158 is the same characters with stroke-order boxes; hand it out for whoever wants the grid. This is the only writing practice these characters get, so don't let it slide when the cycle overruns.

**Recall + summary board:** all ten words so far — 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 · 空调 · 洗衣机 · 里面 · 冰箱 · 烤箱 · 电炉 — characters and pictures only. Stays up through the practice phase.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**有 vs 在** — the grammatical spine of the whole unit, and this is the lesson that nails it.

1. 厨房里有一个冰箱。 — 有 puts something new into a place you already know about.
2. 冰箱在厨房里。 — 在 tells you where a thing you already know about sits.
3. 客厅里有一张沙发；沙发在电视柜前面。 — both, back to back, same two objects.
4. 我们家的房子有两层，楼下有厨房和客厅，我的房间在楼上，房间里面有一张床。 *(hardest — four clauses alternating 有 and 在)*

Write the test on the board and leave it there: **new information → 有. Known thing, unknown place → 在.**

#### Text 1 complete (p.132, CD track 66) + Listening (p.137 Ex.9, CD track 68)
Read Text 1 in full — the kitchen sentence now has no blanks. Class reads chorally.

Then hand out the Ex.9 grid and play **CD 68** twice: two speakers describe a living room and a kitchen. Students tick what's there. The second speaker says 可是我们没有烤箱 — mark it together and ask who caught it. Comprehension in Chinese: 他们家有没有烤箱？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Finish the kitchen — summary-board task (7 min)
Summary board up. Two-part task, both on the same sheet:

**Part A (3 min):** Write out Text 1's kitchen sentence complete, from the picture prompts alone.
**Part B (4 min):** Rewrite it as *your* kitchen. Every one of the ten words on the board must appear somewhere across your sentences — so the living-room words have to go into a second sentence about the 客厅. Four to five sentences.

Two students read theirs out while the board is still up.

**Extension —** Write it as a comparison of two kitchens — yours and one you know (a relative's, a holiday house, the school canteen). Every word on the board still has to appear, joined with 可是 and 也: 我们家的厨房不大也不小，里面有冰箱和电炉，可是没有烤箱；我奶奶家的厨房特别大… No board.

#### Activity 2: p.134 Ex.3 measure words — We Do (6 min)
Whiteboards, whole class, projected from the textbook. Sixteen blanks, the bank across the top: 个 口 头 朵 位 台 条 家 间 辆 张 门.

Run it fast — teacher reads the noun, everyone writes the measure word, all hold up on three. Teacher scans the room and says the answer only after the boards are up. Anything below about 70% correct in the room, stop and do two more of the same type before moving on.

Group them on the board as you go, so the pattern shows rather than being memorised:
- **间** — rooms · **层** — storeys · **辆** — vehicles · **张** — flat things (沙发、床、书桌、纸) · **台** — machines (电视、电脑、洗衣机) · **个** — everything else

**Extension —** Don't fill the blanks, write the rule. For each measure word in the bank, give the category in English and two nouns that take it, at least one not on the sheet. Then find the two nouns on the sheet that can take more than one measure word and explain the difference.

#### Activity 3: p.134 Ex.4 有／在 translation — You Do (5 min)
The nine E→C sentences, on paper, silent, independent. Students mark their own as the class goes through, and write **有** or **在** in the margin of each to show which rule they used.

**Extension —** After the nine, write three more English sentences of your own that would trap a classmate into the wrong choice, swap with a partner, and mark each other's.

#### Game: p.135 Ex.6 whole-class knockout (5 min)
Everyone stands. Teacher says an English word or phrase from anywhere in Lessons 13–14; the student pointed at says it in Chinese. Wrong or silent for three seconds, sit down. Last three standing win.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing. Teacher works from the summary board plus the L13 room list.
- **Harder version:** Once fewer than eight are standing, switch the prompt — the teacher says a **measure word** and the student must supply a noun that takes it and use it in a full sentence. No noun may be used twice in the game.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each. Pay attention to criterion three — if 有/在 is shaky, it opens Lesson 4's review.
- **Exit ticket:** Two short answers on one slip: (1) 厨房里有什么？ one sentence, three items. (2) The measure word for 洗衣机.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we leave the shared rooms and go into your own bedroom — a desk, a chair, and a measure word that exists for almost nothing else."
