# My Own Room 一把椅子、一张书桌

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Furniture 家具 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe the furniture in our own bedroom and say exactly where each piece is.

## Success Criteria
- I can name a desk and a chair in Chinese (书桌、椅子)
- I can use 把 for chairs and 张 for desks and beds (一把椅子、一张书桌)
- I can say where one thing is relative to another (电脑在书桌上面)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.138–139 — Text 2, Ex.12 and Ex.13 displayed
- Audio: CD track 69 (p.138 Text 2)
- Printed blank A4 bedroom sheets — a plain rectangle with eight ruled lines beneath, one per student
- Printed p.139 Ex.13 picture sheet (13 furniture items) — one per pair
- Workbook pp.160–161 (character copying 桌 把 椅)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 有 vs 在, and the measure-word grid from last lesson.

**How:** Mini whiteboards, six items, fast. Teacher says the English; students write the Chinese and hold up.

1. There is a fridge in the kitchen. (厨房里有一个冰箱。)
2. The fridge is in the kitchen. (冰箱在厨房里。)
3. one washing machine (一台洗衣机)
4. three bedrooms (三间卧室)
5. two cars (两辆车)
6. Our living room is not that big. (我们家的客厅不算大。)

Items 1 and 2 go up side by side on the board afterwards, with 有 and 在 circled. Ask the rule back: *new information* → 有; *known thing, unknown place* → 在.

**Connection:** "You've furnished the rooms everyone shares. Today it's your own room — and the sentence you'll be able to write by the end is the longest one in this whole lesson."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. On criterion two: "把 is the measure word for chairs. It exists for chairs, knives, umbrellas and almost nothing else you'll ever need. Learn it with 椅子 and you'll never need it separately."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 椅子 | yǐzi | chair | Line drawing: simple four-leg chair with a back, side-on |
| 1 | 把 | bǎ | measure word for chairs | Diagram: 一把椅子 · 两把椅子 · 三把椅子, three chairs in a row with the numbers above |
| 2 | 书桌（書桌） | shūzhuō | desk | Line drawing: writing desk with a drawer and a lamp on top |

These two are taught together on purpose — 把 is worth nothing on its own, and a chair without its measure word is where every student loses a test mark.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. **椅子**在电视前面。 — The chair is in front of the TV.
2. 我的房间里有一**把椅子**。 — There's a chair in my room.
3. 餐厅里有六**把椅子**。 — There are six chairs in the dining room. *(L13's 餐厅)*

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的房间里有 ________ 把椅子。
**Extension —** Count the chairs in three different rooms of your house in one sentence, joined, and say which room has the most: 客厅里有两把椅子，餐厅里有六把，我的房间里有一把。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我的房间里有一张**书桌**。 — There's a desk in my room.
2. 电脑在我的**书桌**上。 — The computer is on my desk. *(the last line of Text 2)*
3. **书桌**前面有一把椅子。 — In front of the desk is a chair.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我的书桌上有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Four things on or around your desk, each positioned relative to the last, in one connected sentence — 我的书桌上有电脑，电脑前面有书，书桌下面有一个书包。

**Character writing (90 sec, inside the cycles):** after each cycle's *write your own*, before students put the pens down — teacher writes 桌 · 把 · 椅 once on the board, calling the stroke order aloud; students copy each one three times on whiteboards, then hold up. Workbook pp.160–161 is the same characters with stroke-order boxes; hand it out for whoever wants the grid. This is the only writing practice these characters get, so don't let it slide when the cycle overruns.

**Recall + summary board:** thirteen items now — 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 · 空调 · 洗衣机 · 里面 · 冰箱 · 烤箱 · 电炉 · 椅子 · 把 · 书桌 — characters and pictures only. Stays up for the rest of the lesson.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**A 在 B 上面／前面／左面／右面／下面** — precise position, not just "in the room".

1. 电脑在书桌上面。
2. 椅子在书桌前面。
3. 我的床在房间里面，书桌在床的左面。
4. 我的房间不算大，里面有一张床和一张书桌；电脑在书桌上面，椅子在书桌前面。 *(hardest — 有 to open, then three 在 clauses)*

Note the 的 in 床**的**左面 — with a possessor in front, 的 goes in. 书桌上 and 书桌上面 are both fine.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Measure word for 椅子 — everyone say it. Now for 书桌. Now for 床."
- Whiteboards: "Write *there are two chairs in my room*."
- "我的电脑在书桌 — thumbs up or down?" *(Down. 在书桌上. A position word is not optional after 在.)*
- Cold call three: 你的房间里有什么？ One item each, with its measure word, no repeats.

#### Text 2, first half (p.138, CD track 69 — trimmed)
> A: 你的房间里有什么？
> B: 有一张床、一张**书桌**和一**把椅子**。
> A: 你的房间里有电脑吗？
> B: 有，在我的**书桌**上。

Play CD 69 and stop it after B's second answer — the full version has two words that arrive next lesson. Read in pairs both ways, then swap roles. Comprehension in Chinese: 他的电脑在哪儿？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Draw and label your bedroom — summary-board task (9 min)
Teacher models first on the board for 90 seconds: draw a rough room, add three items, write one sentence under each as the class dictates.

Then the blank sheets. Students draw their own bedroom and write **one sentence per item**, using a correct measure word every time. Minimum five sentences; the last one must use 在 and a position word rather than 有. Five minutes working, teacher circulating. Two students share while the board is up.

**Extension —** Not your room — a room you'd design if you could, with a budget of six items. Write it as a connected paragraph rather than a list: open with the size (不算大 / 不大也不小), place every item relative to another with 在, use every one of the thirteen words on the board that could plausibly be in a bedroom, and finish with one sentence saying why you chose it — 因为我喜欢看书，所以书桌在窗户前面. No reference to the summary board.

#### Activity 2: p.139 Ex.13 measure-word naming — We Do (5 min)
Picture sheet, one per pair, thirteen furniture items. A points at a picture, B names it **with its measure word** — 一把椅子, not 椅子. Wrong measure word, A points at the same picture again. Straight through, then swap and go faster.

Then as a class: teacher points, whole room answers chorally. Anything the room hesitates on goes on the board.

**Extension —** B names the item, its measure word, *and* which room it belongs in, in one sentence — 一把椅子，椅子在餐厅里面 — and may not use the same room twice in a row, which forces them to justify the odd placements.

#### Activity 3: p.139 Ex.12 — six sentences on the 在／有 models — You Do (5 min)
Silent, independent, in books. Six sentences: three must use 有, three must use 在, and all six must be true of the student's own house. Underline 有 or 在 in each.

**Extension —** Six sentences that alternate 有 and 在 and *chain* — each one has to pick up a noun from the sentence before it, so the six read as one description of a route through the house rather than six separate facts.

#### Game: 20 Questions — the hidden room (4 min)
One student thinks of a room in their house. The class asks yes/no questions in Chinese only — 房间里有沙发吗？床在房间里面吗？房间大不大？ — up to twenty, to work out which room it is. Two or three rounds.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing.
- **Harder version:** The student answering may not say 有 or 没有 — every answer has to be a full sentence that carries new information without giving the room away directly. Guessers who ask a question already asked are out for that round.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads all three from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** One sentence: *There's a desk and a chair in my room.* (我的房间里有一张书桌和一把椅子。) Correct measure words or it doesn't count.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson the wardrobe and the bookshelf finish the room — and then a new way of asking questions that gets rid of 吗 completely."
