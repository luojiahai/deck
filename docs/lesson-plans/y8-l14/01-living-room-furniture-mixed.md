# The Living Room 客厅里有一张沙发

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Furniture 家具 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what furniture is in a living room, using the right measure word for each piece.

## Success Criteria
- I can name three pieces of living-room furniture in Chinese (沙发、茶几、电视柜)
- I can use 张 for a sofa and 个 for the other pieces (一张沙发、一个茶几)
- I can say what is in a room using 客厅里有…

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.132–133, p.135 — p.133 Ex.1 and p.135 Ex.5 displayed
- Printed blank A4 "my living room" sheets — a plain rectangle with six ruled lines beneath, one per student
- Printed Slap the Character cards: 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 plus six L13 room words as distractors — two sets, A3
- Workbook pp.154–155 (copy new words Text 1)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 13's whole house — 房间、卧室、书房、浴室、客厅、餐厅、厨房、洗手间、花园、车库 — and the three measure words that came with it: 间 for rooms, 层 for storeys, 辆 for vehicles.

**How:** Mini whiteboards, rapid fire. Teacher says the English, students write the Chinese and hold up: *upstairs · garage · three bedrooms · two cars · a two-storey house*. Then two sentences to fix, written on the board with deliberate errors:

- ✗ 我的房间有楼上。 → 我的房间在楼上。
- ✗ 楼上在三间卧室。 → 楼上有三间卧室。

Ask for the rule in English: 有 introduces something new into a place, 在 says where a known thing is. Both come back today.

**Connection:** "Last lesson you built a house — rooms, storeys, a garage. Today we start putting things *in* it. The first room we furnish is the 客厅."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on the projector and leave up for the whole lesson. Read the three criteria aloud, then point at the second one: "一张 or 一个 — that's the part that will catch you out, and it's the part the unit test asks about."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 沙发（沙發） | shāfā | sofa | Line drawing: three-seat sofa, side view, cushions visible |
| 1 | 茶几 | chájī | tea table | Line drawing: low rectangular table with a teacup on it |
| 2 | 电视柜（電視櫃） | diànshìguì | TV cupboard | Line drawing: low cabinet with a TV standing on top |
| 2 | 张（張） | zhāng | measure word for flat things | Diagram: 一张沙发 · 两张沙发 · 三张沙发, three sofas in a row with the numbers above |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 客厅里有一个**沙发**。 — There's a sofa in the living room.
2. **茶几**上有一个电话。 — There's a phone on the tea table.
3. **沙发**前面有一个**茶几**。 — In front of the sofa is a tea table.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家的客厅里有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two pieces of furniture and where one is relative to the other: 我家的客厅里有一个沙发和一个茶几，茶几在沙发前面。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 客厅里有一个**电视柜**。 — There's a TV cupboard in the living room.
2. 我家有一**张**大沙发。 — We have one big sofa.
3. 我们家的客厅里有一**张**沙发和一个**电视柜**。 — Our living room has a sofa and a TV cupboard.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家的客厅里有一 ________ 沙发。
**Extension —** Write the three-item sentence from Text 1 from memory, then add where the TV is: 电视在电视柜上面。

**Character writing (90 sec, inside the cycles):** after each cycle's *write your own*, before students put the pens down — teacher writes 沙发 · 茶几 · 柜 · 张 once on the board, calling the stroke order aloud; students copy each one three times on whiteboards, then hold up. Workbook pp.154–155 is the same characters with stroke-order boxes; hand it out for whoever wants the grid. This is the only writing practice these characters get, so don't let it slide when the cycle overruns.

**Recall + summary board:** 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 — characters and pictures only, no pinyin, no English. Class reads them back. This board stays on screen for the rest of the lesson and is the only reference during practice.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**place 里有 + 量词 + object** — the spine of the whole textbook lesson.

1. 客厅里有一个沙发。
2. 客厅里有一**张**沙发和一个茶几。
3. 我们家的客厅里有一张三人沙发、一个茶几和一个电视柜。 *(this is Text 1, p.132)*
4. 我家的房子有两层，楼下有客厅，客厅里有一张三人沙发、一个茶几和一个电视柜。 *(hardest — L13's 层／楼下 carrying today's pattern)*

Note 三人沙发 on the board as a bonus: 三人 + 沙发, a three-seater. Students can use it but don't have to.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Measure word for 沙发 — hold up one finger for 个, two fingers for 张." *(Both are acceptable in the book; 张 is the one Text 1 uses and the one the test wants.)*
- "我有一个茶几 — thumbs up if that's fine, thumbs down if something's wrong."
- Whiteboards: "Write *There's a TV cupboard in the living room.*"
- Cold call three students: 你家的客厅里有什么？ One answer each, no repeats.

#### Text (p.132, CD track 66 — first half only)
> 我们家的客厅不算大。客厅里有一张三人沙发、一个茶几、一个电视柜和一个空调(冷气机)。

Read the second sentence aloud, class repeats. Leave 不算大 and 空调 alone for now — point at them and say "two things in this text you can't say yet. Next lesson." Comprehension question in Chinese: 他们家的客厅里有几个茶几？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Design your 客厅 — We Do → You Do (10 min)
Summary board stays up. Start guided: teacher draws a living room on the board, class calls out what to add, teacher writes the sentence beneath each item as students dictate it — 客厅里有一张沙发。

Then hand out the blank sheets. Students draw their own living room and write **four** sentences beneath, one per item, using all four new words. Three minutes drawing, four minutes writing. Two students share while the board is still up.

**Extension —** Don't draw a room, describe one you've been in and can't change — your grandparents', a waiting room, the school library. Write it as a connected paragraph, not a list: five sentences joined, each one saying where the item is relative to the last (沙发在电视柜前面，茶几在沙发前面…), and end with a judgement using 特别 (客厅特别大). No reference to the summary board.

#### Activity 2: 客厅里有什么？ — chain drill then pair interview (7 min)
**Chain first (3 min):** Round the class, each student repeats what came before and adds one item. 客厅里有沙发 → 客厅里有沙发和茶几 → 客厅里有沙发、茶几和电视柜 → … Allow L13 rooms and objects in once the furniture runs out. When someone breaks, restart from them.

**Pairs (4 min):** Off p.133 Ex.1 pictures. A asks 你家的客厅里有什么？ B answers with three items and correct measure words, then swaps. Both partners then ask 你家的客厅大不大？ — 大 or 不大, one word is enough today.

**Extension —** The interviewer role, with follow-ups they have to invent: after B answers, A must ask one more question about a single item (你的沙发是什么颜色的？你们家的电视大吗？), then report back to the class about their partner in the third person — 他家的客厅里有一张沙发，可是没有茶几.

#### Game: Slap the Character (6 min)
Two A3 sets on the board: 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 plus six L13 room words. Two students up, flyswatters or hands. Teacher says the English, they slap the character. Winner stays on.

- **Setup needed:** Cards printed and blu-tacked, two sets side by side.
- **Harder version:** Teacher says a whole sentence and the pair must slap **every** noun in it, in order — 客厅里有一张沙发和一个茶几 is three slaps. For a fluent pair, teacher says only the measure word (一张…) and they slap any character that could follow it.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads all three aloud from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each criterion in turn — 👍 / 😐 / 👎. Watch the second one; if the room is mostly 😐 on measure words, that's the Review for Lesson 2.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, one sentence: *There is a sofa and a tea table in my living room.* Collected at the door.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson the air-conditioner and the washing machine — and how to say a room is *not that big*, which is the most useful thing in this whole unit."
