# Appliances and 里面 不大也不小

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Furniture 家具 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what appliances are inside a room, and to describe a room's size without saying just 大 or 小.

## Success Criteria
- I can name an air-conditioner and a washing machine in Chinese (空调／冷气机、洗衣机)
- I can say what is inside something using 里面有…
- I can describe a room's size with 不算大 or 不大也不小

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.132, 136 — Text 1 and p.136 Ex.7 displayed
- Audio: CD track 66 (p.132 Text 1)
- Printed Text 1 tick-grid: the six items of the living room down one side, ✓/✗ column — one per student
- Printed p.136 Ex.7 question strips, cut up — one set per pair
- Sentence Jumble strips: 里面／有／厨房／一个／洗衣机 and five more, cut up — one envelope per pair
- Workbook pp.155–156 (character copying 空 调 机)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's four words — 沙发、茶几、电视柜、张 — and the pattern 客厅里有…

**How:** Two rounds. **First**, flashcard rapid fire: characters only on screen, class says the Chinese chorally, then one student gives the English. Four cards, twice through, second time faster.

**Then**, the exit tickets from last lesson go back up on the board — three anonymous ones, at least one with a measure-word error in it. Class fixes them on whiteboards. The most likely error is 一个沙发 for 一张沙发; if nobody wrote it, write it yourself.

**Connection:** "You can furnish a living room now. Two things are missing: the machines, and a way to say how big the room is that isn't just 大 or 小."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. On the third criterion, say it plainly: "不大也不小 is four characters you already know, in an order you haven't used. It's the single most useful phrase in Unit 5 — it appears in both texts and in the test."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 空调（空調） | kōngtiáo | air-conditioner | Line drawing: wall-mounted split unit with three airflow arrows beneath |
| 1 | 洗衣机（洗衣機） | xǐyījī | washing machine | Line drawing: front-loader, circular door, clothes visible through it |
| 2 | 里面 | lǐmiàn | inside | Diagram: a box in outline with a dot clearly *inside* it, and a second dot outside for contrast |

Note on the board, not as a new word: **冷气机 lěngqìjī** is the same machine — the book gives both. 空调 is the mainland form, 冷气机 is used in Hong Kong and Taiwan. Students may use either.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 客厅里有一个**空调**。 — There's an air-conditioner in the living room.
2. 厨房里有一个**洗衣机**。 — There's a washing machine in the kitchen.
3. 客厅里有**空调**，厨房里有**洗衣机**。 — The living room has an air-conditioner, the kitchen has a washing machine.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家的 ________ 里有一个 ________ 。
**Extension —** Where in your house is each machine, and say one thing about it — 我家的洗衣机在厨房里面，空调在客厅里面，客厅的空调特别大。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 厨房**里面**有一个洗衣机。 — Inside the kitchen there's a washing machine.
2. 车库**里面**可以停两辆车。 — Inside the garage two cars can park. *(L13's 车库、停、辆)*
3. 我的房间**里面**有一张床。 — Inside my room there's a bed.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 里面有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Use 里面 and 外面 in one sentence about the same place: 车库里面有一辆车，外面也有一辆车。Then add why with 因为…所以…

**Character writing (90 sec, inside the cycles):** after each cycle's *write your own*, before students put the pens down — teacher writes 空 · 调 · 机 once on the board, calling the stroke order aloud; students copy each one three times on whiteboards, then hold up. Workbook pp.155–156 is the same characters with stroke-order boxes; hand it out for whoever wants the grid. This is the only writing practice these characters get, so don't let it slide when the cycle overruns.

**Recall + summary board:** 空调 · 洗衣机 · 里面 alongside yesterday's 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 — seven items, characters and pictures only. Stays up for the rest of the lesson.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**不算大 / 不大也不小** — hedged size, and the reason 里 and 里面 keep appearing next to it.

1. 我们家的客厅不算大。 — not that big.
2. 我们家的厨房不大也不小。 — neither big nor small.
3. 我的卧室不算大，可是里面有很多书。
4. 我们家的房子不大也不小，楼上有三间卧室，楼下的客厅不算大，可是里面有一张沙发、一个茶几和一个电视柜。 *(hardest — two hedges, a 可是 clause, and four items)*

Say why it matters: 大 and 小 are absolute and sound blunt; 不算大 is what a person actually says about their own home.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "不大也不小 — is that room big or small? Tell your partner in English in five seconds."
- Whiteboards: "Write *the kitchen is not that big*."
- "我的房间不算大也不小 — thumbs up or down?" *(Down. It's either 不算大 or 不大也不小, not a hybrid.)*
- Cold call: 你的卧室大不大？ Push for 不算大 rather than 大 or 不大.

#### Text 1 in full (p.132, CD track 66)
> 我们家的客厅不算大。客厅里有一张三人沙发、一个茶几、一个电视柜和一个空调(冷气机)。我们家的厨房不大也不小，里面有冰箱、洗衣机、烤箱和电炉。

The living-room half is now fully readable. Play CD 66 twice with the tick-grid; students tick the six living-room items as they hear them.

The kitchen half has three words they don't have yet — 冰箱、烤箱、电炉. Show them as pictures only, with the sentence frame 里面有 ____、洗衣机、____ 和 ____ on screen. Tell them the blanks get filled next lesson. Comprehension question in Chinese: 他们家的厨房大不大？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 客厅 or 厨房? — sorting the summary board (8 min)
Summary board on screen. Two headings on the whiteboard: 客厅 and 厨房. In pairs, students place all seven words under a heading — and three of them genuinely could go either way (空调、电视柜、里面 isn't a thing at all, which is the trap).

Take the board back as a whole class. For each word, one student must say *why* in a sentence rather than just naming the column: 洗衣机在厨房里面，因为我们家的洗衣机在那儿。Accept disagreement — 空调 in a bedroom is correct for many families.

**Extension —** Sort by a different axis entirely: which of these does your family actually have, and which don't you? Write two sentences per column — 我们家有空调，可是没有洗衣机，我们家的洗衣机在楼下 — and add one item per column from Lesson 13 so the paragraph runs to six sentences. No board.

#### Activity 2: p.136 Ex.7 pair dialogue (9 min)
Question strips, one set per pair, face down. A draws a strip, asks it; B answers in a full sentence; swap. Six exchanges each, roughly:

- 你家住什么样的房子？ · 你家的房子有几层？ · 你家有几间卧室？ · 你家的客厅大不大？ · 客厅里有什么？ · 厨房里面有什么？

The size question is the one to listen for — push every answer to 不算大 or 不大也不小 rather than 大.

Circulate and take the word bank away from pairs who are fluent; keep it in front of the pairs who need it.

**Extension —** Answer as someone else — a grandparent, a character in a film, someone living in a house nothing like yours — and stay consistent across all six answers. Then B summarises A's house back to the class in the third person, three sentences, no notes: 他家住洋房，房子有两层，客厅不算大。

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (6 min)
Envelopes of cut-up strips, one per pair. Six sentences to rebuild, each using today's words and the 里面有 pattern:

厨房／里面／有／一个／洗衣机 · 客厅／里／有／一个／空调 · 我家的／客厅／不算大 · 车库／里面／可以／停／两辆车 · 我们家的／厨房／不大／也／不小 · 客厅／里面／有／一张／沙发

First pair with all six correct wins. Teacher checks word order, not handwriting.

- **Setup needed:** Six envelopes' worth of strips per pair, cut in advance.
- **Harder version:** One envelope contains strips from *two* sentences mixed together and no indication where one ends — they have to work out the split first. Then they write a seventh sentence of their own using every leftover strip.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud by a student, then ask which one felt hardest.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic light on whiteboards — a green, orange or red dot for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** One sentence on a slip: *Our kitchen is not that big, and inside there's a washing machine.* (我们家的厨房不算大，里面有一个洗衣机。)
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we fill in the three blanks from Text 1 — the fridge, the oven and the stove — and then every measure word in the book comes back at once."
