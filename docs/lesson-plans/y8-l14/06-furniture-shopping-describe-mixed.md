# Furniture Shopping, and Describing a Whole House 应该上几楼？

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Furniture 家具 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask which floor of a store sells what we want, and to describe a furnished house to the class.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask which floor sells something (我要买衣柜，应该上几楼？)
- I can direct someone to a floor (请上三楼)
- I can describe a furnished house or room out loud for a minute, without reading it out

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides) — the fifteen-word summary board from Lesson 5 displayed throughout
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.136, 140, 141 — Ex.8, Ex.15 and Ex.17 displayed
- Printed blank store-directory sheets — a four-storey building outline with an empty label beside each floor, one per pair
- Printed p.140 Ex.15 interior picture — one per student
- Printed p.141 Ex.17 interview question strips, cut up — one set per pair
- Workbook pp.162–163 (E→C translation of the appliance and furniture names)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The whole of Lesson 14 — all fifteen words, the measure words, 有/在, and V-not-V.

**How:** Two minutes of V-not-V transformation on whiteboards, straight into a **matching race against the clock**. The fifteen-word board goes up as pictures only, numbered 1–15. Students have 90 seconds to write the Chinese for as many as they can, with the measure word. Best score read out; class fills the gaps together.

Then three whiteboard sentences to fix, drawn from the last five lessons:

- ✗ 我的房间有一个椅子。 → 一把椅子
- ✗ 洗衣机有厨房。 → 洗衣机在厨房里面。
- ✗ 你有不有书架？ → 你有没有书架？

**Connection:** "You can furnish a house. Today you go and buy the furniture — and then you describe the whole thing to the class, which is the last thing this unit asks of you."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Say plainly that today's plenary looks back over all six lessons, not just this one, and that the self-assessment at the end covers the whole of Lesson 14.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — none
Every word today is already on the board. What's new is one exchange built entirely from parts the class already has: 应该 (Unit 4), 楼 and 几 (Lesson 13), 上 (Year 7), 请 (Year 7). Say so out loud — it's a good moment for students to notice how much they can now assemble.

**Summary board:** the fifteen words from Lesson 5 — 沙发 · 茶几 · 电视柜 · 张 · 空调 · 洗衣机 · 里面 · 冰箱 · 烤箱 · 电炉 · 椅子 · 把 · 书桌 · 衣柜 · 书架 — characters and pictures, on screen for the entire lesson.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**我要买 X，应该上几楼？ → 请上 N 楼。** The p.136 Ex.8 department-store exchange.

1. 我要买衣柜，应该上几楼？ — 请上三楼。
2. 我要买冰箱，应该上几楼？ — 请上四楼。
3. 请问，你们有没有书架？ — 有，在二楼。 *(V-not-V from last lesson, carried straight in)*
4. 请问，我要买一张书桌和一把椅子，应该上几楼？ — 书桌在三楼，椅子也在三楼，请上三楼。 *(hardest — two items, two measure words, an answer that handles both)*

请问 as the opener is worth thirty seconds: it's what you say before asking a stranger anything, and it comes back all through Lesson 15's 问路.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "How do you ask which floor? Everyone, chorally." Then: "Now for a fridge."
- Whiteboards: "Write *Please go to the fourth floor.*"
- "我要买衣柜，应该几楼？ — thumbs up or down?" *(Down. 应该**上**几楼. The verb isn't optional.)*
- Cold call three: each gives a different item and the class answers with a floor.

#### Dialogue (p.136 Ex.8)
Model the full exchange with a student twice, roles both ways. Then class reads chorally, then pairs — four exchanges each, using four different items from the summary board.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Design a furniture store — summary-board task (8 min)
Directory sheets, one per pair. A four-storey 家具店. Pairs sort **all fifteen** words onto floors and write the floor labels in Chinese — so every word has to land somewhere, and a floor label like 厨房用的东西 has to be justified.

Three minutes sorting and labelling. Then two minutes writing: three exchanges from their own directory, written out in full, 我要买 X，应该上几楼？／请上 N 楼。

Two pairs read their directory aloud while the board is still up; the class checks that nothing was left off.

**Extension —** Five floors, not four, and the fifth is an odd one — a floor for things that don't belong anywhere else. The pair has to name that floor in Chinese and defend two items being on it: 电视柜不是厨房的东西，也不是卧室的东西，所以在五楼。Then write the exchange for a customer who wants something the store doesn't sell, and answer them: 对不起，我们没有…

#### Activity 2: Store role-play — We Do → You Do (6 min)
Pairs, directories out, one is the customer and one works there. The customer has a list of four items — teacher gives each pair a different four from the summary board, on a slip. Four exchanges, then swap roles with four new items.

Insist on 请问 to open and a full-sentence answer, not a number on its own.

Circulate; take the directory away from the pairs who no longer need it.

**Extension —** The customer doesn't name the item — they describe it and the staff member has to work out what they want before answering: 我要买一个大的，可以放很多书，在房间里面。Staff answers with the item name *and* the floor. Then the staff member has to try to sell them one more thing they didn't come for.

#### Activity 3: p.140 Ex.15 — describe the interior, then your own — You Do (6 min)
Ex.15 picture out. Three minutes silent: students write five to six sentences describing the drawn interior — rooms, furniture, positions, sizes.

Then books closed, pictures turned over. Two minutes: in pairs, each student describes it out loud from memory while their partner ticks off how many of the fifteen words they used. Highest count in each pair reports theirs to the class.

**Extension —** Describe your own house instead of the picture, and do it to the class for a full minute without notes. Open with the house (L13 — 层、楼上、楼下), go room by room, use 有 to introduce each room's contents and 在 to place at least three items, and close with a judgement using 不算大 or 特别. Then take two V-not-V questions from the audience and answer them.

#### Game: 20 Questions — what am I buying? (3 min)
One student picks an item from the summary board. Class asks V-not-V and yes/no questions in Chinese only to identify it — 是不是厨房的东西？有没有门？大不大？ Up to twenty questions. Two or three rounds; use p.141 Ex.17 strips as prompts if the questions dry up.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing beyond the summary board.
- **Harder version:** The answerer may not say 是 or 不是 — every answer must be a full sentence that gives partial information, and the class must ask in V-not-V form only. A yes/no-shaped question is a wasted turn.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud, then widen the frame — put all six lessons' criteria on screen together. Eighteen criteria, one lesson's worth at a time.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards, six rows, one per lesson: 👍 / 😐 / 👎. Collect them — this is the clearest picture you'll get of where the class stands before the unit test.
- **Exit ticket:** Two lines on a slip: (1) 我要买 ______，应该上几楼？ completed with any item. (2) One sentence describing any room in your house, with a correct measure word.
- **Preview:** "Next textbook lesson we leave the house completely — Lesson 15 is the neighbourhood. Shops, how far away they are, and how you get there."
