# 找到了吗？ Lost Property

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Asking the Way 问路 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say whether an action reached its result — found it or didn't, finished it or didn't.

## Success Criteria
- I can attach a result to a verb (找到、吃完、借走、听见)
- I can say what I could **not** do, with 没有 or 不 (我没有找到 / 我找不到)
- I can go to a service desk, report what's missing and answer what was in it

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Exercise books
- Printed A5 lost-property forms, one per student: 姓名 ／ 电话 ／ 丢了什么 ／ 在哪儿丢的 ／ 里边有什么
- Role-play cards for the service desk, three situations: 手机 / 钱包 / 书包 — one card per pair
- Printed workbook p.158 Ex 11 reading passage (spare copies from Lesson 3)
- No audio this lesson

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 5's 被 — word order, and the missing 了/result.
**How:** Correct the Teacher, four minutes. Five sentences on the board, three broken:
1. 我的手机被偷了。
2. 我的自行车被哥哥骑。
3. 被弟弟蛋糕吃完了。
4. 请留下你的姓名和电话号码。
5. 我的书被同学借。
Pairs find and fix on whiteboards, 90 seconds, then hands up. Draw out *why* 2 and 5 are wrong — the verb is standing alone — because that is precisely what today's grammar fixes.
Then two minutes on **workbook p.162 Ex 22** — fill in the twenty verbs. Do it as a race against the clock, in the exercise book; the verbs are the raw material for the complements of result coming in ten minutes.
Then two minutes of spoken revision on textbook **p.141 Act 12**: the five shopping questions, in pairs — 你家附近有购物商场吗？你常去哪儿购物？
**Connection:** 上节课你们说「我的书被同学借」— 不行，少了一个字。少的那个字，就是今天的语法。Last lesson the sentences that failed were all missing the same thing. That thing is today's lesson.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Give the whole idea in one pair of sentences before any examples: 我找了 — 我找了很久，可是不知道找到没有。我找**到**了 — 东西在我手里。「找」是做，「找到」是做成了。

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 身份证（證） | shēnfènzhèng | identity card | SVG — an ID card with a photo box and two ruled lines |
| 1 | 学生证（學證） | xuéshēngzhèng | student card | SVG — a card with a photo box and a small school crest |
| 2 | 借书证（書證） | jièshūzhèng | library card | SVG — a card with a small stack of books printed on it |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- All three end in 证 — a **card or certificate that proves something**. The first part says what it proves: 身份 (identity), 学生 (student), 借书 (borrowing books). Once they see the pattern, the three words are one word.
- 身份 = 身 (body) + 份 (portion) — who you are, your share of the world. 份 is also the measure word in 一份报纸.
- 借书证 reuses 借, which they have from 借书 and which comes back in ten minutes as 借走了.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我的**身份证**在钱包里。 — My ID card's in my wallet.
2. 学校给我们每个人一张**学生证**。 — School gives each of us a student card.
3. 我的钱包里有**身份证**和**学生证**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的 ________ 在 ________ 里。 · 我有一张 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write three sentences saying what each card lets you do, using 可以: 用学生证可以买便宜的车票。Then say which one you'd least want to lose and why.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 用**借书证**可以借书。 — You can borrow books with a library card.
2. 图书馆的**借书证**是绿色的。 — The library card is green.
3. 我的**借书证**、学生证和身份证都被偷了。 — reaching back to 被.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我的借书证 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write the contents of a stolen wallet as one sentence with 除了……以外，还有……, including all three cards and the cash — then one more sentence saying which you'd report first and why.

**Recall slide (characters only):** 身份证 · 学生证 · 借书证 · 手提包 · 钱包 · 钥匙 · 手机 · 现金 · 服装

#### Key Sentence Pattern — complement of result

```
动词  +  结果  (+ 了)
 找       到        了     ←  looked, and got there
 吃       完        了     ←  ate, and it's gone
 借       走        了     ←  borrowed, and took it away
 听       见               ←  listened, and heard
 做       完        了     ←  did, and finished
 穿       上        了     ←  put on, and it's on
```

1. 我**找到**我的手机了。
2. 我的自行车被哥哥**骑走**了。
3. 钱都**花完**了。
4. 我找了三次都没有**找到**，后来老师在体育馆门口**找到**了我的书包。 *(a failed attempt, then a successful one, two clauses — the hardest one)*

🔴 **The negative is the part that trips people.** 我**没有**找到 — I looked and didn't find it (it happened, it failed). 我**找不到** — I can't find it (right now, no matter what I do). Both appear in the workbook; both are on the test's translation section.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 白板：写 — I finished my homework.
- 「找」跟「找到」有什么不一样？用手比一比：一个是做，一个是做成了。
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 我找我的钱包到了。
- 全班一起：我的钱都被花完了 — 这句话里，钱是谁花的？我们知道吗？
- 白板：写 — I looked for it three times but didn't find it.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 失物招领 — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** the three new cards plus everything from Lessons 4–5, characters + pictures, stays on screen throughout.
Each student writes a lost-property notice on the printed form and then, underneath, four sentences in the exercise book. The four sentences must contain **all three card words**, at least one 被 sentence, and at least one complement of result.
> 昨天我的手提包在服装店被偷了。包里有钱包、钥匙和手机。钱包里有身份证、学生证和借书证。我找了很久，可是没有找到。

**Share:** Two students read theirs aloud; the class listens specifically for the complement of result and puts a hand up when they hear one.

**Extension —** Write it as a **notice on a board** rather than a story — third person, no 我, plus a line telling the finder what to do: 如果你找到了，请打电话给…… Then add one sentence about the reward. (This is workbook p.160 Ex 16's 失物招领 format.)

#### Activity 2: 结果补语 on paper — You Do (8 min)
Exercise books, in order.
1. **Textbook p.139 Act 8** (4 min) — complete the eight sentences with a complement of result, then **It is your turn!** write two of your own.
2. **Workbook p.161 Ex 18** (4 min) — translate the eight complement-of-result sentences into English. This is the direction the test uses, and it's the one students get less practice in.
3. **Workbook p.162 Ex 19** (if time, or as the early-finisher task) — write a dialogue for each of the three pictures. These are the written version of the service-desk role play that follows, so pairs who do this go into the game with a script they wrote themselves.

**Extension —** **Workbook p.163 Ex 23** — read the pickpockets passage, then write the 80–100 word account of losing a bag. It must contain at least two 被 sentences and three different complements of result. Anyone who reaches this instead of finishing Ex 18 has made the better trade.

#### Game: Role-play Cards — 服务台 (9 min)
Textbook p.140 Act 10. Pairs, cards face down, three situations in rotation: lost phone, lost wallet, lost school bag. A is at the service desk, B has lost the item. The desk must establish, in order: 你丢了什么？ 什么时候丢的？ 在哪儿丢的？ 里边有什么？ 请留下你的姓名和电话。 B answers from their Activity 1 form.
Swap roles after each situation so everyone works both sides.
- **Setup needed:** the three situation cards printed and cut; B reuses their own lost-property form.
- **Harder version:** the desk staff member says the item **has** been handed in — but then has to check it's really theirs, by asking what was inside before showing it: 你说说包里有什么，我看看对不对。The claimant has to recall their own list without looking. If the two lists don't match, the desk refuses.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
**This plenary looks back over the whole six-lesson sequence, not just today.**

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** today's three read aloud by a volunteer, then the screen switches to the six learning intentions from Lessons 1–6 in a single list.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs on each of the six lesson intentions in turn, not just today's criteria. The two to watch are Lesson 2 (the bus questions) and Lesson 5 (被) — they carry the most weight in the unit test, and a soft show of hands on either is worth five minutes at the start of Lesson 15.
- **Exit ticket:** 写两句 — 第一句用「被」，第二句用一个结果补语（找到／吃完／借走／听见）。两句要说同一件事。 Two sentences about one incident, collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下一课是第十五课 — 邻居。你家楼上的邻居，半夜还开着电视，很响。你怎么办？ Next lesson starts Lesson 15: neighbours, and what you say to the ones upstairs.
