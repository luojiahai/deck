# 被偷了 Text 2 — Reporting a Theft

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Asking the Way 问路 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use 被 to say that something happened to you rather than that you did it.

## Success Criteria
- I can build a 被 sentence in the right order (我的钱包被偷了)
- I can say who did it when I know (我的自行车被哥哥骑走了)
- I can report a theft to a police officer and answer their questions

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Exercise books
- Printed A4 copies of the textbook p.137 Text 2, one per student, characters only with the five new words glossed in the margin
- Role-play cards, one per pair: **A** = 警察 (with the four questions printed as prompts), **B** = 报案的人 (with a list of what was in the bag, different on each card)
- Printed A5 slips for the written police report, one per student
- Audio: CD 55 (textbook p.137), CD 56 (textbook p.140)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4's six words and the list structure.
**How:** Bingo, three minutes. Students draw a 3×3 grid and fill it with any nine from a pool of twelve on the board — 手提包 钱包 钥匙 手机 现金 服装 自行车 车站 广场 购物 庙 桥. Teacher calls the **English**; students cross off. First line shouts 中了.
Then three minutes on whiteboards: 写一句 — 你的手提包里有什么？至少三样东西，用「除了……以外，还有……」。
Two minutes: three students read theirs aloud.
**Connection:** 昨天我们数了包里的东西。今天，包不见了。Yesterday we counted what was in the bag. Today the bag is gone — and Chinese has a particular way of saying that.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Say the point of 被 in one line before any grammar: 中文平常先说「谁做的」。可是有的时候，你不知道是谁做的，或者你不在乎 — 你只在乎东西没有了。这时候用「被」。Chinese normally leads with who did it. 被 is for when you don't know, or don't care — you only care what happened to the thing.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 偷 | tōu | steal | SVG — a hand reaching into an open handbag from behind |
| 1 | 警察 | jǐngchá | policeman | SVG — an officer in cap and uniform, notebook in hand |
| 2 | 被 | bèi | passive marker | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 2 | 留 | liú | leave (behind) | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 3 | 姓名 | xìngmíng | full name | SVG — a form with a ruled line labelled 姓名 |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- 偷 takes 亻 on the left — a person doing something to someone. Same 亻 as 你, 他, 们.
- 警察 — both characters are only ever used together for this word at this level. Learn it as one block of two.
- 留 — 请留下你的电话号码 is the polite form students will hear in every office, shop and clinic. 留下 = leave behind, not 走.
- 姓名 = 姓 (surname) + 名 (given name). They have had 姓 since Book 1's 你姓什么？ This is that character doing a new job.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 有人在商店里**偷**东西。 — Someone's stealing in the shop.
2. **警察**就在广场的车站旁边。 — The police officer is right by the stop on the square.
3. **警察**问我：谁**偷**了你的钱包？ — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 警察在 ________ 。 · 我的 ________ 被人偷了。
**Extension —** Write the officer's next two questions, not the one on the screen — what would they actually need to know?

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我的钱包**被**偷了。 — My wallet was stolen.
2. 请**留**下你的电话号码。 — Please leave your phone number.
3. 我的手机**被**偷了，警察请我**留**下电话号码。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我的 ________ 被 ________ 了。 · 请留下你的 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write three 被 sentences where nothing has been stolen at all — 我的自行车被哥哥骑走了 / 蛋糕被弟弟吃完了 / 我的书被同学借走了. 被 is not a crime word.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 请写下你的**姓名**。 — Please write your full name.
2. 请留下你的**姓名**和电话号码。 — Please leave your name and phone number.
3. 老师叫我们在本子上写**姓名**。 — Teacher tells us to write our names on our books.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 请留下你的 ________ 和 ________ 。
**Extension —** 「姓名」跟「名字」有什么不一样？用中文写一句说明，然后说哪一个用在表格上。

**Recall slide (characters only):** 偷 · 警察 · 被 · 留 · 姓名

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 被 (textbook p.139 NOTE box)

```
受事（the thing）  +  被  +  [做的人]  +  动词  +  了 / 走了 / 完了
    我的钱包          被     （人）       偷       了
    我的自行车        被      哥哥        骑      走了
```

Three rules, said once each and written on the board:
1. **The thing comes first**, not the person. 我的钱包被偷了 — not *偷了我的钱包被*.
2. **Who did it is optional.** Drop it when you don't know: 我的钱包被偷了。
3. **The verb never stands alone.** It needs 了, or a result attached — 骑**走**了, 吃**完**了, 借**走**了. This is the piece students forget.

1. 我的钱包**被**偷了。
2. 我的自行车**被**哥哥骑走了。
3. 蛋糕**被**弟弟吃完了。
4. 我的手提包在服装店**被**偷了，里边的现金、钥匙和手机都不见了。 *(a 被 clause with a location, then a second clause — the hardest one)*

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 白板：写 — My bicycle was taken by my brother.
- 举手投票：「被」后面一定要写是谁做的吗？ 然后说说为什么。
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 我的钱包偷了被。
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 我的自行车被哥哥骑。
- 转过去问同伴：你被偷过吗？什么东西被偷了？ 三十秒。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 课文二 + 写你自己的报案 — We Do → You Do (8 min)
**Summary board:** 偷 警察 被 留 姓名, characters only, stays on screen throughout.

**First 4 minutes.** Play CD 55 twice with books closed, then hand out the character-only sheet. Choral read, then pairs read it aloud swapping roles.

> 小青：我在购物广场买东西的时候手提包被偷了。
> 警察：在哪家商店被偷的？ 小青：在四彩服装店。
> 警察：包里有什么？ 小青：有钱包、钥匙和手机。
> 警察：钱包里有什么？ 小青：有两百多块现金、身份证、学生证和借书证。
> 警察：请留下你的姓名和电话号码。 小青：好。

Three comprehension questions, answered in Chinese: 小青的包是在哪儿被偷的？ 包里有什么？ 警察最后要什么？

**Last 4 minutes.** On the printed slip, each student writes their **own** four-sentence report of a theft — a different place, different contents — using all five new words. It goes to the officer in Activity 3, so it has to make sense to someone else.

**Extension —** Write the report from the **officer's** side instead: a four-line note recording what was reported, in the third person — 小青说她的手提包在四彩服装店被偷了，包里有…… — then add the one question the officer forgot to ask.

If the room has devices, **workbook p.162 Ex 21** — typing the stolen-handbag passage — replaces the handwritten slip for anyone who wants it. Typing is the one place students have to know the pinyin cold, so it is worth offering rather than skipping.

#### Activity 2: 被 on paper — You Do (7 min)
Exercise books, in this order. Circulate; the two errors to catch are the missing 了/result and the reversed word order.
1. **Textbook p.139 Act 9** (4 min) — complete the seven 被 sentences, then **It is your turn!** write two of your own.
2. **Workbook p.161 Ex 17** (3 min) — the seven further 被 sentences.

**Extension —** Take any four sentences from Act 9 and rewrite each one **without** 被, putting the doer back in front: 哥哥把我的自行车骑走了 / 哥哥骑走了我的自行车. Then one sentence in English on when you'd choose each version. (把 is next term's Lesson 15 — they meet it early here and that's fine.)

#### Game: Role-play Cards — 报案 (8 min)
**First 3 minutes:** CD 56 (textbook p.140 Act 11), tick a, b or c for the six items. It's the stolen-phone conversation — 是什么时候被偷的？你的书包放在哪儿了？ — and it models exactly the role play that follows.

**Last 5 minutes:** pairs, role-play cards face down. A is 警察 and works through the four questions; B is reporting, and must use the report they wrote in Activity 1. Swap cards and run it again with the other partner's report.
- **Setup needed:** the A/B cards printed and cut before the lesson; B uses their own written slip.
- **Harder version:** the officer's card is blank — they invent their own questions and must ask at least six, including one the textbook doesn't have (什么时候被偷的？当时旁边有人吗？你去问过商店了吗？). The person reporting answers without looking at their slip.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student volunteer reads all three.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs, one criterion at a time. Criterion 1 is the one that matters — if the room isn't confident on word order, open Lesson 6 with two more minutes of it.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：说一样东西被偷了，或者被别人拿走了。不能用「钱包」。 One sentence, collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下节课是这个单元的最后一节 — 东西丢了以后怎么办？你去服务台，说：我找不到我的书包。 Next lesson closes the sequence: what you do *after* something goes missing.
