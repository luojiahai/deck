# 从你家怎么去？ Journeys From Home

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Asking the Way 问路 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to interview someone about a real journey from their home, and to report what they said to the class.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask how long a journey takes (走路要多长时间？坐车要多长时间？)
- I can answer with a real journey from my own home, not a made-up one
- I can report a classmate's answer in the third person (他每天骑自行车上学，大概要十五分钟)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers (used only for the report-back, not for the interview)
- Printed A5 interview grids, one per student — six rows for the six questions, two columns (我 / 我的同伴)
- Printed A5 copies of the textbook p.135 map, one per pair
- Printed workbook p.158 Ex 11 reading passage (getting to the supermarket by 地铁 / 小巴 / 出租车), one per pair
- A set of 12 place cards for 20 Questions: 机场 火车站 购物广场 教堂 邮局 银行 诊所 公园 体育中心 咖啡馆 图书馆 菜市场
- Audio: CD 54 (textbook p.136)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lessons 1–2 — the three bus questions, 或者, 座, and the facilities vocabulary students will need to name destinations today.
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire then a pair dialogue, both spoken — no writing this lesson until the report-back.
Two minutes: teacher shows a facility card (Chinese side), class chorally says it. Run the fifteen facilities from workbook p.156 Ex 6 — 邮局 银行 诊所 百货公司 市政大楼 教堂 公园 咖啡馆 购物广场 车站 服装店 机场 火车站 图书馆 体育中心.
Four minutes: pairs run the Text 1 dialogue from memory, then swap roles. The prompt on screen is only the three questions — 坐几路车？要坐几站？在哪站下车？
Two minutes: 你记得吗 — 一座什么？一条什么？Three quick answers cold-called.
**Connection:** 前两节课的路线都是书上的。今天的路线是真的 — 从**你家**出发，去**你**常去的地方。Every route so far has been the book's. Today they're yours, from your own front door.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Flag criterion 3 as the one that gets assessed: 你听同伴说，然后告诉全班 — 要用「他」「她」，不能用「我」。The third-person report is exactly the format of the unit test's oral questions, so it is worth naming as such.

### I Do (10–20 min)

**No new vocabulary this lesson.** Everything is Lessons 1–2 plus the facilities stock. The I Do time goes on the question set and on modelling a full answer.

#### Listening input — CD 54 (p.136 Act 6), 4 min
Play once straight through; students tick a or b for the six items on the textbook page. Play a second time, pausing after each item, and ask what word decided it. These six recordings are the model for the interview — 从你家怎么去电影院？你家附近有什么商店？你生病去哪儿看医生？ — so treat them as examples to copy, not just a comprehension test.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — the six journey questions (p.136 Act 5)

| Question | What it asks for |
|---|---|
| 从你家去学校怎么走？ | the route |
| 走路要多长时间？ | walking time |
| 需要坐车吗？坐几路？ | whether transport is needed |
| 从你家去市中心远吗？ | distance |
| 你常去哪儿购物？怎么去？ | habit + method |
| 从你家坐飞机去中国要飞几个小时？ | long-haul duration |

**Teacher think-aloud — building one full answer:**
从我家去学校，我先走五分钟到车站，然后坐25路公共汽车，坐三站。下车以后过马路，学校就在我的右手边。走路加坐车，大概二十分钟。

Four modelled answers, each a little longer:
1. 走路要十分钟。
2. 需要坐车。我坐97路，坐四站。
3. 从我家去市中心不远，坐地铁十五分钟就到了。
4. 从我家去机场，你或者坐火车，或者坐出租车。坐火车便宜，可是要换两次；坐出租车快，可是很贵。我一般坐火车，因为我不想花那么多钱。 *(two options, two comparisons, a reason — the hardest one)*

🔴 Say this once, plainly: 离 vs 从. 我家**离**学校很远 — distance between two fixed points. **从**我家去学校 — the start of a journey. The test's translation section uses both.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 用中文问我：How long does it take on foot? 全班一起问。
- 举手：「多长时间」问的是什么 — 多远，还是多久？
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 从我家学校很远。
- 转过去问同伴一句：你怎么上学？ 三十秒。听到几个人用了「或者」？
- 我说一个句子，你们说这是「离」还是「从」：________ 我家去机场要两个小时。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 地图上的路线 — We Do (6 min)
Pairs, one p.135 map between them. Four situations, spoken only, no notes: 去购物广场 / 去飞机场 / 去公共图书馆 / 去电影院. A asks with the three bus questions, B answers off the map. Swap after two.
Then, still in pairs, work orally through **workbook p.158 Ex 10** — the four route questions — saying the answers rather than writing them.

**Extension —** B answers without looking at the map at all; A holds it and checks. B must also give one wrong-turn warning: 别在第一个路口拐，那是去医院的路 — 要在第二个路口拐。

#### Activity 2: 同伴访问 + 报告 — You Do (10 min)
**Interview (6 min).** Each student has the six-row grid. Fill your own column first (90 seconds, in your head — no writing yet), then interview your partner and note their answers in short form. Mixed pairs, and the more fluent partner interviews first so their partner hears the questions modelled before having to ask them.

**Report back (4 min).** Everyone writes **two** sentences about their partner on a mini whiteboard, in the third person, then six or seven students are cold-called to read theirs aloud:
> 他家离学校不远，他每天骑自行车上学，大概要十五分钟。
> 她常去市中心购物，她或者坐地铁，或者坐公共汽车。

The class listens for 他/她 slipping back into 我 — that's the error to catch.

**Extension —** Report on your partner **and** compare them with yourself in the same breath, using 比: 他家比我家离学校近。我要坐车，可是他走路十分钟就到了。Then add one sentence of opinion with 我觉得. If they finish, they interview a second person and report which of the two has the easier journey and why.

#### Game: 猜猜我要去哪儿 — 20 Questions (7 min)
One student takes a place card and doesn't show it. The class works out where they're going, but **may only ask route questions** — 你走路去吗？要坐几站？路上过桥吗？那个地方在市中心吗？ — never 那是什么地方？ Twenty questions maximum. The student answering may only say 对 / 不对 / 我不知道.
- **Setup needed:** the twelve place cards printed and shuffled.
- **Harder version:** the student holding the card answers in full sentences rather than 对/不对 — 不对，我不走路去，我坐公共汽车 — which gives the class more to work with and forces the answerer to produce every time.

Where the remaining workbook exercises land: **p.158 Ex 11** (the 地铁/小巴/出租车 reading) goes to the pairs who finish Activity 1 early, and again in Lesson 6's review; **p.159 Ex 12** (add detail to a map and write a paragraph) is the written follow-through and is picked up as Lesson 4's Review. **Workbook p.157 Ex 7** — one sentence with each of the eight connectors — is the standing extension: any student who lands early today writes two of them.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student reads them; ask 哪一条最难？ and take two answers.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** spoken, at the door — each student says one sentence about their partner's journey, in the third person, before they leave.
- **Preview:** 下节课换话题 — 你的包里有什么？下下节课，那个包被偷了。 Next lesson: what's in your bag. The lesson after that, someone steals it.
