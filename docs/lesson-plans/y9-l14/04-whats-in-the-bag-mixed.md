# 包里有什么？ What's In The Bag

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Asking the Way 问路 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what is inside a bag, a wallet or a pocket, with the right measure words.

## Success Criteria
- I can name what I carry (手提包、钱包、钥匙、手机、现金)
- I can list three or more things in one sentence with ……里有……和……
- I can use 除了……以外，还有…… to add something extra to a list

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Exercise books
- A real handbag or backpack containing eight objects for the memory task: a wallet, keys, a phone (or a phone-shaped card), a few banknotes of play money, a book, a bottle of water, a pen, a pair of glasses
- A cloth or tea towel to cover the objects
- Printed A5 copies of the textbook p.138 Act 7 situation list, one per pair
- No audio this lesson

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lessons 1–3 — the bus questions, 或者, and journey vocabulary — plus a written catch-up on the map task that did not fit last lesson.
**How:** Three minutes on mini whiteboards, five questions, hold up after each:
1. 写 — I'll either cycle or take the bus.
2. 写 — How many stops?
3. 一 ___ 桥
4. 写 — It's about fifteen minutes on foot.
5. 从我家去机场，你要 ________ 。 (finish it however you like)
Then five minutes in exercise books on **workbook p.159 Ex 12** — add three more buildings to the map and write a short paragraph describing how to reach one of them. Collect these; they are the written evidence from Lesson 3's speaking lesson.
**Connection:** 你到了购物广场，买了东西。买东西要什么？钱。钱在哪儿？在包里。今天我们打开包看一看。You've arrived at the shopping centre. Now: what have you actually got with you?

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Note that criterion 3 is a reach back to Book 3 Unit 2 — 除了……以外 is not new, it is being reused, and it is on the unit's revision list.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 手提包 | shǒutíbāo | handbag | SVG — a handbag with a curved handle and a clasp |
| 1 | 钱包（錢） | qiánbāo | wallet; purse | SVG — a folded wallet with notes showing along the top |
| 2 | 钥匙（鑰） | yàoshi | key | SVG — two keys on a ring |
| 2 | 手机（機） | shǒujī | mobile phone | SVG — a phone, screen up, one button |
| 3 | 现金（現） | xiànjīn | cash | SVG — three banknotes fanned out, coins beside them |
| 3 | 服装（裝） | fúzhuāng | clothing | SVG — a shirt and a pair of trousers on hangers |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- 包 is doing the work in both words of cycle 1 — 手提包 is a bag you *carry in your hand*, 钱包 is the bag your *money* lives in. They already have 书包 and 面包; the first is a bag, the second is not, and that is worth a laugh.
- 钥匙 — the 匙 is neutral tone, 钥匙 yào**shi**. It is the one word in this set students consistently mispronounce with a fourth tone on the second syllable.
- 现金 — 金 is gold, and it is inside 钱 too, on the left as 钅. Money words carry metal.
- 服装 pairs with 服装店 — the clothing shop. This is the shop where the theft happens in Text 2 next lesson, so the word is being planted deliberately.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 妈妈的**手提包**很大。 — Mum's handbag is big.
2. 我的**钱包**在书包里。 — My wallet's in my school bag.
3. **钱包**在**手提包**里，**手提包**在车上。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我的钱包在 ________ 里。 · ________ 里有我的钱包。
**Extension —** Write where three different things of yours are, each in a different place, joined into one paragraph — then one sentence saying which one you'd be most upset to lose, and why.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我有两把**钥匙**。 — I've got two keys.
2. 我的**手机**是新的。 — My phone is new.
3. 我的**手机**和**钥匙**都在钱包旁边。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我的手机在 ________ ，钥匙在 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「一……就……」写一句：我一到家就把钥匙放在桌子上。Then say what happens on the days you don't.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我有一百块**现金**。 — I've got a hundred yuan in cash.
2. 我妈妈在**服装**店工作。 — My mum works in a clothes shop.
3. 我在**服装**店买衣服，付了两百块**现金**。 — both words.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我（有 / 没有）现金，我常常在 ________ 买 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「比」写一句：在服装店买衣服比在百货公司便宜。Then justify it with 因为……所以……。

**Recall slide (characters only):** 手提包 · 钱包 · 钥匙 · 手机 · 现金 · 服装

#### Key Sentence Pattern — listing what's inside

Structure: place + 里有 + item、item 和 item。 Measure words go in when the number matters.

1. 我的书包里有书、本子和文具盒。
2. 钱包里有两百多块**现金**。
3. **手提包**里有**钱包**、**钥匙**和**手机**。
4. 我的**手提包**里**除了钱包、钥匙和手机以外，还有**一本书和一瓶水。 *(a list plus an addition — the hardest one)*

Two things to model out loud: the Chinese list comma 、 between items with 和 only before the last one; and 两百**多**块 — 多 after a round number means "over", which is exactly how Text 2 puts it next lesson.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「钥匙」怎么读？全班一起说两次，注意第二个字的声调。
- 白板：写「我的钱包里有……」，写三样东西，用「、」和「和」。
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 我的手提包里有钱包和钥匙和手机。
- 举手：谁的书包里有手机？用一句中文说说还有什么。
- 「除了……以外」后面一定要有哪两个字？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 我的购物日 — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters + pictures, stays on screen throughout.
Four to six sentences in the exercise book, and **every one of the six words** has to appear: 今天我去服装店买衣服。我带了手提包……

**Share:** Two students read theirs aloud with the board still up; the class counts on fingers how many of the six they heard.

**Extension —** Write it as a *problem*: you get to the till and something is missing. Same six words, but now joined with 可是, 因为……所以…… and ……的时候, and ending with what you did about it. No reference to the board.

#### Activity 2: 包里有什么？— the chain dialogue — You Do (8 min)
Textbook p.138 Act 7, spoken, in pairs, moving through the six situations on the printed sheet: 汉语教室 / 学校 / 学校的图书馆 / 家的客厅 / 你的房间 / 家的厨房. The dialogue chains — 书包里有什么？ leads to 钱包里有什么？ leads to 文具盒里有什么？ Each answer must name at least three things.
Halfway through, take the sentence frame off the screen and let them run the last three situations unsupported.

**Extension —** The asker keeps a tally and, at the end, reports back to the class in the third person on what their partner carries and what that says about them: 他的书包里除了书以外，还有三个足球。我觉得他每天都踢球。Two sentences, delivered standing.

Written follow-through, last 2 minutes: **workbook p.160 Ex 15** — write the eight clothing and accessory words in Chinese, in the exercise book.

#### Game: Two Truths, One Lie — 包里有什么 (9 min)
Stage one, 4 minutes: the real bag comes out. Eight objects on a table, uncovered for thirty seconds, then covered. Everyone writes as many as they can in Chinese on a whiteboard. Uncover and score — one point per item correctly written in characters.
Stage two, 5 minutes: in groups of four, each student says **three sentences** about what's in their own bag — two true, one false. 我的包里有两把钥匙。我的钱包里有五十块现金。我有两个手机。 The group votes on the lie. One point for fooling the group, one point for spotting it.
- **Setup needed:** the bag and eight objects, plus the cloth, ready before the lesson.
- **Harder version:** the three sentences must each use a different structure — one with a measure word and a number, one with 除了……以外, one with 可是 — and the group may ask one follow-up question in Chinese before voting.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** choral read.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：用「除了……以外，还有……」说你的包里有什么。 One sentence, collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下节课，小青去服装店买东西，她的手提包不见了 — 被偷了。我们学「被」。 Next lesson: that handbag gets stolen, and we learn the grammar for saying so.
