# 坐几路车？ Text 1 — The Bus Route

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Asking the Way 问路 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask for and write a bus route — which bus, how many stops, and where to get off.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask the three bus questions (坐几路公共汽车？要坐几站？在哪站下车？)
- I can use 座 with buildings, temples and bridges (一座庙、一座桥)
- I can write a route that tells someone what they will pass on the way (看见一条河，过了大桥以后……)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Exercise books (this is a writing lesson — most work goes in the book, not on whiteboards)
- Printed A4 copies of the textbook p.132 Text 1, one per student, characters only with the four new words glossed in the margin
- Blank A4 route-map sheets, one per student (a plain grid of four roads across, four down)
- Cut-up Sentence Jumble strips: 6 sets, each set making one bus-route question or answer
- Audio: CD 53 (textbook p.132)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's six words and 或者……，或者……。
**How:** Correct the Teacher, then a writing burst.
Teacher writes four sentences on the board, three of them wrong:
1. 我还是骑自行车去，还是坐公共汽车去。
2. 车站就在教堂对面。
3. 我坐公共汽车25路上学。
4. 周末我或者去购物广场，或者在家看书。
Pairs have 90 seconds to find and fix the broken ones on whiteboards, then hands up.
Then three minutes in exercise books: write two sentences about how you get to school, one with 或者, one saying where your nearest 车站 is.
**Connection:** 你们已经会说「我或者骑车，或者坐车」。可是坐车呢 — 坐哪一路？坐多久？在哪儿下车？今天课文一就回答这三个问题。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Point at criterion 3 and say plainly: 光说「往前走」不够。别人在车上，看不见路口 — 你要告诉他看见什么。A passenger can't see intersections from a bus, so a bus route has to name landmarks instead.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 座 | zuò | measure word (buildings, bridges, temples, mountains) | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 1 | 庙（廟） | miào | temple | SVG — a temple front, upturned eaves, two red columns, three steps |
| 2 | 河 | hé | river | SVG — a winding river between two banks, a few reeds |
| 2 | 桥（橋） | qiáo | bridge | SVG — an arched stone bridge over water |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- 座 is for things that sit heavily on the ground and don't move — 一座庙、一座桥、一座山、一座大楼. Not for 车, not for 人.
- 河 and 桥 both carry 氵 (water). 桥 adds 木 — a bridge made of wood over water. The character says what it is.
- 庙 — the students have 广 from 广场 in Lesson 1; 庙 has the same 广 outside it, a building with an open front.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家附近有一**座**大楼。 — There's a tall building near my home.
2. 车站就在那**座庙**的对面。 — The stop is right opposite that temple.
3. 那**座庙**很老，我爷爷常常去。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家附近有一座 ________ 。 · ________ 就在那座庙的 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write two sentences that use 座 with two *different* nouns, then one sentence explaining why 一座自行车 is wrong.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我家后面有一条**河**。 — There's a river behind my house.
2. 过了大**桥**就到了。 — Once you're over the big bridge, you're there.
3. **河**上有一座大**桥**，过了**桥**你就看到广场了。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 过了 ________ 以后，你就看到 ________ 了。
**Extension —** 用「先……然后……」写两句，路上要经过一座桥和一条河。Then say roughly how long the trip takes: 大概二十分钟。

**Recall slide (characters only):** 座 · 庙 · 河 · 桥 · 骑 · 自行车 · 购物 · 广场 · 车站 · 路

#### Key Sentence Pattern — the three bus questions, and 过了……以后

| Question | Answer shape |
|---|---|
| 坐几路公共汽车？ | 坐25路或97路都可以。 |
| 要坐几站？ | 大概坐五六站。 |
| 在哪站下车？ | 在购物广场那站下车。 |

Sequencing on the way: 看见 + landmark，过了 + landmark + 以后，……

1. 你坐几路公共汽车？ — 我坐25路。
2. 要坐几站？ — 大概坐五六站。
3. 在哪站下车？ — 在广场那站下车。
4. 你**或者**坐25路，**或者**坐97路，都可以。看见一条河，过了大桥以后，下一站就下车。 *(Lesson 1's 或者 carrying the new sequencing — the hardest one)*

Worth flagging: 大概 and 五六站 — Chinese puts two numbers side by side to mean "about five or six". 三四个 and 十几分钟 work the same way.

#### Text 1 (p.132, CD 53)
Play CD 53 twice, books closed, then hand out the character-only sheet.

> 请问，去八方购物广场怎么走？— 你或者骑自行车去，或者坐公共汽车去。
> 坐几路公共汽车？— 坐25路或97路都可以。
> 车站在哪儿？— 就在前面那座庙的对面。
> 我要坐几站？— 大概坐五六站。
> 看见一条河，过了大桥以后，在购物广场那站下车。

Choral read, then pair read swapping roles once.

**Comprehension questions (answer in Chinese):**
1. 去购物广场，有几个办法？
2. 车站在哪儿？
3. 怎么知道快到了？

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「一座庙」还是「一个庙」？举手投票，然后说为什么。
- 白板：写 — How many stops do I need to take?
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 我坐公共汽车几路？
- 手势比一比：「过了大桥以后」— 你现在在桥的前面还是后面？
- 白板：课文里坐几路车？写数字。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 画路线，写路线 — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** 座 庙 河 桥 + Lesson 1's six, characters + pictures, stays on screen.
On the blank map sheet, each student draws a route from 我家 to 购物广场. The route **must pass** 一座庙, 一条河, 一座大桥 and 一个车站. Then four to six sentences underneath, in the exercise book, describing it — every word on the summary board has to appear.

**Share:** Two students read their route aloud while a third follows it on the board map with a marker. If the marker ends up somewhere else, the route wasn't specific enough.

**Extension —** Write the route twice: once for someone walking (using Lesson 13's 路口 / 拐 / 红绿灯), once for someone on the bus (using 几路 / 几站 / 下车). Then one sentence on which is faster and why, with 比. Anyone still ahead moves to **workbook p.156 Ex 8** — the paragraph describing the park picture — which wants the same descriptive writing at greater length.

#### Activity 2: Text 1 in writing — You Do (9 min)
Three written tasks in the exercise book, in order. Circulate and mark on the move.
1. **Answer in full sentences** (4 min): the three comprehension questions above, plus 你觉得骑自行车去好还是坐车去好？为什么？
2. **Workbook p.154 Ex 1** (3 min): write the ten geography and map words in Chinese.
3. **Measure-word check** (2 min): eight nouns on the board — 庙, 桥, 河, 自行车, 山, 大楼, 公共汽车, 手 — write the right measure word in front of each. Only three of them take 座.
4. **Workbook p.155 Ex 5** (2 min): translate the sentences into Chinese. Say out loud that this is a test format — Part 3 of the unit test is translation both ways — so it gets done properly, not at speed.

**Extension —** Rewrite the whole of Text 1 as a **narrative** rather than a dialogue, in the third person: 小明问路，那个人告诉他……。It has to keep every piece of route information and read as one connected paragraph.

#### Game: Writing Relay — bus-route edition (8 min)
Teams of four, one whiteboard per team at the front. Teacher reads an English prompt (*Which bus should I take?* / *Get off at the shopping-centre stop.* / *The stop is opposite that temple.* / *Once you're over the big bridge, you're there.*). Student 1 runs up and writes the first two or three characters, runs back, hands over the marker; student 2 continues. No talking, no correcting from the seats. First team with a complete correct sentence scores; a sentence with a wrong character scores nothing.
- **Setup needed:** three or four mini whiteboards at the front, one marker each.
- **Harder version:** fluent speakers write the sentence **and** must then add a second clause of their own to it before the point counts — 在广场那站下车，然后过马路，服装店就在你的右手边。

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** choral read from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs, one criterion at a time. Criterion 2 (座) is the one to check honestly — measure words slide fast.
- **Exit ticket:** 写两个问题 — 一个问几路车，一个问在哪站下车。 Two questions, one slip, collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下节课不学新词 — 我们用嘴巴，问同学：从你家去机场怎么走？要多长时间？ Next lesson: no new words, all speaking — interviewing each other about real journeys from home.
