# 骑车还是坐车？ Getting Around

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Asking the Way 问路 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say there is more than one way to get somewhere, using 或者……，或者……。

## Success Criteria
- I can name how I travel (骑自行车 / 坐公共汽车 / 走路 / 坐地铁)
- I can offer two options in one sentence with 或者……，或者……
- I can say where a stop is, using the position words from Lesson 13 (车站就在……对面)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Printed A5 copies of the textbook p.133 vehicle pictures (14 items), one per pair
- Card set for Slap the Board: 自行车 公共汽车 地铁 火车 出租车 飞机 船 电车 小巴 车站 地铁站 火车站 机场 码头 公路 马路 (A4, large characters)
- Blu-tack
- No audio this lesson

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 13's six direction phrases and 看到……就……, plus the facilities vocabulary (邮局 银行 诊所 百货公司 市政大楼 教堂 公园 咖啡馆).
**How:** Two rounds on mini whiteboards, 90 seconds each.
Round 1 — teacher says a facility in English, students write the characters. Six items.
Round 2 — teacher gives a route in Chinese (一直往前走，看到红绿灯往左拐), students draw the arrow diagram. Four items.
Then thirty seconds of pair talk: 从学校去邮局怎么走？
**Connection:** 上个星期我们学会了走路怎么走。可是有的地方太远了，走不到。今天我们说：不走路的时候，怎么去？ Last week every route was on foot. Today: what happens when the place is too far to walk?

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible for the whole lesson. Read criterion 2 aloud twice and point out that 或者 comes **twice** in the sentence, once before each option — that is the shape students are aiming for by the plenary.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 骑（騎） | qí | ride | SVG — a figure seated on a bike, legs on the pedals |
| 1 | 自行车（車） | zìxíngchē | bicycle | SVG — a bicycle side-on, two wheels, frame, handlebars |
| 2 | 购物（購） | gòuwù | shopping | SVG — two shopping bags with handles |
| 2 | 广场（廣場） | guǎngchǎng | square | SVG — an open paved plaza, fountain in the middle, two benches |
| 3 | 车站（車） | chēzhàn | station; stop | SVG — a bus-stop sign on a post beside a bench |
| 3 | 路 | lù | route (bus number) | SVG — the front of a bus with 25 路 on the destination board |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes worth making:
- 骑 takes the 马 radical — you ride a bike the way you ride a horse, astride it. 坐 is for anything you sit *inside*: 坐车, 坐飞机, 坐船.
- 广场 — 广 is *wide*, 场 is a *ground / open area*. They already know 场 from 停车场 and 足球场.
- 路 has two jobs now. 马路 and 路口 are the *road*; 25路 is the *route number*. Same character, and the second use is the one Lesson 2 needs.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我会**骑**车。 — I can ride a bike.
2. 我的**自行车**很新。 — My bicycle is new.
3. 我不会**骑**车，可是我姐姐会**骑自行车**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我（会 / 不会）骑 ________ 。 · 我的自行车 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「因为……所以……」写一句：因为我家离学校很近，所以我每天骑自行车上学。Then add what you do when it rains.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 妈妈周末去**购物**。 — Mum goes shopping at the weekend.
2. 我家附近有一个大**广场**。 — There's a big square near my home.
3. 我们去**购物广场**买衣服。 — both words together.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我（常常 / 不常）去 ________ 购物。
**Extension —** 用「除了……以外」写一句：除了衣服以外，我还在购物广场买鞋。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. **车站**就在教堂对面。 — The stop is right opposite the church.
2. 我坐25**路**公共汽车上学。 — I take the number 25 bus to school.
3. 我在**车站**等25**路**车，等了十分钟。 — both words.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我坐 ________ 路公共汽车 ________ 。 · 车站就在 ________ 。
**Extension —** Say where your nearest stop is using two position words in one sentence: 车站在银行和邮局中间，就在诊所对面。

**Recall slide (characters only):** 骑 · 自行车 · 购物 · 广场 · 车站 · 路

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 或者……，或者……

Structure: 或者 + option A ，或者 + option B 。 Both halves are full clauses.

1. 你**或者**骑自行车去，**或者**坐公共汽车去。
2. 周末我**或者**在家看书，**或者**去公园跑步。
3. 去购物广场，你**或者**坐25路，**或者**坐97路。
4. 如果下雨，我**或者**坐地铁去，**或者**请爸爸开车送我；如果天气好，我一定骑自行车去。 *(two clauses plus a condition — the hardest one)*

🔴 **The one thing to say out loud:** 或者 is for **statements**. 还是 is for **questions**. 你骑自行车**还是**坐车？ (question) → 我**或者**骑自行车，**或者**坐车。 (answer) The textbook's Act 2 is built entirely on this split, and it's the error students will make all lesson.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「自行车」怎么读？全班一起说两次。
- 白板：写一句用「或者……，或者……」，说你周末做什么。举起来。
- 这句话哪儿错了？ 我还是骑自行车去，还是坐公共汽车去。
- 举手：「广场」的第一个字是什么意思？
- 手势：我说一个交通工具 — 要坐**进去**的，两只手比一个框；要**骑**的，两只手比车把。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 我的一天，怎么去？ — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters + pictures, stays on screen throughout.
Every student writes one day's travel plan — three trips, three different ways of getting there — and it must use **all six** words. Three or four sentences.
> 早上我骑自行车去学校。下午我跟朋友去购物广场，车站就在广场旁边，我们坐25路车回家。

**Share:** Two students read theirs aloud while the summary board is still up; the class listens for which of the six words they missed.

**Extension —** Write it without looking at the board, and join every sentence to the next with a connector — 先……然后……, 因为……所以……, ……的时候. Then add one sentence saying which trip takes longest and why.

#### Activity 2: 或者 — from the page to your partner — We Do → You Do (9 min)
**First 5 minutes, written (textbook p.134 Act 2):** complete the six 或者 sentences, then **It is your turn!** — write two of your own. Circulate; the sentences to stop at are the ones that have slipped into 还是. Anyone who finishes goes straight on to **workbook p.154 Ex 3** — six more of the same — and then **p.155 Ex 4**, the cloze with 或者 离 还是 方便 不远 旁边 走路 中间, which is the one place 或者 and 还是 sit in the same exercise.
**Last 4 minutes, spoken:** chain round the room. Student 1 asks 你怎么去学校？ Student 2 answers with 或者……，或者……, then turns and asks the next person a *different* question — 你周末做什么？你去哪儿购物？ No repeats; if the chain breaks, it restarts from that student.

**Extension —** In the chain, you must answer with 或者……，或者…… **and** then say which of your two options you actually prefer and why: 我或者骑自行车去，或者坐公共汽车去。我比较喜欢骑自行车，因为骑车比坐车快，也不用等。Take the 比 comparison from Lesson 10.

#### Game: Slap the Board — transport edition (8 min)
Sixteen cards on the board (see Materials). Two students up at a time, backs to the board until "开始". Teacher says the word in **English**; first hand on the right card scores for their team. Rotate every two rounds so everyone comes up once.
- **Setup needed:** the sixteen cards printed and blu-tacked before the lesson.
- **Harder version:** for fluent speakers, don't say the English — say a definition in Chinese (你在这儿等公共汽车 → 车站；船在这儿停 → 码头). They may also be asked to use the word in a sentence after slapping it, or the point doesn't count.

This also covers textbook p.134 Act 3 — the nine transport-place words (公路 小巴站 码头 马路 电车站 地铁站 火车站 机场 公共汽车站) are all in the card set, so hand out the p.134 matching sheet in the last minute for pairs to check off what came up.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student volunteer reads all three aloud from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time. Watch criterion 2 — if the room is mostly 😐, 或者 needs re-modelling at the top of Lesson 2.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：用「或者……，或者……」说你怎么去购物广场。 One sentence on a slip, collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们看课文一 — 坐几路车？坐几站？在哪站下车？ Next lesson we read Text 1 and learn to ask which bus, how many stops, and where to get off.
