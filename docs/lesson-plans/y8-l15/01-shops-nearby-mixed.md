# Shops Near My Home 我家附近有花店

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the shops near our home and list them in one sentence.

## Success Criteria
- I can name four shops in Chinese (花店、文具店、家具店、附近的商店)
- I can say what is near my home using 我家附近有…
- I can list several things in one sentence using 、 and 等

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.142–143 — Text 1 and p.143 Ex.1 displayed
- Printed "我家附近" street strips: A4 landscape, five empty shopfronts in a row with a ruled line under each, one per student
- Printed Slap the Character cards: 附近 · 花店 · 文具店 · 家具店 · 饭店 plus five L14 furniture words as distractors — two sets, A3
- Workbook pp.164–165 (copy the new words of Text 1)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 14's furniture and appliances — 沙发、茶几、衣柜、书桌、椅子、书架、冰箱、洗衣机 — and the two question forms that carried them: 你的房间里有什么？ and the V-not-V 你有没有…？

**How:** Turn and talk, 90 seconds: partner A asks 你的房间里有什么？ partner B answers with at least three items and a measure word. Swap. Then four items on mini whiteboards, English → Chinese: *wardrobe · bookshelf · washing machine · a desk and a chair*.

Write one word on the board and leave it there: **家具**.

**Connection:** "你们家有家具 — sofas, wardrobes, desks. Where did they come from? Someone bought them at a 家具店. Today we step outside the house and name the shops around it."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on the projector and leave up all lesson. Read the three criteria aloud. Point at 家具 still on the board, then add 店 next to it: "One character you know plus one new one. Four of today's words work exactly like that."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 附近 | fùjìn | nearby | Line drawing: a house with a dotted circle around it, three small shops inside the circle |
| 1 | 花店 | huādiàn | flower shop | Line drawing: shopfront with an awning, buckets of flowers on the pavement |
| 2 | 文具店 | wénjùdiàn | stationery shop | Line drawing: shopfront, window showing pens, a ruler and notebooks |
| 2 | 家具店（家具店） | jiājùdiàn | furniture shop | Line drawing: shopfront, window showing a sofa and a wardrobe |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家**附近**有一个学校。 — There is a school near my home.
2. **花店**里有很多花。 — There are lots of flowers in the flower shop.
3. 我家**附近**有一个**花店**。 — There is a flower shop near my home.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家**附近**有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two places, one sentence, joined with 和, then say which one you go to more often: 我家附近有一个学校和一个花店，我常常去花店。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我常常去**文具店**。 — I often go to the stationery shop.
2. **家具店**里有沙发和书桌。 — There are sofas and desks in the furniture shop.
3. 我家附近有**文具店**和**家具店**。 — There's a stationery shop and a furniture shop near my home.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我在 ________ 买 ________ 。
**Extension —** Say what you buy at two different shops in one sentence: 我在文具店买笔，在家具店买书桌。

*(Two cycles, so no separate recall slide — the summary board below does that job.)*

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**X 附近有 A、B、C 等。** — "Near X there are A, B, C and so on."

Note for the class: 等 is the 等 they already met in 等等 from Unit 4. The 、 between items is the Chinese list comma — it is not the same mark as 。

1. 我家附近有花店。
2. 我家附近有花店和文具店。
3. 我家附近有**饭店**、文具店、家具店、花店等。 *(= Text 1, p.142. 饭店 is decodable — 饭 you know, 店 is in all four of today's shops. Gloss it on the board as recognition only, not a word to write.)*
4. 学校附近有文具店和家具店等，我常常去那儿买东西。 ← the hardest one: two clauses, and 那儿 reaching back to Year 7.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Thumbs up if 附近 means *nearby*." (Then: "Thumbs down if it means *far*." — catch the students copying their neighbour.)
- "How do you say *flower shop*? Write it on your whiteboard." Hold up, scan, correct.
- "What's wrong with this sentence? 我家花店附近有。" (word order — 附近 belongs with 我家: 我家附近有花店。)
- "I say a shop, you tell me one thing you buy there — in Chinese. 家具店。文具店。花店。"

#### Text 1 (p.142, CD track 71) — first two lines only
> 我家附近有饭店、文具店、家具店、花店等。超市离我家也不远，走路五分钟就到了。

Read line 1 aloud, class repeats. Read line 2 aloud **without explaining it** — "two new words and a new pattern are hiding in there, and they're next lesson's job."

Comprehension questions on line 1: 他家附近有几个店？ 他家附近有没有家具店？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Build my street — We Do (7 min)
Summary board stays on screen: all four words, characters and pictures only, no pinyin, no English.

Hand out the street strips. Every student labels the five shopfronts — 花店、文具店、家具店、饭店 and one more of their choosing — then writes one sentence underneath that names **all of them**: 我家附近有花店、文具店、家具店、饭店等。

Three minutes to draw and write, then two students read theirs out while the summary board is still up.

**Extension —** Write it as two sentences that hang together, the second one saying which shop you go to most and why, using 因为…所以…: 我家附近有花店、文具店、家具店和饭店等。因为我很喜欢吃快餐，所以我常常去饭店。 Then close your book and say the whole thing again with nothing on the desk.

#### Activity 2: Neighbourhood survey — You Do (9 min)
Students stand and find four different partners. To each they ask **你家附近有什么？** and note the answer in Chinese characters on their street strip's back. After four partners, sit down; three students report on someone else: 小明家附近有超市和书店。

The reporting turn is the point of the activity — it forces third-person and it forces them to have actually written down what they heard.

**Extension —** The fluent students take the harder role: they ask **你家附近有没有文具店？没有的话，你去哪儿买笔？** — a V-not-V question from Lesson 14 plus a follow-up that their partner has to answer in a full sentence. They then report two classmates back to the class in one connected turn, comparing: 小明家附近有文具店，可是小红家附近没有，她要坐公共汽车去买笔。

#### Game: Slap the Character (7 min)
Two students at the board, one set of A3 cards spread out (今天的四个词 plus 饭店 plus five furniture distractors from Lesson 14). Teacher says the English; first to slap the right card wins the point and stays on. Run through the class in pairs.

- **Setup needed:** Two printed A3 card sets, cut. Board or two desks pushed together.
- **Harder version:** Teacher stops saying the English. Instead the teacher says a *sentence* with the word missing — 我在 ______ 买沙发 — and the players slap the shop that fits. For the strongest pair, the teacher says nothing at all: the previous winner composes the clue sentence in Chinese themselves.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student volunteer reads the three criteria aloud from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍/😐/👎 against each criterion in turn. Note who puts 👎 on criterion 3 (the 等 list) — that one comes back next lesson.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, write one sentence: 我家附近有 ________ 。 At least two shops and 等. *(Standing extension for anyone finished early: write it as a short paragraph of three sentences instead.)*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: the supermarket. And the word that tells you how far away it is — 离."
