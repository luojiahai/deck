# Right Across the Road 就在马路对面

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say exactly where a place is, and to stress that it is close by.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 马路、对面、火车站 in Chinese
- I can use 就 to mean *right there* (就在马路对面)
- I can say how quickly I get somewhere using 走路…分钟就到了

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.145, 152 — p.145 Ex.5 and p.152 Ex.14 displayed
- Printed A4 town map, one per pair: a crossroads with 超市 · 花店 · 文具店 · 家具店 · 饭店 · 火车站 · 学校 · 电影院 placed around it, each labelled in characters
- Printed Sentence Jumble strips: six 就 sentences cut into word cards, one envelope per group of four
- Workbook pp.166–167 (就 practice, map description)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 离 + 远/近 and 挺…的 from last lesson, plus the four shops from Lesson 1.

**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards, five items, English → Chinese:
1. The supermarket isn't far from my home.
2. School is quite far from my home.
3. Is the flower shop far from here?
4. near / nearby (附近)
5. quite close (挺近的)

Hold up after each. On item 1, ask two students to read theirs aloud so the class hears 离 in the middle of the sentence rather than at the front.

**Connection:** "我家离超市不远 — you can say that now. But 'not far' is vague. Today you learn to say *exactly* where it is, and to say it's **right** there."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Read aloud. Then write the Text 1 line from Lesson 2 on the board — 走路五分钟就到了 — and circle 就: "This is the character we skipped. It's small, it has no English translation of its own, and it changes the whole feeling of the sentence."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 马路（馬路） | mǎlù | road; street | Line drawing: a two-lane road seen from above, dashed centre line, a shop on each side |
| 1 | 对面（對面） | duìmiàn | opposite | Diagram: the same road, an arrow from the left shop across to the right shop |
| 2 | 火车站（火車站） | huǒchēzhàn | train station | Line drawing: a station building with a platform sign and a train alongside |
| 2 | 路 | lù | route; (bus) number | Diagram: the front of a bus with 5 路 on its destination board |

Note on slide A of cycle 2: the 路 in 五路公共汽车 is the same 路 they just met in 马路 — the character for a route.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家附近有一条大**马路**。 — There's a big road near my home.
2. 花店在超市**对面**。 — The flower shop is opposite the supermarket.
3. **马路对面**有一个花店。 — There's a flower shop across the road.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 在 ________ 对面。
**Extension —** Place three shops relative to each other in one connected pair of sentences, using 对面 and one of 左面／右面 from Lesson 13: 花店在超市对面，文具店在花店右面。我常常先去超市，再去文具店。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我家离**火车站**不远。 — My home isn't far from the train station.
2. 我坐五**路**公共汽车上学。 — I take the number 5 bus to school.
3. **火车站**在马路对面，我坐十**路**公共汽车去。 — The station is across the road; I take the number 10 bus there.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我坐 ________ 路公共汽车去 ________ 。
**Extension —** Say how you would get to the train station from school, with the bus number *and* the walking time, in one sentence: 我坐十路公共汽车去火车站，十五分钟就到了。 Then say what you'd do if the bus didn't come.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**就** — *right; just*. It goes **before the verb**, never after it.

1. 超市就在我家附近。 — The supermarket is *right* near my home.
2. 火车站就在马路对面。 — The station is *just* across the road.
3. 走路五分钟就到了。 — Five minutes on foot and you're *there*.
4. 我家离花店挺近的，走路两分钟就到了，家具店就在花店对面。 ← three clauses, 离 from last lesson, 就 twice, 对面 from today.

**The error to pre-empt, on the board in red:**
- ✗ 走路五分钟到了就。 → 就 comes before the verb: 走路五分钟**就**到了。
- ✗ 火车站在就马路对面。 → 就在, not 在就.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Where does 就 go — before the verb or after it? Point with your finger at the board." (Everyone points; look for the hands pointing at the wrong half.)
- "什么意思？ 书店就在学校对面。" Ask for the English, then ask *why* you'd say 就 instead of leaving it out.
- "Whiteboards: *The furniture shop is right across the road.*" (家具店就在马路对面。)
- "Correct me: 我家离超市很近，走路三分钟到了就。"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Map talk — We Do (7 min)
Summary board up: 马路 · 对面 · 火车站 · 路, characters and pictures only. The town map goes on the projector and one copy per pair.

Pairs take turns: A asks 超市在哪儿？ B answers with 就 and a position word — 就在马路对面。／就在花店右面。 Eight places on the map, so four turns each. Every answer must contain 就.

Then whole class: teacher points at a place, class answers chorally.

**Extension —** B answers without looking at the map, from memory, after thirty seconds of studying it — and A's questions switch to 从学校怎么去火车站？ so B has to produce a route, not a location. Nothing on the desk.

#### Activity 2: The 就 sentences — You Do (9 min)
**p.145 Ex.5, 5 min.** Translate the six 就 sentences into English in books, then write three of their own about real places near the school. Teacher circulates; collect one good one from three different students to put on the board.

**p.152 Ex.14, 4 min.** Describe the textbook map using 前面、后面、左面、右面、对面 — four sentences written, at least two with 就.

**Extension —** Skip the translation and write the reverse: a six-line description of the route from the school gate to the train station that a Year 7 student could follow, using 就 three times, 对面 once and 离 once. Then hand it to a partner, who has to draw the route from the description alone.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (7 min)
Groups of four. Each envelope holds six sentences cut into word cards, all of them built on 就 and today's vocabulary — 火车站 / 就 / 在 / 马路 / 对面, 走路 / 五 / 分钟 / 就 / 到了, and four more. First group to lay out all six correctly wins.

- **Setup needed:** Six sentences printed at 48pt, cut into word cards, one envelope per group. Reusable.
- **Harder version:** The group gets the cards for four sentences only, but there are three extra distractor cards in the envelope (一个 / 很 / 了) that belong in none of them. They must lay out the four sentences *and* say aloud, in Chinese, why each leftover card doesn't fit.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads them from the screen; class gives a Chinese example for each one.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic light on whiteboards against each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip: 写一句用「就」的句子 — one sentence using 就, about a real place. *(Early finishers: write two, one with 就在 and one with 就到了.)*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson is a games lesson — and you'll need it, because you're going to build journeys with two legs: 先坐船，然后坐公共汽车。"
