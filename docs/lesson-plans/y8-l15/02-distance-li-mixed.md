# Far or Near 超市离我家不远

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say how far one place is from another.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 超市 and 远 in Chinese
- I can say how far one place is from another using A 离 B 远／近
- I can soften a judgement with 挺…的 (挺远的 = quite far)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.142, 144, 147 — Text 1, p.144 Ex.2, p.147 Ex.8 displayed
- Audio: CD track 71 (Text 1), CD track 73 (p.147 Ex.8)
- Printed "distance ladder" sheets: a vertical line labelled 很近 at the top and 很远 at the bottom, six blank rungs, one per student
- Workbook pp.165–166 (离 sentence building)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Yesterday's four shops — 附近、花店、文具店、家具店 — and the list pattern X 附近有 A、B、C 等。

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the four characters-only cards (30 seconds), then Correct the Teacher. Three sentences on the board, all wrong:

- ✗ 我家有附近花店。 → 我家附近有花店。
- ✗ 我家附近有花店、文具店、家具店。等 → …家具店等。
- ✗ 我买笔在文具店。 → 我在文具店买笔。

Students find the error in pairs, one pair fixes each on the board.

**Connection:** "You can now say what's near you. What you can't say yet is *how* near. That's today — one character does the whole job: 离."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Read them aloud, then draw two dots on the board with a line between them and write 离 on the line: "这个字的意思是 *the distance from*. It sits between two places. It is not a verb — it always needs 远 or 近 after it."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 超市 | chāoshì | supermarket | Line drawing: shopfront with a trolley outside and a sliding door |
| 1 | 远（遠） | yuǎn | far | Diagram: two houses with a long dotted line between them, 远 above it |
| 2 | 离（離） | lí | distance away from | Diagram: the same two houses, 离 written on the line, 远/近 at the end |
| 2 | 挺 | tǐng | rather; quite | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家附近有一个**超市**。 — There's a supermarket near my home.
2. 花店不**远**，走路五分钟。 — The flower shop isn't far, five minutes on foot.
3. **超市**不**远**，我常常走路去。 — The supermarket isn't far, I often walk there.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 不远，走路 ________ 分钟。
**Extension —** Two places in one sentence, one near and one far, joined with 可是: 超市不远，走路五分钟，可是家具店很远，我要坐公共汽车去。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 超市**离**我家不远。 — The supermarket isn't far from my home.
2. 我家**挺**大的。 — Our house is quite big.
3. 学校**离**我家**挺**近的。 — School is quite close to my home.

Point out 近 on slide B: "You already own this character — it's the 近 in 附近. On its own it means *near*."

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 离我家 ________ 。
**Extension —** Use 挺…的 twice with opposite meanings in one connected pair of sentences, and add the reason: 超市离我家挺近的，所以我天天去。机场离我家挺远的，我一年去一次。

*(Two cycles, so the summary board below is the recall check.)*

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**place 1 + 离 + place 2 + 远／近** — and its question form **…离…远吗？**

1. 超市离我家很近。
2. 我家离学校不远。
3. 花店离我家挺近的。
4. 我家离学校挺远的，可是超市离我家很近，走路五分钟。 ← two clauses, both structures, a contrast.

Question form, modelled twice: 你家离学校远吗？ → 挺远的。／不远。

**The error to pre-empt, on the board in red:**
- ✗ 我家离学校。 → 离 is not a verb. It needs 远 or 近: 我家离学校很远。
- ✗ 我离家学校很远。 → the two places stay whole: 我家 / 离 / 学校.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Give me a sentence with 离. Whiteboards, 20 seconds. Hold up." Scan for the missing 远/近 — that's the one to catch now.
- "挺远的 — is that further or nearer than 很远? " (Slightly softer — *quite* far, not *very* far.)
- "Translate: *The stationery shop isn't far from school.*" (文具店离学校不远。)
- Thumbs: "我家离超市挺近的。 Is this correct?" 👍 "我家离超市。" 👎

#### Text 1 (p.142, CD track 71) — complete
> 我家附近有饭店、文具店、家具店、花店等。超市离我家也不远，走路五分钟就到了。我家离学校也挺近的。我每天走路上学。

Play CD 71. Read together chorally. Three comprehension questions in Chinese: 超市离他家远吗？ 他家离学校远不远？ 他每天怎么上学？

Say out loud that 就 in line 2 is being left alone until next lesson — students should read past it, not ask about it yet.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Distance ladder — We Do (7 min)
Summary board up: 超市 · 远 · 离 · 挺, characters and pictures only.

Each student gets a ladder sheet (很近 at the top, 很远 at the bottom) and writes six **real** places into the rungs, ordered by how far they are from the school gate — 超市、花店、文具店、家具店、火车站 and their own home. Then, under the ladder, they write three sentences that between them use all four new words:

超市离学校很近。 / 家具店离学校挺远的。 / 我家离学校不远。

Three minutes working, then two students read their top rung and bottom rung aloud.

**Extension —** Rank all six and then justify two of the placements out loud, unprepared, using 因为…所以…: 因为家具店在马路那边，所以它离学校挺远的。 Then compare two of them in one sentence: 超市离学校比家具店近。

#### Activity 2: Listening then writing — You Do (9 min)
**First, 4 min — p.147 Ex.8 (CD track 73).** Three short passages about a 家具店, a 饭店 and a 公园 near the speaker's home. Play twice. Students write, for each one, what the place is and one thing mentioned about it. Take answers chorally.

**Then, 5 min — p.144 Ex.2.** Complete the seven sentences with 离 + 远/近 from the bracketed prompts, in books. Then write three more of their own about places they actually know. Teacher circulates; the sentence to hunt for is the one with 离 and no complement.

**Extension —** Instead of three sentences, write a five-sentence paragraph about the walk from home to school that uses 离 twice, 挺…的 once, and names three of the shops from last lesson — no word bank, nothing on the desk. Then read it aloud without looking down.

#### Game: Distance Bingo (7 min)
Students draw a 3×3 grid and fill it with any nine places from the two lessons (超市、花店、文具店、家具店、饭店、学校、火车站、我家、公园). Teacher reads out sentences — 这个地方离学校很远，要坐火车去 — and students cross off the place they think it is. Three in a row wins.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing printed. Students draw their own grids.
- **Harder version:** The winner takes over as caller and must invent the clue sentences in Chinese on the spot, using 离 and 挺…的 each at least once. Everyone else keeps playing on the same grid.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read of the three criteria from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards — students draw 👍/😐/👎 for each criterion and hold up. Criterion 2 is the one to watch; if a quarter of the room is 😐, the 离 drill opens next lesson too.
- **Exit ticket:** Translate onto a slip: *"The supermarket isn't far from my home — five minutes on foot."* (超市离我家不远，走路五分钟。) *(Early finishers: add a second sentence using 挺…的.)*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we finally deal with 就 — the little word in 走路五分钟就到了 — and how to say something is *right across the road*."
