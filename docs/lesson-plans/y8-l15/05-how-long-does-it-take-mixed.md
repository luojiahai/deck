# How Long Does It Take? 要坐多长时间？

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask and answer how long a journey takes.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 要坐多长时间？ and answer with a length of time
- I can give an approximate answer using 大约
- I can say 飞机 and 机场 in Chinese

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.148, 152, 153 — Text 2, p.152 Ex.13, p.153 Ex.15 displayed
- Audio: CD track 74 (Text 2), CD track 75 (p.153 Ex.15)
- Printed journey-time charts: four columns — 去哪儿 / 怎么去 / 多长时间 / 远不远 — six blank rows, one per student
- Workbook pp.168–169 (new words Text 2, reading comprehension)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 先…，然后… and the two words that came with it — 电影院、船 — plus 从 A 怎么去 B？

**How:** Pair dialogue from memory, 2 minutes. Partner A asks 从你家怎么去电影院？ and 从学校怎么去火车站？; B answers both with two legs. Swap. Then three students perform theirs for the class with nothing on the desk.

Then one item on whiteboards: *First I take the boat, then the number 5 bus.* (我先坐船，然后坐五路公共汽车。)

**Connection:** "You can now say *how* you get there. Every single one of you is about to be asked the obvious next question — 要坐多长时间？ — and today you can answer it."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Read aloud, then write 多长时间 on the board and break it apart: 多 (how) + 长 (long) + 时间 (time). "You already know 多 and 长. Only the middle word is new."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 时间（時間） | shíjiān | time | Line drawing: a clock face with an arc arrow sweeping from 12 to 4, showing a duration not a moment |
| 1 | 大约（大約） | dàyuē | about; approximately | Diagram: 20 分钟 with a wavy ≈ sign in front of it |
| 2 | 飞机（飛機） | fēijī | plane | Line drawing: passenger aircraft, side view |
| 2 | (飞)机场（機場） | (fēi)jīchǎng | airport | Line drawing: terminal building with a control tower and a plane on the apron |

Point out on cycle 2 slide A: 机 is the same 机 as in 洗衣机 and 冷气机 from Lesson 14 — the character for a machine.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 你要坐多长**时间**？ — How long do you have to travel?
2. **大约**二十分钟。 — About twenty minutes.
3. 从我家到学校要多长**时间**？ — **大约**半个小时。 — How long from my home to school? About half an hour.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 从我家到 ________ 要大约 ________ 。
**Extension —** Answer for two different journeys in one connected turn and compare them: 从我家到超市大约五分钟，可是到火车站要大约半个小时，所以我常常走路去超市，坐公共汽车去火车站。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我坐**飞机**去中国。 — I go to China by plane.
2. 我家离**机场**很远。 — My home is far from the airport.
3. 我家离**机场**挺远的，要先坐火车，然后坐**飞机**。 — The airport is quite far; first the train, then the plane.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家离机场 ________ ，要坐 ________ 去。
**Extension —** Describe a real journey to the airport with three legs, the times for each, and why you'd leave when you do: 我家离机场挺远的。我先坐十路公共汽车，大约十五分钟，然后坐火车，大约一个小时，所以我要很早出门。

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**要坐多长时间？ → 大约 + 时间。** And the 挺…的 hedge from Lesson 2 comes back for the "far?" question.

1. 你家离火车站远吗？ — 不远，就在马路对面。
2. 要坐多长时间？ — 大约二十分钟。
3. 你家离机场远吗？ — 挺远的，要坐火车去。 *(= Text 2, p.148)*
4. 从你家怎么去机场？要坐多长时间？ — 我先坐火车，然后坐公共汽车，大约一个小时。 ← two questions answered in one turn, two legs, an approximate time.

**The error to pre-empt, on the board in red:**
- ✗ 你要多长时间坐？ → 多长时间 comes after the verb: 你要坐**多长时间**？
- ✗ 大约二十分钟的时间要坐。 → 大约 sits in front of the amount: **大约**二十分钟。

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "多长时间 — is that asking *when* or *how long*? Hands up for how long."
- "Whiteboards: *About forty minutes.*" (大约四十分钟。)
- "Ask me how long it takes me to get to school. In Chinese." Cold call two students; the second must ask it differently.
- "Correct me: 我要多长时间坐去机场？"

#### Text 2 (p.148, CD track 74) — complete
> A: 你们家附近有电影院吗？ B: 没有。
> A: 从你们家怎么去电影院？ B: 我先坐船，然后再坐五路公共汽车。
> A: 要坐多长时间？ B: 大约二十分钟。
> A: 你家离火车站远吗？ B: 不远，就在马路对面。
> A: 你家离(飞)机场远吗？ B: 挺远的，要坐火车去。

Play CD 74 in full. Choral read, then pairs read twice swapping roles. This dialogue is the model for the whole unit — say so. Comprehension in Chinese: 他家离火车站远不远？ 去电影院要多长时间？ 他家离机场远吗？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: My journey-time chart — We Do (7 min)
Summary board up: 时间 · 大约 · 飞机 · 机场, characters and pictures only.

Each student fills the four-column chart for six real destinations — 学校、超市、火车站、电影院、机场 and one of their own. Columns: 去哪儿 / 怎么去 / 多长时间 / 远不远. Everything written in characters; every time entry must start with 大约.

Three minutes filling, then pairs interview each other from their charts using 从你家怎么去…？要坐多长时间？ Two students report a partner's longest journey to the class.

**Extension —** Fill the chart, then close it and give the whole thing as a spoken sixty-second report with nothing in front of you, ordering the destinations from nearest to furthest and joining them: 超市离我家最近，走路五分钟就到了。机场最远，要先坐火车，然后…

#### Activity 2: Listening, then the question set — You Do (9 min)
**First, 4 min — p.153 Ex.15 (CD track 75).** Two sets of A/B/C about the journey to school and to the supermarket. Play twice. Students write the transport and the time for each. Take answers chorally, then play once more so they hear 挺远的 and 大约十五分钟 in the stream.

**Then, 5 min — p.152 Ex.13.** Complete the eight questions using 多长时间, in books. Teacher circulates; the error to hunt for is 多长时间 landing in front of the verb.

**Extension —** Skip the gap-fill and write a complete Text-2-style dialogue of eight lines about your own neighbourhood, using 附近有…、离…远吗、从…怎么去、要坐多长时间 and 大约 — then perform it live with a partner who has not seen it, so they have to answer for *their* home, not read your answers.

#### Game: 20 Questions — mystery destination (7 min)
One student thinks of a place from the unit (超市、花店、文具店、家具店、饭店、火车站、电影院、机场、学校). The class asks yes/no and short questions in Chinese only — 离学校远吗？ 要坐多长时间？ 在马路对面吗？ 那儿有沙发吗？ — and gets 是／不是／挺远的／大约二十分钟 back. Twenty questions to guess it.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing. Keep the unit word list on the board as a guessing bank.
- **Harder version:** The person thinking of the place may not answer with a single word — every answer must be a full sentence, and at least three answers across the round must use 先…，然后…. The class asking may not repeat a question stem.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read. Ask: did we achieve criterion 1? Prove it — everyone answers 你家离学校要坐多长时间？ chorally.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards, 👍/😐/👎 per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, answer in one sentence: 从你家到学校要坐多长时间？ Must contain 大约. *(Early finishers: add how you get there, with 先…，然后…。)*
- **Preview:** "Last lesson of the unit. You'll put the whole neighbourhood together — four more shops, a map, and you giving directions to a stranger."
