# My Whole Neighbourhood 我们的社区

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe a whole neighbourhood and tell someone how to get around it.

## Success Criteria
- I can name four more shops in Chinese (水果店、书店、理发店、快餐店)
- I can describe a map using 附近、离、就、对面
- I can tell a visitor how to reach a place, and how long it takes

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.145, 146, 149, 151, 153 — Ex.4, Ex.6, Ex.10, Ex.12, Ex.16 displayed
- Printed A3 town map, one per group of three: the Lesson 3 crossroads extended — 超市 · 花店 · 文具店 · 家具店 · 饭店 · 火车站 · 电影院 · 学校 · 水果店 · 书店 · 理发店 · 快餐店 · 公园 · 医院, all labelled in characters
- Blank A3 sheets and coloured markers for the dream-city task, one per pair
- Blu-tack for the gallery walk
- Workbook pp.170–173 (map description, reading comprehension, neighbourhood essay)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The whole sequence — 附近、离…远/近、挺…的、就、对面、先…，然后…、要坐多长时间、大约.

**How:** p.145 Ex.4, the eight-question interview, run as a standing pair activity. Partner A asks all eight, B answers; swap at four minutes. The eight cover the neighbourhood, the house and the room, so Lessons 13 and 14 come back in too.

Circulate and listen for two things specifically: 离 with no 远/近 after it, and 就 landing after the verb. Note names; those students get the front-row seat for Activity 2.

**Connection:** "That interview is the unit test's speaking half, and you just did it from memory. Today you add the last four shops and then you put the whole neighbourhood on one map."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Read aloud. Then: "Every one of today's four words ends in 店. You have met that character in four shops already. This is the easiest vocabulary of the unit — which means today's real work is the map and the directions."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 水果店 | shuǐguǒdiàn | fruit shop | Line drawing: shopfront with crates of apples, bananas and oranges out front |
| 1 | 书店（書店） | shūdiàn | bookshop | Line drawing: shopfront, window of shelved books, a hanging sign |
| 2 | 理发店（理髮店） | lǐfàdiàn | hairdresser's | Line drawing: shopfront with a barber's pole and a chair in the window |
| 2 | 快餐店 | kuàicāndiàn | fast-food shop | Line drawing: shopfront with a counter, a burger and a drink cup on the sign |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. **水果店**里有苹果和香蕉。 — There are apples and bananas in the fruit shop.
2. 我常常去**书店**买书。 — I often go to the bookshop to buy books.
3. **书店**就在**水果店**对面。 — The bookshop is right opposite the fruit shop.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 就在 ________ 对面。
**Extension —** Place both shops and say which you go to more often and why, in one connected pair of sentences: 书店就在水果店对面。因为我很喜欢看书，所以我常常去书店，可是妈妈天天去水果店买苹果。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **理发店**在花店右面。 — The hairdresser's is to the right of the flower shop.
2. 我们常常去**快餐店**吃饭。 — We often eat at the fast-food shop.
3. **理发店**和**快餐店**都在马路对面。 — Both the hairdresser's and the fast-food shop are across the road.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家附近有 ________ 和 ________ 。
**Extension —** All four of today's shops in one sentence with 等, then a second sentence saying which is furthest and how you get there: 我家附近有水果店、书店、理发店、快餐店等。理发店最远，我要先走路，然后坐五路公共汽车，大约十五分钟。

*(Also on the board as recognition only, from p.149 Ex.10's answer bank: 医院、公园、地铁站、加油站、玩具店. Students read them, they don't write them.)*

#### Key Sentence Pattern
No new pattern today — the job is running all of them together. Model one paragraph, building it live on the board sentence by sentence and naming the structure each time:

> 我家附近有水果店、书店、快餐店等。**(附近有…等)**
> 超市离我家不远，走路五分钟就到了。**(离…不远 / 就)**
> 书店就在马路对面。**(就在…对面)**
> 我家离电影院挺远的。**(挺…的)**
> 从我家去电影院，我先坐船，然后坐五路公共汽车，大约二十分钟。**(先…，然后… / 大约)**

Then wipe it and ask the class to rebuild it chorally from the structure labels alone.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "水果店 — what do they sell? Answer in Chinese." (苹果、香蕉、橘子…)
- "Whiteboards: *The bookshop is right across the road.*" (书店就在马路对面。)
- "I'll say five sentences. Thumbs down on the wrong one." — 我家离超市很近。／书店在就马路对面。／大约二十分钟。／我先走路，然后坐船。／理发店在花店右面。
- "Give me a sentence with 等 that has four shops in it. Thirty seconds."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Read the map — We Do (6 min)
Summary board up: all four words, characters and pictures only. A3 map to each group of three, same map on the projector.

Round the group: each student in turn says one sentence about the map, and no structure may be used twice — so the group has to get through 附近有…等, 离…远/近, 就在…对面, 挺…的 and 先…，然后… between them. Six sentences minimum per group.

Then whole class: teacher points at a shop, class answers chorally 那是什么店？

**Extension —** The group describes the map with the projector switched off, from memory, and one member writes down what the other two say — then they check against the map and correct their own errors in Chinese: 不对，理发店不在书店对面，在书店右面。

#### Activity 2: The information desk — You Do (9 min)
p.151 Ex.12, in pairs, using the A3 map. One student is behind the information desk at the train station; the other is a visitor. The visitor asks for **five** places and must get, for each one:
1. where it is — 在哪儿？
2. how to get there — 怎么去？
3. how long it takes — 要多长时间？

> 请问，电影院在哪儿？ — 电影院就在马路对面。
> 从这儿怎么去超市？ — 你先走路，然后坐五路公共汽车。
> 要多长时间？ — 大约十五分钟。

Swap roles at four and a half minutes. The students you flagged in the Review phase sit at the desk first — the desk role forces 就 and 离 into every answer.

**Extension —** The visitor is awkward on purpose: they are carrying a heavy suitcase (so 走路 is refused), they have only twenty minutes, and they want to visit **two** places before their train. The desk has to propose an order and justify it — 你先去水果店，因为它很近，然后去书店，书店就在对面，大约二十分钟就够了 — with no map visible to the visitor.

#### Dream city — gallery walk (8 min)
p.153 Ex.16, in pairs on A3. Four minutes to draw a dream city: at least eight labelled places in characters, at least six of them shops from this lesson sequence. Then write **two** sentences of advice underneath for a friend visiting — where to go and how to get there.

Blu-tack all sheets along the wall. Two minutes: everyone walks the gallery and puts a tick next to the one city they'd most like to visit. The top two pairs read their advice aloud.

- **Setup needed:** A3 sheets, coloured markers, Blu-tack, wall space.
- **Harder version:** The advice is written as a short letter to the visiting friend — five sentences, opening with 你好, using 附近有…等, 离…挺远的 and 先…，然后… at least once each, and closing with a recommendation and a reason. This is the workbook's neighbourhood essay (Test Part 11) done early and in public; keep the best two to show next time.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria — and the whole sequence.** Put all six lessons' learning intentions on one slide. Read them down the list; for each one, the class gives a Chinese example. This is the wider look back, not just today.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards, 👍/😐/👎 against the six sequence intentions, not just today's three. Collect the boards visually — whichever intention draws the most 😐 is what opens the revision, if you run one.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, three sentences about your own neighbourhood: one with 附近有…等, one with 离…远/近, one with 先…，然后…. *(Early finishers: add a fourth with 就.)*
- **Preview:** "That's Unit 5. Bring your exit tickets back next lesson — the ones with all three structures right are what the test's writing section looks like."

## Note for the teacher — what this sequence does not cover

By request, the radical and pinyin work in Lesson 15 was left out: p.147 Ex.7 (s/sh pinyin, CD 72), p.147 Ex.9 (simple characters 反 血 习 山), p.144 Ex.3 (the 36-character memorise-and-dictate grid) and the workbook's "write one word for each radical". If the class sits the Unit 5 Test as printed, **Part 3** (write the radical and its meaning) and **Part 4** (find the simple character) have not been prepared. Everything else on the test — the floor plan, the furniture matching, measure words, the 离/有/在/就/先…然后… sentence building, translation both ways, opposites, reading comprehension and the essay — is covered across Lessons 13–15.
