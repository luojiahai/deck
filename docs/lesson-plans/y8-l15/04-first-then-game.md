# First… Then… 我先坐船，然后坐公共汽车

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe a journey that has two parts.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 电影院 and 船 in Chinese
- I can join two stages of a journey with 先…，然后…
- I can ask how someone gets somewhere using 从…怎么去…？

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Textbook pp.148, 150 — Text 2 and p.150 Ex.11 displayed
- Audio: CD track 74 (Text 2)
- Printed Journey Relay card sets: two decks of transport cards (走路 · 坐校车 · 坐公共汽车 · 坐船 · 坐火车 · 坐飞机 · 开车 · 骑自行车) and two decks of destination cards (电影院 · 火车站 · 超市 · 学校 · 家具店 · 花店), A5, four sets total
- Two board markers and a clear stretch of whiteboard, divided into two team columns
- Workbook pp.167–168 (先…然后… practice)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 就 and 对面 from last lesson, plus the bus-number 路.

**How:** Slap the Board, straight into it — eight words on the board in characters only (马路 · 对面 · 火车站 · 路 · 超市 · 离 · 远 · 挺), two students at a time, teacher says the English. Three pairs, ninety seconds each.

Then one sentence to fix on whiteboards: ✗ 电影院在马路对面就。 → 电影院**就**在马路对面。

**Connection:** "电影院 — that word on the board is new, and it's today's. You can already say where it is. What you can't say yet is how to *get* there when it takes two buses."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Read aloud, then draw three dots on the board — 家 → ? → 电影院 — and write 先 above the first arrow, 然后 above the second: "Two legs, two words. That's the whole grammar of today."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 电影院（電影院） | diànyǐngyuàn | cinema | Line drawing: cinema front with a marquee, a ticket window and two doors |
| 1 | 船 | chuán | boat; ship | Line drawing: small passenger ferry, side view, one funnel, water lines beneath |
| 2 | 然后（然後） | ránhòu | then; after that | Diagram: two arrows end to end, 先 over the first, 然后 over the second |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家附近有一个**电影院**。 — There's a cinema near my home.
2. 我坐**船**去学校。 — I go to school by boat.
3. **电影院**离我家很远，要坐**船**去。 — The cinema is far from my home; you have to go by boat.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 离我家很远，要坐 ________ 去。
**Extension —** Two sentences: one place you reach on foot and one you reach by boat or train, contrasted with 可是, plus the times: 超市离我家很近，走路三分钟就到了，可是电影院挺远的，要坐船去。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我先走路，**然后**坐五路公共汽车。 — First I walk, then I take the number 5 bus.
2. 我先坐船，**然后**坐火车去电影院。 — First I take the boat, then the train to the cinema.
3. 我先坐公共汽车，**然后**走路十分钟就到了。 — First the bus, then ten minutes on foot and I'm there.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我先 ________ ，然后 ________ 。
**Extension —** Three legs, not two — add 再 in the middle: 我先走路，然后坐船，再坐五路公共汽车。 Then say the whole journey backwards, from the cinema home.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**从 A 怎么去 B？** → **我先…，然后…。**

1. 从我家怎么去学校？ — 我先走路，然后坐校车。
2. 从你们家怎么去电影院？ — 我先坐船，然后再坐五路公共汽车。 *(= Text 2, p.148)*
3. 从学校怎么去超市？ — 走路五分钟就到了。 ← the answer doesn't *have* to be two legs; check they notice.
4. 从学校怎么去火车站？ — 我先坐十路公共汽车，然后走路十分钟就到了。 ← two legs, a bus number, 就 from last lesson.

**The error to pre-empt, on the board in red:**
- ✗ 我先坐船，然后我坐公共汽车。 → the second 我 is unnecessary once the subject is set.
- ✗ 我坐船先，坐公共汽车然后。 → 先 and 然后 come before the verb, like 就.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "先 or 然后 — which one goes first? Say it with me."
- "Whiteboards: *First I walk, then I take the bus.*" (我先走路，然后坐公共汽车。)
- "怎么去电影院？ Tell your partner. 30 seconds. Go." Then cold call two.
- "Correct me: 我坐船先，然后坐火车。"

#### Text 2 (p.148, CD track 74) — first four lines
> A: 你们家附近有电影院吗？ B: 没有。
> A: 从你们家怎么去电影院？ B: 我先坐船，然后再坐五路公共汽车。

Play CD 74 up to this point. Choral read, then pairs read it twice swapping roles. Comprehension in Chinese: 他家附近有电影院吗？ 他怎么去电影院？

The rest of the dialogue — 要坐多长时间？ — stays covered until Lesson 5.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min) — games

#### Game 1: Journey Relay — We Do into You Do (9 min)

**Round 0 — p.150 Ex.11, 2 min, seated.** The six pictures of family members' journeys go on the projector. Teacher points at each one and the class says it chorally with 先…，然后…. That is the textbook exercise done, and it doubles as the model for the relay.

**Then the relay, 7 min.** Two teams, board split into two columns. Each team gets a transport deck and a destination deck face down.

A runner from each team draws **two** transport cards and **one** destination card, runs to the board and writes the full sentence: 我先坐船，然后坐公共汽车去电影院。 Then back, tag, next runner. Wrong sentence = the next runner must fix it before writing their own. Six runners each, fastest correct column wins.

The decks are stacked so that 船 and 电影院 come up for every team — the summary board (电影院 · 船 · 然后, characters and pictures only) stays on screen throughout and every team will have used all three words by the end.

**Extension —** The fluent students run last and draw **three** transport cards, so their sentence needs 先…，然后…，再… and a stated reason for the order: 我先走路去火车站，然后坐火车，再坐公共汽车去电影院，因为坐船太慢了。 They also become the referee for the other team's column, saying in Chinese what is wrong with any sentence they reject.

#### Game 2: Telephone 传声筒 (7 min)
Rows of six. Teacher whispers a full two-leg journey sentence to the first student in each row — 我先坐船，然后坐十路公共汽车去电影院 — and it passes down the row by whisper. The last student writes it on a mini whiteboard and holds it up. Closest to the original wins.

Run three rounds with three different sentences, each one using 然后 plus one of today's words.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing printed. Three sentences prepared on a card for the teacher.
- **Harder version:** The last student in the winning row composes round four's sentence themselves and whispers it — it must contain 先…，然后…, a bus number, and a destination, and they must be able to write it correctly when challenged.

#### Game 3: 从…怎么去…? chain (7 min)
Whole class standing in a circle. Student 1 asks student 2: 从学校怎么去电影院？ Student 2 answers with 先…，然后…, then turns to student 3 with a *new* destination question. Anyone who repeats a destination, pauses longer than five seconds, or drops 然后 sits down. Last three standing win.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing. Keep the summary board on screen.
- **Harder version:** Sit the remaining strong students out of the circle and make them the questioners — they ask from the outside, they may not repeat a question form, and they must add a follow-up in Chinese to any answer they judge too short (那要坐多长时间？ is not allowed yet — 你为什么不走路？ is).

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read from the screen; ask for one Chinese example of criterion 2 from a student who has not spoken this lesson.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs against each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip: 从你家怎么去电影院？ Answer in one sentence with 先…，然后…。 *(Early finishers: answer for the train station as well.)*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: the question B never got asked today — 要坐多长时间？ How long does it take?"
