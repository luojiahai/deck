# Lesson 9.8: Review + Assessment Prep

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 8 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to review and consolidate everything from Lesson 9 (Dance) ahead of the unit test.

## Success Criteria
- I can write the 4 simple characters from this lesson from dictation (平 / 土 / 角 / 页)
- I can use all 5 Lesson 9 grammar patterns correctly in a mixed review
- I can recall the Lesson 9 test vocabulary list (跳舞, 舞蹈, 篮球场, 网球场, and more)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (Test 3.3 vocabulary list, 5-pattern grammar chart, common-error slide)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Character dictation grid template (5×5, Act.15 style)
- Test 3.3-style practice worksheet (picture matching, translation, sentence completion)
- Odd One Out category cards

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The whole Lesson 9 unit — vocabulary and all 5 grammar patterns from Lessons 1–7
**How:** Rapid mini-whiteboard quiz — 5 quick-fire prompts, one per grammar pattern, students write a sentence or translation for each, hold up after every prompt.
**Connection:** "You've built up five grammar patterns and a full set of dance/hobby vocabulary this week. Today is entirely about pulling it together and making sure it's solid before Test 3.3."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read aloud together. Show the Test 3.3 vocabulary list so students know exactly what's being assessed.

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### Test 3.3 Vocabulary Recap
跳舞, 游戏, 舞蹈, 电脑游戏, 功课, 做作业, 什么时候, 干什么, 时候, 篮球场, 网球场, 一点儿 (a little bit), 一点 (1 o'clock), 一起 (together), 一块/一元 (1 dollar), 一会儿 (a little while), 跟……一起 (together with), 一边……一边…… (doing while doing), 除了……以外 (besides)

Point out 篮球场/网球场 extend 球场 (Lesson 6) with the specific sport; 功课 is a synonym for 作业 that may appear on the test though not in the New Words list; 一点儿/一点/一块/一会儿 are near-homophones worth distinguishing carefully (small amount / 1 o'clock / 1 dollar / a little while).

#### 5-Pattern Grammar Recap
Quick teacher-led review of all 5 patterns (chart from Lesson 7, still displayed): 除了……以外 / 从……开始 / 在……呢 / 吧 / 什么时候 vs 几点.

#### Common Errors Slide
- 从**四岁**开始跳舞 — not 五岁 (a common mixup from workbook exercises)
- 什么时候 (when — any time frame) vs 几点 (what time — hour only) — don't swap them
- 吧 (suggestion) vs 呢 (continuation) — don't confuse the two particles
- 拉小提琴 vs 弹钢琴 — instrument verbs are not interchangeable

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Thumbs up if 欢欢 started dancing at age 4, not 5."
- "What's the difference between 什么时候 and 几点? Turn and tell your partner."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Act.15-Style Character Dictation Grid — We Do (8 min)
A 5×5 grid of simple characters from across the unit (including 平, 土, 角, 页 plus review characters from Lessons 7–8: 包, 止, 士, 豆, 夕, 心, 目, 方) is displayed briefly for memorisation, then hidden. Teacher dictates characters one at a time by pinyin/meaning; students write them into the correct grid square. Run as a team competition — most correct squares wins.
- **Scaffold:** Grid stays partially visible (positions labelled with pinyin) for students who need it; teacher repeats each dictated item twice.
- **Extension:** Students also write one word that uses each dictated character as part of a compound, where they know one.
- **OTR:** Every student writes every dictated item — team format keeps engagement high.

#### Activity 2: Test 3.3-Style Practice Worksheet — You Do (9 min)
Individually, students complete a short practice worksheet mirroring the real test format: picture-to-Chinese matching, one grammar pattern translation per pattern (5 items), and 2 personal-answer questions (e.g. "你从几岁开始学中文？").
- **Scaffold:** Word bank provided for the picture-matching section; sentence frames for the translation section.
- **Extension:** Students complete an additional free-writing item: 3 sentences about their own hobbies using 3 different L9 patterns, with no frame.
- **OTR:** Independent work, then peer-swap for checking against a projected answer key — every student both produces and checks.

#### Game: Odd One Out (6 min)
Projector shows sets of 4 words (3 from one category, 1 from another — e.g. 3 sports + 1 music word, or 3 items using 吧 vs 1 using 呢). Students identify and explain the odd one out on their whiteboards.
- **Setup needed:** Pre-made slide deck of 6–8 "odd one out" sets covering the whole unit.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; ask "Did we achieve this today?" for each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria — this is the final self-check before the test.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write 3 sentences about your own hobbies, each using a different Lesson 9 grammar pattern."
- **Preview:** "Well done — that's the full Dance unit covered. Next up: Test 3.3, then a new unit."
