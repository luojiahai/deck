# Lesson 9.5: Text 2 Vocabulary + 在……呢

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning new vocabulary (homework, computer games, basketball) and how to ask and answer "what are you doing right now" using 在……呢.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 作业, 做作业, 电脑游戏, 篮球, 打篮球 in Chinese
- I can ask "你在干什么呢？" and answer using 在……呢
- I can listen and identify these activities by ear

## Materials Needed
- Projector (vocab slides, audio-style listening script read by teacher)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Listen-and-tick worksheet (Act. 7 style — 6 people, 12 activities)
- Bingo grids (pre-made, today's vocabulary)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4 — the 欢欢 passage and 正在 pictures
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on 正在 + verb phrases from last lesson (teacher shows a picture, class says the 正在 sentence chorally). Then quick mini-whiteboard check: "Write one sentence using 正在."
**Connection:** "You know 正在 for 'happening right now.' Today you'll meet an even stronger version — 在……呢 — plus three new everyday words: homework, computer games, and basketball."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read aloud together. Ask: "Thumbs up if you did homework or played a computer game yesterday — we'll use exactly those words today."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 就 | jiù | just; exactly |
| 干 | gàn | do |
| 干什么 | gàn shénme | what to do; doing what |
| 呢 | ne | particle: continuation of action |
| 业 | yè | profession |
| 作业 | zuòyè | homework; assignment |
| 做作业 | zuò zuòyè | do homework |
| 玩 | wán | play |
| 游戏 | yóuxì | game |
| 电脑游戏 | diànnǎo yóuxì | computer game |
| 篮 | lán | basket |
| 篮球 | lánqiú | basketball |
| 打篮球 | dǎ lánqiú | play basketball |

Introduce each word: 汉字 → pinyin → English, choral repetition ×2, pinyin visible. Note 篮球 follows the same 打 + ball-sport rule from Lesson 8 (打篮球, like 打网球).

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 你在干什么呢？ (What are you doing [right now]?)
> 我在做作业呢。 (I'm doing homework [right now].)

Model 3 examples, thinking aloud: compare 正在做作业 (plain "in progress") with 在做作业呢 (stronger — "right this second, and it continues"). 我在玩电脑游戏呢。 我在打篮球呢。 Insert a silly one: 你在干什么呢？我在跟猫说话呢！(pause for reactions).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 干什么 mean? Thumbs up if you know."
- "How do you say 'I'm playing basketball right now'? Write it on your whiteboard."
- "Listen: 我在做作业呢。 What is the person doing?" (spot-check listening before the main practice)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Listen and Tick — Act.7 Style — We Do (8 min)
Teacher reads 6 short sentences aloud (each describing one person doing one of 12 possible activities, mixing today's new words with L7–L9 vocabulary). Students tick the matching picture/word on their worksheet for each.
- **Scaffold:** All 12 activities are pre-printed with pinyin and pictures on the worksheet; teacher repeats each sentence twice.
- **Extension:** Students also write the full Chinese sentence they heard, not just tick the picture.
- **OTR:** Every student ticks every item — teacher reveals answers on the projector for instant self-marking.

#### Activity 2: Listen and Match (6 people, Act.13 style) — You Do (8 min)
Teacher (or a fluent-speaking student volunteer) reads 6 short first-person statements ("我在做作业呢。" / "我在打篮球呢。" etc.) one at a time; students match each to a person's name/number on their worksheet.
- **Scaffold:** Fewer distractor activities (6 instead of 12) and slower reading pace.
- **Extension:** After matching, students write one full sentence per person from memory, without looking at the word bank.
- **OTR:** Listen-and-match — every student responds to every item.

#### Game: Bingo (5 min)
Students fill a 3×3 grid with today's vocabulary (their choice of which squares — some repeat). Teacher calls out English meanings or pinyin one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字. First to a full line calls 宾狗 (Bingo).
- **Setup needed:** Blank grids (photocopied), markers/pens.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads each criterion; class responds with the target word/sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write what you're doing right now, using 在……呢."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we'll read a phone-call dialogue and learn how to make a suggestion — 'let's go play basketball!' — plus the difference between 什么时候 and 几点."
