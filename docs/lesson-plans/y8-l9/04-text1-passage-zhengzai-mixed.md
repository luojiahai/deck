# Lesson 9.4: Text 1 Passage (欢欢) + 正在 Review

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to read and understand the Text 1 passage about 欢欢's hobbies, and describe actions in progress using 正在.

## Success Criteria
- I can read and understand 欢欢's passage about her hobbies (dance and violin)
- I can answer comprehension questions about the text, in Chinese where possible
- I can describe pictures of people using 正在 + verb

## Materials Needed
- Projector (passage displayed with pinyin, 9 action pictures for 正在 practice)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed copies of the Text 1 passage with 3 comprehension questions
- Sentence-strip cards for "Find Someone Who" (正在 questions)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 3 — 拉小提琴, instrument verb rule, 从……开始 + duration
**How:** Pair dialogue: students recreate a "从……几岁开始……" mini-exchange from memory, then swap roles. Follow with 2 mini-whiteboard translation checks.
**Connection:** "This week you've learned 除了……以外, 从……开始, and violin vocabulary. Today you'll see it all used together in one real passage — about a girl called 欢欢 — and revisit 正在 (action in progress), which you first met with music."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read aloud together. Tell students: "By the end of today you should be able to read this whole passage without help."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### Text / Dialogue
**Text 1 (p. 80, CD Track 41)**
> 你好！我叫欢欢。除了音乐以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。我从四岁开始学跳舞。我每个星期二、星期四跳两个小时舞。我从六岁开始拉小提琴。我每天拉四十五分钟小提琴。

Read aloud with pinyin visible, students follow. Read together chorally. Then read again with pinyin hidden, pointing to characters as a comprehension check.

#### CFU / Comprehension Questions
- 欢欢几岁开始跳舞？(check: 四岁 — flag this is a common error point, not 五岁)
- 她每个星期几跳舞？跳几个小时？(check: 星期二、星期四，两个小时)
- 她几岁开始拉小提琴？她每天拉多久？(check: 六岁；四十五分钟)

Y8 target: push for Chinese answers where possible; accept English for the first attempt, then ask fluent speakers to restate in Chinese.

#### 正在 Recap
Quick recap of the pattern from L7/L8: 正在 + verb = action happening right now. Show one example picture: 她正在跳舞。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Choral + Partner Reading with Comprehension Q&A — We Do (8 min)
Students read the passage chorally once more, then in pairs take turns asking/answering the 3 comprehension questions aloud (one asks, one answers, then swap).
- **Scaffold:** Questions and answer sentence frames printed alongside the passage; pinyin visible.
- **Extension:** Partners add one extra question of their own about the passage (e.g. "她喜欢什么音乐？" — inferring beyond the text, or noting what's not stated).
- **OTR:** Turn-and-talk — every student both asks and answers.

#### Activity 2: Act.3-Style Picture Writing (9 pictures, 正在) — You Do (8 min)
Using 9 projected pictures of people doing different activities, students write a 正在 sentence for each in their books.
- **Scaffold:** Word bank of the 9 activity verbs/phrases provided; pinyin visible for less confident students.
- **Extension:** Students add a time or duration detail to 3 of the 9 sentences (e.g. 她正在跳舞，她每个星期跳两次).
- **OTR:** Cold call 4–5 students to share one sentence each before the game.

#### Game: Find Someone Who (7 min)
Students circulate asking classmates "你正在做什么？" — but reframed as a mingling game: each student is secretly assigned an activity card (跳舞, 拉小提琴, 打篮球, 做作业, etc.) and must find classmates whose activity matches clues given by the teacher ("Find someone who is dancing"), asking 你正在做什么？to check.
- **Setup needed:** One activity card per student, clue list for the teacher to call out.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; ask "Did we achieve this today?"
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Answer in Chinese: 欢欢几岁开始拉小提琴？ Then write one 正在 sentence about yourself right now."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we meet a new set of words — homework, computer games, basketball — and a stronger way to say 'right now': 在……呢."
