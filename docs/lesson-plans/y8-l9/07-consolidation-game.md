# Lesson 9.7: Listening + Speaking Consolidation

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use all five Lesson 9 grammar patterns together in speaking and listening tasks.

## Success Criteria
- I can listen and identify which activity/grammar pattern is being used
- I can make a correct sentence for each of the 5 key grammar patterns (除了……以外 / 从……开始 / 在……呢 / 吧 / 什么时候 vs 几点)
- I can ask and answer questions about hobbies fluently with a partner

## Materials Needed
- Projector (5-pattern recap chart, listening script)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Act.8-style grammar relay cards (10 prompt items covering all L9 grammar)
- Act.10-style question sheet (8 questions about what people were/are doing)
- "Two Truths, One Lie" prompt cards

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 6 — 吧, 什么时候 vs 几点, and the 大生 phone dialogue
**How:** Pair dialogue: students redo the invitation dialogue from memory (Text 2 or their own from Lesson 6), then swap roles. Follow with one mini-whiteboard check: "Write one 什么时候 or 几点 question."
**Connection:** "This week you've learned five different grammar points for this unit. Today is about using all five *together*, fast, in listening and speaking — getting ready for the review lesson and the test."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read aloud together. Show the 5-pattern recap chart (除了……以外 / 从……开始 / 在……呢 / 吧 / 什么时候 vs 几点) and keep it visible all lesson as a reference.

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### Recap of All 5 Grammar Patterns
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| 除了……以外 | 除了音乐以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。 |
| 从……开始 | 我从四岁开始学跳舞。 |
| 在……呢 | 我在做作业呢。 |
| 吧 | 我们一起去打篮球吧！ |
| 什么时候 vs 几点 | 你什么时候去？——下午四点。 |

Quick teacher-led walkthrough of all 5 on the projector — one example sentence each, no new content.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- Cold call: "Give me one example of 除了……以外." / "Give me one example of 吧." (one cold call per pattern, 5 total, rapid pace)
- "Which pattern would I use to ask 'what time'? Thumbs up for 几点."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Act.8-Style Grammar Relay — We Do (8 min)
Teams line up at the board. Teacher reveals a prompt card (one of 10 items, each requiring one of the 5 grammar patterns, e.g. "besides basketball…", "starting from age 5…", "invite a friend to swim"). The first student in each team runs up and writes a correct sentence; team scores a point if correct (teacher or class checks).
- **Scaffold:** The 5-pattern recap chart stays projected throughout; struggling teams may consult it before writing.
- **Extension:** Once a team has written a correct sentence, a second team member must add a follow-up sentence using a *different* pattern before the next prompt is revealed.
- **OTR:** Whole-team engagement — every student gets at least 2 turns across the 10 items.

#### Activity 2: Act.10-Style Mingle Speaking — You Do (8 min)
Students circulate asking 8 classmates questions about what they were/are doing at specific times (e.g. "你昨天晚上八点在干什么呢？" / "你星期六几点打篮球？"), noting down one-word answers on their question sheet.
- **Scaffold:** All 8 questions pre-printed with pinyin; sentence-frame answers modelled on the board.
- **Extension:** Students ask one unscripted follow-up question of their own choosing after each scripted one.
- **OTR:** Every student asks and answers 8 times each — high-volume oral practice.

#### Game: Two Truths, One Lie (7 min)
In small groups, each student says 3 sentences about their hobbies using 3 different L9 grammar patterns — two true, one false (e.g. 除了游泳以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。我从三岁开始拉小提琴。我在学校打篮球呢。). The group listens and votes on which is the lie; the student reveals the answer.
- **Setup needed:** None beyond the 5-pattern chart still visible for reference.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads each criterion; class gives an example for each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** Teacher says one sentence aloud (e.g. "我们一起去游泳吧！"); students write which of the 5 grammar patterns it uses.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson is our final review before the test — we'll practise characters, do a full practice test, and make sure everything from this unit is solid."
