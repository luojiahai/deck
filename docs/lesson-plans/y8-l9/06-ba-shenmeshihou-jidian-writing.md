# Lesson 9.6: 吧 + 什么时候 vs 几点

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 6 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to make suggestions using 吧 and ask "when" correctly using 什么时候 or 几点.

## Success Criteria
- I can read and understand the Text 2 dialogue
- I can make a suggestion using 吧 (我们一起去打篮球吧)
- I can correctly choose 什么时候 or 几点 depending on what's being asked

## Materials Needed
- Projector (dialogue displayed with pinyin, 什么时候 vs 几点 comparison chart)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed 什么时候/几点 completion worksheet (Act. 14 style)
- Printed phrase cards for card-matching (Act. 11 style)
- Blank invitation-dialogue writing frame (Act. 12 style)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 5 — 干什么, 作业, 电脑游戏, 篮球, and the 在……呢 pattern
**How:** Mini-whiteboard quick quiz: teacher asks "你在干什么呢？" as a written prompt, students write a full-sentence answer using different vocabulary each time (3 rounds).
**Connection:** "You can now say what you're doing right now. Today you'll read a real phone conversation where one friend invites another to play basketball — and learn how to make that kind of suggestion yourself."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read aloud together. Preview: "By the end of today you'll be able to invite a friend to do something in Chinese."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 吧 | ba | particle: suggestion/proposal |
| 什么时候 | shénme shíhòu | when; at what time |
| 可 | kě | agree |
| 可以 | kěyǐ | can; fine; may |
| 场 | chǎng | place for activities |
| 球场 | qiúchǎng | sports court/ground |

#### Text / Dialogue
**Text 2 (p. 84, CD Track 44)**
> A: 你好！大生在家吗？
> B: 我就是。
> A: 你在干什么呢？
> B: 我在一边做作业一边玩儿电脑游戏呢。
> A: 我们一起去打篮球吧！
> B: 好。什么时候去？
> A: 下午四点，好吗？
> B: 可以。我们四点在球场上见！
> A: 好吧。一会儿见！

Read aloud with pinyin visible, students follow, then read chorally in two halves (A/B roles).

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 我们一起去……吧！ (Let's go … together!)
> 什么时候去？— 下午四点。 (When? — 4pm.)

Model: 我们一起去游泳吧！我们一起去打网球吧！Point out 吧 softens a statement into a friendly suggestion — without it, 我们一起去打篮球 sounds like a flat statement, not an invitation.

Compare **什么时候** (when — any time frame: morning, a day, a weekend) vs **几点** (what time — an hour only). Show two questions side by side: 你什么时候去打篮球？(could be answered "this afternoon" or "Saturday") vs 你几点去打篮球？(must be answered with a clock time, e.g. 四点).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 吧 do to a sentence — command or suggestion? Thumbs up for suggestion."
- "Which question do I use if I want to know the exact hour: 什么时候 or 几点? Write your answer."
- "Error check: 我们一起去打篮球呢！What's wrong for a suggestion?" (check: 呢 marks continuation, not suggestion — needs 吧)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 什么时候 vs 几点 Completion Worksheet — We Do (8 min)
Students complete 8 sentences by filling in either 什么时候 or 几点, based on context clues (e.g. "你____上课？——下午两点。" → 几点; "你____去打球？——星期六。" → 什么时候).
- **Scaffold:** First 2 items done together as a class on the projector before students continue independently.
- **Extension:** Students write 2 of their own question-answer pairs, one for each word, and swap with a partner to solve.
- **OTR:** Whole-class simultaneous writing; teacher circulates and cold-calls to check answers.

#### Activity 2: Invitation Dialogue Writing — Act.12 Style — You Do (8 min)
In pairs, students write their own short invitation dialogue using 一起去……吧, choosing an activity from an Extra Words list (打排球, 打羽毛球, 踢足球, 滑冰, 逛街, 唱卡拉ok, etc.), then read it aloud to another pair.
- **Scaffold:** Dialogue skeleton provided (4 lines: invite → accept/ask when → give time → confirm meeting place) with the Text 2 dialogue as a model to adapt.
- **Extension:** Students add a reason for declining and re-negotiating the time ("不行，我四点在做作业，五点可以吗？").
- **OTR:** Every pair writes and performs — cold call 2–3 pairs to read aloud to the class.

#### Game: Card-Matching Relay — Act.11 Style (7 min)
Teams have a shared set of phrase cards (今天/什么时候/几点/球场/吧/可以, etc.) spread on a desk. Teacher calls out an English meaning or reads a phrase aloud; the first student from each team to grab (or point to and copy) the correct card scores a point for their team.
- **Setup needed:** One set of phrase cards per team, spread face-up.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; ask "Did we achieve this today?"
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one sentence inviting a friend to do something, using 吧."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we'll bring together all five grammar points from this unit — 除了……以外, 从……开始, 在……呢, 吧, 什么时候/几点 — with games and listening practice."
