# Lesson 9.3: 从……开始 + Duration (Violin)

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 3 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say when we started an activity (从……开始) and how long we do it for, using a new instrument word — violin.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 拉, 小提琴, 拉小提琴 and know when to use 拉 vs 弹 for instruments
- I can use 从……开始 to say "starting from age/time X"
- I can combine 从……开始 with a duration phrase (我每天拉四十五分钟小提琴)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (vocab slides, instrument-verb chart)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Running Dictation sentence sheets (posted around the room)
- Pinyin listening worksheet (Act. 5 style — add vowels with tones)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 2 — 除了……以外 and 有的人在……有的人在……; also recap L7 instrument verb 弹 (弹钢琴) and L7 duration phrases (半个小时, 一个小时, etc.)
**How:** Mini-whiteboard quick quiz: "Write one 除了……以外 sentence." then flashcard rapid fire on L7 duration phrases (teacher shows a clock/duration image, class says the Chinese phrase chorally).
**Connection:** "You already know 弹钢琴 and how to say durations like 半个小时. Today you'll meet a new instrument — violin — which uses a *different* verb, and combine duration with a new pattern: 从……开始 (starting from)."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read together. Ask: "Thumbs up if you play an instrument — we'll come back to this in a speaking activity later."

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 拉 | lā | pull; play (bowed string instrument) |
| 提 | tí | carry; lift |
| 小提琴 | xiǎotíqín | violin |
| 拉小提琴 | lā xiǎotíqín | play the violin |

Introduce each word: 汉字 → pinyin → English, choral repetition ×2, pinyin visible throughout. Teach the **instrument verb rule** explicitly: 拉 is for bowed string instruments (violin — you "pull" the bow), 弹 is for keyboard/plucked instruments (piano, guitar — you "flick/pluck"). Write two columns on the board: "拉 — 小提琴" vs "弹 — 钢琴/吉他." Tell students these two verbs are not interchangeable — it's a common mistake.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 我从X岁开始学Y。(I started learning Y from age X.)
> 我每天Z拉小提琴。(I play violin for Z [duration] every day.)

Model 3 examples, thinking aloud: "To say 'I started dancing from age 4', I need 我 + 从 + 四岁 + 开始 + 学跳舞 → 我从四岁开始学跳舞。" Second: 我从六岁开始拉小提琴。 Combine with duration: 我每天拉四十五分钟小提琴。 Insert a silly one: 我从一岁开始拉小提琴！(pause for reactions — a one-year-old playing violin).

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What's the verb for 小提琴? Thumbs up if you remember it's 拉, not 弹."
- "How do you say 'I started swimming from age 7'? Write it on your whiteboard."
- "Error check: 我从六岁弹小提琴。What's wrong?" (check: missing 开始; wrong verb — should be 拉)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Act.4-Style Oral Milestone Practice — We Do (7 min)
In pairs, students take turns saying when they started four things: walking, talking, learning Chinese, and (if applicable) playing an instrument — using 我从……岁/年开始……
- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame on the board: 我从____岁开始____。 Word bank of the 4 milestone verbs (走路, 说话, 学中文, 拉/弹 + instrument).
- **Extension:** Students add a follow-up duration clause for one milestone (我从六岁开始学中文，我每个星期学三个小时).
- **OTR:** Every student speaks in pairs simultaneously — teacher circulates and cold-calls 2–3 students to share with the class afterward.

#### Activity 2: Pinyin Listening — Act.5 Style (ou/uo) — You Do (7 min)
Teacher reads a series of words/syllables aloud (mix of ou and uo sounds, drawing on Unit 3 vocabulary like 舞, 说, 都, 走). Students write the pinyin with correct tone marks on the worksheet.
- **Scaffold:** Word bank of possible syllables provided at the bottom of the worksheet.
- **Extension:** Students also write the character and English meaning for each syllable they hear, not just the pinyin.
- **OTR:** Listen-and-write — every student responds to every item; teacher reveals answers on the projector for self-marking.

#### Game: Running Dictation (7 min)
Sentence strips using 从……开始 (e.g. 我从四岁开始学跳舞。我每天拉四十五分钟小提琴。) are posted around the room. In pairs, one student runs to read a sentence, memorises it, runs back, and dictates it to their partner, who writes it down. Partners swap roles each round. First pair to correctly transcribe all sentences wins.
- **Setup needed:** Sentence strips taped up around the classroom/corridor, pens and paper for each pair.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Student volunteer reads each criterion; class responds with the target word/sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Write one 从……开始 sentence about yourself."
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we'll read the full passage about 欢欢 and her hobbies, putting everything from this week together."
