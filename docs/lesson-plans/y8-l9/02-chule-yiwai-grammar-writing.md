# Lesson 9.2: 除了……以外 Grammar Deep-Dive

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 3 — 爱好 (Hobbies)
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Hobby (3): Dance 舞蹈 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to write longer, more accurate 除了……以外 sentences, including translating from English, and describe a scene using 有的人在……有的人在……

## Success Criteria
- I can translate English sentences into Chinese using 除了……以外
- I can describe what several people are doing in a picture using 有的人在……有的人在……
- I can write an original sentence combining 除了……以外 with an activity of my choice

## Materials Needed
- Projector (grammar slides, park-scene picture)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed translation worksheet (English → Chinese, 除了……以外 sentences)
- Printed park-scene picture (multiple people doing different activities) — one per pair

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1 — 舞蹈, 跳舞, 从, 开始, and the 除了……以外 frame
**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards: teacher gives 2 English sentences ("Besides dancing, I also like reading." / "Besides sport, I also like music."), students write the Chinese and hold up.
**Connection:** "Yesterday you built 除了……以外 sentences with word cards. Today you'll write your own from scratch — including translating from English — and use a similar idea, 有的人……有的人……, to describe a whole scene of people doing different things."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on projector. Read aloud together. Point out that today's success criteria build directly on yesterday's — same grammar, harder application (no word cards).

### I Do (10–20 min)
#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 有的人 | yǒude rén | some people |
| 有的人在……有的人在…… | yǒude rén zài… yǒude rén zài… | some people are …, some people are … |

(Recap, pinyin still visible: 除了……以外, 舞蹈, 跳舞, 从……开始 from Lesson 1.)

#### Key Sentence Pattern
> 除了X以外，我还喜欢Y。 (recap)
> 有的人在跳舞，有的人在唱歌。(Some people are dancing, some are singing.)

Model the translation process step by step on the board for one example: "Besides music, I also like dance." → find the "besides" item (music, 音乐) → find the "also like" item (dance, 舞蹈) → slot into 除了____以外，我还喜欢____。→ 除了音乐以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。Do a second, slightly different example: "Besides swimming, she also likes tennis." → 除了游泳以外，她还喜欢打网球。

For 有的人在……有的人在……, show the park-scene picture and model: "有的人在跑步，有的人在游泳，有的人在打篮球。" Point out this pattern re-uses 正在/在 (action in progress) from Lesson 7/8 but with 有的人 (some people) as the subject.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Translate: 'Besides reading, I also like watching TV.' Write it on your whiteboard."
- "What's wrong with 除了以外音乐，我还喜欢舞蹈？" (check: word order — 除了 + item + 以外)
- "Look at the picture — tell your partner one 有的人在…… sentence."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Act.6-Style Translation Worksheet — We Do → You Do (9 min)
Students translate 5 English sentences into Chinese using 除了……以外 (e.g. "Besides music, I also like sport." / "Besides homework, I also like computer games."), individually first, then peer-check with a partner using a projected answer key.
- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame printed above each translation line with blanks only for the two hobby words; word bank of hobby vocabulary from L7–L9 provided.
- **Extension:** Students translate 2 extra sentences with no frame provided, and add a third clause of their own invention (e.g. "…and I also don't really like homework," reusing 不太 from L8).
- **OTR:** Every student writes every translation — peer-check ensures a second pass on each answer.

#### Activity 2: Park-Scene Picture Writing — You Do (9 min)
In pairs, students look at the printed park-scene picture and write 4–5 sentences describing what different people are doing, using 有的人在……有的人在……
- **Scaffold:** Picture has 4 clearly distinct activities labelled with word banks underneath each figure.
- **Extension:** Students combine two activities per person using 一边……一边…… from Lesson 7 (e.g. "有的人在一边跑步一边听音乐").
- **OTR:** Cold call 3–4 pairs to read one sentence aloud.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (5 min)
Pre-cut sentence-piece sets (word-by-word) for 4–5 除了……以外 and 有的人在…… sentences are distributed to pairs/small groups. Students race to arrange the pieces into a correct sentence and write it out on their whiteboard.
- **Setup needed:** Pre-cut word-strip envelopes, one set per pair/group.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read; class gives an example for each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each of the 3 criteria.
- **Exit ticket:** "Translate: 'Besides basketball, I also like dance.'"
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we'll learn how to say when you *started* doing something — 从……开始 — and a new instrument word: violin."
