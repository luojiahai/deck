# Colours (2): 蓝色、红色、粉红色 and 和

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say that someone likes two colours, using 和.

## Success Criteria
- I can say three more colours in Chinese (蓝色、红色、粉红色)
- I can join two colours with 和 (我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色)
- I can understand Text 1 and say which colour each family member likes

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books; coloured pencils
- Audio: CD T60 (Text 1), T62 (p.98 Ex.4)
- Workbook p.136 (Ex.3), p.138 (Ex.8)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's 喜欢 and 黑色, 白色, 黄色 — and the word order that puts 喜欢 before the colour.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the three colours, twice — first with pinyin on screen, then without. Then Correct the Teacher: three sentences go up, two of them wrong.
> 我喜欢黑色。 ✓ · 我白色喜欢。 ✗ · 我妈妈喜欢黄。 ✗ (missing 色)

Students write the correction on whiteboards and hold up. The second error is the one to dwell on — 黄 is not a colour word on its own.

**Connection:** "Three colours is enough to talk about one person. Today you get three more, and a word that lets you give someone two colours at once."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Teacher reads the Learning Intention; students read the criteria. Hold up two fingers on the second one: "和 joins exactly two things. Not three, not one."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 蓝色（藍） | lánsè | blue | Blue swatch |
| 1 | 红色（紅） | hóngsè | red | Red swatch |
| 2 | 粉红色（紅） | fěnhóngsè | pink | Pink swatch |

On the 粉红色 slide, point at what students already know: 红色 was on the previous slide, so the only new character is 粉 — "powder". Powder-red is pink. Three characters, one new one.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我哥哥喜欢**蓝色**。 — My older brother likes blue.
2. 我姐姐喜欢**红色**。 — My older sister likes red.
3. 我喜欢**蓝色**，我不喜欢**红色**。 — I like blue, I don't like red.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢 ________ ，我不喜欢 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write two people whose tastes disagree, then say who's right: 我哥哥喜欢蓝色，我妈妈不喜欢蓝色，她喜欢红色。No board.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢**粉红色**。 — I like pink.
2. 我妈妈喜欢红色**和粉红色**。 — My mum likes red and pink.
3. 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色，我喜欢**粉红色**。 — My dad likes black and white, I like pink.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我 ________ 喜欢 ________ 和 ________ 。
**Extension —** One sentence, three people, four colours, 和 used twice and 也 once: 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色，我哥哥也喜欢蓝色和红色。

#### Recall slide
All six Text 1 colours, characters only — no pinyin, no English, no swatches: **黑色 白色 黄色 蓝色 红色 粉红色**. Class reads them back. If this stalls, reteach before the text goes up.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**人 + 喜欢 + X色 + 和 + Y色。**

Think aloud: "Two colours for one person. 我妈妈 · 喜欢 · 红色 · 和 · 粉红色. 和 sits between the two colours, never at the end."

1. 我喜欢红色和蓝色。
2. 我妈妈喜欢黄色和粉红色。
3. 我哥哥不喜欢黑色和白色。
4. 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色，我妈妈喜欢黄色，我喜欢粉红色。 ← three people, one sentence, only the first has 和.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "How many colours can 和 join? Show me on your fingers."
- "Write 'I like red and blue'." (whiteboards)
- "什么不对？ 我喜欢红色蓝色。" — the missing 和.

#### Text 1 (CD T60, textbook p.96)
> 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色。我妈妈喜欢黄色。我哥哥喜欢蓝色。我姐姐喜欢红色。我喜欢粉红色。

Play T60 twice, students following in the book. Choral read, then pairs read it to each other. Three comprehension questions, English accepted for Y7: Who likes red? How many colours does dad like? Which family member likes the same colour as you?

Ask one fluent student to answer the first in Chinese: 姐姐喜欢红色。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 我家的颜色 — six colours, five people — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
Summary board up and staying up: all six Text 1 colours, characters and swatches.

Students write five sentences, one per family member (real or invented), so that **all six colours appear at least once** across the five sentences and **at least two sentences use 和**. Six colours over five sentences forces at least one person to get two.

Model the first line together, then 4 minutes writing. Take three aloud.

**Extension —** Write it as a connected passage rather than a list, using 也 and one 不, and finishing with yourself: 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色，我哥哥也喜欢黑色，我妈妈不喜欢黑色，她喜欢黄色和粉红色，我喜欢蓝色和红色。Deliver it to the class with the board covered.

#### Activity 2: Listening — You Do (5 min) | 27–32
**Textbook p.98 Ex.4 (CD T62)** — match six family members to colours A–F. Play twice, students tick, then check as a class with a show of hands per item.

**Extension —** Close the book and write out what you heard as full sentences from memory — 爸爸喜欢…, 妈妈喜欢… — then invent two more items in the same style for the teacher to read aloud to the class at the end.

#### Activity 3: Stroke order and colouring — You Do (4 min) | 32–36
**Workbook pp.135–136 Ex.2** — copy 蓝, 红, 粉 with stroke order, completing the Text 1 set begun in Lesson 1. Model 蓝 first: 艹 on top (grass — it was a plant dye), then 监 underneath, thirteen strokes. 红 is quick by comparison — 纟 on the left, silk.

Then **workbook p.136 Ex.3, boxes 2, 5 and 6** — draw something in 红色, 蓝色 and 粉红色 and write the colour word underneath in characters. The six boxes of Ex.3 are now complete.

**Extension —** Skip the drawing and do **workbook p.138 Ex.8** instead — four boxes, each labelled with two or three colours (黑色、白色 · 红色、白色、蓝色 · 黄色、黑色 · 蓝色、白色). Draw using exactly those colours and write a full sentence under each with 和: 我喜欢黑色和白色。

#### Game: Telephone 传声筒 (7 min) | 36–43
Rows of five or six. Teacher whispers a phrase to the first student in each row; it passes down the row; the last student says it aloud. Three rounds, getting longer:
> 粉红色 → 蓝色和红色 → 我姐姐喜欢粉红色。

The target is the three-syllable 粉红色 (fěn-hóng-sè) and the 蓝/红 tone pair, which is where the chain usually breaks.

- **Setup needed:** nothing.
- **Harder version:** The fluent student starts the chain with a sentence they invent themselves using 和, and the student at the end must write it on the board in characters rather than say it.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Translate into Chinese — *My older brother likes blue and pink.*
- **Preview:** "下一课 — colours go in front of things. 黑色的火车, 黄色的校车. There's one small word that decides whether you're right."
