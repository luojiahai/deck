# Colours (4): 你喜欢什么颜色？and 橙色、紫色、棕色

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask someone what colour they like, and to answer with three new colours.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 你喜欢什么颜色？and answer it
- I can say 橙色、紫色、棕色
- I can write 颜 and 橙 with the correct stroke order

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books
- Printed Slap the Board cards: all ten colours so far (10 cards)
- Workbook p.141 (Ex.15), p.142 (Ex.16)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The 的 frame from Lesson 3, and the six colours it was built on.

**How:** Three whiteboard translations, held up together — *the black train*, *the pink bus*, *the white taxi*. Then Correct the Teacher on two sentences: 我喜欢红色出租车。(missing 的) and 校车黄色的。(colour goes first). Cold call two students to read their corrections aloud.

**Connection:** "You can describe a colour and you can say you like one. Today you learn to *ask* — which means you can find out what everybody else in this room likes."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Write 你喜欢什么颜色？ across the top of the board in large characters and leave it there for the whole lesson — every activity today uses it.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 颜色（顏） | yánsè | colour | A row of six small swatches (SVG) |
| 1 | 橙色 | chéngsè | orange | Orange swatch, with an orange fruit beside it |
| 2 | 紫色 | zǐsè | purple | Purple swatch |
| 2 | 棕色 | zōngsè | brown | Brown swatch |

On the 颜色 slide: 颜色 is the word for "colour" itself — the category, not one of the colours. It's what makes the question possible.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 你喜欢什么**颜色**？ — What colour do you like?
2. 我喜欢**橙色**。 — I like orange.
3. A: 你喜欢什么**颜色**？ B: 我喜欢**橙色**和红色。 — both words, one exchange.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 你喜欢什么颜色？→ 我喜欢 ________ 和 ________ 。
**Extension —** Answer for three people in one go, including one who disagrees: 我喜欢橙色，我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色，我妈妈不喜欢橙色。Then ask the teacher the question in Chinese and write down the answer.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我姐姐喜欢**紫色**。 — My older sister likes purple.
2. 我不喜欢**棕色**。 — I don't like brown.
3. 我妈妈喜欢**紫色**和**棕色**。 — My mum likes purple and brown.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我 ________ 喜欢 ________ 色。
**Extension —** Ask the person next to you 你喜欢什么颜色？, write their answer down as a sentence about them (他喜欢紫色和蓝色。), then report it to the class without looking at what you wrote.

#### Recall slide
All ten colours so far, characters only: **黑色 白色 黄色 蓝色 红色 粉红色 橙色 紫色 棕色** — plus 颜色 at the end, which is not a colour. Class reads them back and says which one is the odd one out and why.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**你喜欢什么颜色？→ 我喜欢……（和……）。**

Think aloud: "什么 goes in the middle, where the answer will be: 你 · 喜欢 · 什么颜色. Then the answer drops the 什么 and puts the colour in its place: 我 · 喜欢 · 橙色."

1. 你喜欢什么颜色？— 我喜欢橙色。
2. 你妈妈喜欢什么颜色？— 她喜欢紫色。
3. 你哥哥喜欢什么颜色？— 他喜欢棕色和黑色。
4. 你每天坐什么颜色的校车上学？— 我坐黄色的校车上学。 ← 什么颜色 inside a 的 phrase, carrying the whole Unit 4 transport sentence.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Does 什么 come before or after 喜欢?"
- "Write the question 'What colour does your dad like?'" (whiteboards)
- "什么不对？ 什么颜色你喜欢？" — this exact error is workbook Ex.20 #6; students will meet it again on paper.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 这是什么颜色？ — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
Summary board up and staying up: today's four words plus the ten colours, characters and swatches.

**Textbook p.98 Ex.3**, working through the items whose colours have been taught — grapes, cloud, lemon, bow, rabbit, orange, fire extinguisher. Pairs, taking turns: A points and asks 这是什么颜色？, B answers. Grass and anything grey get left for next lesson, when 绿色 and 灰色 arrive.

The question form is what's being drilled, so every turn uses 颜色 and every answer uses one of today's colours at least once across the set.

**Extension —** B answers and then turns it back: 葡萄是紫色。你喜欢紫色吗？ — and A has to answer, then name a different colour they prefer. Run the whole set as a conversation rather than a naming drill, no word bank.

#### Activity 2: Stroke order and odd-one-out — You Do (9 min) | 27–36
**Workbook p.141 Ex.15** — copy 颜, 橙, 紫, 棕 with stroke order. (绿 and 灰 from the same exercise wait until next lesson.)

Model 颜 on the board before they start: fifteen strokes, 彦 on the left and 页 on the right. It is the hardest character in Lesson 13 and the one most likely to come out as a scribble. 橙 next: 木 on the left tells you it's a tree — the fruit came first, the colour second.

Then **workbook p.142 Ex.16** — circle the odd one out in six sets (上午/上学/早上 · 吃饭/睡觉/怎么 · 今天/明天/哪儿 · 黄色/起床/放学 · 商人/上班/工作 · 火车/汽车/电话).

**Extension —** Don't just circle it — write *why* in Chinese for two of the six: 火车、汽车是车，电话不是车。Then build a seventh set of your own with two colours and one non-colour and try it on a partner.

#### Game: Slap the Board — ten colours (7 min) | 36–43
Class into four groups, one player from each at the board, ten colour cards up. Teacher calls a colour in Chinese; first correct hand scores a point for the group. Rotate through every student.

- **Setup needed:** ten printed colour cards, blu-tack.
- **Harder version:** Teacher asks 你喜欢什么颜色？ instead of naming one. The player must answer with a full sentence — 我喜欢紫色 — and only then slap the card they named. Wrong sentence, no point, even if the right card gets hit. Run one round this way with the fluent students while the rest watch, then open it back up.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student volunteer reads each aloud.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Write the question 你喜欢什么颜色？and then your own answer, with two colours joined by 和.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — the last two colours, the Text 2 dialogue, and how to say you *don't* like something and that you agree."
