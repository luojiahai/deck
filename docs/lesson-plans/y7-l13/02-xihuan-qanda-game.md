# Lesson 13.2: Asking About Colour Preferences (你喜欢什么颜色？)

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask and answer questions about colour preferences using 你喜欢什么颜色？and 你喜欢……吗？

## Success Criteria
- I can ask "what colour do you like?" (能问"你喜欢什么颜色？")
- I can ask a yes/no colour question and answer it (能问、答"你喜欢……吗？")
- I can say "I also don't like ___" using 也 (能用"也"说"我也不喜欢")

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Six colour flashcards per pair (黑, 白, 黄, 蓝, 红, 粉红 — from Lesson 1)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 1's six colours (黑色, 白色, 黄色, 蓝色, 红色, 粉红色) and the statement pattern 我喜欢……。

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire — teacher shows a colour flashcard (Chinese side), class chorally says pinyin + English. Then quick whiteboard check: "Write 'I like blue' in Chinese." Cold call two students to read their sentence aloud.

**Connection:** "上节课我们学会了说'我喜欢黑色'。今天我们要学怎么**问**别人喜欢什么颜色。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project and read aloud. A student volunteer reads the Success Criteria back. Keep visible throughout.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Recap Vocabulary (no new colour words this lesson — pattern-focused)

Quick choral sweep of all six Lesson 1 colours before moving to the new pattern.

#### Key Sentence Patterns

**From Text 2 dialogue (p.100), pattern only — new Text 2 colour vocabulary comes next lesson:**

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| 你喜欢什么颜色？ | What colour do you like? |
| 你喜欢X吗？ | Do you like X? |
| 喜欢。/ 不喜欢。 | Yes. / No. |
| 我也不喜欢。 | I don't like it either. |
| 我也喜欢。 | I like it too. |

**Modelled examples (project each, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**
1. A: 你喜欢什么颜色？ B: 我喜欢红色。
2. A: 你喜欢黑色吗？ B: 不喜欢。
3. A: 我不喜欢黄色。 B: 我也不喜欢。
4. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) A: 你喜欢粉红色的老师吗？("Do you like pink teachers?")

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 你喜欢什么颜色？means 'what colour do you like?'"
- **(Error correction)** Project: 什么颜色你喜欢？— "What's wrong with this sentence?" (word order — 什么颜色 must come after 喜欢, not before)
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the question you'd ask to find out if someone likes green — even though we haven't learned 绿色 yet, just leave a blank for the colour word."

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Chain Drill — We Do (7 min) | 20–27 min

**What:** Whole class in a circle/row chain: Student 1 asks Student 2 "你喜欢什么颜色？"; Student 2 answers with a Lesson-1 colour, then asks Student 3; continues around the room.

**How:** Model the first 2 exchanges yourself with a volunteer. Set a brisk pace — no repeats of the same colour twice in a row.

**OTR:** Every student asks once and answers once as the chain passes through.

- **Scaffold:** Colour flashcards visible on desks for students who need a prompt.
- **Extension:** Once comfortable, add "为什么？" follow-ups are not yet taught — instead extension students must add 也: "我也喜欢红色。"

---

#### Activity 2: Find Someone Who — You Do (10 min) | 27–37 min

**What:** Students mingle with a simple tally sheet (self-drawn: 6 colours down the side). They ask 3 different classmates "你喜欢黑色吗？" (rotating which colour they ask about) and tick Yes/No for each.

**How:**
- Model the mingling format with two volunteers first.
- Students stand, mingle, ask, tally — teacher circulates listening for correct question form.
- Sit down; quick show of hands: "Who found someone who likes 红色?"

**OTR:** All students up and moving, each asks at least 3 questions.

- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame card: 你喜欢____吗？ with colour word bank attached.
- **Extension:** Students ask a 4th question using 什么颜色 (open form) instead of yes/no, and note the answer.

---

#### Game: Slap the Board — Question Version (6 min) | 37–43 min

**How to play:**
1. Six colour flashcards spread on the board (or projected in a grid).
2. Two students stand at the board. Teacher asks a question aloud, e.g. "谁喜欢蓝色？" or simply calls a colour in English.
3. Both students race to slap the matching card; the faster one stays up for the next round.

**What language it practises:** Rapid listening + colour recognition under time pressure.

**Setup needed:** Printed colour flashcards or a projected grid; nothing else.

- **Scaffold:** Keep pinyin under each character on the flashcards.
- **Extension:** The winner must answer a follow-up question in a full sentence before the next round starts, e.g. "你喜欢蓝色吗？" → "喜欢，我喜欢蓝色。"

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud together; ask "Did we achieve this today?"

- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light thumbs (👍/😐/👎) against each of the three criteria.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write a full question-and-answer pair:
  > 你喜欢____吗？ 不喜欢，我喜欢____。
  filling in two different Lesson-1 colours.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学五个新颜色——包括紫色和棕色！"
