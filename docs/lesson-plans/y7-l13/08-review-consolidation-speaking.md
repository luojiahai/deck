# Lesson 13.8: Review & Consolidation — Colours 颜色

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 8 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to bring together everything from this lesson — vocabulary, questions, radicals, and colour mixing — through spoken review before moving to the next topic.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and write all 11 colour words plus 颜色 and 喜欢 (能说、写十一个颜色词、"颜色"和"喜欢")
- I can hold a full spoken exchange combining questions, preferences, and colour+noun descriptions (能进行完整的颜色对话)
- I can read the unit's reading passage and answer comprehension questions (能读懂课文并回答问题)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Rapid cumulative recall of the whole lesson: all 11 colours, the six radicals, and the question/answer pattern.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire through all 11 colours (choral pinyin + English), then one quick whiteboard check: "Write the radical that means 'fire'." Cold call to confirm.

**Connection:** "这是'颜色'这一课的最后一节课。今天我们把学过的东西全部串起来，准备好进入下一课：穿着。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project and read aloud together. A student volunteer reads the Success Criteria back. Keep visible throughout.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Consolidated Recap — Common Errors (no new vocabulary)

Address the recurring mistakes from across the unit with "Correct the Teacher":

1. 我喜欢的黑色。→ (wrong — 的 not needed after 喜欢: 我喜欢黑色。)
2. 什么颜色你喜欢？→ (wrong order: 你喜欢什么颜色？)
3. 火车黑色的。→ (missing structure: 黑色的火车)
4. 粉色 used alone for pink → acceptable colloquially, but this textbook's term is 粉红色 — remind students of the full form.

#### Full Dialogue Model

Project and read aloud a combined dialogue drawing on every pattern from the unit:

> A: 你喜欢什么颜色？
> B: 我喜欢蓝色和绿色。你呢？
> A: 我喜欢黑色和白色，不喜欢紫色。
> B: 我也不喜欢紫色。你喜欢黑色的汽车吗？
> A: 喜欢！

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Cold call)** "Tia, say one full colour preference sentence."
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write one colour-mixing equation from memory."
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 颜色 means 'colour' (the general word)."

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

This final lesson is deliberately speaking- and listening-dominant, giving students maximum oral consolidation before assessment.

#### Activity 1: Full Combined Dialogue — We Do → You Do (10 min) | 20–30 min

**What:** In pairs, students build and perform a dialogue that must include: (1) a "what colour" question, (2) a yes/no question, (3) at least one colour+noun description, (4) at least one use of 也.

**How:**
- Model the combined dialogue above once more with a volunteer, pointing out each of the 4 required elements as they occur.
- Pairs draft their own version (can write key phrases on a whiteboard as a memory aid), rehearse, then perform for another pair.
- Circulate, listening for the 4 required elements; note any pairs needing support.

**OTR:** Every student speaks both roles across the paired performance.

- **Scaffold:** Provide a checklist card listing the 4 required elements with a sentence frame for each.
- **Extension:** Add a 5th element — a colour-mixing statement worked into the conversation naturally (e.g. "我喜欢橙色，因为红色加黄色是橙色。" — note: 因为 "because" may need a quick gloss if not yet formally taught).

---

#### Activity 2: Reading Comprehension — Oral Read + Q&A (8 min) | 30–38 min

**(Based on workbook Ex.23, p.145 — 王黑白 passage)**

**What:** Class reads the workbook's reading passage about 王黑白 (a boy named after his parents' favourite colours) chorally, then answers the 8 comprehension questions as a whole-class oral activity (cold call, not written).

**How:**
- Choral read the passage once as a class (projected).
- Cold call students one at a time for each of the 8 questions (name/age, dad's job, dad's commute, mum's job, dad's colours, mum's colours, does 王黑白 like black, where the family lives).
- Accept English answers for comprehension (Y7 rule), but ask 1–2 fluent speakers to restate their answer in Chinese.

**OTR:** All students read chorally; a wide spread of students answer individual questions.

- **Scaffold:** Allow students to point to the relevant sentence in the text before answering.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers summarise the whole passage in 2–3 Chinese sentences unprompted.

---

#### Game: Cumulative Bingo + Quickfire Quiz (5 min) | 38–43 min

**What:** One final Bingo round using all 11 colours plus 喜欢 and 颜色 (13 items total) as a high-energy consolidation, followed by a 60-second quickfire round: teacher fires colour/vocab questions at random students, no hands up.

**How:** Standard Bingo rules (grid, cross off, shout Bingo, read winning line aloud); then immediately into quickfire mode for the final minutes.

**What language it practises:** Full-unit vocabulary recall under time pressure — the last consolidation push before assessment.

**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only.

- **Scaffold:** Winners read their line with pinyin support if needed.
- **Extension:** Quickfire questions for confident students include full sentence production, not just single words.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project all three from today (and implicitly the whole lesson's journey). Ask: "过去八节课，我们学了什么？" — brief whole-class recap in English is fine here.

- **Self-assessment:** Students rate themselves against the full unit's Success Criteria (name colours, ask/answer preference questions, describe coloured objects, mix colours) using 👍/😐/👎 for each.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write ONE sentence that uses at least two things from this unit (a colour + either 喜欢, 的, or a mixing equation) — this doubles as informal assessment prep.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学新单元：穿着——怎么用中文说衣服！"
