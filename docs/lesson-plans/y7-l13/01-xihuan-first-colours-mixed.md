# Colours (1): 喜欢 and the First Three Colours

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 1 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what colour someone likes.

## Success Criteria
- I can say three colours in Chinese (黑色、白色、黄色)
- I can use 喜欢 to say what someone likes (我爸爸喜欢黑色)
- I can write 喜 and 色 with the correct stroke order

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens, one per student
- Exercise books; coloured pencils
- Printed Slap the Character cards: 喜欢 · 黑色 · 白色 · 黄色 · plus 校车 · 火车 · 地铁 from Lesson 12 (8 cards)
- Workbook pp.135–136 (Ex.2), p.136 (Ex.3)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 12's transport words and 你怎么上学？ — 校车, 火车, 汽车, 出租车, 公共汽车, 地铁, 电车, 走路, 开车.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire — teacher shows the picture, class says the Chinese chorally, two rounds. Then four whiteboard items: teacher asks 你怎么上学？and names a student, who answers in a full sentence (我坐校车上学。); class echoes. Finish with one written item — "Write 'my dad drives to work'."

**Connection:** "You can say *how* your family gets around. Starting today you can say what colour those buses and cars are — and in two lessons we'll put the two together: 黄色的校车."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up all lesson. Teacher reads the Learning Intention aloud in English then Chinese; a student volunteer reads each criterion. Point at the third one: "喜 has twelve strokes. By the end of the lesson you'll write it without looking."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 喜欢（歡） | xǐhuan | like | Smiling face (SVG) |
| 1 | 黑色 | hēisè | black | Solid black swatch |
| 2 | 白色 | báisè | white | White swatch with a thin grey outline |
| 2 | 黄色 | huángsè | yellow | Yellow swatch |

On the 黑色 slide only, add the character clue: **色 = colour, and every colour word ends with it.** 黑 on its own is not "the colour black" — you always need 黑色. Say it once here; it does not go on any other slide.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我**喜欢**我的老师。 — I like my teacher.
2. 我爸爸**喜欢黑色**。 — My dad likes black.
3. 我**喜欢黑色**，我妈妈不**喜欢黑色**。 — I like black, my mum doesn't.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写两个人，一句话：我喜欢黑色，我爸爸不喜欢黑色。Then add a third with 也: 我妈妈也喜欢黑色。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我姐姐喜欢**白色**。 — My older sister likes white.
2. 我妹妹喜欢**黄色**。 — My younger sister likes yellow.
3. 我姐姐喜欢**白色**，我妹妹喜欢**黄色**。 — both words, one sentence.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我 ________ 喜欢 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写三个人，一句话，其中一个人用「不」：我姐姐喜欢白色，我妹妹喜欢黄色，我不喜欢黄色。Cover the board first.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**人 + 喜欢 + 颜色。**

Think aloud: "To say 'my dad likes black' — 我爸爸 · 喜欢 · 黑色. The person first, then 喜欢, then the colour. Nothing goes in between."

1. 我喜欢黑色。
2. 我爸爸喜欢白色。
3. 我妈妈不喜欢黄色。
4. 我哥哥喜欢黑色，我姐姐喜欢白色，我不喜欢白色。 ← three clauses and a negative. This is the one the fluent students copy down.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 色 do at the end of every colour word? Thumbs up if you can tell me."
- "Write 'my dad likes yellow' on your whiteboard."
- "什么不对？ 我黑色喜欢。" — word order, 喜欢 before the colour.

#### Text 1, first half (CD T60, textbook p.96)
> 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色。我妈妈喜欢黄色。

Play T60 — the whole passage runs, so tell students they'll only work on the first two sentences today and the rest next lesson. Choral read twice. Two comprehension questions, English answers accepted: What colour does mum like? How many colours does dad like?

**和 appears here.** Students met it in the family lesson (我家有三口人：爸爸、妈妈和我). Point at it and move on — Lesson 2 is where it gets taught properly.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 我家的颜色 — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
Summary board goes up and stays up: all four new words, characters and colour swatches only, no pinyin.

Students write four sentences about four people (real family or invented), one person per sentence. Between the four sentences **all three colours must appear at least once**, and **one sentence must be negative**.

Model the first together on the board: 我爸爸喜欢黑色。Then 4 minutes writing, teacher circulating. Take three aloud with the summary board still on screen.

**Extension —** Write it as one connected passage instead of four separate sentences, using 也 and 不: 我喜欢黑色，我爸爸也喜欢黑色，我妈妈不喜欢黑色，她喜欢黄色。Then read it to the class with the board covered.

#### Activity 2: Stroke order and draw-the-colour — You Do (9 min) | 27–36
Workbook pp.135–136 Ex.2 — copy 喜 欢 黑 色 白 黄 with stroke order. Model 喜 on the board first: 士 on top, 口, then 丷, then 口 again. Then 黑 — and point at the four dots underneath, 灬, which is 火 (fire) squashed flat. That radical comes back in Lesson 3.

Then workbook p.136 Ex.3, boxes 1, 3 and 4 — draw a quick picture in 黑色, 白色 and 黄色 and write the colour word in characters underneath. Speed over artistry; a ball and a flower are fine.

**Extension —** Write the pinyin with tone marks beside each character from memory, then write one character you already know that also has 灬 underneath — 点, from the time lesson. Say what the two characters have in common.

#### Game: Slap the Character (7 min) | 36–43
Two teams, one player each at the board, eight cards: 喜欢 · 黑色 · 白色 · 黄色 · 校车 · 火车 · 地铁 · 走路. Teacher calls a word in Chinese; first hand on the right card scores. Rotate so everyone plays.

- **Setup needed:** printed cards, blu-tack.
- **Harder version:** Teacher calls a full sentence — 我爸爸喜欢白色 — and the player slaps only the colour. Then reverse it: teacher calls the English, and the player must say the Chinese aloud *before* slapping.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each one aloud from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Translate into Chinese — *My mum likes black.*
- **Preview:** "下一课 — three more colours, the whole of Text 1, and the little word that joins two colours together."
