# Lesson 13.4: Describing Objects — 颜色 + 的 + Noun

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe objects using colour + 的 + noun (e.g. 黑色的火车), recycling the transport vocabulary from Lesson 12.

## Success Criteria
- I can describe an object's colour using X色的Y (能用"颜色+的+名词"描述东西)
- I can say colour + vehicle combinations for all 7 transport words from Lesson 12 (能说出七种"颜色+交通工具"组合)
- I can understand a spoken description and pick the matching coloured picture (能听懂并选出正确的颜色图片)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- 7 transport pictures in different colours (double-decker bus, plane, trolley bus, car, school bus, taxi, subway train — from textbook p.97 Ex.1)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** All 11 colours learned so far (Lessons 1 + 3) and the 7 transport words from Lesson 12 (火车, 汽车, 飞机, 出租车, 公共汽车, 校车, 地铁).

**How:** Quick whiteboard quiz — teacher projects 4 transport pictures and 4 colour swatches alternately; students write the Chinese word for each. Cold call to check answers chorally.

**Connection:** "上节课我们学了颜色，上上节课我们学了交通工具。今天我们把它们放在一起：颜色 + 的 + 交通工具，比如'黑色的火车'。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project and read aloud together. Keep visible throughout.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Key Sentence Pattern (no new vocabulary — grammar-focused)

**Structure:** X色的Y — "the Y that is X-coloured"

**Modelled examples (project each, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**
1. 黑色的火车 (from textbook p.97 example)
2. 蓝色的汽车
3. 黄色的校车
4. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 粉红色的出租车会飞！("The pink taxi can fly!")

#### Textbook Exercise 1 Model (p.97)

Project the 7 transport pictures (each drawn in a specific colour): double-decker bus (pink/red), plane (white), trolley bus (blue), car (black), school bus (yellow), taxi (red), subway train (blue). Model 2–3 examples using the pattern, then have the class say the remaining ones chorally as you point to each.

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Error correction)** Project: 火车黑色 — "What's wrong with this?" (missing 的; wrong order would be 黑色火车的)
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write 'the yellow school bus' in Chinese."
- **(Cold call)** "Leo, how do you say 'the blue car'?"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Picture Description Round — We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min

**What:** Teacher projects one coloured transport picture at a time (all 7 from Ex.1); whole class chorally names it, then individual cold calls repeat it alone.

**How:** Go through all 7 pictures twice — first chorally, then popcorn-style individual cold calls (no hands up) to build confidence before pair work.

**OTR:** Every student says at least 2 items chorally; several individual cold calls sample the room.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the transport vocabulary list (with pinyin) projected in a corner.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers add "我喜欢/我不喜欢" in front of their description: 我喜欢黑色的火车。

---

#### Activity 2: Partner Speaking — Describe & Guess — You Do (10 min) | 28–38 min

**What:** In pairs, Student A is given a card with a coloured object (from the 7 transport items, or extended to family colour preferences from Text 1/2) and describes it using X色的Y without showing the card; Student B guesses which picture it is from a shared set of 7 pictures on the desk.

**How:**
- Model the guessing game with a volunteer first.
- Pairs work through all 7 cards, swapping the "describer" role halfway.
- Whole-class check: cold call 2 pairs to demonstrate.

**OTR:** All students describe at least 3 items and guess at least 3 items.

- **Scaffold:** Provide the full colour + transport word bank on a shared card.
- **Extension:** Describer must add one extra detail, e.g. 这是黑色的，很大 ("It's black and very big" — using known adjective 大 from earlier units).

---

#### Activity 3: Listening — Textbook Ex.4 Adapted (5 min) | 38–43 min

**(Based on p.98 Ex.4, CD T62 — originally family-colour matching, adapted here to reinforce listening for colour + noun)**

**What:** Teacher reads out 4–5 short sentences combining a family member and a colour + noun (e.g. "爸爸喜欢黑色的汽车。"); students tick the matching picture from a grid of coloured vehicle options.

**How:** Read each sentence twice at natural speed. Students mark their answer sheet (or mini whiteboard grid). Reveal answers on the projector; students self-correct.

**OTR:** Every student listens and marks all 5 items.

- **Scaffold:** Slow down and repeat a third time for any sentence with a show of confused faces.
- **Extension:** After marking, students write out the full sentence they heard from memory.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud; ask "Did we achieve this today?"

- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍/😐/👎 against each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write one sentence describing a coloured vehicle of their choice, e.g. 我喜欢红色的出租车。

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学新的偏旁部首，还要学怎么把两个颜色混在一起变成新颜色！"
