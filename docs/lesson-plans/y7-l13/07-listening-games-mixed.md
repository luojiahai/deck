# Lesson 13.7: Listening Practice + Colour Games

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to identify colours by listening and to recall colour vocabulary quickly through games.

## Success Criteria
- I can listen to a short description and tick the correct colour (能听懂并选出正确的颜色)
- I can name the colour of a real-world object in Chinese (能用中文说出物品的颜色)
- I can recall and spell colour words under time pressure (能快速回忆并拼写颜色词)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (for listening audio/script and visuals)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- A few real or pictured everyday objects (panda, banana, grass, fire truck, etc. — for the naming game)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 6's survey reporting pattern ([number]个人喜欢[colour]).

**How:** Quick pair recall — students tell their partner one real result from last lesson's survey using the full sentence. Cold call 2 pairs to share with the class.

**Connection:** "上节课我们做了调查、报告了结果。今天我们要练习**听**颜色，还要玩几个好玩的游戏来复习所有颜色。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project and read aloud together. Keep visible throughout.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Modelled Listening Example

Teacher reads one sample sentence aloud twice at natural speed: "小月喜欢红色，不喜欢黄色。" Ask: "小月喜欢什么颜色？不喜欢什么颜色？" — establish the listening task format before the independent activity.

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Listen and write)** "Write the colour you hear: 蓝色。" (say once, students write)
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if you're ready to listen to 6 people talk about their favourite colours."
- **(Cold call)** "Explain in one sentence what we're about to do."

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

This lesson mixes listening, a speaking/naming game, and a writing-recall game.

#### Activity 1: Listen and Tick — We Do → You Do (12 min) | 20–32 min

**(Based on textbook Ex.10, p.103, CD T64)**

**What:** Six people (大生, 小文, 小月, 京京, 王星, 王小明) each say which colour(s) they like from 3 pictured options; students tick the correct colour for each person.

**How:**
- Project the 6 name-and-3-colour-option grids.
- Read each person's sentence twice at natural pace (e.g. "大生喜欢绿色。"); students tick their answer sheet or mini whiteboard grid.
- After all 6, reveal answers on the projector; students self-correct and note any they missed.

**OTR:** Every student listens and marks all 6 items independently.

- **Scaffold:** Slow down and repeat a third time for any item with visible confusion; keep the colour word list projected.
- **Extension:** After marking, students write the full sentence they heard from memory for 2 of the 6 people.

---

#### Activity 2: Object → Colour Naming Game — You Do (10 min) | 32–42 min

**(Based on textbook Ex.9, p.102)**

**What:** Teacher says an everyday object in English (e.g. "panda," "banana," "grass," "fire truck," "the sky"); the student(s) chosen say its colour(s) in Chinese (e.g. panda → 黑色、白色).

**How:**
- Model with the textbook's own example: Teacher says "panda"; Student 1 says 黑色; Student 2 says 白色.
- Go around the room calling on different students for different objects (10–12 objects total, mixing single-colour and two-colour answers).
- Keep pace brisk — this should feel energetic, not slow and effortful.

**OTR:** Most of the class gets at least one turn; students not currently answering listen and mentally rehearse.

- **Scaffold:** Allow a partner "lifeline" — if stuck, the student may ask a neighbour quietly before answering.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers must answer in a full sentence: 熊猫是黑色和白色的。

---

#### Game: Hangman (老师说) (4 min) | 42–46 min

**(Based on textbook Ex.11, p.103 — adjust timing slightly to fit within the 23-minute block)**

**How to play:**
1. A student comes to the board and silently chooses a colour word or phrase from the unit (e.g. 紫色, 我喜欢, 颜色).
2. They draw dashes for each pinyin syllable on the board (e.g. h_o_n_g / s_e for 红色).
3. Class members take turns guessing letters; correct letters are filled in, incorrect ones add a line to the hangman drawing.
4. Game ends when the word is guessed or the hangman figure is complete.

**What language it practises:** Spelling/pinyin recall and vocabulary retrieval under gentle pressure.

**Setup needed:** Whiteboard/projector only.

- **Scaffold:** Restrict word choices to single colour words (not phrases) for early rounds.
- **Extension:** Once guessed, the class must use the word in a full sentence together, chorally.

---

### Plenary (46–50 min)

*(Plenary compressed slightly to 4 min to accommodate the listening + two games above; keep it brisk.)*

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Quick choral read; thumbs up/down check.

- **Self-assessment:** One quick round — students hold up 1, 2, or 3 fingers for overall confidence today.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 1 min):** Write the colour of one object the teacher names on the spot.

- **Preview:** "下一课是这个单元的总复习——我们会玩最后的大游戏，准备好迎接下一课：穿着（衣服）！"
