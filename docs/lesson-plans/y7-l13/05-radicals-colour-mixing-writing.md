# Lesson 13.5: Radicals + Mixing Colours (X色+Y色=Z色)

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning six new radicals and how to describe mixing two colours to make a new one.

## Success Criteria
- I can name and recognise the six new radicals: 疒, 火, 爫, 弓, 力, 礻 (能认六个新偏旁部首)
- I can complete a colour-mixing sentence using X色+Y色=Z色 (能完成颜色混合句子)
- I can write all 11 core colour characters correctly (能正确写出十一个颜色汉字)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Coloured pencils/crayons (red, yellow, blue, black, white at minimum)
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 4's colour + 的 + noun pattern with transport words.

**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards — teacher says an English colour + vehicle combo (e.g. "the green plane"); students write the Chinese. 4 items, cold call to check.

**Connection:** "上节课我们把颜色和东西放在一起说。今天我们学新的偏旁部首，还要学一个有趣的问题——颜色可以'混合'吗？"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project and read aloud together. Keep visible throughout.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Radicals (p.99, Exercise 5)

Display each radical with its meaning and an example character containing it.

| 部首 | Meaning | Example character |
|---|---|---|
| 疒 | disease | 病 |
| 火 | fire | 炒 |
| 爫 | claw | 爱 |
| 弓 | bow | 弹 |
| 力 | strength | 加 |
| 礻 | ritual | 视 |

Model: "Notice 病 (illness) has 疒 — the 'disease' radical wraps around the character, like a sick person lying down." Point out 力 (strength) appears in 加 (add) — "you need strength to add things up!"

#### Key Sentence Pattern: Colour Mixing (p.102, Exercise 8)

**Structure:** X色 + Y色 = Z色

**Modelled examples (project each, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**
1. 红色 + 黄色 = 橙色 (given as the worked example in the textbook)
2. 白色 + 黑色 = 灰色
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 如果你把所有颜色都混在一起呢？("What if you mix ALL the colours together?")

*New bonus word from the textbook's answer key:* 天蓝色 (tiānlánsè) — "sky blue", which appears as one of the mixing answers (紫色+黄色 mixes toward a muddy tone, but 天蓝色 appears for one of the blue-based mixes). Introduce briefly as an extension word, not core vocabulary.

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 疒 is the fire radical." (it's not — 火 is fire; 疒 is disease)
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the radical that means 'strength'."
- **(Cold call)** "Zoe, what colour do you get from 红色+黄色?"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Radical Copying — We Do (7 min) | 20–27 min

**(Based on workbook Ex.1 and Ex.9, pp.134, 138)**

**What:** Students copy each of the six radicals following the stroke sequence shown, then match each radical to the correct character from a mixed list (workbook Ex.9: 炒, 爱, 病, 弹, 加, 视).

**How:** Project stroke order for each radical; students trace then copy 3 times. Then complete the matching exercise independently.

**OTR:** Every student copies all 6 radicals and completes the matching task; teacher circulates to check.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the radical-meaning table projected throughout.
- **Extension:** Students find one additional character (from any previous unit) that contains each radical, if they can recall one.

---

#### Activity 2: Colour Mixing Worksheet — You Do (10 min) | 27–37 min

**(Based on textbook Ex.8, p.102 and workbook Ex.17, p.142)**

**What:** Students complete the 7 remaining colour-mixing equations in writing (white+black, blue+yellow, white+blue, purple+yellow, red+blue, white+red, red+yellow+blue), writing the answer in characters, and for the worksheet version, physically colour in the mixed result using crayons.

**How:**
- Students work independently on the character-writing side.
- For 2–3 of the equations, they also mix the actual crayon colours in a small box to check their answer visually (cross-curricular fun, low stakes).
- Cold call to check answers as a class using the projected answer key.

**OTR:** All students complete all 7 equations in characters.

- **Scaffold:** Colour word bank with pinyin available throughout.
- **Extension:** Students invent one additional mixing equation of their own and write it in characters (does not need to be scientifically accurate).

---

#### Activity 3: Character Stroke Count — Consolidation Writing (6 min) | 37–43 min

**(Based on workbook Ex.6, p.137)**

**What:** Students write out and count the strokes for 8 of today's/previous colour characters (粉, 色, 黑, 黄, 红, 白, 蓝, 喜).

**How:** Students work individually; partner-check stroke counts against a projected answer key.

**OTR:** All students complete all 8 items.

- **Scaffold:** Allow students to trace over a light-grey printed version of tricky characters (喜, 蓝, 黑) before counting strokes independently.
- **Extension:** Students order all 8 characters from fewest to most strokes.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud; ask "Did we achieve this today?"

- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍/😐/👎 against each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write one colour-mixing equation from memory (not one already used in class), e.g. 蓝色+白色=？

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要在班上做一个调查——看看有多少同学喜欢每一个颜色！"
