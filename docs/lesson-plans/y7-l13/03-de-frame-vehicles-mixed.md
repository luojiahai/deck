# Colours (3): 颜色 + 的 + noun

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 3 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe things by their colour, using 的.

## Success Criteria
- I can put a colour in front of a noun with 的 (黑色的火车)
- I can translate colour-and-object phrases from English into Chinese
- I can write the radicals 疒, 火 and 爫, and name a character that uses each

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books
- Printed Sentence Jumble card sets, one per group of four (see the game)
- Workbook p.134 (Ex.1), p.138 (Ex.9 items 1–3), p.139 (Ex.10)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** All six colours from Lessons 1–2, plus Unit 4's vehicles — both halves of what today's sentence needs.

**How:** Two quick rounds on whiteboards. Round one: teacher says a colour in English, students write the Chinese — six items, held up together. Round two: teacher shows a vehicle picture, students write the Chinese — 校车, 火车, 出租车, 公共汽车, 地铁, 电车.

**Connection:** "You've got the colours on one side and the vehicles on the other. Today you join them — and there's a small word in the middle that decides whether the sentence is right or wrong."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Write 黑色 ___ 火车 on the board with a gap, and leave it there while a student reads the criteria out. Fill the gap at the start of I Do.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### No new vocabulary today
Everything in today's sentences has already been taught. The new thing is the frame, so the whole I Do goes on that — and the radicals are taught inside the practice block.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**颜色 + 的 + noun**

Think aloud: "To say 'a black train' — Chinese says the colour first, then a little 的, then the thing: 黑色 · 的 · 火车. English has nothing in the middle. Chinese has to have 的. Leave it out and it's wrong."

1. 黑色的火车 — a black train
2. 黄色的校车 — a yellow school bus
3. 红色的出租车 — a red taxi
4. 我爸爸喜欢白色的汽车，我妈妈每天坐蓝色的电车上班。 ← two clauses, two 的 phrases, and the whole of Unit 4's transport frame carried inside it.

**Write this on the board and leave it up all lesson:**
> 颜色 + 的 + 东西 ✓ 　　颜色 + 东西 ✗

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Where does 的 go — before or after the colour? Point at the board."
- "Write 'the yellow school bus'." (whiteboards — this is the item most students get wrong)
- "什么不对？ 火车黑色的。" — the colour goes first.
- "How would I say 'my dad likes the white taxi'?"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Seven vehicles, six colours — We Do (8 min) | 20–28
**Textbook p.97 Ex.1.** Seven pictures of vehicles, each in a colour. Whole class first: teacher points, class says the phrase chorally — 黑色的火车. Then pairs: one points, the other says it, then swap.

Then into books — write all seven phrases, and the instruction is that **all six colours must appear at least once** across the seven. Students choose which vehicle gets which colour where the picture leaves it open.

**Extension —** Turn each phrase into a full sentence with a person and a verb from Unit 4, and write four of them: 我弟弟每天坐黄色的校车上学。我妈妈坐蓝色的地铁上班。Then say all four aloud with the board covered.

#### Activity 2: Radicals, then translation — You Do (8 min) | 28–36
The three radicals go up on their own slide first, each with its example character — **疒 disease (病) · 火 fire (炒) · 爫 claw (爱)** — because Unit Test Parts 2 and 3 ask for radical → meaning and radical → character, and the workbook never shows them next to their example characters.

One board moment before they write: 黑, which they copied in Lesson 1, has 灬 underneath. That is 火 flattened out. Students find the 火 in 炒 and the 灬 in 黑 in their own books before copying anything.

Then **workbook p.134 Ex.1** — copy 疒, 火, 爫 with stroke order — and **p.138 Ex.9 items 1–3** — write the radical of 炒, 爱, 病.

Then **workbook p.139 Ex.10** — translate six phrases into Chinese. The first is given: black taxi 黑色的出租车. The remaining five: blue car, red public bus, yellow school bus, pink watch, white bed. Put 手表 and 床 on the board as recognition words for items 5 and 6 — students are being asked for the colour and the 的, not for two new nouns.

**Extension —** Write four more colour + 的 + noun phrases using any noun from Book 1, then turn two of them into sentences with a person and a time: 我姐姐七点坐红色的公共汽车上学。

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (7 min) | 36–43
Groups of four, each with a set of cards:
> 我弟弟 · 每天 · 坐 · 黄色的 · 校车 · 上学

Teams race to lay them out in the right order, hands up when done, teacher checks. Three rounds with different sets (我妈妈 / 喜欢 / 白色的 / 汽车 — and one with 出租车 and 红色的).

- **Setup needed:** printed card sets, one per group. Cut them up before the lesson.
- **Harder version:** In the fourth round, remove the 的 card from one team's set without telling them. They have to notice it's missing, say where it belongs and why, then build the longest correct sentence they can from a shared pool of leftover cards in 90 seconds.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Two items — translate *the red taxi*, then translate *My mum likes the white car.*
- **Preview:** "下一课 — how to ask someone 你喜欢什么颜色？and four more colours to answer with."
