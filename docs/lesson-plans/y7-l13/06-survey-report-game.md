# Lesson 13.6: Class Survey & Reporting Results

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 6 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to survey classmates about their favourite colours and report the results using numbers.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 11 yes/no colour questions in a survey (能问十一个"你喜欢……吗？"的调查问题)
- I can tally responses correctly (能正确记录调查结果)
- I can report results using "几个人喜欢……" (能用"几个人喜欢……"报告结果)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Survey tally sheets (one per student, pre-drawn with the 11 colours down the side — or students copy the grid from the board)
- Mini whiteboards + markers

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Colour-mixing equations from Lesson 5 (红色+黄色=橙色, etc.) and quick recall of all 11 colours.

**How:** Mini whiteboard quiz — teacher gives 4 colour-mixing prompts; students write the answer in characters. Cold call to check.

**Connection:** "上节课我们学会了怎么把颜色混在一起。今天我们要做一个调查——问全班同学喜欢什么颜色，然后把结果告诉大家。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project and read aloud together. Keep visible throughout.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Survey Questions Recap (p.101, Exercise 7 — all 11 colours)

Project the full list of 11 yes/no questions in a tally-sheet format:

| Questions |
|---|
| 1) 你喜欢黑色吗？ |
| 2) 你喜欢白色吗？ |
| 3) 你喜欢黄色吗？ |
| 4) 你喜欢蓝色吗？ |
| 5) 你喜欢红色吗？ |
| 6) 你喜欢粉红色吗？ |
| 7) 你喜欢橙色吗？ |
| 8) 你喜欢紫色吗？ |
| 9) 你喜欢棕色吗？ |
| 10) 你喜欢绿色吗？ |
| 11) 你喜欢灰色吗？ |

#### Key Sentence Pattern: Reporting Results

**Structure:** [Number]个人喜欢[colour]。

**Modelled example (project, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**
> 五个人喜欢黑色。……(textbook's own example, p.101)

Model reading out a short mock result set: "十个人喜欢红色，八个人喜欢蓝色，两个人喜欢灰色。" — point out the numbers come first, then 个人喜欢, then the colour.

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Gesture)** "Thumbs up if 几个人喜欢红色？ means 'how many people like red?'"
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write: 'Three people like green.'"
- **(Cold call)** "Ryan, how would you report that 6 people like yellow?"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Model Survey Round — We Do (5 min) | 20–25 min

**What:** Teacher asks the whole class 3 of the 11 questions as a show-of-hands poll (e.g. 黑色, 紫色, 绿色); a volunteer tallies on the board as hands go up. Teacher models reporting: "十二个人喜欢黑色。"

**How:** Quick, high-energy warm-up to the full activity — establishes the tally + report format before students do it in pairs/small groups.

**OTR:** Every student votes with a hand-raise for all 3 questions.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the report sentence frame (____个人喜欢____。) projected throughout.
- **Extension:** Volunteer tallier also calculates and reports the most popular colour of the 3.

---

#### Activity 2: Find Someone Who — Full Survey — You Do (13 min) | 25–38 min

**(Based on textbook Ex.7, p.101)**

**What:** Students mingle and ask 6–8 classmates all 11 yes/no colour questions (or a teacher-set subset if time is tight), tallying responses on their sheet using 正-character tally marks (as modelled in the textbook).

**How:**
- Students stand, move around the room, ask each question to different classmates, and mark Yes/No tallies.
- Set a clear time limit (10 min) and a minimum number of classmates to survey (6).
- Return to seats; in pairs, students total their tallies for 2–3 colours each.

**OTR:** All students are up, moving, and asking — this is the lesson's primary speaking OTR.

- **Scaffold:** Provide a partially-filled example tally sheet as a model; allow English clarification if a student is confused about a question.
- **Extension:** Students also ask an open 你喜欢什么颜色？(non yes/no) to 2 classmates and record the free-text answer.

---

#### Game: Class Results Countdown (5 min) | 38–43 min

**How to play:**
1. Teacher calls out each of the 11 colours one at a time.
2. For each colour, students who tallied data for it race to be first to shout out their total using the full sentence: "十个人喜欢粉红色！"
3. First correct full-sentence answer for each colour "wins" that round; teacher tracks a running score on the board.

**What language it practises:** Fast retrieval and production of the reporting sentence pattern under time pressure.

**Setup needed:** Nothing beyond students' own tally sheets.

- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame remains projected for students who need it.
- **Extension:** Winning students must also say which colour was least popular overall from their own data.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud; ask "Did we achieve this today?"

- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍/😐/👎 against each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write one report sentence using their own real survey data, e.g. 七个人喜欢蓝色。

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要听录音选颜色，还要玩几个新游戏！"
