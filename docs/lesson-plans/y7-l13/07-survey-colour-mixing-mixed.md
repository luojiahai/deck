# Colours (7): The Class Survey, Mixing Colours, and 天蓝色

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to survey the class about colours and report what we found.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 你喜欢X色吗？and record the answers
- I can report a result: 五个人喜欢黑色。
- I can say what two colours make (蓝色和白色是天蓝色)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books; coloured pencils
- Printed survey tally sheets, one per student (eleven colours down the left, space for ticks)
- Workbook p.140 (Ex.14), p.142 (Ex.17)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The 吗 question and its verb-repeat answer, from Lesson 5 — the exact form the survey runs on.

**How:** Chain around the room. Teacher starts: 你喜欢绿色吗？ First student answers 喜欢。or 不喜欢。then turns to the next with a different colour. Two laps, picking up speed. Anyone who answers 是 hands the question on and tries again next lap.

Then one whiteboard item: *I don't like brown either.*

**Connection:** "That question is the whole lesson today. You're going to ask it about eleven colours, to everyone in this room, and then tell us what you found out."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Write 五个人喜欢黑色。on the board next to 我家有三口人。 and point at the difference: "口 is only for family. For everybody else it's 个."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 1 word, one short cycle
One new word today, so the cycle runs across two slides rather than three — the word, then the sentences and the write-your-own together.

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 天蓝色（藍） | tiānlánsè | sky blue | Pale sky-blue swatch with a small cloud |

Not in the New Words box — it's the answer to one of the colour-mixing sums, and Lesson 14 uses it on its very first page (天蓝色的衬衫). 天 is already known from 今天 and 明天, and 蓝色 from Lesson 2, so nothing here is new except the combination.

**Example sentences:**
1. 蓝色和白色是**天蓝色**。 — Blue and white make sky blue.
2. 我喜欢**天蓝色**的汽车。 — I like sky-blue cars.

**Write your own:** 我喜欢天蓝色的 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write a sum with three colours in it: 红色、黄色和蓝色是棕色。Then say which of the two blues you prefer and why, in Chinese: 我喜欢天蓝色，我不喜欢蓝色。

#### Key Sentence Patterns
**Asking and tallying:** 你喜欢X色吗？→ 喜欢。／不喜欢。
**Reporting:** 数字 + 个人 + 喜欢 + 颜色。
**Mixing:** X色和Y色是Z色。

1. 你喜欢绿色吗？— 喜欢。
2. 五个人喜欢黑色。
3. 三个人不喜欢灰色。
4. 十个人喜欢蓝色，两个人喜欢天蓝色，我也喜欢天蓝色。 ← two counts, a contrast between the two blues, and 也 attaching the speaker to the smaller group.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "口 or 个 — which one for this class? Why?"
- "How do you say 'five people like black'?" (whiteboards)
- "红色和黄色是什么颜色？" — answer in Chinese.
- "什么不对？ 五口人喜欢红色。"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Mixing the colours — We Do (5 min) | 20–25
Summary board up and staying up: all twelve colours including 天蓝色, characters and swatches.

**Textbook p.102 Ex.8 and workbook p.142 Ex.17** — seven sums, written into books and said aloud as full sentences. The first is given:
> 白色和黑色是灰色。· 蓝色 + 白色 · 红色 + 黄色 · 红色 + 黄色 + 蓝色 · 白色 + 红色 · 黄色 + 蓝色 · 紫色 + 黄色

Between them these use almost every colour in the lesson, which is the point — students who only remember four are found out here rather than on the test.

**Extension —** Invent three sums of your own and write each as a sentence with a 的 phrase attached: 蓝色和黄色是绿色，我喜欢绿色的校车。Then quiz a partner orally with the board covered — you say two colours, they say the third.

#### Activity 2: The class survey — You Do (9 min) | 25–34
**Textbook p.101 Ex.7.** Every student gets a tally sheet with the eleven colours down the left. They circulate and ask 你喜欢X色吗？, ticking as they go. Five minutes of circulation — teacher moves with them and listens for the answer form, not the question.

Then back to seats, count the columns, and write three findings as full sentences: 五个人喜欢黑色。 Take four or five aloud and build a class tally on the board.

**Extension —** Report as a comparison rather than a count: 五个人喜欢黑色，两个人不喜欢黑色，我也喜欢黑色。Then work out the most and least popular colour in the room and announce both in Chinese. Give the interviewer role in mixed pairs to the fluent students — they ask, their partner records.

#### Activity 3: Reading — 王红 (5 min) | 34–39
**Workbook p.140 Ex.14.** A short passage: 王红 introduces her family of three, her father the lawyer, her mother who doesn't work, and everyone's favourite colours. Read aloud together, then the four questions answered orally in pairs, two of them written.

The passage contains 都 (爸爸和妈妈都喜欢黑色) — gloss it on the board as "both / all" and move on. It's not a word from this lesson and it doesn't need teaching, only understanding.

**Extension —** Answer all four in full sentences rather than short answers, then add a fifth question of your own about the passage and put it to a partner.

#### Game: Hangman (4 min) | 39–43
**(Textbook p.103 Ex.11)**

Teacher draws the blanks for a pinyin phrase — hóng sè, xǐ huan, fàng xué — and the class guesses letters. Wrong letters build the figure. On a win the class says the word and its meaning together.

- **Setup needed:** nothing.
- **Harder version:** The guesser must supply the tone mark along with the vowel to score it, and whoever solves the word has to use it in a full sentence before the next round starts.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Two items — 红色和黄色是什么颜色？ and one result from your survey, written as a full sentence.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — everything from Lesson 13 in one lesson, and a reading about a boy whose parents named him after two colours."
