# Colours (5): Text 2 — 你喜欢灰色吗？and 我也不喜欢

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask whether someone likes a colour, answer no, and agree with them.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 绿色 and 灰色
- I can answer 你喜欢灰色吗？with 喜欢。or 不喜欢。
- I can agree with someone using 我也不喜欢

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD T63 (Text 2), T64 (p.103 Ex.10)
- Workbook p.141 (Ex.15, 绿 and 灰), p.143 (Ex.19 Q1–4)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 你喜欢什么颜色？and the ten colours — with the question form under pressure this time.

**How:** Pair dialogue from memory, 90 seconds: A asks 你喜欢什么颜色？, B answers with two colours and 和, then swap, then swap partners and do it again. Teacher circulates and listens for 什么 landing in the right place. Then two whiteboard items: write the question for "What colour does your mum like?" and answer it about your own mum.

**Connection:** "Right now you can only ask an open question. Today you learn to ask about one specific colour — 你喜欢灰色吗？ — and the answer to that one is not 是."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. On the second criterion, say it out loud twice: "The answer repeats the verb. 喜欢。or 不喜欢。Never 是。" This is the single thing most likely to go wrong today.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 绿色（綠） | lǜsè | green | Green swatch |
| 1 | 灰色 | huīsè | grey | Grey swatch |

On the 绿色 slide, drill the vowel on its own before the word: **lǜ**, not "loo". Same ü as in 女. Say it three times, class repeats three times. The tone is fine; the vowel is what slips.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢**绿色**。 — I like green.
2. 我不喜欢**灰色**。 — I don't like grey.
3. 我哥哥喜欢**绿色**，他不喜欢**灰色**。 — both words, one sentence.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢 ________ ，我不喜欢 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write two sentences using 也 to agree with someone: 我不喜欢灰色，我姐姐也不喜欢灰色。Then a third with 和 that gives one person two colours.

#### Recall slide
All eleven colours, characters only, no pinyin: **黑色 白色 黄色 蓝色 红色 粉红色 橙色 紫色 棕色 绿色 灰色**. Class reads them back in order, then the teacher points at random. This is the full set — everything from here on recycles rather than adds.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**你喜欢X色吗？→ 喜欢。／不喜欢。 · 我也不喜欢。**

Think aloud: "You've used 吗 before, for 你爸爸工作吗？ Same 吗 here. But look at the answer — it doesn't say 是. It picks up the verb from the question and says it back: 喜欢。Or puts 不 in front: 不喜欢。"

1. 你喜欢灰色吗？— 不喜欢。
2. 你喜欢绿色吗？— 喜欢。
3. 我不喜欢灰色。— 我也不喜欢。
4. 你妈妈喜欢棕色吗？— 不喜欢，她喜欢紫色和绿色，我也喜欢紫色。 ← a negative answer, a correction with two colours, and 也 agreeing with only part of it.

**Board note, left up all lesson:** 我**也**不喜欢。✓ 　我不喜欢**也**。✗ — 也 goes in front of the verb. (This is workbook Ex.20 #1, which the class meets on paper next lesson.)

#### Text 2 (CD T63, textbook p.100)
> A: 你喜欢什么颜色？
> B: 我喜欢橙色、紫色、棕色和绿色。
> A: 你喜欢灰色吗？
> B: 不喜欢。
> A: 我也不喜欢。

Play T63 twice, students following. Choral read, then pairs perform it, then swap roles.

Point at B's first line: four colours in a list. The small marks between them are 、 — the Chinese list comma — and only the last join uses 和. This is the first list of four students have seen.

Comprehension: How many colours does B like? Who doesn't like grey? Does A like orange, or don't we know?

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Text 2, your own colours — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
Summary board up and staying up: all eleven colours, characters and swatches.

Open with the three items left over from **textbook p.98 Ex.3** — the grass and the grey ones, which had no words for them last lesson. 60 seconds, chorally, and that exercise is finished.

Then pairs run Text 2 with their own colours substituted — four colours in A's list using 、 and 和, then a 吗 question about a fifth, then the agreement line. Then they swap roles and run it again with different colours. The instruction: **between the two of you, across both runs, all eleven colours must be said at least once.** They will have to plan it, which is the point.

Take two pairs performing in front of the class with the summary board still up.

**Extension —** Run it as a three-turn conversation instead of two. Each person adds a 也 or a 不, and on the last turn one partner reports the other to the class in the third person: 他喜欢橙色和绿色，他不喜欢灰色，我也不喜欢灰色。No script, no board.

#### Activity 2: Listening, then written answers — You Do (9 min) | 27–36
**Textbook p.103 Ex.10 (CD T64)** — tick which colours each of six people likes. Play twice. Check as a class, item by item.

Then **workbook p.143 Ex.19, questions 1–4**, answered in writing in full sentences: 你喜欢紫色吗？你喜欢什么颜色？你爸爸喜欢什么颜色？你妈妈喜欢什么颜色？ Question 1 is the 吗 form — watch for anyone answering 是.

Then finish **workbook p.141 Ex.15** with 绿 and 灰, stroke order. Anyone who gets that far takes Ex.19 questions 5 and 6 as well — 你每天怎么上学？ and 你晚上几点睡觉？ — which are Unit 4 and should now be automatic.

**Extension —** After the listening, write three sentences about what you heard using 也: 王月喜欢红色，小文也喜欢红色。Then write one sentence about someone in the recording who agrees with *you*.

#### Game: Colour Bingo (7 min) | 36–43
**(Textbook p.99 Ex.6)**

Students draw a 4×4 grid on a mini whiteboard and fill it, in any order, with sixteen words from the board: the eleven colours, 喜欢, 颜色, and 校车, 火车, 地铁 from Lesson 12. Teacher calls words in Chinese; students cross them off. First to a full line calls 有了！and reads the line back aloud in Chinese to confirm.

- **Setup needed:** mini whiteboards and the sixteen words on the projector while grids are drawn.
- **Harder version:** The teacher calls full sentences — 我姐姐喜欢粉红色 — and students cross off only the colour inside. The winner has to read back not the line but a sentence for each word in it.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each aloud.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one at a time. Linger on the second one.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Translate into Chinese — *I don't like grey.* — *I don't like it either.*
- **Preview:** "下一课 — characters. Where the radical hides inside a character, and how to hear a word and write its tone."
