# Lesson 13.1: Core Colours 1 (黑白黄蓝红粉) + Text 1

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing & Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 — Colours 颜色 | Lesson 1 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name six core colours in Chinese and say what colour someone likes using 喜欢.

## Success Criteria
- I can say six colours in Chinese: 黑色, 白色, 黄色, 蓝色, 红色, 粉红色 (能说六个颜色)
- I can say "I/someone likes ___ colour" using 喜欢 (能用"喜欢"说出喜欢的颜色)
- I can read the Text 1 sentences about my family's favourite colours (能读懂课文一)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens
- Coloured pencils/crayons (for the drawing activity)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 12's transport vocabulary and 你怎么上学？pattern (校车, 走路, 开车, 坐公共汽车, 坐地铁, etc.) — this is the last lesson students had, and it will resurface later this week when colours combine with vehicles.

**How:** Project 5 transport pictures one at a time; students write the Chinese on mini whiteboards and hold up on three ("怎么上学？" prompt under each picture). Cold call one student per picture to say the full sentence aloud (e.g. 我坐校车上学。); class echoes chorally.

**Connection:** "上节课我们学了交通工具——怎么上学、怎么上班。今天我们开始一个新单元：颜色。以后我们会把颜色和交通工具放在一起说，比如'黑色的出租车'。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and all three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the Learning Intention aloud in English then Chinese; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion. Keep these visible in a corner of the screen for the rest of the lesson.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary

Display each row on the projector: 汉字 → pinyin → English. Keep pinyin visible throughout (Y7 rule). Teacher says the word twice; class repeats twice. After every four words, do one quick choral sweep of all words so far.

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 喜 | xǐ | happy; like |
| 欢(歡) | huān | happy |
| 喜欢 | xǐhuan | like |
| 黑 | hēi | black |
| 色 | sè | colour |
| 黑色 | hēisè | black |
| 白 | bái | white |
| 白色 | báisè | white |
| 黄 | huáng | yellow |
| 黄色 | huángsè | yellow |
| 蓝(藍) | lán | blue |
| 蓝色 | lánsè | blue |
| 红(紅) | hóng | red |
| 红色 | hóngsè | red |
| 粉 | fěn | powder; pink |
| 粉红色 | fěnhóngsè | pink |

*Point out:* 色 (colour) attaches to every colour word — 黑, 白, 黄, 蓝, 红 are never used alone to mean "the colour X"; you always need 色 (黑色, not 黑). Pink is 粉红色 (powder-red), not just 粉色, in this textbook.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**Structure:** 我(的X)喜欢Y颜色。— 喜欢 is a verb that takes the colour directly as its object, no "of" needed.

**Modelled examples (project each, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**
1. 我喜欢黑色。
2. 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色。
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 我喜欢粉红色的大象！("I like pink elephants!")

#### Text 1 Passage (p. 96, CD T60)

> 我爸爸喜欢黑色和白色。
> 我妈妈喜欢黄色。
> 我哥哥喜欢蓝色。
> 我姐姐喜欢红色。
> 我喜欢粉红色。

Read aloud once (teacher only), then read together (choral). Ask:
- 爸爸喜欢什么颜色？(黑色和白色) — English answer OK for Y7
- 谁喜欢红色？(姐姐)
- 我喜欢什么颜色？(粉红色)

#### Pinyin Warm-up (p.97, Exercise 2, CD T61)

Quick 2-minute tone-marking drill recycling known vocabulary (not colour-specific): teacher reads 4 of the 9 words aloud (fàndiàn, jīngcháng, xuésheng, piàoliang); students write the pinyin with correct tone marks on mini whiteboards and hold up. Reveal and self-correct. This keeps general tone discrimination active alongside the new colour content.

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Gesture)** "Thumbs up if 黄色 means yellow."
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the Chinese for 'blue'. Hold it up on three."
- **(Cold call)** "Jamie, how do you say 'I like black' in Chinese?" → "Priya, 'red' 怎么说？"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: New Word Copying — We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min

**What:** Students copy the new words into their exercise books following stroke order, modelled on workbook Ex.2 (Copy the new words of Text 1).

**How:**
- Project each character with its stroke-order animation/sequence (喜, 欢, 黑, 色, 白, 黄, 蓝, 红, 粉).
- Students trace then copy each character 3 times in their books.
- Teacher circulates, checking stroke order on 喜 and 黑 in particular (both have many strokes).

**OTR:** Every student writes all 9 characters; teacher scans books during circulation.

- **Scaffold:** Provide a dotted/traceable version for the first repetition of each character.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers also write the pinyin with correct tone marks next to each character, unprompted.

---

#### Activity 2: Draw-the-Colour — You Do (8 min) | 28–36 min

**(Based on workbook Ex.3, p.136)**

**What:** Students draw a small picture in each of six boxes according to the colour labelled underneath (黑色, 红色, 白色, 黄色, 蓝色, 粉红色), then write the colour word in characters under their drawing.

**How:**
- Project the six colour labels one at a time as a model.
- Students draw quickly (simple shapes — a ball, a flower, etc. — speed matters more than art) and label each in characters.
- Partner-check: swap books, read partner's characters aloud to them.

**OTR:** All students complete all six boxes; partner check ensures every student reads characters aloud once.

- **Scaffold:** Word bank of all six colours (with pinyin) projected throughout.
- **Extension:** Students add one extra sentence per drawing, e.g. 这是红色的苹果。("This is a red apple.")

---

#### Game: Colour Bingo (7 min) | 36–43 min

**How to play:**
1. Students draw a 2×3 grid on a mini whiteboard and write the six colours (汉字 only) in any order.
2. Teacher calls out the English colour names one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字.
3. First student to cross off all six shouts "Bingo!" and reads all six back aloud in Chinese to confirm.

**What language it practises:** Rapid English → 汉字 recall for all six new colours.

**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only (already on desks).

- **Scaffold:** Allow students to keep the vocabulary table projected while drawing their grid.
- **Extension:** Winners must say a full sentence for one colour in their line, e.g. "黑色——我爸爸喜欢黑色。"

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria again. Teacher reads the first; a student volunteer reads the other two. "今天我们做到了吗？"

- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion in turn.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE complete sentence:
  > 我喜欢______。
  using one of today's six colours. Teacher circulates and scans for correct characters.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学怎么问别人：'你喜欢什么颜色？'"
