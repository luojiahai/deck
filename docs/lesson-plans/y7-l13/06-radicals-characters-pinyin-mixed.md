# Colours (6): Radicals 弓 力 礻, Strokes and Tone Marks

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 6 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to find the radical inside a character, and to write what we hear with the right tone mark.

## Success Criteria
- I can write the radicals 弓, 力 and 礻 and name a character that uses each
- I can find the radical in a character and say what it means
- I can hear a word and write its pinyin with the correct tone mark

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD T61 (p.97 Ex.2)
- Workbook p.134 (Ex.1, last three), p.137 (Ex.5, Ex.6), p.138 (Ex.9 items 4–6), p.144 (Ex.20, Ex.21)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** All eleven colours, and the 也 word order from Lesson 5.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire, characters only, no pinyin — eleven cards, twice through, second time faster. Then three whiteboard items on 也:
> *I don't like grey either.* · *My mum likes purple too.* · Correct this: 我不喜欢也。

**Connection:** "You can say all eleven colours. Today we go inside the characters — because the test doesn't only ask what a word means, it asks which radical it's built from."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Write 视 on the board and circle the left-hand side: "This little piece has a name, a meaning, and it's on the test. By the end of the lesson you'll know three more of them."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### No new vocabulary today
The radicals are the content, and they get taught the way vocabulary does — one slide each, with the example character beside it.

#### The three radicals

| Radical | Meaning | Example character |
|---|---|---|
| 弓 | bow | 弹 |
| 力 | strength | 加 |
| 礻 | ritual | 视 |

With 疒, 火 and 爫 from Lesson 3, that's all six from textbook p.99 Ex.5 in place.

**礻 gets its own board moment.** Write 礻 and 衤 side by side. 礻 has one dot, 衤 has two. 衤 means clothing, and next lesson — Lesson 14 — every garment character carries it: 衬, 衫, 裤, 裙. The extra dot is the most common writing error in that lesson, and students can be ahead of it before they get there.

#### How to find a radical
Three places to look, in this order: **left side · top · bottom.**

- 炒 → 火 on the left
- 视 → 礻 on the left
- 弹 → 弓 on the left
- 爱 → 爫 on top
- 病 → 疒 wrapped around the outside
- 黑 → 灬 underneath, which is 火 flattened
- 加 → 力 on the right ← the awkward one. Most radicals sit left, top or bottom; this one doesn't. Say so rather than letting students discover it as a contradiction.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Point at the radical in 炒. Which side?"
- "礻 or 衤 — which one has two dots, and what does it mean?"
- "Write a character you already know that has 灬 underneath." (点, from the time lesson)
- "What does 病 mean, just from its radical? Guess." (something to do with illness)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Listen and mark the tone — We Do (8 min) | 20–28
**Textbook p.97 Ex.2 (CD T61)** — nine words, students write the pinyin with tone marks on whiteboards. Play in groups of three, reveal and self-correct after each group:
> fàndiàn · jīngcháng · shòushāng · xuésheng · shítou · jiǎngbēi · jīnyú · yúncai · piàoliang

Watch for the neutral tones in 学生 and 石头 — students tend to invent a tone mark where there isn't one.

Then two short written ones straight into books, checked as a class: **workbook p.137 Ex.6** — count the strokes of 粉, 色, 黑, 黄, 红, 白, 蓝, 喜 — and **p.137 Ex.4** — write the character for each pinyin given (huǒ, rén, bàn, gōng, shàng, xià, qù, zhōng) and count its strokes. Ex.4 is all Unit 1–4 characters; it should be fast, and if it isn't, that's worth knowing.

Finish with **workbook p.144 Ex.21** — circle the colour words in the sixteen-cell grid (红级白铁绿黄蓝租 / 灰紫课黑棕走地橙). Two minutes, and it catches anyone still reading 级 as 红.

**Extension —** After the nine, write three words from this lesson in pinyin with tone marks from memory, no board: fěnhóngsè, yánsè, lǜsè. The ü in lǜsè is the target. Then dictate two words of your own choosing to a partner and mark their work.

#### Activity 2: Radicals on paper — You Do (8 min) | 28–36
Four short workbook exercises, in this order:

- **p.134 Ex.1** — copy 弓, 力, 礻 with stroke order.
- **p.138 Ex.9, items 4–6** — write the radical of 弹, 加, 视.
- **p.137 Ex.5** — find a character for each of twelve radicals: 目 纟 灬 艹 士 米 钅 冫 ⻊ 王 亻 方.
- **p.144 Ex.20** — spot the mistake and rewrite, six sentences. Every one is word order, and #6 is 什么颜色你喜欢？→ 你喜欢什么颜色？ — the error the class was warned about in Lesson 4.

Ex.5 and Ex.20 are Unit Test Parts 3 and 8 in the exact format they'll appear in.

**Extension —** For Ex.5, supply a *colour* character wherever one exists rather than any character at all: 纟→红 or 绿, 米→粉, 灬→黑. Then explain one of them aloud in Chinese or English — 红 has 纟 because 纟 is silk, and silk was dyed red.

**Standing extension for anyone who finishes early —** **workbook p.137 Ex.7**, the stroke ladder: write a one-stroke character, then a two-stroke, then three, all the way up to nine (the book ends on 美). It has no ceiling and no word bank, and the last three rungs are genuinely hard.

#### Game: Writing Relay (7 min) | 36–43
Two teams, one line each at the board. Teacher shows a phrase; the first student writes the first character, runs back, the next writes the second, and so on. Three rounds:
> 喜欢黑色 · 粉红色的校车 · 紫色和棕色

A point for the phrase being correct, and a second point if the stroke order held up and no 的 went missing. Teacher checks between rounds, class watching.

- **Setup needed:** board space for two teams, one pen each.
- **Harder version:** The phrase goes up in pinyin only, or in English, so the team has to produce the characters from scratch. For the last round, the team must also write a pinyin line underneath with correct tone marks — and the tone marks are worth the point, not the characters.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Two items — write the radical of 视 and what it means; then write any character that uses 火.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — we ask the whole class what colours they like, count the answers, and find out what happens when you mix two colours together."
