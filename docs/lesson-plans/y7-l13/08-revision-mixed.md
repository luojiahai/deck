# Colours (8): Revision 复习

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Colours 颜色 | Lesson 8 of 8 — revision
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use everything from Lesson 13 together — the colours, 喜欢, 的, and the two ways of asking.

## Success Criteria
- I can name all twelve colours without pinyin
- I can ask and answer both 你喜欢什么颜色？and 你喜欢X色吗？
- I can describe something with 颜色 + 的 + noun
- I can find the radical in a character and repair a sentence's word order

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books
- Printed Find Someone Who grids, one per student
- Workbook p.140 (Ex.13), p.143 (Ex.18), p.144 (Ex.22), p.145 (Ex.23)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** All twelve colours, characters only — the whole set from Lessons 1–7.

**How:** Rapid fire on the characters with no pinyin and no swatches, twice through, the second time in random order. Then **textbook p.102 Ex.9** as a two-team round: the teacher names an object in English — *a lemon, the sky, an elephant, a school bus, grass, a taxi* — and the first team to call its colour in Chinese takes the point. Some objects have two acceptable answers; both score. Anything that stalls goes on the board in a corner and stays there for the rest of the lesson.

**Connection:** "Everything today is something you've already done. The point of this lesson is to do all of it at once, and to see the shapes the questions come in."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Read the Learning Intention, then have four students read one criterion each. Say plainly what this lesson is: "Nothing new today. If something on that list isn't solid yet, this is the lesson to say so."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### No new vocabulary
The summary board goes up and stays up for the whole lesson: all twelve colours, 喜欢, 颜色, 的.

#### Four shapes, one worked example each
These are the formats the questions come in. Each gets modelled once on the board, with the class supplying the answer to the second half.

**1. Sorting into categories.** Three headings — 颜色 · 车 · 每天做的事 — and a pile of words: 橙色, 出租车, 起床, 灰色, 地铁, 睡觉, 紫色, 校车, 吃饭.
> Worked: 橙色 → 颜色. 出租车 → 车. Class does the rest chorally.

**2. Radical → meaning.** 弹 · 爱 · 病 · 视 · 炒 · 加
> Worked: 弹 → 弓 → bow. Class does 爱 and 病.

**3. Word-order repair.** The three errors that have come up all sequence:
> 什么颜色你喜欢？ → 你喜欢什么颜色？
> 我不喜欢也。 → 我也不喜欢。
> 火车黑色的。 → 黑色的火车

**4. Translation, both directions.**
> 黑色的出租车 → a black taxi
> *I like red and green colours.* → 我喜欢红色和绿色。

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Which of the twelve is not actually a colour?" (颜色)
- "Give me the two questions — the open one and the yes/no one. Write both."
- "什么不对？ 我喜欢红色出租车。"
- "How many 的 are in 我喜欢黑色的火车？ Where?"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Reading — 王黑白 — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
**Workbook p.145 Ex.23.** The longest reading in the lesson, and the one worth the time: 王黑白 gives his name, his city, his family of three, his father the teacher who drives to work and likes black and white — 所以他给我起名黑白 — his mother the secretary who takes the subway and likes blue and brown, then his own age, year level and colours, and where the family lives now.

Read it aloud once, then choral. 所以 and 给我起名 are recognition only — gloss them on the board, don't teach them.

The eight questions: all eight answered orally in pairs, four of them written. It recycles name, city, family, jobs, transport and time from Units 1–4 and hangs all of it on colour, which is exactly the range the Unit Test works in.

**Extension —** Write your own 王黑白 paragraph: name, city, family, one parent's job and how they get to work, and what colours each person likes. At least five sentences, at least three colours, at least one 的 phrase. Board covered.

#### Activity 2: The test formats on paper — You Do (8 min) | 27–35
Three workbook exercises, in this order — the teacher picks up whichever the Review surfaced as shaky:

- **p.140 Ex.13** — make a sentence from the words given: 喜欢/我妹妹 · 红色/不喜欢 · 每天/校车 · 怎么/你爸爸 · 十点/睡觉. This is Unit Test Part 4's format exactly.
- **p.144 Ex.22** — translate four into Chinese: *I like red and green colours.* · *I walk to school every day.* · *I do not eat breakfast.* · *It is a quarter past three.* Note that only the first is about colour — the other three are Unit 4, and that's deliberate.
- **p.143 Ex.18** — fill the blanks in the self-introduction passage: age, year level, a family of five, both parents' jobs, and every person's favourite colours.
- **p.139 Ex.11** — answer eight cumulative questions in writing (age, year level, nationality, whether dad works and what he does, how he gets to work, what time you get up and go to bed). No colour in it at all; it's the Book 1 material the colour questions sit on top of.

**Extension —** Do **workbook p.140 Ex.12** first — write six questions of your own using 几 (他几岁？你几点起床？) and put two of them to the class — then write Ex.18 from scratch about yourself instead of filling in the blanks — every family member gets two colours joined with 和, and at least one sentence uses 的 with something from Unit 4 (我爸爸开白色的汽车上班). Then read it aloud to the class in the plenary.

#### Game: Find Someone Who (8 min) | 35–43
Each student gets a grid of prompts and has to find a different classmate for each, asking in Chinese and writing the name down:

> …someone who likes 紫色 · …someone who does **not** like 灰色 · …someone whose 爸爸 likes 黑色 · …someone who takes a 黄色的校车 · …someone who likes 天蓝色 · …someone who likes the same colour as you

Only Chinese questions count — 你喜欢紫色吗？ 你爸爸喜欢什么颜色？ Teacher circulates and listens.

- **Setup needed:** printed grids, one per student.
- **Harder version:** Every name recorded must come with a follow-up answer, not just a tick — ask a second question and write the answer down. At the end, report two of your findings to the class in the third person with 也: 她喜欢紫色，我也喜欢紫色。

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** All four, read by four different students — and this time the scope is the whole of Lesson 13, not just today. Ask which of the four the class wants one more go at.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each of the four criteria, held up together so the teacher can see the shape of the room.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Two items — write one sentence using a colour, 的, and a noun; then write any radical with its meaning.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — Lesson 14, clothes. Same 的 frame, but what comes after it is 衬衫 and 裙子 — and you already know which radical they're going to have."
