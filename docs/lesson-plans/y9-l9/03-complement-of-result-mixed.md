# Lesson 9.3: Complement of Result + Listening

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 3 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use complement-of-result verb pairs to show that an action was completed and achieved its result.

## Success Criteria
- I can explain the difference between an action alone and an action + result complement (能解释动作和"结果补语"的区别)
- I can make sentences using at least 3 different complement-of-result pairs (能用至少三个"结果补语"造句)
- I can understand a listening passage about students' stationery items (能听懂关于文具的听力短文)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (with audio playback for CD Track 34)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 2's 一……就…… pattern and the 12 characters from the character flash.

**How:** Project a jumbled 一……就…… sentence for students to reorder on mini whiteboards (30 sec), then rapid-fire flashcards on the 12 characters from Lesson 2 (目足心舌叉勺巾血皿刀包豆) — teacher shows 汉字, class says the English meaning chorally.

**Connection:** "上节课我们学了'一……就……'——两个动作一起发生。今天我们学另外一种句型——'结果补语'，说一个动作**做完了**、**完成了**。比如：我买**到**了铅笔——不只是'买'，是'买'并且**成功了**。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. Teacher reads aloud; class echoes chorally. Keep visible for the lesson.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Key Grammar: Complement of Result (p. 83, Act. 4)

Present the four categories, one row at a time, with the projector:

| Category | Pairs | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Perceive with senses | 看见 / 听见 | see / hear (successfully perceived) |
| Achieve / attain | 看到 / 找到 / 买到 | see / find / buy (successfully) |
| Finish completely | 吃光 / 喝光 / 卖光 | eat / drink / sell (all gone) |
| Finish successfully | 买好 / 做好 | finish buying / finish doing |

**Explain the concept:** "这些词都是'动词 + 补语'。补语告诉我们这个动作的**结果**——不只是'做'，而是'做**完**了'或'做**到**了'。"

**Modelled examples (project each one, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**

1. 我在文具店里找**到**了一支铅笔。(I found a pencil in the stationery store — successfully.)
2. 蛋糕已经吃**光**了。(The cake is completely eaten — none left.)
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 老师说的话，我一个字都没听**见**！

**Contrast:** Project 我看铅笔 vs 我看**见**铅笔。 "哪个说明我真的看到了？" (第二个)

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write one complement-of-result pair from the 'finish completely' category."
- **(Cold call)** "Jamie，'我买______了课本' — what goes in the blank if I successfully bought it?" (到)
- **(Choral)** Project 找、找到 side by side. "哪一个说明'找到了'？大家一起说！"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Pair Sentence-Making — We Do, Speaking (7 min) | 20–27 min

**(Based on Act. 4, p. 83)**

**What:** In pairs, students take turns making a sentence with each of the 4 complement-of-result pairs, quizzing each other orally.

**How:**
- Teacher models one example per category first (as in I Do).
- Partner A picks a category and says a sentence; Partner B must identify which category it belongs to. Then swap roles.
- Cold call 3–4 pairs to share their best sentence with the class.

**OTR:** Turn-and-talk requires every student to produce at least 2 sentences during the pair activity.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the 4-category table projected throughout.
- **Extension:** Students must use a stationery item as the object in at least 2 of their sentences (e.g. 我在书包里找到了橡皮。).

---

#### Activity 2: Listening — You Do, Writing (8 min) | 27–35 min

**(Based on Act. 5, p. 83, CD Track 34)**

**What:** Students listen to 6 short student descriptions and tick which stationery items each one has, writing answers in their exercise books.

**How:**
- Play the audio once straight through; students listen only.
- Play again, pausing after each of the 6 items; students write the item(s) they hear in their exercise books.
- Play a third time for students to check their answers.
- Reveal answers on the projector; students self-mark.

**OTR:** All students listen and write independently; every student self-marks their own answers.

- **Scaffold:** Project the word bank of possible stationery items (from Lesson 1's vocabulary) before playing the audio.
- **Extension:** Students also write one full sentence summarising what item(s) one of the six speakers has, using a complement-of-result verb if possible (e.g. 他买到了一个文具盒。).

---

#### Game: 结果补语 Slap the Board (8 min) | 35–43 min

**How to play:**

1. Project the 8 complement-of-result words (看见, 听见, 看到, 找到, 买到, 吃光, 喝光, 卖光/买好, 做好) scattered on the screen.
2. Two students come to the front (or use mini whiteboards for a seated version — students write the answer instead of touching the screen).
3. Teacher says an English meaning (e.g. "sold out"); students race to point to/write the matching word.
4. First correct answer wins the round; repeat with new pairs of students until most students have had a turn.

**What language it practises:** Rapid recall of the complement-of-result pairs and their meanings.

**Setup needed:** Projector only (or mini whiteboards for the seated version).

- **Scaffold:** Allow students to keep the 4-category table visible in a corner of the screen.
- **Extension:** Winning students must use the word in a full sentence before the next round starts.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the first two; a student volunteer reads the third.

- **Self-assessment:** Students rate themselves 👍 / 😐 / 👎 against each criterion via show of hands.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE sentence using any complement-of-result pair from today.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学文具的量词——'一支铅笔'、'一本练习本'——还有更多东西的名字。"
