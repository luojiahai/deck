# Lesson 9.7: Borrowing Roleplay + 行/可以 Translation

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask to borrow and lend items politely, and to translate sentences using 行 and 可以.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask to borrow an item and respond by lending it or politely declining (能礼貌地借东西、回答借东西的请求)
- I can translate sentences using 行 and 可以 (能翻译用"行"和"可以"的句子)
- I can explain what I would and wouldn't lend, and why (能说出我会借什么、不会借什么，为什么)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 5's borrowing dialogue and Lesson 6's price-asking pattern, as a bridge into today's full borrowing roleplay + survey.

**How:** Project the full Text 2 dialogue with 5 words blanked (忘记, 借给, 行, 还, 别); cold call students to fill each in turn, class echoes chorally. Quick mini-whiteboard check: "Translate: may I borrow this?" (可以借一下吗？/ 借一下，行吗？)

**Connection:** "这节课我们要更深入地练习'借东西'——不只是问，还要练习**答应**或者**拒绝**，还要想一想：什么东西你会借给同学，什么不会？"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. Teacher reads aloud; class echoes chorally. Keep visible for the lesson.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Recap: Key Sentence Patterns

Project each pattern with its modelled example; class repeats chorally.

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Lend me X to use for a bit, OK? | 借给我一支用一下，行吗？ |
| I'll return it to you soon | 我一会儿就还给你。 |
| Politely decline + redirect | 对不起，我正用呢。你跟别的同学借吧。 |
| Asking permission (informal) | 行吗？ |
| Asking permission (formal) | 可以吗？ |

**Modelled examples:**

1. 借你的橡皮用一下，可以吗？——可以，给你。
2. 借你的课本看一下，行吗？——对不起，我正在看呢。你跟别的同学借吧。

#### Grammar Recap: 行 vs 可以

行 = informal "OK/all right" (used in casual requests among classmates). 可以 = more formal "may/can" (slightly more polite, works in any context). Both are interchangeable in most casual requests, but 可以 sounds more polite/formal.

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Translate: 'May I borrow your ruler?'"
- **(Cold call)** "Sam，how would you politely decline if a classmate asks to borrow your calculator while you're using it?"
- **(Choral)** "'行' 比较正式还是比较随便？" (随便/informal) "那'可以'呢？" (比较正式)

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Borrowing Roleplay — We Do → You Do, Speaking (7 min) | 20–27 min

**(Based on Act. 10, p. 88)**

**What:** In pairs, Student A tries to borrow an item from a projected list of 7 (铅笔, 尺子, 橡皮, 卷笔刀, 计算器, 课本, 练习本); Student B either lends it or politely declines using the sentence patterns above.

**How:**
- Teacher models one round with a volunteer first (borrow → lend), then a second round (borrow → decline + redirect).
- In pairs, students work through all 7 items, swapping roles halfway.
- Cold call 3–4 pairs to perform one exchange for the class.

**OTR:** Every student practises both borrowing and lending roles across the 7 items.

- **Scaffold:** Sentence frames projected throughout:
  - A: 借你的______用一下，行吗？/可以吗？
  - B: 行。/可以。 or 对不起，我正用呢。你跟别的同学借吧。
- **Extension:** Students must vary their decline reason beyond "我正用呢" (e.g. 对不起，这是新的，我不想借。) for at least one item.

---

#### Activity 2: 行/可以 Translation — You Do, Writing (8 min) | 27–35 min

**(Based on Act. 9, p. 87)**

**What:** Students translate 7 sentences (mix of English→Chinese and Chinese→English) using 行 and 可以, written independently in exercise books.

**How:**
- Project the 7 sentences one at a time (e.g. "May I use your pencil sharpener?" / "行，没问题。" / "Can I borrow your calculator for a moment?").
- Students write translations in exercise books; after each one, cold call a student to share, class checks together.

**OTR:** All students attempt every sentence independently before each answer is revealed.

- **Scaffold:** Word bank of key vocabulary (借, 用, 一下, 行, 可以) projected alongside the sentences.
- **Extension:** For the final 2 sentences, students must produce the answer without a word bank and add one extra clause (e.g. a reason or a time frame with 就).

---

#### Survey + Mingling: 你会借给同学什么？ — Game (8 min) | 35–43 min

**(Based on Act. 11 & Act. 12, p. 88)**

**What:** A "Find Someone Who" style mingling survey — students move around asking classmates what they would and wouldn't lend, then report back.

**How:**
1. Project the question frame: 你会借给同学什么？你不会借给同学什么？为什么？
2. Students stand and interview 3–4 classmates, noting one answer from each in their exercise books (item + reason).
3. Reconvene as a class; cold call 4–5 students to share what they found ("Sam说他会借铅笔，但是不会借他的日记本，因为……").
4. Wrap with 2–3 short partner borrowing dialogues from the Act. 12 style (a–j item list), performed on the spot by volunteer pairs.

**What language it practises:** Free-speaking application of 借/借给/行/可以 in an unscripted, personalised context.

**Setup needed:** Projector for the question frame; students move around the room.

- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame projected: 我会借给同学______，因为______。我不会借给同学______，因为______。
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers must interview at least 5 classmates and report using reported speech ("Sam说他……").

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the first; a student volunteer reads the second and third.

- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE sentence:
  > Write one thing you would lend a classmate, and why. (我会借给同学______，因为______。)

- **Preview:** "下一课是这个单元最后一课——我们要复习所有关于文具的内容，准备考试3.3。"
