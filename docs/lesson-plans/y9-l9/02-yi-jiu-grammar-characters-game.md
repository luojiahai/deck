# Lesson 9.2: 一……就…… Grammar + Character Flash

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use 一……就…… to say "as soon as X happens, Y happens," and to recognise 12 new characters.

## Success Criteria
- I can build a sentence using 一……就…… (能用"一……就……"造句)
- I can recognise and read the 12 characters 目、足、心、舌、叉、勺、巾、血、皿、刀、包、豆
- I can use 进 and 看见 correctly in a sentence

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 1's 11 stationery vocabulary items and the 里边/里面 pattern.

**How:** Rapid-fire flashcards on the projector — teacher shows 汉字, class says pinyin + English chorally, cycling through all 11 items twice. Then project the Text 1 sentence with a blank: 你一进校门就能______图书馆。 Cold call one student to guess the missing word (看见); class echoes the full sentence.

**Connection:** "你们看见句子里的'一……就……'了吗？今天我们要专门学这个句型——还有十二个新汉字。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. Teacher reads aloud; class echoes chorally. Keep visible for the lesson.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 进 | jìn | enter |
| 看见 | kànjiàn | see |

#### Key Sentence Pattern: 一……就……

**Structure:** Subject + 一 + action 1 + 就 + action 2 — "as soon as action 1 happens, action 2 happens."

**Modelled examples (project each one, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**

1. 你一进校门就能看见图书馆。(As soon as you enter the school gate, you can see the library.)
2. 我一到家就吃饭。(As soon as I get home, I eat.)
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 老师一进教室，学生就睡觉！

**Common error (write on projector):** ❌ 我进校门就能看见……以后 → ✅ 我**一**进校门**就**能看见……。 "一……就……包住两个动作，不可以只用'就'，也不可以放在句子最后。"

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Choral)** Project: 我______起床______刷牙。(一…就…) Class calls out the missing words together.
- **(Cold call)** "Sam，怎么用'一……就……'说'as soon as I see my friend, I say hello'？"
- **(Gesture)** "Thumbs up if 看见 means 'to see', thumbs down if it means 'to hear'."

#### Character Flash (p. 82, Act. 3)

Project the 12 characters together for 1 minute so students can look them over: **目、足、心、舌、叉、勺、巾、血、皿、刀、包、豆**. Give brief English glosses (eye, foot, heart, tongue, fork, spoon, cloth, blood, dish, knife, bag, bean) — no deep teaching, this is memorisation practice. Hide the grid.

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 一……就…… Sentence-Building — We Do (7 min) | 20–27 min

**(Based on Act. 2, p. 82)**

**What:** Project 3 pictures in turn (图书馆, 客厅, 礼堂, 厨房— from Act. 2). Students build a 一……就…… sentence about each on mini whiteboards.

**How:**
- Teacher models the first picture aloud, thinking through the two actions: "图书馆——一进图书馆就要安静。"
- For pictures 2–4, students write their own sentence on mini whiteboards and hold up on three; teacher scans and confirms.

**OTR (every student responds):** All students write and hold up a whiteboard sentence for every picture.

- **Scaffold:** Project a sentence frame: 一______就______。 with a word bank of possible actions (安静, 看电视, 吃饭, 睡觉, 做饭) alongside each picture.
- **Extension:** Students must use a different pair of actions from the word bank each time and add one extra detail (e.g. 我一进客厅就打开电视看新闻。).

---

#### Game 1: 汉字大挑战 Character Recall Relay — Act. 3 Style (6 min) | 27–33 min

**How to play:**

1. Recap: briefly re-project the 12-character grid for 15 seconds.
2. Hide the grid. Divide the class into two teams (left half vs right half).
3. Teacher calls out an English meaning (e.g. "knife"); the next student in rotation from alternating teams writes the matching 汉字 on their mini whiteboard and holds it up.
4. Correct = 1 point for the team; incorrect = other team may steal.

**What language it practises:** Rapid English → 汉字 recall for the 12 new characters.

**Setup needed:** Projector (character grid), mini whiteboards.

- **Scaffold:** If a student hesitates, teacher may give the radical/component as a hint (e.g. "目 looks like an eye turned sideways").
- **Extension:** Winning students must also give the pinyin, not just the character.

---

#### Game 2: 一……就…… Sentence Jumble (5 min) | 33–38 min

**How to play:**

1. Project a jumbled sentence, word by word out of order (e.g. 就 / 图书馆 / 看见 / 校门 / 一 / 你 / 进 / 能).
2. In pairs, students write the correctly ordered sentence on a mini whiteboard.
3. First pair to hold up a fully correct sentence wins the round. Repeat with 2 more jumbled sentences.

**What language it practises:** Correct word order within the 一……就…… frame — directly targets the common error flagged in I Do.

**Setup needed:** Projector only.

- **Scaffold:** Give the first word (你) as a starting point for the first jumble.
- **Extension:** For the third jumble, remove one word entirely and have students supply it from memory before reordering.

---

#### Free Practice: Extra Sentences — You Do (5 min) | 38–43 min

**What:** Students write 2 more 一……就…… sentences about their own daily life (e.g. getting to school, arriving home) in their exercise books, without a word bank.

**OTR:** All students write independently; cold call 2–3 to share aloud.

- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame still projected for students who need it: 我一______就______。
- **Extension:** One sentence must use a verb not modelled today (e.g. 一放学就……).

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Student volunteer reads each; class responds with a matching example.

- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs up / sideways / down against each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE sentence using 一……就……, about anything they choose.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学一个新语法——'看见/买到/吃光'这种'结果补语'，还会练听力。"
