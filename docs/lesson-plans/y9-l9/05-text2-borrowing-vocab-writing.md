# Lesson 9.5: Text 2 — Borrowing & Lending Vocabulary

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to read and understand a dialogue about borrowing and lending stationery, using 借, 还, 别, and 行/可以.

## Success Criteria
- I can use the new vocabulary 忘记, 借, 计算器, 还, 同学 correctly (能正确使用新词汇)
- I can read and understand the Text 2 borrowing dialogue (能读懂借东西的对话)
- I can explain the difference between 借 (borrow) and 借给 (lend), and between 还 (huán, return) and 别 (don't) (能解释这些词的区别)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 4's measure words (支/本/把) and item names, as a bridge into today's borrowing dialogue — which uses exactly those items.

**How:** Project 5 quick-fire prompts: teacher shows an item picture, students write "measure word + item" on mini whiteboards, hold up on three (e.g. picture of a calculator → students should write 一个计算器, previewing today's new word). Cold call to confirm 2–3.

**Connection:** "如果你没有铅笔，但是同学有，你可以怎么办？——你可以'借'！今天我们学一段对话，是关于'借'东西和'还'东西的。"

### I Do (10–20 min)

Display each row: 汉字 → pinyin → English. Teacher says the word twice; class repeats twice.

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 忘记 | wàngjì | forget |
| 借 | jiè | borrow; lend |
| 支 | zhī | measure word (pens, pencils) |
| 行 | xíng | be all right; OK |
| 计算器 | jìsuànqì | calculator |

| 还 | huán | return (something borrowed) |
| 别 | bié | other; don't (imperative) |
| 同学 | tóngxué | classmate |

#### Text 2 Dialogue (p. 85, CD Track 35)

> — 你有铅笔吗？
> — 有。
> — 我今天忘记带铅笔了。借给我一支用一下，行吗？
> — 行。
> — 借你的计算器用一下，可以吗？我一会儿就还给你。
> — 对不起，我正用呢。你跟别的同学借吧。
> — 好吧。

Read aloud once (teacher only, playing both roles or using two students), then read together in pairs (each student takes one role). Ask:
- 第一个人忘记带什么了？(铅笔)
- 第二个人借给他铅笔了吗？(借了)
- 第二个人借给他计算器了吗？为什么？(没有，因为他正在用)

#### Key Grammar

**借 — borrow AND lend.** 借 alone typically means "borrow" (我借你的铅笔 = I borrow your pencil). 借给 means "lend to" (借给我一支 = lend me one). Context determines direction.

**还 (huán, 2nd tone) — return something borrowed.** Do not confuse with 还 (hái) = also/still — same character, same tone, different meaning; context distinguishes them. 我一会儿就还给你 = I'll return it to you soon.

**别 + verb — negative imperative.** 别忘记 = don't forget. 别 is informal; 不要 is also acceptable.

**行 vs 可以.** 行 = informal "OK/all right." 可以 = more formal "may/can." Both appear in the dialogue: 行吗？/ 可以吗？

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the Chinese for 'forget'."
- **(Cold call)** "Jamie，'借给我一支铅笔' — who is doing the lending, and who is borrowing?"
- **(Error correction, choral)** Project: 我一会儿就借给你。(should be 还给你) "这句话对不对？错在哪儿？"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Vocab Dictation + Text 2 Gap-Fill — We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min

**What:** Vocab dictation on mini whiteboards (teacher says English, students write 汉字), followed by a gapped version of the Text 2 dialogue projected with 5 blanks; students copy and complete it in their exercise books.

**How:**
- Dictation round: 5 items from today's vocabulary list, hold up on three each time.
- Gap-fill: project the dialogue with 借, 还, 别, 行, 计算器 removed; students fill blanks in exercise books; cold call to check answers.

**OTR:** Every student writes and holds up a whiteboard for the dictation; every student completes the gap-fill independently.

- **Scaffold:** Word bank of the 5 missing words projected alongside the gapped dialogue.
- **Extension:** After filling the gaps, students underline which word is 借 (borrow) vs 借给 (lend) in the dialogue and label each.

---

#### Activity 2: Write Your Own Borrowing Dialogue — You Do (10 min) | 28–38 min

**What:** Using a sentence frame, students write a 3–4 line borrowing dialogue between two classmates, choosing their own item (from Lesson 4's vocabulary) to borrow.

**How:**
- Teacher models one new example live, thinking aloud about word choice: "如果我想借一本练习本，我可以说……'借给我一本练习本，行吗？'"
- Individual writing (6 min): students write their own dialogue in exercise books.
- Peer-check (2 min): students swap books with a neighbour and check for 借/借给 and 还/别 accuracy.
- Cold call 2–3 pairs to read their dialogue aloud.

**OTR:** All students write independently; peer-check requires every student to read and mark a partner's work.

- **Scaffold:** Sentence frame projected throughout:
  - A: 我忘记带______了。借给我______，行吗？
  - B: 行。/ 对不起，我正用呢。你跟别的同学借吧。
- **Extension:** Students must add a third line using 还 (e.g. A: 我一会儿就还给你。) and use 可以 instead of 行 in at least one question.

---

#### Game: 借东西 Bingo (5 min) | 38–43 min

**How to play:**

1. Students draw a 3×3 grid on the back of a mini whiteboard and fill it with 9 of today's vocabulary items (汉字 only).
2. Teacher calls out English meanings one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字.
3. First student to cross off a full row, column, or diagonal calls "Bingo!" and reads their winning line aloud.

**What language it practises:** Rapid English → 汉字 recall for the borrowing/lending vocabulary.

**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only.

- **Scaffold:** Allow students to keep the vocabulary table projected for their first grid.
- **Extension:** Winning students must use their winning word in a full sentence from today's dialogue pattern.

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the first; a student volunteer reads the second and third.

- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE sentence:
  > Translate: "I forgot to bring my pencil today."
  Teacher circulates and checks for 忘记 and correct word order.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要去'文具店'买东西——练习问价钱，还有五个新的文具词。"
