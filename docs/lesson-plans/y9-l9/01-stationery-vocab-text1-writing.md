# Lesson 9.1: Stationery Store Vocabulary + Text 1 (文具店)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 1 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name stationery items in Chinese and read a description of our school's stationery store using 我们学校有一个文具店，里边有……

## Success Criteria
- I can name the 11 core stationery/shop items in Chinese (能说出十一个文具/商店相关的词)
- I can read and understand the Text 1 passage about the school's stationery store (能读懂关于文具店的短文)
- I can describe what is inside a place using 里边/里面 (能用"里边/里面"描述位置)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (all visuals displayed and controlled from projector)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 8's location words (旁边, 隔壁, 中间, 左边/右边, 楼上/楼下) and the measure words 个/幢, as a bridge into today's new location word 里边/里面.

**How:** Project 6 quick-fire prompts one at a time (e.g. 图书馆在体育馆的______ [旁边], 教室在______层 [三]). Students write answers on mini whiteboards and hold up on three. Cold call one student per item to say the full sentence aloud; class echoes chorally.

**Connection:** "上节课我们学了怎么说'东西在哪儿'——旁边、隔壁、中间。今天我们学一个新地方：文具店，还有一个新词——'里边'，说说文具店**里边**有什么。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and all three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the Learning Intention aloud in Chinese; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion. Leave these visible in a corner of the screen for the rest of the lesson.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary

Display each row on the projector: 汉字 → pinyin → English. Teacher says the word twice; class repeats twice. After every four words, do one quick choral sweep of all words so far.

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 里边 = 里面 | lǐbiān = lǐmiàn | inside |
| 铅笔 | qiānbǐ | pencil |
| 尺子 | chǐzi | ruler |
| 橡皮 | xiàngpí | rubber; eraser |
| 卷笔刀 | juǎnbǐdāo | pencil sharpener |
| 练习本 | liànxíběn | exercise book |
| 课本 | kèběn | textbook |
| 日记本 | rìjìběn | diary (book) |
| 文具盒 | wénjùhé | pencil case |
| 小说 | xiǎoshuō | novel |
| 卡片 | kǎpiàn | card |

*Component breakdown (quick mention, not deep teaching):* 练 (practise) + 习 (practise) + 本 (book) = 练习本 "exercise book"; 日 (day) + 记 (record) + 本 (book) = 日记本 "diary"; 文 (writing) + 具 (tool) + 盒 (box) = 文具盒 "pencil case". Point these out briefly while presenting the table above — one sentence each is enough.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**Structure:** 我们学校有一个文具店，里边有……(list items with 、 and 等 at the end).

**Modelled examples (project each one, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**

1. 我们学校有一个文具店，里边有铅笔、尺子、橡皮、卷笔刀等。
2. 文具店还卖小说、课本、卡片等。
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 我的文具盒里边有一百支铅笔！

#### Text 1 Passage (p. 80, CD Track 33)

> 我们学校有一个文具店，里边有铅笔、尺子、橡皮、卷笔刀、练习本、日记本、文具盒等。除了这些，文具店还卖小说、课本、卡片等。文具店在五号教学楼里，在一楼。你一进校门就能看见。

Read aloud once (teacher only), then read together (choral). Ask:
- 文具店里边有什么？(Name 3 items.)
- 文具店在哪儿？(五号教学楼一楼)
- 除了文具，文具店还卖什么？(小说、课本、卡片)

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Gesture)** "Thumbs up if 文具盒 means 'pencil case'."
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the Chinese for 'ruler'. Hold it up on three."
- **(Cold call)** "Alex，'exercise book' 用中文怎么说？" → "Priya，'novel' 呢？"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Vocab Dictation on Mini Whiteboards — We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min

**What:** Teacher says an English word from today's list; students write the Chinese on mini whiteboards.

**How:**
- Round 1 (pinyin allowed): teacher says the English meaning, students write pinyin or 汉字, hold up on three. Teacher scans and confirms.
- Round 2 (汉字 only): repeat with 汉字 required, no pinyin credit.
- After each round, reveal the correct answer on the projector so students self-correct.

**OTR (every student responds):** Every student writes and holds up a whiteboard for all 11 items.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the vocabulary table projected in a corner during Round 1.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers must also write one sentence using the word, not just the word itself.

---

#### Activity 2: Text 1 Gap-Fill — You Do (10 min) | 28–38 min

**(Based on Act. 1, p. 81)**

**What:** Students copy a gapped version of the Text 1 passage into their exercise books (projected with 6 blanks in place of key vocabulary) and fill in the missing words. Then, using the location-word pattern from Lesson 8, they write 2 sentences describing where two items are positioned in a room scene projected on screen (Act. 1 style).

**How:**
- Project the gapped Text 1; students copy and complete independently (5 min).
- Cold call students to read one completed sentence each; class checks together.
- Project a stationery-scene picture (pencil case on the desk, ruler in the drawer, etc.); students write 2 sentences using 里边/里面 or Lesson 8 location words (5 min).

**OTR:** All students complete both the gap-fill and the 2 position sentences independently; cold call at the end for the whole-class check.

- **Scaffold:** Word bank of the 11 vocabulary items projected alongside the gapped text.
- **Extension:** Students write a 3rd sentence combining 里边 with a Lesson 8 location word (e.g. 铅笔在文具盒里边，文具盒在书包旁边。).

---

#### Game: 文具 Bingo (5 min) | 38–43 min

**How to play:**

1. Students draw a 3×3 grid on the back of a mini whiteboard and fill it with 9 of the 11 vocabulary items (汉字 only), in any order.
2. Teacher calls out English meanings one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字.
3. First student to cross off a full row, column, or diagonal calls "Bingo!" and reads their winning line aloud in Chinese to confirm.

**What language it practises:** Rapid English → 汉字 recall for all 11 new words.

**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only (already on desks).

- **Scaffold:** Allow students to keep the vocabulary table projected for their first grid to draw from.
- **Extension:** Winning students must read their winning line as a full sentence, not just the word (e.g. "尺子——我的尺子在文具盒里边。").

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria again. Teacher reads the first; a student volunteer reads the second and third. "今天我们做到了吗？"

- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion in turn.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE complete sentence:
  > 我的书包里边有______和______。
  They must use two vocabulary items from today. Teacher circulates and scans whiteboards for correct word choice.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学一个新句型——'一……就……'，还有十二个新汉字。"
