# Review + Test 3.3 Prep — Stationery 文具

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 8 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to consolidate all Lesson 9 vocabulary and sentence patterns, and to prepare accurate written responses for Test 3.3.

## Success Criteria
- 我能翻译Lesson 9所有测验词汇（中文↔英文）(I can translate all Test 3.3 vocabulary in both directions)
- 我能正确使用"一……就……"和"结果补语"造句 (I can correctly use 一……就…… and complement of result in sentences)
- 我能写出5个正确的中文句子，对应英文提示 (I can write 5 correct Chinese sentences from English prompts, Test 3.3 format)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (display vocabulary grid, patterns, quiz questions, sentence frames, exit ticket)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Rapid recall across all of Lesson 9 — vocabulary, 一……就……, complement of result, and the borrowing dialogue.

**How:** Project 10 quick-fire prompts one at a time. For vocabulary prompts (items 1–6), display the Chinese word and cold-call one student, then confirm with a choral repeat. For grammar prompts (items 7–10), display the sentence starter and cold-call a student to complete it; class echoes the full sentence chorally.

Quick-fire sequence:
1. 文具盒 → *pencil case*
2. 卷笔刀 → *pencil sharpener*
3. 计算器 → *calculator*
4. 忘记 → *forget*
5. 同学 → *classmate*
6. 礼品纸 → *gift wrapping paper*
7. Starter: 你一进校门______能看见图书馆。→ *就* (choral echo of full sentence)
8. Starter: 我在书包里找______了铅笔。→ *到*（elicit completion, choral echo）
9. Starter: 借给我一支用一下，______吗？→ *行*（cold call + choral echo）
10. Starter: 我一会儿就______给你。→ *还*（cold call + choral echo）

OTR: cold call (individual) + choral echo (whole class) on every item.

**Connection:** "今天是第八节课，也是这个单元的最后一课。我们复习所有的内容，然后练习考试会考的问题。这节课结束后你们就准备好考测验3.3了。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Display the Learning Intention and all three Success Criteria on the projector. Teacher reads each line in Chinese; class echoes chorally. Point out that the three SC map directly onto what Test 3.3 will assess. Leave SC visible on a corner of the board for the remainder of the lesson.

**Delivery (10–14 min, 4 min):** Display the full grid with the English column initially hidden. Students write the English meaning for each word on mini whiteboards, cycling through in batches of 6. After each batch, reveal the English column — students self-check.

#### Key Sentence Pattern Summary

Display all patterns with modelled examples. Students copy into exercise books.

| # | Pattern | Modelled example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 一……就…… — as soon as X, Y | 你一进校门就能看见图书馆。 |
| 2 | Complement of result | 我找到了铅笔。/ 蛋糕吃光了。 |
| 3 | 借 (borrow) vs 借给 (lend) | 我借你的橡皮。/ 借给我一支用一下。 |
| 4 | 还 (huán, return) vs 别 (don't) | 我一会儿就还给你。/ 别忘记带铅笔。 |
| 5 | 行 (informal) vs 可以 (formal) | 行吗？/ 可以吗？ |

**Delivery (14–16 min, 2 min):** Flash each row; teacher reads aloud, class repeats chorally. Point out the Pattern 1 common error: 一……就…… must wrap both actions, never appear only at the end.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- After vocabulary grid: "Hold up your whiteboard — show me your translation for 文具盒 and 计算器."
- After patterns: "Give me ONE sentence using 一……就……." — cold call two students, class echoes the better one chorally.
- Error check: Project 我进校门就看见图书馆。 "找出错误！" — cold call one student to fix it (missing 一).

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 造句练习（引导）— We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min

**What:** Guided walkthrough of the Test 3.3 sentence-writing format, using 2 of the 5 practice sentences.

**How:**
- Project the first two Test 3.3 sentences one at a time: "I forgot to bring my pencil today." / "May I borrow your Chinese textbook?"
- Teacher models the first aloud, thinking through word order: "'忘记'——动词。'今天'——时间词，放在前面。'我今天忘记带铅笔了。'"
- Turn-and-talk (3 min): partners work out the second sentence together (我可以借你的汉语课本吗？/ 借你的汉语课本，可以吗？).
- Cold call two pairs to share; class checks together.

**OTR (every student responds):** Turn-and-talk requires every student to attempt the second sentence.

- **Scaffold:** Project a word bank (忘记, 带, 借, 可以, 汉语课本) alongside the two sentences.
- **Extension:** Students must produce a second correct version of sentence 2 using 行 instead of 可以.

---

#### Activity 2: 计时练习 Timed Practice Test — You Do (10 min) | 28–38 min

**What:** Independent, timed practice mirroring the Test 3.3 format — no talking, silent individual work.

**How:**
1. **Sentence writing (7 min):** Project the remaining 3 Test 3.3 sentences. Students write the Chinese in their exercise books, working independently against a visible countdown:
   - As soon as you enter the school gate, you can see the library.
   - I can return your calculator tomorrow.
   - Don't forget your pencil case.
2. **Vocabulary check (3 min):** Project 8 vocabulary items (mixed 中文/English); students write the translation for each.
3. Teacher reveals model answers on the projector; students self-mark both sections.

**OTR 2 (every student responds):** All students complete both timed sections independently.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the 5-pattern summary table projected throughout for reference.
- **Extension:** Students write a 6th sentence of their own choosing using any two patterns from today's summary table.

---

#### Game: 复习问答接力 Review Relay (5 min) | 38–43 min

**What:** A short, high-pace team relay reviewing vocabulary and sentence patterns before the final written check.

**Setup:** Divide the class into 4 teams (reuse groupings from earlier lessons if convenient). Teacher draws/reuses the scoreboard on the whiteboard corner. Teams raise hands to buzz in; teacher cold-calls if no hands after 5 seconds.

**Round 1 — 词汇闪卡 (3 questions, ~1 min):** 卷笔刀 → *pencil sharpener*; 日记本 → *diary*; 别 → *don't*.

**Round 2 — 改错句 (2 questions, ~2 min):** 我进校门就看见图书馆。→ *我一进校门就看见图书馆。*; 我一会儿就借给你。→ *我一会儿就还给你。*

**Round 3 — 量词问答 (3 questions, ~2 min):** 一__铅笔 → *支*; 一__课本 → *本*; 一__尺子 → *把*.

- **Scaffold:** Allow teams 5 seconds to confer before answering on Round 2.
- **Extension:** Bonus point in Round 2 if the team also names which word was missing or wrong.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Display the three SC on projector. Teacher reads each one in Chinese; students rate themselves silently: 👍 ready / ✋ getting there / 👎 need more practice. Quick show of hands for each rating — teacher notes which SC has the most 👎 responses (signals what to address before Test 3.3).

- **Self-assessment:** Students write a single Chinese sentence at the bottom of today's page: 我最需要复习______，因为______。Teacher cold-calls 1–2 students to share.

- **Exit Ticket:** Display on projector:
  > 用中文写两三句话：说说你今天忘记带了什么，你跟同学借了什么。
  >
  > (Write 2–3 sentences in Chinese: say what you forgot to bring today, and what you borrowed from a classmate.)

  Students write in exercise books. Teacher circulates and reads over shoulders — look for correct use of 忘记, 借, and word order. This mirrors the exact format Test 3.3 will expect.
