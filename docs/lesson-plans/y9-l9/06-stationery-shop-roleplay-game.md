# Lesson 9.6: Stationery Shop Roleplay + Extra Words

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 6 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to roleplay buying stationery, asking prices with 多少钱, and using 5 new Extra Words.

## Success Criteria
- I can name the 5 Extra Words for stationery items (能说出五个新的文具词)
- I can ask and answer "how much does X cost" using 多少钱 (能用"多少钱"问价钱)
- I can perform a short shop dialogue with a partner (能和同伴表演买文具的对话)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lesson 5's borrowing/lending vocabulary and dialogue pattern.

**How:** Project the Text 2 dialogue with 4 words blanked out (忘记, 借给, 行, 还); cold call students to fill each blank aloud, class echoes chorally. Then quick mini-whiteboard check: "Write the Chinese for 'calculator'."

**Connection:** "上节课我们学了怎么'借'东西。今天我们要去文具店——学怎么**买**东西，还有怎么问价钱。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. Teacher reads aloud; class echoes chorally. Keep visible for the lesson.

---

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — Extra Words (p. 84, Act. 6)

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 支 | zhī | measure word for pens/pencils (recap) |
| 礼品纸 | lǐpǐnzhǐ | gift wrapping paper |
| 钢笔 | gāngbǐ | fountain pen |
| 毛笔 | máobǐ | Chinese calligraphy brush |
| 笔记本 | bǐjìběn | notebook |

*Component note:* 钢 (steel) + 笔 (pen) = 钢笔 "fountain pen"; 毛 (hair/fur) + 笔 (pen) = 毛笔 "calligraphy brush" — the brush is traditionally made with animal hair. 笔记本 (notebook) vs 练习本 (exercise book, Lesson 1) — both use 本, but 笔记 means "notes."

#### Key Sentence Pattern: Asking Prices

**Sample dialogue (p. 87):**
> A: 一本汉语课本多少钱？ B: 一百一十八块。
> A: 一支铅笔多少钱？ B: 三块九毛。

**Structure:** [Measure word + item] + 多少钱？ Answer: [number] + 块 (+ [number] + 毛).

**Modelled examples (project each one, teacher reads aloud, class repeats):**

1. 一本笔记本多少钱？——五块。
2. 一支钢笔多少钱？——二十五块。
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 一支毛笔多少钱？——一千块！

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Choral)** "Thumbs up if 毛笔 is used for calligraphy, thumbs down if it's a ballpoint pen."
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the price '15 块 5 毛' as you would say it."
- **(Cold call)** "Priya，怎么问'how much is this notebook'？"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Guided Roleplay Model — We Do (6 min) | 20–26 min

**(Based on Act. 6, p. 84)**

**What:** Teacher models the full shop dialogue with one student volunteer, using the projected sample pattern (buyer asks for an item, asks the price, seller answers).

**How:**
- Teacher (seller) and student volunteer (buyer) perform 2 rounds using items from the vocabulary lists (Lessons 1, 4, 6).
- Class observes and notes the pattern; teacher highlights the price-answer structure (X块Y毛) on the projector.

**OTR:** Whole class watches and then chorally repeats each line of the modelled dialogue once.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the sentence pattern and a price list (5块, 15块5毛, 118块, 3块9毛) projected throughout.
- **Extension:** Ask a fluent volunteer to extend the model dialogue with one extra line (e.g. asking for a second item).

---

#### Game 1: 猜价钱 Price Guessing (6 min) | 26–32 min

**How to play:**

1. Project a stationery item (e.g. 钢笔) without a price.
2. In teams, students discuss briefly and write a guessed price in Chinese on a mini whiteboard (X块Y毛), hold up on three.
3. Teacher reveals the "real" price (teacher-set for the game); closest team without going over wins the round.
4. Repeat for 4–5 items.

**What language it practises:** Producing prices in the X块Y毛 format under light time pressure.

**Setup needed:** Projector only.

- **Scaffold:** Provide a price range on the board (0–200 块) to narrow guesses.
- **Extension:** Winning teams must say their guess as a full question-answer pair: "一支钢笔多少钱？——二十五块。"

---

#### Game 2: Shop Roleplay Relay (6 min) | 32–38 min

**How to play:**

1. In pairs, students perform a short shop dialogue (buyer asks for an item + price, seller answers), using any items from Lessons 1, 4, and today.
2. After 90 seconds, students swap partners (move one seat over) and repeat with a new item, alternating who plays buyer/seller.
3. Repeat for 2–3 rounds so students practise with multiple partners and multiple items.

**What language it practises:** Fluent, repeated production of the shop dialogue pattern with varied vocabulary.

**Setup needed:** None — projector displays the sentence pattern as a reminder throughout.

- **Scaffold:** Students may keep a card in front of them listing 5 items + made-up prices to draw from.
- **Extension:** Fluent pairs must negotiate ("太贵了！便宜一点，行吗？") as an improvised extra line — flagged as bonus, not required.

---

#### Quick Game: Extra Words Slap the Board (5 min) | 38–43 min

**How to play:** Project the 5 Extra Words scattered on screen. Teacher calls an English meaning; students write the matching 汉字 on mini whiteboards and hold up. Fastest correct answer scores a point for their team.

- **Setup needed:** Projector, mini whiteboards.
- **Scaffold:** Keep pinyin visible next to each word.
- **Extension:** Winning students must also give the price-question sentence for that item.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the first; a student volunteer reads the second and third.

- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE shop dialogue line of their choice, e.g. "一本笔记本多少钱？"

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要练习'借'东西的对话，还要做一个调查——你会借给同学什么，不会借什么？"
