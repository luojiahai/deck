# Lesson 9.4: Measure Words for Stationery (支/本/把) + Naming Items

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 3 — School Life
**Lesson:** Lesson 9 — Stationery 文具 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name all the stationery/school items from this unit and use the correct measure word — 支, 本, or 把 — for each.

## Success Criteria
- I can name at least 12 stationery/school items in Chinese (能说出至少十二个文具的名称)
- I can say the correct measure word + item for at least 10 items (能正确使用量词"支"、"本"、"把")
- I can explain which category of object each measure word is used for (能解释每个量词用于哪一类东西)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)

**What:** Lessons 1–3 vocabulary (11 stationery items, 一……就……, complement-of-result pairs) as a rapid consolidation before adding measure words today.

**How:** Project 6 quick-fire prompts mixing all three lessons' content (e.g. "尺子" → *ruler*; "一……就……" sentence starter → complete it; "找______" → complete with the correct complement). Students write answers on mini whiteboards, hold up on three. Cold call to confirm 2–3 items.

**Connection:** "你们已经会说很多文具的名字了。但是中文说'一个东西'的时候，需要用对的量词——不是所有东西都用'个'！今天我们专门学文具的量词。"

---

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)

Project the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. Teacher reads aloud; class echoes chorally. Keep visible for the lesson.

#### Key Grammar: Measure Words for Stationery

Present the measure word table explicitly, building on 个/幢 from Lesson 8 (School Facilities):

| 汉字 | 拼音 | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 支 | zhī | pens, pencils (long, thin, held items) | 一支铅笔 |
| 本 | běn | books, notebooks | 一本练习本 / 一本课本 |
| 把 | bǎ | items with handles | 一把尺子 |

Write on projector: ❌ 一**个**铅笔 → ✅ 一**支**铅笔。 "铅笔、钢笔这些细长的东西，用'支'。本子、课本这些书本类的东西，用'本'。有'柄'（handle）的东西，比如尺子，用'把'。"

#### CFU Checkpoints

- **(Gesture)** Project "一**支**铅笔" and "一**个**铅笔" side by side. "哪个对？右手指对的那个。"
- **(Cold call)** "Priya，'一______练习本'？" → "Alex，'一______尺子'？"
- **(Choral — error correction)** Project: 我有一个课本。 "这个句子有错误。哪里错了？大家一起说！" → Class calls out "一**本**课本！"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 看图说名字 — We Do, Speaking (7 min) | 20–27 min

**(Based on Act. 7, p. 86)**

**What:** Display the 14 stationery/school item pictures from Act. 7 on the projector, one at a time. Students name each item chorally.

**How:**
- Teacher projects each picture for 3 seconds: class chorally names the item.
- After naming, the 汉字 fades in to confirm.
- After all 14: display all at once. Cold call individual students to point and name.
- Final round: teacher says the English name, class chorally says the Chinese — no visual support.

**OTR (every student responds):** Choral naming for every picture; final round is 100% response by design.

- **Scaffold:** Keep pinyin visible during the first pass through all 14 pictures; remove pinyin for the second and third passes.
- **Extension:** After naming each item, fluent speakers must immediately add the measure word: "铅笔——一支铅笔。"

---

#### Activity 2: 量词接龙 Measure Word Chain Drill — You Do, Pair Speaking (8 min) | 27–35 min

**(Based on Act. 8, p. 87)**

**What:** In pairs, students take turns saying "measure word + item" for the 13 items in Act. 8, building speed and accuracy.

**How:**
- Teacher models 3 examples first: 一支铅笔，一本练习本，一把尺子.
- In pairs, Partner A says an item name; Partner B must respond with the correct measure word + item (e.g. A: "课本" → B: "一本课本"). Swap roles after 6 items.
- Cold call 4–5 students individually to say one measure word + item pair each, no partner support.

**OTR:** Pair drill requires every student to respond multiple times; cold call checks individual accuracy.

- **Scaffold:** Keep the measure word table (支/本/把) projected throughout.
- **Extension:** Students must also say a full sentence: "我的书包里有一支铅笔。"

---

#### Game: Telephone (传声筒) Measure Word Relay (8 min) | 35–43 min

**How to play:**

1. Divide the class into 2–3 teams, each in a line.
2. Teacher whispers a "measure word + item" phrase (e.g. 一把尺子) to the first student in each line.
3. Each student whispers what they heard to the next student down the line. The last student says the phrase aloud.
4. Compare to the original — correct = 1 point for the team. Repeat with 4–5 phrases.

**What language it practises:** Careful listening and accurate reproduction of measure word + item combinations.

**Setup needed:** None beyond the classroom — teacher needs a list of 5 target phrases ready.

- **Scaffold:** Start with shorter, more familiar phrases (一支铅笔) before moving to less familiar ones (一本日记本).
- **Extension:** For the final round, use a full sentence instead of just the phrase (e.g. 我借了一把尺子。) to raise the difficulty.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the first; a student volunteer reads the second and third.

- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion in turn.

- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write the correct measure word for 3 items:
  > 一__铅笔，一__课本，一__尺子。
  Teacher circulates and scans for 支/本/把 accuracy.

- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学怎么'借'东西和'还'东西——一段新的对话。"
