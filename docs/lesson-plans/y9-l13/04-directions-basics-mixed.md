# Asking the Way 请问，怎么走？

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood and Getting Around 社区、问路
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask for directions politely and to give a simple two-step route.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 请问，……在哪儿？ and 请问，去……怎么走？
- I can say 过马路 and 一直往前走
- I can join two steps with 先……，然后…… (先过马路，然后一直往前走)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed A5 copies of the textbook p.129 map, one per pair
- Six large arrow cards for the matching activity (or project p.129 Ex 8)
- Clear floor space at the front for TPR
- Audio: CD 51 (textbook p.127)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lessons 1–3 — the ten neighbourhood words and all five position patterns.
**How:** Two parts.
**(a) Pair dialogue from memory, 5 min.** Students take out the neighbourhood maps they drew at the end of last lesson. A describes their map to B for sixty seconds without stopping; B listens and asks one question at the end. Swap. Then three students describe theirs to the class. Teacher circulates and notes two sentences to praise and one word-order slip to fix on the board afterwards.
**(b) Workbook p.147 Ex 8 cloze, 3 min.** On the board or projected. Word box: 船 中间 后面 方便 附近 前面 有 离 上边. The 离岛 passage — 小明家住在一个离岛上，生活不太＿。他们家＿没有超级市场… Students fill it on whiteboards; check as a class. Every gap is a Lesson 1–3 word, so this is the cumulative recall for the first half of the sequence.
**Connection:** 你们已经会说「银行在邮局对面」。可是如果你在街上，不知道怎么去银行呢？今天我们学问路。You can describe where a bank *is*. Today you learn what to do when you're standing in the street and don't know how to get there.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Say plainly that today's language is the most *usable* in the whole unit — it is what you'd actually say in a Chinese city — and that 请问 is the word that makes the difference between asking and demanding.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 咖啡馆 | kāfēiguǎn | café | SVG — shopfront with an awning, a steaming cup on the sign |
| 1 | 过 | guò | cross | SVG — a zebra crossing with an arrow going across it |
| 2 | 一直 | yìzhí | straight | SVG — a long straight arrow along a road |
| 2 | 往前 | wǎngqián | forward | SVG — an arrow pointing ahead from a standing figure |
| 3 | 红绿灯 | hónglǜdēng | traffic lights | SVG — a traffic light, three lamps, top one red |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- 咖啡馆 — 咖啡 they met in Lesson 12 as a drink; 馆 is the building word they already have in 图书馆, 体育馆. A coffee-building.
- 过马路 — 过 = cross. 马路 is already known.
- 红绿灯 — red-green-lamp. Chinese names it by its colours, English by its function.
- 往 and 向 both mean "towards"; they'll meet 向 next lesson and the two are interchangeable here.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. **咖啡馆**就在银行对面。 — The café is right opposite the bank.
2. 你先**过**马路。 — First cross the road.
3. **过**马路，**咖啡馆**就在你的左边。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 咖啡馆在 ________ 。 · 你先过 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write a two-line exchange: one person asks where the café is, the other answers with a position *and* a distance in minutes.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 你**一直**走。 — Keep going straight.
2. 请**往前**走。 — Please walk forward.
3. 你**一直****往前**走，五分钟就到了。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 你一直 ________ 走。 · ________ 往前走，就到了。
**Extension —** Give the same instruction twice, once to a friend and once politely to a stranger — what changes? (请问 / 请 / 您)

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 前面有**红绿灯**。 — There are traffic lights ahead.
2. 你看到**红绿灯**就过马路。 — When you see the lights, cross the road.
3. 一直往前走，看到**红绿灯**，咖啡馆就在对面。 — pulls in both earlier cycles.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 看到红绿灯，________ 。
**Extension —** 用「先……，然后……」把三个动作连起来：先过马路，然后一直往前走，看到红绿灯就到了。

#### Recall slide
After cycle 3: all five words on one board, characters only, no pinyin, no English. Class reads them back. If it stalls, reteach before moving on.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 先……，然后……

Think aloud: "A route is a sequence, and Chinese marks the sequence out loud. 先 = first, 然后 = then. Put the two actions in the order you'd actually walk them."

1. 你先过马路。
2. 你先过马路，然后一直往前走。
3. 你一直往前走，看到红绿灯就过马路。
4. 请问，去银行怎么走？— 你先过马路，然后一直往前走，走五分钟就到了。 *(hardest — the full question-and-answer pair, three steps plus a duration)*

#### Text 2 — dialogues 1 and 2 (p.127, CD 51)
Play CD 51, dialogues 1 and 2 only. Students follow in the book. Play again, class reads chorally with the audio.

> 1. A: 请问，咖啡馆在哪儿？ B: 你先过马路，然后一直往前走，走五分钟就到了。
> 2. A: 请问，去银行怎么走？ B: 你一直往前走，看到红绿灯向左拐，再走五分钟就到了。

Comprehension questions, in Chinese:
- 去咖啡馆要走几分钟？
- 去银行的时候，在哪儿要转弯？

(Dialogue 3 and 向左拐 are next lesson — if a student asks about 拐, say it's turning and that they'll learn it properly next time. Don't teach it today.)

#### CFU Checkpoints
*(No answer slides — pose the check and take responses live.)*
- Thumbs up/down: "过马路 means 'cross the road'." / "一直 means 'turn'."
- Whiteboards: 写 — first cross the road, then walk straight ahead.
- Error correction on the board: 你先走一直，然后过马路。 — fix the word order.
- Cold call: 请问，去咖啡馆怎么走？ — take a two-step answer from one student.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Arrow matching + the shop wall — Vocabulary consolidation, We Do (8 min)
**Summary board:** all five new words, characters and arrow diagrams, up throughout.

Textbook p.129 Ex 8. Six arrow diagrams, six phrases. Students match them on whiteboards:
a) 往右拐 b) 向左转 c) 一直往前走 d) 过马路 e) 在第一个路口往右拐 f) 看见红绿灯往左拐

Only c and d are taught; a, b, e and f are next lesson's — so this runs as a **recognition** task. Students match what they know first, then guess the rest from the arrows and say what they think each new phrase means. That guess *is* the pre-teaching for Lesson 5.

**Extension —** Write your own seventh instruction, draw the arrow diagram for it, and hand it to a partner to decode.

**Then textbook p.128 Ex 7, the shop wall — recognition only, 3 min.** Twelve shop pictures, twelve names: 中药房 · 剧场 · 灯具店 · 五金店 · 礼品店 · 糕饼店 · 理发店 · 电器店 · 茶馆 · 钟表店 · 便利店 · 西洋乐器店. **None of these is a New Word and none is examinable** — say so before you start, so nobody tries to memorise twelve shops. Run it as a two-minute matching race in pairs, then take the answers. The value is that almost every one is decodable from characters they already have: 中药 + 房, 灯具 + 店, 理发 + 店, 钟表 + 店. Ask two students to explain how they worked one out; that reasoning is the point, not the vocabulary.

#### Activity 2: Text 2 role-play — You Do (7 min)
Pairs perform dialogues 1 and 2 from p.127, first reading, then with books closed. Then they substitute: keep the structure, change the destination to a building from Lessons 1–3 and change the number of minutes.
> A: 请问，教堂在哪儿？ B: 你先过马路，然后一直往前走，走三分钟就到了。

Take three pairs to the front. Keep the frame on the board for round one, take it down for round two.

**Extension —** B gives a route that is deliberately *wrong* for the projected map, and A has to notice and correct it in Chinese: 不对吧，过马路以后应该往前走，不是往回走。Then the pair runs a version where A is in a hurry — 我很急，走路要几分钟？坐车呢？ — and B has to answer both.

#### Activity 3: Map pair dialogue — You Do (4 min)
Textbook p.129 Ex 9, one A5 map per pair, using only the reference sentences available today (b, c and the 请问 question forms). Situations: 1. 去邮局 2. 去教堂 3. 去市政大楼.

**Extension —** Do all three situations as one continuous journey — from the post office to the church to the city hall — so that each new route starts where the last one ended. Say the whole thing without stopping.

#### Game: Simon Says 老师说 — TPR (4 min)
Clear space at the front. Teacher gives direction commands; students act them out. Only obey if it starts with 老师说.
> 老师说：一直往前走！ · 老师说：过马路！ · 老师说：停！ · 往前走！（trap — no 老师说）
Practises instant comprehension of the direction verbs with no English in the room.
- **Setup needed:** nothing, just floor space
- **Harder version:** the commands get chained — 老师说：先过马路，然后一直往前走，看到红绿灯就停 — and students have to execute all three steps in order. One wrong step and they're out. A fluent student takes over as caller for the last two rounds and must invent their own chains.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; ask a student to demonstrate criterion 3 with a live sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 by show of hands for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** on a slip — 翻译：First cross the road, then walk straight ahead for five minutes.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们学「在第二个路口往右拐」——怎么说转弯。Next lesson: turning, and counting intersections.
