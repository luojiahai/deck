# Build the Town, Give the Route 我在哪儿？

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood and Getting Around 社区、问路
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to give a full multi-step route across a map and to follow one we hear.

## Success Criteria
- I can use 看到……就…… to say what to look out for on the way
- I can finish a route with 就在你的右手边 or 你就可以看到……了
- I can give a three-step route across a map that someone else can actually follow (说一条别人走得通的路线)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Large blank street grid drawn on the whiteboard before the lesson (four roads across, four down)
- A4 cards, one per pair, for drawing a building to add to the class map — coloured pencils
- Blu-tack
- Printed A5 copies of the textbook p.131 street map (lettered A–H), one per student
- A few printed copies of the textbook p.130 map, for groups who finish Activity 2 early
- Audio: CD 52 (textbook p.131)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lessons 4–5 — the six direction phrases, 先……然后……, and 第……个路口.
**How:** Slap the Board, direction edition. Six cards on the board: 往右拐 · 向左转 · 一直往前走 · 过马路 · 第一个路口 · 红绿灯. Two students up, teacher says the English, first to touch scores. Rotate every two rounds until everyone's been up once.
Then thirty seconds on whiteboards: 写 — turn right at the second intersection.
**Connection:** 到现在为止，你们的路线一次只有两三步。今天我们走完整条路——而且要让别人真的找得到。So far your routes have been two or three steps. Today we do the whole journey — and the test is whether someone else can actually follow it.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Point out criterion 3 is judged by other students, not by you: if your partner ends up at the wrong building, the route wasn't good enough. That's the standard for the rest of the lesson.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 看到 | kàndào | see | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 1 | 右手 | yòushǒu | right hand | SVG — a right hand, palm out, with a small arrow to the right |
| 2 | 钟 | zhōng | clock | SVG — a round wall clock reading five past |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- 看到 — 看 is *look*, 到 makes it *arrive at looking*: you looked and it landed. Same 到 as in 走到, 就到了. Chinese marks the difference between looking and seeing.
- 右手边 — literally "right-hand side". 左手边 works the same way. This is how the book ends nearly every route.
- 钟 on its own is a clock; the reason it's here is 分钟 — minutes — which they have been using since Lesson 1 without ever meeting the character.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 你**看到**红绿灯就过马路。 — When you see the lights, cross the road.
2. 银行就在你的**右手**边。 — The bank is on your right-hand side.
3. 一直往前走，**看到**教堂，公园就在你的**右手**边。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 你看到 ________ 就 ________ 。 · ________ 就在你的右手边。
**Extension —** Write the same route twice — once ending 就在你的右手边 and once ending 你就可以看到……了 — then say which sounds more helpful to a lost person and why.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 那个**钟**在市政大楼的前面。 — That clock is in front of the city hall.
2. 走五分**钟**就到了。 — Five minutes' walk and you're there.
3. 看到那个大**钟**，再走两分**钟**就到了。 — pulls in cycle 1.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 走 ________ 分钟就到了。
**Extension —** Give two versions of the same journey — walking and by bus — with a different duration for each: 走路要二十分钟，坐车十分钟就到了。

#### Key Sentence Pattern — closing a route properly

Think aloud: "A route isn't finished when you stop giving instructions. It's finished when the other person knows they've arrived. Chinese has two ways to land it — tell them which side it's on, or tell them they'll be able to see it."

1. 医院就在你的右手边。
2. 你就可以看到教堂了。
3. 看到红绿灯向右拐，银行就在你的右手边。
4. 你先过马路，然后一直走，在第三个路口向右拐，再一直往前走五分钟。你就可以看到市中心公园了。 *(hardest — the full p.131 model, four steps and a landing)*

Read the p.131 Ex 13 model paragraph together, chorally, then ask: 一共有几步？ Count them on fingers as a class — 过马路、一直走、向右拐、往前走五分钟. Four steps, then the landing.

#### CFU Checkpoints
*(No answer slides — pose the check and take responses live.)*
- Thumbs up/down: "看到 and 看 mean exactly the same thing." *(No — 到 adds the result.)*
- Whiteboards: 写 — the post office is on your right-hand side.
- Error correction on the board: 你看红绿灯就过马路。 — one character missing. Which?
- Cold call: 「走五分钟就到了」是什么意思？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Build the class town — We Do (8 min)
**Summary board:** every word from all six lessons — 市中心 生活 方便 邮局 诊所 银行 百货公司 市政大楼 教堂 公园 咖啡馆 过 一直 往前 红绿灯 向 拐 转 路口 第 看到 右手 钟 — characters only, on screen throughout.

The blank street grid is already on the whiteboard. Each pair gets an A4 card, picks **one** building from the summary board, draws it, and labels it in characters. Pairs come up one at a time, blu-tack their card onto the grid wherever they like, and — this is the price of a spot — say one sentence in Chinese placing it: 我们的银行在第二个路口，就在邮局对面。

By the end there is a shared town on the board with twelve to fifteen labelled buildings, and every student has said one placement sentence aloud.

**Extension —** Instead of placing your own building, listen to the placement sentence of the pair before you and put *their* card up where their sentence says. If you put it in the wrong place, they get to correct you in Chinese.

#### Activity 2: 我在哪儿？ — the guessing game, You Do (11 min)
This is the main event, played against the class map.

Groups of three. Each group secretly picks a destination on the board and writes a **three-step route** to it, starting from a fixed point marked 你 at the bottom of the grid. Four minutes to write.

Then each group reads its route aloud — slowly, once — and the rest of the class traces it in their heads and writes the destination on their whiteboards. On "三、二、一" everyone holds up. Any group whose route lands more than half the class in the right place scores 2 points; a route that lands nobody anywhere scores 0 and gets rewritten.

Target language on the board for the first two rounds only, then removed:
> 你先过马路，然后一直往前走。在第二个路口向左拐。看到红绿灯，________ 就在你的右手边。

**Extension —** The group gives its route **backwards** — from the destination to 你 — and the class has to work out where it started. Or: the group gives a four-step route containing one deliberate error, and the class has to say which step is wrong and fix it in Chinese, not just point at the map.

**For groups who finish their route early:** hand them the textbook p.130 Ex 11 map and its four Situations — 去医院 · 去体育中心 · 去咖啡馆 · 去市政大楼. It is a harder map than the class grid, with more streets and no help, and the book's model answer there is a genuine three-turn route. They write all four and run them on each other while the rest of the class is still drafting.

#### Activity 3: CD 52 listening — team scoring, You Do (4 min)
Textbook p.131 Ex 12, one A5 map each, played as a team round. Six directions, six places to letter: 超市 · 邮局 · 诊所 · 银行 · 市政大楼 · 教堂. Play twice. Teams total their correct letters and add them to the running score from Activity 2.

**Extension —** One listen only, and instead of letters write down the *turn* in each of the six — 往右拐 / 过马路 / 往右转两次 / 在第二个路口过马路 / 往左拐 / 往右拐. Then read your list back and see if the class agrees.

### Plenary (43–50 min)

This is the last lesson of the sequence, so the plenary looks back over all six, not just today.

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read today's three aloud. Then put all six lesson titles on screen and ask: 哪一节课最难？ Take three answers, in Chinese if they can.
- **Self-assessment:** six-point check on whiteboards, one line per lesson, 👍 / 😐 / 👎 —
  1. 我家住在哪儿 (离……远／不远)
  2. 社区里有什么 (诊所、银行、百货公司、市政大楼)
  3. 前面、后面 + 说一段话
  4. 请问，怎么走？(过马路、一直往前走)
  5. 第……个路口、往左／右拐
  6. 看到……就……、右手边
  Scan the room and note which line collects the most 😐 — that's what to warm up before the unit test.
- **Exit ticket:** on a slip — write a **complete route** from the classroom to any place in the school, three steps minimum, ending with 就在你的右手边 or 你就可以看到……了.
- **Preview:** 下一课是第十四课《问路》——一样是问路，可是这次要坐公共汽车：坐几路车？坐几站？在哪站下车？Next textbook lesson keeps asking the way, but on a bus: which route number, how many stops, where to get off.
