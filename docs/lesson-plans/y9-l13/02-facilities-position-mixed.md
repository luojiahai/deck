# What's in My Neighbourhood 我家附近有什么

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood and Getting Around 社区、问路
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the main buildings in a neighbourhood and say where each one is.

## Success Criteria
- I can name four neighbourhood buildings in Chinese (诊所、银行、百货公司、市政大楼)
- I can use A 就在 B 对面／旁边 to place one building against another
- I can use A 的左边／右边是 B to describe a row of buildings

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Two sets of character cards for Slap the Character: 诊所 · 银行 · 百货公司 · 市政大楼 · 邮局 · 市中心
- Printed A5 copies of the textbook p.125 map, one per pair
- No audio this lesson

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1 — 市中心, 生活, 方便, 邮局 and the A 离 B 远／不远 pattern.
**How:** Slap the Board warm-up on the four words from last lesson (two students at the board, teacher says the English). Then whiteboards, three quick ones: (1) 写：the post office is not far from my home. (2) 写：I live in the city centre. (3) Fix this — 我家离学校很方便。 The third is the one to spend time on: 方便 describes *life*, not *distance*.
**Connection:** 上节课我们说了「我家离……远／不远」。今天我们说「附近有什么」——把那些地方的名字学会。Last lesson you could say how far away things were. Today you learn what those things are called.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Read intention chorally. Point out that criterion 2 and criterion 3 are two different shapes for the same job — one uses 就在, one uses 是 — and by the end of the lesson they should be able to pick either.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 诊所 | zhěnsuǒ | clinic | SVG — small building with a red cross, stethoscope in the window |
| 1 | 银行 | yínháng | bank | SVG — a classical façade with columns and a coin stack |
| 2 | 百货公司 | bǎihuò gōngsī | department store | SVG — a multi-storey shopfront with a jacket and a handbag in the display window |
| 2 | 市政大楼 | shìzhèng dàlóu | city hall | SVG — a wide civic building with columns, steps and a flag |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Useful character notes:
- 诊所 — 所 = place, as in 厕所. A 所 is a small place; that's why a clinic is a 诊所 and a hospital is a 医院.
- 银行 — 银 is *silver*. The silver trade: a bank. Note the reading is **háng**, not xíng.
- 百货公司 — literally "hundred goods company". 公司 they already know.
- 市政大楼 — 市 city (from 市中心 last lesson) + 政 government + 大楼 big building.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. **诊所**离我家很近。 — The clinic is very close to my home.
2. 我妈妈在**银行**工作。 — My mum works at a bank.
3. **银行**就在**诊所**旁边。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 离我家很近。 · 银行就在 ________ 旁边。
**Extension —** 写两句，用「因为……所以……」：因为诊所离我家很近，所以生病的时候很方便。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我们常常去**百货公司**买东西。 — We often go to the department store to buy things.
2. **市政大楼**在市中心。 — The city hall is in the city centre.
3. **市政大楼**就在**百货公司**对面。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我们常常去 ________ 买东西。 · ________ 就在 ________ 对面。
**Extension —** Place all four of today's buildings in one street, in one sentence chain: 银行的左边是诊所，右边是百货公司，市政大楼在百货公司对面。

#### Key Sentence Pattern — two ways to place a building

**Shape A — A 就在 B 对面／旁边／附近**
Think aloud: "就 here means *right* — not 'it's near the bank', but 'it's right opposite the bank'. Drop 就 and the sentence still works; keep it and it sounds like a local."

**Shape B — A 的左边／右边 是 B**
Think aloud: "This one flips it round. You start from the landmark, then say what's on its side. 邮局的左边是诊所 — on the post office's left is the clinic."

1. 银行就在邮局对面。
2. 诊所就在我家附近。
3. 邮局的左边是诊所，右边是银行。
4. 市政大楼在百货公司的前面，百货公司的左边是银行，右边是邮局。 *(hardest — three placements in one breath, and it previews 前面 for next lesson)*

#### CFU Checkpoints
*(No answer slides — pose the check and take responses live.)*
- Quick translate, cold call: "How do you say 'bank'?" then "…and what's the tone on 行?"
- Whiteboards: 写 — the clinic is right opposite the city hall.
- Error correction on the board: 银行在对面邮局。 — What's wrong? (word order: the landmark comes before 对面)
- Point at the projected p.125 map and ask three students each one: 银行在哪儿？ 诊所在哪儿？ 百货公司在哪儿？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 我的一条街 — Vocabulary consolidation, We Do (8 min)
**Summary board:** all four new words plus 邮局 from Lesson 1, characters and pictures only, on screen throughout.

**Open with the textbook p.123 Ex 1 naming pass (2 min).** Project the 14 building pictures and run down them fast, the class calling each one out chorally. Most are known stock — 超级市场, 电影院, 书店, 飞机场, 教堂, 公园, 文具店, 家具店, 医院 — and four are today's. The point is speed: they should come without thinking. Mark the ones that stall and come back to them in the game.

Then each student draws a quick row of five buildings on their whiteboard — all five words, in any order they like — then writes **four** sentences describing the row, using a different position word each time (对面, 旁边, 的左边是, 的右边是). The instruction is the constraint: four sentences, four different position words, all five buildings mentioned somewhere.

Three minutes drawing and writing, then two students hold up their whiteboard and read their street out while the class checks the description matches the drawing.

**Extension —** Don't draw first. Listen to a partner describe *their* street in Chinese, draw it from the description alone, then compare drawings. Whoever's drawing is wrong has to say in Chinese which sentence they misheard.

#### Activity 2: Map pair dialogue — You Do (9 min)
Textbook p.125 Ex 4, one A5 map per pair. A asks where a place is, B answers from the map, then swap:
> A: 医院在哪儿？ B: 在我家后面。
> A: 百货公司在哪儿？ B: 就在银行对面。

Work through at least six places each. Keep the word bank (对面 旁边 附近 的左边是 的右边是) on the board for the first round and take it down for the second.

**Extension —** B answers without naming the place directly — B describes only its *position*, and A has to work out which building it is: B: 它就在邮局对面，银行的左边。 A: 是诊所吗？ Then A and B build one route across the map together, naming three buildings in order.

#### Game: Slap the Character (6 min)
Two teams, one player each at the board. Six cards up: 诊所 · 银行 · 百货公司 · 市政大楼 · 邮局 · 市中心. Teacher says the word in English, first to touch the correct card scores. Rotate players every two rounds so everyone gets a turn.
- **Setup needed:** two sets of the six character cards, blu-tack
- **Harder version:** the teacher stops saying the word and says its *position* instead — 就在邮局对面的那个 — and the player has to slap the card the description points to. Requires listening to the whole sentence, not just spotting a noun.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud, then ask "Which one was hardest today?" and take two answers.
- **Self-assessment:** traffic light on mini whiteboards — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 held up for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** workbook p.148 Ex 9, items 4 and 5 — complete both stems on a slip: 超市 ____________ 。 · 市政大楼 ____________ 。 Each must place the building against something else.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们学「前面」和「后面」，然后把整个社区写下来。Next lesson we add front and back, and then describe a whole neighbourhood in one go.
