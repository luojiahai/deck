# Front, Back and the Whole Street 前面、后面

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood and Getting Around 社区、问路
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to use 前面 and 后面, and to describe a whole neighbourhood in a connected paragraph.

## Success Criteria
- I can name 教堂 and 公园 in Chinese
- I can use A 在 B 的前面／后面 to place two things
- I can describe a neighbourhood in four or more sentences that hang together (说一段话介绍我的社区)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Sentence cards for the walkaround game — one word or phrase per card, enough for one card per student (see Activity 2)
- A4 paper + coloured pencils, one sheet per student
- Audio: CD 50 (textbook p.126)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lessons 1–2 — 市中心, 生活, 方便, 邮局, 诊所, 银行, 百货公司, 市政大楼, and the two placement shapes (就在……对面／旁边 · 的左边／右边是).
**How:** Correct the Teacher. Put four sentences on the board, three of them wrong, and give the class ninety seconds on whiteboards to find and fix them:
1. 银行在对面邮局。 *(→ 银行在邮局对面。)*
2. 我家离市中心很方便。 *(→ …很远／不远)*
3. 诊所的左边是百货公司。 *(correct — leave it)*
4. 市政大楼就在旁边公园。 *(→ …公园旁边。)*
Take the fixes from the room, not from the front.
**Connection:** 到现在为止，我们只会说「左边、右边、对面、旁边」。今天加上「前面」和「后面」——然后第一次把整个社区连起来说。So far every placement has been sideways. Today we add depth — front and back — and then put the whole street together in one paragraph.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Flag criterion 3 as the big one: today is the first time they produce a *paragraph*, not single sentences, and that is what the unit test's essay section will ask for.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 前面 | qián mian | front | SVG — two boxes on a ground line, the near one shaded, arrow labelling it |
| 1 | 后面 | hòu mian | back | SVG — same two boxes, the far one shaded |
| 2 | 教堂 | jiàotáng | church | SVG — a church with a pointed roof, arched door and a cross |
| 2 | 公园 | gōngyuán | park | SVG — trees, a path, a bench and a fountain |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes worth making:
- 面 on its own means *side; aspect*. They now have 前面, 后面 and already know 对面 — same 面 in all three.
- Both 前面 and 后面 take a neutral tone on 面 in the book's pinyin: qián mian, hòu mian.
- 公园 — 公 public (as in 公共汽车) + 园 garden. A public garden.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 银行在邮局的**前面**。 — The bank is in front of the post office.
2. 我家的**后面**有一个超市。 — There's a supermarket behind my house.
3. 诊所在银行的**前面**，百货公司在银行的**后面**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 在 ________ 的前面。 · 我家的后面有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write one sentence placing three buildings using three *different* position words, at least one of which is 前面 or 后面.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **教堂**离我家很近，走路十分钟就到了。 — The church is close; ten minutes' walk.
2. **公园**在市政大楼的后面。 — The park is behind the city hall.
3. **教堂**的旁边是一个**公园**。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 教堂在 ________ 。 · ________ 的旁边是一个公园。
**Extension —** 用「因为……所以……」：因为我家附近有一个大公园，所以我每天都去跑步。Then add where the park is, relative to two other buildings.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — stringing placements into a paragraph

This is the day the single sentences join up. Model Text 1 (p.122) line by line on screen, then show what makes it a *paragraph* rather than a list: it starts from 我家, and every following sentence hangs off something already mentioned.

1. 我家住在市中心，生活很方便。 *(opens with the speaker)*
2. 邮局离我家不远。 *(hangs off 我家)*
3. 超级市场就在邮局对面。 *(hangs off 邮局)*
4. 邮局的左边是诊所，右边是银行。市政大楼在百货公司的前面。我家附近还有一个大教堂。教堂的后面是一个小学，旁边是一个公园。 *(hardest — the chain runs the full length of the text; note how 还有 introduces a new landmark and 教堂 then becomes the anchor for the next two)*

Read Text 1 aloud with the class following, then chorally, then ask three comprehension questions in Chinese:
- 超级市场在哪儿？
- 邮局的右边是什么？
- 教堂的后面有什么？

#### CFU Checkpoints
*(No answer slides — pose the check and take responses live.)*
- Gesture response: "I'll name two buildings and a position word. Point forward if it's 前面, point back if it's 后面." Run six.
- Whiteboards: 写 — the park is behind the church.
- Error correction on the board: 公园在后面教堂。 — fix it.
- Cold call: 教堂的后面是什么？ (answer from the text, in Chinese)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 社区总览 — Vocabulary consolidation, We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** every word from Lessons 1–3 — 市中心, 生活, 方便, 邮局, 诊所, 银行, 百货公司, 市政大楼, 教堂, 公园 — characters and pictures only, up for the whole activity.

Pairs get one minute to look, then the board is **covered**. Each pair writes down as many of the ten as they can from memory in characters, then uncovers and checks. Second round: for each word they missed, they write one sentence placing it next to a word they *did* remember.

**Extension —** Instead of a list, write a connected description of an imaginary street that contains all ten, in four sentences, each hanging off the one before. Board stays covered throughout.

#### Activity 2: Sentence-card walkaround — We Do (7 min)
Textbook p.124 Ex 3. Every student gets one card with a word or phrase on it — 我们学校 · 离 · 很远 · 有 · 附近 · 市中心 · 银行 · 每个星期天 · 在 · 对面 · 百货公司 · 教堂 · 公园 · 的前面 · 的后面 · 是 · 一个 · 大 (make up the numbers to match the class). Students walk around, find others whose cards combine with theirs, and form a grammatical sentence. Groups read their sentence out; the class votes 对 or 不对.

Run it twice, redistributing cards between rounds.

**Extension —** A fluent student takes the spare-card role: they hold a card the sentence does *not* need (a stray 很 or 了), and their job is to try to join every group that forms. Each group has to reject them out loud in Chinese and say why — 不行，因为这句不要「了」。

#### Activity 3: Listen and label the house — You Do (5 min)
Textbook p.126 Ex 5, **CD 50**. Two-storey floor plan, boxes A–J. Students write the letter for each of the nine rooms: 哥哥的卧室 · 客厅 · 浴室 · 爸爸、妈妈的卧室 · 厨房 · 书房 · 小明的卧室 · 电视房 · 活动室. Play twice. Check as a class by asking 浴室是哪个？ and taking the letter plus the reason in Chinese.

This one is doing double duty — the rooms are Book 2 revision, but the *position language* in the audio (在……对面, 在……的隔壁, 在右手边, 一上楼就看见了) is exactly today's target, heard at speed.

**Extension —** Listen once only, and instead of letters write down the five position phrases you hear. Then use three of them in your own sentences about the school building.

#### Activity 4: Translation + your own map — You Do (5 min)
Two parts, three minutes then two.

**(a) Workbook p.147 Ex 7 — translate from English to Chinese.** All four items, written in exercise books. Every one of them needs a position word taught in the last three lessons, and this is the format of **Unit 5 Test Part 3**:
1. The post office is not far from my home.
2. The clinic is in front of the park.
3. The church is opposite to the city hall.
4. The bank is behind the department store.

Take the four answers from the room and write the agreed version on the board.

**(b) Textbook p.126 Ex 6 — sketch your own neighbourhood.** A4 paper, rough map, five places minimum, labelled in characters. No writing yet — the map is the artefact, and it is what the exit ticket and next lesson's review both run off.

**Extension —** After the four translations, write four more of your own in English, swap with a partner, and translate theirs. Yours must each use a position word the book's four didn't.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; ask specifically whether criterion 3 — the paragraph — felt possible today.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** on a slip — write **three** sentences about the map you just drew, each using a different position word, at least one of them 前面 or 后面. (This also closes off workbook p.148 Ex 9 items 3, 7 and 8 — 公园＿, 教堂＿, 我家附近＿ — so steer students towards those three stems if they stall.)
- **Preview:** 下节课我们走到街上——学怎么问路：「请问，咖啡馆在哪儿？」Next lesson we leave the description behind and start asking for directions.
