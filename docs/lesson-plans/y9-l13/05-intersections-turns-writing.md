# Turning and Counting Intersections 第二个路口往右拐

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood and Getting Around 社区、问路
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to write directions that tell someone where to turn.

## Success Criteria
- I can use 往／向 + 左／右 + 拐／转 to say which way to turn
- I can count intersections with 第……个路口
- I can write a three-step route for someone reading a map (写路线)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed A4 copies of the workbook p.152 town map, one per student
- Printed A5 copies of the workbook p.148 Ex 10 reading passage, one per student
- Exercise books — this is a writing lesson, students need somewhere to keep the routes
- No audio this lesson

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4 — 咖啡馆, 过, 一直, 往前, 红绿灯 and 先……，然后……
**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards, five items, hold up after each:
1. 写：cross the road
2. 写：walk straight ahead
3. 写：traffic lights
4. 翻译：你先过马路，然后一直往前走。
5. Fix this — 你先一直走往前。
Then one cold call: 请问，去咖啡馆怎么走？ — take a full two-step answer.
**Connection:** 上节课你们会说「一直往前走」。可是路不会一直是直的。今天我们学怎么转弯，还有怎么数路口。Last lesson your routes could only go straight. Real streets turn — so today we learn how to turn, and how to say *which* corner to turn at.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Be explicit that today is a writing lesson: the routes go in exercise books, not just into the air, because **the unit test asks them to write directions from a map** (Test Part 7, workbook p.178).

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 拐 | guǎi | turn | SVG — an arrow going straight then bending right |
| 1 | 转 | zhuǎn | turn | SVG — an arrow going straight then bending left |
| 2 | 向 | xiàng | towards | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 2 | 路口 | lùkǒu | intersection | SVG — a crossroads seen from above, the corner point circled |
| 3 | 第 | dì | (makes ordinal numbers) | SVG — three crossroads in a row along a road, numbered 一 二 三 |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes:
- **拐 and 转 mean the same thing here.** Both are "turn". The book uses them interchangeably: 往右拐 = 向右转. Say so plainly — students waste time hunting for a difference that isn't there at this level.
- **往 and 向 likewise.** 往右拐 = 向右拐. They know 往 from last lesson; 向 is the twin.
- 路口 — 路 road + 口 mouth. The mouth of the road: where roads meet.
- 第 is not a word on its own. It's a prefix that turns a number into an ordinal: 一 → 第一, 二 → 第二. And the measure word stays: 第一**个**路口.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 你往右**拐**。 — Turn right.
2. 看到红绿灯向左**转**。 — When you see the lights, turn left.
3. 先往右**拐**，然后向左**转**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 你往 ________ 拐。 · 看到 ________ 向左转。
**Extension —** Write the same instruction twice, once with 拐 and once with 转, and once with 往 and once with 向 — four versions of one turn — then say out loud which one you'd actually use.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 请**向**前走。 — Please walk forward.
2. 前面有一个**路口**。 — There's an intersection ahead.
3. 到**路口****向**右拐。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 前面有一个 ________ 。 · 到路口 ________ 右拐。
**Extension —** Write a route with two turns in it, in opposite directions, using 向 for one and 往 for the other.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 这是**第**一个路口。 — This is the first intersection.
2. 在**第**二个路口过马路。 — Cross the road at the second intersection.
3. 你在**第**一个路口往右拐，走五分钟就到了。 — pulls in cycle 1.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 在第 ________ 个路口 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「第三个路口」写一句，再加上「就在你的右手边」结尾。

#### Recall slide
After cycle 3: 拐 · 转 · 向 · 路口 · 第 on one board, characters only. Class reads back, then one student uses all five in a single sentence.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 在第 X 个路口 + action

Think aloud: "The *where* comes before the *what*. English says 'turn right at the second intersection' — action first. Chinese puts the place first: 在第二个路口 + 往右拐. Location, then instruction, every time."

1. 往右拐。
2. 在第一个路口往右拐。
3. 你在前边的第一个路口过马路，然后往右转。
4. 你一直往前走，在第二个路口向左拐，再走五分钟，医院就在你的右手边。 *(hardest — four steps, two different turn verbs, and it lands on a destination)*

#### Text 2 — dialogue 3 (p.127)
Read dialogue 3 with the class, now that 拐, 转, 第 and 路口 are all available:
> A: 请问，附近有医院吗？ B: 你在前边的第一个路口过马路，然后往右转。医院就在你的右手边。

Two comprehension questions in Chinese:
- 要在第几个路口过马路？
- 医院在左手边还是右手边？

#### CFU Checkpoints
*(No answer slides — pose the check and take responses live.)*
- Gesture response: teacher reads six instructions, students point left or right — 往右拐 · 向左转 · 往左拐 · 向右转 · 一直往前走 (point ahead) · 过马路 (point across).
- Whiteboards: 写 — turn left at the second intersection.
- Error correction on the board: 你拐右在第二路口。 — two things are wrong. Find both. *(word order, and the missing 个)*
- Quick translate, cold call: "How do you say 'the third intersection'?"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 六个方向 — Vocabulary consolidation, We Do (4 min)
**Summary board:** the six canonical direction phrases, characters only, up throughout — 往右拐 · 向左转 · 一直往前走 · 过马路 · 在第一个路口往右拐 · 看见红绿灯往左拐.

Students copy all six into their exercise books, then **cover the board** and write the English beside each from memory. Uncover and self-correct in a different colour. Then, still covered, they write a seventh phrase of their own that combines two of the six.

**Extension —** Skip the copying. Write all six from the English alone, then write three more that the book doesn't give: turning at the *third* intersection, crossing *after* the lights, and turning left then immediately right.

#### Activity 2: Write the route — You Do (10 min)
Workbook p.152 Ex 18, one A4 map each. Students write out full directions, in their books, for three destinations:
1. 去电影院怎么走？
2. 去书店怎么走？
3. 去花店怎么走？

Each answer must have **at least three steps** and use 第……个路口 at least once. Model the first one on the board together, then they work alone for six minutes while you circulate. Hand the printed phrase bank to the three or four students who need it rather than projecting it for everyone.

Then pairs swap books and trace each other's routes on the map with a finger. If you end up somewhere else, the writer finds the sentence that sent you wrong.

**Extension —** Write the route to a fourth destination of your own choosing, but write it as a *dialogue* — 请问，去……怎么走？ with a full reply — and include one thing the walker might get wrong: 不要在第一个路口拐，要在第二个。

#### Activity 3: Reading comprehension — You Do (6 min)
Workbook p.148 Ex 10. This is the sequence's only extended reading, and it is the format of **Unit 5 Test Part 9** — so it earns its slot even though the passage is about position rather than directions.

> 我们最近搬了家。新家比老家大，也比老家的房子新，我很喜欢。新家离地铁站很近，走三分钟就到了。但是新家的周围还不太方便：去超级市场要坐一站地铁；附近也没有邮局、诊所、饭店、银行等公共设施；去菜市场要走十到十五分钟。所有的设施可能一两年后才建好。

Read it silently, then six true/false items. Check as a class — but for each answer, ask *which sentence in the passage proves it*, and have the student read that sentence aloud in Chinese. That habit is what makes Test Part 9 straightforward.

Note for the room: 周围 and 公共设施 are new and not examinable here. Gloss them once — surroundings, public facilities — and move on.

**Extension —** Answer the six without the passage in front of you, from one silent read. Then write two more true/false statements of your own that a classmate could get wrong, and try them on your partner — the statements have to be genuinely decidable from the text, not guesses.

#### Game: Writing Relay (3 min)
Two teams in lines at the board. Teacher calls a direction phrase in English; the first student runs up and writes the first character, the next writes the second, and so on — no talking, no correcting the person in front of you. First team to finish a correct phrase scores. Run four: *turn right · cross the road · the second intersection · when you see the traffic lights, turn left*.
- **Setup needed:** nothing but board space and two markers
- **Harder version:** the caller gives a whole route of three steps and the team has to write it out in relay, one character each, including the punctuation. Any team that gets the 个 missing from 第二个路口 loses the point.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; ask who managed a three-step route without checking the board.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 in the margin of their exercise book beside today's date, one mark per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** on a slip — 写一句：在第 ______ 个路口 ____________ 。 Complete it so it makes sense.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们把所有的都放在一起——做一张大地图，互相问路。Next lesson we put it all together: one big map, and everyone giving directions across it.
