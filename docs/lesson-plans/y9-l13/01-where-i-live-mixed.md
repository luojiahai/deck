# Where I Live 我家住在哪儿

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood and Getting Around 社区、问路
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 Neighbourhood 社区 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say where we live and how far it is from the places we go.

## Success Criteria
- I can say where I live (我家住在……)
- I can use A 离 B 远／不远／很近 to talk about distance
- I can say whether life where I live is convenient (生活方便吗？)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Sentence Jumble strips: 6 sets of cut-up cards, each set making one 离 sentence
- No audio this lesson

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 12 外出就餐 — 饭店, 咖啡, 点菜, and the 来 + 数量 + 杯／瓶 ordering pattern from the last lesson of that sequence.
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on 饭店 · 自助餐 · 服务员 · 菜单 · 好吃 (characters only, class says each chorally). Then whiteboards: 写一句 — order two green teas and one cola for the table. Take two answers aloud, correct the measure words on the board.
**Connection:** 上节课我们在饭店点菜。今天我们走出饭店，看看饭店在哪儿——看看我们住的地方。Last lesson we ordered food inside the restaurant. Today we step outside it and describe the whole neighbourhood: where you live, and how far everything is from you.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project them and leave them in the corner of every slide for the rest of the lesson. Read the intention aloud in Chinese; a student volunteer reads each 中文 criterion, teacher glosses the English. Point at criterion 2 and say it is the one the whole lesson turns on.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 市中心 | shì zhōngxīn | city centre | SVG — a cluster of tall buildings with a ring road around them |
| 1 | 方便 | fāngbiàn | convenient | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 2 | 生活 | shēnghuó | life | — abstract, leave the space empty |
| 2 | 邮局 | yóujú | post office | SVG — a small building with a postbox and an envelope sign |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. For 市中心, point out it is three known characters stacked — 市 city, 中 middle, 心 heart: the city's heart. For 邮局, note 局 = bureau, and that they will meet it again in 电视局, 药局.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家住在**市中心**。 — I live in the city centre.
2. 在我家买东西很**方便**。 — Buying things where I live is very convenient.
3. 我家在**市中心**，买东西很**方便**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家住在 ________ 。 · 在我家 ________ 很方便。
**Extension —** 用「因为……所以……」写一句：因为我家住在市中心，所以买东西很方便。Then add one thing that is *not* convenient where you live.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我很喜欢我的**生活**。 — I like my life a lot.
2. **邮局**就在学校旁边。 — The post office is right beside the school.
3. 我家旁边有**邮局**，**生活**很方便。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家旁边有 ________ 。 · 我的生活 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write two sentences that contrast: 我家附近有邮局，可是没有 ________ ，所以 ________ 。

#### Key Sentence Pattern — A 离 B 远／不远／很近

Think aloud: "To say 'the post office is not far from my house', Chinese puts the two places first and the distance last — 邮局 + 离 + 我家 + 不远. The thing you're measuring comes first, not the thing you're measuring *from*."

1. 我家离学校很近。
2. 邮局离我家不远。
3. 我家离市中心很远，坐车三十分钟。
4. 我家离市中心不远，走路十分钟就到了，所以生活很方便。 *(hardest — two clauses, reaches back to 走路…就到了 and pulls in both cycles)*

#### CFU Checkpoints
*(No answer slides — pose the check and take responses live.)*
- "方便 means what? Thumbs up if you're sure, flat hand if you're not."
- Whiteboards: "Write — the post office is not far from my school."
- Error correction, on the board: 我家离很远学校。 — What's wrong with this? Who can fix it?
- Cold call two students: 你家离学校远吗？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 我住的地方 — Vocabulary consolidation, We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** all four words, characters only, on screen for the whole activity.

Every student writes **four** sentences about where they live, one per prompt, and every new word has to appear at least once:
1. 我家住在 ________ 。
2. 我家离学校 ________ 。
3. 我家离市中心 ________ 。
4. 我家 ________ 有邮局／没有邮局，生活 ________ 。

Three minutes writing, then three students read theirs out while the summary board is still up.

**Extension —** Write it as one connected paragraph instead of four separate sentences, joined with 因为……所以…… and 可是, and finish with a sentence saying which *one* thing would make life where you live more convenient: 如果我家附近有 ________ ，生活就更方便了。No looking at the board.

#### Activity 2: Pair interview + report back — You Do (10 min)
Textbook p.124 Ex 2, questions 1–3. A interviews B, then swap:
1. 你们学校离你家远吗？你每天怎么上学？
2. 你家离市中心远吗？你怎么去市中心？
3. 你家住的地方生活方便吗？附近有什么商店？

Then the **report back** — and this is the part that matters. Each student reports their *partner's* answers to the class in the third person: 他们学校离他家不远。他每天走路上学。Take four or five.

Pair deliberately here: put a fluent speaker with someone who needs the model, and give the fluent student the interviewer role both times.

**Extension —** The interviewer asks all three questions, notes the answers, then reports back *without* notes and adds a comparison the partner never made: 他家离市中心比我家近，可是我家附近的商店比他家多。Then the class asks that reporter one follow-up question in Chinese, unseen.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (6 min)
Six sets of cut-up cards on six tables. Groups race to lay theirs out correctly, then rotate to the next table. Practises word order in the 离 pattern, which is where Y9 students slip.

**Use the workbook's own items** — this is workbook p.145 Ex 4, played rather than written, and it is the format of **Unit 5 Test Part 4**. Two of the six sets are the book's:
- 住 / 我家 / 市中心 / 在 / 。
- 很远 / 邮局 / 市政大楼 / 离 / 。

The other four you make up on today's vocabulary: 邮局 / 离 / 我家 / 不远 / 。 and similar. The 市政大楼 set is a deliberate stretch — they meet that word properly in Lesson 2, so let them work it out from 市 and 大楼.

- **Setup needed:** printed strips, cut up, six sets in six envelopes
- **Harder version:** two of the six envelopes contain cards for a *two-clause* sentence (我家离市中心不远，走路十分钟就到了) with one extra card that doesn't belong. Groups getting those must also say which card is the spare and why.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student volunteer reads each one aloud in Chinese.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs 👍 / 😐 / 👎 against each criterion, teacher scans the room.
- **Exit ticket:** on a slip — 写一句：我家离 ____________ 。 One sentence, collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们看看家附近有什么——银行、诊所、百货公司。Next lesson we fill the neighbourhood in with the buildings that are actually in it.
