# Lesson 10.3: Text 1 Reading + Market Shopping Talk (菜市场买菜)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 3 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to read and understand a passage about shopping at a fresh market, and to describe our own shopping experience in writing.

## Success Criteria
- I can read and understand the Text 1 passage about the fresh market (能读懂关于菜市场的短文)
- I can use 卖 to say what a shop sells (能用"卖"说商店卖什么)
- I can write a short passage describing a shopping trip, including items bought and total price (能写一段描述买菜经历的短文)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (with audio playback for CD Track 37, if available — otherwise teacher reads the script aloud)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 2's 比 comparison pattern and market vocabulary.
**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards — project 4 prompts (e.g. "牛肉___猪肉贵。" fill the blank; "写一句比较'苹果'和'香蕉'的句子"). Students write and hold up on three.
**Connection:** "上节课我们学了'比'字句。今天我们要读一篇关于菜市场的短文，里面就用到了'比'——比如'那里的菜比超市里的新鲜'。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read the Learning Intention aloud; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 附近 | fùjìn | nearby |
| 卖 | mài | to sell |
| 各种 | gèzhǒng | all kinds of |
| 蔬菜 | shūcài | vegetables |
| 超市 | chāoshì | supermarket |

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**Structure:** [shop] + 卖 + [items] ("The shop sells...")

**Modelled examples:**
1. 这家水果店卖各种新鲜水果。
2. 菜市场卖新鲜猪肉、牛肉、鸡肉、活鱼、活虾、豆腐，还卖各种新鲜蔬菜和水果。
3. (Silly example:) 这家书店卖猪肉！(wait for reaction — no, that's wrong, a bookshop sells books)

**Quick oral practice (based on Textbook Act. 3, p. 92):** Project 6 shop-type pictures (服装店, 家具店, 中药店, 书店, 电器店, 文具店). Cold call one student per picture to say a 卖 sentence aloud (e.g. 这家服装店卖各种衣服).

#### Text 1 Passage (p. 90, CD Track 37)
> 我家附近有一个菜市场，那里卖新鲜猪肉、牛肉、鸡肉、活鱼、活虾、豆腐，还卖各种新鲜蔬菜和水果。我妈妈特别喜欢去菜市场买菜。她说那里的菜比超市里的新鲜，还便宜。在菜市场，一公斤苹果八块钱，在超市要卖十二块钱。

Play the audio (or read aloud) once, students follow the text on the projector. Read together (choral). Then ask:
- 菜市场卖什么？(Name 4 items.)
- 妈妈为什么喜欢去菜市场买菜？(比超市新鲜、便宜)
- 苹果在菜市场和超市分别多少钱一公斤？(8元 / 12元)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 附近 means 'nearby'."
- **(Quick translate)** "How do you say 'all kinds of' in Chinese?"
- **(Cold call)** "Priya，菜市场里的苹果比超市便宜还是贵？"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 卖 Sentence Writing — We Do (3 min) | 20–23 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 6 and Ex. 8)**
**What:** Project 3 shop pictures from Workbook Ex. 6 (flower shop, bakery, bookstore). Students write one 卖 sentence per picture in exercise books (e.g. 这家花店卖各种花). Then complete 2 of the 5 Workbook Ex. 8 sentence completions ("你在___店可以买到___" for 家具店/水果店).
**How:** Teacher models 1 example; students write the rest independently; quick whole-class check via cold call.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the shop-name word bank.
- **Extension:** Complete the remaining Ex. 6/Ex. 8 items as a fast-finisher task.

#### Activity 2: Listen-and-Write Prices — We Do (6 min) | 23–29 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 6, p. 94)**
**What:** Project a 6-row table (猪肉, 牛排, 羊肉, 鸡腿, 活鱼, 活虾, 豆腐). Teacher reads out 6 short price statements (or plays Track 38 if available); students write down each price in the correct row.
**How:** Read each price statement twice, pause for writing. Reveal correct prices on the projector afterwards for self-correction.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the price range in Chinese numerals visible for reference (一到一百).
- **Extension:** Students write a full sentence for 2 of the items (e.g. 猪肉十六块一斤), not just the number.
- **OTR:** All students complete the table individually.

#### Activity 3: Written Market Trip Retell — You Do (9 min) | 29–38 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 7, p. 94, and Workbook Ex. 7)**
**What:** Project the model passage: 上个星期天我跟妈妈一起去菜市场买菜了。我们买了一斤半猪肉、一斤青菜、一条活鱼、两斤西红柿，一共花了三十六块五。Students write their own version in exercise books, changing the items and prices, following the same structure. They must also complete Workbook Ex. 7 (translate 8 phrases using 各: 各个学校, 各位老师, 各家餐厅, 各种书报, 各个菜市场, 各家饭店, 各种文具, 各家医院).
**How:** Students draft their retell individually (5 min), then Workbook Ex. 7 (4 min). Cold call 2–3 students to read their retell aloud.
- **Scaffold:** Provide a sentence frame: "上个星期___我跟___一起去菜市场买菜了。我们买了___、___、___，一共花了___。"
- **Extension:** Add a 比 comparison sentence at the end (e.g. 菜市场的菜比超市便宜). Fast finishers move on to Workbook Ex. 11 (reading comprehension passage about a mother's diet, with 6 comprehension questions).
- **OTR:** Cold call for the retell; mini whiteboards for 2 of the Ex. 7 translations.

#### Game: Odd One Out (5 min) | 38–43 min
**How to play:** Project 4 sets of 4 words each; students identify and write down the odd one out on mini whiteboards, then explain why in Chinese or English.
- Set 1: 猪肉 / 牛肉 / 鸡肉 / 豆腐 (odd: 豆腐 — not meat)
- Set 2: 菜市场 / 超市 / 水果店 / 图书馆 (odd: 图书馆 — doesn't sell food)
- Set 3: 新鲜 / 便宜 / 贵 / 附近 (odd: 附近 — not a price/quality word)
- Set 4: 活鱼 / 活虾 / 蔬菜 / 水果 (odd: could argue either way — accept reasoned answers)

**What language it practises:** Vocabulary categorisation and quick recall from the lesson.
**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only.
- **Scaffold:** Give English glosses alongside each word for Set 1–2.
- **Extension:** Students must justify their answer in a full Chinese sentence.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project and choral-read the three Success Criteria. "今天我们做到了吗？"
- **Self-assessment:** Show of hands for each criterion (👍/😐/👎).
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write one sentence using 卖 to describe what a shop near your home sells.
- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学新单词——三明治、盒饭、薯片、薯条，学怎么说学校午饭吃什么。"
