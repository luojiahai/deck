# Lesson 10.4: School Lunch & Snack Vocabulary (午饭和零食)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 4 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name school lunch and snack foods in Chinese and sort them into categories.

## Success Criteria
- I can name key lunch and snack items in Chinese (能说出午饭和零食的名称)
- I can sort food into categories: 中餐/快餐/零食/饮料 (能把食物分类)
- I can build new words by adding a character, e.g. 薯片/薯条 (能通过加字组成新词)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 3's Text 1 vocabulary and the 卖 pattern.
**How:** Bingo — pre-made 3×3 grids with 9 of the market/Text 1 words (猪肉, 牛肉, 鸡肉, 豆腐, 新鲜, 便宜, 附近, 卖, 各种). Teacher calls English meanings; students cross off matching 汉字. First to complete a line calls "Bingo!" and reads the line aloud.
**Connection:** "上节课我们学了菜市场卖什么。今天我们学另一种'吃的'——学校午饭和零食，比如三明治、薯片。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read the Learning Intention; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 三明治 | sānmíngzhì | sandwich |
| 平时 | píngshí | usually |
| 盒饭 | héfàn | box lunch |
| 薯片 | shǔpiàn | chips; crisps |
| 薯条 | shǔtiáo | French fries |
| 炸 | zhá | deep-fry |
| 翅膀 | chìbǎng | wing |
| 糖果 | tángguǒ | candy; sweets |
| 咖喱 | gālí | curry |
| 鱼柳 | yúliǔ | fish fillet |
| 猪排 | zhūpái | pork chop |

Show each row on the projector; model twice, class repeats twice. *Word-building note:* 薯 (potato/yam) + 片 (slice) = 薯片 "chips"; 薯 + 条 (strip) = 薯条 "fries" — same root, different shape. Point this out as you present the pair.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**Structure:** 炸 + [food] ("deep-fried X") — 炸鸡翅 (fried chicken wings), 炸鱼 (fried fish)

**Modelled examples:**
1. 我喜欢吃炸鸡翅。
2. 盒饭里有咖喱牛肉、鱼柳和猪排。
3. (Silly example:) 我早饭吃炸薯条！(wait for reaction)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 盒饭 means 'box lunch'."
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the Chinese for 'chips'. Hold it up."
- **(Cold call)** "Leo，'candy' 用中文怎么说？" → "Mia，'curry' 呢？"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Add-a-Character Word Building — We Do (7 min) | 20–27 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 14 and the chicken diagram in Workbook Ex. 9)**
**What:** Project 4 word-building prompts: 1.(_)肉, 2.薯(_), 3.(_)果, 4.(_)常. Students complete each with the correct character in exercise books. Then label a simple rooster diagram with 5 parts: 鸡冠, 尾巴, 羽毛, 翅膀, 爪子 — guessing meaning first, then checking against the projected answer key.
**How:** Teacher models the first word-building item, students complete the rest independently; pair-check before whole-class reveal.
- **Scaffold:** Provide a word bank (猪/鸡/牛, 片/条, 糖/水, 平/正) for the add-a-character task.
- **Extension:** Use each completed word in an original sentence.
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards for 2 of the 4 word-building items.

#### Activity 2: Categorise the Food — You Do (10 min) | 27–37 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 15)**
**What:** Students sort 12 food/drink terms into 4 categories — 中餐/快餐/零食/饮料 — in their exercise books: 炒菜, 炒面, 糖果, 炸鸡翅, 比萨饼, 可乐, 汽水, 炒青菜, 炒大虾, 红烧豆腐, 热狗, 薯片, 汉堡包, 三明治, 咖喱牛肉.
**How:** Independent sorting (6 min), then pair-check with a neighbour, then whole-class reveal with reasoning for any disputed items (e.g. 咖喱牛肉 could be 中餐 or arguably other — accept justified answers).
- **Scaffold:** Provide the 4 category headers with one worked example already placed in each.
- **Extension:** Add 2 more food items of their own choosing to each category.

#### Game: Odd One Out — 零食 Edition (5 min) | 37–42 min
**How to play:** Project 3 sets of 4 words; students write the odd one out and a one-sentence reason.
- Set 1: 薯片 / 薯条 / 糖果 / 猪排 (odd: 猪排 — a main dish, not a snack)
- Set 2: 三明治 / 盒饭 / 汉堡包 / 可乐 (odd: 可乐 — a drink, not food)
- Set 3: 炸鸡翅 / 鱼柳 / 咖喱牛肉 / 薯片 (odd: 薯片 — not part of a box lunch filling)

**What language it practises:** Rapid categorisation and vocabulary recall.
**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only.
- **Scaffold:** Give English glosses for Set 1.
- **Extension:** Justify the answer in a full Chinese sentence.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project and choral-read the three Success Criteria.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light thumbs for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write one sentence naming your favourite snack from today's list, e.g. 我最喜欢吃薯条。
- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要读一篇关于学校午饭的对话，还要学'有时候……有时候……'这个句型。"
