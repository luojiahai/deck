# 炸鸡翅、糖果 and 平时……，但是……

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Fresh Market 菜市场
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the rest of the canteen's food and to contrast what we usually do with what happened today.

## Success Criteria
- I can name fried chicken wings, sweets, curry, fish fillet and pork chop in Chinese (能说出「炸鸡翅」「糖果」「咖喱」「鱼柳」「猪排」)
- I can contrast a habit with an exception: 我平时……，但是…… (能用「平时……，但是……」说两件事)
- I can write a short paragraph about what I eat (能写一段话，说说自己平时吃什么)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l10/l6-snacks-habits/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD track 40 (textbook p.97)
- Textbook pp.97–99 (Act. 10, 11, 12); workbook pp.114–117 (Ex. 16, 18, 19, 23)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 5's canteen words and the 多少钱一…？ question.
**How:** Pair dialogue from memory — 30 seconds each way on 你平时午饭吃什么？多少钱？ Then three whiteboard items: 写「盒饭」。 翻译：Crisps are cheaper than fries. 「不算太贵」是什么意思？
**Connection:** 我们已经会说盒饭和三明治了。今天学餐厅卖的其他东西，还要学怎么说「我平时这样，但是今天不一样」。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Say explicitly that this is the last lesson of 第十课, and that the plenary will look back over all six.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 炸 | zhá | deep-fry | a wire basket lifting out of oil |
| 1 | 翅膀 | chìbǎng | wing | a chicken wing (炸鸡翅 on a plate) |
| 2 | 糖果 | tángguǒ | candy; sweets | wrapped sweets |
| 2 | 咖喱 | gālí | curry | a bowl of curry over rice |
| 3 | 鱼柳 | yúliǔ | fish fillet | a fillet on a plate |
| 3 | 猪排 | zhūpái | pork chop | a chop with the bone showing |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences (from Text 2):**
1. 我有时候买**炸**薯条。 — I sometimes buy fries.
2. 我也会买**炸鸡翅**。 — I also buy fried chicken wings.
3. **炸鸡翅**十块，比**炸**鸡腿贵。 — both, plus 比.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢／不喜欢吃＿＿＿＿。
**Extension —** 用「炸」写两样东西，再用「比」说哪个贵。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我有时候买**糖果**。
2. **咖喱**牛肉饭十五块。
3. 我买了**咖喱**牛肉饭，还买了**糖果**。

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我买了＿＿＿＿，还买了＿＿＿＿。
**Extension —** 加一句说为什么，用「因为……所以……」。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. **鱼柳**饭十六块。
2. 我常买**猪排**饭。
3. **鱼柳**饭比**猪排**饭贵两块。

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** ＿＿饭比＿＿饭贵／便宜＿＿块。
**Extension —** 三种盒饭排价钱，写成一段两句话，中间用「还」连起来。

**Recall slide:** 炸 · 翅膀 · 糖果 · 咖喱 · 鱼柳 · 猪排 — characters only, class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**我平时……，但是……。** (p.99 Act. 12; the book's own example)
1. 我平时八点到学校，但是今天七点就到了。
2. 我通常买盒饭，但是今天带了三明治。
3. 我一般不吃零食，但是昨天吃了三包薯片。
4. 我平时在学校餐厅买午饭，但是这个星期餐厅关门，所以我从家里带饭。 ← hardest: habit, exception, and a reason.

The family of time words the book drills here: 平时 · 通常 · 一般 · 有时候 · 经常.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「炸鸡翅」是怎么做的？
- 小白板：写「我平时＿＿，但是今天＿＿」。
- 「鱼柳」和「猪排」哪个贵？(from the p.95 price board)

#### Listening — CD 40 (p.97 Act. 10)
Play the canteen dialogue twice; students match the six stems (学校有 / 餐厅卖 / 午饭有 / 盒饭 / 她午饭常吃 / 她常喝) to A–I (各种饮料。炸鸡翅。一家餐厅。十九块一盒。热狗。饮料。盒饭。炸鸡腿。三明治。). Check as a class.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 词汇总览 + 平时……但是…… (TB Act. 12) — We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** all six new words plus 三明治 · 盒饭 · 薯片 · 薯条, characters and pictures, up throughout.
**Task:** Textbook Act. 12's five stems — 我平时…，但是… / 我通常… / 我一般… / 我有时候… / 我经常… — completed in exercise books, all five, each about food or lunch so the summary board gets used. Teacher models the book's example first, then takes three aloud.

**Extension —** Write all five, then rewrite two of them about **someone else** in the third person, with a reason attached: 我哥哥平时不吃零食，但是考试的时候他一直吃糖果，因为他说糖果让他不紧张。 Then ask a partner one question that would make them produce a 平时……但是…… sentence, and report their answer to the class.

#### Activity 2: 商店对话 Shop dialogues (TB Act. 11) — You Do (6 min)
Pairs, 售货员 and 顾客, from the book's model: 你想看哪块手表？ — 这块，一千五百块的。 Work through four of the book's eight prompts, each tagged 买 / 看 / 试穿 with two priced options: 金鱼 ¥10.00/条 · 西瓜 ¥2.18/斤 · 计算器 ¥65 / ¥85 个 · 短裤 ¥50 / 牛仔裤 ¥186 条 · 耳环 ¥130/副 与项链 ¥145/条 · 书 ¥55 / ¥75 本 · 运动鞋 ¥580 / ¥465 双 · 皮带 ¥126 / ¥150 条.

**Extension —** The 顾客 has 200 块 and must leave having bought **three** things from three different prompts. Every choice is justified with 比 out loud (这本比那本便宜，所以我买这本), and at the end they report the total and one thing they couldn't afford. The 售货员's job is to talk them into the dearer option each time — no word bank, no frames. Pairs who finish take workbook **Ex. 19**: the two menus 菜单A 中式 and 菜单B 西式 with their six questions (中餐里，哪个菜最贵？如果你吃中餐，你可以喝到汤吗？吃中餐，你可以吃到虾吗？西餐中有牛肉吗？你想吃鸡肉，你可以吃哪个菜？哪个菜里有羊肉？) answered in full Chinese sentences, not single words.

#### Game: 排句子 Sentence Jumble (WB Ex. 16) (4 min)
The workbook's own five jumbles, one per round, projected: 吃/每天/在学校餐厅/我/午饭/买。· 觉得/猪排饭/我/贵/有一点儿。· 通常/晚饭/我们家/吃/中餐。· 羊肉/我/喜欢/或者/都/牛肉，/吃。· 差不多/每天/我/炒菜、/都/米饭/吃。 Pairs write the ordered sentence on a whiteboard; fastest correct wins.
- **Setup needed:** projector and whiteboards; nothing printed.
- **Harder version:** after ordering it, the pair must produce a true sentence of their own on the same structure before the point counts — and for the 或者…都 and 差不多…都 items, the new sentence has to use the same adverb.

#### Activity 3: 写一段话 (WB Ex. 23 writing task) — You Do (6 min)
Lead in with two of workbook **Ex. 18**'s questions asked round the room — 你喜欢吃哪种饭菜？ 你喜欢喝什么饮料？ — so everyone has said an answer before they write one.

Then workbook Ex. 23's task, cut to a manageable size: write 5–6 sentences about what you eat — 早饭、午饭、晚饭 each get a sentence, then your favourite snack, then one 平时……但是…… sentence. Summary board stays up; two read aloud in the plenary.

**Extension —** The full 80–100 words the workbook asks for, with no summary board, including where and when you eat your snacks and whether your diet is balanced. This is the Unit 4 Test's essay question almost word for word.

### Plenary (43–50 min) — looking back over all six lessons
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** today's three read aloud, then a fast sweep back: 第一节课我们学了什么？ (市场、猪肉、新鲜、便宜) 第三节课？ (比) 第四节课？ (部首)
- **Self-assessment:** 会 / 差不多 / 还不会 across the whole lesson — name a market food, make a 比 sentence, give a price with a measure word, say what you usually eat.
- **Exit ticket:** 用「平时……，但是……」写一句话，说说你吃的东西。 Two students read theirs aloud.
- **Preview:** 下一课是第十一课 —— 零食。你们已经会说糖果和薯片了，所以开头会很快。
