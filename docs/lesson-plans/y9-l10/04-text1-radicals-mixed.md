# Text 1 in Full + Radicals and Character Building

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Fresh Market 菜市场
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to read Text 1 in full, and to see how this unit's characters are built from their radicals.

## Success Criteria
- I can read Text 1 aloud and answer questions about it in Chinese (能读完课文一，用中文回答问题)
- I can name the radical in 鲜, 活, 虾, 猪 and say what it means (能说出「鲜、活、虾、猪」的部首和意思)
- I can build a new word by adding a character to 肉, 果 or 常 (能给「肉」「果」「常」加字组词)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l10/l4-text1-radicals/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Board space for four relay teams
- Textbook pp.90, 92 (Act. 2), 94 (Act. 7); workbook pp.108–116 (Ex. 2, 3, 9, 12, 14, 17, 20)
- Dictionaries (print or the ones on the classroom shelf)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 比 from last lesson, and the market vocabulary from Lessons 1–2.
**How:** Workbook **Ex. 12** on the projector — the cloze paragraph about a new home with no fresh market nearby, filled from the box 除了……以外 / 贵 / 超级市场 / 便宜 / 但是 / 新鲜 / 附近 / 因为. Students write the eight answers on whiteboards, one gap at a time, holding up after each. It recycles 比, 新鲜 and 便宜 in a connected text rather than in isolated sentences.
**Connection:** 这些字你们现在会读了。今天我们看看它们是怎么组成的 —— 为什么「鲜」里边有鱼，「虾」里边有虫。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Write 鲜 alone on the board and ask the class to point out the two halves before you explain anything.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Character focus — 3 cycles, components rather than new spoken words
This lesson carries no new spoken vocabulary. The cycles teach the **written** structure of words the class already says, which is what workbook Ex. 17 tests.

| Cycle | 字 | Radical | Other part | What the radical tells you |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 鲜（鮮） | 鱼 fish | 羊 sheep | fresh food = fish + meat |
| 1 | 猪 | 犭 animal | 者 | it's an animal |
| 2 | 活 | 氵 water | 舌 tongue | alive — water is life |
| 2 | 虾（蝦） | 虫 insect/small creature | 下 | a small creature |
| 3 | 翅 | 羽 feather | 支 | a wing has feathers |
| 3 | 膀 | 月 (flesh) | 旁 | a body part |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 菜市场的鱼很新**鲜**。 — 鲜 = 鱼 + 羊.
2. 那里卖新鲜**猪**肉。 — 猪 has 犭, the animal radical.
3. 新**鲜**的**猪**肉比超市的便宜。 — both characters, one sentence.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** Write 鲜 and 猪 stroke by stroke, then one sentence containing both.
**Extension —** 再写两个有「鱼」的字和两个有「犭」的字，每个写出意思。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 菜市场卖**活**鱼。 — 活 has 氵.
2. 那里的**虾**又大又新鲜。 — 虾 has 虫.
3. **活虾**十五块一斤。 — both characters.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** Write 活 and 虾, then a price sentence using 活虾.
**Extension —** 「氵」和「虫」各写三个字。不看书。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 鸡有两个**翅膀**。 — from workbook Ex. 9's rooster.
2. 我喜欢吃炸鸡**翅**。 — previewing Lesson 6.
3. 鸡的**翅膀**很好吃。

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** Write 翅膀 and label the two radicals.
**Extension —** 「月」是身体的意思。再写三个有「月」的字（脸、腿、脑……），写出意思。

**Recall slide:** 鲜 · 猪 · 活 · 虾 · 翅 · 膀 — characters only, class names the radical in each.

#### Text 1 in full (p.90, CD 37)
> 我家附近有一个菜市场，那里卖新鲜猪肉、牛肉、鸡肉、活鱼、活虾、豆腐，还卖各种新鲜蔬菜和水果。
>
> 我妈妈特别喜欢去市场买菜。她说那里的菜比超市里的新鲜，还便宜。在菜市场，一公斤苹果八块钱，在超市要卖十二块钱。

Character-only on screen — Y9, no pinyin. Play track 37 whole, read chorally, then three comprehension questions answered **in Chinese**: 菜市场卖几种肉？ 妈妈为什么喜欢去那里？ 苹果在超市贵多少？

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「鲜」的部首是什么？
- 小白板：写「虾」，圈出部首。
- 「活」为什么有三点水？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 部首表 — the radical breakdown (WB Ex. 17) — We Do (9 min)
Workbook Ex. 17 as a whole-class build, then completed individually: 治→台/氵 · 翅→羽/支 · 膀→旁/月 · 鲜→羊/鱼 · 时→寸/日 · 活→舌/氵 · 虾→下/虫 · 新→斤/亲 · 猪→者/犭. Teacher does the first two at the board; students finish the remaining seven in exercise books. Then workbook Ex. 3 — nine simple characters from English clues (1/30 metre 寸, rice 米, foot 足, fight 斗, strength 力, blue/green 青, arrow 矢, utensil 皿, down 下).

**Extension —** Finish Ex. 17 and Ex. 3, then take workbook Ex. 9 — label the rooster (鸡冠, 尾巴, 羽毛, 翅膀, 爪子), write what each means from the radical alone, then check in the dictionary and note which guesses were wrong and why. Report one to the class.

#### Activity 2: 组词 — word building (WB Ex. 14 + Ex. 2) — You Do (8 min)
Workbook Ex. 14: add characters around 肉, 薯, 果, 常 to make real words. Then the Ex. 2 word search — a 5×5 grid (猪牛羊新鲜 / 鸡肉活鱼土 / 卷片虾豆腐 / 便心青冻南 / 宜蔬菜黄瓜) — circle ten phrases and write their meanings.

**Extension —** Ten phrases isn't the ceiling; find every phrase in the grid, including the two-character ones read downward, and write each with its meaning **and** a measure word. Then take workbook **Ex. 20** — guess the meaning of 柳树, 炸弹, 壁球, 肩膀, 海鲜, 龙虾, 水平, 烤鸭, 卷心菜, 胶水, 度假, 口香糖 from their characters alone, write the guess, then check each in the dictionary and mark which ones the radicals got you and which ones they didn't.

#### Game: 汉字接力 Writing Relay (6 min)
Textbook p.92 Act. 2 gives twelve components to memorise in five minutes: 金 匕 亡 尸 丁 入 户 井 弓 方 习 皿. Show them for two minutes, then hide. Four teams line up; the teacher calls a component's meaning or a word containing it; the first student in each team runs up and writes it, then tags the next.
- **Setup needed:** board space split into four columns; nothing printed.
- **Harder version:** the teacher calls nothing at all — she writes a **whole character** from this unit on the board, and the runner must write the radical it contains **and** one other character using that same radical. Both must be right to score.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; the class names the radical in 鲜 and 虾 chorally.
- **Self-assessment:** 会 / 差不多 / 还不会 on each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一个字，圈出部首，写出部首的意思。
- **Preview:** 下节课我们离开菜市场，去学校餐厅 —— 午饭你们平时吃什么？

### If time runs over
Workbook Ex. 2's word search is the first thing to push — it reappears as a fast-finisher any lesson. Do not cut Ex. 17: the Unit 4 Test asks for radicals directly.

### Textbook Act. 7 — 上个星期天我跟妈妈一起去菜市场买菜了
If Activity 1 runs short, or as the fast-finishers' second task: the book's shopping recount on p.94 (一斤半猪肉 / 一斤青菜 / 一条活鱼 / 两斤西红柿 … 我们一共花了三十六块五毛 … 因为那里的菜比超市的新鲜，还便宜). Read it as the worked model, then students write their own — four items with quantities, a total, and a 比 sentence for why they go there. It uses Lessons 1–3 whole, so it also works as a spare Review for Lesson 5.
