# The Market and What It Sells — 菜市场

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Fresh Market 菜市场
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe what a fresh market sells, and to say that its food is fresh and cheap.

## Success Criteria
- I can name a market and a fresh market in Chinese (能说出「市场」和「菜市场」)
- I can name two kinds of meat sold there (能说出「猪肉」和「牛肉」)
- I can describe food as fresh or cheap (能用「新鲜」「便宜」描述食物)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l10/l1-market-vocab/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books and pens
- Board space for the game (two students at a time)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 9 文具 — 文具店, 铅笔, 橡皮, 文具盒, 尺子, and the 里边 pattern.
**How:** Project five stationery characters; class reads pinyin + English chorally. Then two students each make one sentence with 里边 — 我的文具盒里边有三支铅笔。
**Connection:** 上个单元我们在文具店买东西。今天我们去另一个地方买东西 —— 菜市场。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project the WALT and the three criteria; a student reads each criterion aloud, once in English and once in Chinese. Leave on screen — they come back in the plenary.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 市场 | shìchǎng | market | market stalls under an awning |
| 1 | 菜市场 | càishìchǎng | fresh market | the same stall, piled with vegetables |
| 2 | 猪肉 | zhūròu | pork | a cut of pork on a board |
| 2 | 牛肉 | niúròu | beef | a cut of beef, darker |
| 3 | 新鲜 | xīnxiān | fresh | — (quality, not a thing) |
| 3 | 便宜 | piányi | cheap | — (quality, not a thing) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家附近有一个**市场**。 — There is a market near my home.
2. 妈妈喜欢去**菜市场**买菜。 — Mum likes going to the fresh market to buy food.
3. 我家附近的**市场**是**菜市场**。 — The market near my home is a fresh market.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家附近有一个＿＿＿＿。/ 我喜欢去＿＿＿＿买菜。
**Extension —** 用「因为……所以……」把两句连起来，说说你妈妈为什么去菜市场买菜。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 菜市场卖**猪肉**。 — The fresh market sells pork.
2. 我不喜欢吃**牛肉**。 — I don't like eating beef.
3. 那里卖**猪肉**，也卖**牛肉**。 — It sells pork, and beef too.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我喜欢吃＿＿＿＿，不喜欢吃＿＿＿＿。
**Extension —** 两种肉写在一句话里，再说你为什么喜欢那一种。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 那里的菜很**新鲜**。 — The food there is very fresh.
2. 菜市场的水果很**便宜**。 — Fruit at the fresh market is cheap.
3. 那里的菜很**新鲜**，还很**便宜**。 — The food there is fresh, and cheap too.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** ＿＿＿＿的＿＿＿＿很新鲜。/ ＿＿＿＿很便宜。
**Extension —** 用「还」把新鲜和便宜写进同一句，主语换成一样具体的东西（猪肉、水果、鱼）。

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 市场 · 菜市场 · 猪肉 · 牛肉 · 新鲜 · 便宜 — characters only, class reads back. If this stalls, reteach before the pattern.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**我家附近有一个……，那里的……很……。**
1. 我家附近有一个市场，那里的菜很新鲜。
2. 我家附近有一个菜市场，那里的水果很便宜。
3. 我家附近有一个菜市场，那里的猪肉很新鲜，牛肉也很便宜。 ← two items, two descriptions; this is the one to aim for.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「新鲜」是什么意思？会的举手。
- 小白板：写「牛肉」。举起来。
- 这句话对不对？我家附近有一个菜市场，那里的菜很便宜很新鲜。(missing 还 / 也 — the class fixes it)

#### Text 1 — opening sentence (p.90, CD 37)
> 我家附近有一个菜市场，那里卖新鲜猪肉、牛肉、鸡肉、活鱼、活虾、豆腐，还卖各种新鲜蔬菜和水果。

Play track 37 for this sentence only, read it together, then two questions in Chinese: 菜市场在哪里？ 那里卖什么肉？ (Students name only 猪肉 and 牛肉 today — the rest arrives next lesson.)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 词汇总览 + 给妈妈写一张便条 — We Do (12 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters and pictures only, on screen for the whole activity.
**Task:** Write Mum a note of four sentences. Every one of the six words has to appear, 一个都不能少:
1. 你去哪里 —— 市场 / 菜市场
2. 你买了什么肉 —— 猪肉、牛肉
3. 那里的菜怎么样 —— 新鲜、便宜
4. 你为什么去那里

4 minutes writing in exercise books, teacher circulating, then three students read theirs aloud with the summary board still up.

**Extension —** 不看词汇板写。加上「因为……所以……」，再写一样你**没**买的东西和原因 — 我没买牛肉，因为牛肉不便宜。 Then hand the note to a partner, who has to say back in Chinese what you bought and why you went there.

#### Activity 2: Pattern production — You Do (6 min)
On mini whiteboards, each student writes two sentences on the 我家附近有一个……，那里的……很……。 frame — one about a 市场, one about a 菜市场. Hold up; teacher scans; two are read out.

**Extension —** 写三句，第三句把两样东西和两个形容词放进去（那里的猪肉很新鲜，牛肉也很便宜）。Then, without writing it down, say aloud one sentence about a shop that is **not** a market — 我家附近有一个书店，那里的书很便宜。

#### Game: 拍字 Slap the Character (5 min)
Six words written large on the board. Two students at a time, one at each end. Teacher says the English; the first to slap the right word scores.
- **Setup needed:** board and chalk/marker only.
- **Harder version:** the teacher stops saying English and says a full Chinese sentence with the target word replaced by 什么 — 那里的菜很什么？ The student must slap the word **and** say the complete sentence before the point counts.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** project the three criteria; a student reads each in Chinese.
- **Self-assessment:** hands up for each criterion — 会 / 差不多 / 还不会.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句话，用「新鲜」或者「便宜」，说说菜市场。 (mini whiteboards, collected by eye on the way out)
- **Preview:** 下节课：市场里还卖什么 —— 鸡肉、活鱼、活虾、豆腐，还有怎么说价钱。
