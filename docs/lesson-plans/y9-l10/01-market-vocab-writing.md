# Lesson 10.1: Fresh Market Vocabulary — Meat, Seafood & Tofu (菜市场)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 1 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name fresh-market foods in Chinese and say their prices using measure words.

## Success Criteria
- I can name key meat, seafood and tofu items in Chinese (能说出肉类、海鲜和豆腐的名称)
- I can say a price using a measure word: item + price + 一斤/一公斤 (能用"……一斤/一公斤"说价钱)
- I can write 新鲜 and 便宜 correctly and use them in a sentence (能正确书写并使用"新鲜"和"便宜")

## Materials Needed
- Projector (all visuals displayed and controlled from projector)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 9's stationery vocabulary (文具店, 铅笔, 尺子, 橡皮, 文具盒 etc.) — closing out Unit 3 before starting Unit 4.
**How:** Project 5 stationery items (Chinese side); class says pinyin + English chorally. Then cold call 2 students to say one full sentence each using 里边 from Lesson 9's pattern.
**Connection:** "上个单元我们学了文具店。今天我们开始一个新单元——菜市场 (Fresh Market)。你们知道'便宜'怎么说吗？今天我们要学怎么在市场买菜、说价钱。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. Teacher reads the Learning Intention; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion aloud. Keep visible on screen throughout.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 市场 | shìchǎng | market |
| 菜市场 | càishìchǎng | fresh market |
| 新鲜（鮮） | xīnxiān | fresh |
| 猪肉 | zhūròu | pork |
| 牛肉 | niúròu | beef |
| 鸡肉 | jīròu | chicken meat |
| 活 | huó | alive; live |
| 虾（蝦） | xiā | shrimp |
| 豆腐 | dòufu | tofu; bean curd |
| 各 | gè | all; every |
| 比 | bǐ | compare |
| 便宜 | piányi | cheap |
| 公斤 | gōngjīn | kilogram |

Show each row on the projector: 汉字 → pinyin → English. Teacher models pronunciation twice, class repeats twice (choral). After every 4 words, do a quick choral sweep of all words so far.

*Component note (brief, one sentence):* 鲜 has 鱼 (fish) + 羊 (sheep) — "fresh" food is fish and meat; 腐 in 豆腐 is a component students will meet again — no deep teaching needed here, just flag it.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**Structure:** item + price + 一斤/一公斤 ("X costs Y per jin/kilogram")

**Modelled examples (project, read aloud, class repeats):**
1. 猪肉十五块一斤。(Pork is $15 per jin.)
2. 牛肉比猪肉贵，二十块一斤。(Beef is more expensive than pork, $20 per jin.)
3. (Silly example — wait for reaction:) 豆腐一百块一公斤！太贵了！

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 新鲜 means 'fresh'."
- **(Mini whiteboards)** "Write the Chinese for 'shrimp'. Hold it up on three."
- **(Cold call)** "Jamie，'tofu' 用中文怎么说？" → "Wei，'cheap' 呢？"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Guided Price-Writing — We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min
**What:** Project 6 of the market pictures from Textbook Act. 1 (猪肉, 牛肉, 鸡肉, 活鱼, 活虾, 豆腐), each with a price tag.
**How:** Teacher models the first 2 as full written sentences on the board (e.g. 猪肉十五块一斤). Students copy the pattern and write the remaining 4 price sentences into their exercise books. Circulate and cold call 2 students to read their sentences aloud for the class to check.
- **Scaffold:** Keep pinyin visible under each picture; provide the sentence frame ___十___一斤。
- **Extension:** Students must also add a comparison using 比 (previewing next lesson), e.g. 牛肉比猪肉贵。
- **OTR:** Every student writes all 4 sentences; whole-class check via cold call.

#### Activity 2: Workbook Vocabulary Consolidation — You Do (10 min) | 28–38 min
**What:** Workbook Ex. 1 (match 8 pictures of meat/seafood/vegetables to the word bank: 猪肉, 牛肉, 羊肉, 鸡肉, 活鱼, 活虾, 豆腐, 蔬菜) and Workbook Ex. 4 (write the Chinese for 15 pictured food items — lettuce is given as an example, then tomato, broccoli, orange, potato, banana, apple, watermelon, pear, pizza, hamburger, baguette, steak, drink).
**How:** Students complete both individually in exercise books. Pair-check answers with a neighbour before teacher reveals the answers on the projector.
- **Scaffold:** For Ex. 4, provide a partial word bank on the board for items not yet taught explicitly.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers write one full sentence (not just the word) for 3 of the Ex. 4 items, e.g. 我喜欢吃西瓜。 Students who finish Ex. 1 and Ex. 4 early move on to Workbook Ex. 2 (circle 10 vocabulary phrases hidden in a 5×5 character grid and write their meanings) as a fast-finisher task.
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards — "Write the Chinese for 'fresh'. Hold it up."

#### Game: Writing Relay — 汉字接力 (5 min) | 38–43 min
**How to play:**
1. Use the 12 flash characters from Textbook Act. 2 (金匕亡尸丁入户井弓方习皿). Split the class into 4 teams, each lined up facing the board.
2. Teacher calls out one character's English/pinyin meaning; the first student in each team runs up and writes the correct 汉字 on the board, then tags the next teammate.
3. First team to correctly write all 12 characters wins.

**What language it practises:** Character recognition and handwriting accuracy for the Lesson 10 component characters.
**Setup needed:** Board space for 4 teams; no printed materials needed.
- **Scaffold:** Keep the 12 characters projected in a corner for teams that fall behind.
- **Extension:** Winning team must use one of the characters in a real word from today (e.g. 户 → 户口, though not required content — accept any sensible attempt).

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria. Teacher reads the first; students read the second and third together. "今天我们做到了吗？"
- **Self-assessment:** Students hold up fingers — 3 = 完全可以, 2 = 差不多, 1 = 还不行 — for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Students write ONE price sentence using a market item and 新鲜 or 便宜, e.g. 新鲜的鱼很便宜，十块一斤。Teacher circulates and scans.
- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学怎么用'比'字句比较两样东西——比如'牛肉比猪肉贵'。"
