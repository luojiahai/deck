# Lesson 10.5: Text 2 Dialogue + Sentence Patterns (学校午饭对话)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 5 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to talk about our school lunch habits using 有时候……有时候…… and 不算太……

## Success Criteria
- I can use 有时候……有时候…… to describe alternating between two options (能用"有时候……有时候……"描述两种情况)
- I can ask and answer a price question with 多少钱一 + measure word (能用"多少钱一……"问价钱)
- I can use 不算太 + adjective to soften a description (能用"不算太……"缓和描述)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (with audio playback for CD Track 39, if available — otherwise teacher reads the script aloud)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4's snack vocabulary and categories.
**How:** Correct the teacher — project 3 deliberately mis-categorised statements (e.g. "可乐是零食。"). Students find and fix each on mini whiteboards, then a cold-called student reads the corrected sentence aloud.
**Connection:** "上节课我们学了午饭和零食的词。今天我们要读一段关于学校午饭的对话，学怎么说'有时候……有时候……'。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read aloud; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 通常 | tōngcháng | usually |
| 哪种 | nǎ zhǒng | which kind |
| 算 | suàn | to count as |

#### Key Sentence Patterns
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| 有时候……有时候…… | 我有时候从家里带三明治，有时候在学校餐厅买东西吃。 |
| 通常……也会…… | 我通常买盒饭吃，有时候也会买薯片、薯条…… |
| 多少钱一 + measure word | 多少钱一盒？ |
| 不算太 + adjective | 十九块，不算太贵。 |
| 哪种 + noun | 你喜欢吃哪种盒饭？ |

**Modelled examples (project, read aloud, class repeats):**
1. 我有时候骑自行车上学，有时候坐公共汽车上学。
2. 这件衣服不算太贵，只要五十块。
3. (Silly example:) 我通常吃早饭，有时候也会吃三次早饭！(wait for reaction)

#### Text 2 Passage (p. 95, CD Track 39)
> — 你每天午饭吃什么？
> — 我有时候从家里带三明治，有时候在学校餐厅买东西吃。
> — 你平时买些什么？
> — 我通常买盒饭吃，有时候也会买薯片、薯条、炸鸡翅（膀）、糖果等。
> — 盒饭贵吗？多少钱一盒？
> — 十九块，不算太贵。
> — 你喜欢吃哪种盒饭？
> — 我常买咖喱牛肉饭、鱼柳饭、猪排饭等。

Play the audio (or read aloud) once, students follow along. Read together in pairs (one student each role), then swap roles. Ask:
- 这个人午饭有时候吃什么？有时候呢？(三明治 / 学校餐厅的东西)
- 盒饭多少钱一盒？(十九块)
- 他喜欢吃哪几种盒饭？(咖喱牛肉饭、鱼柳饭、猪排饭)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 通常 means 'usually'."
- **(Make a sentence)** "Give me a sentence with 有时候……有时候…… Write it on your whiteboard."
- **(Cold call)** "Zoe，'not too expensive' 用中文怎么说？"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Antonym Warm-Up — We Do (6 min) | 20–26 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 5)**
**What:** Students write the opposite word for 9 items in their exercise books: 胖, 暖和, 有用, 进, 有趣, 容易, 贵, 难看, 有意思.
**How:** Teacher models the first 2 as a class, students complete the remaining 7 independently. Cold call for answers; project the answer key.
- **Scaffold:** Provide a word bank of the 9 opposites, scrambled, for students to match rather than recall freely.
- **Extension:** Use 2 of the antonym pairs in an original sentence each. Fast finishers move on to Workbook Ex. 17 (radical/component decomposition — write the meaning of each radical and the resulting character for 9 Lesson 10 words: 治, 翅, 膀, 鲜, 时, 活, 虾, 新, 猪), consolidating the character components met across Text 1 and Text 2.

#### Activity 2: Sentence Reordering — You Do (12 min) | 26–38 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 16)**
**What:** Students rearrange 5 sets of scrambled words/phrases into correct sentences about food/meals, writing the final sentence in exercise books.
**How:** Independent work (8 min), then pair-check with a neighbour, then whole-class reveal with 2–3 cold-called readings.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the English translation next to each scrambled set.
- **Extension:** After correctly reordering, students write one additional original sentence using 有时候……有时候…… or 不算太……
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards for a 6th "bonus" scrambled sentence created live by the teacher.

#### Game: Chain Response — 有时候接龙 (5 min) | 38–43 min
**How to play:**
1. Students sit in a circle or row. First student starts: "我有时候……" and completes it with a lunch-related activity, e.g. 我有时候带三明治。
2. Next student repeats the full pattern and adds their own: "我有时候带三明治，有时候买盒饭。"
3. Continue around the group, each student repeating the growing chain and adding one more item. If a student makes an error or forgets, they are gently corrected and the chain continues.

**What language it practises:** 有时候……有时候…… pattern, memory, listening.
**Setup needed:** None.
- **Scaffold:** Allow students to use the vocabulary list on the projector for ideas.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers must add a price or opinion to their item (e.g. 有时候买盒饭，十九块，不算太贵。).

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project and choral-read the three Success Criteria.
- **Self-assessment:** Show of hands (👍/😐/👎) for each.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write one sentence about your own lunch habits using 有时候……有时候……
- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要学怎么说零食的价钱，还要玩一个量词配对游戏。"
