# Lesson 10.6: Snack Prices & Measure Words (零食价钱和量词)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 6 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say snack prices with the correct measure word and to match nouns to their correct measure words.

## Success Criteria
- I can say a snack's price using the correct measure word (能用正确的量词说零食价钱)
- I can match at least 10 common nouns to their correct measure word (能把名词和正确的量词配对)
- I can understand a short listening passage about school food and answer questions (能听懂关于学校食物的短文并回答问题)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (with audio playback for CD Track 40, if available — otherwise teacher reads the script aloud)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 5's sentence patterns (有时候……有时候……, 不算太……) and snack vocabulary.
**How:** Pair dialogue — students redo the Text 2 dialogue from memory in pairs (A asks "你每天午饭吃什么？", B answers), then swap roles. Cold call one pair to perform for the class.
**Connection:** "上节课我们学了怎么说午饭习惯。今天我们要学怎么说零食的价钱，还要复习量词——比如'一包薯片'的'包'。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read the Learning Intention; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Recap Vocabulary (measure words in context)
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一卷 | yī juǎn | a roll (of) |
| 一包 | yī bāo | a bag/pack (of) |
| 一份 | yī fèn | a portion (of) |
| 一块 | yī kuài | a piece (of) |
| 一盒 | yī hé | a box (of) |

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**Structure:** 一 + [measure word] + [item] + [price] ("one [unit] of X costs Y")

**Modelled examples:**
1. 一卷糖果五块八毛。
2. 一包薯片三块钱。
3. (Silly example:) 一块三明治一百块钱！(wait for reaction — clearly absurd)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 一包 is the right measure word for 薯片."
- **(Quick translate)** "How do you say 'a box of' in Chinese?"
- **(Cold call)** "Oscar，一份炸鸡翅多少钱？(make up a price and answer in Chinese)"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Price Sentences — We Do (7 min) | 20–27 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 8, p. 96, and Workbook Ex. 13)**
**What:** Project 8 food items with price tags (三明治, 薯条, 咖喱牛肉盒饭, 炸鸡腿, 炸鸡翅, 薯片, 鱼柳盒饭, 猪排盒饭). Teacher models 2 price sentences; students write the remaining 6 in exercise books, then also complete Workbook Ex. 13 (12 more food items with prices, e.g. 一斤羊肉十八元).
**How:** Guided modelling first 2, independent for the rest; cold call to check answers.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the correct measure word next to each picture.
- **Extension:** "It is your turn" — list 4 of your own food/drink items, each with a price tag and correct measure word.
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards for 2 spot-check items.

#### Game: Measure-Word Matching Competition (10 min) | 27–37 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 9, p. 97 — the textbook's own "Group game")**
**How to play:**
1. Split the class into small groups. Each group gets a list of 30 nouns (办公室, 西装, 领带, 连衣裙, 耳环, 足球, 计算器, 帽子, 手套, 电冰箱, 大楼, 汉语, 三明治, 汽车, 电炉, 游泳池, 钢琴, 电池, 烤箱, 报纸, 围巾, 豆腐, 蔬菜, 校服, 项链, 电脑, 小狗, 薯片, 皮靴, 椅子) and a bank of measure words.
2. Groups race to match each noun to its correct measure word, writing the pairs in their exercise books.
3. First group with the most correct matches after 6 minutes wins. Teacher reveals the answer key on the projector for whole-class checking.

**What language it practises:** Measure word–noun pairing across the whole vocabulary range studied so far.
**Setup needed:** Printed noun lists per group, or projected list with groups copying into exercise books.
- **Scaffold:** Give struggling groups a shortened list of 15 nouns.
- **Extension:** Groups that finish early write one full sentence for 3 of their matched pairs.

#### Activity 2: Listen and Match — You Do (5 min) | 37–42 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 10, p. 97)**
**What:** Play Track 40 (or teacher reads the script). Students match 6 prompts (学校有/餐厅卖/午饭有/盒饭/她午饭常吃/她常喝) to 9 lettered options (A–I) about school food/drink, writing their answers in exercise books.
**How:** Play/read the passage twice; students write their matches individually; pair-check before the reveal.
- **Scaffold:** Pause after each relevant section and repeat if needed.
- **Extension:** Students write one summary sentence in Chinese about what the speaker eats for lunch.
- **OTR:** All students complete the matching individually.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project and choral-read the three Success Criteria.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light thumbs for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write one price sentence with the correct measure word for a snack of your choice.
- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要做买东西的角色扮演，还要学一个新句型——'但是'，来对比不同的习惯。"
