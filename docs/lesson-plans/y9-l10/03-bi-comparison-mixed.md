# 比 — Comparing Two Things

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Fresh Market 菜市场
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to compare two things with 比, and to say that a shop sells all kinds of something with 各种.

## Success Criteria
- I can compare two things: A 比 B + adjective (能用「A比B……」比较两样东西)
- I can leave out 很 after 比 (知道「比」后面不用「很」)
- I can say what a shop sells using 各种 (能用「这家……店卖各种……」说一句话)

- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l10/l3-bi-comparison/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's measure words and prices, plus workbook Ex. 5 opposites as the way in to comparison adjectives.
**How:** Correct the teacher. Project four price sentences, three of them wrong: 猪肉十块钱。/ 活鱼十五块一条。/ 豆腐一块五一斤。/ 一公斤苹果八块钱。 Class finds and fixes the errors on whiteboards. Then a 30-second run at workbook Ex. 5 opposites — 贵→便宜, 胖→瘦, 容易→难, 进→出 — said chorally.
**Connection:** 「贵」和「便宜」是一对。今天我们要把两样东西放在一起比 —— 哪个贵，哪个便宜。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Write 比 alone on the board, very large, and leave it there for the lesson.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 比 | bǐ | compare | — (function word) |
| 1 | 各 | gè | all; every | — (function word) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences (all from Text 1 or the book's NOTE):**
1. 那里的菜**比**超市里的新鲜。 — The food there is fresher than at the supermarket.
2. 那里还卖**各**种新鲜蔬菜和水果。 — It also sells all kinds of fresh vegetables and fruit.
3. 这家菜市场卖**各**种肉，肉**比**超市的便宜。 — both new words, one sentence.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 菜市场的＿＿比超市的＿＿。/ 这家＿＿店卖各种＿＿。
**Extension —** 两句连成一句，用「因为……所以……」说明你家为什么去菜市场买菜。

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**A 比 B + adjective** — the book's NOTE, p.93:
1. 我比弟弟高。
2. 我的头发比妹妹的长。
3. 牛肉比猪肉贵。
4. 这个菜市场的菜比超市里的新鲜，还比超市的便宜。 ← hardest: two comparisons joined by 还.

🔴 **The rule to drill:** 比 takes **no** 很. ✗ 牛肉比猪肉很贵 → ✓ 牛肉比猪肉贵。
🔴 **The second 的:** when B repeats A's noun, B keeps 的 and drops the noun — 我的头发比妹妹**的**长。

**各 in phrases (WB Ex. 7):** 各个学校 every school · 各位老师 · 各家餐厅 · 各种书报 · 各个菜市场 · 各家饭店 · 各种文具 · 各家医院.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 这句话对不对？我比弟弟很高。(the 很 has to go)
- 小白板：写「牛肉比猪肉贵」。
- 「各种文具」是什么意思？

#### Text 1 — second paragraph (p.90, CD 37)
> 我妈妈特别喜欢去市场买菜。她说那里的菜比超市里的新鲜，还便宜。在菜市场，一公斤苹果八块钱，在超市要卖十二块钱。

Play, read together, then two questions in Chinese: 妈妈为什么喜欢去菜市场？ 苹果在哪里便宜？便宜多少？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 完成句子 — guided 比 writing (TB Act. 4 + WB Ex. 10) — We Do (8 min)
Teacher models the first of the textbook's five stems aloud, thinking it through — 牛肉比＿＿好吃 → 牛肉比猪肉好吃。 Students then complete the other four in exercise books: 我的书包比＿＿便宜。/ 今年夏天比＿＿热。/ 这双凉鞋比＿＿舒服。/ 这个空调比＿＿小。 Straight on to workbook Ex. 10 — six of its ten stems (我们家的汽车比…, 这个菜市场的菜比…, 我的电脑比…, 这条项链比…, 这双皮靴比…, 这件衬衫比…). Teacher circulates, watching specifically for a stray 很.

**Extension —** Finish all ten of Ex. 10, then rewrite three of them as **two-sided** comparisons that also say why: 这个菜市场的菜比超市的新鲜，因为菜市场的菜是今天早上到的。 Then write one sentence comparing two things where the comparison is **false**, for the class to challenge in the plenary.

#### Activity 2: 角色扮演 — Role play (TB Act. 5) — You Do (7 min)
Pairs, 老师 and 学生, from the book's model:
> 老师：这双运动鞋是你的吗？ 学生：不是。我的是白色的。
> 老师：这双白色的是不是你的？ 学生：也不是。我的比这双新。
> 老师：这双新的运动鞋是你的吗？ 学生：就是这双。谢谢！

Run it once as written, then again swapping roles with a different item from the book's six: 雨伞, 外套, 文具盒, 裤子, 手机, 拖鞋.

**Extension —** The 学生 may not use 新 or 旧. Every rejection has to use a different adjective with 比 — 我的比这把大 / 我的比这件便宜 / 我的比这条长 — and after four turns the 老师 asks 你的到底是哪一把？ and the 学生 describes it in two full sentences without the object in front of them. Then swap: the fluent partner plays 老师 and must invent the prompts unscripted.

#### Activity 3: 这家……店卖各种…… (TB Act. 3 + WB Ex. 6, 8) — You Do (4 min)
Textbook Act. 3's seven shop pictures on screen (水果店 as the book's example — 这家水果店卖各种新鲜水果。 — then 服装店, 家具店, 中药店, 书店, 电器店, 文具店). Students write one sentence per shop in exercise books, using 各种 every time. Workbook Ex. 6 is the same task with a different picture set (花店 given) and Ex. 8 flips it — 你在家具店可以买到床、＿＿ — so run Ex. 8's five stems orally as the check, cold-calling round the room.

**Extension —** Every sentence must also say why you'd go to that shop rather than another one, with 比: 我去这家书店，因为这家的书比那家的便宜。 Then write two sentences for a shop that is **not** on the screen, and let the class guess which shop you mean.

#### Game: 排句子 Sentence Jumble (4 min)
Six 比 sentences cut into word cards (or written jumbled on the board), one set per pair. Fastest correct order wins the round. Use the workbook's own jumbles where they fit, e.g. 觉得/猪排饭/我/贵/有一点儿。
- **Setup needed:** cut card sets, or just the board.
- **Harder version:** the pair must order the sentence **and** then say a true sentence of their own using the same structure, before the point counts. Any sentence containing 很 after 比 loses the point.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student reads each; the class gives a thumbs up or down on criterion 2 (the no-很 rule).
- **Self-assessment:** 会 / 差不多 / 还不会 on each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 用「比」写一句话，比较菜市场和超市。(mini whiteboards)
- **Preview:** 下节课我们把课文一整篇读完，还要看这些字是怎么写出来的 —— 鲜、活、虾、猪。
