# Lesson 10.8: Review + Test Prep (复习和测验准备)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 8 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are consolidating everything learned in the Fresh Market unit — vocabulary, 比, 有时候……有时候……, 但是 — and preparing for the test.

## Success Criteria
- I can recall and correctly write the unit's key vocabulary (能正确回忆并书写本单元的重点词汇)
- I can use 比, 有时候……有时候…… and 但是 accurately in writing (能准确使用"比"、"有时候……有时候……"和"但是")
- I can write an 80–100 word passage describing my own eating habits (能写一篇80–100字描述自己饮食习惯的短文)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (display vocabulary grid, patterns, quiz questions, sentence frames, exit ticket)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** A rapid-fire recall of all 7 previous lessons — market vocabulary, 比, Text 1, snack vocabulary, Text 2 patterns, measure words, shopping roleplay/但是.
**How:** Quick quiz on mini whiteboards — project 6 questions, one per prior lesson (e.g. "写'新鲜'的拼音", "完成：牛肉___猪肉贵。", "写一句用'有时候……有时候……'的句子", "一___薯片" [量词], "完成：我平时……，但是……"). Students write and hold up on three; teacher scans for gaps to target in today's review.
**Connection:** "过去七节课我们学了菜市场的整个单元。今天我们把所有内容串起来复习，为测验做准备。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read the Learning Intention aloud; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Vocabulary Grid Review
Project a consolidated grid of all Lesson 10 vocabulary (market items, comparison words, snack/lunch items, pattern words) grouped by lesson. Choral read through the whole grid once at speed, then a second pass with pinyin hidden — class must produce pinyin + English from 汉字 alone.

#### Pattern Recap
Project all 4 key patterns side by side with one example each:
1. A + 比 + B + adjective (+ 还 + adjective2)
2. 有时候……有时候……
3. 通常……也会……/ 不算太……
4. 平时/通常/一般……，但是……

**CFU Checkpoints:**
- **(Pinyin ↔ Hanzi)** "Write the characters for dòufu."
- **(Make a sentence)** "Give me a sentence with 但是. Write it on your whiteboard."
- **(Exit-style check)** "How would you say 'the box lunch is not too expensive'?"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Vocabulary & Dictionary Extension — We Do (7 min) | 20–27 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 20)**
**What:** Project 12 related words (柳树, 炸弹, 壁球, 肩膀, 海鲜, 龙虾, 水平, 烤鸭, 卷心菜, 胶水, 度假, 口香糖). Students guess the meaning from known components first, write their guess, then teacher reveals the correct meaning for whole-class discussion.
**How:** Pair guessing (4 min), whole-class reveal and discussion (3 min) — reinforces the habit of using known radicals/components to guess unfamiliar words, useful for the test's reading section.
- **Scaffold:** Point out a known component in each word before guessing (e.g. 海鲜 contains 鲜, already known).
- **Extension:** Use 2 of the 12 words in an original sentence. Fast finishers complete Workbook Ex. 12 — a cloze passage about moving to a new home with no market nearby, filling blanks from a word box (除了…以外, 贵, 超级市场, 便宜, 但是, 新鲜, 附近, 因为), which doubles as a final consolidation of 附近/新鲜/便宜/但是 before the writing task.

#### Activity 2: Reading Comprehension + Writing Task — You Do (16 min) | 27–43 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 23)**
**What:** Students read a passage about 小明, who dislikes food and has a thin/small build, and answer 6 True/False questions in exercise books (6 min). Then students write their own 80–100 word passage describing their own eating habits — breakfast/lunch/dinner food and drinks, favourite food/snacks, and their own height and build — using at least one 比 sentence, one 有时候……有时候…… sentence, and one 但是 sentence (10 min).
**How:** Reading comp done individually then whole-class check. Writing task done independently in exercise books; teacher circulates offering targeted support. Collect books at the end for marking.
- **Scaffold:** Provide a paragraph frame with sentence starters for each required section (breakfast/lunch/dinner; favourite food; height/build) and the 3 required patterns pre-written with blanks.
- **Extension:** Extend to 120+ words and add a comparison between two meals (e.g. 午饭比早饭好吃).
- **OTR:** Every student completes both tasks independently; cold call 2–3 volunteers to read their writing task aloud at the end.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project all three Success Criteria; students self-rate silently first, then a volunteer reads each aloud for a final class check-in.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light thumbs (👍/😐/👎) against each Success Criterion — teacher notes any 👎 responses for targeted revision before the test.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write ONE sentence that uses two of today's patterns together, e.g. 我平时吃三明治，但是有时候也会买盒饭，盒饭比三明治贵。
- **Preview:** "下一个单元，我们要学一个新话题——你们准备好了吗？先祝大家测验顺利！"

---

## Note to teacher: workbook items adapted or omitted for this sequence

Two Lesson 10 workbook exercises fall outside what a projector + mini-whiteboards classroom (no 1:1 devices) can deliver as designed, and were not scheduled into Lessons 1–8:

- **Workbook Ex. 21** (internet research — look up 3 traditional Chinese festival names + the date of the coming Chinese New Year): general cultural content, not tied to Fresh Market vocabulary/grammar, and requires internet access. Recommended as an optional take-home extension only if devices become available, or a 2-minute teacher-fronted share if you'd like it included live.
- **Workbook Ex. 22** (typing a passage about eating habits on a computer): the content itself is folded into Lesson 8's Activity 2 writing task (handwritten instead of typed) — the same 80–100 word output is produced by hand in exercise books.

All other numbered activities and workbook exercises in the Lesson 10 textbook/workbook range (pp. 90–99 / pp. 108–117) are covered across Lessons 1–8 above.
