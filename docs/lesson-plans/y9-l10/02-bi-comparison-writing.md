# Lesson 10.2: 比 Comparison Grammar (比字句)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 2 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to compare two things in Chinese using 比.

## Success Criteria
- I can build a comparison sentence: A + 比 + B + adjective (能用"A比B……"比较两样东西)
- I can extend a 比 sentence with 还 to say "even more" (能用"比……还……"加强比较)
- I can write correct 比 sentences about market and shopping items (能写出正确的"比"字句)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's market vocabulary and price pattern (猪肉/牛肉/鸡肉/豆腐 + price + 一斤).
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire — teacher shows a market item (Chinese side), class chorally says pinyin + English. Then project 2 price pictures side by side (e.g. 猪肉十五块一斤 / 牛肉二十块一斤) and ask: "哪个贵？哪个便宜？" to surface the comparison need.
**Connection:** "上节课我们学了怎么说价钱。牛肉二十块，猪肉十五块——牛肉比猪肉贵。今天我们学怎么用'比'字句来比较两样东西。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read aloud; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion. Keep visible throughout.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary / Recap Items
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 比 | bǐ | compare |
| 还 | hái | even more; still |
| 好吃 | hǎochī | tasty |
| 书包 | shūbāo | schoolbag |
| 铅笔盒 | qiānbǐhé | pencil case |

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**Structure:** A + 比 + B + adjective ("A is more [adjective] than B")
**Stacked structure:** A + 比 + B + adjective + 还 + adjective2 ("A is more [adj] than B, and even more [adj2]")

**NOTE box (from the textbook, project this exactly):** 比 is used to compare two nouns, e.g. 我比弟弟高。/ 我的头发比妹妹的长。

**Modelled examples (project, read aloud, class repeats):**
1. 牛肉比猪肉好吃。(Beef tastes better than pork.)
2. 我的书包比铅笔盒便宜。(My schoolbag is cheaper than my pencil case.)
3. 那里的菜比超市里的新鲜，还便宜。(The vegetables there are fresher than the supermarket's, and even cheaper.) — this introduces the stacked 比…还… pattern.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Error correction)** Project: 我比高弟弟。— "What's wrong with this sentence?" (Word order — should be 我比弟弟高。)
- **(Make a sentence)** "Give me a sentence with 比. Write it on your whiteboard."
- **(Cold call)** "Sam，用'比'比较'苹果'和'香蕉'。"

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Component Recall Warm-Up — We Do (3 min) | 20–23 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 3)**
**What:** Project 9 English meanings (1/30 metre, rice, foot, fight, strength, blue/green, arrow, utensil, down) linked to simple characters/components that build several Lesson 10 characters (e.g. 羊+鱼 in 鲜, 犭 in 猪). Students write the matching simple 汉字 for each on mini whiteboards.
**How:** Rapid-fire — teacher gives the English, students write and hold up on three; reveal the correct character immediately after each.
- **Scaffold:** Give the first letter of the pinyin as a hint for the 3 hardest items.
- **Extension:** Name one Lesson 10 character (already learned) that contains each component.

#### Activity 2: Guided 比 Sentence Completion — We Do (6 min) | 23–29 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 4, p. 93)**
**What:** Project the 5 fill-in-the-blank 比 sentences from Act. 4, covering 牛肉/猪肉, 书包, 夏天热, 凉鞋舒服, 空调小.
**How:** Teacher models the first 2 as a think-aloud on the board. Students complete the remaining 3 independently in exercise books. Cold call students to read completed sentences; class checks together.
- **Scaffold:** Keep the NOTE box example sentence projected as a model.
- **Extension:** "It is your turn" — students write 2 more original 比 sentences about market items not given in the textbook.
- **OTR:** All students write all 5 sentences; whole-class check via cold call.

#### Activity 3: Ownership Roleplay — We Do/You Do (5 min) | 29–34 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 5, p. 93)**
**What:** Model the sneaker dialogue with a confident student first: A: 这双运动鞋是我的。 B: 我的比这双新。 Pairs then practise their own version for 6 further item prompts (umbrella, jacket, pencil case, pants, phone, sandals), swapping roles after 3 items.
**How:** Teacher models once at the front; pairs pick 3 of the 6 prompts each. Cold call 1 pair to perform for the class.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the dialogue frame on the projector: "这___是我的。" / "我的比这个___。"
- **Extension:** Add a second comparison with 还 (e.g. 我的比这个新，还便宜。).
- **OTR:** Every pair performs at least 3 exchanges.

#### Activity 4: Workbook 比 Practice — You Do (6 min) | 34–40 min
**(Based on Workbook Ex. 10)**
**What:** Students complete as many of the 10 比 sentences as they can in exercise books, covering 汽车, 菜, 电脑, 学生, 帽子, 项链, 皮靴, 耳环, 衬衫 (example given: 我的头发比姐姐的长).
**How:** Independent writing; pair-swap books to check 3 sentences each before teacher reveals answers. Any unfinished items are completed as a quick 2-minute consolidation at the very start of the next lesson's Review.
- **Scaffold:** Provide a bank of adjectives (贵/便宜/新/旧/长/短/大/小/好吃/好看) on the board for students who need support choosing the comparison word.
- **Extension:** Fluent speakers add 还 to stack a second comparison onto at least 2 of their sentences.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble — 比字句拼句子 (3 min) | 40–43 min
**How to play:**
1. Project 2 sets of scrambled words for a 比 sentence, e.g. [新鲜 / 比 / 超市 / 菜市场 / 的菜 / 的菜].
2. In pairs, students race to reorder the words into a correct 比 sentence on their whiteboards.
3. First pair with the correct sentence holds it up and reads it aloud for the class to confirm.

**What language it practises:** 比 sentence word order.
**Setup needed:** Mini whiteboards only.
- **Scaffold:** Give the sentence's English translation alongside the scrambled words.
- **Extension:** After solving, pairs write one additional original 比 sentence using the same adjective.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project the three Success Criteria; students read them chorally. "今天我们做到了吗？"
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light thumbs (👍/😐/👎) for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Correct the teacher — project 我豆腐比便宜牛肉。Students fix the word order and write the corrected sentence.
- **Preview:** "下一课，我们要读一篇关于菜市场的短文，还要学怎么描述自己去市场买菜的经历。"
