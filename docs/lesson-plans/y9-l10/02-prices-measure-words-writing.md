# 活鱼、活虾、豆腐 — Prices by 斤 and 公斤

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Fresh Market 菜市场
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the rest of the market's food and to write its price with the right measure word.

## Success Criteria
- I can name chicken, live fish, live shrimp and tofu in Chinese (能说出「鸡肉」「活鱼」「活虾」「豆腐」)
- I can say that something is alive/fresh using 活 (能用「活」说活鱼、活虾)
- I can write a price sentence: item + 多少钱 + 一斤/一公斤/一块/一条 (能写「猪肉十块钱一斤」这样的句子)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l10/l2-prices-measure-words/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD track 38 (textbook p.94)
- Textbook p.91 Act. 1 and p.97 Act. 9; workbook pp.108–109, 113 (Ex. 1, 4, 13)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's six words and the 我家附近有一个……，那里的……很……。 pattern.
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on 市场, 菜市场, 猪肉, 牛肉, 新鲜, 便宜 — characters only, class says pinyin + English. Then workbook **Ex. 1** on the projector — eight pictures matched to 猪肉 / 牛肉 / 羊肉 / 鸡肉 / 活鱼 / 活虾 / 豆腐 / 蔬菜 (the book gives 牛肉 as the example). Students call the letters out; four of the eight are today's new words, so it doubles as a preview.
**Connection:** 上节课我们说了菜市场卖猪肉和牛肉。今天我们看看那里还卖什么，还要学怎么写价钱。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Point at criterion 3 and say plainly: 今天最重要的是量词 —— 斤、公斤、块、条。

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles (last cycle is 1 word + the 斤/公斤 pair)

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 鸡肉 | jīròu | chicken meat | a drumstick and wing |
| 1 | 豆腐 | dòufu | tofu; bean curd | three pale cubes on a board |
| 2 | 活 | huó | alive; live | a fish mid-flip, water splashing |
| 2 | 虾（蝦） | xiā | shrimp | two shrimps, whiskers curled |
| 3 | 公斤 | gōngjīn | kilogram | a stall scale with a pan |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 菜市场卖**鸡肉**。 — The fresh market sells chicken.
2. **豆腐**很便宜。 — Tofu is cheap.
3. 那里的**鸡肉**很新鲜，**豆腐**也很便宜。 — The chicken there is fresh and the tofu is cheap too.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ＿＿＿＿很新鲜。/ 我喜欢吃＿＿＿＿。
**Extension —** 一句话里用上「鸡肉」「豆腐」和「还」 —— 那里的鸡肉很新鲜，还很便宜。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 菜市场卖**活**鱼。 — The fresh market sells live fish.
2. 我妈妈买了两只**虾**。 — My mum bought two shrimps.
3. 那里的**活虾**又大又新鲜。 — The live shrimp there are big and fresh. *(又…又… is known from Book 2)*

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 菜市场卖＿＿鱼和＿＿虾。/ 那里的活虾很＿＿。
**Extension —** 用「活」写两句，一句说鱼，一句说虾，两句都要有价钱。

**Cycle 3 — example sentence (from Text 1):**
1. 在菜市场，一**公斤**苹果八块钱，在超市要卖十二块钱。

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 一公斤＿＿＿＿＿＿块钱。
**Extension —** 同一样东西写两次 —— 一次用斤，一次用公斤。价钱要对得上。

**Recall slide:** 鸡肉 · 豆腐 · 活 · 虾 · 公斤 — characters only, class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**item + 价钱 + 一 + 量词。** (from p.91 Act. 1: 大白菜两块五毛一斤。)
1. 猪肉十块钱一斤。
2. 活鱼十五块钱一条。
3. 豆腐一块五一块。
4. 活虾十五块一斤，比牛排便宜。 ← hardest: a price plus a comparison, previewing next lesson.

**斤 vs 公斤:** 一斤 = half a kilo, the unit the market actually uses. 一公斤 = 两斤, the formal/metric one. Almost every price tag in the textbook says 斤.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「活」是什么意思？举手。
- 小白板：写「豆腐一块五一块」。
- 这句话哪里错了？猪肉十块钱。(missing the measure word — 一斤)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 词汇总览 + 摆摊子 price board — We Do (10 min)
**Summary board:** 鸡肉 · 豆腐 · 活鱼 · 活虾 · 公斤 plus last lesson's 猪肉 · 牛肉 · 新鲜 · 便宜, characters and pictures, on screen throughout.
**Task:** Textbook p.91 Act. 1. Project the fifteen priced items (土豆 ¥1.50/斤, 南瓜 ¥3.00/斤, 西红柿 ¥3.10/斤, 冬瓜 ¥1.30/斤, 豆角 ¥2.80/斤, 菜花儿 ¥3.00/斤, 黄瓜 ¥2.10/斤, 活鱼 ¥15.00/条, 活虾 ¥15.00/斤, 猪肉 ¥10.00/斤, 羊肉 ¥12.00/斤, 牛排 ¥14.00/斤, 鸡翅·鸡腿 ¥13.00/斤, 豆腐 ¥1.50/块, 豆腐干 ¥1.00/块). Teacher models the book's example — 大白菜两块五毛一斤。 Students write eight of the fifteen as full price sentences in their exercise books. Pair-check, then the teacher takes three aloud.

Straight on to workbook **Ex. 4** — fifteen pictured foods written in Chinese (生菜 given, then 西红柿, 西兰花, 橙子, 土豆, 香蕉, 苹果, 南瓜, 西瓜, 梨, 比萨饼, 汉堡包, 面包, 牛排, 饮料). Most of these are Book 2 words the class already has; treat it as a naming sweep, not new teaching, and give it 3 minutes.

**Extension —** Write all fifteen, then add a two-line stall cry for the three cheapest, in the style of CD 38: 快来买豆腐！两块钱一块！ Then work out and write, in Chinese, what 一斤猪肉加一条活鱼 costs altogether.

#### Activity 2: 听价钱 — listen and write (WB Ex.13 + CD 38) — You Do (8 min)
Play CD 38 twice (textbook p.94 Act. 6 grid: 猪肉, 牛排, 羊肉, 鸡腿, 活鱼, 活虾, 豆腐). Students write the price they hear against the right picture. Then, straight on from the grid, workbook Ex.13 — twelve priced pictures written up as full sentences, the book's example being 一斤羊肉十八元。 Students do six of the twelve.

**Extension —** After the second play, write each item's price **as a sentence** rather than a figure, and add whether it is 贵 or 便宜 compared with the item above it — 黄鱼二十八块一斤，比豆腐贵多了。 No replay of the audio.

#### Game: 量词大赛 — measure-word race (p.97 Act. 9) (5 min)
Project the thirty nouns (办公室, 西装, 领带, 连衣裙, 耳环, 足球, 计算器, 帽子, 手套, 电冰箱, 大楼, 汉语, 三明治, 汽车, 电炉, 游泳池, 钢琴, 电池, 烤箱, 报纸, 围巾, 豆腐, 蔬菜, 校服, 项链, 电脑, 小狗, 薯片, 皮靴, 椅子). Small groups race to match each noun with a correct measure word on a shared whiteboard. Most correct matches in the time wins.
- **Setup needed:** one mini whiteboard per group; nothing printed.
- **Harder version:** the group must write the measure word **inside a price sentence** — 一双皮靴四百六十五块 — not just the bare word. A bare measure word scores nothing.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud in Chinese by three students, one each.
- **Self-assessment:** 会 / 差不多 / 还不会 on fingers for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一个价钱句，用「活鱼」或者「豆腐」，量词不能错。
- **Preview:** 下节课我们学「比」—— 怎么说菜市场的菜比超市的便宜。
