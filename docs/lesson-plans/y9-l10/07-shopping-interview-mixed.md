# Lesson 10.7: Shopping Roleplay + Frequency Contrast + Interview (买东西和日常习惯)

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Fresh Market
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 7 of 8
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to roleplay a shopping conversation and to contrast our usual habits with an exception using 但是.

## Success Criteria
- I can roleplay buying an item, asking to see/try it and asking the price (能表演买东西、试穿和问价钱的对话)
- I can contrast a usual habit with an exception using a frequency adverb + 但是 (能用"平时/通常……但是……"对比习惯和例外)
- I can interview a partner about their daily eating routine and report back (能采访同伴的日常饮食习惯并汇报)

## Materials Needed
- Projector
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Students' exercise books and pens

---

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 6's measure words and snack prices.
**How:** Slap the board — 2 students at the board with 10 measure-word cards stuck up (卷/包/份/块/盒/斤/公斤/条/个/瓶); teacher says a noun, students slap the matching measure word. Winner stays for the next round.
**Connection:** "上节课我们学了量词和价钱。今天我们要用这些词做买东西的角色扮演，还要学怎么对比'平时'和'今天'的不同。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read aloud; a student volunteer reads each Success Criterion.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### Recap Vocabulary
| 汉字 | 拼音 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 试穿 | shìchuān | to try on |
| 看 | kàn | to look at |
| 但是 | dànshì | but |
| 一般 | yìbān | generally |
| 经常 | jīngcháng | often |

#### Key Sentence Patterns
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| 你想看/试穿哪个/哪件……？ | 你想看哪块手表？ |
| 这个/这件……，一……块的。 | 这块，一千五百块的。 |
| 平时/通常/一般/有时候/经常……，但是…… | 我平时八点到学校，但是今天七点就到了。 |

**Modelled examples:**
1. A: 你想看哪双鞋？ B: 这双，两百块的。
2. 我平时买盒饭吃，但是今天我带了三明治。
3. (Silly example:) 我平时坐公共汽车上学，但是今天骑了一头大象！(wait for reaction)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- **(Thumbs up/down)** "Thumbs up if 试穿 means 'to try on'."
- **(Error correction)** Project: 我但是平时吃三明治。— "What's wrong here?" (word order — 但是 goes at the start of the second clause)
- **(Cold call)** "Ivy，用'但是'说一句对比今天和平时的句子。"

---

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Shopping Roleplay — We Do (8 min) | 20–28 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 11, p. 98)**
**What:** Model the watch-shopping dialogue with a confident student, then pairs practise their own version using 8 item prompts (goldfish, watermelon, calculators, shorts, necklace/earrings, books, sandals/shoes, belt/tie), each with the verbs 买/试穿/看 and a price.
**How:** Teacher models once at the front. Pairs pick 3 of the 8 item prompts and perform each dialogue for their partner, then swap. Cold call 1–2 pairs to perform for the class.
- **Scaffold:** Provide a dialogue frame with blanks: "你想___哪___？" / "这___，___块的。"
- **Extension:** Add a 比 comparison or 不算太 comment to the dialogue (e.g. 这个比那个便宜，不算太贵。).
- **OTR:** Every pair performs at least 3 exchanges; cold call for 2 pairs to share with the class.

#### Activity 2: 但是 Contrast Sentences — You Do (8 min) | 28–36 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 12, p. 99, and Workbook Ex. 18)**
**What:** Students complete 5 sentences using a frequency adverb + 但是 contrast structure in exercise books, then answer 3 of the 9 personal questions from Workbook Ex. 18 that relate to food preference and daily habits.
**How:** Independent writing (6 min); pair-check with a neighbour; cold call 2 students to read a completed sentence.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the frequency-adverb word bank (平时/通常/一般/有时候/经常) and one worked example.
- **Extension:** Write one additional original 但是 sentence about your own eating habits. Fast finishers move on to Workbook Ex. 19 — compare a Chinese menu (菜单A：中式) and a Western menu (菜单B：西式) with prices, and answer the 6 comprehension questions.
- **OTR:** Mini whiteboards for one live "bonus" 但是 sentence created by the teacher.

#### Activity 3 / Game: Partner Interview — 日常饮食习惯采访 (7 min) | 36–43 min
**(Based on Textbook Act. 13, p. 99)**
**How to play:** In pairs, students interview each other using the 8 textbook prompts (wake time & breakfast; school commute; morning/afternoon periods; lunch time & food; school canteen habits; dismissal & journey home; dinner time & food; evening activities & bedtime), writing brief notes on their partner's answers. 2–3 pairs then report one interesting fact about their partner to the class ("Report to the class").

**What language it practises:** All of today's patterns in a communicative, personalised context — question forms, 有时候/通常/但是, food vocabulary.
**Setup needed:** None — projected question list as prompt.
- **Scaffold:** Provide the 8 questions fully written out (not just prompts) for students who need support.
- **Extension:** Students write up their partner's answers as a short paragraph instead of notes, using 但是 to note any exception to their partner's routine.

---

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Project and choral-read the three Success Criteria.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic-light thumbs for each criterion.
- **Exit ticket (mini whiteboards, 2 min):** Write one sentence about your partner using 但是, e.g. "他平时吃三明治，但是今天买了盒饭。"
- **Preview:** "下一课是复习课——我们会复习整个单元的词汇、句型，为测验做准备。"
