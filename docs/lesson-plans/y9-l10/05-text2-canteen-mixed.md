# Text 2 — 学校餐厅 Lunch

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Fresh Market 菜市场
**Lesson:** Lesson 10 — Fresh Market 菜市场 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what we usually buy for lunch at school, and how much it costs.

## Success Criteria
- I can name a sandwich, a box lunch, crisps and chips in Chinese (能说出「三明治」「盒饭」「薯片」「薯条」)
- I can say what I usually do with 平时 (能用「平时」说日常的事)
- I can ask and answer 多少钱一盒？ (能问价钱，也能回答)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l10/l5-text2-canteen/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD track 39 (textbook p.95)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Text 1's food vocabulary and the radicals from Lesson 4, through a reading passage.
**How:** Workbook **Ex. 11** — the 我妈妈最近在减肥 passage on the projector, read chorally, then its six questions answered orally in Chinese (他妈妈现在吃牛肉吗？她现在只吃什么？她每天要吃几种蔬菜和水果？她每天喝什么？她工作忙吗？她最近瘦了很多吗？). Every food word in it came from Lessons 1–4. Close with one whiteboard item: 写「鲜」，圈部首。
**Connection:** 菜市场是买菜的地方。今天我们去一个你们每天都去的地方 —— 学校餐厅。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Ask the class in Chinese before starting: 你们今天午饭吃了什么？ Take three answers in whatever Chinese they have; leave the gaps hanging as the reason for today's words.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 三明治 | sānmíngzhì | sandwich | a triangular sandwich on a plate |
| 1 | 盒饭 | héfàn | box lunch | a lidded lunch box, rice and two dishes |
| 2 | 薯片 | shǔpiàn | chips; crisps | an open packet, crisps spilling |
| 2 | 薯条 | shǔtiáo | French fries | a red carton of fries |
| 3 | 平时 | píngshí | usually | — (time word) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences (from Text 2):**
1. 我有时候从家里带**三明治**。 — I sometimes bring a sandwich from home.
2. 我通常买**盒饭**吃。 — I usually buy a box lunch.
3. 我有时候带**三明治**，有时候买**盒饭**。 — both words, one sentence.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我午饭有时候吃＿＿＿＿。/ 我通常买＿＿＿＿。
**Extension —** 用「有时候……，有时候……」写一句，再加上价钱。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **薯片**七块六。 — from the price board on p.95.
2. 我不常吃**薯条**。 — I don't often eat fries.
3. **薯片**比**薯条**便宜。 — both words plus 比 from Lesson 3.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ＿＿＿＿比＿＿＿＿便宜。
**Extension —** 三样东西排价钱，从便宜到贵，用两个「比」句写出来。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 你**平时**买些什么？ — from Text 2.
2. 我**平时**在学校餐厅买东西吃。
3. 我**平时**吃盒饭，有时候吃三明治。

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我平时午饭＿＿＿＿。
**Extension —** 用「平时……，但是今天……」写一句，两边要不一样。

**Recall slide:** 三明治 · 盒饭 · 薯片 · 薯条 · 平时 — characters only, class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**多少钱一 + 量词？ → 价钱 + 一 + 量词。** (Text 2)
1. 盒饭贵吗？多少钱一盒？ — 十九块，不算太贵。
2. 三明治多少钱一个？ — 七块六。
3. 薯条多少钱一份？ — 五块五。
4. 咖喱牛肉饭比鱼柳饭便宜吗？— 不便宜，鱼柳饭十六块，咖喱牛肉饭十五块。 ← hardest: a price question answered with a comparison.

**不算太 + adj.** = "not all that…" — 不算太贵。 Worth teaching as a set phrase; it's how you decline to call something expensive without saying it's cheap.

#### Text 2 (p.95, CD 39)
Full dialogue, characters only. Play track 39, read chorally, then the class runs it as a pair dialogue with roles swapped once.

> A: 你每天午饭吃什么？
> B: 我有时候从家里带三明治，有时候在学校餐厅买东西吃。
> A: 你平时买些什么？
> B: 我通常买盒饭吃，有时候也会买薯片、薯条、炸鸡翅（膀）、糖果等。
> A: 盒饭贵吗？多少钱一盒？
> B: 十九块，不算太贵。
> A: 你喜欢吃哪种盒饭？
> B: 我常买咖喱牛肉饭、鱼柳饭、猪排饭等。

Three comprehension questions in Chinese: B 平时买什么？ 盒饭多少钱？ B 喜欢哪几种盒饭？

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「平时」是什么意思？举手。
- 小白板：写「盒饭十九块一盒」。
- 「不算太贵」是贵还是便宜？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 词汇总览 + 小吃价目表 (TB Act. 8) — We Do (9 min)
**Summary board:** 三明治 · 盒饭 · 薯片 · 薯条 · 平时, characters and pictures, up throughout.
**Task:** Textbook p.96 Act. 8 — eight priced snacks (三明治 ¥15.00, 薯条 ¥5.50, 咖喱牛肉盒饭 ¥15.00, 炸鸡腿 ¥8.40, 炸鸡翅 ¥10.00, 薯片 ¥7.60, 鱼柳盒饭 ¥16.00, 猪排盒饭 ¥14.00). The book's example is 一卷糖果五块八毛。 Students write all eight as price sentences in exercise books. Then the book's own follow-on: list four foods or drinks of your own, each with a price tag.

**Extension —** Write the eight, then turn the list into a **canteen menu with a budget**: you have 30 块 for lunch and must buy three different things. Write what you'd buy, the total, and one thing you left out, with 因为 for why. Every sentence must carry a measure word.

#### Activity 2: Text 2 as a pair dialogue — You Do (8 min)
Pairs run Text 2 with the script on screen, then again with the screen blanked, then a third time substituting their own true answers for B's lines. Teacher takes two pairs in front of the class.

**Extension —** A drops the script and uses textbook **Act. 13**'s canteen questions instead — 你们学校有餐厅吗？你经常去那里买什么吃？你们中午几点吃午饭？你一般吃什么？ — plus two of their own. B answers unprepared, in full sentences. Then, as the book's **Report to the class** step asks, A reports back in the third person: 他午饭一般吃盒饭，他说不算太贵。 Swap and run again — the fluent student takes A both times.

#### Activity 3: 分类 Categorize (WB Ex. 15) — You Do (5 min)
Workbook Ex. 15: sort fifteen items (炒菜 炒面 糖果 炸鸡翅 比萨饼 可乐 汽水 炒青菜 炒大虾 红烧豆腐 热狗 薯片 汉堡包 三明治 咖喱牛肉) into 中餐 / 快餐 / 零食 / 饮料. Two students justify one placement each aloud, in Chinese.

**Extension —** Sort all fifteen, then argue two of them into a **different** column and defend it in Chinese — 我觉得三明治也是零食，因为我平时下午吃三明治，不是午饭。 This is the Unit 4 Test's first question in a harder form.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; a student answers 你平时午饭吃什么？ in a full sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** 会 / 差不多 / 还不会 on each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 用「平时」写一句，说说你在学校午饭吃什么，多少钱。
- **Preview:** 下节课是这一课的最后一节 —— 炸鸡翅、糖果，还有「我平时……，但是……」。
