# 中餐还是西餐？ Chinese Food or Western Food?

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐 | Lesson 1 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name types of food in Chinese and ask someone to choose between two things using 还是.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and read six food words in Chinese (中餐、西餐、快餐、热狗、汉堡包、比萨饼)
- I can ask a choice question with 还是 (你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？)
- I can answer a 还是 question without repeating 还是 (我喜欢吃中餐 / 我两种都喜欢吃)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l1-food-types-haishi`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed character cards for Slap the Character: 中餐 西餐 快餐 热狗 汉堡包 比萨饼 (two A3 sets)
- Printed half-slip for the exit ticket

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 10's food language — 蔬菜、水果 vocabulary, the 喜欢 degree scale, and the two grammar words 应该 and 可是.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on eight L10 words in characters only (蔬菜、生菜、黄瓜、土豆、西红柿、苹果、香蕉、西瓜) — class says pinyin + English chorally. Then mini whiteboards: "Write what your mother would say to you about vegetables." Target: 妈妈说我应该每天吃蔬菜。Take two boards, then one more prompt using 可是 — 我不太喜欢吃蔬菜，可是……。

**Connection:** "Last lesson you could say which vegetables and fruits you like. Today we widen it to whole *types* of food — Chinese, western, fast food — and you learn how to make someone choose between two of them."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
On the projector, left on screen all lesson. Read the intention aloud together. Before any Chinese appears, put the six pictures up one at a time and have the class shout the English — the images land first, so the characters attach to something.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 中餐 | zhōngcān | Chinese food | bowl of rice + chopsticks |
| 1 | 西餐 | xīcān | western food | plate with knife and fork |
| 2 | 快餐 | kuàicān | fast food | takeaway bag + cup |
| 2 | 热狗 | règǒu | hot dog | hot dog in a bun |
| 3 | 汉堡包 | hànbǎobāo | hamburger | hamburger |
| 3 | 比萨饼 | bǐsàbǐng | pizza | pizza slice |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢吃**中餐**。 — I like eating Chinese food.
2. 我不太喜欢吃**西餐**。 — I don't much like eating western food.
3. **中餐**、**西餐**，我都喜欢吃。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃中餐，______ 喜欢吃西餐。
**Extension —** Two clauses with 可是, reporting someone else: 我很喜欢吃西餐，可是我妈妈说我应该多吃中餐。 Then say it aloud without reading it off the board.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我很喜欢吃**快餐**。 — I really like eating fast food.
2. 我不喜欢吃**热狗**。 — I don't like eating hot dogs.
3. 我喜欢吃**快餐**，可是我不喜欢吃**热狗**。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃快餐。我 ______ 喜欢吃热狗。
**Extension —** Write what your own mother would say, using 说……应该 and naming two things you should eat instead: 妈妈说我应该多吃蔬菜和水果，不应该每天吃热狗。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢吃**汉堡包**。 — I like eating hamburgers.
2. **比萨饼**是西餐。 — Pizza is western food.
3. **汉堡包**和**比萨饼**都是快餐。 — both words, carrying 快餐 from cycle 2.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** ______ 和 ______ 都是快餐。
**Extension —** 除了汉堡包以外，我还喜欢吃比萨饼和热狗。 Then add a 可是 clause naming something you don't like, so it runs as one connected turn.

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 中餐 西餐 快餐 热狗 汉堡包 比萨饼 — characters only, no pinyin, no English. Class reads them back. If a pair stalls, reteach it before moving on.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**A 还是 B？** — "or" in a **question**. The answer never contains 还是.

1. 你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？ — 我喜欢吃中餐。
2. 你想吃热狗还是汉堡包？ — 我想吃汉堡包。
3. 我们去打球还是看电影？ — 我们去看电影。
4. 你想跟爸爸去吃快餐还是跟妈妈去吃中餐？ — two full clauses either side of 还是.

Think-aloud on #4: "Everything before 还是 and everything after it has to balance — 跟爸爸去吃快餐 / 跟妈妈去吃中餐. Same shape both sides."

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "什么是快餐？Give me two examples in Chinese." (热狗、汉堡包、比萨饼)
- Error correction on the board: 我喜欢吃中餐还是西餐。 — what's wrong? (还是 only lives in questions; here it needs 和 or 都)
- Mini whiteboards: "Write — Do you want pizza or a hot dog?" (你想吃比萨饼还是热狗？)

#### Text — Text 1, first half (p.100, CD track 51)
> A: 你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？
> B: 我两种都喜欢吃。
> A: 你喜欢吃快餐吗？
> B: 也喜欢。我喜欢吃热狗、汉堡包和比萨饼。

Play track 51 to this point, students follow. Read chorally. Two questions: B 喜欢吃中餐吗？ B 喜欢吃什么快餐？ Point out 两种 — 种 is last lesson's measure word doing new work.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 三个问题 — We Do (8 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters + pictures, stays on screen for the whole activity.

Pairs. Write **three** 还是 questions that between them use **all six** words — so each question pairs two of them. Then ask three different classmates (stand up, move), record the answers, and report two back to the class.

Model on the board: 你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？ / 你想吃热狗还是汉堡包？ / 你喜欢吃快餐还是比萨饼？

**Share:** two students report — 我问了小明，他喜欢吃中餐。

**Extension —** The three questions must each use a different verb (吃／想吃／喜欢吃), and the report-back is in the third person with a 可是 clause: 小明喜欢吃汉堡包，可是他妈妈说他不应该每天吃快餐。 No notes when reporting.

#### Activity 2: 我们应该……还是……？ — You Do (8 min)
Workbook Ex.9 (p.121), on mini whiteboards. Build questions from the three columns, all starting 我们应该:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 坐 | 电车 | 还是 地铁？ |
| 吃 | 中餐 | 还是 西餐？ |
| 学 | 法语 | 还是 西班牙语？ |
| 穿 | 裤子 | 还是 裙子？ |

Students write all four, hold up, then ask a partner and get a real answer.

**Extension —** Write two more of your own using verbs from this column that aren't listed, then answer each in a full sentence that explains the choice: 我们应该学法语还是西班牙语？我想学西班牙语，可是我姐姐说我应该学法语。

#### Game: Slap the Character (7 min)
Two students at the board, six A3 character cards spread out. Teacher says a word in Chinese; first to slap it scores. Rotate every three calls so most of the class gets a turn.

- **Setup needed:** two printed A3 sets of 中餐 西餐 快餐 热狗 汉堡包 比萨饼
- **Harder version:** the teacher doesn't say the word — the teacher says a *clue sentence* (这是西餐，是圆的 / 妈妈说我不应该每天吃这个) and the students slap what it describes. Fluent students take the caller's chair and must invent the clue themselves in Chinese.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** student volunteer reads all three aloud from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs on each criterion — 👍 / 😐 / 👎. Note who shows 😐 on the 还是 criterion; they get the word bank first in Lesson 2.
- **Exit ticket:** on a half-slip — write **one** 还是 question about food, and your own answer to it. Collected at the door.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson — what you *drink*. 你喜欢喝什么饮料？And you'll design your own fast-food restaurant."
