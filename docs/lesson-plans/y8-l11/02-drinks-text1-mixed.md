# 你喜欢喝什么饮料？ Drinks

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐 | Lesson 2 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what we drink in Chinese, and to sort food and drink into Chinese, western, fast food and beverages.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and read four drink words in Chinese (喝、饮料、可乐、汽水)
- I can ask and answer 你喜欢喝什么饮料？
- I can sort a food or drink into 中餐 / 西餐 / 快餐 / 饮料 and say why (比萨饼是快餐)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l2-drinks-text1`), speakers for CD tracks 51, 52, 53
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed four-column sorting grid (中餐 / 西餐 / 快餐 / 饮料), one per pair
- Blank A4 menu templates for the restaurant project, one per pair
- Coloured pens for the menus
- Squared paper for the character relay
- Exercise books or squared copying sheets for the 写一写 block (餐 喝 饮 料)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's six food words and the 还是 question, plus the unit's pinyin focus — **ie / üe**.

**How:** Two halves.
1. *(4 min)* Flashcard rapid fire on 中餐 西餐 快餐 热狗 汉堡包 比萨饼, characters only. Then cold call four students with a 还是 question each; they must answer *without* 还是.
2. *(4 min)* Textbook p.102 Ex.3, CD track 52 — listen and fill in the vowels with tone marks. Six items: jièyuè · quèyuè · xièjué · quēlüè · quèqiè · jiéyuē. Play twice, students write on whiteboards, answers up on screen.

**Connection:** "You can already say what you like to *eat*. Today we finish the other half of the sentence — what you like to *drink* — and then you'll read the whole of Text 1."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Displayed on the projector and left up. Read aloud together. Hold up a bottle of water and a can — "两种。一种是水。另一种叫什么？" — to open the gap the lesson fills.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 喝 | hē | to drink | figure drinking from a cup |
| 1 | 饮料 | yǐnliào | drink; beverage | three bottles in a row |
| 2 | 可乐 | kělè | coke | cola bottle with a straw |
| 2 | 汽水 | qìshuǐ | soda water | glass with bubbles |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢**喝**水。 — I like drinking water.
2. 我不喜欢**喝**这些**饮料**。 — I don't like drinking these beverages.
3. 你喜欢**喝**什么**饮料**？ — both words, and it's the question they'll use all lesson.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢喝 ______ 。我不太喜欢喝 ______ 。
**Extension —** 妈妈说我应该多喝水，不应该喝太多饮料。 Write it, then add a 可是 clause of your own so it runs as two joined sentences.

Point out the radical clue: 喝 has 口 (mouth) on the left — drinking happens at the mouth. 汽水 has 氵 (water) twice.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢**喝可乐**。 — I like drinking coke.
2. 我不太喜欢**喝汽水**。 — I don't much like drinking soda water.
3. 你想喝**可乐**还是**汽水**？ — both words, carrying last lesson's 还是.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 你想喝 ______ 还是 ______ ？
**Extension —** Ask three classmates 你想喝可乐还是汽水？ then report the count in Chinese: 我问了三个同学。两个同学喜欢喝可乐，一个同学喜欢喝汽水。 Report standing, no notes.

**Recall slide:** 喝 饮料 可乐 汽水 — characters only. Class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**你喜欢喝什么饮料？ → 我喜欢喝……。**

1. 你喜欢喝什么饮料？ — 我喜欢喝可乐。
2. 你喜欢喝什么饮料？ — 可乐和汽水，我都喜欢喝。
3. 你喜欢喝什么饮料？ — 我不喜欢喝饮料，我喜欢喝水。
4. 你喜欢吃什么快餐？喜欢喝什么饮料？ — 我喜欢吃汉堡包，喝可乐。 Both halves in one turn.

#### Text — Text 1 in full (p.100, CD track 51)
> A: 你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？
> B: 我两种都喜欢吃。
> A: 你喜欢吃快餐吗？
> B: 也喜欢。我喜欢吃热狗、汉堡包和比萨饼。
> A: 你喜欢喝什么饮料？
> B: 我喜欢喝可乐和汽水。

Play track 51 through. Students follow, then choral read, then pairs read it twice swapping roles. Two questions in Chinese: B 喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？ B 喜欢喝什么饮料？

#### Simple Characters (p.104 Ex.9)
Introduce the four for the relay — 虫 chóng insect · 贝 bèi shell · 刀 dāo knife · 叉 chā fork. Stroke order modelled on the board once each (虫 6 strokes, 贝 4, 刀 2, 叉 3). Note 刀 and 叉 are what you eat 西餐 with — the pair that names today's contrast.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "饮料 — 是吃的还是喝的？" (喝的)
- Thumbs up/down: 汽水是饮料。/ 汉堡包是饮料。
- Mini whiteboards: "Write — I like drinking coke, but my mother says I should drink water." (我喜欢喝可乐，可是我妈妈说我应该喝水。)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Listen and tick, then sort — We Do (8 min)
*(4 min)* Textbook p.104 Ex.7, CD track 53 — listen and tick, six three-option items. Play twice, mark together.

*(4 min)* **Summary board:** all ten words from Lessons 1–2, characters + pictures, up on screen for the rest of the activity.

Pairs get the printed four-column grid — 中餐 / 西餐 / 快餐 / 饮料 — and must put **four items in every column**. Six come from Lesson 1, four from today; the remaining six they supply from Lesson 10 and their own knowledge. Then two pairs read out one column each and justify two placements: 比萨饼是快餐。

**Extension —** Every placement justified in a full sentence, and at least two of them contrasted: 热狗和汉堡包都是快餐，可是比萨饼也是西餐。 Then add a fifth row: one item that could go in two columns, and argue for both.

#### Activity 2: 设计一家快餐店 — You Do (7 min)
Textbook p.104 Ex.8 / Workbook Ex.20 (p.127). Pairs design a fast-food restaurant menu on the blank A4 template. It must carry:

- 店名 (restaurant name, ending 店)
- 四种吃的 — four foods, in Chinese
- 三种喝的 — three drinks, in Chinese
- 价钱 (prices in ¥)
- 地址、电话

Model from the book: 乐乐快餐店 — 鱼肉汉堡包 ¥9.80 · 热狗 ¥6.50 · 沙拉 ¥8.60 · 可乐 ¥5.00 · 橙汁 ¥4.50. Extra Words on screen for anyone who wants them: 薯条、苹果派、沙拉、咖啡.

**Extension —** The menu carries a one-line 广告 in Chinese underneath, using 还是 to address the customer directly: 你想吃汉堡包还是比萨饼？我们店都有！ Then the pair must *sell* it to another pair aloud — the buyers ask two 还是 questions before choosing, and the sellers answer without reading off their own menu.

#### 写一写 — Character copying (4 min)

Workbook Ex.1 (p.118). Quiet, individual, stroke order shown on the projector one character at a time. Four characters, three copies each:

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Strokes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 餐 | cān | food; meal | 16 |
| 喝 | hē | drink | 12 |
| 饮 | yǐn | drink | 7 |
| 料 | liào | material | 10 |

餐 is the hard one — model the top-left 卜-shape and the 夕 beside it slowly before anyone writes. Teacher circulates and checks grip and stroke direction rather than speed; this is the only quiet block in the lesson, so keep it quiet.

The remaining Text 1 characters — 快 狗 饼 — are structurally simple and already contain radicals the class knows (忄 犭 饣). Point that out, copy them once each if there's time, and let the relay below do the rest.

**Extension —** Copy each character once, then write a two-character word containing it and its English: 餐 → 中餐 Chinese food · 喝 → 喝水 drink water · 饮 → 饮料 beverage.

#### Game: Writing Relay (4 min)
Teams of four, one sheet of squared paper per team, one marker. Teacher calls a character — 虫 / 贝 / 刀 / 叉 — student 1 runs up, writes it with correct stroke order, passes the marker. Wrong stroke order = the next student must fix it before adding theirs. Most correct characters in four rounds wins.

- **Setup needed:** squared paper, four markers, board space
- **Harder version:** the teacher calls the *meaning in English* (insect, shell, knife, fork) instead of the sound, and the runner must write the character **and** its pinyin with the tone mark.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** choral read from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** traffic light on mini whiteboards — students write 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion and hold up together.
- **Exit ticket:** translate into Chinese — *"I like eating fast food and drinking coke."* (我喜欢吃快餐，喝可乐。)
- **Preview:** "Next lesson — 一日三餐. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, and how to say what you *normally* eat."
