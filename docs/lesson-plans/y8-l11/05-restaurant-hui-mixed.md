# 去饭店吃饭 Eating Out + 会

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐 | Lesson 5 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to talk about eating at a restaurant and to say that something is likely to happen, using 会.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and read 饭店 and 吃饭, and build other 店 words (快餐店、水果店)
- I can use 会 to say something is likely (这个周末我们会去饭店吃饭)
- I can read Text 2 aloud and answer questions about it in Chinese

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l5-restaurant-hui`), speakers for CD tracks 54, 55
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed restaurant role-play cards (customer / waiter), one set per pair
- Squared paper + markers for the character relay
- Printed half-slip for the exit ticket
- Workbook p.122 Ex.10 (the 田方 passage + 4 true/false) projected for the Review

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Cumulative — Lesson 1's food types, Lesson 2's drinks, Lesson 4's 还是 / 或者 split. This is the last lesson before the unit test, so the review reaches back over the whole sequence rather than just one lesson.

**How:** Two halves.

1. *(5 min)* **Reading comprehension** — Workbook Ex.10 (p.122), projected. Students read silently, then in pairs:

> 田方住在香港。她非常喜欢香港，因为香港有很多好吃的东西。在香港，除了中餐、西餐，她还可以吃到各种快餐。她最喜欢吃汉堡包，还经常吃比萨饼、热狗、炸鸡翅、炸薯条等。她每个星期六都去吃汉堡包。她妈妈说她应该少吃快餐，多吃蔬菜和水果。

   Four true/false on mini whiteboards, held up together:
   ① 田方不太喜欢住在香港。(F) ② 她除了爱吃汉堡包，还爱吃比萨饼。(T) ③ 她每天都吃汉堡包。(F — 每个星期六) ④ 她妈妈叫她多吃蔬菜和水果。(T)

   Almost every word is from Lessons 1–2 plus Lesson 10's 应该 and 可是. Don't pre-teach; let them meet 炸鸡翅 and 炸薯条 as unknowns and work around them — that's the skill the test's reading section actually asks for.

2. *(3 min)* Rapid whiteboard discrimination — teacher reads six sentence halves aloud, students write only 还是 or 或者 for each. Three questions, three statements, mixed order.

**Connection:** "You can now say what you eat at home, every meal, every day. Today you go out — 饭店 — and you learn to talk about something that hasn't happened yet."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Displayed and left up. Read aloud together. Ask in Chinese: 明天会下雨吗？ Let them guess at the meaning from context — 明天 and 下雨 are both known, so only 会 is new, and most of the class will get close before it's taught.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 饭店 | fàndiàn | restaurant | shopfront with a 饭店 sign |
| 1 | 吃饭 | chīfàn | to eat a meal | family around a table |
| 2 | 快餐店 | kuàicāndiàn | fast-food shop | takeaway counter |
| 2 | 水果店 | shuǐguǒdiàn | fruit shop | fruit stall with crates |

Cycle 2 is the **店 family** — both words are built from things they already know (快餐 + 店, 水果 + 店). Name that explicitly: 店 is a shop, and it goes on the end.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我们周末去**饭店**。 — We go to a restaurant at the weekend.
2. 我们全家人一起**吃饭**。 — Our whole family eats together.
3. 我们周末去**饭店吃饭**。 — both words, straight from Text 2.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我们 ______ 去饭店吃饭。
**Extension —** Two clauses contrasting home and out: 我们全家人一般在家吃饭，可是周末有时候去饭店吃饭。 Then add what you eat there, using 或者.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我喜欢去**快餐店**吃饭。 — I like eating at a fast-food shop.
2. **水果店**有很多水果。 — The fruit shop has a lot of fruit.
3. 我喜欢去**快餐店**，我妈妈喜欢去**水果店**。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** Build two more 店 words from words you already know, then use one in a sentence: ______ 店 有 ______ 。
**Extension —** Workbook Ex.13's full family — 玩具店、书店、花店 — write what each one sells, one sentence each, all three using 有: 花店有很多花。 Then rank them with a 可是 clause: 我最喜欢去书店，可是我妈妈最喜欢去花店。

**Recall slide:** 饭店 吃饭 快餐店 水果店 — characters only. Class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**会 + V** — "is likely to / will". Goes before the verb, like 应该 and 一般.

1. 今天**会**下雨。 — It's likely to rain today.
2. 今晚爸爸不**会**回家。 — Dad won't be home tonight. *(negative: 不会)*
3. 我午饭**会**吃快餐。 — I'll probably eat fast food for lunch.
4. 我们下个周末**会**去一家北京饭店吃饭。 — Text 2's own final sentence: 会 + a measure word 家 + a two-part verb phrase.

Think-aloud on #4: "一家北京饭店 — 家 is the measure word for a restaurant, the same 家 as 家人. And 会 sits right at the front of everything the family is going to do."

Warn on the clash: 会 also means "be able to" (我会游泳, Unit 3). Which meaning it carries comes from the time word — 明天会下雨 is likely, 我会游泳 is ability.

#### Text — Text 2 in full (p.105, CD track 54)
> 我们全家人都喜欢吃中餐。早餐我们一般吃粥或者面条。我们午饭吃炒面或者包子。
> 我们晚饭一般吃米饭、炒菜。我们家周末有时候去饭店吃饭。我们下个周末会去一家北京饭店吃饭。

Play track 54, students follow. Choral read, then pairs read it to each other. Three questions **answered in Chinese**: 他们全家人喜欢吃什么？ 他们早餐一般吃什么？ 他们下个周末会去哪儿？

Point out that every sentence in this text is something they've built themselves over the last four lessons — the only genuinely new word is 会, in the last sentence.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "会 before or after the verb?" (before)
- Mini whiteboards, from Workbook Ex.16: "Is it likely to rain this afternoon?" (今天下午会下雨吗？)
- Mini whiteboards: "We are likely to watch a movie this weekend." (我们这个周末会看电影。)
- Error correction: 我明天吃会快餐。 — fix it.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 这个周末 — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** all four of today's words plus the five dishes from Lesson 4, characters + pictures, on screen throughout.

Everyone writes a four-sentence weekend plan for their family. Every sentence uses **会**, and at least one uses **或者**:

> 这个周末我们全家人会去饭店吃饭。
> 我们会吃面条或者炒面。
> 我会喝可乐。
> 星期天我们会去水果店。

**Share:** three students read theirs aloud while the summary board stays up.

**Extension —** Six sentences instead of four, covering all three meals across two days, and two of them negative with 不会: 星期六我们不会在家吃饭。 Then a partner asks you two 还是 questions about the plan and you answer live, without your paper.

#### Activity 2: 饭店 Role-play Cards — You Do (9 min)
*(3 min first)* Textbook p.108 Ex.14, CD track 55 — listen and tick, six three-option items. Play twice, mark together.

*(6 min)* Pairs take a role-play card each — **Customer** / **Restaurant**. The restaurant side must ask at least two 还是 questions; the customer must answer with 或者 at least once and use 会 at least once.

> A：你们想吃中餐还是西餐？
> B：中餐。我们会吃面条或者炒面。
> A：你们想喝什么饮料？
> B：可乐，谢谢。

Swap cards and run it again.

**Extension —** The waiter's card says the restaurant has **run out** of whatever the customer asks for first, so the customer has to change the order in Chinese without restarting the dialogue: 对不起，今天没有炒面。／没关系，那我们吃面条或者包子。 No word bank on screen for these pairs; fluent students take the restaurant role, which carries the questioning.

#### Game: Writing Relay — 简单字 (8 min)
Workbook Ex.19 (p.126). Teams of four, one sheet of squared paper, one marker. Teacher calls the **English meaning**; the runner writes the character *and* its pinyin with tone mark, then passes the marker.

**Round 1 — simple characters (4 min).** Twelve in play: 虫 insect · 贝 shell · 刀 knife · 民 folk · 食 food · 果 fruit · 欠 owe · 石 stone · 东 east · 南 south · 西 west · 北 north.

**Round 2 — radicals (4 min).** Workbook Ex.6 (p.120). Same relay, but now the teacher calls a radical's meaning and the runner writes the radical only: 革 leather · 厂 cliff · 车 vehicle · 广 shelter · 贝 treasure · 礻 ritual · 犭 animal · 虫 insect.

贝 appears in both rounds with two different glosses — shell as a simple character, treasure as a radical. Name that when it comes up; it's the kind of thing the test's Part 3 turns on.

- **Setup needed:** squared paper, four markers, board space
- **Harder version:** in round 1 the runner must also write one **two-character word** containing that character (食 → 食物, 果 → 水果, 西 → 西餐, 北 → 北京); in round 2 they must write one character that *uses* that radical (犭 → 狗, 虫 → 虽, 饣 → 饭). Worth two points instead of one, and a wrong answer costs a point — so guessing is punished.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** student volunteer reads all three. Then widen it — the whole sequence's criteria go up in one list, all five lessons.
- **Whole-sequence recall:** all **26** words from Lesson 11 on one board, characters only. Class reads them down in four chunks: food types, drinks, meals, dishes. Anything that stalls gets named aloud — that's what to look at before the Unit 4 test.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs across the whole sequence, one show of hands per group of words rather than per criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** on a half-slip — translate: *"I will probably eat fast food for lunch."* (我午饭会吃快餐。) Collected at the door.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we start Lesson 12 — 外出就餐. Same restaurant, but this time you pay: prices, money, and how much things cost."
