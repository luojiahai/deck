# 一日三餐 Three Meals a Day

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐 | Lesson 3 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the three meals of the day and say what we normally eat at each one.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and read the three meals and 一般 (早餐、午饭、晚饭、一般)
- I can say what I normally eat, using 我 + meal + 一般 + 吃 (我早餐一般吃粥)
- I can talk about my whole family with 全家人 (我们全家人都喜欢吃中餐)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l3-three-meals-yiban`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed meal grid — 早餐 / 午餐 / 晚餐 rows × 吃 / 喝 columns, one per student
- Printed role-play slips for the lunch dialogue, one per pair
- A soft ball or beanbag for the 20 Questions game

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Everything from Lessons 1–2 — the six foods, the four drinks, and the 还是 question.

**How:** Bingo. Students draw a 3×3 grid and fill it with any nine of the ten words so far (中餐、西餐、快餐、热狗、汉堡包、比萨饼、喝、饮料、可乐、汽水) written in **characters**. Teacher calls each word in Chinese only — no English, no pinyin. First to a line calls 我赢了！Play on to two winners. Then one whiteboard question: 你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？— everyone writes their own answer, holds up.

**Connection:** "You can name the food and you can name the drink. What's missing is *when*. Today you get the three meals, so you can finally say what you eat at breakfast and not just what you like in general."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Displayed and left up. Read aloud together. Then draw a clock on the board and mark three times — 七点, 十二点, 六点半 — and ask in Chinese what happens at each. The gap is the vocabulary; the times are Year 7 revision they already own.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 早餐 | zǎocān | breakfast | sun rising over a bowl |
| 1 | 午饭 | wǔfàn | lunch | sun high, lunchbox |
| 2 | 晚饭 | wǎnfàn | dinner | moon over a dinner plate |
| 2 | 一般 | yìbān | normally | — (abstract) |
| 3 | 全 | quán | whole | family of four together |
| 3 | 粥 | zhōu | porridge; congee | bowl of congee with a spoon |

Note for the class: the book uses **早餐** in Text 2 and **早饭** in the workbook. They are the same meal — 餐 and 饭 swap freely here, which is why 午饭 and 晚饭 take 饭.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我**早餐**吃水果。 — I eat fruit for breakfast.
2. 我**午饭**吃快餐。 — I eat fast food for lunch.
3. 我**早餐**吃水果，**午饭**吃快餐。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我早餐吃 ______ ，午饭吃 ______ 。
**Extension —** Write about a family member, three clauses, ending with a contrast: 我爸爸早餐吃中餐，午饭吃西餐，可是他不喜欢吃快餐。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我**晚饭**吃中餐。 — I eat Chinese food for dinner.
2. 我**一般**喝水。 — I normally drink water.
3. 我**晚饭一般**吃中餐。 — both words. This is the pattern for the whole lesson.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我晚饭一般吃 ______ 。
**Extension —** Two clauses contrasting weekdays and the weekend: 我晚饭一般吃中餐，可是周末我们一般吃西餐。 Then say which you prefer and why, in one more sentence.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我们**全**家人都喜欢吃中餐。 — My whole family likes eating Chinese food. *(straight from Text 2)*
2. 我早餐一般吃**粥**。 — I normally eat congee for breakfast.
3. 我们**全**家人早餐一般都吃**粥**。 — both words, carrying 一般 from cycle 2.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我们全家人 ______ 喜欢吃 ______ 。
**Extension —** 我们全家人早餐一般吃粥，可是我不太喜欢吃粥，我一般吃水果。 Write it, then deliver it aloud to the class without looking at your board.

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 早餐 午饭 晚饭 一般 全 粥 — characters only. Class reads back.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**我 + [meal] + 一般 + 吃／喝 + X。** — 一般 goes **before** the verb, after the time word.

1. 我早餐一般吃粥。
2. 我午饭一般吃快餐，喝可乐。
3. 我们全家人晚饭一般吃中餐。
4. 我早餐一般吃水果，可是周末我一般吃汉堡包。 — the pattern twice, joined by 可是.

Think-aloud on word order: "时间先，一般第二，动词第三 — 我 / 早餐 / 一般 / 吃 / 粥。"

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "一般 means 'normally'. Where does it go — before 吃 or after 吃?" (before)
- Error correction on the board: 我吃一般早餐粥。 — fix it. (我早餐一般吃粥。)
- Mini whiteboards: "Write — My whole family normally eats congee for breakfast."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 一日三餐表 — We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters + pictures, up on screen throughout.

Textbook p.101 Ex.2. Every student fills the printed grid with their own real food:

| | 吃 | 喝 |
|---|---|---|
| 早餐 | | |
| 午餐 | | |
| 晚餐 | | |

Chinese only, using words from Lessons 1–3. Then report to a partner: 我早饭一般吃……，喝……。

**Share:** three students report their whole grid aloud while the summary board stays up.

**Extension —** Every row uses a frequency word from Workbook Ex.4 rather than plain 一般 — 有时候、经常、常常、差不多每天都. Then fill a second grid for a family member and present *that* one instead of your own, in the third person: 我妈妈早饭差不多每天都喝咖啡，很少吃东西。

#### Activity 2: 我们一起去吃午饭吧！ — You Do (9 min)
Textbook p.103 Ex.5. Pairs work from the printed slip and build the lunch dialogue, then perform it twice, swapping roles:

> A: 我们一起去吃午饭吧！
> B: 好啊。你想吃西餐还是中餐？
> A: 我想吃中餐。
> B: 你想喝什么饮料？
> A: 我想喝可乐。

Then the partner interview: ask about all three meals (你早饭一般吃什么？午饭呢？晚饭呢？), note the answers, and report **two** of them back to the class in the third person.

**Extension —** A three-turn version where the two of you *disagree* — B wants 西餐, A wants 中餐 — and you must resolve it in Chinese without either side switching to English. Use 可是 to object and 应该 to argue: 可是我们昨天吃了西餐，今天应该吃中餐。 Then report the outcome to the class in the third person: 小明想吃西餐，可是我们去吃中餐。

#### Game: 20 Questions — 猜一猜 (7 min)
One student holds a card with a food or drink from Lessons 1–3. The class asks yes/no or 还是 questions in Chinese only — 是饮料吗？是中餐还是西餐？早餐吃吗？ — up to twenty, then guesses. Beanbag passed to whoever asks next, so the questioning moves around the room.

- **Setup needed:** the character cards from Lesson 1 plus four new ones (饮料、可乐、汽水、粥)
- **Harder version:** the holder may only answer in **full sentences**, never 是 or 不是 — 这不是饮料，这是快餐。 And the fluent students take the card first, so they carry the answering load rather than the guessing.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** student volunteer reads all three aloud.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs on each criterion. Then a 30-second check of Workbook Ex.13's phrase family on screen — 早饭 / 午饭 / 晚饭 / 米饭 — class gives the English for each. The fourth one is new: park it, it arrives next lesson.
- **Exit ticket:** on a mini whiteboard — write one sentence using **一般**, about any meal. Held up together; teacher scans for 一般 sitting after the verb and corrects on the spot.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson — the dishes themselves. 面条、炒面、米饭、炒菜、包子 — and the second word for 'or'."
