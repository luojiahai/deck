# 中餐菜 Chinese Dishes + 或者

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Three Meals a Day 一日三餐 | Lesson 4 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name common Chinese dishes and to use 或者 to say "or" in a statement.

## Success Criteria
- I can say and read five Chinese dishes (面条、炒面、米饭、炒菜、包子)
- I can use 或者 in a statement (我午饭一般吃面条或者炒面)
- I can choose correctly between 还是 and 或者 (还是 asks, 或者 tells)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck `l4-dishes-huozhe`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed character cards for Slap the Character: 面条 炒面 米饭 炒菜 包子 (two A3 sets)
- Printed blank one-day menu sheet (早餐 / 午饭 / 晚饭), one per pair
- Workbook p.125 Ex.15 projected
- Workbook pp.123–124 Ex.12 stroke-order frames projected
- Exercise books or squared copying sheets for the 写一写 block (粥 或 者 面 条 炒)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 3's three meals and 一般, and the 还是 question from Lesson 1 — because today's new word collides with it.

**How:** Correct the Teacher. Four sentences go up on the board, three of them wrong:

1. 我吃一般早餐粥。 ✗ (一般 after the verb)
2. 我们全家人都喜欢吃中餐。 ✓
3. 我午饭一般吃快餐还是汉堡包。 ✗ (还是 in a statement — leave this one unresolved)
4. 我晚饭吃一般中餐。 ✗

Students find and fix each on mini whiteboards. Take #3 last and **don't fix it yet** — "We haven't got the word for this one. You'll have it in fifteen minutes."

**Connection:** "Sentence 3 is broken and you can hear it's broken, but you can't fix it yet — because 还是 only works in questions. Today you get the other 'or', the one that works in statements."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Displayed and left up. Read aloud together. Leave broken sentence #3 on a corner of the board all lesson; it gets fixed in the plenary.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 面条 | miàntiáo | noodles | bowl of noodles in broth |
| 1 | 炒面 | chǎomiàn | stir-fried noodles | wok with noodles |
| 2 | 米饭 | mǐfàn | cooked rice | bowl of white rice |
| 2 | 炒菜 | chǎocài | fried dishes | plate of stir-fried vegetables |
| 3 | 包子 | bāozi | steamed stuffed bun | two buns in a steamer basket |
| 3 | 或者 | huòzhě | or (in statements) | — (abstract) |

Radical clue worth naming: 炒 has 火 (fire) on the left — both 炒面 and 炒菜 are cooked in a hot wok. 米饭 has 米 (rice), a simple character they already know.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我午饭一般吃**面条**。 — I normally eat noodles for lunch.
2. 我很喜欢吃**炒面**。 — I really like eating stir-fried noodles.
3. **面条**和**炒面**我都喜欢吃。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ______ 喜欢吃面条。我 ______ 喜欢吃炒面。
**Extension —** 除了面条以外，我还喜欢吃炒面。 Then add a second sentence saying which of the two you prefer and when you eat it: 我晚饭一般吃面条，可是我最喜欢吃炒面。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我们晚饭一般吃**米饭**。 — We normally eat rice for dinner.
2. 我妈妈做**炒菜**。 — My mother makes stir-fried dishes.
3. 我们晚饭一般吃**米饭**、**炒菜**。 — both words, straight from Text 2.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我们晚饭一般吃 ______ 和 ______ 。
**Extension —** Report on the whole family, then break it with one exception: 我们全家人晚饭一般吃米饭、炒菜，可是我爸爸不太喜欢吃炒菜。 Deliver it standing, no notes.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我早餐一般吃**包子**。 — I normally eat steamed buns for breakfast.
2. 我午饭一般吃面条**或者**炒面。 — I normally eat noodles or stir-fried noodles for lunch.
3. 我早餐一般吃粥**或者包子**。 — both words, and it's Text 2's own sentence shape.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我早餐一般吃 ______ 或者 ______ 。
**Extension —** Write three 或者 sentences, one for each meal, then turn **one** of them into a 还是 question and write both side by side: 我早餐一般吃粥或者包子。／ 你早餐吃粥还是包子？ That pair is the thing the test asks for.

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 面条 炒面 米饭 炒菜 包子 或者 — characters only. Class reads back.

#### Extra Words — recognition only (2 min)

Textbook p.106. Six more Chinese dishes, on screen with pictures, characters only — read them once, name them once, move on. **These are for recognition, not production; they are not in the Unit 4 test.**

春卷 chūnjuǎn · 馄饨 húntun · 饺子 jiǎozi · 小笼包 xiǎolóngbāo · 生煎包 shēngjiānbāo · 豆浆 dòujiāng

Worth one line: 小笼包 and 生煎包 both end in 包 — the same 包 as today's 包子.

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**A 或者 B** (statement) vs **A 还是 B** (question). Two columns on one slide:

| 或者 — 说 (telling) | 还是 — 问 (asking) |
|---|---|
| 我午饭吃面条或者炒面。 | 你午饭吃面条还是炒面？ |
| 苹果或者香蕉，都可以。 | 你想吃苹果还是香蕉？ |
| 他晚上一般看书，或者看电视。 | 他晚上看书还是看电视？ |

4. The hardest one — both in a single exchange: 你早餐一般吃粥还是包子？ — 粥或者包子，都可以。

Think-aloud: "句号用或者，问号用还是。 Look at the punctuation at the end of the sentence and it tells you which word you needed."

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "还是 or 或者 — 我们去打球____看电影？" (还是, it's a question)
- "还是 or 或者 — 今天来____明天来，都可以。" (或者)
- Mini whiteboards: "Write — I normally eat rice or noodles for dinner."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 一天的菜单 — We Do (7 min)
**Summary board:** all six words, characters + pictures, up on screen throughout.

Pairs get the blank one-day menu sheet with three rows — 早餐 / 午饭 / 晚饭. Write **one sentence per row**, and every sentence must use **或者**. Between the three rows you must use all five dishes plus 粥 from last lesson — so nothing can be repeated.

Model row: 早餐：我们一般吃粥或者包子。

**Share:** two pairs read their whole menu aloud while the summary board stays up.

**Extension —** Add a fourth row — 周末 — and write it as a *contrast* across two clauses: 周末我们一般不吃米饭，我们吃炒面或者面条，可是我妈妈一般吃粥。 Then have a second pair ask you three 还是 questions about your menu, which you answer without looking at the sheet.

#### Activity 2: 还是 还是 或者？ — You Do (8 min)
*(5 min)* Workbook Ex.15 (p.125), projected, answered on mini whiteboards. Six gaps, each filled with 还是 or 或者:

1. 我们坐地铁 ____ 坐出租车去？
2. 晚饭我们吃炒饭 ____ 炒面。
3. 苹果 ____ 香蕉，都可以。
4. 你想去美国 ____ 英国上大学？
5. 今天来 ____ 明天来，都可以。
6. 他晚上一般看书，____ 看电视。

Whole class holds up per item; mark together. This is the exact Unit 4 Test format.

*(3 min)* Workbook Ex.5 (p.120) — match a verb from the box (做 看 画 弹 唱 吃 听 拉 打 说 喝 上) to each object: ____油画儿 · ____作业 · ____电视 · ____钢琴 · ____小提琴 · ____电话 · ____汉语 · ____音乐 · ____美术课 · ____儿歌 · ____中餐 · ____可乐. Rapid-fire, called out.

**Extension —** For each of the six Ex.15 items, write the *other* version underneath — turn every statement into a question and every question into a statement, swapping the conjunction: 我们坐地铁或者坐出租车去，都可以。 Six rewrites, then read two aloud.

#### 写一写 — Character copying (4 min)

Workbook Ex.12 (pp.123–124). Quiet, individual, stroke order projected one character at a time. Six characters, three copies each:

| 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Strokes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 粥 | zhōu | porridge; congee | 12 |
| 或 | huò | or | 8 |
| 者 | zhě | person or thing | 8 |
| 面 | miàn | flour; measure word | 9 |
| 条 | tiáo | stripe; measure word | 7 |
| 炒 | chǎo | stir-fry | 8 |

粥 is the one to model slowly — it's 弓 + 米 + 弓, a rice-between-bows shape, and students who see that stop trying to draw it as one blob. 炒 is 火 + 少: fire on the left, which is the clue to what it means.

The remaining Text 2 characters — 全 般 店 — copy once each if time allows; 店 comes back next lesson in 饭店, 快餐店, 水果店.

**Extension —** After copying, write each character inside a two-character word from this lesson and give the English: 炒 → 炒面 stir-fried noodles · 面 → 面条 noodles · 或 → 或者 or.

#### Game: 接龙 — Category Chain (4 min)
Textbook p.109 Ex.16. Whole class standing. Teacher names a category and one item — 中餐：炒面 — and students around the room add one item each. Repeating an item, or failing to add one within three seconds, and you sit down. Run two rounds: 中餐 · 饮料.

- **Setup needed:** nothing
- **Harder version:** the addition must be a **full sentence using 或者** that links your item to the previous student's — 他说炒面，我早餐一般吃包子或者粥。 Fluent students start each round, so they set the bar and can't coast on a one-word answer.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** choral read from the screen.
- **Fix the broken sentence:** go back to the board — 我午饭一般吃快餐还是汉堡包。 Class calls the fix (或者). Name it: "That's what you couldn't do at the start of the lesson."
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs on each criterion, with a second show of hands specifically on 还是 vs 或者.
- **Exit ticket:** on a half-slip — write **two** sentences about the same two dishes: one statement with 或者, one question with 还是.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson — 去饭店吃饭. Eating out, and how to say something is *likely* to happen."
