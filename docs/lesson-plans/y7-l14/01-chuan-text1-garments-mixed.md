# Clothing (1): 穿 and the First Three Garments

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Clothing 穿着 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l1-chuan-text1`](../../../index/designs/y7-l14/l1-chuan-text1/) — 19 slides (also exported as `l1-chuan-text1.pdf`)

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what people like to wear.

## Success Criteria
- I can name three items of clothing in Chinese (衬衫、牛仔裤、裙子)
- I can use 穿 to say what someone wears (他喜欢穿……)
- I can write 穿 and 衬 with the correct stroke order, and I know why clothing characters take 衤

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l1-chuan-text1`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens, one per student
- Printed Slap-the-Board cards: 衬衫 · 牛仔裤 · 裙子 · 穿 · plus 8–10 colour words from Lesson 13 (10–15 cards total)
- Workbook p.146 (Ex.1), pp.147–148 (Ex.2), p.148 (Ex.4)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** All eleven colours from Lesson 13, and the 颜色 + 的 + noun frame — 黑色的火车, 红色的出租车.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire. Teacher shows the colour swatch, class chorally says the Chinese. Two rounds: first with pinyin on screen, second without. Then four quick whiteboard items — teacher says an English phrase ("the yellow school bus", "the blue train"), students write the Chinese and hold it up. Watch for the missing 的.

**Connection:** "Last lesson every 的 was followed by a vehicle. Today the same frame, but what comes after 的 is clothes — and we get a new verb to go with them."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up all lesson. Read the Learning Intention aloud, then have a student read each criterion. Point at the third one: "By the end you'll know why 衬 and 衫 both have that little 衤 on the left."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 穿 | chuān | wear | Figure pulling a shirt over their head (SVG) |
| 1 | 衬衫 | chènshān | shirt | Button-up shirt, collar and buttons (SVG) |
| 2 | 牛仔裤 | niúzǎikù | jeans | Jeans with pockets and a belt loop (SVG) |
| 2 | 裙子 | qúnzi | skirt | A-line skirt (SVG) |

On the 衬衫 slide only, add the character clue: **衤 = clothing**. Point at it in 衬 and 衫 and say it will come back three more times this lesson. Do not put this on any other slide.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 这是**衬衫**。 — This is a shirt.
2. 我爸爸**穿**白色的**衬衫**。 — My dad wears a white shirt.
3. 我喜欢**穿**蓝色的**衬衫**。 — I like wearing a blue shirt.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢穿 ________ 色的衬衫。
**Extension —** 用两个人写一句：我爸爸喜欢穿白色的衬衫，我妈妈喜欢穿红色的衬衫。Then add 也 or 不: 我也喜欢穿白色的衬衫。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我哥哥喜欢穿**牛仔裤**。 — My older brother likes wearing jeans.
2. 我姐姐喜欢穿**裙子**。 — My older sister likes wearing skirts.
3. 我妈妈喜欢穿蓝色的**牛仔裤**和粉红色的**裙子**。 — My mum likes wearing blue jeans and a pink skirt.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我 ________ 喜欢穿 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写三个人，一句话：我爸爸喜欢穿牛仔裤，我妈妈喜欢穿裙子，我不喜欢穿裙子。No word bank — cover the board.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**这是我 + 家人。他/她喜欢穿 + 衣服。**

Think aloud: "To say 'This is my dad, he likes wearing a shirt' — 这是我爸爸 · 他喜欢 · 穿 · 衬衫."

1. 这是我爸爸。他喜欢穿衬衫。
2. 这是我妈妈。她喜欢穿裙子。
3. 我哥哥喜欢穿黑色的牛仔裤。
4. 我姐姐喜欢穿白色的衬衫和蓝色的牛仔裤，她不喜欢穿裙子。 ← two clauses, a colour on each garment, and a negative. This is the one the fluent students copy down.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "What does 穿 mean? Thumbs up if you know."
- "How do I say 'a red skirt'? Write it." (checking the 的 survives)
- "什么不对？ 我喜欢裙子穿。" — word order, verb before object.

#### Text 1 (CD T65, textbook p.104)
> 这是我爸爸。他喜欢穿衬衫和牛仔裤。
> 这是我妈妈。她喜欢穿裙子。

Play T65, students follow in the book. Choral read twice. Three comprehension questions, English answers accepted: Who likes jeans? What does mum like to wear? Does dad wear a skirt? Ask one fluent student to answer question 2 in Chinese.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Family clothing profile — We Do (8 min) | 20–28
Summary board stays up: all four new words, characters and pictures only, no pinyin.

Students write four sentences about four people in their family (or made-up family), each sentence naming a different garment and a colour. Between them the four sentences must use **穿, 衬衫, 牛仔裤 and 裙子** — every word from today.

Model the first one on the board together: 我爸爸喜欢穿灰色的衬衫。Then 4 minutes writing, teacher circulating. Take three aloud with the summary board still on screen.

**Extension —** Write it as one connected paragraph rather than four separate sentences, joining with 和 and 也, and include one person who **doesn't** like something: 我姐姐喜欢穿牛仔裤，她也喜欢穿衬衫，她不喜欢穿裙子。Then read it to the class without looking at the page.

#### Activity 2: Stroke order and the 衤 radical — You Do (8 min) | 28–36
The three radicals go up on their own slide first — **冂 border (周) · 牛 cow (物) · 贝 shell (贵)** — because Unit Test Parts 2 and 3 ask for radical → meaning and radical → character, and the workbook alone never shows them with their example characters. Then workbook p.146 Ex.1 (copy the radicals) and pp.147–148 Ex.2 (copy 穿 衬 衫 牛 仔 裤 裙 with stroke order).

Before they start, one board moment: write 衤 and 礻 side by side. 礻 was Lesson 13's radical (视). 衤 has the extra stroke and means clothing. Every garment character today uses 衤. Students circle the 衤 in all four characters in their workbook before copying.

Then workbook p.148 Ex.4 — written answers to 你喜欢穿衬衫吗？and 你喜欢穿牛仔裤吗？

**Extension —** Instead of answering Ex.4 with 喜欢／不喜欢, write a full sentence with a colour and a reason-free second clause: 我喜欢穿衬衫，我不喜欢穿裙子。Then write two more clothing characters that take 衤 without looking them up — 袜, 裤 (they've met 裤 today).

#### Game: Slap the Board (7 min) | 36–43
**(Textbook p.107 Ex.5)**

Class into four groups. 10–15 printed cards on the board — today's four words plus colour words from Lesson 13. One member from each group at the board. Teacher calls a phrase in Chinese; first hand on the right card scores a point. Rotate so everyone has a turn.

- **Setup needed:** printed cards, board magnets or blu-tack.
- **Harder version:** Teacher calls a **full sentence** (我姐姐喜欢穿粉红色的裙子) and the player must slap **two** cards in the right order — colour first, then garment. Fluent students take this round while the rest watch, then it opens back up.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each one aloud from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Translate into Chinese — *My older sister likes wearing blue jeans.*
- **Preview:** "下一课 — colours and clothes together, and two kinds of trousers: 长裤 and 短裤."
