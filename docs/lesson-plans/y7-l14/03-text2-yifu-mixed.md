# Clothing (3): 你喜欢穿什么衣服？— Text 2

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Clothing 穿着 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask someone what clothes they like to wear, and to answer with a list.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask 你喜欢穿什么衣服？
- I can answer with several garments, using 、between them and 和 before the last one
- I can name 衣服, 校服, 毛衣 and 外套

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** All six garments so far — 衬衫 牛仔裤 裙子 长裤 短裤 汗衫 — and 颜色 + 的 + 衣服.

**How:** **Workbook p.152 Ex.11** — circle the clothing words in the 18-cell grid. The grid mixes garments with distractors from earlier units (年级, 出租车, 睡觉, 起床, 上课, 地铁, 放学), so it's a recall test of the whole year, not just this lesson. 3 minutes solo, then answers by cold call. Two of the words in that grid (毛衣, 外套) are today's — leave them uncircled and say so: "those two you'll be able to circle by the end of the lesson."

Then 2 minutes of pair speaking: A names a colour, B names a garment, together they say the phrase. Ten rounds, fast.

**Connection:** "You can describe one item of clothing. Today you learn to answer with everything you're wearing at once."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Write the three 顿号 marks on the board as you read criterion 2 — 、、、 — and say they are a comma that only lists, and they are not a full stop.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 衣服 | yīfu | clothing | Small rail of mixed garments (SVG) |
| 1 | 校服 | xiàofú | school uniform | Shirt, tie and dark trousers together (SVG) |
| 2 | 毛衣 | máoyī | sweater | Knitted jumper with ribbed cuffs (SVG) |
| 2 | 外套 | wàitào | coat | Buttoned coat (SVG) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 你喜欢穿什么**衣服**？ — What clothes do you like to wear?
2. 我上学穿**校服**。 — I wear school uniform to school.
3. 我不喜欢**校服**，我喜欢穿我的**衣服**。 — I don't like the uniform, I like wearing my own clothes.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢穿 ________ 衣服。
**Extension —** Answer the question in two clauses, one positive one negative: 我喜欢穿汗衫和牛仔裤，我不喜欢穿校服。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我妈妈喜欢穿红色的**毛衣**。 — My mum likes wearing a red sweater.
2. 我爸爸喜欢穿黑色的**外套**。 — My dad likes wearing a black coat.
3. 我穿**毛衣**和**外套**。 — I'm wearing a sweater and a coat.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我穿 ________ 色的毛衣和 ________ 色的外套。
**Extension —** Three garments in one sentence with 、and 和, and a second clause about someone else: 我穿汗衫、毛衣和外套，我弟弟穿短裤。

#### Vocabulary summary board
All ten garments now known, characters and pictures only:
**衬衫 · 牛仔裤 · 裙子 · 长裤 · 短裤 · 汗衫 · 衣服 · 校服 · 毛衣 · 外套**
Stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**你喜欢穿什么衣服？→ 我喜欢穿 A 和 B。**
**Listing three or more: A、B、C 和 D** — 顿号 between the middle items, 和 only before the last.

1. 你喜欢穿什么衣服？
2. 我喜欢穿汗衫和短裤。
3. 我穿衬衫、毛衣、外套和长裤。
4. 我喜欢穿汗衫和短裤，我不喜欢穿毛衣和外套。 ← two clauses, four garments, one negative.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "衣服 or 校服 — which one means *school* uniform? Which character tells you?" (校, the same 校 as 校车 and 学校)
- "I say four things. How many 和 do I need?" (one)
- "什么不对？ 我穿衬衫和毛衣和外套和长裤。"

#### Text 2 (CD T67, textbook p.108)
> A: 你喜欢穿什么衣服？
> B: 我喜欢穿汗衫和短裤。
> A: 你上学穿校服吗？
> B: 穿。我穿衬衫、毛衣、外套和长裤。

Play T67, students follow. Choral read. Then read it in two halves, boys/girls, and swap. Comprehension: What does the girl like wearing? Does the boy wear uniform? Name all four things in his uniform.

Flag but do not drill the last answer — **穿。** not 是。 That's next lesson's whole job.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: What's in your wardrobe — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
Summary board up. Each student writes **one sentence** listing at least four garments with 、and 和, each with a colour: 我有白色的衬衫、蓝色的牛仔裤、灰色的毛衣和黑色的外套。

Model one on the board first, counting the punctuation aloud as you write it. Then 4 minutes, teacher circulating specifically to check the 顿号 and that 和 appears exactly once. Three read aloud.

**Extension —** Write it about someone else and turn it into a guessing game: list four garments a person in this room owns or wears, ending 他是谁？The class guesses. No summary board — they turn away from the screen.

#### Activity 2: Text 2 dialogue, then the characters — You Do (9 min) | 27–36
**Part A, 4 min.** Pairs perform Text 2 from the book, then again with the book closed and only the summary board for help, swapping roles. Two pairs perform to the class.

**Part B, 5 min.** Workbook **pp.151–152 Ex.9** — copy 衣 服 毛 外 套 with stroke order. Then **p.149 Ex.5**, matching radicals to characters: this is where 衤 pays off, because 衬 and 衫 both sit under it in the box. Then **p.153 Ex.13** item 2 — make a sentence from 喜欢 / 外套.

The second radical set gets its own slide before this activity — **冫 ice (冷) · 户 household (房) · 忄 feeling (忙)** — then copied into workbook p.146 Ex.1 alongside the three from Lesson 1. That completes all six, and both Unit Test radical questions draw from them plus Lesson 13's 疒 火 爫 弓 力 礻.

**Extension —** For Ex.13, write a **question** rather than a statement (你喜欢穿外套吗？), and add the answer in the verb-repeating form (穿。/ 不穿。) — a head start on next lesson.

#### Game: Sentence Jumble (7 min) | 36–43
Printed envelopes, one per pair. Each envelope holds the four sentences of Text 2 cut into individual words, shuffled together. Pairs race to rebuild all four. First three pairs finished read one sentence aloud to check.

The 、and 和 cards are separate pieces, so word order **and** punctuation both have to be right.

- **Setup needed:** printed and cut word cards, one envelope per pair.
- **Harder version:** One envelope has an extra sentence in it that was never in Text 2 (我不喜欢穿校服，我喜欢穿牛仔裤。) — the pair has to build all five and work out which one is the intruder.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each aloud; class thumbs on each.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Answer in Chinese — 你喜欢穿什么衣服？Use at least two garments and one 和.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — 校服. And the strangest answer in Chinese: when someone asks 你穿校服吗？you don't say 是, you say 穿。"
