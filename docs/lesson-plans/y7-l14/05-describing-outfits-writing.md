# Clothing (5): Writing What People Wear

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Clothing 穿着 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l5-describing-outfits`](../../../index/designs/y7-l14/l5-describing-outfits/) — 17 slides (also exported as `l5-describing-outfits.pdf`)

## Learning Intention
We are learning to write a description of what someone is wearing.

## Success Criteria
- I can write 他／她穿 + colour + 的 + garment 和 colour + 的 + garment
- I can find a wrong character in a clothing sentence and write the right one
- I can name 皮鞋, 运动鞋, 西装 and 领带

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l5-describing-outfits`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Exercise books (this lesson's writing goes in books, not only on whiteboards)
- Audio: CD **T69** (textbook p.111 Ex.9)
- Workbook p.153 (Ex.12), p.154 (Ex.14), p.156 (Ex.18, Ex.19)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** All ten garments and the four accessories, plus the 穿 / 是 answer from last lesson.

**How:** **Workbook p.153 Ex.12, 4 min** — match nine garment pictures to 校服 / 短裤 / 毛衣 / 衬衫 / 裙子 / 长裤 / 汗衫 / 外套 / 牛仔裤. Solo, then answers by cold call.

**3 min oral:** teacher fires 你上学穿校服吗？你喜欢穿裙子吗？你喜欢穿短裤吗？at random students, no hands up. Listening only for the verb-repeat answer.

**Connection:** "You've said it and heard it. Today you write it — which is where the 的 goes missing and 牛 turns into 午."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Write 牛仔裤 and 午仔裤 side by side on the board as you read criterion 2, and ask the class which is right. Leave both up.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 皮鞋 | píxié | leather shoes | Polished lace-up shoe (SVG) |
| 1 | 运动鞋 | yùndòngxié | sneakers | Trainer with a striped sole (SVG) |
| 2 | 西装 | xīzhuāng | suit | Suit jacket and trousers (SVG) |
| 2 | 领带 | lǐngdài | tie | Knotted tie (SVG) |

> Textbook p.110 **Extra Words** again — recognition level, not on the Unit Test list. But unlike the hats and gloves, all four of these take **穿**: 穿皮鞋, 穿西装. Only 领带 doesn't (the correct verb is 打, not taught) — use 有 or 的 with it.
>
> **鞋** carries the 革 (leather) radical, which is Lesson 15's radical set. Mention it on the 皮鞋 slide in one line; don't teach it yet.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我爸爸穿黑色的**皮鞋**上班。 — My dad wears black leather shoes to work.
2. 我每天穿**运动鞋**上学。 — I wear sneakers to school every day.
3. 我有**皮鞋**和**运动鞋**，我喜欢穿**运动鞋**。 — I have leather shoes and sneakers; I like wearing sneakers.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我每天穿 ________ 色的 ________ 上学。
**Extension —** Two people, two verbs from earlier units: 我爸爸开车上班，他穿黑色的皮鞋；我坐校车上学，我穿白色的运动鞋。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我爸爸上班穿**西装**。 — My dad wears a suit to work.
2. 他的**领带**是红色的。 — His tie is red.
3. 我哥哥穿黑色的**西装**和蓝色的**领带**。 — My older brother is wearing a black suit and a blue tie.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 穿 ________ 色的西装。
**Extension —** Describe a whole outfit for someone going to work — suit, shirt, tie, shoes, four colours, one sentence, 、and 和 correctly placed.

#### Vocabulary summary board
Everything from the whole lesson so far, characters and pictures only, no pinyin:
**衬衫 · 牛仔裤 · 裙子 · 长裤 · 短裤 · 汗衫 · 衣服 · 校服 · 毛衣 · 外套 · 帽子 · 手套 · 围巾 · 袜子 · 皮鞋 · 运动鞋 · 西装 · 领带**

This is a recall board, not a teaching slide — it stays up for the whole of Flexible Practice as the students' reference while they write.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**他／她穿 + 颜色的衣服 + 和 + 颜色的衣服。**

1. 他穿白色的汗衫和橙色的短裤。 *(textbook p.109 Ex.6 example)*
2. 她穿粉红色的衬衫和紫色的裙子。
3. 京京穿绿色的外套和黑色的长裤。
4. 王小明今天穿橙色的衬衫、黑色的长裤和白色的运动鞋，他不穿校服。 ← three garments, 顿号 and 和, plus a negative second clause.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Write 'brown leather shoes'." (棕色的皮鞋)
- "什么不对？ 他穿白色汗衫。" (missing 的)
- "什么不对？ 他穿午仔裤。" (牛, not 午)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Dictation and check — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
**(Textbook p.111 Ex.9, CD T69)**

Six people, six outfits. Play each line **twice**. Students write what they hear in Chinese in their exercise books — full sentence, not just a tick. Then project the six pictures and students tick or cross their own sentence against the picture.

Script for the teacher:
> 1) 大生穿白汗衫，蓝短裤。 2) 小文穿粉红色的衬衫，紫色的裙子。 3) 小月穿红色的毛衣，棕色的裙子。 4) 京京穿绿色的外套，黑色的长裤。 5) 王星穿灰色的汗衫，黑色的牛仔裤。 6) 王小明穿橙色的衬衫，黑色的长裤。

Note item 1 aloud: the recording says 白汗衫, not 白色的汗衫. Spoken Chinese drops it; written work in this course keeps it.

**Extension —** Write each sentence **and** then rewrite the two that were false so they match the picture — which means producing a corrected sentence, not just marking a cross.

#### Activity 2: Written descriptions and mistake-hunting — You Do (10 min) | 27–37
Three parts, in books.

**Part A, 4 min — textbook p.109 Ex.6.** Write a full sentence for each of the four people pictured. Model the first together.

**Part B, 3 min — textbook p.110 Ex.8.** Fifteen items, colour + 的 + garment. Written, not spoken this time. Students do as many as they can in three minutes; nobody is expected to finish fifteen. Take the last three aloud as answers.

**Part C, 3 min — workbook p.156 Ex.19**, spot the mistake in five sentences and write the correct character. Item 1 is given as an example (午仔裤 → 牛). This is Unit Test Part 8 in miniature.

**Extension —** For Part A, write **two** sentences per person: what they're wearing, and one thing they're *not* wearing — 他穿白色的汗衫和橙色的短裤，他不穿外套。For Part C, after correcting the five, write one new sentence of your own containing a deliberate character error, swap with a partner and have them find it.

#### Game: Word-search race (6 min) | 37–43
**(Workbook p.156 Ex.18)**

The 6×6 character grid holds twelve phrases running across and down — 衬衫, 汗衫, 校服, 地铁, 走路, 公共汽车, 粉红色, 长裙, 短裤, 上学, 中午, 独生子 among them. Teams of three race to circle and write down all twelve. First team with twelve correct wins; second place gets a point for most-correct if nobody finishes.

- **Setup needed:** the workbook page, or project the grid and use whiteboards.
- **Harder version:** The winning team must then say each of their twelve phrases aloud in Chinese and give the English — no reading off the page for the English.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud. Point back at 牛仔裤 / 午仔裤 on the board from the start of the lesson.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Look at the person on the screen. Write one sentence describing what they're wearing, with two garments and two colours.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — the last one. You'll read about 黄铁牛, interview each other about everything we've done this year, and write a profile of a stranger at a party."
