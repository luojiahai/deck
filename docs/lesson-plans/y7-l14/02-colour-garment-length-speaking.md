# Clothing (2): Colour and Length — 长裤, 短裤, 汗衫

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Clothing 穿着 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l2-colour-garment`](../../../index/designs/y7-l14/l2-colour-garment/) — 17 slides (also exported as `l2-colour-garment.pdf`)

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe clothing by its colour and its length.

## Success Criteria
- I can put any colour in front of any garment using 的 (天蓝色的衬衫)
- I can tell 长裤 and 短裤 apart, and I know 长 means long and 短 means short
- I can hear a Chinese word and write it in pinyin with the right tone mark

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l2-colour-garment`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- 1:1 devices for Quizlet Live
- Audio: CD **T66** (textbook p.105 Ex.2)
- Coloured pencils for the workbook colouring
- Workbook p.148 (Ex.3), p.149 (Ex.7), p.155 (Ex.17)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's four words (穿 衬衫 牛仔裤 裙子) and the Lesson 13 colour-mixing sums.

**How:** Two parts.
1. **Pair dialogue, 3 min.** A: 你喜欢穿什么颜色的衬衫？B answers. Swap. Then two pairs perform.
2. **Colour mixing, 3 min.** Back to Lesson 13 p.102: 红色 + 黄色 = ？ 白色 + 黑色 = ？ Then the one that matters today — **白色 + 蓝色 = 天蓝色** (tiānlánsè, sky blue). Put it on the board; it is the worked example on today's textbook page.

**Connection:** "You can already name a colour and you can already name a shirt. Today we bolt them together — and add the two words that tell trousers apart."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Read aloud, then ask: "Which of these three can you already half-do?" (the first — they met 的 with vehicles). Name that explicitly; today is about speed and accuracy, not a new idea.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 长裤 | chángkù | trousers | Full-length trousers (SVG) |
| 1 | 短裤 | duǎnkù | shorts | Above-the-knee shorts (SVG) |
| 2 | 汗衫 | hànshān | T-shirt | Short-sleeved round-neck tee (SVG) |
| 2 | 天蓝色 | tiānlánsè | sky blue | Sky-blue swatch (SVG) |

On the cycle 1 slide, under the two words, one line: **长 cháng = long · 短 duǎn = short.** Same 裤 both times; only the first character changes. Nothing else on the slide.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我爸爸喜欢穿**长裤**。 — My dad likes wearing trousers.
2. 我弟弟喜欢穿**短裤**。 — My younger brother likes wearing shorts.
3. 我喜欢穿黑色的**长裤**，不喜欢穿**短裤**。 — I like wearing black trousers, I don't like wearing shorts.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢穿 ________ 色的 ________ 。 (frame on screen: 颜色 + 的 + 裤子)
**Extension —** 两个人，一句话，用「也」或者「不」：我哥哥喜欢穿短裤，我也喜欢穿短裤，我姐姐不喜欢。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 他穿白色的**汗衫**。 — He's wearing a white T-shirt.
2. 我喜欢**天蓝色**。 — I like sky blue.
3. 我妹妹穿**天蓝色**的**汗衫**和黑色的短裤。 — My little sister is wearing a sky-blue T-shirt and black shorts.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 穿 ________ 色的汗衫。
**Extension —** Describe someone actually in the room, using two garments and two different colours, without naming them — the class guesses who.

#### Vocabulary summary board
All six garments met so far, characters and pictures only, no pinyin, no English:
**衬衫 · 牛仔裤 · 裙子 · 长裤 · 短裤 · 汗衫**
This board stays on screen through Activity 1 and Activity 3.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**颜色 + 的 + 衣服**

1. 红色的衬衫
2. 天蓝色的衬衫
3. 灰色的长裤
4. 我姐姐今天穿天蓝色的衬衫和灰色的长裤。 ← the full sentence, two colours, two garments, joined with 和.

Say the rule out loud and write it: the 的 is **not optional**. ✗红色衬衫 ✓红色的衬衫.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "长裤 or 短裤?" — teacher mimes, class calls out. Six fast rounds.
- "What's wrong with 蓝色牛仔裤?" — the missing 的.
- "Write 'orange shorts'." (橙色的短裤)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Rapid-fire naming — We Do (7 min) | 20–27
**(Textbook p.105 Ex.1 and p.106 Ex.3)**

Project the twelve pictures across both exercises. Round 1: teacher points, class answers chorally — 天蓝色的衬衫, 橙色的长裤, 蓝色的牛仔裤… Round 2: cold call, no hands up, faster. Round 3: teacher points at a picture and says only the colour; a student supplies the garment, the next student makes it a full sentence with 穿.

Textbook p.106's Extra Words box (校服 汗衫 长裤 短裤 毛衣 外套) is on that page — tell the class that three of those six are today's and the other three come next lesson, so they don't try to learn all six now.

**Extension —** Take the question-asker's role. The fluent students run round 3 themselves: they point and ask 这是什么颜色的衣服？and must correct a wrong answer in Chinese (不对，是橙色的，不是红色的).

#### Activity 2: Tone-mark sprint — You Do (4 min) | 27–31
**(Textbook p.105 Ex.2, CD T66)**

Nine words, whiteboards. Play each twice, students write the pinyin with the tone mark, hold up. Answers: gōngniú · kùnnan · duǎnkù · chuántǒng · tiáozi · tàozhuāng · duànliàn · yǒuqíng · lǜsè.

Point out two of them are today's language hiding in the list — **duǎnkù** and **lǜsè**.

**Extension —** Write the **characters** as well as the pinyin for the three they can: 短裤, 绿色, 牛.

#### Activity 3: Describe and draw — You Do (7 min) | 31–38
Pairs, back to back or with a book between them. A describes an outfit in Chinese — two garments, two colours (我穿天蓝色的汗衫和黑色的短裤). B draws it and colours it in. Then swap. Compare drawings at the end: if the drawing is wrong, work out together whether it was the saying or the hearing.

Then straight into the workbook while the language is warm: **p.148 Ex.3** and **p.155 Ex.17** — colour the pictures to match the Chinese (蓝色的牛仔裤, 紫色的短裤, 橙色的汗衫, 粉红色的毛衣, 棕色的外套, 灰色的校服). Students colour only the four they can already read; the other two get done next lesson.

**Extension —** B may not ask A to repeat, and A must describe **three** garments in one sentence with 、and 和: 我穿天蓝色的汗衫、黑色的短裤和白色的运动鞋. Then B describes back what they drew, in Chinese, as a check.

#### Game: Quizlet Live (5 min) | 38–43
Set built from all six garments plus the eleven colours. Devices out, teams assigned by the app, picture ↔ 汉字 matching against the clock. Two rounds if time.

- **Setup needed:** Quizlet set prepared before class, 1:1 devices.
- **Harder version:** Run the second round in **汉字 ↔ pinyin** mode with the pictures switched off, so there's no visual shortcut.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read from the screen.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic light on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Write in Chinese — *sky-blue shorts* — and then one full sentence using it.
- **Preview:** "下一课 — Text 2: 你喜欢穿什么衣服？and three more words, including the one for school uniform."
