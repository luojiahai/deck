# Clothing (4): 校服 — 你上学穿校服吗？

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Clothing 穿着 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l4-school-uniform`](../../../index/designs/y7-l14/l4-school-uniform/) — 18 slides (also exported as `l4-school-uniform.pdf`)

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask and answer questions about school uniform.

## Success Criteria
- I can answer a 穿……吗？question by repeating the verb — 穿。/ 不穿。 — not with 是
- I can ask and answer 你穿什么校服？and 你喜欢你的校服吗？
- I can name 帽子, 手套, 围巾 and 袜子

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l4-school-uniform`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens
- Audio: CD **T68** (textbook p.109 Ex.7)
- Printed role-play cards: 8–10 cards, each a fictional school with its uniform described in Chinese (colours + 2–3 garments)
- Printed Slap-the-Board cards for the four accessories plus six known garments
- Workbook p.149 (Ex.6), p.154 (Ex.15)

> **Why this lesson exists.** Workbook Revision p.171 asks 你穿什么校服？and 你喜欢你的校服吗？ — two questions that appear **nowhere** in the textbook lesson but are on the revision list students will be tested from. This lesson teaches them.

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 你喜欢穿什么衣服？and the ten garments.

**How:** **Pair dialogue from memory, 3 min.** A asks 你喜欢穿什么衣服？B answers with at least two garments and a colour each. Swap. Then two pairs perform.

**Whiteboard quiz, 3 min.** Four items: write 'a grey coat', 'a red sweater', 'school uniform', and translate 我穿衬衫、毛衣和长裤。

**Workbook p.149 Ex.6, 2 min** — find the missing word to complete the phrase. Item 1 is 校＿ (校服 or 校车 — both correct, both known) and item 2 is 裤＿. Quick oral run-through, not written.

**Connection:** "You can say what you like to wear. Today: what you *have* to wear."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. Before reading them, ask the class in English: "If I ask you 你上学穿校服吗？ what's the Chinese for yes?" Collect the wrong answers (是 / 对 / 好). Then say: none of those. Read criterion 1.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 帽子 | màozi | hat | Soft cap with a brim (SVG) |
| 1 | 手套 | shǒutào | gloves | Pair of gloves (SVG) |
| 2 | 围巾 | wéijīn | scarf | Long scarf, looped (SVG) |
| 2 | 袜子 | wàzi | socks | Pair of ankle socks (SVG) |

> These four are textbook p.110 **Extra Words** — recognition level. They are not on the Unit Test vocabulary list, so accept recognition rather than production. Say so to the class; it changes how hard they push.
>
> **Use 有 with them, not 穿.** The book gives no verb for hats, gloves and scarves, and the correct verb (戴) is not taught in Book 1 — don't introduce it. The slide says only 帽子和手套用「有」，不用「穿」, which is what students need. 袜子 is the exception: 穿袜子 is correct, and it's the one the uniform role-play needs.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我姐姐喜欢黑色的**帽子**。 — My sister likes black hats.
2. 我妈妈有粉红色的**手套**。 — My mum has pink gloves.
3. 我有**帽子**和**手套**，是蓝色的。 — I have a hat and gloves, they're blue.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我有 ________ 色的 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two clauses, one about you and one about someone in your family, joined with 也 or contrasted: 我有黑色的帽子，我妹妹也有，她的是粉红色的。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我有绿色的**围巾**。 — I have a green scarf.
2. 我弟弟喜欢黄色的**袜子**。 — My little brother likes yellow socks.
3. 我的**围巾**和**袜子**是白色的。 — My scarf and socks are white.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我的 ________ 是 ________ 色的。
**Extension —** List four things you own with four different colours in one sentence, using 、and 和.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — the verb-repeat answer

**Q: 你上学穿校服吗？ → A: 穿。／不穿。**

Put the wrong answer on the board next to the right one and leave it there:

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| 你上学穿校服吗？→ 是。 | 你上学穿校服吗？→ **穿。** |

Then the other two questions from Revision p.171:

1. 你上学穿校服吗？→ 穿。
2. 你穿什么校服？→ 白色的衬衫和蓝色的长裤。
3. 你喜欢你的校服吗？→ 不喜欢。
4. 我上学穿校服。我穿白色的衬衫、蓝色的长裤和黑色的袜子。我不喜欢我的校服，我喜欢穿汗衫和短裤。 ← the full answer, four garments, two clauses, a negative preference.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "你喜欢穿裙子吗？" — cold call five students in a row, listening only for 喜欢／不喜欢, not 是.
- **Correct the Teacher:** teacher says 你上学穿校服吗？— 是。 Class shouts the correction.
- "Write the answer to 你穿什么校服？for *our* school." (whiteboards — every answer should differ only in colour)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Class uniform survey — We Do (6 min) | 20–26
**(Format borrowed from textbook p.101 Ex.7, which the class already knows from Lesson 13.)**

Two questions on the board with a tally box each:
- 你喜欢你的校服吗？喜欢 ／ 不喜欢
- 你喜欢穿校服还是自己的衣服？ *(teacher asks this one in English — 还是 is not taught)*

Students move round the room asking the first question and tallying. Then report back in the Lesson 13 frame: **十五个人不喜欢校服。**

**Extension —** Ask a follow-up question after the tally answer — 为什么？is not taught, so instead: 你喜欢穿什么衣服？— and report back **two** facts in one sentence: 十五个人不喜欢校服，他们喜欢穿汗衫和牛仔裤。

#### Activity 2: Uniform role-play cards — You Do (9 min) | 26–35
Pairs. Each pair gets two printed cards, each describing a fictional school's uniform in Chinese (e.g. *红星中学：白色的衬衫、灰色的裙子、黑色的袜子*). A holds a card and is a student at that school; B interviews:

> B: 你上学穿校服吗？
> A: 穿。
> B: 你穿什么校服？
> A: 我穿白色的衬衫、灰色的裙子和黑色的袜子。
> B: 你喜欢你的校服吗？
> A: 不喜欢。

Then swap cards and roles. Every student runs it twice.

- **Setup needed:** 8–10 printed cards. Make two of them uniform-free schools so 不穿 gets used.

**Extension —** The interviewer works **without a card**: they must invent follow-up questions from what they hear (你喜欢什么颜色？你今天穿什么衣服？), and at the end report the other school's uniform back to the class from memory — 他穿白色的衬衫和灰色的裙子，他不喜欢他的校服。Pair a fluent student with a less confident one and give the fluent student the interviewer's role.

#### Activity 3: Listen and choose the colour — You Do (4 min) | 35–39
**(Textbook p.109 Ex.7, CD T68)**

Six items — 衬衫, 牛仔裤, 裙子, 出租车, 校车, 电车 — and a colour grid A–F. Play twice. Students write the letter on whiteboards and hold up after each item.

Note the mix: three garments and three vehicles. Tell them that's deliberate — the Unit Test does the same.

**Extension —** Write the **whole sentence** you hear, not just the letter. (Script: 爸爸穿白色的衬衫。哥哥穿蓝色的牛仔裤。姐姐穿棕色的裙子。我喜欢红色的出租车。弟弟坐黄色的校车上学。妈妈坐紫色的电车上班。)

#### Game: Slap the Board — accessories round (4 min) | 39–43
Four teams, cards on the board: today's four accessories plus six known garments. Teacher calls; first hand wins the point.

- **Setup needed:** printed cards.
- **Harder version:** Teacher calls in **English** rather than Chinese, so there's no sound-matching shortcut.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read aloud. Point at criterion 1 and ask the 是/穿 question one more time.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards.
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 2 min):** Answer both in Chinese — 你上学穿校服吗？ and 你喜欢你的校服吗？
- **Preview:** "下一课 — writing. You'll describe six people from a recording, and hunt for the deliberate mistakes."
