# Clothing (6): Profiles — Describing a Whole Person

**Year Level:** Year 7
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Colours, Clothing, Parts of the Body
**Lesson:** Lesson 14 Clothing 穿着 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Slides:** [`l6-profiles`](../../../index/designs/y7-l14/l6-profiles/) — 19 slides (also exported as `l6-profiles.pdf`)

## Learning Intention
We are learning to write a profile of a person, including what they wear.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask and answer 你喜欢穿什么衣服？and 你今天穿什么衣服？
- I can read a passage about someone and answer questions about their clothes
- I can write a short profile of a person, using at least three things I've learned this year

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `l6-profiles`)
- Mini whiteboards + pens, and exercise books
- Chinese–English dictionaries, or 1:1 devices with a dictionary app
- Workbook p.157 (Ex.20 — the 黄铁牛 passage), p.152 (Ex.10), p.154 (Ex.16)
- Textbook p.112 (Ex.11), p.113 (Ex.12)

> **This is not a revision lesson.** It teaches four new words and a new task type. The revision has been happening in every Review phase for six lessons; the plenary at the end of this one looks back across the whole sequence.

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Everything wearable — eighteen items across six lessons.

**How:** **Matching race, 5 min.** Project the full summary board with pictures only, numbered 1–18. Students write the 汉字 for as many as they can in three minutes on whiteboards. Then answers together; students count their own score out of 18.

**3 min:** the three uniform questions from Lesson 4, fired at random students — 你上学穿校服吗？你穿什么校服？你喜欢你的校服吗？

**Connection:** "You can describe what one person is wearing. Today you describe the whole person — name, age, year, how they get to school, and what they wear."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected. As you read criterion 3, list on the board what "things I've learned this year" means: 名字 · 年龄 · 年级 · 国家 · 家人 · 时间 · 上学 · 颜色 · 衣服. Nine things from nine lessons. Leave the list up — it is the scaffold for the writing task.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 长衫 | chángshān | long gown | Traditional Chinese long gown (SVG) |
| 1 | 大衣 | dàyī | overcoat | Long heavy overcoat (SVG) |
| 2 | 风衣 | fēngyī | windbreaker | Belted trench-style coat (SVG) |
| 2 | 连衣裙 | liányīqún | dress | One-piece dress (SVG) |

> These four come from workbook p.152 Ex.10's dictionary list. Every one of them is built from characters the class already knows — 长 + 衫, 大 + 衣, 风 + 衣, 连 + 衣 + 裙. Say that out loud on each slide: **these are words you can almost read already.**
>
> 长衫 also appeared in workbook p.148 Ex.3 back in Lesson 1, where students coloured a 棕色的长衫 without being told what it was. Point at it.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 这是中国的**长衫**。 — This is a Chinese long gown.
2. 我妈妈喜欢黑色的**大衣**。 — My mum likes black overcoats.
3. 我妈妈喜欢穿**大衣**，我不喜欢，我喜欢**长衫**。 — My mum likes wearing an overcoat; I don't, I like a long gown.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我 ________ 喜欢穿 ________ 。
**Extension —** Work out the meaning of 长裙 and 短裙 from the characters alone, then use one of them in a sentence.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我姐姐有粉红色的**连衣裙**。 — My older sister has a pink dress.
2. 我爸爸喜欢穿**风衣**。 — My dad likes wearing a windbreaker.
3. 我妈妈喜欢穿**连衣裙**，我爸爸喜欢穿**风衣**。 — My mum likes wearing dresses, my dad likes wearing a windbreaker.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 有 ________ 色的连衣裙。
**Extension —** Explain in Chinese why 连衣裙 is a good name for a dress, using only words you know: 连衣裙是衣服和裙子。

#### Key Sentence Pattern — the profile

**这是 + 人。他／她今年 ______ 岁，上 ______ 年级。他／她每天 ______ 上学。他／她喜欢穿 ______ 。**

1. 这是王小明。
2. 他今年十二岁，上七年级。
3. 他每天坐校车上学。
4. 这是王小明。他今年十二岁，上七年级。他每天坐校车上学。他上学穿校服：白色的衬衫和蓝色的长裤。他不喜欢校服，他喜欢穿汗衫和牛仔裤。 ← the full model. Six sentences, five topics from five different units.

#### Text: 黄铁牛 (workbook p.157 Ex.20)
> 我叫黄铁牛。我是北京人。我今年十二岁，上七年级。我们一家人现在住在香港。
> 我家有三口人：爸爸、妈妈和我。我爸爸每天九点上班。他坐公共汽车上班。他喜欢穿衬衫和长裤。我妈妈不工作。她喜欢穿裙子。
> 我每天八点上学，下午四点放学。我喜欢穿汗衫和牛仔裤。

Choral read once, projected with pinyin. Then read again in three parts, three rows of the class taking a paragraph each.

Three quick comprehension questions here, the other five in Activity 1: What year is 黄铁牛 in? Where does the family live now? What does dad wear?

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "这是我爸爸 or 这个人是谁 — which one is a question?"
- "How do you say 'he's in Year 7'?" (他上七年级)
- "Write 'he likes wearing a T-shirt and jeans'."

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Interview — We Do (6 min) | 20–26
**(Textbook p.112 Ex.11, shortened)**

Six questions from the fourteen, chosen to feed the profile — the sheet's full fourteen won't fit in six minutes:

1. 你叫什么名字？
2. 你今年多大了？
3. 你上几年级？
4. 你每天怎么上学？
5. 你喜欢什么颜色？
6. 你喜欢穿什么衣服？
7. 你今天穿什么衣服？

Students interview **two** partners and note the answers in the Notes column. Then two students report back in the textbook's frame: **他叫……**

**Extension —** Interview in Chinese without looking at the question sheet, add one question of your own, and report back about your partner in a connected paragraph rather than a list — 他叫大生，今年十二岁，上七年级。他每天走路上学，他喜欢蓝色，今天穿蓝色的汗衫和黑色的短裤。

#### Activity 2: Write the profile — You Do (10 min) | 26–36
Two halves, both in exercise books.

**Part A, 5 min.** Write a profile of one of the people you interviewed, using the board's nine-topic list. Minimum four sentences, at least two of them about clothes. The 黄铁牛 passage stays projected as the model.

**Part B, 5 min — textbook p.113 Ex.12.** The party scene. Choose two of the people in the picture and write a profile for each — but these are strangers, so everything is invented. Start each with 这个人是…… or 这是……

**Extension —** Write three profiles, not two, and make one of them a person the class can identify from the picture by their clothes alone — then read it aloud and let the class point. No word bank, no summary board: turn away from the screen.

#### Activity 3 / Game: Dictionary relay (7 min) | 36–43
**(Workbook p.152 Ex.10)**

Ten words to look up: 和服 · 长衫 · 袜子 · 皮鞋 · 西装 · 手套 · 大衣 · 风衣 · 帽子 · 连衣裙.

Teams of three, one dictionary or device per team. Nine of the ten are already known from this sequence — only 和服 is genuinely new. So this is a **dictionary-skills** race, not a vocabulary hunt: the point is finding a word by its first character and its radical, fast. Teams write all ten meanings; first team correct wins.

- **Setup needed:** dictionaries or devices, one per team of three.
- **Harder version:** Look up by **radical**, not pinyin — 和服 under 禾, 袜子 under 衤. And the team must say which radical they used for each word.

### Plenary (43–50 min)

**This plenary looks back over all six lessons, not just today.**

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Today's three, read by a student. Then the sequence's arc on one slide: Lesson 1 穿 → Lesson 2 colour and length → Lesson 3 the question → Lesson 4 the uniform → Lesson 5 writing → Lesson 6 the whole person.
- **Self-assessment:** Six statements, one per lesson, 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards for each:
  1. I can use 穿 to say what someone wears
  2. I can put a colour before a garment with 的
  3. I can answer 你喜欢穿什么衣服？with a list
  4. I can answer 你上学穿校服吗？with 穿。not 是。
  5. I can write a description of what someone is wearing
  6. I can write a profile of a person
- **Exit ticket (whiteboards, 3 min) — workbook p.154 Ex.16:** Translate into Chinese: *I do not like wearing school uniform.* Then a second one if time: *My mother wears a shirt and skirt to work every day.* Both are on the Unit Test (Part 10).
- **Preview:** "下一课 — Lesson 15: 人体部位. The same 的 you've used all week, but what comes after it is eyes, noses and hair."
