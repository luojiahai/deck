# How It's Cooked 红烧、蒸、炒

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Food and Eating 饮食
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say how a dish is cooked, and to order a whole meal over the phone.

## Success Criteria
- I can name three ways of cooking in Chinese (红烧、蒸、炒)
- I can name a dish by its cooking method (红烧豆腐、蒸鱼、炒青菜)
- I can order a takeaway by phone and ask the total (叫外卖，问一共多少钱)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed dish cards from p.116 Act. 8 (14 dishes), one set per pair
- Two toy/disconnected phones, or students use their hands
- Audio: CD 47 (textbook p.115)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 菜单, 点菜, 饿, 烤鸭, 只, and 来 + 数量 + 量词 + 菜名.
**How:** Pair dialogue from memory — 30 seconds each way. A is 服务员 and opens with 各位好，这是菜单……请问可以点菜了吗？B orders two dishes using 来. Then swap. Teacher cold-calls three pairs to perform their exchange.
Then a 3-minute Sentence Jumble from WB Ex. 18 — another Unit Test format. Four items on the slide, students rebuild each on whiteboards:
1. 吗／请问，／点菜／了／可以？ 2. 一碗米饭／了／要／弟弟／又。 3. 了／我们大家／饿／都。 4. 和／炒青菜／点／红烧豆腐／了／我。
**Connection:** 我们会点烤鸭了。可是菜单上还有红烧豆腐、蒸鱼——这些字是什么意思？今天我们就学这个。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left visible.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 红烧 | hóngshāo | braise in soy sauce | SVG — a dark-glazed dish in a bowl |
| 1 | 蒸 | zhēng | steam | SVG — a bamboo steamer with steam lines |
| 2 | 肉丝 | ròusī | shredded meat | SVG — fine strips of meat on a plate |
| 2 | 青菜 | qīngcài | green vegetables | SVG — a bunch of leafy greens |

Model twice, class repeats twice. 烧 has 火 on the left, like 烤 from Lesson 2 — another fire word. 蒸 has 灬 underneath, which *is* 火 in its flattened form, and 艹 on top — a plant cooked over fire. 丝 means *thread*, so 肉丝 is meat cut into threads.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. **红烧**豆腐是我最喜欢的菜。 — Braised tofu is my favourite dish.
2. 妈妈**蒸**的鱼做得非常好吃。 — The fish mum steams is delicious.
3. 我们点了一个**红烧**豆腐和一个**蒸**鱼。 — both words, in Lesson 4's ordering pattern.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢吃红烧 ________ ，也喜欢吃蒸 ________ 。
**Extension —** 烤、红烧、蒸、炒 are four methods. Write one sentence comparing two of them with 比 (我觉得蒸鱼比红烧鱼好吃), then justify it with 因为.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 炒**肉丝**是一个很简单的菜。 — Stir-fried shredded pork is a simple dish.
2. 我每天晚饭都吃**青菜**。 — I eat green vegetables with dinner every day.
3. 来一个炒**肉丝**、一个炒**青菜**。 — both words, ordering.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 来一个炒 ________ 。
**Extension —** 丝 describes the *cut*, not the meat — 土豆丝, 胡萝卜丝 are the same cut of other things. Write three dishes using 丝 with different ingredients, and one sentence saying which you'd order and why.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 烹饪方法 + 食材
**method + ingredient = dish name**

| | 鱼 | 豆腐 | 青菜 | 肉丝 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 烤 | 烤鱼 | — | — | — |
| 红烧 | 红烧鱼 | 红烧豆腐 | — | — |
| 蒸 | 蒸鱼 | — | — | — |
| 炒 | — | — | 炒青菜 | 炒肉丝 |

1. 蒸鱼
2. 红烧豆腐
3. 炒青菜
4. 我们点了半只烤鸭、一个炒肉丝、一个红烧豆腐和一个蒸鱼。（hardest — the full Text 2 order, four methods in one sentence）

#### Text 2 — complete (p.115, CD 47)
Now the whole dialogue, including the dish list held back last lesson:
> 爸爸：我们都很饿了。我们现在就点。来半只烤鸭、一个炒肉丝、一个红烧豆腐、一个蒸鱼。再来一个炒青菜。

Character-only. Class reads it in halves, then the whole thing chorally, then two students perform it.

**Comprehension questions (in Chinese):** 爸爸点了几个菜？· 哪个菜是蒸的？· 「再来」是什么意思？

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「蒸」字下面的四点是什么？跟什么有关系？
- 怎么说 green vegetables？写在小白板上。
- 红烧豆腐、蒸鱼、炒青菜 — 哪一个用最多的油？
- 这句话对不对？✗ 来一个豆腐红烧。 Fix the word order.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary summary board
All four words, characters and pictures only. Stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Activity 1: A few sentences about each dish — We Do (8 min)
p.116 Act. 8. Pairs get the fourteen dish cards (奶酪蛋糕 · 白巧克力 · 寿司 · 烤鸭 · 红烧豆腐 · 蒸鱼 · 炒青菜 · 烤牛排 · 水果沙拉 · 绿茶 · 香肠 · 薯条 · 青豆蛋炒饭 · 冰淇淋). They deal them face down and take turns flipping one, saying **two** sentences about it, following the model:
> 我非常喜欢吃龙虾。我每个月吃一次龙虾。

All fourteen cards must be turned before they stop.

**Extension —** Three sentences per card, not two, and the third must connect the card to the one before it — 蒸鱼比烤鸭清淡，所以我妈妈更喜欢蒸鱼. When the pile is done, they pick the four they'd order together as one meal and justify the combination aloud.

#### Activity 2: Phone takeaway order — You Do (10 min)
p.117 Act. 10. **Situation: order lunch for five people over the telephone.** Pairs sit back to back — no lip-reading, no gestures, which is what makes it a phone call. A works from a menu; B is the customer.

> 服务员：晚上好！家乐快餐店。
> 小明：我想叫外卖。
> 服务员：请说。
> 小明：来一个比萨饼、四个炸鸡腿、两个汉堡包和四瓶可乐。一共多少钱？
> 服务员：一百五十六块。

They write the order first (2 min), then perform it back to back (4 min), then swap and do a second restaurant unscripted (4 min).

**Extension —** The 服务员 mishears one item deliberately and reads the order back wrong; the customer must catch it and correct it live (不是四个，是两个). The customer also asks two questions the script doesn't cover — 要等多长时间？· 你们送到学校吗？ — and the 服务员 has to answer. Neither side writes anything down.

#### Game: Odd One Out — cooking methods (5 min)
Teacher shows three dishes at a time; students say which doesn't belong **and why, in Chinese**.
- 红烧豆腐 · 蒸鱼 · 冰淇淋 → 冰淇淋不用做。
- 烤鸭 · 烤牛排 · 炒青菜 → 炒青菜不是烤的。
- 炒肉丝 · 炒青菜 · 红烧豆腐 → 红烧豆腐不是炒的。
- 薯条 · 炸鸡翅 · 蒸鱼 → 蒸鱼不是炸的。
- **Setup needed:** Reuse the fourteen dish cards from Activity 1.
- **Harder version:** students make the sets, not the teacher — each pair builds one round for the class and must have a defensible reason ready in Chinese. A set the class can't solve is only a good set if the maker's reason holds up.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Students read each aloud in Chinese.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs against each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：来一个 ________ 和一个 ________ 。 Use two different cooking methods.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们喝点儿东西——还要自己开一家饭店，写自己的菜单。
