# Ordering at a Restaurant 点菜

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Food and Eating 饮食
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to order food in a Chinese restaurant using 点 and 来.

## Success Criteria
- I can ask to order using 请问可以点菜了吗？
- I can order a dish using 来 + 数量 + 菜名 (来半只烤鸭)
- I can say I'm hungry and read a menu (说「我饿了」，看得懂菜单)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed copies of the four p.120 menus, one set per pair
- Character cards for 菜单 · 点菜 · 饿 · 烤鸭 (two sets, for Slap the Character)
- Audio: CD 47 (textbook p.115)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 完……以后……, 一共花了……块, 挺……的, and the Text 1 food words.
**How:** Correct the Teacher. Four sentences on the board, three of them wrong:
1. ✗ 以后我吃完饭看电视。
2. ✓ 我们一共花了三百多块。
3. ✗ 我们都很饱吃。
4. ✗ 这家饭店挺贵。
Students find and fix each on whiteboards, then one student per item explains the fix in Chinese.
**Connection:** 上节课我们在菜市场买菜。今天我们不买菜了——我们去饭店，请别人做给我们吃。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left visible for the lesson.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 菜单 | càidān | menu | SVG — an open menu card with ruled lines |
| 1 | 点菜 | diǎncài | order food | SVG — a hand pointing at a menu line |
| 2 | 饿（餓） | è | hungry | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |
| 2 | 烤鸭 | kǎoyā | roast duck | SVG — a whole roast duck on a platter |
| 3 | 只（隻） | zhī | measure word (animals) | — (grammatical; leave the space empty) |

Model twice, class repeats twice. 饿 and 饱 are a pair — both have 饣 on the left, and they are opposites. Put them side by side on the slide: 饿 ↔ 饱. 鸭 has 鸟 on the right — a bird word, like 鸡.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 各位好，这是**菜单**。 — Good evening, here's the menu.
2. 请问可以**点菜**了吗？ — May I take your order?
3. 我们看完**菜单**以后就**点菜**。 — both words, with last lesson's 完……以后.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 服务员：请问可以点菜了吗？ 你：________ 。
**Extension —** 点菜 is separable — 点什么菜, 点了三个菜. Write two sentences that split it, not two that keep it whole.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我们都很**饿**了。 — We're all hungry.
2. 北京**烤鸭**做得非常好吃。 — The Peking duck is delicious.
3. 我很**饿**，我想吃**烤鸭**。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我很饿，我想吃 ________ 。
**Extension —** One sentence containing both 饿 and 饱: 我们进饭店的时候都很饿，吃完以后都很饱。Then add why in a 因为 clause.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 来半**只**烤鸭。 — We'll have half a roast duck.
2. 我家有两**只**猫。 — We have two cats at home.
3. 我们点了一**只**烤鸭和一个炒青菜。 — 只 against 个, side by side.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 来 ________ 只 ________ 。
**Extension —** 只 is for animals and one-of-a-pair. 碗 for rice and soup, 杯 for tea, 瓶 for cola — you'll meet those properly in Lesson 6. Write four short orders, one with each measure word, and don't repeat a dish.

#### Recall slide
菜单 · 点菜 · 饿 · 烤鸭 · 只 — characters only. Class reads back chorally.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 点 and 来
**来 + 数量 + 量词 + 菜名** (the textbook NOTE box, p.120: 点 and 来 both order food)

1. 来半只烤鸭。
2. 来一个炒青菜。
3. 来三碗米饭吧。
4. 来半只烤鸭、一个炒肉丝，再来一个蒸鱼。（hardest — a chained order with 再来）

Note 半只, not ✗只半 — 半 comes before the measure word. 再来 chains the next item within the same turn.

#### Text 2 — first half (p.115, CD 47)
> 服务员：各位好，这是菜单……请问可以点菜了吗？
> 爸爸：我们都很饿了。我们现在就点。来半只烤鸭……
> 服务员：要不要米饭？
> 爸爸：来三碗米饭吧。

Character-only. Teacher reads both parts, class follows; then half the class is 服务员 and half is 爸爸 for a choral read. The dish names after 烤鸭 are held back for Lesson 5.

**Comprehension questions (in Chinese):** 谁先说话，服务员还是爸爸？· 他们要了几碗米饭？· 「各位」是什么意思？

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「饿」和「饱」哪个是 hungry？Thumbs up for 饿, down for 饱 — teacher calls them at random.
- 怎么说 menu？写在小白板上。
- 这句话对不对？✗ 来只半烤鸭。 Fix it.
- 一__烤鸭，一__米饭 — 哪个用「只」？哪个用「碗」？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary summary board
All five words, characters and pictures only. Stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Activity 1: Complete the dialogue in writing — We Do (8 min)
p.120 Act. 14. Pairs get all four printed menus (快乐自助餐厅 · 天天快餐店 · 北京饭店 · 欢欢茶餐厅). They pick one restaurant and write out the missing turns:

> 服务员：各位好，这是菜单……请问可以点菜了吗？
> 爸爸：可以。来 ________ 。
> 服务员：________ ？
> 爸爸：________ 。
> 服务员：几位想喝什么？
> 爸爸：________ 。

The rule: **every dish they order must come off the menu they chose**, and they must order at least three.

**Extension —** Write it for a table of five with one vegetarian and one person who doesn't eat seafood — so the order has to be justified, not just listed. Add a line where the 服务员 recommends something (我们的烤鸭做得最好吃) and the customer either takes it or refuses with a reason.

#### Activity 2: Perform it — You Do (9 min)
Same pairs, scripts face down. They perform their dialogue standing at their table, then swap roles and perform a *different* restaurant's order — so the second run is unscripted. Two pairs perform for the class.

**Extension —** Perform without having written anything: the teacher hands them a menu they haven't seen, gives them thirty seconds, and they order for a table of four. The 服务员 must ask one unscripted question (要不要米饭？想喝点儿什么？几位？) that the customer has to handle live.

#### Game: Slap the Character (5 min)
Four large character cards on the board: 菜单 · 点菜 · 饿 · 烤鸭. Teacher says the word, two students race to slap it.
- **Setup needed:** Two sets of printed character cards, blu-tack.
- **Harder version:** the teacher says a whole sentence with the word missing (我们都很____了，快点菜吧) and the slapper must slap the right card *and* say the complete sentence before the point counts.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Students read each aloud in Chinese.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** 翻译成中文：*May I order now? We'll have half a roast duck.*
- **Preview:** 下节课我们学怎么说一道菜是怎么做的——红烧、蒸、炒。
