# Roast, Steak and Desserts 烤牛排和甜品

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Food and Eating 饮食
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to talk about desserts at a buffet and say how full we are.

## Success Criteria
- I can name the cooking word 烤 and use it with a dish (用「烤」说一道菜)
- I can name three desserts in Chinese (说出三种甜品)
- I can say how much someone ate using 吃得很饱 (用「得」说吃了多少)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed Text 1 (p.110) with pinyin, one per pair
- Six picture cards for Odd One Out (龙虾 · 三文鱼 · 寿司 · 奶酪 · 甜品 · 烤牛排)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's four words — 自助餐, 龙虾, 三文鱼, 寿司 — and 在……你可以吃到……
**How:** Pair dialogue from memory, 90 seconds: A asks 你们去吃自助餐了吗？吃了什么？B answers using at least two of the four words, then they swap. Then a quick whiteboard round: teacher shows a picture, students write the characters (no pinyin allowed).
Then a 3-minute sort, WB Ex. 8 — the Unit 4 Test's Part 1 format, run early so it's familiar long before the test. Eighteen foods on the slide (龙虾 糖果 猪肉 热狗 面条 香肠 牛排 蛋糕 盒饭 红豆汤 汉堡包 三文鱼 巧克力 冰淇淋 三明治) into six columns: 海鲜 · 零食 · 肉类 · 快餐 · 中餐 · 甜品. Pairs, on whiteboards. Take two placements and ask 为什么？ in Chinese.
**Connection:** 上节课我们说了自助餐的主食。今天说甜品——自助餐最好的部分。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left visible. Read the Chinese aloud; students give the English for each criterion before the teacher does.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 烤 | kǎo | bake; roast | SVG — a flame under a grill rack |
| 1 | 牛排 | niúpái | beefsteak | SVG — a steak on a plate with a fork |
| 2 | 甜品 | tiánpǐn | dessert | SVG — a slice of cake and an ice cream cup |
| 2 | 奶酪 | nǎilào | cheese | SVG — a wedge of cheese with holes |
| 3 | 饱（飽） | bǎo | full | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |

Model twice, class repeats twice. 烤 has 火 on the left — fire, so it's a fire word, like 炒 and 炸. 饱 has 饣(食) on the left — a food word, and its opposite 饿 also has 饣. Hold that pair back; it lands properly in Lesson 4.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 这家饭店的**烤**牛排做得最好吃。 — This restaurant's roast beefsteak is the best.
2. 我爸爸每次都要吃**牛排**。 — My dad has steak every time.
3. 我们上个周末吃了**烤牛排**和龙虾。 — both words, with last lesson's vocabulary.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我（喜欢／不喜欢）吃烤 ________ 。
**Extension —** 「烤」can take many nouns — 烤鱼、烤鸡、烤面包. Write two sentences using 烤 with different foods, and one comparing them with 比.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我们还吃了很多**甜品**。 — We also ate a lot of desserts.
2. **奶酪**蛋糕很甜。 — Cheesecake is very sweet.
3. **甜品**有**奶酪**蛋糕、巧克力蛋糕和水果沙拉。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 甜品我最喜欢吃 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写一句用「除了……以外」：除了奶酪蛋糕以外，我还喜欢吃冰淇淋。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我们都吃得很**饱**。 — We all ate until we were full.
2. 我吃了两个龙虾，现在很**饱**。 — I ate two lobsters, I'm full now.
3. 甜品太多了，我吃得非常**饱**。 — with this lesson's other words.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我吃了 ________ ，吃得很饱。
**Extension —** 饱's opposite is 饿 — you'll meet it properly next week. Guess it from the 饣 radical, then write one sentence with each in the same breath: 我去饭店的时候很饿，吃完以后很饱。

#### Recall slide
After cycle 3: 烤 · 牛排 · 甜品 · 奶酪 · 饱 — characters only, no pinyin, no English. Class reads them back chorally. If it stalls, reteach before moving on.

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**V + 得 + 很 / 非常 + adj**  (degree complement — how the action was done)

1. 我们都吃得很饱。
2. 他吃得很快。
3. 他们的烤牛排做得非常好吃。
4. 那家饭店的菜做得好吃，但是挺贵的。（hardest — two clauses, with 但是 and 挺……的）

Think-aloud: 「得」connects the verb to *how much* or *how well*. 吃 + 得 + 很饱. Not ✗我很饱吃.

#### Text 1 — read together (p.110, CD 45)
> 我们一家三口上个周末去吃自助餐了。我们吃了龙虾、三文鱼、寿司、烤牛排、炒面等。我们还吃了很多甜品，有奶酪、巧克力蛋糕和水果沙拉。我们都吃得很饱。我们一共花了三百多块，挺便宜的。

Character-only on the slide (Y9). Teacher reads, class follows; then choral read.

**Comprehension questions (in Chinese):** 他们家有几口人？· 他们吃了什么甜品？· 三百多块贵不贵？他们觉得呢？

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「饱」的意思是什么？Thumbs up if you know.
- 怎么说 cheese？写在小白板上。
- 这句话对不对？✗ 我们都很饱吃。 How do we fix it?
- 「烤」字左边是什么部分？它跟什么有关系？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary summary board
All five words, characters and pictures only. Stays on screen through Activity 2.

#### Activity 1: The buffet dialogue — We Do (8 min)
p.112 Act. 3. Pairs work from the model, then run it without the script, then swap roles:
> A: 我们星期天下午去红山饭店吃自助餐了。
> B: 你们吃了什么？喝了什么？
> A: 我们吃了甜品，有蛋糕、饼干、冰淇淋等。我们还吃了寿司、炒面和炒饭。
> B: 有水果吗？
> A: 当然有。我们吃了西瓜、苹果和香蕉。
> B: 在那里吃饭贵不贵？
> A: 还可以，每位一百五十块。

Everyone runs it twice — second time with the script face down. Hand the printed script to the pairs who need it for round two; take it away from the rest.

**Extension —** B is a food critic, not a friend. B must ask three questions that aren't on the card (什么菜做得最好吃？人多不多？你们排队了吗？) and A must answer each with a reason attached (因为……所以……). At the end B reports the verdict to the class in third person: 他们去了红山饭店，他们觉得……

#### Activity 2: The dessert table — You Do (9 min)
Solo, in exercise books. Write a description of the 甜品 half of a buffet table. The rule that forces every word: **it must say what was roasted (烤), what the desserts were (甜品, 奶酪), what the main dish was (牛排), and how full everyone got (饱)** — all five words, at least four sentences.

> 上个周末我们一家去吃自助餐了。主食有烤牛排……甜品有奶酪蛋糕……我们都吃得很饱。

Two or three read aloud while the summary board is up.

**Extension —** Write it about *someone else* and add the money: 他们一家四口一共花了……块。Then judge it — 挺便宜的 or 挺贵的 — and say why in a 因为……所以…… clause. Written without looking at the summary board.

#### Game: Odd One Out (5 min)
Six picture cards on the board. Teacher shows three at a time; students say which doesn't belong **and why, in Chinese**.
- 龙虾 · 三文鱼 · 奶酪 → 奶酪不是海鲜。
- 甜品 · 蛋糕 · 牛排 → 牛排不是甜品。
- 烤牛排 · 炒面 · 冰淇淋 → 冰淇淋是甜品。
- **Setup needed:** Six printed picture cards.
- **Harder version:** the reason must contain 因为, and no group's reason may repeat a category already used — so the later rounds force students to find a second, non-obvious way the odd one differs.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Choral read of each criterion in Chinese.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic light on whiteboards — students write 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion and hold up.
- **Exit ticket:** 翻译成中文：*We all ate until we were very full.*
- **Preview:** 下节课我们用二百块去买菜——你要买给你们一家人吃。
