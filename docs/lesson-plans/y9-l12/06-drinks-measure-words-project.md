# Drinks, Measure Words and Your Own Restaurant 开一家饭店

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Food and Eating 饮食
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to order drinks with the right measure words, and to design and present a restaurant of our own.

## Success Criteria
- I can order a drink using 来 + 数量 + 杯／瓶 (来两杯绿茶，再来两瓶可乐)
- I can match measure words to the right nouns (量词用对)
- I can write a menu and describe my restaurant in Chinese (写菜单，介绍我的饭店)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- A4 paper, two sheets per pair (one for the floor plan, one for the menu)
- Coloured pencils or markers
- Audio: CD 48 (textbook p.119)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The whole sequence — Text 1 and Text 2 vocabulary, cooking methods, ordering with 来.
**How:** CD 48 listening (p.119 Act. 12). Six people — 小天, 小文, 小山, 冬冬, 小云, 小明 — matched to eight statements A–H. Play twice. This is the sequence's cumulative recall: the audio sweeps 蔬菜, 水果, 快餐, 零食, 盒饭, 烤鸭, 红烧豆腐, 炒肉丝 across all six lessons.
Check answers as a class; ask 小明家爱吃什么？ and get the three dishes named in Chinese.
**Connection:** 小明家点了烤鸭、红烧豆腐、炒肉丝——他们喝了什么？菜单上还有饮料。今天我们说饮料，然后你们自己开饭店。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left visible. Note aloud that today's plenary looks back over all six lessons, not just this one.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 3 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 杯 | bēi | cup | SVG — a teacup with a saucer |
| 1 | 绿茶 | lǜchá | green tea | SVG — a glass of pale green tea |
| 2 | 瓶 | píng | bottle | SVG — a capped drinks bottle |

Model twice, class repeats twice. 杯 has 木 on the left — cups were wooden. 茶 has 艹 on top — it's a plant.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 来两**杯绿茶**。 — Two green teas, please.
2. 我每天早上喝一**杯**牛奶。 — I drink a glass of milk every morning.
3. 吃完烤鸭以后，我们要了两**杯绿茶**。 — both words, with Lesson 3's 完……以后 and Lesson 4's 烤鸭.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我每天喝 ________ 杯 ________ 。
**Extension —** 杯 counts cups *and* glasses. Write two sentences, one hot drink and one cold, and join them with 一边……一边…… or 完……以后.

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 再来两**瓶**可乐。 — And two bottles of cola as well.
2. 一**瓶**汽水多少钱？ — How much is a bottle of soft drink?
3. 来两**杯**绿茶，再来两**瓶**可乐。 — 杯 and 瓶 in the same order, straight from Text 2.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 来 ________ 杯 ________ ，再来 ________ 瓶 ________ 。
**Extension —** Order drinks for a table of five where no two people want the same thing, using at least three different measure words, and give the total in 块.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — measure words in review
**数量 + 量词 + 名词**

| 量词 | 用在 |
|---|---|
| 只 | 烤鸭、鸡、猫 |
| 碗 | 米饭、汤、面条 |
| 杯 | 绿茶、咖啡、牛奶 |
| 瓶 | 可乐、汽水、水 |
| 个 | 寿司、龙虾、蛋糕 |
| 块 | 三文鱼、巧克力 |

1. 三碗米饭
2. 两杯绿茶
3. 两瓶可乐
4. 来半只烤鸭、三碗米饭、两杯绿茶，再来两瓶可乐。（hardest — four measure words, one sentence, the whole Text 2 order）

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 一__绿茶，一__可乐 — 哪个用「杯」？哪个用「瓶」？
- 怎么说 *two bottles of cola*？写在小白板上。
- 这句话对不对？✗ 来两个绿茶。 What should it be?
- 烤鸭用「只」，米饭用什么？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Measure-word race — We Do (5 min)
p.117 Act. 9, run fast. Class splits into groups of four. On the board: 瓶 杯 只 碗 个 块 斤 包. Each group finds **two** nouns for each measure word and writes them on a single sheet. Three minutes on the clock. The group with the most correct matches wins; wrong matches cost a point, so guessing is penalised.

**Extension —** Groups that finish early take the harder set — 辆 本 节 件 位 张 双 家 — from the textbook's full list, and must use two of them in complete sentences.

#### Activity 2: Design your restaurant — You Do, in pairs (14 min)
p.121 Act. 15, the unit project. Pairs get two sheets of A4.

**Sheet 1 — the floor plan.** Draw the layout: how many floors, how many tables, where the kitchen and toilets are. Label everything in Chinese.

**Sheet 2 — the menu.** Write the restaurant's menu — a name, then dishes grouped into 主食 / 甜品 / 饮料, each with a price. The rule that forces the sequence's vocabulary: **the menu must use at least six dishes from Lesson 12 and at least three different measure words.**

Then they write three or four sentences introducing it, following the model:
> 这家饭店叫中港西餐馆，一共有两层。一楼有十张餐桌，可以坐下五十个人。二楼有十二张餐桌，可以坐下六十个人。厨房在一楼。男、女厕所一楼、二楼都有。在这家饭店你可以吃到……

Teacher circulates. Pairs who stall get the sequence's summary vocabulary sheet; the rest work from memory.

**Extension —** Write the restaurant's *review* as well as its menu — four to six connected sentences from a customer's point of view, using 挺……的, one 比 comparison against another restaurant, and 完……以后. The pair must also be ready to defend one pricing decision in Chinese when the class questions them (为什么你们的龙虾这么贵？).

#### Activity 3: Present and judge (4 min)
Three or four pairs hold up their plan and menu and read their introduction aloud. The class judges on the textbook's three criteria — design, quality of drawing, language used — with a show of hands. Remaining pairs display their work on the wall.

**Harder version for the presenters:** the class may order from the menu, and the presenting pair must take the order live as 服务员, using 来 and the right measure words.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
**Wider scope — this looks back over all six lessons, not just today.**

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Today's three read aloud, then the projected list of all six lessons' intentions — students say 会 or 还不会 for each.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards. Students write the one word from the whole sequence they're least sure of and hold it up. The teacher reads the room and names the two or three that recur — those are what the revision before the unit test starts with.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：来 ________ 杯 ________ ，再来 ________ 瓶 ________ 。
- **Preview:** 第四单元学完了。下节课我们开始第五单元——社区，说一说你家附近有什么。
