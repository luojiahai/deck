# Telling the Story of a Meal Out 说一说你去饭店吃饭

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Food and Eating 饮食
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe a meal out from start to finish — the order of the courses, what it cost, and what we thought of it.

## Success Criteria
- I can use 完……以后…… to say what I do after finishing something
- I can say what a meal cost using 一共花了……块
- I can give an opinion using 挺……的 (挺便宜的／挺贵的)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Audio: CD 46 (textbook p.114)
- Printed price board from p.113, one per pair (龙虾¥60/个 · 三文鱼¥68/块 · 寿司¥5/个 · 鸡腿¥12/斤 · 活鱼¥12/条 · 牛排¥22/斤 · 活虾¥25/斤 · 鸡翅¥10/斤 · 香肠¥18/斤)
- Printed Act. 6 answer grid, one per student

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Text 1's nine words — 自助餐, 龙虾, 三文鱼, 寿司, 烤, 牛排, 甜品, 奶酪, 饱 — and V+得+很+adj.
**How:** Bingo. Students draw a 3×3 grid and fill it with any nine words from the board (all nine of the above are up). Teacher calls them in Chinese with a short definition attached (这是一种很大的红色海鲜) rather than the bare word — so recognition has to run through meaning, not sound.
**Connection:** 我们已经会说自助餐吃什么了。今天我们说得更完整——先吃什么，后吃什么，一共花了多少钱。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Projected and left up. Read aloud in Chinese; students translate each criterion before the teacher confirms.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### No new vocabulary this lesson
This is the consolidation lesson in the sequence — all nine Text 1 words get used at speed, and three patterns get modelled. Tell the class that up front: 今天没有生词，但是要说很多话。

#### Key Sentence Pattern 1 — 完……以后……
**V + 完 + (O) + 以后 + 再 + V**

1. 我一般喝完汤以后再吃主食。
2. 我一般吃完主食以后再吃甜品。
3. 我一般踢完球以后洗澡。
4. 我一般做完作业以后才能看电视，所以我每天都做得很快。（hardest — 才 and a 所以 clause）

Think-aloud: 完 goes straight after the verb, and 以后 closes the clause. Not ✗以后我吃完饭做作业 — the word order in the workbook's error-correction exercise is exactly this mistake.

#### Key Sentence Pattern 2 — money and opinion
**一共花了 + 金额** · **挺 + adj + 的**

1. 我们一共花了三百多块。
2. 挺便宜的。
3. 我们一共花了六百多块，挺贵的。
4. 那家饭店的菜做得挺好吃的，但是我们一共花了八百块，太贵了。（hardest）

Note 多 in 三百多块 — "three hundred *odd*", not exactly 300.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「完」放在哪里？动词前面还是后面？
- 这句话对不对？✗ 以后我看完书做作业。 Rewrite it on your whiteboard. *(This is WB Ex. 21 item 2 — the test format, run live.)*
- 「挺便宜的」和「很便宜」哪个语气轻一点？
- 三百多块 — 是三百块吗？还是更多？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 完……以后…… drill — We Do (5 min)
p.112 Act. 4, on whiteboards. Teacher gives the stem, students complete and hold up. Five rounds, fast:
1. 我一般喝完汤以后再 ________ 。
2. 我一般吃完主食以后再 ________ 。
3. 我一般踢完球以后 ________ 。
4. 我一般做完 ________ 。
5. 我一般 ________ 。

Take two answers aloud each round. By round 5 students are building the whole sentence themselves.

**Extension —** Chain three actions into one sentence, not two: 我一般做完作业以后先吃晚饭，吃完晚饭以后再看电视。Then write the same chain about a family member, in third person.

#### Activity 2: CD 46 listening (6 min)
p.114 Act. 6. Six multiple-choice items about a family's eating-out habits. Play twice: first straight through with pens down, then again with the grid.

Afterwards, unpack item 5 as a class — 他们的饭菜又好吃又便宜 — and put 又……又…… on the board. It's not on the success criteria, but it's in the audio and it's the natural partner to 挺……的.

**Extension —** While the rest check answers, these students write down *why* for each item — the sentence in the audio that gave the answer away. Then they rewrite item 6's answer (爸爸出差了) as a full sentence with 因为.

#### Activity 3: It is your turn — describe a real meal out (3 min)
p.114 Act. 7. The model paragraph goes up on screen (mum's birthday buffet: 上个星期天是我妈妈的生日……爸爸一共花了六百多块，挺便宜的。). Thirty seconds of turn-and-talk — every student tells their partner about a real meal out — then the teacher takes three aloud to the class. Each one must include a total with 一共花了 and a verdict with 挺……的.

**Extension —** Tell it in third person about the family member who paid, and add what you'd order differently next time (下次我要点……，因为……).

#### Activity 4: ¥200 at the market — You Do, in pairs (9 min)
p.113 Act. 5. Pairs get the price board. A is the 售货员, B the 顾客 with ¥200 to feed the family. B must ask the price of at least three items and buy at least two. Then they swap.

> 售货员：我能帮你吗？
> 顾客：我想买几个龙虾。多少钱一个？
> 售货员：六十块。
> 顾客：挺贵的。
> 售货员：不算贵，已经很便宜了。
> 顾客：我觉得很贵。我不买了。

Teacher circulates. Pairs who stall get the printed model; the rest work from the price board alone.

**Extension —** The ¥200 must be spent as close to exactly as possible, and B must say the running total aloud in Chinese after each purchase (现在一共花了一百三十四块). B compares two stalls' items with 比 (这里的牛排比那里的便宜) and, at the end, reports to the class what they bought, what they *didn't* buy and why (我没买龙虾，因为太贵了). Then they swap — and the new 售货员's goal is to talk the customer *up* to a more expensive item.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each aloud; class gives a live example for each.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs against each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句用「完……以后……」，说你放学以后做什么。
- **Preview:** 下节课我们进饭店坐下来——怎么看菜单，怎么点菜。
