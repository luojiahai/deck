# At the Buffet 自助餐

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 4 — Food and Eating 饮食
**Lesson:** Lesson 12 Eating Out 外出就餐 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the main dishes at a buffet and say what you can eat there.

## Success Criteria
- I can name four buffet dishes in Chinese (说出四种自助餐的菜)
- I can use 在……你可以吃到…… to say what a place serves
- I can sort foods into 主食 and 甜品 (把食物分成主食和甜品)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Printed A5 copies of the p.111 buffet picture, one per pair
- Character cards for 自助餐 · 龙虾 · 三文鱼 · 寿司 (two sets, for Slap the Character)

---

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 11 零食 vocabulary — 薯片, 巧克力, 冰淇淋, 饼干, 糖果, 蛋糕 — and the price question 多少钱一包/一个.
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the six 零食 words (characters only, class says the word chorally), then whiteboards: "写一句 — 薯片多少钱一包？" and two prices in characters (四块五, 十二块).
**Connection:** 上节课我们学了零食。今天我们去饭店——去吃自助餐。Snacks are what you buy for yourself; today we look at what a restaurant puts out for you.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on the projector and leave up in the corner of every slide. Read the intention aloud in Chinese, then the three criteria — a student volunteer reads each 中文 part, the teacher glosses the English.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 自助餐 | zìzhùcān | buffet | SVG — a long table of dishes with a stack of plates |
| 1 | 龙虾 | lóngxiā | lobster | SVG — lobster, red, claws and tail |
| 2 | 三文鱼 | sānwényú | salmon | SVG — a pink fillet with a pale stripe |
| 2 | 寿司 | shòusī | sushi | SVG — three nigiri pieces on a board |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Point out 自助 = "self-help" — 助 is *help*, so 自助餐 is literally a self-help meal. For 龙虾, note 龙 is *dragon*: a dragon-prawn.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 这家饭店的**自助餐**很好吃。 — The buffet at this restaurant is delicious.
2. 我最喜欢吃**龙虾**。 — I like eating lobster best.
3. 上个周末我们去吃**自助餐**，我吃了三个**龙虾**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我（喜欢／不喜欢）吃 ________ 。 · 我们去吃 ________ 了。
**Extension —** 用「比」写一句：龙虾比三文鱼贵。然后加上原因：因为……所以……

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **三文鱼**很新鲜。 — The salmon is fresh.
2. 我妹妹非常爱吃**寿司**。 — My little sister loves sushi.
3. 在自助餐厅你可以吃到**三文鱼**和**寿司**。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 在自助餐厅你可以吃到 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写两句，一句用「除了……以外」：除了寿司以外，我还喜欢吃三文鱼。

*(No recall slide this lesson — only two cycles. The summary board below does that job.)*

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**在 + 地方 + 你可以吃到 + 菜名**

1. 在自助餐厅你可以吃到龙虾。
2. 在快餐店你可以吃到汉堡包和薯条。
3. 在菜市场你可以买到新鲜的鱼和青菜。（买到 not 吃到 — the verb changes with the place）
4. 在这家饭店，主食你可以吃到三文鱼、寿司，甜品你可以吃到蛋糕和冰淇淋。（hardest — two halves, five items, joined）

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「自助餐」是什么意思？Thumbs up if you know.
- 怎么说 lobster？写在小白板上。
- 这句话对不对？✗ 我吃到可以龙虾在自助餐厅。 What's wrong with it?
- 龙虾、三文鱼、寿司 — 哪一个是日本菜？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary summary board
All four words on one slide, characters and pictures only, no pinyin and no English. Leave it on screen through Activity 1.

#### Activity 1: Describe the buffet — We Do (8 min)
p.111 Act. 1. Pairs get the A5 picture. The table is split 主食 (savoury) on the left and 甜品 (sweet) on the right. Partners take turns, one item each, going round the picture:
> A: 在这个自助餐厅，主食你可以吃到龙虾。
> B: 主食你可以吃到三文鱼。

Every item on the picture must be named before they stop. Teacher circulates and feeds 蛋炒饭 / 炒面 / 沙拉 to pairs who stall — those words are already taught.

**Extension —** Don't list the dishes; *sell* the restaurant. In four connected sentences, recommend it to a friend who has ¥150: say what the best dish is and why (我觉得龙虾最好吃，因为很新鲜), compare two items with 比 (三文鱼比寿司贵), and say whether ¥150 is enough. No picture in front of them for the second half — turn it face down.

#### Activity 2: Build a buffet menu — You Do (9 min)
Solo, in exercise books. Each student designs their own 自助餐厅 and writes its menu. The rule that forces every word: **the menu must have a 主食 section and a 甜品 section, and all four of today's words must appear**, each with a price using 块.

> 快乐自助餐厅 · 主食：龙虾 ¥60 · 三文鱼 ¥68 · 寿司 ¥5 · 甜品：……
> 在我的自助餐厅你可以吃到……

Three students read theirs to the class while the summary board is still up.

**Extension —** Write it as a connected paragraph rather than a list — 5–6 sentences that hang together, including the total a family of three would spend (我们一家三口一共要花……块) and one 因为……所以…… justifying the most expensive item's price.

#### Game: Slap the Character (5 min)
Two students at the board, four large character cards taped up (自助餐 龙虾 三文鱼 寿司). Teacher says the word in Chinese — first to slap it wins the point, winner stays on. Run through the class quickly.
- **Setup needed:** Two sets of printed character cards, blu-tack.
- **Harder version:** the teacher doesn't say the word — she says a clue in Chinese (这是一种很大的红色海鲜／这是日本菜／这是一种粉红色的鱼). The slapper must then use the word in a full sentence before the point counts.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads each criterion aloud in Chinese; class gives the English.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs 👍 / 😐 / 👎 against each of the three criteria, one at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip — 写一句：在自助餐厅你可以吃到 ________ 。 Use a word you learned today. Collected at the door.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们学甜品——蛋糕、奶酪，还有怎么说「吃饱了」。
