# Rooms Upstairs 楼上有什么

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 House 房子 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the rooms upstairs and count them with the right measure word.

## Success Criteria
- I can name the upstairs rooms (卧室、书房、浴室、洗澡间)
- I can count bedrooms with 间 and bathrooms with 个 (三间卧室、两个浴室)
- I can understand someone describing their house when I hear it

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed blank floor-plan grids (an empty rectangle divided into six boxes) — two per student
- Printed Bingo grids, 3×3, pre-filled with today's four words plus 房间 · 楼上 · 楼下 · 层 · 楼 in varied orders — enough for the class
- Audio: CD track 63 (textbook p.125 Ex.4)
- Workbook pp.144–145 (Ex.1 character copying), p.145 (Ex.2 room matching), p.147 (Ex.8 word chains)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's six words and the 有／在 split — the exit tickets will have shown who is still swapping them.

**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the six characters (房子 · 房间 · 层 · 楼 · 楼上 · 楼下), class says each chorally, then three Correct the Teacher sentences written on the board with deliberate errors:
1. 我的房间有楼上。 → 在
2. 我家的房子在两层。 → 有
3. 楼上有三个层。 → 三层 (层 counts itself; no 个)

Students find and fix each on whiteboards before anyone says the answer aloud.

Then **textbook p.131 Ex.13** as a 90-second turn-and-talk: 说一说你每天做什么。Students describe their daily routine to a partner in Chinese — Year 7 material, deliberately revisited here because one of today's new words, 洗澡, belongs in it and will land better attached to a routine they can already say.

**Connection:** "You can already say *my house has two storeys* and *my room is upstairs*. Today you say what else is up there — and you'll find Chinese counts bedrooms with a word we haven't used yet."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Introduce criterion 2 by writing 三 ____ 卧室 on the board with the measure word blank, and telling the class the blank is the one thing in today's lesson that has no English equivalent at all.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 卧室 | wòshì | bedroom | Line drawing: bed, bedside lamp, window |
| 1 | 书房 | shūfáng | study room | Line drawing: desk, chair, shelf of books |
| 2 | 浴室 | yùshì | bathroom | Line drawing: bathtub and shower head |
| 2 | 洗澡 | xǐzǎo | to bathe; have a shower | Line drawing: shower running, person under it |

Show 洗澡间 on the Cycle 2 slide beside 浴室, with an equals sign — the textbook itself presents them as alternatives (浴室(洗澡间), p.122). One gloss, not three: *bathroom*.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家有三间**卧室**。 — My house has three bedrooms.
2. 我爸爸的**书房**在楼上。 — My dad's study is upstairs.
3. 楼上有三间**卧室**和一个**书房**。 — Upstairs there are three bedrooms and a study. *(from Text 1)*

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家有 ________ 间卧室。／我家的书房在 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two clauses joined: say how many bedrooms your house has *and* whether it has a study, using 可是 or 还有 — 我家有四间卧室，可是我们没有书房。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我家有两个**浴室**。 — My house has two bathrooms.
2. 我每天晚上**洗澡**。 — I have a shower every evening. *(links back to Y7 daily routine)*
3. 我在**浴室洗澡**。 — I shower in the bathroom.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家有 ________ 个浴室。／我每天 ________ 洗澡。
**Extension —** Use 洗澡 with a time expression from Year 7 and put both new words in: 我每天晚上七点在楼上的浴室洗澡。

**Recall slide after cycle 2:** 卧室 · 书房 · 浴室 · 洗澡 — characters only. Class reads back.

*(Two cycles, not three — this lesson carries four words rather than six because the measure-word contrast below needs the extra minutes.)*

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**间 counts rooms you sleep in and live in; 个 is the default for everything else.**

Put the two columns side by side and model:

1. 楼上有三**间**卧室。 — bedrooms take 间
2. 楼下有一**间**卧室。 — still 间, wherever it is
3. 我家有两**个**浴室。 — bathrooms take 个
4. *(hardest)* 我家的房子有两层，楼上有三**间**卧室和一**个**书房，楼下有一**间**卧室和一**个**浴室。

Say out loud what the rule is *not*: it isn't about size, and it isn't about upstairs or downstairs. 卧室 takes 间; 浴室、书房、房间 take 个. The book uses 间 for 卧室 throughout Text 1 and the listening.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- Thumbs up/down: "书房 means *bedroom*." (down — it's *study room*)
- Whiteboards: "间 or 个? 楼上有三 ____ 卧室。"
- Whiteboards: "间 or 个? 我家有两 ____ 浴室。"
- Error correction: "三个卧室 — what's wrong, and what should it be?"
- Make a sentence: "Give me a sentence with 洗澡 and a time. Whiteboards, 30 seconds."
- Cold call: "Which two words on the board mean the same thing? … Sam."

#### Text / Dialogue
The upstairs half of Text 1, on screen with pinyin:

> 楼上有三间卧室、一个书房和一个浴室(洗澡间)。
> *Lóushàng yǒu sān jiān wòshì, yí ge shūfáng hé yí ge yùshì (xǐzǎojiān).*
> Upstairs there are three bedrooms, a study and a bathroom.

Read aloud, class repeats, then read together chorally. Comprehension questions, answers in Chinese:
1. 楼上有几间卧室？
2. 楼上有书房吗？
3. 浴室在楼上还是楼下？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Listen and draw the upstairs — We Do (9 min)
Hand out the blank six-box floor-plan grids. Play **CD track 63** (p.125 Ex.4) — two dialogues about a house.

- **First pass:** students just tick the textbook's multiple-choice answers on p.125.
- **Second pass:** they stop ticking and draw. Dialogue 1's house goes in grid one, dialogue 2's in grid two, each room labelled in characters.

Then, without the audio, dictate a third house yourself while they draw:
> 这套房子有两层。楼上有四间卧室、一个书房和两个浴室。楼下有一间卧室。

Hold up and compare. The measure words are the point — if a student wrote 四个卧室 on their labels, that's the thing to name.

Summary board (卧室 · 书房 · 浴室 · 洗澡) stays on screen the whole time.

**Extension —** Don't draw — transcribe. Write out dialogue 1 in characters as you hear it, then answer in Chinese, in full sentences, why the speaker says 你可以用一间做书房 rather than 我家有书房. Then write a fourth house description of your own, four clauses, for a partner to draw; it must use every word from today plus two from Lesson 1.

#### Activity 2: Floor-plan pairs — You Do (9 min)
Each student designs an upstairs floor on their second grid, without showing their partner. Then, back to back or with a book between them, A describes and B draws:

> A: 楼上有三间卧室。 B: *(draws)* 有书房吗？ A: 有，书房在左边。／没有。

Compare drawings at the end. Then run **Workbook p.145 Ex.2** (match the numbered rooms in the cutaway house to the Chinese) — today only 卧室, 书房 and 浴室 are answerable; tell them the other four boxes get filled in next lesson, and leave them blank.

Close the block with **Workbook p.147 Ex.8** (word chains — write a word starting with the last character of the previous one: 客房 → 房子 → …). Eighteen chains; students do as many as they can in three minutes. It is the fastest vocabulary-recall format in the workbook and it pulls in words from every unit so far.

**Extension —** B may not ask yes/no questions — only 楼上有什么？ and 有几间？ — so A has to produce full unprompted sentences. When they swap, A's house must include a room that *isn't* upstairs, so B has to catch 楼下 in the description and put it in the right place. Then both write a three-sentence summary of their partner's house in the third person: 他家的房子有两层，楼上有…

#### Game: Room Bingo (5 min)
Hand out the pre-filled 3×3 grids (today's four words plus 房间 · 楼上 · 楼下 · 层 · 楼). Call the words **in sentences**, not in isolation — 我每天晚上**洗澡**。楼上有三间**卧室**。 — so students have to pick the target out of running speech. First to a line calls 我赢了.

- **Setup needed:** Printed Bingo grids in several arrangements
- **Harder version:** Winners don't call 我赢了 — they read their winning line back as three full sentences, one per word, and only then do they win.

#### Character writing: Workbook pp.144–145 Ex.1, continued (last 3 min of the block)
Stroke-order rows for today's five characters: 卧 (8) · 室 (9) · 浴 (10) · 洗 (9) · 澡 (16). Point out that 浴, 洗 and 澡 all carry 氵 — three of today's words, one water radical. 厅 and 厨 stay blank until Lesson 3.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read the three criteria; ask which one felt hardest and why.
- **Self-assessment:** Traffic light on whiteboards — students write 👍 / 😐 / 👎 for each criterion and hold up. Scan for 😐 on criterion 2 (measure words).
- **Exit ticket:** *Write in Chinese: Upstairs there are three bedrooms and one bathroom.* (楼上有三间卧室和一个浴室。) The measure words are what you're marking.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we go downstairs — and by the end of it you'll be able to read the whole of Text 1."
