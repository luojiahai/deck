# Where Things Are 在哪儿 — Position Words

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 House 房子 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say exactly where something is, using position words with 在 and 有.

## Success Criteria
- I can use the six position words (上面、下面、里面、外面、左面、右面)
- I can say where a thing is with 在 (电视在客厅里面)
- I can say what is in a place with 有 (客厅里面有电视)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides) — textbook p.124 Ex.3 and p.128 Ex.8 displayed large
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- A few classroom objects to move around during TPR — a book, a bag, a bottle
- Printed copies of p.128 Ex.8 (nine pictures with blanks) — one per student
- Workbook p.147 (Ex.7 opposites), p.151 (Ex.14, Ex.15)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The full thirteen-word room set from Lessons 1–3, and 在 vs 有.

**How:** Slap the Board, two students at a time, with the thirteen room words written up. Teacher says the word inside a sentence, not on its own. Four or five pairs, fast.

Then three whiteboard items:
1. 有 or 在? 楼下 ____ 客厅和餐厅。
2. 有 or 在? 我的卧室 ____ 楼上。
3. Translate: *The kitchen is downstairs.* (厨房在楼下。)

**Connection:** "So far 在 has only told us the *floor* — 楼上 or 楼下. Today it gets precise: above, below, inside, outside, left and right. Six words, and they all end the same way."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Write the six words in a column so the shared 面 lines up vertically — that's the pattern, and pointing at it is faster than explaining it.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 上面 | shàngmiàn | above; on top | Box diagram: dot above a rectangle |
| 1 | 下面 | xiàmiàn | below; underneath | Box diagram: dot below a rectangle |
| 2 | 里面 | lǐmiàn | inside | Box diagram: dot inside a rectangle |
| 2 | 外面 | wàimiàn | outside | Box diagram: dot beside a closed rectangle |
| 3 | 左面 | zuǒmiàn | left | Box diagram: dot to the left |
| 3 | 右面 | yòumiàn | right | Box diagram: dot to the right |

These are the Extra Words from textbook p.128 Ex.8. Keep all six diagrams in the same visual style so the only thing that changes between slides is where the dot sits.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我的房间在客厅**上面**。 — My room is above the living room.
2. 厨房在卧室**下面**。 — The kitchen is below the bedroom.
3. 我的房间在**上面**，厨房在**下面**。 — My room is above, the kitchen is below.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 在 ________ 上面。／________ 在 ________ 下面。
**Extension —** Use two rooms neither of which is your bedroom, and add which floor each is on: 我家的餐厅在厨房下面，餐厅在一楼，厨房在二楼。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我在房子**里面**。 — I'm inside the house.
2. 我家的车在房子**外面**。 — Our car is outside the house.
3. 妈妈在房子**里面**，爸爸在房子**外面**。 — Mum's inside, Dad's outside.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** ________ 在房子里面，________ 在房子外面。
**Extension —** Say it about three people, and give each of them something they're doing — 我妈妈在厨房里面做饭，我弟弟在客厅里面看电视，我爸爸在外面。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 厨房在客厅**左面**。 — The kitchen is to the left of the living room.
2. 餐厅在客厅**右面**。 — The dining room is to the right of the living room.
3. 客厅的**左面**是厨房，**右面**是餐厅。 — To the left of the living room is the kitchen, to the right is the dining room.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** ________ 在 ________ 左面。／________ 在 ________ 右面。
**Extension —** Describe your own bedroom's position relative to two other rooms, using 左面 and 右面 in one sentence, and then say the same thing the other way round (if A is left of B, what is B of A?).

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 上面 · 下面 · 里面 · 外面 · 左面 · 右面 — characters only. Class reads back, then a second pass where you say the English and they say the Chinese.

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**The textbook's own NOTE, p.124** — put it on the board word for word:
> 在 indicates where a person or thing is: 我的卧室在楼上。
> 有 means "there is / are": 楼上有三间卧室。

Now extend both with the position words:

1. 电视**在**客厅里面。 — thing + 在 + place + 面
2. 客厅里面**有**电视。 — place + 面 + 有 + thing
3. 我的书房**在**卧室右面。
4. *(hardest)* 我的卧室**在**楼上，卧室左面**是**浴室，右面**有**一个小书房。 — three clauses, and the middle one uses 是 rather than 在, which is what native speakers do when the place comes first.

Make the reversal explicit: sentences 1 and 2 contain the same facts. The difference is which end you start from.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- Gesture response: "I'll say a position word — point to where it is in this room. 上面…外面…左面…"
- Whiteboards: "在 or 有? 客厅里面 ____ 电视。"
- Whiteboards: "在 or 有? 电视 ____ 客厅里面。"
- Quick translate: "*inside*? *outside*? *underneath*?"
- Error correction: "我的书在桌子 — what's missing?" (上面 — the position word can't be dropped)
- Make a sentence: "Look at my desk. One sentence with 上面, one with 下面. Whiteboards, 60 seconds."

#### Text / Dialogue
No new text today. The p.124 NOTE box and the p.128 example sentence carry the lesson:

> 蔬菜在餐桌上面。
> *Shūcài zài cānzhuō shàngmiàn.*
> The vegetables are on the dining table.

(餐桌 is shown for recognition only — it's formally taught in Lesson 14.)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: TPR, then describe the room — We Do (8 min)
Start with the objects. Put a book on a chair, under a chair, inside a bag, outside the door. Each time: 书在哪儿？ — whole class answers chorally. Then hand the book to a student and give the instruction in Chinese only: 把书放在椅子下面。Four or five rounds, getting faster.

Then **textbook p.124 Ex.3** on the projector — the cluttered room. Work through it as a class, one sentence at a time by cold call, using the book's Extra Words as recognition support only (沙发 · 椅子 · 书架 · 杯子 · 牙刷 · 相框 · 咖啡桌 — written up with English, not to be memorised today).

> 电视机在地上。 *(the book's example)*
> 书在书架里面。
> 杯子在咖啡桌上面。

Summary board with the six position words stays on screen throughout.

**Extension —** The picture has more than a dozen objects in it. Describe it as a continuous paragraph rather than a list — at least six sentences, each using a different position word, no repeats, and at least two joined with 还有 or 可是. Then close your eyes and have a partner ask you 相框在哪儿？ 牙刷呢？ from memory.

#### Activity 2: Nine pictures — You Do (10 min)
Printed copies of **textbook p.128 Ex.8**, one each. Nine pictures, each with a sentence to complete:

> 蔬菜在 __餐桌上面__ 。 *(example given)*
> 牛仔裤在 ________ 。
> 汽车在 ________ 。
> 厨房在 ________ 。
> 水彩画儿在 ________ 。
> 书房在 ________ 。
> 校车在 ________ 。
> 杂志在 ________ 。
> 花园在 ________ 。

Silent independent writing, then compare with a neighbour and take three aloud.

Then **Workbook p.151 Ex.14** — 钢琴上面有 ________ 。钢琴的下面有 ________ 。— which flips the pattern to place-first + 有, so students have to switch structures mid-task. That switch is the point of pairing the two exercises.

Then **Workbook p.147 Ex.7** as a two-minute speed finish — write the opposite of each: 大→小 · 高 · 黑 · 短 · 这 · 热 · 买 · 对 · 左 · 东 · 北 · 多. Four of the twelve are position-related (左, 东, 北, 这), which is why it sits here rather than anywhere else in the sequence — and the Unit 5 test asks for exactly this format.

**Extension —** Do Ex.8 without the printed sheet — from the projected pictures only — and write each answer *twice*, once with 在 and once with 有 (汽车在车库里面。／车库里面有汽车。). Then go straight to **Workbook p.151 Ex.15**, translating the six English sentences into Chinese, with no word bank.

#### Game: 老师说 Simon Says, position edition (5 min)
Instructions in Chinese only, whole class standing:
> 老师说：把手放在头上面。把书放在桌子下面。老师说：站在椅子右面。

Anything not preceded by 老师说 and they sit down. Three or four minutes, the pace increasing.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing — students use whatever is on their desk
- **Harder version:** The last three standing take over as 老师 in turn and must give five instructions each, in Chinese, without repeating a position word.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read them; ask which of the six words is hardest to remember and why (usually 里面 vs 外面).
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards, 👍 / 😐 / 👎 per criterion, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** *Write one sentence about this classroom using a position word, then rewrite the same fact starting from the place instead of the thing.* (e.g. 我的书在桌子上面。／桌子上面有我的书。)
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we go outside the house altogether — the garden, the garage, and what kind of house you live in."
