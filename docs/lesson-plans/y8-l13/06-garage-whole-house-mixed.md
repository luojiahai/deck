# The Garage, and Your Whole House 停两辆车

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 House 房子 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe a whole house — inside and out — in a presentation to the class.

## Success Criteria
- I can say how many cars a garage holds (车库可以停两辆车)
- I can use the right measure word for rooms, storeys and cars (间、个、层、辆)
- I can present a description of a house to the class for a minute, without reading it out

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides) — textbook p.125 Ex.5, p.129 Ex.9 and p.131 Ex.12 displayed
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed blank house-design sheets (A4, blank rectangle for a section drawing plus ruled lines beneath) — one per student
- Printed word cards for the p.131 Ex.12 categorisation race — one 25-word set per group of four, cut up
- Printed copies of the p.130 Ex.11 grid (楼上／楼下／房前／房后 × eight items) — one per student
- Audio: CD track 65 (textbook p.130 Ex.11)
- Workbook p.146 (Ex.3), p.152 (Ex.18), p.153 (Ex.20)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The whole lesson so far, as a listening task — this is the unit's best cumulative recap and it happens to be a textbook exercise.

**How:** **Textbook p.130 Ex.11, CD track 65.** Hand out the grid. Students tick which of 卧室 · 浴室 · 洗手间 · 客厅 · 厨房 · 车库 · 大花园 · 小花园 is 楼上, 楼下, 房前 or 房后.

Play twice. Mark it together by asking for the answer in a full sentence rather than a tick — 厨房在哪儿？→ 厨房在楼下。

The script mentions a 车库 at the end. Ask: "What is a 车库 *for*? You can't say it yet — by 20 minutes you will."

**Connection:** "That passage used almost every word from the last five lessons. Today you write and say one of your own — and you get the two words that finish Text 2."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Say explicitly that today's plenary looks back over all six lessons, not just this one — the self-assessment at the end covers the whole of Lesson 13.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 停 | tíng | to stop; (of cars) be parked | Line drawing: car stationary inside a garage, handbrake up |
| 1 | 辆（輛） | liàng | measure word for vehicles | Diagram: 一辆车 · 两辆车 · 三辆车, three cars in a row with the numbers above |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家的车库可以**停**两**辆**车。 — Our garage holds two cars. *(the last line of Text 2)*
2. 车**停**在房前。 — The car is parked in front of the house.
3. 我家有两**辆**车，都**停**在车库里面。 — We have two cars, both parked in the garage.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家有 ________ 辆车。车停在 ________ 。
**Extension —** Two clauses with a reason: say how many cars your family has, where each one parks, and why one of them isn't in the garage — 我家有三辆车，可是车库只可以停两辆，所以一辆停在房前。

**Recall slide:** 停 · 辆 — characters only, then immediately the **full eighteen-word summary board**: 房子 · 房间 · 层 · 楼 · 楼上 · 楼下 · 卧室 · 书房 · 浴室 · 洗澡 · 客厅 · 餐厅 · 厨房 · 洗手间 · 洋房 · 花园 · 车库 · 停 · 辆, characters and pictures only, no pinyin, no English. This board stays up for the entire rest of the lesson and is the students' only reference during the practice phase.

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**Measure words, all four together.** This is the last new grammar in the lesson and the Unit 5 test asks for it directly:

| Measure word | What it counts | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 间 | bedrooms, rooms you live in | 三**间**卧室 |
| 个 | most other rooms and spaces | 两**个**浴室、一**个**花园、一**个**车库 |
| 层 | storeys (counts itself — no extra word) | 房子有两**层** |
| 辆 | vehicles | 两**辆**车 |

Model four, the last one hardest:
1. 楼上有三**间**卧室。
2. 我家有两**个**浴室和一**个**书房。
3. 我家的房子有两**层**。
4. *(hardest)* 我家住洋房，房子有两**层**，楼上有四**间**卧室和两**个**浴室，房后有一**个**大花园和一**个**车库，车库可以停两**辆**车。 — every measure word in the unit, one sentence.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- Whiteboards: "间, 个, 层 or 辆? 我家有三 ____ 车。"
- Whiteboards: "间, 个, 层 or 辆? 楼下有一 ____ 卧室。"
- Error correction: "我家的房子有两个层 — what's wrong?" (层 counts itself)
- Quick translate: "*Our garage holds three cars.*"
- Cold call, ask then name: "Which two rooms in this unit take 间 rather than 个? … Ben."
- Thumbs up/down before the final read: "停 is a noun." (down — it's a verb)

#### Text / Dialogue
**Text 2 complete** — read the whole dialogue once more now that 停 and 辆 are known, without pinyin on screen. Pairs read it to each other, swapping roles, then close the book and do it again with only the four questions on the board.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Categorisation race — We Do (7 min)
**Textbook p.131 Ex.12.** Groups of four, one cut-up set of the 25 word cards each: 浴室 · 热狗 · 米饭 · 黄瓜 · 西红柿 · 土豆 · 香蕉 · 书房 · 炒菜 · 菜花儿 · 白粥 · 卧室 · 西瓜 · 游泳 · 弹钢琴 · 苹果 · 炒面 · 网球 · 橘子 · 汉堡包 · 跑步 · 厨房 · 唱歌 · 跳舞 · 比萨饼.

Groups sort them under headings on their desk — 房子, 蔬菜, 水果, 饭菜, 运动, 爱好 — and add three more words of their own to the 房子 pile from the summary board. Most correctly placed wins.

This is the sort-into-categories consolidation task: the 房子 pile is checked word by word out loud, and each group has to justify two placements in Chinese — 为什么把「浴室」放在「房子」？

**Straight after the race, on paper — Workbook p.146 Ex.3 and p.152 Ex.18.** Both are the same task in written form: a category word is given and students add three more of their own (客厅 ____ ____ ____ · 浴室 ____ ____ ____ · 苹果 · 橘子 · 跑步 · 炒菜 · 西红柿 · 冷开水 · 油画儿). Two minutes, straight into books; the house categories come from the pile they have just sorted.

**Extension —** Sort with the cards face down, turning one at a time, so there's no scanning ahead. Then, when finished, write a sentence for each category that uses three words from that pile — 我家的房子有浴室、卧室和厨房。— and one sentence that deliberately crosses two categories (我在厨房吃比萨饼).

#### Activity 2: Design your house — You Do (10 min)
**Textbook p.125 Ex.5.** Hand out the blank design sheets. The brief is the book's, and it is deliberately tight so that every word from the unit is needed:

Design a multi-storey house containing:
> 两间卧室 · 一个客房 · 一个浴室 · 一个洗手间 · 一个书房 · 一个客厅、一个餐厅 · 一个正门、一个后门

Add a 花园 and a 车库, and say how many cars the 车库 holds.

Draw the house in section on the top half of the sheet, label every room in characters, then write the description on the ruled lines beneath, starting with the book's opening:

> 这是我家的房子。我家的房子有两层。我家有一个正门和一个后门。楼上有……

The summary board is their only reference. Circulate; hand the word bank on paper to the two or three students who need it rather than putting it back on screen.

**Alternative for anyone who would rather describe than design:** **Workbook p.153 Ex.20** — a drawn three-storey house to describe instead of inventing one, opening line given (我家的房子有三层。). Same writing demand, less blank-page cost, and it lands the same success criteria.

**Extension —** Write it as a connected paragraph of at least eight sentences, not a labelled list — joined with 还有, 可是, 除了…还有… — and include one comparison between two rooms (房后的花园比房前的大) and one opinion with a reason (我特别喜欢我的房间，因为它在楼上). Then turn the sheet over and prepare to present it without looking at either the drawing or the text.

#### Presentation: Oral share-back (6 min)
**Textbook p.129 Ex.9** format. Four or five students present their house to the class, drawing held up, speaking for about a minute. Model the book's example once before the first one:

> 我家住洋房。我家的房子有两层。楼上有一间主人房，还有一个洗澡间。我的房间也在楼上。楼下有……我们家有一个前花园和一个车库。

After each presentation, two students from the class ask the presenter a question in Chinese — 你的卧室在几楼？车库可以停几辆车？

- **Harder version:** Present with nothing in your hands and nothing on screen, and take three questions instead of two — including one from the teacher that the presenter hasn't prepared for.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read today's three. Then put all six lessons' learning intentions on one slide and read down the list — this is the whole of Lesson 13.
- **Self-assessment:** Whiteboards, one rating per lesson across the whole sequence — six columns, 👍 / 😐 / 👎 each. Hold up together. Scan for the column with the most 😐; that's what the Lesson 14 review phase opens with.
- **Exit ticket:** *Write two sentences about your house: one using 有, one using 在, and between them use at least three of the eighteen words from this lesson.*
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we move into Lesson 14 — the furniture. You can name every room in a house; now you fill them."
