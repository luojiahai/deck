# Outside the House 洋房、花园、车库 — Text 2

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 House 房子 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to write about what kind of house we live in and what is outside it.

## Success Criteria
- I can name what is outside a house (洋房、花园、车库、房前、房后)
- I can answer 你家住什么样的房子？ in writing
- I can write the characters 洋、园、前、后、库 with the correct stroke order

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Everything from Lessons 1–4, read rather than spoken — and a first sight of today's opening word.

**How:** **Workbook p.148 Ex.9**, reading comprehension. The passage describes 天喜's house:

> 天喜家住**洋房**。他家的房子很大，有两层，还有一个地下室。二楼有三间卧室：爸爸妈妈的卧室、姐姐的卧室和天喜的卧室。除了卧室，楼上还有两个浴室和爸爸的书房。一楼有一间客房、一个大厨房、一个大客厅和一个洗手间。地下室里有天喜小时候的玩具和他的自行车。

Students read silently, then answer the book's four questions in Chinese: 天喜家有几间卧室？天喜的卧室在楼上还是在楼下？他家有书房吗？在几楼？厨房在几楼？

Gloss 地下室 (basement) and 客房 (guest room) on the board as recognition words. Leave **洋房** unglossed and ask the class what they think it means from the picture of the house — that guess is the hook into the first new word of the lesson.

Then two whiteboard items on Lesson 4's grammar before moving on:
1. 在 or 有? 卧室右面 ____ 一个浴室。
2. Rewrite starting from the place: 我的书在桌子上面。→ ?

**Connection:** "天喜 lives in a 洋房 — and in a minute you'll be able to say what that is, and whether you live in one. Today we walk out the front door, and the position words come with us: 房前, 房后."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Name today's shape honestly: "This is a writing lesson. You'll talk less than usual and write more, and the three success criteria are all things I'll be able to read on your page at the end."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 洋房 | yángfáng | western-style house | Line drawing: detached house with a pitched roof and a path |
| 1 | 花园 | huāyuán | garden | Line drawing: fenced plot with flowers and a tree |
| 2 | 前 | qián | front | Diagram: house with the area in front shaded, labelled 房前 |
| 2 | 后（後） | hòu | back | Diagram: same house, the area behind shaded, labelled 房后 |
| 3 | 车库 | chēkù | garage | Line drawing: open garage with a car inside |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家住**洋房**。 — We live in a western-style house. *(from Text 2)*
2. 我家有一个**花园**。 — We have a garden.
3. 我家住**洋房**，房子有一个大**花园**。 — We live in a western-style house with a big garden.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家住 ________ 。／我家有 ________ 花园。
**Extension —** Two clauses, and describe the garden rather than just naming it — size, and one thing that's in it, using a position word from Lesson 4: 我家住洋房，花园在房子外面，花园里面有很多花。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **房前**有一个花园。 — There's a garden in front of the house.
2. **房后**有一个大花园。 — There's a big garden behind the house.
3. 我家有两个花园，**房前**一个，**房后**一个。 — We have two gardens, one in front and one behind. *(from Text 2)*

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 房前有 ________ 。／房后有 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write the pair as a single sentence with a size contrast built in, the way the CD 65 speaker does: 房前的花园小，房后的花园大。Then add why you prefer one of them.

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我家有一个**车库**。 — We have a garage.
2. **车库**在房后。 — The garage is behind the house.
3. 我家有一个花园和一个**车库**，**车库**在房前。 — We have a garden and a garage; the garage is in front.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我家 ________ 车库。车库在 ________ 。
**Extension —** Three clauses with no frame: house type, garden position, garage position, joined so it reads as one description.

*(Three cycles, five words — Cycle 3 carries one new word on its own, so use the spare minute to recycle 前／后 inside its example sentences rather than introducing anything extra.)*

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 洋房 · 花园 · 前 · 后 · 车库 — characters only. Class reads back.

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**你家住什么样的房子？ — asking and answering what kind of house.**

1. 你家住什么样的房子？ → 我家住洋房。
2. 你家住什么样的房子？ → 我家住楼房。 *(the apartment-block answer, from the Unit Revision list)*
3. 你家有花园吗？ → 有两个，房前一个，房后一个。
4. *(hardest)* 我家住洋房，房子有两层。房前有一个小花园，房后有一个大花园和一个车库。 — four facts, two clauses joined, both new position phrases.

Write the question form up and leave it — it is the opening line of the Lesson 6 presentation and of the Unit 5 test's essay.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- Quick translate: "*garden*? *garage*? *western-style house*?"
- Whiteboards: "Write the character for *front*. Now *back*."
- Whiteboards: "房前 or 房后? *There's a car park behind the house.*"
- Thumbs up/down: "洋房 and 楼房 mean the same thing." (down — detached house vs apartment block)
- Error correction: "我家住在洋房 — the book says 我家住洋房. Which is in the text?"
- Pinyin ↔ hanzi check, spoken only, no writing: "I'll say 车库 — how many characters, and which one means *storage*?"

#### Text / Dialogue

**Text 2 (p.127, CD track 64)**

> A: 你家住什么样的房子？
> B: 我家住洋房。
> A: 你家有花园吗？
> B: 有两个，房前一个，房后一个。
> A: 你家有车库吗？
> B: 有。我家的车库可以停两辆车。
>
> *A: Nǐ jiā zhù shénme yàng de fángzi? B: Wǒ jiā zhù yángfáng. A: Nǐ jiā yǒu huāyuán ma? B: Yǒu liǎng ge, fáng qián yí ge, fáng hòu yí ge. A: Nǐ jiā yǒu chēkù ma? B: Yǒu. Wǒ jiā de chēkù kěyǐ tíng liǎng liàng chē.*

Play the track, students follow. Choral read. Then take the pinyin down and read once more.

Comprehension questions, answers in Chinese:
1. B 家住什么样的房子？
2. B 家有几个花园？在哪儿？
3. B 家的车库可以停几辆车？

Tell them the last line has two words they don't have yet — 停 and 辆. Those are Lesson 6, and that's deliberate: they'll meet them tomorrow and then the dialogue is complete.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Stroke order and the five characters — We Do (8 min)
**Workbook p.149 Ex.11.** Model each character on the board stroke by stroke, counting aloud in Chinese, students writing in the air with you before they write on paper:

- 洋 (9 strokes) — 氵 water on the left; point out it shares its radical with 浴 and 洗 from Lesson 2
- 园 (7 strokes) — the enclosure is written last but one; the 元 goes inside before the box closes
- 前 (9 strokes)
- 后 (6 strokes)
- 库 (7 strokes) — 广 on the outside, 车 inside; the 车 is a word they already know

Then the workbook rows, three of each. Circulate and check grip and stroke direction, not speed.

Summary board (the five new words, characters and pictures) stays on screen.

**Extension —** Finish the rows, then write each of the five characters inside a phrase rather than alone — 洋房、花园、房前、房后、车库 — and beside each, one more word you already know that shares a component with it (洋/浴/洗, 库/车, 园/国). Then write a four-sentence description of a house using all five characters, no summary board.

#### Activity 2: Rearrange, then type — You Do (11 min)
**Workbook p.152 Ex.17.** Six jumbled sentences; students write out the correct order in their books:

1. 住／我家／楼房。
2. 三间／有／楼上／卧室。
3. 二楼／在／我的房间。
4. 大花园／有／房后／一个。
5. 上海饭店／楼下／有／他家／一家。
6. 的／车库／右边／我家房子／在。

Then, as the workbook itself instructs, they type the six corrected sentences on their devices. Typing is not filler here — it forces them to produce the characters from pinyin rather than copying shapes, which is a different skill and the one the Unit 5 test's translation section actually needs.

**Extension —** After the six, write six more of your own to the same pattern — jumble them, type the jumbled version, and swap devices with a partner to solve. Each of yours must contain at least one word from today and one position word from Lesson 4, and at least two must be two-clause sentences.

#### Game: Writing Relay (4 min)
Teams of four, one board pen each, one word at a time. Teacher calls a word in English; runner one writes the first character, runs back, runner two writes the second, and so on. Words: 洋房 · 花园 · 车库 · 房前 · 房后 · 卧室 · 客厅 · 厨房.

Points for correct characters, not for speed — a team that finishes first with a wrong stroke order loses the point to the team behind it.

- **Setup needed:** Board space divided into team columns, one pen per team
- **Harder version:** The word is called in Chinese, not English, and the team must write a whole short sentence rather than a word — one character per runner, so the team has to agree the sentence before the first runner leaves.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** Read them. Criterion 3 is checkable on the spot — ask everyone to hold up the workbook page.
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each criterion, and one show of hands on "which character was hardest to write?"
- **Exit ticket:** In exercise books, collected — *Answer in Chinese: 你家住什么样的房子？你家有花园吗？* Two sentences minimum.
- **Preview:** "Next lesson: the two words missing from the end of Text 2, and then you build and present a house of your own."
