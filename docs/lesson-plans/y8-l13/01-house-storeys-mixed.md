# The House and Its Storeys 房子有几层

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 House 房子 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say how many storeys and rooms a house has, and where a room is.

## Success Criteria
- I can name the parts of a house I have learnt today (房子、房间、层、楼上、楼下)
- I can use 有 to say what a house contains (我家的房子有两层)
- I can use 在 to say where a room is (我的房间在楼上)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- Printed character cards for Slap the Character — 房子 · 房间 · 层 · 楼 · 楼上 · 楼下, two sets, A4 size
- Printed A5 blank "house section" sheets (an empty rectangle divided into two storeys) — one per student
- Workbook p.144 (Ex.1 character copying), p.146 (Ex.4 sentence completion, Ex.5 phrase grid)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Unit 4 language that this lesson immediately reuses — the 有 pattern with numbers and measure words (我每天吃两三种水果), plus numbers 一–十 and the Y7 daily-routine verbs (起床、睡觉、上课、放学、走路).

**How:** Mini-whiteboard quick quiz, five items, hold up after each:
1. 你每天吃几种水果？(any answer, must use 种)
2. Translate: *My family eats vegetables every day.* → 我们家每天吃蔬菜。
3. 应该 or 可是? 妈妈说我 ____ 每天吃蔬菜。
4. How do you say *three*? *five*? *ten*?
5. Write two of: 起床 · 睡觉 · 放学

**Connection:** "Last unit you used 有 and a number to say how many kinds of fruit your family eats. Today the same pattern counts something different — the storeys and rooms in your house. 我家的房子有两层。"

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display on the projector and leave up for the whole lesson. Read the WALT aloud, then have a student read the three success criteria. Point at the 有／在 pair in criteria 2 and 3 and say explicitly: "These two are the whole lesson. By the plenary you should know which one to reach for."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 房子 | fángzi | house | Simple line-drawn two-storey house |
| 1 | 房间 | fángjiān | room | Line drawing of an empty room, doorway and window |
| 2 | 层（層） | céng | storey; layer | Side-on house with two floors, each band highlighted |
| 2 | 楼（樓） | lóu | floor; multi-storey building | Tall apartment block with floors numbered 一楼 二楼 三楼 |
| 3 | 楼上 | lóushàng | upstairs | Same block, top floor shaded, arrow pointing up |
| 3 | 楼下 | lóuxià | downstairs | Same block, ground floor shaded, arrow pointing down |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家的**房子**很大。 — My house is big.
2. 我的**房间**不大。 — My room isn't big.
3. 我家的**房子**有五个**房间**。 — My house has five rooms.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家的房子 ________ 。／我的房间 ________ 。
**Extension —** Write both in one sentence and add a contrast with 可是: 我家的房子很大，可是我的房间不大。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我家的房子有两**层**。 — My house has two storeys. *(straight from Text 1)*
2. 我的房间在三**楼**。 — My room is on the third floor.
3. 这个房子有五**层**，我住在四**楼**。 — This house has five storeys; I live on the fourth floor.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家的房子有 ________ 层。我的房间在 ________ 楼。
**Extension —** Say it about someone else and add a comparison: 我家的房子有两层，我朋友家的房子有三层。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我的房间在**楼上**。 — My room is upstairs. *(from Text 1)*
2. 我妈妈在**楼下**。 — My mum is downstairs.
3. 我在**楼上**，我妈妈在**楼下**。 — I'm upstairs, my mum is downstairs.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** ________ 在楼上，________ 在楼下。
**Extension —** Three clauses, no frame on screen: say where you are, where one family member is, and how many storeys the house has.

**Recall slide after cycle 3:** 房子 · 房间 · 层 · 楼 · 楼上 · 楼下 — characters only, no pinyin, no English. Class reads them back chorally. If this stalls, reteach the pair that stalled before moving on.

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**有 = "there is / there are" · 在 = "is located at"** (the textbook's own NOTE, p.124)

Model on the board, thinking aloud each time:

1. 我家的房子**有**两层。 — "I'm saying what exists in the house, so 有."
2. 我的房间**在**楼上。 — "I'm saying where a room I already mentioned is, so 在."
3. 楼上**有**三个房间。 — "Place first, then 有, then the thing."
4. *(hardest)* 我家的房子**有**三层，我的房间**在**三楼，我爸爸妈妈的房间**在**二楼。 — three clauses, both patterns, in one breath.

Write the two skeletons up and leave them there:
> place + 有 + thing
> thing + 在 + place

#### CFU Checkpoints
- "Thumbs up if 层 means *storey*, thumbs down if it means *room*."
- Whiteboards: "有 or 在? 我的房间 ____ 楼上。"
- Whiteboards: "有 or 在? 楼下 ____ 两个房间。"
- Error correction, read aloud: "我的房间有楼上 — what's wrong with this?"
- Cold call, ask then name: "How would I say *my house has three storeys*? … Priya."
- Quick translate: "How do you say *downstairs*? And *upstairs*?"

#### Text / Dialogue
Not yet — Text 1 is read in full in Lesson 3, once all its room words are taught. Today only its opening sentence is on screen, as the model for Cycle 2:

> 我家的房子有两层。
> *Wǒ jiā de fángzi yǒu liǎng céng.*
> My house has two storeys.

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Build your house section — We Do (9 min)
Hand out the blank A5 house-section sheets. On the board, build one together first: draw a house in section, mark the two storeys, label 楼上 and 楼下, write a number of 房间 on each floor, and mark 我的房间 with an arrow.

Then students do their own, and write **three** sentences underneath — the instruction is tight so every word is needed:
> Your house must have at least two 层. Label 楼上 and 楼下. Write one sentence with 有, one with 在, and one that says how many 房间.

Summary board stays on screen throughout: the six words, characters and pictures only.

Take three students' sentences aloud while their sheets are still out.

**Extension —** Write it as a connected paragraph of five sentences, not three separate ones, joined so it reads as one text. Include one 可是 contrast (我家的房子很大，可是我的房间不大) and describe *someone else's* room as well as your own. No frames, no summary board — turn the sheet over and write from memory.

#### Activity 2: 有 or 在 — sort and build — You Do (9 min)
Twelve sentences on the board with the verb blanked. Students copy the number and write 有 or 在 on whiteboards, then hold up. Run it fast, six at a time.

1. 我的房间 ____ 楼上。
2. 我家的房子 ____ 两层。
3. 楼下 ____ 三个房间。
4. 我 ____ 楼下。
5. 这个房子 ____ 五层。
6. 我妈妈的房间 ____ 二楼。

Then, without the board, each student writes three sentences of their own about their house — at least one with 有 and one with 在.

Then **Workbook p.146 Ex.4** — five sentences with the ending missing, the first of which is today's pattern exactly: 我家的房子有两层，我的房间 ________ 。Students complete all five; the last two reach back into Unit 4 territory (每次吃饭我们大概 ________ ) and are fair game.

Finish with **Workbook p.146 Ex.5** (combine two characters from the grid into a phrase). Today only the phrases they can read: 楼上 · 楼下 · 房间 · 起床 · 睡觉 · 上课 · 下课 · 放学 · 走路. Tell them the grid holds more house words they'll unlock in the next two lessons — 客厅, 厨房, 卧室, 书房, 浴室, 洗手间 — and they finish the exercise in Lesson 3.

**Extension —** Instead of the fill-in, write six sentences where 有 and 在 appear in the *same* sentence (楼上有三个房间，我的房间在楼上). Then find the three sentences on the board that could be rewritten the other way round and write both versions.

#### Game: Slap the Character (5 min)
Two sets of the six character cards on the board, two students at a time facing them. Teacher says the word in Chinese (not English) — first to slap it wins the point and stays on. Run through all six twice, then start saying short sentences instead of single words, so students have to hear the word inside a sentence: 我的房间在**楼上**。

- **Setup needed:** Two printed A4 sets of the six words, blu-tack
- **Harder version:** The slapper must say a full sentence using the word they slapped before the point counts, and it can't repeat a sentence anyone has already used.

#### Character writing: Workbook p.144 Ex.1 (last 3 min of the block)
Stroke-order rows for the four characters this lesson actually used: 房 (8) · 层 (7) · 楼 (13) · 间 (7). Model each on the board counting strokes aloud, students write three of each. The remaining characters in the exercise (卧 室 浴 洗 澡 厅 厨) are left blank today and completed in Lessons 2 and 3 as those words are taught.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads the three criteria aloud from the screen. Ask the class: "Did we get all three?"
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each criterion in turn — 👍 / 😐 / 👎. Note who signals 😐 on the 有／在 criterion; that's Lesson 2's starting point.
- **Exit ticket:** On a slip, collected at the door — *Translate: My house has three storeys. My room is upstairs.* (我家的房子有三层。我的房间在楼上。)
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we go upstairs and name the rooms that are up there — and you'll learn why 卧室 takes a different counting word from 房间."
