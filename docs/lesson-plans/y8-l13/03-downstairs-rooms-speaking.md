# Rooms Downstairs 楼下有什么 — Text 1 Complete

**Year Level:** Year 8
**Unit:** Unit 5 — House 房子 · Furniture 家具 · Neighbourhood 社区
**Lesson:** Lesson 13 House 房子 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the rooms downstairs and describe a whole house out loud.

## Success Criteria
- I can name the downstairs rooms (客厅、餐厅、厨房、洗手间)
- I can say what someone is doing in a room (他在厨房吃饭)
- I can describe a whole house, upstairs and downstairs, without reading from a script

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides) — textbook p.123 Ex.2 cutaway-house picture displayed large
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Printed role-play cards for the house tour (8 different house briefs, A5) — one per pair
- Workbook pp.144–146 (Ex.1 character copying, Ex.2 room matching, Ex.5 phrase grid), p.150 (Ex.12 describe the picture)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 2's upstairs rooms and the 间／个 measure-word split.

**How:** Pair dialogue from memory, then swap roles. Put the frame up for thirty seconds, then take it down:
> A: 你家有几间卧室？ B: 我家有 ____ 间卧室。
> A: 你家有几个浴室？ B: 有 ____ 个。
> A: 你家有书房吗？ B: 有，在楼上。／没有。

Circulate and listen for 个卧室. Then one round of cold calls with the frame gone.

**Connection:** "You've furnished the upstairs. Today you go down the stairs — and once you have these four words you can read the whole of Text 1, which is what the rest of this unit is built on."

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Display and leave up. Flag criterion 3 as the one that matters most today: "By 43 minutes you will be talking about a house for a minute without looking at anything."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 客厅 | kètīng | living room | Line drawing: sofa, television, lamp |
| 1 | 餐厅 | cāntīng | dining room | Line drawing: table with four chairs, bowls |
| 2 | 厨房 | chúfáng | kitchen | Line drawing: stove, pan, sink |
| 2 | 洗手间 | xǐshǒujiān | toilet | Line drawing: toilet and small basin |

Point out the shared 厅 in 客厅 and 餐厅 on the Cycle 1 slide — one character, two halls. And the shared 洗 between today's 洗手间 and yesterday's 洗澡.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我家的**客厅**很大。 — Our living room is big.
2. 我们在**餐厅**吃饭。 — We eat in the dining room.
3. 楼下有**客厅**和**餐厅**。 — Downstairs there's a living room and a dining room.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我家的客厅 ________ 。／我们在 ________ 吃饭。
**Extension —** Two clauses, and use 不算大 or 不大也不小 from the Lesson 2 listening rather than 很大: 我家的客厅不算大，可是餐厅很大。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我家的**厨房**不大。 — Our kitchen isn't big.
2. **洗手间**在楼下。 — The toilet is downstairs.
3. 楼下有**厨房**和**洗手间**。 — Downstairs there's a kitchen and a toilet. *(from Text 1)*

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我家的厨房 ________ 。／洗手间在 ________ 。
**Extension —** One sentence containing all four of today's words in the right order, and say which floor each is on.

**Recall slide after cycle 2:** 客厅 · 餐厅 · 厨房 · 洗手间 — characters only. Then a second slide with all thirteen room words from Lessons 1–3, characters and pictures only, no pinyin. This one stays up for the rest of the lesson.

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice — see below)

#### Key Sentence Pattern

**在 + place — saying what someone is doing where.** This is the pattern behind p.123 Ex.2, and the book's own example uses 里:

1. 他**在房间里**打电话。 — He's making a phone call in his room. *(the textbook's example, p.123)*
2. 她**在厨房**吃饭。 — She's eating in the kitchen.
3. 我妈妈**在客厅**看电视。 — My mum is watching TV in the living room.
4. *(hardest)* 我在**楼上的卧室**看书，我弟弟在**楼下的客厅**看电视。 — two clauses, both with a floor *and* a room.

Note for the class: 在房间里 and 在房间 both work here. The full set of position words — 里面、上面、外面 — arrives next lesson; today 里 is enough.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- Quick translate: "How do you say *kitchen*? *living room*? *dining room*?"
- Thumbs up/down: "洗手间 and 浴室 are the same room." (down — toilet vs bathroom; the book distinguishes them)
- Whiteboards: "Write the character the two hall-words share." (厅)
- Make a sentence: "Someone is cooking. Where are they and what are they doing? Whiteboards."
- Error correction: "我在吃饭厨房 — what's wrong with the word order?" (在厨房吃饭 — place before the verb)
- Cold call after the choral read: "Where is the study, upstairs or downstairs? … Maya. In Chinese."

#### Text / Dialogue

**Text 1 (p.122, CD track 61)** — the whole passage, now fully readable:

> 我家的房子有两层。楼上有三间卧室、一个书房和一个浴室(洗澡间)，楼下有客厅、餐厅、厨房和洗手间。我的房间在楼上。我特别喜欢我的房间。
>
> *Wǒ jiā de fángzi yǒu liǎng céng. Lóushàng yǒu sān jiān wòshì, yí ge shūfáng hé yí ge yùshì (xǐzǎojiān), lóuxià yǒu kètīng, cāntīng, chúfáng hé xǐshǒujiān. Wǒ de fángjiān zài lóushàng. Wǒ tèbié xǐhuan wǒ de fángjiān.*

Play the track, students follow. Read together chorally. Then take the pinyin off the screen and read it once more.

Comprehension questions, Chinese answers expected:
1. 这个房子有几层？
2. 楼下有几个房间？说一说。
3. 说话的人的房间在楼上还是楼下？
4. 他喜欢他的房间吗？你怎么知道？ *(pushes them to 特别喜欢)*

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: One sentence per person — We Do (8 min)
Textbook **p.123 Ex.2** on the projector, large: the cutaway house with nine people in nine rooms.

Model the book's example first — 他在房间里打电话 — then work across the picture as a class, taking one sentence per person by cold call, ask-then-name. When someone gives a bare 他在厨房, push once: 他在厨房做什么？

Then pairs, one more full pass through all nine, alternating who speaks. Circulate; the thirteen-word summary board stays on screen as their reference.

**Extension —** Go round the picture once more without looking at it — from memory, name all nine people's rooms and activities in order. Then link them into a paragraph with 一边…一边… or 还有, so it reads as one description of a household rather than nine separate sentences. Finish by answering in Chinese: 这家人一共有几个人？你怎么知道？

#### Activity 2: House tour role-play — You Do (10 min)
Hand each pair a role-play card with a house brief on it — number of storeys, which rooms are on which floor, one unusual detail (no dining room; two kitchens; the study is downstairs).

A is showing B around their house; B has never been there and wants to know where everything is. B must ask about at least five rooms.

> B: 你家的房子有几层？ A: 有两层。
> B: 厨房在楼上还是楼下？ A: 在楼下。
> B: 客厅大吗？ A: 不算大，可是也不小。

Swap cards and run it again with the roles reversed.

**Then, standing, mingling — textbook p.126 Ex.6.** The seven survey questions go on the board; students move around the room and must get answers from four different classmates, writing each answer down:

> 1. 你家的房子有几层？ 2. 你家有几间卧室？ 3. 客厅在几楼？ 4. 厨房在楼上还是楼下？
> 5. 你家有书房吗？有几个？ 6. 你的卧室在几楼？ 7. 你爸爸、妈妈的卧室大还是你的卧室大？

**To close the pair work — textbook p.130 Ex.10.** Back in pairs, one more short dialogue, this time arranging a visit to the house they have just described. The model is the book's, and the language (可以／不可以 plus time words) is Year 7 revision put to a new purpose:

> A: 我今晚可以去你家吗？ B: 对不起，我今晚不在家。
> A: 我明天上午去，好吗？ B: 不可以，我上午要去买东西。
> A: 周末去，可以吗？ B: 可以，你周末来吧，我在家等你。

B must refuse at least three times with a different reason each time before agreeing.

**Extension —** A's card is taken away before they start, so they're describing a house they have to hold in their head. B is not a visitor but a buyer who doesn't want the house — B must object to two things (这个房子没有书房吗？厨房太小了) and A must talk them round, giving a reason each time with 可是 or 因为. At the end B reports back to the class, in Chinese, on whether they'd buy it and why.

#### Game: 20 Questions — guess the room (5 min)
One student thinks of a room from the thirteen-word board. The class asks yes/no questions in Chinese only — 这个房间在楼上吗？你在这个房间吃饭吗？这个房间大吗？ — up to twenty, until someone guesses 是不是厨房？

Run three or four rounds. Any English question costs the class one of its twenty.

- **Setup needed:** Nothing beyond the summary board on screen
- **Harder version:** The student who guesses correctly takes over as the answerer, and the class gets only ten questions instead of twenty — so the questions have to be efficient, not random.

#### Character writing and workbook close (last 4 min of the block)
**Workbook pp.144–145 Ex.1, finished** — the last two characters, 厅 (4 strokes) and 厨 (12). With those done, every character in the exercise is written.

**Workbook p.145 Ex.2** is now fully answerable (all seven rooms), and **p.146 Ex.5** can be completed — 客厅 · 厨房 · 卧室 · 书房 · 浴室 · 洗手间 · 餐厅 · 楼上 · 楼下 · 房间 all now unlock, alongside the daily-routine phrases from Lesson 1.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** A student reads them. Ask specifically about criterion 3: "Who talked about a house for a whole minute with nothing in front of them?"
- **Self-assessment:** Thumbs on each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** Spoken, at the door, one per student — name one room that is 楼上 in your house and one that is 楼下, in a full sentence.
- **Written follow-up if the room runs quick:** **Workbook p.150 Ex.12** — the four-storey apartment block with eight residents. Same pattern as p.123 Ex.2 but on paper and with a floor number attached: 小明住在四楼。他正在看电视。大力也住在 ________ 。
- **Preview:** "Next lesson we stop naming rooms and start saying where things are inside them — above, below, inside, outside, left and right."
