# 烦人的邻居 What You Can Hear

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbours 邻居 | Lesson 2 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to write about the noises we hear from our neighbours, and to say how loudly something is done using V + 得 + adjective.

## Success Criteria
- I can say what I hear, using 听见 + a clause (我听见有人在房间里跑步)
- I can say how something is done (电视机开得很响 / 他跑得很快)
- I can use 半夜 and 哭 in a written sentence
- I can write four sentences about a noisy night without looking at the board

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio CD 58 (p.146). If the audio isn't available, the teacher reads the six-item script from the unit reference
- Blank 3×3 bingo grids, one per student (students fill them in themselves)
- Word pool printed or projected for the bingo fill-in: 听见 响 半夜 哭 跑步 电视机 狗叫 鸟叫 跳舞 踢球 看电影 邻居

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 1's four words — 最近 搬家 邻居 烦人 — and the 最近……有没有……？ question.
**How:** Written, since today is a writing lesson.
Round 1 (3 min) — exercise books. Four English prompts on the board; students write the Chinese sentence for each: *we moved recently* · *the neighbours upstairs are annoying* · *my neighbour is Chinese* · *have you moved recently?*
Round 2 (3 min) — pairs swap books and check each other's against the board, then one pair reads a corrected sentence aloud.
Two minutes: cold call three students with a 最近……有没有……？ question, answered aloud.
**Connection:** 上一课你们说了邻居很烦人。可是——到底烦在哪儿？今天我们写下来：你半夜听见了什么。 Last lesson the neighbours were annoying. Today we write down exactly what you can hear.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Read criterion 2 twice and write 得 large on the board beside it — flag now that this character is *not* 的, and that today every sentence about *how* something is done needs it.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 听见（聽見） | tīngjiàn | hear | SVG — an ear with three curved sound lines arriving at it |
| 1 | 响（響） | xiǎng | loud; noisy | SVG — a television with three widening arcs coming out of the speaker |
| 2 | 半夜 | bànyè | midnight | SVG — a clock face at twelve, a crescent moon and two stars beside it |
| 2 | 哭 | kū | cry | SVG — a child's face, mouth open, two tears |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes worth making:
- 听 is *listen*, 听见 is *hear* — the 见 is the result. You can 听 and not 听见. Same shape as 看 / 看见, which they already have.
- 响 is what a sound *is*, not how you make it. The television 开得很响; you don't 响 it.
- 半夜 is built from 半 (*half*) + 夜 (*night*) — the halfway point of the night. They know 半 from 半个小时 and 五点半.
- 哭 is a picture: two eyes over a mouth, tears falling. Worth putting on the board stroke by stroke.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我**听见**有人在房间里跑步。 — I hear someone running in the room.
2. 他们的电视机开得很**响**。 — Their television is on very loud.
3. 我**听见**楼上的电视机开得很**响**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我听见 ________ 。 · ________ 开得很响。
**Extension —** 用「一……就……」写一句：我一回家就听见…… 。Then add the time and who it was: 晚上十点，楼上的邻居…… 。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. **半夜**我还听见孩子**哭**。 — At midnight I can still hear a child crying.
2. 我弟弟每天**半夜哭**。 — My little brother cries every midnight.
3. **半夜**的时候，我听见楼下的小孩子**哭**得很响。 — both words, with 得.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 半夜我听见 ________ 。 · ________ 哭得很 ________ 。
**Extension —** 两个小句，用「因为……所以……」：因为楼上的小孩子半夜哭，所以…… 。Then say what you did about it.

*(Four words, two cycles — straight to the summary board, no separate recall slide.)*

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice)
**Summary board:** 听见 · 响 · 半夜 · 哭, characters and pictures only, stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — V + 得 + adjective
Write the frame on the board: **动词 + 得 + 很 + 形容词**
Think aloud through the first one: "要说 the television is on loud，我不能说 *电视机很响开*。动词先说——开，然后 得，然后 很响：电视机开得很响。"
Model four, the last one hardest:
1. 他跑得很快。
2. 小孩子哭得很响。
3. 楼上的邻居半夜跑步跑得很响。 (verb repeated after the object — 跑步跑得)
4. 我听见楼上的孩子哭得很响，可是我不知道他为什么哭。

Flag the two errors to avoid before they happen: 得 is not 的, and the adjective needs 很 (or 太／非常) in front of it — *他跑得快 on its own sounds unfinished.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「听」和「听见」有什么不一样？(cold call, answer in Chinese or English)
- 白板：写「电视机开得很响」。Hold up — teacher scans specifically for 的 written instead of 得.
- 错在哪儿？「他很快跑得。」 (whiteboards — write the corrected version)
- 「半夜」是几点？(thumbs up if it's around 12, thumbs down if it's the evening)
- 快速翻译：*I hear a child crying* → ？ (whiteboards, full sentence)

#### Text 1 — complete (p.142, CD 57)
Characters only, no pinyin. The class has seen the first two sentences already.
> 我们家最近搬家了。新房子很好，可是楼上的邻居很烦人。我早上五点就听见有人在房间里跑步。晚上十二点，他们的电视机还开得很响。每天半夜还听见孩子哭。

Read aloud, class reads chorally, then three comprehension questions in Chinese:
- 他早上五点听见了什么？
- 晚上十二点，楼上在做什么？
- 课文里，哪一句用了「得」？请读出来。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 半夜，我听见…… — We Do (7 min)
On screen: a cutaway apartment block at midnight, five lit windows, something different happening in each (a dog, a television, a child, someone running, someone dancing).
Exercise books, 5 minutes. Students write **four sentences** describing the building, and the instruction on the board is tight: 每一句都要用「听见」，四句里要用上今天的四个词，至少两句要用「得」。
Pairs swap books and check the 得 sentences against each other. Two students read theirs aloud with the summary board still up.

**Extension —** Write it as one connected paragraph from the point of view of the person trying to sleep on the ground floor — five or more sentences, joined with 可是／因为……所以／一……就, every one of today's words used, and at least three 得 phrases with *different* adjectives (响, 快, 好). End with one sentence saying what you are going to do about it tomorrow. Nothing on screen to copy from.

#### Activity 2: 听写 — Listen and Write (8 min) — You Do
CD 58 (p.146), six items, each played twice. This is the textbook's tick-a-b-c exercise turned into writing.
- First pass: for each item, students write **in Chinese** what the noise is (two or three characters is enough — the animal, the object or the action).
- Second pass: students pick any two items and write the whole complaint as a full sentence with 得: 小明家的邻居每天晚上看电视，电视机开得很响。
Teacher circulates during the second pass. Three students read a sentence aloud at the end.

**Extension —** After the second pass, write a reply for each of the two items as the *neighbour* — one sentence apologising and one saying what you'll change: 对不起，我以后…… 。Then write one item of your own in the same format as the recording, which the teacher may read out to the class as a seventh item.

#### Game: Bingo (8 min)
Students draw their own 3×3 grid and fill it with any nine words from the projected pool of twelve — filling the grid is itself the writing practice, and the teacher should insist on characters, not pinyin.
The teacher does not call single words. She calls a **sentence** containing one of them (每天半夜，楼上的小孩子哭得很响), and students cross off the word it contains. First to three in a row calls 中了 and reads back the three words.
- **Setup needed:** blank grids, the word pool on screen
- **Harder version:** winners must read back their three words *and* make one sentence containing all three before the line counts. If they can't, play continues and they may try again on their next line.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** teacher reads each one, class gives the Chinese example that matches it.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time. Criterion 2 (得) is the one to watch — if a third of the class is 😐, reteach the frame at the start of Lesson 3.
- **Exit ticket:** 用「得」写一句话，说你的邻居半夜做什么。One sentence, exercise book, shown at the door.
- **Preview:** 下一课：邻居把你吵醒了——我们学一个新的句子「把」。
