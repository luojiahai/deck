# 我们家最近搬家了 Moving In

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbours 邻居 | Lesson 1 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say that we have recently moved house, and to describe what our neighbours are like.

## Success Criteria
- I can say we moved recently (我们家最近搬家了)
- I can name the rooms of a house (书房 阳台 浴室 卧室 厨房 客厅 餐厅)
- I can ask and answer 最近……有没有……？
- I can say whether my neighbours are annoying (楼上的邻居很烦人)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers, one per student
- A4 copies of the textbook p.143 cutaway house, one per pair
- Card set for Slap the Character: 最近 搬家 邻居 烦人 房子 阳台 卧室 厨房 客厅 餐厅 浴室 书房 楼上 楼下 隔壁 附近 (A4, large characters)
- Blu-tack
- No audio this lesson; devices not needed until Lesson 5

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** The close of the Lesson 14 sequence — 被 with result complements (被偷走了／被骑走了), and the journey language 坐几路车／坐几站／在哪站下车.
**How:** Two rounds, 90 seconds each.
Round 1 — mini whiteboards. Teacher reads four English sentences of the *my bike was taken* type; students write the Chinese. Four items.
Round 2 — pair talk, 30 seconds each way: 从你家怎么去学校？坐几路车？
Then one cold call for a 被 sentence said aloud.
**Connection:** 上一课我们一直在路上——坐车、走路、找地方。今天我们回家，说说家里边和住在我们旁边的人。 Last lesson everyone was out on the road. Today we come home and talk about who lives next door.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible all lesson. Read criterion 1 aloud and point out 了 at the end of 搬家了 — the move already happened. Ask the class to watch for that 了 in Text 1.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 最近 | zuìjìn | recently | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |
| 1 | 搬家 | bānjiā | move house | SVG — a removal van at the kerb, three boxes on the path, a house behind |
| 2 | 邻居（鄰） | línjū | neighbour | SVG — two houses side by side sharing a fence, a figure waving over it |
| 2 | 烦人（煩） | fánrén | annoying | — (a quality; leave the space empty) |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes worth making:
- 搬家 is two characters doing one job — 搬 is *to move (something)*, 家 is *home*. You can 搬 a table without 搬家.
- 邻居 splits cleanly: 邻 is *neighbouring*, 居 is *to live*. They have met 居 nowhere yet, but they know 住 — same idea, more formal.
- 烦人 = 烦 (*to annoy*) + 人. Literally *annoys people*. It describes the noisy neighbour, not the person being annoyed.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我**最近**很忙。 — I've been busy recently.
2. 我们家**搬家**了。 — We've moved house.
3. 我们家**最近搬家**了，新家离学校很近。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我最近 ________ 。 · 我们家 ________ 搬家。
**Extension —** 用「因为……所以……」写两个小句：我们家最近搬家了，因为……所以…… 。没搬过家的同学，说说你的朋友或者你的邻居。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 楼上的**邻居**很**烦人**。 — The neighbours upstairs are annoying.
2. 我的**邻居**是中国人。 — My neighbour is Chinese.
3. 隔壁的**邻居**不**烦人**，可是楼上的**邻居**很**烦人**。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我的邻居 ________ 。 · ________ 的邻居很烦人。
**Extension —** 用「虽然……但是……」写一句：虽然我的邻居很烦人，但是…… 。Then add who exactly — 楼上 / 楼下 / 隔壁 — in the same sentence.

*(Four words, two cycles — go straight to the summary board; no separate recall slide needed.)*

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice)
**Summary board:** 最近 · 搬家 · 邻居 · 烦人, characters and pictures only, stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 最近……有没有……？ (p.144, Act 3)
Model four, the last one hardest:
1. 你最近有没有搬家？
2. 你最近有没有去新的地方？
3. 你最近有没有见到你的邻居？他们怎么样？
4. 你最近有没有住过很烦人的邻居的旁边？为什么烦人？

Point out that the answer reuses the verb: 有，我们上个月搬家了。/ 没有，我们没搬家。

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「最近」说的是过去还是将来？过去的比大拇指朝上。(thumbs)
- 白板：写「我们家最近搬家了」。(hold up)
- 错在哪儿？「我搬家最近了。」 (word order — time word before the verb)
- 「邻居」里边，哪个字是「住」的意思？(cold call)
- 快速翻译：annoying → ？ recently → ？

#### Text 1 — first half (p.142, CD 57)
Characters only, no pinyin.
> 我们家最近搬家了。新房子很好，可是楼上的邻居很烦人。

Read aloud, class reads chorally, then two comprehension questions in Chinese:
- 他们家什么时候搬家的？
- 新房子好不好？为什么他还是不高兴？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 介绍我的新家 — We Do (8 min)
Pairs, one A4 cutaway house (textbook p.143) between them. The scenario is on the board: 你们家最近搬家了，这是你的新房子。
Each student describes the house to their partner using **every one of the four new words** plus at least four room names: 这是我的新家。我们家最近搬家了。楼下有客厅和厨房，楼上有三个卧室…… 我的邻居…… 。
Teacher circulates, then two students describe theirs to the class while the summary board is still up.

**Extension —** Describe the house **and** the people in it as one connected paragraph of five or more sentences: who moved, when, which room is whose, which neighbour is 烦人 and what they do that makes them so. Every clause joined — 因为……所以……, 可是, 虽然……但是…… — and no looking at the summary board. Then swap: your partner asks you three 最近……有没有……？ questions about the move and you answer live.

#### Activity 2: 我的邻居 — You Do (8 min)
Exercise books, individually, 6 minutes. Four sentences about the student's own neighbours, one per frame on the board:
- 我住在 ________ 。
- 我家 ________ 的邻居是 ________ 。
- 我的邻居 ________ 烦人。
- 我最近有没有见到我的邻居：________ 。
Hand the word bank (楼上 楼下 隔壁 对面 附近) to the pairs who need it rather than projecting it — the rest write from memory. Teacher circulates and takes three aloud at the end.

**Extension —** Write it as a short paragraph instead of four separate sentences, and include one neighbour you *like* and one who is 烦人, with a reason for each. Finish with a question you would ask a new neighbour on the first day. No frames, no word bank.

#### Game: Slap the Character (7 min)
Sixteen cards blu-tacked to the board — the four new words plus the room and position vocabulary. Two students at the board; teacher says the word in English; first to slap it wins the point; loser sits, next student up.
- **Setup needed:** printed cards + blu-tack
- **Harder version:** the teacher stops saying the English. Instead she says a sentence with the word missing (楼上的 ________ 很烦人) or a definition in Chinese (住在我们旁边的人), and the players slap the word that fits.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student volunteer reads all four aloud.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time, held up together.
- **Exit ticket:** 用「最近」写一句话，说你们家或者你的邻居。One sentence, on the whiteboard, shown at the door.
- **Preview:** 下一课我们说说邻居到底做了什么——你听见了什么声音。
