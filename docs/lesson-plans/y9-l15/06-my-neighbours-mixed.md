# 我的邻居 Describing the Whole Block

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbours 邻居 | Lesson 6 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to describe everyone who lives in a block of flats, to suggest what to do about a difficult neighbour, and to write a paragraph introducing our own neighbours.

## Success Criteria
- I can say who lives on which floor (三零一住着一家人 / 楼上是……)
- I can suggest a solution (我会想办法 / 我会跟他说…… / 如果……，我应该……)
- I can write a paragraph about my neighbours — how many people, where they're from, what they do at weekends, what I think of them
- I can use 把, 被 and 得 correctly inside that paragraph

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- A4 copies of the textbook p.152 Act 15 apartment block (301–303 / 201–203 / 101–103), one per pair
- Workbook p.170 Ex 16 想想办法 — the five situations, on screen or printed
- Blank A4 paper and coloured pens for the Act 16 extension
- Student devices for Kahoot; quiz prepared in advance covering all fourteen Lesson 15 words plus 把／被 word order, 能不能……？ and V+得
- The Unit 5 Revision question list (workbook p.175) on the teacher's copy for the plenary

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Not just Lesson 5 — this review reaches back across the whole sequence, because it is the last lesson before the unit test.
**How:** Three fast rounds on whiteboards, 2 minutes each.
Round 1 — six words from anywhere in Lessons 1–5, English to characters: *recently · move house · annoying · midnight · wake up · way/method*.
Round 2 — one sentence each with 把, with 被, and with 得. Teacher gives the situation in English, students write the Chinese.
Round 3 — pair talk, 30 seconds each way: 你的邻居烦人吗？为什么？
**Connection:** 这六课我们从搬家开始，说了声音、「把」字句、有礼貌的请求。今天把它们放在一起，写一段完整的话。 Six lessons, from the removal van to the polite knock on the door. Today they all go into one paragraph.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Say plainly that criterion 3 is the exact task on the last page of the unit test, and that everything done today is practice for it in the same format.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### No new vocabulary today
The full Lesson 15 set stays on screen as a summary board for the whole lesson — characters only, no pinyin, no English:
最近 · 搬家 · 邻居 · 烦人 · 听见 · 响 · 半夜 · 哭 · 吵 · 醒 · 声 · 注意 · 办法 · 把

#### Modelling the paragraph — 我的邻居
Build one on the board with the class, thinking aloud about the order rather than the words. Four moves, written up as a skeleton the students keep:
1. **谁** — 我家隔壁住着一家人。／我们楼上是一对老夫妻。
2. **几口人，哪国人** — 他们家有四口人，是从中国来的。
3. **平时做什么** — 他们平时很忙，周末常常在花园里种花。
4. **我觉得** — 我觉得他们很好，可是他们的狗很吵，半夜常常把我吵醒。

Model the finished paragraph aloud once at full speed, then point at each of the four moves in turn. Flag that the last move is where 把, 被 and 得 belong — that is how a Year 9 paragraph gets above the sentence-by-sentence level.

#### Key Structures Recycled
- **住着 / 楼上是……** — who is where
- **如果……，我应该／我会……** — the shape every 想想办法 answer takes
- **把 / 被 / V得** — the three graded structures of the unit; at least one of each in the final paragraph

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「三零一」是几楼？(cold call)
- 白板：写「他们的狗半夜把我吵醒了」。 Teacher scans for word order and the final 了.
- 错在哪儿？「我的自行车把哥哥骑走了。」 (whiteboards — the 把/被 swap, the sequence's likeliest error)
- 快速翻译：*My neighbour's television is on too loud* → ？ (whiteboards, full sentence)
- 说一说：四个部分，第三个部分是什么？(cold call on the paragraph skeleton)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 这栋楼住着谁？ — We Do (6 min)
Textbook p.152 Act 15, the nine-flat block. One A4 sheet per pair.
4 minutes: pairs invent the residents of all nine flats and describe the block to each other — 三零一住着一家人，他们有两个孩子…… . The instruction on the board keeps it tight: 九家都要说到，至少三家要说他们正在干什么，至少一家很烦人.
2 minutes: two pairs describe three flats each to the class.

**Extension —** Textbook p.153 Act 16 in compressed form: on blank A4, draw the layout of *one* of the nine flats and list what is in each room, then present it to another pair in Chinese without notes — rooms, furniture, who sleeps where, and one thing about that flat that annoys the neighbours below. (As a full project this needs more time than this sequence has; it works well as a standalone follow-up if the teacher wants one.)

#### Activity 2: 想想办法 — We Do → You Do (6 min)
Workbook p.170 Ex 16, the five situations. Pairs talk first, then each student writes their own answer for three of the five.
Every answer takes the shape 如果……，我会…… or 我会想办法…… , and at least one must end in a 能不能……？ request said directly to the neighbour.
Teacher circulates, then takes one answer for each of three situations aloud — deliberately choosing three different students' solutions to the same problem where that happens.

**Extension —** Answer all five, and for two of them write what you would do if your first idea *didn't work* — a second attempt, escalating politely (第一次我会……，如果他还是不注意，我会……). One of the five must be answered entirely without 我 — reporting what someone else should do, using 你应该…… or 他应该…… .

#### Activity 3: 我的邻居 — You Do (7 min)
Exercise books, individually and silently. The paragraph, in the four-move skeleton from the I Do, about the student's own neighbours: how many people, where they are from, what they do at weekends, and what the student thinks of them.
Length: eight sentences or more. The requirement on the board: 一定要用「把」、「被」、「得」各一次.
Teacher circulates, reading over shoulders and pointing at the skeleton rather than supplying words. Collect the books at the end of the lesson.

**Extension —** Write about two neighbours instead of one and compare them across the paragraph — 楼上的……，可是隔壁的…… — using 虽然……但是…… at least once. Finish with a sentence saying which of the two you would rather live next to, and why. No skeleton on screen, written from memory.

#### Game: Kahoot (4 min)
Whole-sequence quiz, twelve questions, prepared in advance:
- six vocabulary items, characters to English and English to characters
- three word-order questions — pick the correct 把 sentence from four options, the same for 被, and one 能不能……？
- two V+得 questions — pick the correct completion of 电视机开得____
- one on the phrase bank — 邻居跟你说「对不起」，你说什么？
Run it straight through; spend no more than a minute total on discussing wrong answers, and note which two questions the class split on.
- **Setup needed:** quiz built beforehand, student devices
- **Harder version:** for the three word-order questions, the top scorers must also say aloud *why* the other three options are wrong before the point stands.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
This plenary looks back over all six lessons, not just today.
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** today's four, read by a volunteer — then the teacher puts up the six learning intentions from the whole sequence and asks the class which one they would most want to go over again.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards against the sequence as a whole, one structure at a time — 把, 被, V+得, 能不能……？. Whichever of the four draws the most 😐 is the one to open the next lesson with.
- **Exit ticket:** four of the Unit 5 Revision oral questions (workbook p.175), asked aloud at the door, one per student, answered in Chinese: 你们家的邻居怎么样？他们烦人吗？· 你家隔壁邻居的电视会开得很响吗？· 你们最近会搬家吗？· 你被偷过吗？
- **Preview:** 第十五课学完了。下一次我们把第五单元的三课放在一起复习，准备考试。
