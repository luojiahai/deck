# 能不能小点儿声？ Complaining Politely

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbours 邻居 | Lesson 4 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to ask a neighbour politely to change something, and to answer a complaint — either by agreeing to be careful, or by saying there's nothing we can do.

## Success Criteria
- I can make a polite request with 能不能……？ (能不能小点儿声？)
- I can ask for more or less of something with 一点儿 (请说慢一点儿 / 把声音开得小一点儿)
- I can promise to be careful (我以后注意)
- I can say there's nothing I can do (对不起，我没有办法)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio CD 60 (p.151). If unavailable, the teacher reads the six-item script from the unit reference
- Printed role-play cards — the four p.150 Act 11 situations, one card per pair, plus the four p.151 Act 14 situations held back for the extension
- Textbook open at p.149 for Act 10

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 3's 把 sentence, plus 吵 醒 声.
**How:** Three rounds.
Round 1 (3 min) — mini whiteboards. Teacher reads four sentences in English of the *my brother took my phone* type; students write the Chinese with 把.
Round 2 (2 min) — the same four, rewritten with 被. Pairs check each other.
Round 3 (2 min) — the word-order chant 谁—把—什么—动词—了, then one cold call: 昨天晚上什么把你吵醒了？
**Connection:** 上一课，邻居把你吵醒了，你很生气。可是生气没有用——你要去敲门，跟他们说话。怎么说才有礼貌？ Last lesson you got woken up. Today you knock on the door — politely.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Read criterion 1 and point out that 能不能 already contains both halves of the question, so no 吗 at the end — a common slip. Say once that criteria 3 and 4 are the *two* possible replies, and that today every complaint will get one or the other.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 注意 | zhùyì | pay attention; be careful | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |
| 1 | 办法（辦） | bànfǎ | way; method | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes worth making:
- 注意 is what you promise *after* someone complains. 我以后注意 = from now on I'll watch it. It is not "I will notice".
- 办法 pairs with 有 and 没有 far more often than with anything else: 有办法 / 没有办法. 想办法 (*think of a way*) comes back in Lesson 6.
- 法 on its own is *method* — they will meet it again in 方法 and 语法.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 好，我以后**注意**。 — All right, I'll be careful from now on.
2. 对不起，我没有**办法**。 — Sorry, there's nothing I can do.
3. 我会**注意**的，可是小孩子哭，我真的没有**办法**。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 好，我以后注意 ________ 。 · ________ ，我没有办法。
**Extension —** Write a three-line exchange: a complaint, a reply using 注意, then a second complaint that the same person genuinely *can't* fix, answered with 没有办法 and a reason (因为……所以……).

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs inside Activity 1)
Only two new words today, so the consolidation rides on the listening: every written reply in Activity 1 must use 注意 or 办法.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 能不能……？ and Adj/V得 + 一点儿
Two frames on the board, one under the other:
**能不能 + 动词 + 一点儿／小点儿声？**
**动词 + 得 + 形容词 + 一点儿**
Think aloud on the second: "昨天我们说『电视机开得很响』。今天我不是说它有多响，我是要它*改变*——开得小一点儿。所以「很」换成「一点儿」。"
Model four, the last one hardest:
1. 请说慢一点儿。
2. 能不能小点儿声？
3. 能不能把电视机开得小一点儿？ (把 from last lesson, carried straight in)
4. 我知道你们在看球赛，可是已经半夜了，能不能小点儿声？我明天要考试。

Flag the two slips before they happen: no 吗 after 能不能, and 一点儿 goes *after* the adjective (快一点儿), never before.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「能不能……？」后边要不要加「吗」？(thumbs up for yes, down for no)
- 白板：写「能不能把声音开得小一点儿？」 Teacher scans for a stray 吗 and for 得 vs 的.
- 错在哪儿？「请你一点儿快说。」 (whiteboards — write the fix)
- 邻居说「对不起」，你怎么回答？(cold call — 没关系)
- 快速翻译：*I'll be careful from now on* → ？ *There's nothing I can do* → ？

#### Text 2 — complete (p.147, CD 59)
Characters only. Four pairs of lines; the class has the first pair from Lesson 3. Two students take the roles, then swap, then the whole class reads the downstairs part chorally while the teacher reads upstairs.
> 我就住在楼下。每天早上有人在房间里跑步，对吗？— 对。
> 你把我吵醒了。— 对不起。我以后晚一点儿跑。
> 每天晚上，你们的电视机开得很响。请问能不能小点儿声？— 好，我以后注意。
> 每天半夜，我还听见小孩子哭。— 对不起，小孩子哭，我没有办法。

Three comprehension questions in Chinese:
- 楼下的人一共提了几件事？
- 哪一件事楼上的人没有办法解决？为什么？
- 课文里哪一句是有礼貌的请求？请读出来。

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 听 CD 60 — We Do (7 min)
Six short exchanges, each played twice.
- First pass: for each item, students write **what the problem is** — two or three characters (狗叫／跳舞／电视太响／踢球).
- Second pass: for three items of their choosing, students write the **request** as a full 能不能……？ sentence, and the **reply** using 注意 or 办法.
Pairs compare their three, then two students read a complete request-and-reply pair aloud.

**Extension —** Write all six, and make each reply different — one agrees with 注意, one says 没有办法 with a reason, one offers an alternative (我以后在外面……), one promises a time (我以后十点以后不……). Then write a seventh exchange of your own, set in your building, and be ready to read both parts aloud.

#### Activity 2: ……一点儿 — You Do (7 min)
Textbook p.149 Act 10 — the ten sentences to complete with 快一点儿／大一点儿／高一点儿 and the rest. Exercise books, individually, 5 minutes.
On the board for anyone who finishes, three more from p.146 Act 6: write one sentence each using **多……点儿**, **如果……应该……**, and **……的时候**.
Teacher circulates; three sentences read aloud at the end, one from each of the three extra structures if anyone got there.

**Extension —** Once the ten are done, take four of them and turn each into a polite request to a real person — a neighbour, a teacher, a little brother — using 能不能. Then write two sentences using the remaining p.146 structures (**在……过**, **从小就**, **不用**), both about living in a block of flats.

#### Game: Role-play Cards (9 min)
Textbook p.150 Act 11, the four situations: 音乐开得很响 · 半夜有人在房间里唱歌 · 小孩儿在房间里踢球 · 两个男孩儿在楼上打乒乓球.
Pairs get one card, 2 minutes to prepare, then perform to another pair. Each performance must contain a complaint, a 能不能……？ request, and a reply using 注意 or 办法. Swap cards and run it a second time with the roles reversed.
Two pairs perform to the whole class at the end; the class votes on which reply was the most polite.
- **Setup needed:** four printed cards, enough for one per pair
- **Harder version:** hand these pairs the p.151 Act 14 borrowing situations instead — the neighbour borrowed something and hasn't given it back. No reference sentences, nothing written down beforehand, and the complainer must get to the request without ever saying 对不起 themselves. The neighbour's job is to refuse the first request politely and only agree to the second.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** teacher reads each one; the class supplies the Chinese sentence that matches.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** 你的邻居半夜开派对。用「能不能」写一句有礼貌的话。One sentence, whiteboard, shown at the door.
- **Preview:** 下一课：他们正在干什么呢？我们说说楼里每个人现在在做什么，还有借东西不还的邻居。
