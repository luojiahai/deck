# 他们正在干什么呢？ In the Building Right Now

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbours 邻居 | Lesson 5 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what people are doing right now, to thank people and accept apologies properly, and to ask a neighbour to give something back.

## Success Criteria
- I can say what someone is doing at this moment (他们正在看电视呢)
- I can use the right phrase for the moment (谢谢你提醒我 / 不用客气 / 没关系)
- I can join sentences with 因为……所以……, 可是, 平时, 通常, 经常
- I can ask for something back politely (能不能把我的……还给我？)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- A4 copies of the textbook p.149 Act 9 picture set (11 pictures), one per pair
- A4 copies of the textbook p.145 Act 4 picture set (7 activities) with the connector word bank printed underneath, one per student
- Printed 借东西 cards — the four p.151 Act 14 situations, one per pair
- Student devices (optional — Quizlet Live as the warm-down if the role play finishes early)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 4's 能不能……？ requests, V得 + adj + 一点儿, and 注意 / 办法.
**How:** Three rounds.
Round 1 (3 min) — whiteboards. Teacher describes a problem in Chinese (你的同学说话太快了); students write the polite request. Four problems.
Round 2 (2 min) — the same four, each given a reply, out loud in pairs: one uses 注意, one uses 没有办法.
Round 3 (2 min) — cold call two students: 你的邻居半夜开派对，你会怎么说？
**Connection:** 你们已经会敲门、会说「能不能」了。可是敲门以前，你得知道他们正在做什么。今天我们说「现在」。 Before you knock, you need to know what they're actually doing. Today: right now.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Point at criterion 1 and underline both ends — 正在 at the front, 呢 at the back. Say once that the 呢 is optional in writing but makes the sentence sound natural aloud, and that today we keep it.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### No new vocabulary today
This lesson consolidates the twelve words from Lessons 1–4 and puts them into longer stretches of speech. The I Do time goes to one pattern and one phrase bank instead of vocabulary cycles.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 正在 + 动词 + 呢
Frame on the board: **谁 + 正在 + 动词（+ 宾语）+ 呢**
Think aloud: "「他看电视」是他平时看。「他正在看电视呢」是——现在，这个时候，我打电话给他，他不能接。"
Model four, the last one hardest:
1. 他正在吃饭呢。
2. 楼上的邻居正在跳舞呢。
3. 我打电话的时候，他们正在吵架呢。
4. 昨天半夜我醒了，楼上的小孩子正在踢球呢，所以我没有办法再睡。

Contrast pair to leave on the board all lesson:
- 我每天晚上看电视。 (平时／习惯)
- 我正在看电视呢。 (现在)

#### Phrase Bank — 谢谢你提醒我 (p.150, Act 12)
Seven set phrases, all previously met, collected here as one bank on screen:
谢谢你 · 非常感谢 · 不用客气 · 没关系 · 对不起 · 请注意 · 谢谢你提醒我
Drill chorally twice, then run the four p.150 picture dialogues as a whole class: teacher reads the first line of each, class supplies the phrase that fits. Take two different answers where more than one phrase works and ask which sounds better and why.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「他看电视」和「他正在看电视呢」有什么不一样？(cold call, answer in Chinese)
- 白板：写「楼上的邻居正在跳舞呢」。
- 错在哪儿？「他正在呢跳舞。」 (whiteboards — write the fix)
- 我说「谢谢你」，你说什么？(choral — 不用客气)
- 我踩了你的脚，我说「对不起」，你说什么？(choral — 没关系)
- 快速翻译：*Thanks for reminding me* → ？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 他们正在干什么呢？ — We Do (7 min)
Textbook p.149 Act 9, the eleven pictures. A4 sheet per pair; each student writes in their own book.
Written first, 4 minutes: one 正在……呢 sentence per picture, as many of the eleven as they can get through. Then 3 minutes aloud — partner points at a picture at random, you answer without looking at what you wrote.
Two students answer three random pictures in front of the class.

**Extension —** Write the eleven as a single sweep through one apartment block at 11 p.m. — 一楼的邻居正在……呢，二楼的……, joined so it reads as one text, and with three of the sentences saying whether the noise reaches you (我听见……／我没听见……). Then close it with what you are going to do about the worst one, using 把 or 能不能.

#### Activity 2: 平时和现在 — You Do (8 min)
Textbook p.145 Act 4, the seven activity pictures, with the connector word bank underneath: 可是／但是 · 因为 · 所以 · 特别喜欢 · 非常不喜欢 · 最喜欢 · 平时 · 通常 · 经常／常常 · 很会.
Exercise books, 6 minutes. For each picture, two or three sentences — one saying what the person usually does (平时／通常／经常), one saying what they are doing right now (正在……呢), and at least four of the connectors used across the whole set.
Teacher circulates. Three students read one picture's worth aloud at the end.

**Extension —** Instead of writing about all seven separately, choose three pictures and write one connected paragraph of six or more sentences about a single neighbour who does all three — what they usually do, what they are doing now, what you think of it (特别喜欢／非常不喜欢) and why, using 因为……所以…… twice and 虽然……但是…… once. Word bank turned face down.

#### Game: 借东西 Role Play (8 min)
Textbook p.151 Act 14, the four borrowing situations — a neighbour borrowed something and hasn't returned it. One card per pair, 2 minutes to prepare, then perform to another pair; swap cards and run it again with roles reversed.
The rules that make it a game: the borrower must **refuse or deflect the first request** (我正在用呢 / 我找不到了 / 对不起，我忘了), and only give in on the second. The owner has to get the thing back without ever losing their temper — every turn must contain a polite form from the phrase bank.
Two pairs perform to the class; the class votes for the most patient owner and the most inventive excuse.
- **Setup needed:** four printed situation cards
- **Harder version:** the borrower has genuinely broken the item and must say so, apologise, and offer a solution — 对不起，我把你的……弄坏了，我会…… . The owner must accept the apology with 没关系 and then negotiate what happens next. No preparation time, performed cold.

*If the role play runs short:* Quizlet Live on the twelve words from Lessons 1–4 (最近 搬家 邻居 烦人 听见 响 半夜 哭 吵 醒 声 办法), two rounds.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a volunteer reads each; the class supplies a matching Chinese sentence for criteria 1 and 4.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** 现在是晚上十一点。写一句话，说你楼上的邻居正在干什么呢。One sentence, whiteboard, shown at the door.
- **Preview:** 下一课是最后一课：我们画一栋楼，说说每一家住的是谁——然后写一段「我的邻居」。
