# 把我吵醒了 The 把 Sentence

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Unit 5 — Neighbourhood, Asking the Way, Neighbours 社区、问路、邻居
**Lesson:** Lesson 15 Neighbours 邻居 | Lesson 3 of 6
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what someone did to something, using the 把 sentence — and to see how 把 and 被 tell the same story from opposite ends.

## Success Criteria
- I can build a 把 sentence in the right order (谁 + 把 + 什么 + 动词 + 了)
- I can say someone woke me up (弟弟的哭声把我吵醒了)
- I can use 吵, 醒 and 声 in a sentence
- I can turn a 被 sentence into a 把 sentence and back

## Materials Needed
- Projector (slides)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Printed A5 slips of the textbook p.148 Act 7 sentences (7 items), one per pair
- Large word cards for the 把 word-order build: 哥哥 / 把 / 我的手机 / 借走 / 了 — two sets, plus blanks and a marker
- The p.148 Act 8 action-phrase list on the teacher's copy only (18 phrases)
- Blu-tack

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 2's 听见 响 半夜 哭 and the V + 得 + adjective frame — this is the one to check, since it was the wobble at the end of last lesson.
**How:** Mini whiteboards, three rounds of 90 seconds.
Round 1 — teacher says a plain sentence (他跑步), students write it again with 得 and an adjective (他跑得很快). Four items.
Round 2 — teacher shows one wrong sentence on screen; students write the fix. (的 for 得, and adjective before the verb.)
Round 3 — pair talk, 30 seconds: 半夜你听见过什么？
**Connection:** 你们已经会说「我听见小孩子哭」。可是那个哭声做了什么？它把你吵醒了。今天学「把」。 You can already say you *heard* it. Today: what that noise *did to you*.

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and leave visible. Point at criterion 1 and read the word order aloud as a chant — 谁，把，什么，动词，了 — twice, class joins in the second time. That chant is the lesson's backbone; come back to it every time a sentence goes wrong.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 吵 | chǎo | noisy; make a noise | SVG — two figures face to face, mouths open, three jagged lines between them |
| 1 | 醒 | xǐng | wake up | SVG — a figure sitting up in bed, eyes open, an alarm clock ringing on the table |
| 2 | 声（聲） | shēng | sound | SVG — a loudspeaker with three widening arcs |
| 2 | 把 | bǎ | (marks what the action is done to) | — (function word; leave the space empty) |

Model each twice, class repeats twice. Notes worth making:
- 吵 has 口 on the left — it comes out of the mouth. It covers both *noisy* (这里很吵) and *to quarrel* (他们在吵).
- 吵醒 is the two-verb chunk they actually need: *noise* + *wake* = wake someone up by being noisy. Teach it as one block.
- 声 and 响 are a pair worth separating now: 声 is the sound itself (a noun), 响 is how loud it is (an adjective). 电视机的声音很响.
- 把 is not a word you translate. It is a signpost telling the listener that the next thing said is what got acted on. Say that plainly and move on to the frame.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 楼上很**吵**。 — It's noisy upstairs.
2. 我半夜**醒**了。 — I woke up at midnight.
3. 楼上太**吵**了，所以我半夜**醒**了。 — both words.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** ________ 很吵。 · 我 ________ 点醒了。
**Extension —** 用「一……就……」写一句：我一听见 ________ 就醒了。Then add why you couldn't get back to sleep, using 因为……所以…… 。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 电视机的**声**音太响了。 — The television is far too loud.
2. 请**把**电视机关上。 — Please turn the television off.
3. 请**把**电视机的**声**音开得小一点儿。 — both words.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 请把 ________ 关上。 · ________ 的声音太响了。
**Extension —** Write the same request twice — once as a 请把…… sentence and once as a plain sentence without 把 — then say which one sounds more like an instruction and why.

*(Four words, two cycles — straight to the summary board.)*

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task (runs as Activity 1 of Flexible Practice)
**Summary board:** 吵 · 醒 · 声 · 把, characters and the two pictures, stays on screen through Activity 1.

#### Key Sentence Pattern — 把 (p.148 NOTE)
Frame on the board, boxed: **谁 + 把 + 什么 + 动词 + 了／补语**
Build it physically first with the word cards on the board: 哥哥 · 把 · 我的手机 · 借走 · 了. Move the cards into the wrong order deliberately, ask the class to fix it, then leave the correct order up.
Model four, the last one hardest:
1. 弟弟把牛奶喝了。
2. 请把你的电话号码留下。
3. 弟弟的哭声把我吵醒了。
4. 楼上的邻居半夜跑步，把我们全家都吵醒了。

Two things the class must notice, both flagged now:
- The verb can never sit on its own at the end. Something has to follow it — 了, 走, 上, 醒, 下. *我把手机借 is not a sentence.
- 把 and 被 are the same event told from the two ends. Put the pair on the board side by side and leave it there for the rest of the lesson:
  - 哥哥**把**我的手机借走了。 (start from the person who did it)
  - 我的手机**被**哥哥借走了。 (start from the thing it happened to)

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「把」后边放什么——人还是东西？(cold call)
- 白板：写「弟弟的哭声把我吵醒了」。Teacher scans for a missing 了 and for 醒 written without the 酉 on the left.
- 错在哪儿？「我把吵醒了你。」 (whiteboards — write the corrected version)
- 把 → 被：「妈妈把蛋糕吃了」，用「被」怎么说？(whiteboards)
- 「吵」和「响」哪个是名词旁边用的？举手说一个例子。

#### Text 2 — first half (p.147, CD 59)
Characters only, no pinyin. Two students take the two roles.
> 我就住在楼下。每天早上有人在房间里跑步，对吗？
> 对。
> 你把我吵醒了。
> 对不起。我以后晚一点儿跑。

Read in role, swap roles, then two comprehension questions in Chinese:
- 说话的人住在几楼？
- 跑步的人说他以后会怎么做？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: 把它改成「把」字句 — We Do (7 min)
On screen, the five noises from Lesson 2's building (the dog, the television, the child, the runner, the dancer). Each is written up as a plain sentence: 楼上的小孩子半夜哭。
Pairs, whiteboards, 5 minutes. Rewrite each as a 把 sentence ending in 吵醒了: 楼上小孩子的哭声把我吵醒了。All four of today's words must appear across the five sentences.
Teacher circulates with the word-order chant ready for any pair that stalls. Two pairs read theirs out with the summary board still up.

**Extension —** Write each of the five *twice* — once with 把 and once with 被 — then write a sixth sentence about a noise nobody complained about, explaining with 因为……所以…… why that one was fine. No frames on screen, and the 被 version must keep the same tense marker.

#### Activity 2: 改写句子 — You Do (8 min)
Textbook p.148 Act 7 — the seven sentences to rewrite with 把. A5 slips, one per pair, but each student writes their own answers in their book. 6 minutes.
As students finish, the board carries three more from p.146 Act 6 for anyone who gets there: write one sentence each with **被**, with **一……就……**, and with **太……了**.
Teacher circulates and takes three sentences aloud at the end — one 把, one 被, one 一……就.

**Extension —** After the seven, take three of them and write the *neighbour's reply* to each, in the 把 form where it fits: 对不起，我马上把电视机关上。Then write two sentences about your own home in which something got done to something — one you did, one someone did to your things — using 把 for one and 被 for the other.

#### Game: 老师说 — TPR Elimination (8 min)
Textbook p.148 Act 8. Everyone stands. The teacher says an action phrase from the eighteen on her list; the class acts it out. Wrong action, or hesitating too long, and you sit down. Last three standing win.
Run the first eight phrases plainly. From phrase nine, the teacher switches to 把 sentences for the actions that take an object — 把书打开 · 把手机放下 · 把眼睛闭上 — so the game becomes a listening test on today's grammar.
- **Setup needed:** the phrase list on the teacher's copy; nothing printed for students
- **Harder version:** the last three standing take over as the caller, and every phrase they give must be a 把 sentence of their own making. A caller whose sentence has the wrong word order is out and hands the call to the next.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** class chants the word order 谁—把—什么—动词—了 once more, then a volunteer reads criterion 4 and gives the matching pair of sentences.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, one criterion at a time.
- **Exit ticket:** 用「把」写一句话，说昨天晚上什么声音把你吵醒了。One sentence, whiteboard, shown at the door.
- **Preview:** 下一课：你已经被吵醒了——现在怎么有礼貌地去跟邻居说？「能不能小点儿声？」
