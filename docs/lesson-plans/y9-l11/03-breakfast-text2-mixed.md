# 谷类、牛奶、面包、煎蛋、香肠 and 加

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Snacks 零食
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Snacks 零食 | Lesson 3 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what we eat for breakfast and to add one thing to another in Chinese.

## Success Criteria
- I can name six breakfast foods in Chinese (能说出「谷类早餐」「牛奶」「面包」「煎蛋」「香肠」)
- I can use 加 to combine two things: 谷类早餐加牛奶 (能用「加」把两样东西放在一起说)
- I can answer 你早饭吃什么？ with two or three sentences (能用中文回答「你早饭吃什么？」)

- Mini whiteboards + markers

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** 对……好／不好, 从小就, 总是, 正餐 from Lesson 2.
**How:** Pair dialogue from memory (3 min) — A asks 你从小就喜欢吃什么？ and 为什么妈妈说你应该多吃正餐？ B answers in two sentences, then swap. Then five whiteboard items (4 min): 写「牙齿」。 翻译：Soft drinks are bad for your teeth. 用「总是」说一句关于你妈妈的话。「我对足球一点儿都不感兴趣」是什么意思？ 「从小」后面要加哪个字？
**Connection:** 妈妈说应该多吃正餐。正餐里最重要的是哪一餐？早饭。今天我们学早饭吃什么。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Note that this lesson turns to Text 2, a dialogue about all three meals — today is breakfast, next lesson is lunch and dinner.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 谷类 | gǔlèi | cereal | SVG: a bowl of cereal flakes with a spoon |
| 1 | 牛奶 | niúnǎi | milk | SVG: a milk carton and a full glass |
| 2 | 面包 | miànbāo | bread | SVG: a sliced loaf and a bread roll |
| 2 | 煎蛋 | jiāndàn | fried egg | SVG: a fried egg, yolk up, in a pan |
| 3 | 香肠 | xiāngcháng | sausage | SVG: two linked sausages |
| 3 | 加 | jiā | add; plus | — (abstract) |

Component clues worth 20 seconds: 奶 has 女 · 肠 has 月 (flesh, as in 翅膀 from Lesson 10) · 煎 sits on 灬, the four-dot fire — 煎 is frying in a *little* oil, 炸 (Lesson 10) is deep-frying in a lot. Put 煎 and 炸 side by side.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我早饭常常吃**谷类**早餐。 — I often have cereal for breakfast.
2. 我每天都喝一杯**牛奶**。 — I drink a glass of milk every day.
3. 我早饭吃**谷类**早餐，喝**牛奶**。 — For breakfast I have cereal and milk.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我早饭吃 ________ 。 · 我每天喝 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「比」写一句，比较牛奶和汽水，说哪个对身体好。再用「妈妈总是对我说……」加一句。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我妈妈每天早上做**面包**。 — My mum makes bread every morning.
2. 我不太喜欢吃**煎蛋**。 — I don't much like fried eggs.
3. 今天早饭我吃了**面包**和**煎蛋**。 — This morning I had bread and a fried egg.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 今天早饭我吃了 ________ 。 · 我不太喜欢吃 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写三句说你昨天的早饭，可是不能用「吃」两次以上——用「喝」「有」换一换。最后一句用「因为……，所以……」说你为什么吃那个。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 我早饭喜欢吃**香肠**。 — I like sausages for breakfast.
2. 我的茶里要**加**牛奶。 — I want milk in my tea. *(lit. add milk)*
3. 我早饭吃谷类早餐**加**牛奶，还有**香肠**。 — For breakfast I have cereal with milk, and sausages too.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 我的茶里要加 ________ 。 · ________ 加 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「加」写两句，一句说吃的，一句说喝的。再写一句问同桌：你的茶里要不要加牛奶和糖？然后真的去问他，把答案写下来。

*(Recall slide after cycle 3: 谷类 · 牛奶 · 面包 · 煎蛋 · 香肠 · 加 — characters only.)*

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**A 加 B** — and the breakfast answer frame it sits inside.

1. 谷类早餐加牛奶
2. 茶里加牛奶和糖
3. 我早饭吃谷类早餐加牛奶。
4. 我早饭一般吃谷类早餐加牛奶，有时候吃面包、煎蛋和香肠。 *(hardest — the actual Text 2 line, two halves contrasted by 一般 vs 有时候)*

Hold on 一般 / 有时候 — both already known from Lesson 10. Say explicitly that this is the shape of a good answer: the usual thing first, the exception second.

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「煎」跟「炸」有什么不一样？ (little oil vs a lot)
- How do you say "cereal with milk"? Whiteboards.
- 这句话对不对？我早饭吃牛奶。 (drink, not eat — 喝牛奶)
- 「香肠」的「肠」是什么部首？ (月)

#### Text 2 — first half (p.105, CD track 43)
Play the whole track once so students hear all three meals, then work only the first exchange.

> A: 你早饭吃什么？
> B: 吃谷类早餐加牛奶，有时候吃面包、煎蛋和香肠。

Choral read, then pairs read it twice, swapping roles. Then close the book and do it from memory.

Comprehension in Chinese:
1. 他早饭一般吃什么？
2. 他有时候吃什么？
3. 他早饭喝什么？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — We Do (6 min)
**Summary board:** all six new words, characters + pictures, on screen throughout.

**Task:** 写一张三天的早饭表——星期一、星期二、星期三。三天加起来要用到黑板上**每一个**词，而且至少有一天要用「加」。写完以后用一句话说哪一天的早饭最好，为什么。 Solo, 4 minutes.

Model on the board:
星期一：谷类早餐加牛奶。星期二：面包、煎蛋和香肠。星期三：……

**Share:** three students read one day each; the class checks which words are still unused.

**Extension —** 不写表，写一段话。四到六句，三天都说到，每句都要连起来（用「星期一……，星期二……，可是星期三……」）。最后一句用「因为……，所以……」说哪一天的早饭对身体最好。不看总览屏幕。

#### Activity 2: Act. 8 picture talk — You Do (7 min)
Textbook p.106. Fourteen food pictures. Pairs, alternating. Two sentences per picture, on the printed model:
我喜欢吃薯条。我每天都吃薯条。

Push past the model: sentence two should vary — 我每天都吃…… / 我不太喜欢…… / 我妈妈常常做…… / 这个对牙齿不好。

Items 1–6 (面包, 牛奶, 沙拉, 香肠, 炸鸡翅, 酸奶) are today's and Lesson 10's; 沙拉 and 酸奶 are next lesson — let students say 我不知道这个中文怎么说 and move on. Do not pre-teach them.

**Extension —** 不是两句，是一个小对话：A 问一个问题（你喜欢吃……吗？为什么？），B 回答两句，第二句一定要用「因为……，所以……」或者「对……好／不好」。十四张图全部做完，中间不能停下来看屏幕。

#### Activity 3: WB 12 picture writing + WB 8 translation — You Do (5 min)
**First**, workbook p.123 Ex. 12, items 1–8 only (面条 given, then 面包, 水果, 香肠, 酸奶, 冰淇淋, 米饭, 煎蛋). Characters, not pinyin. Circulate and check stroke order on 煎 and 肠 — both are new and both are dense.

**Then**, workbook p.121 Ex. 8, items 1, 2 and 4 only — the three food ones:
1. Do not eat fast food too often. *(不要经常吃快餐。)*
2. You should eat more fruit. *(你应该多吃水果。)*
4. You should eat less meat and more vegetables. *(你应该少吃肉，多吃蔬菜。)*

不要 and 应该 came out of Lesson 2's Text 1 (不要吃太多零食，应该多吃正餐) — this is where they get produced rather than just recognised. The Practice Focus box on the page carries the models.

**Extension —** 图写完以后，把八样东西分成两组：早饭吃的 / 早饭不吃的，每组下面写一句话说为什么。翻译题做全部六句，包括第三、五、六句（天气、暑假、爷爷奶奶）——那三句不是说吃的，要用第二册的词。

#### Game: Running Dictation (5 min)
Four sentences taped to the walls, all built from today's words:

1. 我早饭吃谷类早餐加牛奶。
2. 有时候我吃面包、煎蛋和香肠。
3. 我的茶里要加牛奶，不要加糖。
4. 妈妈说早饭对身体很好。

Pairs: one runs and reads, the other writes. Swap after two sentences. First pair with all four correct wins — correct means characters, not pinyin.

- **Setup needed:** four printed sentences, large type, taped around the room
- **Harder version:** the runner may not say the sentence — they must *translate it into English* for the writer, who writes it back in Chinese. No looking.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** student volunteer reads; class answers 你早饭吃什么？chorally as one modelled sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs on each criterion.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：用「加」说你早饭吃什么、喝什么。
- **Preview:** 下节课说午饭和晚饭——沙拉、酸奶、杂菜汤，还要去问四个同学他们家吃什么。
