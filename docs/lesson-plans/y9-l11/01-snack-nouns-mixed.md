# 零食、巧克力、蛋糕 and 各种各样的……，比如……

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Snacks 零食
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Snacks 零食 | Lesson 1 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to name the snacks we eat and to list examples of them in Chinese.

## Success Criteria
- I can name six snack words in Chinese (能说出「零食」「巧克力」「蛋糕」「冰淇淋」「饼干」「各种各样」)
- I can use 各种各样的……，比如：A、B、C 等 to list examples (能用「各种各样……比如」举例子)
- I can say how much I like a snack using 非常／特别／挺／最／不太喜欢 (能用五个程度词说自己爱吃什么)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l11/l1-snack-nouns/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD track 41 (textbook p.100)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 10's canteen words — 糖果, 薯片, 薯条, 炸鸡翅, 盒饭, 咖喱, 猪排 — and 平时……，但是……
**How:** Flashcard rapid fire on the eight canteen words, characters only, class reads chorally (2 min). Then four whiteboard items (4 min): 写「炸鸡翅」。 翻译：Crisps are cheaper than a box lunch. 用「平时……，但是……」说一句今天的事。「不算太贵」是什么意思？ Hold up together after each.
**Connection:** 上一课我们学了学校餐厅卖的东西。今天我们从餐厅走到小卖部——学怎么说各种各样的零食。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project all three criteria and leave them on screen. Read criterion 2 aloud in Chinese first, then gloss it: "各种各样 is 各种 from last lesson, doubled — it's stronger. 比如 is how you open the list."

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 6 words in 3 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 零食 | língshí | snack | SVG: a small spread — wrapped sweet, crisp packet, biscuit |
| 1 | 巧克力 | qiǎokèlì | chocolate | SVG: a bar with two squares broken off |
| 2 | 蛋糕 | dàngāo | cake | SVG: round layer cake, one slice cut |
| 2 | 冰淇淋 | bīngqílín | ice cream | SVG: cone with two scoops |
| 3 | 饼干 | bǐnggān | biscuit; cracker | SVG: two round biscuits, one square cracker |
| 3 | 各种各样 | gèzhǒng gèyàng | all kinds of | — (abstract; leave the space empty) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我很喜欢吃**零食**。 — I really like eating snacks.
2. 这块**巧克力**很好吃。 — This chocolate is delicious.
3. 我最喜欢的**零食**是**巧克力**。 — My favourite snack is chocolate.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我喜欢吃 ________ 。 · ________ 很好吃。
**Extension —** 用「比」写一句：巧克力比薯片贵。然后加上原因：因为巧克力是进口的，所以比较贵。两句要连起来。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我妈妈会做**蛋糕**。 — My mum can make cakes.
2. 夏天我常常吃**冰淇淋**。 — In summer I often eat ice cream.
3. 我不太喜欢**蛋糕**，我最喜欢**冰淇淋**。 — I don't much like cake; ice cream is my favourite.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我常常吃 ________ 。 · 我不太喜欢 ________ 。
**Extension —** 用「比」写两句，比较冰淇淋和蛋糕：哪个好吃？哪个便宜？然后说你买哪个，为什么。不看屏幕。

**Cycle 3 — example sentences:**
1. 这家店卖**饼干**。 — This shop sells biscuits.
2. 这家店卖**各种各样**的零食。 — This shop sells all kinds of snacks.
3. 我妈妈买了**各种各样**的**饼干**。 — My mum bought all kinds of biscuits.

**Cycle 3 — write your own:** 这家店卖各种各样的 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写一句比较两家店：这家店的饼干比超市的便宜。再用「因为……，所以……」说你为什么去那家店买。

*(Recall slide after cycle 3: 零食 · 巧克力 · 蛋糕 · 冰淇淋 · 饼干 · 各种各样 — characters only, class reads back. If it stalls, reteach cycle 2 before moving on.)*

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**各种各样的 + N，比如：A、B、C 等。**

This is where 比如 (bǐrú, *for example*) is taught — it opens the list, 等 closes it.

1. 这家超市卖各种各样的零食，比如巧克力、糖果等。 *(textbook example, p.103)*
2. 我每天都吃各种各样的零食，比如：饼干、薯片等。
3. 这个菜市场卖各种各样的新鲜蔬菜，比如西红柿、黄瓜等。
4. 我们学校的小卖部卖各种各样的东西，比如三明治、盒饭、饮料等，可是那里的东西比外面贵。 *(hardest — two clauses, 比 carried over from Lesson 10)*

Also put up the five degree phrases from Act. 1 (p.101) and drill them as a ladder:
非常喜欢 → 特别喜欢 → 挺喜欢 → 最喜欢 → 不太喜欢

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「各种各样」跟「各种」有什么不一样？ (thumbs, then one student explains)
- How do you say "for example"? Write it on your whiteboard.
- 这句话对不对？我喜欢各种各样零食。 (missing 的 — error correction)
- 「冰淇淋」第一个字是什么声调？ (bīng, first tone)

#### Text 1 (p.100, CD track 41)
Play the track once, books closed. Then open to p.100 and read chorally with the pinyin.

> 我特别喜欢吃零食。我每天都吃各种各样的零食，比如：糖果、巧克力、薯片、蛋糕、冰淇淋、饼干等。我还喜欢喝汽水。

Stop there — the rest of the passage (对牙齿不好 / 从小就 / 总是) is Lesson 2. Say so.

Comprehension, answers in Chinese:
1. 他每天吃什么？
2. 他喜欢喝什么？
3. 课文里有几种零食？请说出来。 *(six — 糖果、巧克力、薯片、蛋糕、冰淇淋、饼干)*

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — We Do (6 min) · textbook Act. 5 (p.103)
**Summary board:** all six new words, characters + pictures, stays on screen for the whole activity.

**Task:** Textbook p.103, the seven shop pictures — 服装店, 肉店, 水果店, 餐厅, 文具店, 家具店, 电器店. One sentence per shop on the printed model:

> 这家超市卖各种各样的零食，比如巧克力、糖果等。

Pairs, 4 minutes. Then the eighth shop is your own school's 小卖部, and that sentence must name **every** snack on the summary board.

**Share:** Take three pairs — one shop sentence each, then one 小卖部 sentence. Ask the class to check the last one used all six words.

**Extension —** 三句，不看屏幕。第一句用「各种各样……比如」，第二句用「比」比较两种零食的价钱，第三句用「因为……，所以……」说你为什么常买那一种。写完以后念给同桌听，同桌要问你一个问题。

#### Activity 2: Act. 1 picture talk — You Do (7 min)
Textbook p.101. Pairs, A and B alternate. For each of the eight pictures, say **two or three** sentences using one of the degree phrases. Model from the textbook example about 三明治.

Target output: 我非常喜欢吃巧克力。我每天都吃巧克力。可是我不太喜欢糖果。

Teacher circulates. Keep the degree-phrase ladder on the board for round one; take it down for round two.

**Extension —** B doesn't choose the phrase — A assigns one ("用『挺喜欢』"), and B must produce three sentences on the spot with no preparation, the last one using 比如 to list two examples. Then swap and A gets the picture B found hardest.

#### Activity 3: Written sentences — You Do (5 min)
Textbook Act. 2 (p.102), items 2–5 in exercise books. Model item 1 is printed: 我特别喜欢买衣服，比如连衣裙、汗衫、毛衣、牛仔裤等。

Each sentence: a degree phrase + 比如 + at least three items + 等。Not all five need to be about food — item 1 is about clothes, so reach back to Book 1 Unit 5 vocabulary.

Anyone who finishes early moves to workbook Ex. 1 (p.118) — twelve snack pictures written in characters (炸鸡翅 is given).

**Extension —** 五句里至少有两句不是说吃的——一句说文具，一句说学校的科目（第三单元的词）。再加第六句，用「各种各样」说你的书包里有什么。

#### Game: Slap the Character (5 min)
Twelve character cards on the board: the six new words plus 糖果, 薯片, 薯条, 炸鸡翅, 盒饭, 三明治. Two students at the board, teacher says a word, they slap it. Winner stays on.

- **Setup needed:** twelve printed A5 character cards, blu-tack
- **Harder version:** the teacher stops saying the word. Instead they say a clue in Chinese — 「这个东西很甜，是黑色的」→ 巧克力 — or a sentence with the word missing. The fluent students take the clue-giving role themselves after two rounds.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** a student volunteer reads all three aloud, criterion 2 in Chinese.
- **Self-assessment:** thumbs on each criterion — 👍 / 😐 / 👎. Note who shows 😐 on criterion 2; that pattern runs through the whole lesson sequence.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句话：用「各种各样」和「比如」说说你家里有什么零食。One sentence, mini whiteboard, held up on the way out.
- **Preview:** 下节课我们学怎么说「吃太多糖果对牙齿不好」——还有怎么说你从小就喜欢的东西。
