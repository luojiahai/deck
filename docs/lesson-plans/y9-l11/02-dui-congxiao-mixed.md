# 牙齿、从小、总是 and 对……好／不好

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Snacks 零食
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Snacks 零食 | Lesson 2 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what is good or bad for us, and to talk about things we have liked since we were small.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 牙齿, 从小, 总是 and 正餐 in Chinese (能说出「牙齿」「从小」「总是」「正餐」)
- I can say something is good or bad for something else: A 对 B 好／不好 (能用「对……好／不好」说一句)
- I can say what I have liked since childhood: 我从小就…… (能用「我从小就……」介绍自己)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l11/l2-dui-congxiao/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Audio: CD track 42 (textbook p.104)
- Textbook pp.100 (rest of Text 1), 103–104 (Act. 4, 6, 7); workbook pp.118–119, 121 (Ex. 2, 3, 4, 9)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Last lesson's six snack words and 各种各样的……，比如……
**How:** Bingo (5 min). Students draw a 3×3 grid and fill it with any nine of: 零食, 巧克力, 蛋糕, 冰淇淋, 饼干, 糖果, 薯片, 薯条, 炸鸡翅, 盒饭, 三明治, 汽水. Teacher calls in Chinese only — no English, no pinyin. First line wins, then full house. Then 3 min: two students each say one sentence with 各种各样……比如.
**Connection:** 我们会说各种各样的零食了。可是妈妈为什么说不要吃太多？今天我们学怎么说原因。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Flag the trap in criterion 2 immediately: 对 here is not 对/错. Write both on the board side by side — 你说得对 vs 对牙齿不好 — and say they are the same character doing two different jobs.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 4 words in 2 cycles

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 牙齿 | yáchǐ | tooth; teeth | SVG: a single tooth, plus a smiling mouth showing teeth |
| 1 | 正餐 | zhèngcān | a proper meal (lunch or dinner) | SVG: a plate with rice, meat and vegetables, chopsticks beside it |
| 2 | 从小 | cóngxiǎo | from childhood | — (abstract) |
| 2 | 总是 | zǒngshì | always | — (abstract) |

Point out the components: 牙 and 齿 both mean *tooth* — the word doubles up, the way 各种各样 doubled up last lesson. 正餐 shares 餐 with 早餐 / 午餐 / 晚餐, already known.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 他的**牙齿**很白。 — His teeth are very white.
2. 我每天都吃三个**正餐**。 — I eat three proper meals every day.
3. 我每天吃三个**正餐**，我的**牙齿**很好。 — I eat three proper meals a day and my teeth are fine.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我每天吃 ________ 个正餐。 · 我的牙齿 ________ 。

*(These three sentences use only language taught before this point — 对……好／不好 and 不要 both arrive later in this lesson, so they stay out of the cycles.)*
**Extension —** 用「比」写一句，比较正餐和零食，再说你自己一天吃几个正餐、几次零食，最后说妈妈会说什么。三句。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 我**从小**就喜欢吃巧克力。 — I've liked chocolate since I was small.
2. 我妈妈**总是**买各种各样的零食。 — My mum is always buying all kinds of snacks.
3. 我**从小**就喜欢吃冰淇淋，妈妈**总是**买给我。 — I've liked ice cream since I was small, and mum always buys it for me.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我从小就喜欢 ________ 。 · 我妈妈总是 ________ 。
**Extension —** 三句，不看屏幕：一句用「我从小就……」说一个爱好（不是吃的——第二册的音乐、运动都行），一句说你现在做得怎么样，一句用「因为……，所以……」说为什么。

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**A 对 B 好／不好。**

1. 汽水对牙齿不好。
2. 我们学校的老师都对学生很好。 *(same 对, different meaning — "good to")*
3. 多吃蔬菜和水果对身体好。
4. 我知道糖果和汽水对牙齿不好，可是我从小就喜欢吃零食。 *(hardest — the actual Text 1 sentence, two clauses, 从小就 carried in)*

Then the related frame from Act. 4 (p.103):
**我对……（非常）感兴趣。／ 我对……一点儿都不感兴趣。**
- 我对中国音乐非常感兴趣。
- 我对足球一点儿都不感兴趣。

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「对」在「你说得对」和「对牙齿不好」里意思一样吗？ (thumbs down, then one student explains)
- 翻译：Eating too much chocolate is bad for your teeth. (whiteboards)
- 这句话对不对？我从小喜欢吃零食。 (missing 就 — 从小就 is the set phrase)
- 「总是」和「常常」有什么不一样？ (总是 = every single time)
- Rapid body-part round, workbook Ex. 4 (p.119) — teacher calls *tooth, mouth, tongue, nose, ear*, students write characters on whiteboards. 牙齿 is today's; the other four are Book 1 Unit 5 revision and should be automatic. Whatever misses, note it — it is on the Year 9 baseline, not this unit.

#### Text 1 — the rest (p.100, CD track 41)
Replay the track. Read from 我知道 to the end, chorally, then in pairs.

> 我知道糖果和汽水对牙齿不好，可是我从小就喜欢吃零食。妈妈总是对我说，不要吃太多零食，应该多吃正餐。

Comprehension in Chinese:
1. 他知道糖果对什么不好？
2. 妈妈总是对他说什么？
3. 他听妈妈的话吗？为什么你这样想？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — We Do (5 min) · textbook Act. 7 (p.104)
**Summary board:** 牙齿 · 正餐 · 从小 · 总是, characters and pictures, on screen throughout.

**Task:** Textbook p.104 — *Make a similar introduction of yourself.* The printed model runs on 我从小就……, repeated:

> 我从小就喜欢吃零食，比如蛋糕、巧克力、薯片、糖果等。我从小就喜欢喝可乐……我从小就喜欢弹钢琴。我现在钢琴弹得很好，已经考过了五级。

Four sentences, each using one word from the summary board, and they must read as one paragraph rather than four separate sentences. Solo, 3 minutes, exercise books.

Worked model to leave on the board: 我从小就喜欢吃零食。妈妈总是说吃太多糖对牙齿不好。她说我应该多吃正餐。可是我的牙齿现在还很好。

**Share:** two students read their paragraph aloud; class listens for all four words. Anyone finishing early answers workbook Ex. 2 (p.118) — 你从小就喜欢吃什么？你从小就喜欢做什么？

**Extension —** 同样四个词，可是写别人——你的弟弟、妹妹或者一个朋友。要用「他／她」，还要加一句「我觉得……」说你的看法。

#### Activity 2: Act. 4 sentence completion — You Do (6 min)
Textbook p.103, all five items, in exercise books.

1. 我对 ________ 非常感兴趣。
2. 多吃糖果对 ________ 不好。
3. 我们学校的老师都对 ________ 很好。
4. 我对 ________ 一点儿都不感兴趣。
5. ________ 对 ________ 。

Then two workbook pieces on the same page spread:
- **Ex. 9 (p.121)**, items 1–4 — the same 对 frames.
- **Ex. 3 (p.119)** — the cloze about a little brother who lives on snacks. Its word box is today's lesson almost exactly: 总是 / 每天 / 最 / 从小 / 各种各样 / 对……好 / 比如 / 正餐. Do this one as a class on the board if the room is slow, solo if not.

**Extension —** 第五题写三个不同的答案，三个都要是真的，而且三个不能都说吃的东西。写完以后，把最有意思的一句读给全班听，全班要用「为什么」问你。

#### Activity 3: Act. 6 listening — You Do (6 min)
Textbook p.104, CD track 42. Six multiple-choice items about a girl and her snacks. Play twice.

Answers to expect: 1 a) 在学校买零食 · 2 c) 糖果和薯片 — check against the script; the recording says 糖果、饼干、薯片, so push students to justify their choice · 3 b) 汽水 · 4 b) 糖果 · 5 b) 吃得少 · 6 a) 四十块零用钱

After marking, replay item 5 and ask: 「吃得多」「吃得少」是什么意思？ Put 我早饭吃得多，但是晚饭通常吃得少 on the board — it feeds the next activity.

**Extension —** 第三遍放录音的时候，不看选项，写下女孩说的每一种零食和她每天有多少零用钱。写完以后用中文说一句你对她的看法：我觉得她……

#### Game: Correct the Teacher (4 min)
Teacher writes five sentences on the board, three of them wrong. Students find and fix on whiteboards.

1. 汽水对牙齿很不好。 ✓
2. ✗ 我从小喜欢吃巧克力。 → 从小**就**
3. ✗ 我对音乐非常感兴趣的。 → drop 的
4. ✗ 吃太多零食对牙齿比不好。 → drop 比
5. ✓ 妈妈总是对我说，应该多吃正餐。

- **Setup needed:** nothing — write them live
- **Harder version:** fluent students write the sentences instead. Each produces one correct and one wrong sentence using today's patterns and runs the round for the class; they must be able to explain *why* their wrong one is wrong.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** choral read of all three; ask the class which was hardest.
- **Self-assessment:** traffic light on whiteboards — one colour word per criterion (红／黄／绿).
- **Exit ticket:** 翻译一句：Eating too much chocolate is bad for your teeth. 然后加一句用「我从小就……」说你自己。
- **Preview:** 下节课我们不说零食了，说早饭——谷类早餐、面包、煎蛋、香肠。
