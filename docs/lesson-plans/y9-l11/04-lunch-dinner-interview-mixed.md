# 沙拉、酸奶、杂菜汤、碗 and 有时

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Snacks 零食
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Snacks 零食 | Lesson 4 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what we eat for lunch and dinner, and to ask four classmates the same questions and report what they said.

## Success Criteria
- I can name four lunch and dinner foods in Chinese (能说出「沙拉」「酸奶」「杂菜汤」「碗」)
- I can use 有时 = 有时候 and the right measure word for a dish (能用「有时」，也会说「一碗汤」「一杯牛奶」)
- I can interview a classmate and report their answer to the class (能问同学，然后向全班报告：他早饭一般吃……)

## Materials Needed
- Projector (deck: `index/designs/y9-l11/l4-lunch-dinner/`)
- Mini whiteboards + markers
- Exercise books
- Printed interview grid (textbook p.107 Act. 9 — six questions, four name columns)
- Textbook pp.105 (rest of Text 2), 102 (Act. 3), 107 (Act. 9); workbook pp.120, 122, 124 (Ex. 7, 11, 14, 15)

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** Lesson 3's breakfast words and 加.
**How:** Slap the Character (4 min) — 谷类, 牛奶, 面包, 煎蛋, 香肠, 加 plus 零食, 巧克力, 蛋糕, 冰淇淋 on the board, two students at a time. Then pair dialogue from memory (3 min): 你早饭吃什么？ answered with 一般…… + 有时候……
**Connection:** 早饭说完了。今天说午饭和晚饭——然后我们要去问四个同学，看看全班吃得一样不一样。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Say that criterion 3 is the lesson's real target: the interview and the report-back are how the rest of the lesson is spent.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 5 words in 2 cycles + 1 in the pattern

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 沙拉 | shālā | salad | SVG: a bowl of leaves, tomato and cucumber, fork resting on the rim |
| 1 | 酸奶 | suānnǎi | yoghurt | SVG: a yoghurt pot with a peeled lid and a spoon |
| 2 | 杂菜汤 | zácàitāng | vegetable soup | SVG: a bowl of soup with vegetable pieces, steam lines |
| 2 | 碗 | wǎn | bowl | SVG: an empty rice bowl seen from the side |

Component clues: 酸 has 酉, the wine jar — sour things and fermented things share it, which is why 酸奶 is yoghurt and not "sour milk gone wrong". 汤 has 氵 (water), 碗 has 石 (stone). 汤 and 场 (from 菜市场, Lesson 10) look alike — put them side by side and make the class point at the difference.

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我午饭常常吃**沙拉**。 — I often have salad for lunch.
2. **酸奶**对身体很好。 — Yoghurt is good for you.
3. 我午饭吃**沙拉**和**酸奶**。 — For lunch I have salad and yoghurt.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我午饭常常吃 ________ 。 · ________ 对身体很好。
**Extension —** 用「比」写一句，比较沙拉和薯条，说哪个对身体好，为什么。再加一句用「可是我从小就喜欢……」说你其实爱吃哪个。

**Cycle 2 — example sentences:**
1. 妈妈做了**杂菜汤**。 — Mum made vegetable soup.
2. 我喝了一**碗**汤。 — I drank a bowl of soup.
3. 我晚饭喝一**碗杂菜汤**。 — For dinner I have a bowl of vegetable soup.

**Cycle 2 — write your own:** 我喝了一碗 ________ 。 · 妈妈做了 ________ 。
**Extension —** 写三句说你们家昨天的晚饭，每句都要有量词（碗、杯、块、条、个）。写完以后把三句连成一段，加上「因为……，所以……」说为什么是妈妈做的。

*(Recall slide: 沙拉 · 酸奶 · 杂菜汤 · 碗 — characters only.)*

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**有时 = 有时候** — the short form, taught here as the fifth new word — inside the frequency ladder.

Put the whole ladder on the board, from known to new:
总是 → 每天 → 常常／经常 → 一般／平时／通常 → 有时／有时候 → 很少

1. 我午饭一般吃三明治，有时吃薯条。
2. 我们家晚饭有时候吃中餐，有时候吃西餐。
3. 我平时喝牛奶，有时喝酸奶。
4. 我午饭一般吃三明治、热狗、酸奶，有时吃薯条、杂菜汤、沙拉等。 *(hardest — the actual Text 2 line, two lists contrasted)*

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「有时」和「有时候」意思一样吗？ (yes — the book prints 有时 = 有时候)
- 汤用什么量词？牛奶呢？ (碗 / 杯)
- 这句话对不对？我喝了一个汤。 (→ 一碗汤)
- 「总是」和「有时」，哪个多？ (ladder check — point at the board)

#### Text 2 — the rest (p.105, CD track 43)
Replay. Read the remaining exchanges chorally, then in pairs with roles swapped.

> A: 午饭呢？ B: 吃三明治、热狗、酸奶，有时吃薯条、杂菜汤、沙拉等。
> A: 你们家晚饭吃什么？ B: 有时候吃中餐，有时候吃西餐。
> A: 你们家平时谁做饭？你会帮忙吗？ B: 一般是妈妈做。有时候吃完晚饭以后，我会帮妈妈洗碗。

Flag the last line and leave it: 帮忙 and 吃完……以后 are next lesson. Students should recognise 碗 in 洗碗 — that's today's word doing a second job.

Comprehension in Chinese:
1. 他午饭一般吃什么？有时吃什么？
2. 他们家晚饭吃中餐还是西餐？
3. 他们家谁做饭？

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Vocabulary Consolidation Task — We Do (5 min)
**Summary board:** 沙拉 · 酸奶 · 杂菜汤 · 碗 · 有时, on screen throughout.

**Task — WB 15 grouping (p.124), then TB Act. 3 (p.102) and WB 7 (p.120):** the twenty-four workbook items go into five columns — 中餐 / 西餐 / 快餐 / 零食 / 饮料. Pairs, 3 minutes, then two placements must be justified aloud in Chinese: 我觉得沙拉是西餐，因为……

This is Test Part 1's exact format. Say so.

Once sorted, turn to textbook Act. 3 (p.102) and workbook Ex. 7 (p.120), which are the same task open-ended: **add five more items of your own to each category**, using nothing that is already on the board. This is where the whole unit's vocabulary gets swept up — Lesson 10's market words belong in 中餐, Lesson 1's snacks in 零食.

**Share:** take the two most argued-about items (比萨饼 and 炸鸡翅 usually split the room) and let two pairs argue it in Chinese.

**Extension —** 每一列加**五**个，而且五个里至少两个要是第十课学的（菜市场的词）。加完以后读 workbook Ex. 11 (p.122) 的短文（我们学校的小卖部卖各种各样的零食……），用中文回答五个问题，再把你们学校餐厅卖的东西填进「正餐／零食／小吃／饮料」四个格子里。全部用汉字。

#### Activity 2: Act. 9 interview four classmates — You Do (10 min)
Textbook p.107, printed grid. Students move around the room and put the six questions to **four** different classmates, writing notes in Chinese.

1. 你早饭一般吃什么？
2. 你午饭一般吃什么？
3. 你晚饭一般吃什么？
4. 你们家周末常常去饭店吃饭吗？
5. 你们一般去哪家饭店吃饭？
6. 你们一般吃什么？

Then **Report to the class** — three or four students report, on the textbook's printed model:
三个同学早饭一般吃水果和面包，喝牛奶。一个同学有时候早饭吃谷类早餐加牛奶。午饭他们一般吃盒饭。

The report is the hard part and the point of the activity: it forces 三个同学…… / 一个同学…… and third-person verbs.

**Extension —** 报告的时候不看笔记。而且要加上比较：「三个同学早饭吃面包，可是只有一个同学喝牛奶。我觉得……早饭吃得最好，因为……」最后用中文问全班一个问题。

#### Activity 3: WB 14 measure words — You Do (4 min)
Workbook p.124. Sixteen measure words, one noun for each: 件 条 碗 辆 支 架 副 顶 个 间 位 套 幢 双 口 种。

This is Test Part 2's format. Do the eight easiest first, then as many of the rest as time allows. 碗 is today's word — it must be one of the eight.

**Extension —** 十六个全部写完，而且每个词组都要写成一个完整的句子，不是只写名词。比如不写「碗：汤」，写「我喝了一碗杂菜汤」。

#### Game: Odd One Out (4 min)
Five rounds, four words each on screen. Students say which is the odd one and **why**, in Chinese.

1. 沙拉 · 酸奶 · 杂菜汤 · 碗 → 碗 (not food)
2. 总是 · 常常 · 有时 · 牛奶 → 牛奶 (not a frequency word)
3. 面包 · 煎蛋 · 香肠 · 冰淇淋 → 冰淇淋 (not breakfast)
4. 碗 · 杯 · 条 · 汤 → 汤 (not a measure word)
5. 巧克力 · 饼干 · 蛋糕 · 正餐 → 正餐 (not a snack)

- **Setup needed:** projector only
- **Harder version:** more than one answer is defensible in rounds 2 and 5 — a student who justifies a *different* odd one out convincingly in Chinese wins the round outright. Fluent students run rounds 6 and 7 with sets they build themselves.

### Plenary (43–50 min)
- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read aloud; ask two students who reported back whether criterion 3 is ticked.
- **Self-assessment:** 👍 / 😐 / 👎 on whiteboards, all three at once.
- **Exit ticket:** 写一句：用「一般……，有时……」说你午饭吃什么。
- **Preview:** 下节课是第十一课最后一节：学「吃完饭以后……」，学十二个部首，还要复习整课。
