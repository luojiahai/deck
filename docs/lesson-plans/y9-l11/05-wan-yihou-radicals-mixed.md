# 帮忙、完 and ……完……以后，……

**Year Level:** Year 9
**Unit:** Book 3 · Unit 4 — Snacks 零食
**Lesson:** Lesson 11 — Snacks 零食 | Lesson 5 of 5
**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Learning Intention
We are learning to say what we do after finishing something, and to recognise the character components behind this unit's food words.

## Success Criteria
- I can say 帮忙 and use 完 to mean "finish" (能说「我帮妈妈洗碗」，会用「吃完」「做完」)
- I can use ……完……以后，…… to link two actions (能用「……完……以后，……」说两件事)
- I can write twelve character components from memory (能默写十二个部首)

- Mini whiteboards + markers

## Lesson Sequence

### Review (0–8 min)
**What:** everything from Lessons 1–4, then the twelve components from Act. 13.
**How:** Two parts.
1. **Whole-lesson recall board (4 min)** — all 24 words of 第十一课 on one screen, characters only, no pinyin, no English: 零食 各种各样 比如 巧克力 蛋糕 冰淇淋 饼干 牙齿 从小 总是 正餐 谷类 加 牛奶 面包 煎蛋 香肠 酸奶 有时 杂菜汤 沙拉 帮忙 完 碗. Class reads across chorally. Then teacher points at five at random and cold-calls for an English gloss.
2. **Act. 13 radical memorisation (4 min)** — textbook p.109. Twelve components on screen for five minutes… compressed to three. 矢 · 斗 · 力 · 寸 · 青 · 旦 · 自 · 己 · 亡 · 立 · 几 · 上. Then screen off, students write as many as they can on whiteboards. Count, don't mark.
**Connection:** 今天是第十一课最后一节课。我们先把这一课的词都过一遍，然后学最后两个词，还有一个很有用的句型。

### Learning Intention & Success Criteria (8–10 min)
Project and read. Say that criterion 2 is the one that appears in the unit test, and that the last part of the lesson looks back over all five lessons, not just today's.

### I Do (10–20 min)

#### New Vocabulary — 2 words in 1 cycle

| Cycle | 汉字 | 拼音 | English | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 帮忙 | bāngmáng | to help | SVG: two figures, one handing a stack of bowls to the other at a sink |
| 1 | 完 | wán | to finish | — (abstract) |

**Cycle 1 — example sentences:**
1. 我常常**帮**妈妈洗碗。 — I often help my mum wash up.
2. 我做**完**作业了。 — I've finished my homework.
3. 我吃**完**晚饭以后，会**帮**妈妈洗碗。 — After I finish dinner I help my mum wash up.

Note the split: 帮忙 separates when an object comes in — 帮忙 on its own (你会帮忙吗？), but 帮妈妈 when you say who. Show both on the slide.

**Cycle 1 — write your own:** 我常常帮 ________ 。 · 我 ________ 完了。
**Extension —** 写三句：一句说你在家帮谁做什么，一句说你上个周末做完了什么，一句用「因为……，所以……」说你为什么帮忙（或者为什么不帮忙）。三句要连起来。

#### Key Sentence Pattern
**……完……以后，……** — "after finishing"

Put the textbook NOTE (p.108) on screen and read it: the two word orders are equivalent —
做完作业以后，他看电视。 = 他做完作业以后看电视。

1. 我吃完早饭以后刷牙。
2. 他游完泳以后去买零食。
3. 我们一家人看完电影以后去饭店吃饭。
4. 我吃完晚饭以后帮妈妈洗碗，洗完碗以后才做作业。 *(hardest — the pattern twice in one sentence, with 才 from Book 2)*

Drill the shape on the board as a formula: **V + 完 + O + 以后，+ 第二件事**

#### CFU Checkpoints
- 「完」放在哪里——动词前面还是后面？ (after — 吃完, not 完吃)
- 翻译：After I finish dinner I watch TV. Whiteboards.
- 这句话对不对？我完吃饭以后做作业。 (→ 我吃完饭以后……)
- 「你会帮忙吗？」和「你会帮妈妈吗？」哪个对？ (both — but 帮忙妈妈 is wrong)

### Flexible Practice (20–43 min)

#### Activity 1: Act. 11 sentence completion — We Do (6 min)
Textbook p.108, all eight items. Items 1–4 give the first clause and need the second; items 5–8 give the second clause and need the 完……以后 clause — that reversal is the point, so don't let students do only 1–4.

1. 我吃完早饭以后 ________ 。 *(刷牙 given)*
2. 他游完泳以后去 ________ 。
3. 我们一家人看完电影以后去 ________ 。
4. 她做完作业以后去 ________ 。
5. ________ 帮妈妈洗碗。
6. ________ 跟姐姐一起去买菜。
7. ________ 去图书馆看书。
8. ________ 跟爸爸一起去钓鱼。

Then *It is your turn!* — three sentences of their own with the structure.

**Extension —** 做 workbook Ex. 16 (p.125) 的四组图，每组写一句「……完……以后」。然后把四句连成一段话，说一个人的一天，从早上到晚上，中间要用「先……然后……最后……」。

#### Activity 2: Act. 12 listening — You Do (6 min)
Textbook p.108, CD track 44. Six multiple-choice items about meals at home. Play twice, mark, then play a third time for the two items the class got worst.

Expected: 1 b) 谷类早餐加牛奶 · 2 c) 牛奶 *(加奶吧，不要加糖)* · 3 b) 炸鸡腿和沙拉 · 4 b) 比萨饼和沙拉 · 5 b) 中餐 · 6 c) 妈妈做饭，她洗碗

Item 6 is the one that lands today's pattern — 吃过晚饭，我经常洗碗。 Replay it and ask what 吃过 is doing there.

**Extension —** 第三遍放的时候不看选项，把六段对话里出现的所有吃的、喝的都写下来（汉字）。写完以后数一数：中餐几样？西餐几样？用一句中文说这家人吃中餐多还是西餐多。

#### Activity 3: Act. 10 pair questions + WB 18 make sentences — You Do (6 min)
**First, spoken (2 min).** Textbook Act. 10 (p.107). Pairs, A asks all five, B answers — the dotted words are the target:

1. 你**有时候**在学校餐厅买午饭吃，你呢？
2. 你**小时候**在哪儿住过？
3. **上课的时候**你可以吃东西吗？
4. **放学以后**，你一般什么时候到家？
5. 你**从小就**喜欢吃什么？不喜欢吃什么？

Then *It is your turn!* — each student invents one new question using one of the dotted words and puts it to their partner.

**Then, written (4 min).** Workbook p.126 Ex. 18. Eight structures, one sentence each — the same words, now on paper:

有时候 · 小时候 · ……的时候 · 从小 · 一……就…… · 除了……以外，…… · 一边……，一边…… · 因为……，所以……

Do 从小, 有时候 and 因为……所以…… first — those are the ones the test asks for. Then as many of the rest as time allows. Every sentence must be about food or meals, so the unit vocabulary gets carried through the older structures.

**Extension —** 八个句型全部写完，而且八句要组成**一段**话，说你自己的一天吃什么。不是八个不相关的句子——要能从头读到尾。写完念给同桌，同桌说哪一句最好。

#### Game: Writing Relay (5 min)
Two teams, two columns on the board. Teacher calls a clue in English; the next student in line runs up and writes the character.

Round 1 — the nine simple characters from WB Ex. 10 (p.122): tongue 舌 · melon 瓜 · sheep 羊 · seedling 苗 · east 东 · south 南 · west 西 · north 北 · bow 弓

Round 2 — WB Ex. 17 (p.125) word building. Teacher calls one half, the runner writes a complete phrase: 煎___ · ___肠 · 面___ · 热___ · 酸___ · ___排 · 沙___ · 盒___ · ___汤 · 鸡___

- **Setup needed:** board space and two markers; nothing printed
- **Harder version:** in round 2 the runner must write the phrase **and** a measure word for it (一碗杂菜汤, 一块牛排). A phrase with no measure word scores nothing. Fluent students take the calling role for the last three.

### Plenary (43–50 min)

The scope here is the whole of 第十一课, not just today.

- **Revisit Success Criteria:** read today's three, then put the five lessons' learning intentions on one slide and ask: 哪一节课最难？
- **Self-assessment:** five criteria on screen, one per lesson of the sequence — 能说各种各样的零食 / 能用「对……不好」/ 能说早饭吃什么 / 能问同学然后报告 / 能用「……完……以后」. Thumbs on each. Anything with a room full of 😐 is what to reopen before the unit test.
- **Exit ticket:** 写两句：第一句用「……完……以后，……」说你今天放学以后做什么。第二句用「从小就」说一样你一直喜欢吃的东西。
- **Preview:** 第十一课学完了。下一课是第十二课——外出就餐：去饭店点菜、看菜单、自助餐。
