轻松学中文 2 · Unit 5 · Lesson 13

房子

House · Year 8

Six 50-minute lessons, 148 slides. Nineteen new words spread across the sequence — two to six per lesson, each pair taught as a three-slide cycle: the words, then the words used, then the students using them. Every lesson carries a CFU board straight after the new pattern, question-only: there is no answer slide anywhere in the series. Revision happens inside every lesson rather than in a review lesson at the end.

Grammar Focus · 本课语法点

L1

有　／　在

地方 + 有 · 东西 + 在 — what a place contains, and where a thing is

L2

几 + 间 + 卧室

间 for bedrooms, 个 for everything else

L3

在 + 地方 + 做什么

Saying what someone is doing where

L4

在 + 地方 + 面

上 / 下 / 里 / 外 / 左 / 右 — and the same fact reversed

L5

住什么样的房子？

House type, and 房前 ／ 房后

L6

间 · 个 · 层 · 辆

All four measure words, which the unit test asks for directly

Lesson 1 of 6

房子有几层

The house & its storeys

The first six words, and the contrast the whole textbook lesson turns on — 有 for what a place contains, 在 for where a thing is. Students draw their own house in section and label it.

New words · 6房子 · 房间 · 层 · 楼 · 楼上 · 楼下

Lesson 2 of 6

楼上有什么

Rooms upstairs

The upstairs rooms, and the measure word that has no English equivalent: 卧室 takes 间, 浴室 takes 个. CD 63 runs twice — tick it, then draw it.

New words · 4卧室 · 书房 · 浴室 · 洗澡

Lesson 3 of 6

楼下有什么

Rooms downstairs · Text 1

The downstairs rooms complete Text 1, which is read in full for the first time. A speaking lesson: the p.123 cutaway house, a house-tour role-play, the class survey, and 20 Questions.

New words · 4客厅 · 餐厅 · 厨房 · 洗手间

Lesson 4 of 6

在哪儿

Position words

Six words in three cycles — the heaviest vocabulary load in the sequence. TPR first, then the p.124 cluttered room, then the nine pictures of p.128 written alone.

New words · 6上面 · 下面 · 里面 · 外面 · 左面 · 右面

Lesson 5 of 6

走出大门

Outside the house · Text 2

A writing lesson. Text 2, then stroke-order rows for the five new characters, then the six jumbled sentences typed up — producing characters from pinyin, which is what the unit test's translation asks for.

New words · 5洋房 · 花园 · 前 · 后 · 车库

Lesson 6 of 6

停两辆车

The garage · your whole house

The last two words finish Text 2, all four measure words come together, and students design and present a house of their own. The plenary looks back over all six lessons.

New words · 2停 · 辆

Six lessons, not seven. The sequence ends on content, not revision — there is no 复习 lesson. Each lesson opens with a cumulative Review phase instead, and Lesson 6's plenary looks back across all six.

No answer slides, and no pinyin or radical practice. Both at the teacher's request, and both enforced in the builder rather than left to care: the CFU helper takes no answers, and there is no radical-slide helper at all. Textbook Ex. 1 and Ex. 7 and workbook Ex. 10 and 13 are out of the series. Unit 5 Test parts 3 and 4 test exactly that material, so students meet those two parts unprepared — noted in the Lesson 6 plan.
