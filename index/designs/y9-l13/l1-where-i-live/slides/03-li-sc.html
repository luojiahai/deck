Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say where we live and how far it is from the places we go.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say where I live

能说出我家住在哪儿

2

I can use A 离 B 远／不远／很近 to talk about distance

能用「A 离 B 远／不远」说距离

3

I can say whether life where I live is convenient

能说我住的地方生活方不方便
