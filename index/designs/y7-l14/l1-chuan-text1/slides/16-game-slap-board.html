1 四个小组。每组派一个人到黑板前。
Four groups. One player from each group at the board.
2 老师说一个词 — 第一个拍到正确卡片的得一分。
Teacher calls a word. First hand on the right card scores.
3 换人，每个人都要上来一次。
Rotate — everyone takes a turn.
更难 老师说一整句 — 我姐姐喜欢穿粉红色的裙子。Slap TWO cards, in the right order: the colour first, then the garment.
