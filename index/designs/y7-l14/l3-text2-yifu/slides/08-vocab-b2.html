Y7 · L14 穿着 I Do · 例句 · 毛衣 · 外套
wǒ māma xǐhuan chuān hóngsè de máoyī
1我妈妈喜欢穿红色的毛衣。
My mum likes wearing a red sweater.
wǒ bàba xǐhuan chuān hēisè de wàitào
2我爸爸喜欢穿黑色的外套。
My dad likes wearing a black coat.
wǒ chuān máoyī hé wàitào
3我穿毛衣和外套。
I'm wearing a sweater and a coat.
问 「外套」的「外」是什么？ Which of the two goes on the outside? The character tells you.
