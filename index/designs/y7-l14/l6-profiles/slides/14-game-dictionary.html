Game 查字典接力 · Dictionary relay 7 min练习册 p.152 第 10 题
1 三人一组，一本字典或者一台平板。练习册 p.152 第 10 题，十个词。
Teams of three, one dictionary or device each. Workbook Ex.10 — ten words.
2 十个里面有九个你今天以前就见过了，真正的新词只有「和服」一个。
Nine of the ten you already know — only 和服 is genuinely new.
3 所以比的不是认字，是查得快不快。全写对的第一组赢。
So this is a dictionary-skills race, not a vocabulary hunt. First team correct wins.
更难 用部首查，不用拼音查 — 和服查「禾」，袜子查「衤」。And the team has to say which radical they used for each word.
