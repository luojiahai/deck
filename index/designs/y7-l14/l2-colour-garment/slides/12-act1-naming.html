1 第一遍：老师指，全班一起说。
Round 1: teacher points, whole class answers together.
2 第二遍：不举手，老师点名，快一点。
Round 2: cold call, no hands up, faster.
3 第三遍：老师只说颜色，一个同学补衣服，下一个同学说成一整句。
Round 3: teacher says the colour only — one student adds the garment, the next makes it a sentence with 穿.
