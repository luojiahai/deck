Y7 · L14 穿着 I Do · 例句 · 汗衫 · 天蓝色
tā chuān báisè de hànshān
1他穿白色的汗衫。
He's wearing a white T-shirt.
wǒ xǐhuan tiānlánsè
2我喜欢天蓝色。
I like sky blue.
wǒ mèimei chuān tiānlánsè de hànshān hé hēisè de duǎnkù
3我妹妹穿天蓝色的汗衫和黑色的短裤。
My little sister is wearing a sky-blue T-shirt and black shorts.
问 汗衫和衬衫，哪里不一样？ One has buttons and a collar. Which one?
