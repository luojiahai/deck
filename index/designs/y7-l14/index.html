两个复习题，课本里没有。 Workbook Revision p.171 asks 你穿什么校服？ and 你喜欢你的校服吗？ — two questions that appear nowhere in the textbook lesson but are on the list students are tested from. They are taught in Lesson 4.

六节课 · The sequence

No revision lesson at the end — the sequence ends on content. Revision happens in each lesson's opening eight minutes, and the Unit Test's own formats (category sorting, radical → meaning, word-order repair, translation both ways, picture description) are used as the practice activities throughout.
