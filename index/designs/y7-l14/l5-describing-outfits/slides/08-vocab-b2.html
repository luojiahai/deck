Y7 · L14 穿着 I Do · 例句 · 西装 · 领带
wǒ bàba shàngbān chuān xīzhuāng
1我爸爸上班穿西装。
My dad wears a suit to work.
tā de lǐngdài shì hóngsè de
2他的领带是红色的。
His tie is red.
wǒ gēge chuān hēisè de xīzhuāng hé lánsè de lǐngdài
3我哥哥穿黑色的西装和蓝色的领带。
My older brother is wearing a black suit and a blue tie.
问 谁上班穿西装？ Answer with a full sentence.
