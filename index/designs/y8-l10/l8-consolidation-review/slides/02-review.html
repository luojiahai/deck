回顾 · Whole-Class Chain Response

How the Chain Works

  1. Student 1 says a food item + their opinion: 我喜欢吃苹果。
  2. Student 2 repeats the whole chain, then adds their own link: 我喜欢吃苹果，我不太喜欢吃西红柿。
  3. Keep going around the circle/rows — each speaker must remember and repeat everyone before them before adding a new link.
