Learning Intention

We are learning to bring together everything from this topic — vegetables, fruits, 应该, 可是, 这些/那些/一些, and 种 — in extended speaking and writing.

1

I can hold a conversation about food likes/dislikes using 应该 and 可是

2

I can ask "whose is this?" and answer using 是……的(是小明的)

3

I can build new words by combining known characters

4

I can write a short paragraph describing someone's food habits and hobbies, in the style of the model essay
