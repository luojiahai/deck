How It Works

1️⃣

In pairs, pick a word from the bank and slot it into the model dialogue above.

2️⃣

Swap roles and repeat with a new word — work through as many of the 8 words as time allows.

✨

Extension: add a follow-up with 可是 — e.g. B: 是小明的，可是他不喜欢看。
