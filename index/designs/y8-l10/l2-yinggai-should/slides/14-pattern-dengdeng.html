10–20 MIN · I DO 句型 · 等等 14 / 18

句型 · Key sentence pattern

A、B、C 等等。

Items separated by 、 — then the list is closed with 等等.

1

我喜欢吃黄瓜、土豆、生菜等等。

I like cucumber, potato, lettuce and so on.

2

老师说我们应该多吃青菜、菜花儿、西红柿等等。

Both structures in one sentence.

Common mistake

等等 needs a list in front of it. 我应该吃青菜等等。 on its own is wrong — one item is not a list.
