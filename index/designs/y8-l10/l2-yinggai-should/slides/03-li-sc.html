8–10 MIN 学习目标 3 / 18

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what someone should do, and to list several things in one sentence.

Success Criteria · 我会……

1

I can use 应该 to give advice

你应该每天吃蔬菜。

2

I can report someone else's advice

妈妈说我应该多吃青菜。

3

I can list three or more things and close the list with 等等

我喜欢吃黄瓜、土豆、生菜等等。

4

I can use 非常 to say I like something a lot

我非常喜欢吃黄瓜。
