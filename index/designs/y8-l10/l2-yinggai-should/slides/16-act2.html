Activity 2 · Translate into English — 练习册 Ex.7

In your books · 8 minutes

1

Translate all six sentences into English.

2

Then "It is your turn!" — two sentences of your own using 应该.

1. 今天很冷，你应该多穿点儿衣服。

2. 妈妈说我应该每天吃水果和蔬菜。

3. 爸爸说我应该每天做两个小时作业。

4. 老师说我应该多看中文小说。

5. 老师说我应该多学一门外语。

6. 我感冒了，医生说我应该多休息、多睡觉。

Extension · 加油题

Translate the other way too, sheet turned over: "The doctor said I should eat green vegetables, cauliflower, tomatoes and so on." Every item on the list, closed with 等等.
