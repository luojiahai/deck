10–20 MIN · I DO 句型 · 应该 13 / 18

句型 · Key sentence pattern

主语 + 应该 + 动词

应该 goes BEFORE the verb — never after it.

1

你应该每天吃蔬菜。

You should eat vegetables every day.

2

今天很冷，你应该穿大衣。

It's cold today — you should wear a coat.

3

妈妈说我应该每天吃青菜。

Mum says I should eat green vegetables every day.

4

老师说我们不应该一边做作业一边听音乐。

Negative, reported, and carrying 一边…一边… from Unit 3.
