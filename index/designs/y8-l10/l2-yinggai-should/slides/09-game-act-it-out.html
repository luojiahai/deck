How to Play

1️⃣

Class splits into two teams, lined up at the front.

2️⃣

Teacher calls out a phrase from the 16-card set — sometimes framed as advice, "你应该……" — and one member from each team acts it out.

🏆

First to act it out correctly wins a point for their team.

🥦

After a few rounds, teacher inserts vegetable-eating mimes (e.g. "你应该吃蔬菜") to loop in today's theme.

🗂️

Setup Needed

16 phrase cards + two teams lined up at the front

Highlighted card (吃饭) shows where a vegetable-themed advice mime can be looped in — e.g. teacher calls "你应该吃蔬菜" instead.
