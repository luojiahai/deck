What does 应该 mean?

应该 = should

yīnggāi — used to give advice about what someone should do.

2

"You should eat vegetables"

你应该吃蔬菜。

nǐ yīnggāi chī shūcài

3

Where does 等等 go in a sentence?

At the end of a list.

我喜欢吃黄瓜、土豆等等 — 等等 comes after all the items are named.
