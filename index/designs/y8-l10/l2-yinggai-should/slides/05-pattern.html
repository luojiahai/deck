1 · Giving advice with 应该

你应该

每天

VP

"You should [VP] every day."

你应该每天吃蔬菜。

You should eat vegetables every day.

今天很冷，你应该穿大衣。

It's cold today, you should wear a coat.

你应该少看电视，多做作业。

You should watch less TV, do more homework.

你应该每天吃一百个土豆！silly example

You should eat 100 potatoes every day!
