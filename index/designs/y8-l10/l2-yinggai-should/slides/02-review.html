Bingo Rapid Fire

  1. Students have a pre-made 3×3 or 4×4 grid of Lesson 1's vegetable words
  2. Teacher calls out an English word or pinyin → students cross off the matching Chinese word on their grid
  3. First to complete a line (or full house) calls "Bingo!"
