Activity 3 · The supermarket project — textbook p.94 Ex.8

A4 paper · 10 minutes

1

Draw the vegetable section of the supermarket near your home.

2

Label every vegetable in Chinese characters. Minimum six labels.

3

At least four must be words from this unit — use the Extra Words for the rest.

4

Last two minutes: gallery walk. Find one vegetable on someone else's drawing that is not on yours.

Extension · 加油题

Label ten, and add a caption line under the drawing using both of this lesson's structures.

我们家附近的超市有青菜、南瓜、生菜、西红柿等等。
我非常喜欢吃西红柿，可是我不喜欢吃南瓜。
