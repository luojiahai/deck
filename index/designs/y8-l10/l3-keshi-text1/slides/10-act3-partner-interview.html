You Do

Q1

What's your name?

Q2

Q3

How old are you? What grade are you in?

Q4

你喜欢做什么？

What do you like doing (hobbies)?

你喜欢什么运动？每星期运动几次？

What sport do you like? How often each week?

你会画画吗？每天做作业多长时间？

Can you draw? How long is homework each day?
