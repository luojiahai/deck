What does 可是 mean?

可是 = but

kěshì — used to contrast two ideas, just like 但是.

2

"I like watching TV, but I should do my homework"

我喜欢看电视，可是我应该做作业。

wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànshì, kěshì wǒ yīnggāi zuò zuòyè
