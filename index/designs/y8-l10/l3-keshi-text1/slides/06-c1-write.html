10–20 MIN · I DO 生词 · Cycle 1 of 1 7 / 16

写一句 · Write your own

我不太喜欢 ____________ ，

可是 ____________ 。

90 seconds. Both halves. The second half must disagree with the first.

Extension · 加油题

Three clauses, not two. Then write a second one where the second clause is the positive.

我非常喜欢吃西红柿，可是我不喜欢吃菜花儿，妈妈说我应该都吃。
