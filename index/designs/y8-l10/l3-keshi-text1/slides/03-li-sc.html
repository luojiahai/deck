8–10 MIN 学习目标 4 / 16

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to join two contrasting ideas with 可是, and to read the whole Text 1 dialogue about vegetables.

Success Criteria · 我会……

1

I can use 可是 to join two clauses that disagree

我不太喜欢吃菜花儿，可是妈妈说我应该吃。

2

I can read Text 1 aloud with a partner and answer questions about it

你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？

3

I can write the simple characters 食、果、欠、石

食 · 果 · 欠 · 石

4

I can hear and mark the tones on ie and üe syllables

ie · üe
