Slap the Board

  1. Write 食, 果, 欠, 石 (plus a few distractor characters) on the board
  2. Teacher says the meaning or pinyin — two students race to slap the correct character
  3. Bonus round: teacher says an ie / üe pinyin pair from Lesson 4 — students call out which sound they hear

Lesson 4 · Simple Characters

食

food; eat

果

fruit; result

欠

owe; yawn

石

stone

Pinyin Focus

ie

e.g. 谢谢 xièxie

üe

e.g. 学 xué
