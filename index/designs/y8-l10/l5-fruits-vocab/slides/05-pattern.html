关键句型 · Key Sentence Pattern

Asking what fruit someone likes, and answering with a list

你喜欢吃什么水果？

→

X、Y、Z，我都喜欢吃。

"What fruit do you like? X, Y, Z — I like them all."

你喜欢吃什么水果？苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃。

What fruit do you like? Apples, bananas, tangerines — I like them all.

我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。

I like eating watermelon and pears.

我不喜欢吃香蕉。

I don't like eating bananas.

我每天吃十个苹果！silly example

I eat ten apples every day!

To build "I like eating watermelon and pears": 我喜欢吃 + 西瓜 + 和 + 梨 → 我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。
