Wǒ měi tiān dōu chī shuǐguǒ.

我每天都吃水果。

I eat fruit every day.

Wǒ fēicháng xǐhuan chī píngguǒ.

我非常喜欢吃苹果。

I really like eating apples.

Píngguǒ shì wǒ zuì xǐhuan de shuǐguǒ.

苹果是我最喜欢的水果。

Apples are my favourite fruit.
