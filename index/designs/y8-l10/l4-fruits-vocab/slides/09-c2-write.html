10–20 MIN · I DO 生词 · Cycle 2 of 2 10 / 18

写一句 · Write your own

______ 和 ______ 我都喜欢吃。

60 seconds. Both blanks, in characters.

Extension · 加油题

Write about three people — you, someone at home, and a friend — one sentence each, and make the three sentences disagree with each other.

我喜欢吃香蕉，可是我妹妹不喜欢吃。
