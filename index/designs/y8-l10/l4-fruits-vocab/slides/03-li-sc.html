8–10 MIN 学习目标 4 / 18

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to name fruits in Chinese and to say we like a whole group of things using 都.

Success Criteria · 我会……

1

I can say and write four fruits in Chinese

水果 · 苹果 · 香蕉 · 橘子

2

I can ask what fruit someone likes

你喜欢吃什么水果？

3

I can use 都 to cover a list

苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃。

4

I can sort food words into vegetables and fruits

蔬菜 / 水果
