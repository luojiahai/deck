How It Works

Students draw 3–4 vegetables from memory or the flashcard set on their template, labelling each in Chinese underneath.

我在超市买了……

"I bought … at the supermarket" — extension sentence, using 都 or 等等 for 2+ items.

Scaffold

Students may copy from the vocabulary sheet if needed; word bank stays visible.

Extension

Add a sentence: 我在超市买了……using 都 or 等等 if they know 2+ items.

OTR

Turn and talk — partners describe their drawing in Chinese, 30 seconds each.
