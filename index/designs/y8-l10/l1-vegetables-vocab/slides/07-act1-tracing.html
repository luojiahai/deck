How It Works

Printed worksheet has all 12 words/phrases, each with 3 tracing lines. Students trace and say each word aloud as they write.

Scaffold

Pinyin printed above every character on the tracing lines.

Extension

Cover pinyin/English columns and self-quiz meanings from memory; then write one sentence using 非常喜欢吃 + a vegetable of choice.

OTR

Every student writes every word — 100% response rate by design.
