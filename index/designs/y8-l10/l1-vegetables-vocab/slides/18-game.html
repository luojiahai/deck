Game · 拍字 Slap the Character

Two teams · 7 minutes

1

One player from each team at the board, facing the six character cards.

2

Teacher says a vegetable in Chinese — first to slap the right card wins the point.

3

Rotate every round so everyone plays.

Extension · 加油题

The teacher says the word inside a sentence instead of on its own — 我妈妈每天都吃这个。 For the last three rounds the slapper must also say the word aloud correctly to keep the point.
