Activity 1 · 分类 Sort them — We Do

Pairs · 7 minutes

1

Take the two-column sheet: 我喜欢吃 / 我不太喜欢吃.

2

Place all six words. Every word goes in a column — nothing left out.

3

Write them in characters, not pinyin.

4

Then say two of your placements aloud as full sentences.

我很喜欢吃生菜。

我不太喜欢吃菜花儿。

Extension · 加油题

Three columns instead of two — 我每天吃 / 我有时候吃 / 我不吃 — then join two placements with 除了…以外, and justify one aloud with no notes.

除了黄瓜以外，我还每天吃西红柿。
