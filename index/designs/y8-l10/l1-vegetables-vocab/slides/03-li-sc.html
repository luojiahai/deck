Learning Intention

We are learning to name common vegetables in Chinese and say how much we like (or don't like) eating them.

1

I can say 蔬菜, 生菜, 黄瓜, 菜花儿, 土豆, 西红柿 in Chinese (用拼音说出新词汇)

2

I can use 非常 to say I like/dislike something a lot — 我非常喜欢吃黄瓜

3

I can name at least 3 extra vegetables from the Extra Words list

4

I can draw and label vegetables I know in Chinese
