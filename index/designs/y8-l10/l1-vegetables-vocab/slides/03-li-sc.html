8–10 MIN 学习目标 3 / 19

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to name common vegetables in Chinese and say which ones we like eating.

Success Criteria · 我会……

1

I can say and read six vegetables in Chinese

蔬菜 · 生菜 · 黄瓜 · 土豆 · 菜花儿 · 西红柿

2

I can ask someone what vegetables they like

你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？

3

I can say how much I like a vegetable using 很 / 不太

我很喜欢吃黄瓜。
