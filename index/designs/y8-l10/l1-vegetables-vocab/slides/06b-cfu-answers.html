What does 黄瓜 mean?

黄瓜 = cucumber

huángguā — literally "yellow melon."

2

"I like eating cauliflower very much"

我非常喜欢吃菜花儿。

wǒ fēicháng xǐhuan chī càihuār

3

Error check: 我喜欢非常吃西红柿。 What's wrong?

非常 must come before 喜欢 — 我非常喜欢吃西红柿。

非常 modifies 喜欢, not 吃 — it can't sit between 喜欢 and 吃.
