Activity 2 · 采访 Vegetable interview — You Do

Stand up · three partners · 8 minutes

1

Ask three people: 你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？

2

Write each answer on your whiteboard — in characters.

3

Back at your seat, write one sentence about one person you asked.

你喜欢吃什么蔬菜？

小明很喜欢吃土豆。

Extension · 加油题

Ask five people and add a follow-up each time — 你每天都吃吗？ Then report the whole set to the class using 都. No notes on screen.

他们都喜欢吃黄瓜，可是没有人喜欢吃菜花儿。
