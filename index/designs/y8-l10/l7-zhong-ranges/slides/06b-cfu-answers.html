What does 种 measure — people, kinds, or time?

种 = kinds / types

zhǒng — a measure word for kinds/types of things, not people or time.

2

"Four or five kinds"

四五种

sì-wǔ zhǒng — two adjacent numbers said together, then 种.

3

Error check: 我喜欢两三蔬菜。 What's missing?

missing 种 — 我喜欢两三种蔬菜。

种 must appear right after the number range, before the noun.
