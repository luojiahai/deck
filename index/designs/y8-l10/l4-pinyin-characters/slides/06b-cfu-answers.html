"Which one did I say — jie or jue?"

jiē → write "jie" (keeps the i) · jué → write "jue" (ü, two dots)

Model process, not a fixed answer — the teacher calls out one syllable live and checks the class writes the matching spelling.

2

"Show me the ü symbol — two dots over the u."

ü

Same symbol students already know from 女 (nǚ) in earlier units — check every whiteboard shows two dots, not one or none.
