Writing Relay · How to Play

1️⃣

Form small teams — one line per team facing the board

2️⃣

One student per team runs to the board, writes one character from memory

3️⃣

Tags the next teammate, who repeats with the next character

Extension

Fastest-finishing team members write one word that contains each character (e.g. 苹果 for 果) on the side of the board.

Scaffold

Extension

Fastest-finishing team members write one word containing each character on the side of the board

OTR

100% write, and every team member gets one relay turn
