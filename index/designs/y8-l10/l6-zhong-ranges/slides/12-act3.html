Activity 3 · Answer in Chinese — 练习册 Ex.12

In your books · full sentences · 4 minutes

1

Full sentences, not one-word answers.

2

Every answer must contain 种.

1. 你会说几种语言？什么语言？

2. 你喜欢做几种体育运动？什么运动？

3. 你今天吃了几种蔬菜？什么蔬菜？

4. 你今天吃了几种水果？什么水果？

Extension · 加油题

Answer all four with a range rather than an exact number, and add a second clause to each with 可是 or 除了…以外.

我今天吃了两三种水果，可是我没吃蔬菜。
