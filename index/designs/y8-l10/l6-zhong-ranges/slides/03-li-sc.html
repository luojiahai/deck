8–10 MIN 学习目标 4 / 18

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say roughly how many kinds of something there are, using 种 and number ranges like 两三、三四.

Success Criteria · 我会……

1

I can use 种 as the measure word for kinds

我每天吃两三种水果。

2

I can make an approximate number from two consecutive numbers

三四天 · 四五种 · 七八个

3

I can ask and answer 你每天吃几种水果？

你每天吃几种水果？

4

I can recognise the food vocabulary from the whole of Lesson 10

蔬菜、水果 — 十八个词
