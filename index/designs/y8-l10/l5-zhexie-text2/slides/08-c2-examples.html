10–20 MIN · I DO 生词 · Cycle 2 of 2 8 / 18

例句 · See them used

Zhèxiē shuǐguǒ hěn hǎo.

这些水果很好。

These fruits are good.

Wǒ bù xǐhuan chī zhèxiē shuǐguǒ.

我不喜欢吃这些水果。

I don't like eating these fruits.

Zhèxiē shuǐguǒ wǒ dōu bù xǐhuan chī, kěshì wǒ xǐhuan chī xīguā hé lí.

这些水果我都不喜欢吃，可是我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。

Both cycles in one sentence.

Common mistake

这些个水果 is wrong — 些 is already the measure word. Never add 个 after it.
