8–10 MIN 学习目标 3 / 18

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to use 这些 / 那些 / 一些 to talk about groups of things, and to read the Text 2 dialogue about fruit.

Success Criteria · 我会……

1

I can say and write 西瓜 and 梨

西瓜 · 梨

2

I can use 这些、那些、一些 with a noun

我不喜欢吃这些水果。

3

I can read Text 2 aloud with a partner and answer questions about it

你喜欢吃什么水果？

4

I can ask who something belongs to

这些是谁的？
