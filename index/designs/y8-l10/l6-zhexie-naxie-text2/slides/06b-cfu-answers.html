What does 那些 mean?

那些 = those

nàxiē — points at a plural group that is far from the speaker.

2

Where does 都 go — before or after the verb?

都 goes BEFORE the verb

我都喜欢吃 (I ALL-like-eat), never 我喜欢吃都.

3

"Some fruits"

一些水果

yìxiē shuǐguǒ — 一些 for an unspecified amount.
