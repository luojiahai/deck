Extension · Workbook Ex.15 Translation

How It Works

Students practise pointing and saying 这些是苹果 / 那些是香蕉, then describe a "some" subset with 一些.

Scaffold

Sentence frame on the board: 这些/那些/一些 + 是/喜欢吃 + noun.

Extension

Combine with 都: 这些水果，我都喜欢吃. Fluent speakers translate 2 sentences from Workbook Ex.15 into English.

OTR

Every student says at least one sentence in pairs.
