How to Play

1️⃣

Project the textbook's cumulative radical grid (p.97) for 2 minutes — students memorise the positions.

2️⃣

Hide the grid. Teacher dictates radicals one at a time by pinyin or meaning.

3️⃣

In teams, students write each radical into its correct grid square from memory on the blank template.

🏆

Most correct squares wins.

🗂️

Setup Needed

Radical grid (p.97) on the projector + blank 6×6 grid template per team
