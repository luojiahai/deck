10–20 min · I Do

关键句型 · Key Sentence Pattern

List several things, then say you like/dislike them all with 都

X、Y、Z

，

我都喜欢吃

"X, Y, Z — I like them all."

苹果、香蕉、橘子，我都喜欢吃。

Apples, bananas, tangerines — I like them all.

我不喜欢吃这些水果。我喜欢吃西瓜和梨。

I don't like these fruits. I like watermelon and pears.

那些杂志，我都不喜欢看。

Those magazines — I don't like any of them.

这些苹果都是我的，一个也不给你！silly example

These apples are ALL mine — you don't get a single one!

都 goes before the verb — never after. Think "I ALL-like-eat", not "I like-eat all".
