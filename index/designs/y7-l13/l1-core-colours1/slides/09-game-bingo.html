颜色 Bingo

Rapid English → 汉字 recall for all six new colour words

SAMPLE 2 × 3 BINGO GRID

黑色

白色

黄色

蓝色

红色

粉红色

How to Play

1️⃣

Students draw a 2×3 grid on a mini whiteboard and write the six colours (汉字 only) in any order.

2️⃣

Teacher calls out the English colour names one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字.

🏆

First student to cross off all six shouts "Bingo!" and reads all six back aloud in Chinese to confirm.

🗂️

Setup Needed

Mini whiteboards only — already on desks

Scaffold

Allow students to keep the vocabulary table projected while drawing their grid

Extension

Winners must say a full sentence for one colour in their line: "黑色——我爸爸喜欢黑色。"
