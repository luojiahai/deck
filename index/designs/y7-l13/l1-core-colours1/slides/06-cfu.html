Check For Understanding

检查理解 · CFU Checkpoints

1

Gesture Check

黄色 = "Yellow"?

Teacher says: "黄色的意思是'yellow'。"

"对不对？" Thumbs up if 黄色 means yellow. Every student shows a thumbs-up or thumbs-down.

2

Mini Whiteboards

Write "Blue"

Teacher asks: "Write the Chinese for 'blue'."

Students write the answer and hold it up on three: "一、二、三，举起来！"

3

Cold Call

Rapid Translation

Teacher asks individual students, in order:

"Jamie，'I like black' 用中文怎么说？"
"Priya，'red' 怎么说？"

Teacher note: Aim for 100% participation on checks 1 and 2. For cold call, pre-identify students who need a stretch and give them a second colour after their first correct answer.
