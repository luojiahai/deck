活动二 · 画颜色 Draw-the-Colour

YOU DO · Draw + Label

1

Project the six colour labels one at a time as a model. Students draw a small picture in each of six boxes according to the colour labelled underneath.

2

Draw quickly — a ball, a flower, etc. — speed matters more than art. Label each drawing with the colour word in characters.

3

Partner-check: swap books, read partner's characters aloud to them.
