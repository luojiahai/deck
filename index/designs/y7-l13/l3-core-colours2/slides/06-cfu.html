L3 · CORE COLOURS 2

I Do · CFU Checkpoints · 10–20 min

10–20 min · Check For Understanding

检查理解 · CFU Checkpoints

1

Gesture Check

绿色 = "Green"?

Teacher says: "Thumbs up if 绿色 means green."

Every student shows a thumbs-up or thumbs-down.

2

Mini Whiteboards

Write "Purple"

Teacher asks: "Write 'purple' in Chinese."

Students write 紫色 on their mini whiteboards and hold up on three: "一、二、三，举起来！"

3

Cold Call

Rapid Translation

Teacher asks individual students, in order:

"Sam, how do you say 'brown' in Chinese?"
"Mia, 'orange' 呢？"

Teacher note: Aim for 100% participation on checks 1 and 2. For cold call, pre-identify 4–5 students who need a stretch — give them 棕色 and 橙色 in full sentences, not just single words.
