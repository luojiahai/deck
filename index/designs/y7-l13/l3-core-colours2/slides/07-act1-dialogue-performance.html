We Do → You Do · Activity 1 · 20–30 min

20–30 min · Flexible Practice

活动一 · Dialogue Performance

WE DO → YOU DO · 10 MIN

What

In pairs, students perform the Text 2 dialogue, then swap it out — Student B must list a different combination of colours than the textbook (using any of the 11 colours learned so far across Lessons 1–3), and Student A asks about a different colour for the yes/no question.

How

1. Model the swapped version once with a volunteer (see below).

2. Pairs rehearse (2 min).

3. Perform for a neighbouring pair.

Model — swapped version

"你喜欢什么颜色？" "我喜欢黑色、红色和绿色。" "你喜欢棕色吗？" "不喜欢。" "我也不喜欢。"

OTR: Every student speaks both roles (swap after first performance).

Scaffold

Colour word bank (all 11 words with pinyin) on the board throughout.

Extension

Add a third exchange of their own invention using 我也喜欢 (agreement) instead of disagreement.

Pairs

Rehearse 2 min, then perform for a neighbouring pair.
