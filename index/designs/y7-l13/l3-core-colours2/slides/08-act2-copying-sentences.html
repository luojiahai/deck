YOU DO · 8 MIN

What

Students copy the six new words into their books (workbook Ex.15 style), then write 2 original sentences: one using 我喜欢 with two of today's colours, one using 我不喜欢 with one.

How

1. Project stroke order for 颜, 橙, 紫, 棕, 绿, 灰.

2. Students copy each 3 times, then write their two sentences independently.

3. Cold call 2–3 students to read a sentence aloud.

我(不)喜欢和。

OTR: All students complete the copying and both sentences; cold call samples the room.

Scaffold

Sentence frame: 我(不)喜欢____和____。

Extension

Write a third sentence combining all 11 colours learned so far into one list using 、 correctly (comma convention from Text 2).

Materials

Exercise books and pens.
