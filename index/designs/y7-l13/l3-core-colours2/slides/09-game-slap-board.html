How to Play

1. All 11 colour flashcards (Lessons 1 + 3) on the board.

2. Two students race to slap the card as the teacher calls out the English name.

3. Winner stays up.

Practises / Setup

Rapid recall across the full colour set so far.

Setup: Flashcards or projected grid.

Scaffold

Pinyin visible under each character.

Extension

Winner must use the colour in a full sentence before the next round.

Pace

Keep rounds brisk — every student watches and predicts even when not up.
