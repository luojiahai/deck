L5 · RADICALS + MIXING

I Do · CFU Checkpoints · 17–20 min

17–20 min · Check For Understanding

检查理解 · CFU Checkpoints

1

Thumbs Up/Down

Is 疒 the Fire Radical?

Teacher says: "Thumbs up if 疒 is the fire radical."

Every student shows a thumbs-up or thumbs-down.

2

Mini Whiteboards

Write "Strength"

Teacher asks: "Write the radical that means 'strength'."

Students write their answer on mini whiteboards and hold up on three.

3

Cold Call

Mix a Colour

Teacher asks: "Zoe, what colour do you get from 红色+黄色?"

Teacher note: Aim for 100% participation on checks 1 and 2. For the cold call, be ready to bring in a second student if the first hesitates.
