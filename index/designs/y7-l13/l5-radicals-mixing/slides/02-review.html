Part B · Whiteboard Quiz

What: Teacher says an English colour + vehicle combo (e.g. "the green plane").

How: Students write the Chinese translation on mini whiteboards.

Items: 4 colour + vehicle combos, one at a time.

Check: Cold call students to confirm the correct answer after each item.
