L6 · SURVEY & REPORT

I Do · CFU Checkpoints · 10–20 min

Check For Understanding

检查理解 · CFU Checkpoints

1

Gesture Check

几个人喜欢红色？

Teacher asks: "Thumbs up if 几个人喜欢红色？ means 'how many people like red?'"

Every student shows a thumbs-up or thumbs-down.

2

Mini Whiteboards

Write: "Three people like green."

Students write the Chinese report sentence on their mini whiteboards and hold up on three: "一、二、三，举起来！"

3

Cold Call

Report: 6 people like yellow

Teacher asks: "Ryan, how would you report that 6 people like yellow?"

Teacher note: keep the sentence frame (____个人喜欢____。) projected throughout all three checks so every student can construct a full sentence.
