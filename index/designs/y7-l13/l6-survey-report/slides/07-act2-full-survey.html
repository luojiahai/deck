活动二 · Find Someone Who — Full Survey

YOU DO · Mingle Survey

1

Students stand, move around the room, and ask all 11 yes/no questions to 6–8 classmates, marking Yes/No tallies with 正-character tally marks.

2

Clear 10-minute time limit and a minimum of 6 classmates to survey.

3

Return to seats; in pairs, students total their tallies for 2–3 colours each.

Scaffold

Partially-filled example tally sheet as a model; allow English clarification if confused about a question

Extension

Also ask an open 你喜欢什么颜色？(non yes/no) to 2 classmates and record the free-text answer

OTR

All students are up, moving, and asking — the lesson's primary speaking OTR
