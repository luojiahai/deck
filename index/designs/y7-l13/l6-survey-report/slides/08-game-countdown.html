+1

How to Play

1️⃣

Teacher calls out each of the 11 colours one at a time.

2️⃣

For each colour, students who tallied data for it race to be first to shout out their total using the full sentence: "十个人喜欢粉红色！"

🏆

First correct full-sentence answer for each colour "wins" that round; teacher tracks a running score on the board.

🗂️

Setup Needed

Nothing beyond students' own tally sheets

Scaffold

Sentence frame (____个人喜欢____。) remains projected for students who need it

Extension

Winning students must also say which colour was least popular overall from their own data
