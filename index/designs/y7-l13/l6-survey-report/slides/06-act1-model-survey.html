Activity 1

⏱ 5 min

We Do · Show of Hands

活动一 · 示范调查

1

Teacher asks the whole class 3 of the 11 questions as a show-of-hands poll (黑色, 紫色, 绿色).

2

A volunteer tallies on the board as hands go up for each question.

3

Teacher models reporting the result using the full sentence pattern.

4

Quick, high-energy warm-up to the full activity — establishes the tally + report format before students do it in pairs/small groups.

Scaffold

Keep the report sentence frame (____个人喜欢____。) projected throughout

Extension

Volunteer tallier also calculates and reports the most popular colour of the 3

OTR

Every student votes with a hand-raise for all 3 questions
