听力示范 · Modelled Listening Example

Worked Example

小月喜欢红色，不喜欢黄色。

Teacher reads this sentence aloud twice, at natural speed, to establish the listening task format before the independent activity.

Teacher thinks aloud to model the task: "小月喜欢什么颜色？" — 红色！    "不喜欢什么颜色？" — 黄色！
