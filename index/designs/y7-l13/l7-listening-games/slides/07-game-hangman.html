猜字游戏 · Hangman

A brisk pinyin-spelling and vocabulary-retrieval game to close out today's colour practice.

How to Play

1

A student comes to the board and silently chooses a colour word or phrase from the unit (e.g. 紫色, 我喜欢, 颜色).

2

They draw dashes for each pinyin syllable on the board (e.g. h_o_n_g / s_e for 红色).

3

Class members take turns guessing letters; correct letters are filled in, incorrect ones add a line to the hangman drawing.

4

Game ends when the word is guessed or the hangman figure is complete.

Board Example

h _ o _ n _ g  /  s _ e

Syllable dashes for 红色 (hóng sè) — filled in as letters are guessed correctly

What This Practises

Spelling/pinyin recall and vocabulary retrieval under gentle time pressure.

🖊️

Setup Needed

Whiteboard/projector only

Scaffold

Restrict word choices to single colour words (not phrases) for early rounds

Extension

Once guessed, the class must use the word in a full sentence together, chorally
