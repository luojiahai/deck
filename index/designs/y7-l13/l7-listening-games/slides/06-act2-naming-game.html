Naming Game · Ex.9 p.102

物品颜色 · Object → Colour Naming Game

Teacher says an object in English; the student(s) chosen say its colour(s) in Chinese.

Model (textbook's own example)

Teacher says "panda" → Student 1 says 黑色 → Student 2 says 白色

banana

grass

fire truck

the sky

an orange

snow

a strawberry

coal

a lemon

an eggplant

chocolate

(teacher's choice)

Scaffold

Allow a partner "lifeline" — if stuck, the student may ask a neighbour quietly before answering

Extension

Fluent speakers must answer in a full sentence: 熊猫是黑色和白色的。

OTR

Go around the room — most of the class gets at least one turn; keep pace brisk and energetic
