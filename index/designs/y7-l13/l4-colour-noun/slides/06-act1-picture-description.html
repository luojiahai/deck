活动一 · Picture Description Round

WE DO

Teacher projects one coloured transport picture at a time (all 7 from Ex.1); whole class chorally names it, then individual cold calls repeat it alone. Go through all 7 pictures twice — first chorally, then popcorn-style individual cold calls (no hands up) — to build confidence before pair work.

Scaffold

Keep the transport vocabulary list (with pinyin) projected in a corner.

Extension

Fluent speakers add 我喜欢/我不喜欢 in front: 我喜欢黑色的火车。

OTR

Every student says at least 2 items chorally; several individual cold calls sample the room.
