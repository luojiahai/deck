学习目标 · Learning Intention & Success Criteria

Learning Intention

We are learning to describe objects using 颜色 + 的 + 名词 (e.g. 黑色的火车), recycling the transport vocabulary from Lesson 12.

Success Criteria

1

I can describe an object's colour using X色的Y

能用"颜色+的+名词"描述东西

2

I can say colour + vehicle combinations for all 7 transport words from Lesson 12

能说出七种"颜色+交通工具"组合

3

I can understand a spoken description and pick the matching coloured picture

能听懂并选出正确的颜色图片
