You Do · Activity 2 · 28–38 min

28–38 min · Flexible Practice · You Do (10 min)

活动二 · Partner Speaking — Describe & Guess

YOU DO

In pairs, Student A is given a card with a coloured object (from the 7 transport items) and describes it using X色的Y without showing the card; Student B guesses which picture it is from a shared set of 7 pictures on the desk.

1

Model the guessing game with a volunteer first.

2

Pairs work through all 7 cards, swapping the "describer" role halfway.

3

Whole-class check: cold call 2 pairs to demonstrate.

A: 这是黑色的……B: 汽车？A: 对！黑色的汽车。

Scaffold

Provide the full colour + transport word bank on a shared card.

Extension

Describer must add one extra detail, e.g. 这是黑色的，很大 ("It's black and very big").

OTR

All students describe at least 3 items and guess at least 3 items.
