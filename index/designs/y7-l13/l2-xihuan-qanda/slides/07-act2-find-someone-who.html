L2 · 你喜欢什么颜色

You Do · Activity 2 · 27–37 min

Activity 2

⏱ 10 min

You Do · Mingling Survey

活动二 · 找人问问题

1

Students draw a simple tally sheet: the 6 colours listed down the side, with Yes/No columns.

2

Teacher models the mingling format with two volunteers first.

3

Students stand and mingle. They ask 3 different classmates "你喜欢黑色吗？" — rotating which colour they ask about each time — and tick Yes/No.

4

Teacher circulates, listening for correct question form. Sit down; quick show of hands: "Who found someone who likes 红色?"

Scaffold

Sentence frame card: 你喜欢____吗？ with colour word bank attached

Extension

Ask a 4th question using 什么颜色 (open form) instead of yes/no, and note the answer

OTR

All students up and moving, each asks at least 3 questions
