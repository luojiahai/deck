Thumbs Up/Down — 你喜欢什么颜色？的意思是"what colour do you like?"，对不对？

👍  对！你喜欢什么颜色？= "what colour do you like?"

2

Error Correction — 什么颜色你喜欢？ 这句话有什么问题？

✅  Word order: 什么颜色 must come after 喜欢, not before.

什么颜色你喜欢？

→

你喜欢什么颜色？

3

Mini Whiteboards — the question you'd ask to find out if someone likes green

✅  你喜欢____吗？

The blank stands in for 绿色 (green) — a colour word we haven't learned yet. Any student who wrote 你喜欢____吗？ with the pattern correct earns full credit, even without the colour word.
