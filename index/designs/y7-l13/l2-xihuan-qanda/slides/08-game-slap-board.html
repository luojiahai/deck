How to Play

1️⃣

Six colour flashcards spread on the board (or projected in a grid).

2️⃣

Two students stand at the board. Teacher asks a question aloud, e.g. "谁喜欢蓝色？" or simply calls a colour in English.

🏆

Both students race to slap the matching card; the faster one stays up for the next round.

🗂️

Setup Needed

Printed colour flashcards or a projected grid; nothing else

Scaffold

Keep pinyin under each character on the flashcards

Extension

The winner must answer a follow-up in a full sentence before the next round starts, e.g. "你喜欢蓝色吗？" → "喜欢，我喜欢蓝色。"
