L2 · 你喜欢什么颜色

We Do · Activity 1 · 20–27 min

Activity 1

⏱ 7 min

We Do · Whole Class Chain

活动一 · 传话问答链

1

Whole class sits in a circle or row. Student 1 asks Student 2: "你喜欢什么颜色？"

2

Student 2 answers with a Lesson-1 colour, then turns and asks Student 3 the same question — the chain continues around the room.

3

Teacher models the first 2 exchanges with a volunteer before starting the chain.

4

Set a brisk pace — no repeating the same colour twice in a row.

Scaffold

Colour flashcards visible on desks for students who need a prompt

Extension

Once comfortable, add 也: "我也喜欢红色。" (为什么？ follow-ups not yet taught)

OTR

Every student asks once and answers once as the chain passes through

Sample Chain Exchange

S1 →S2: 你喜欢什么颜色？

S2: 我喜欢蓝色。

S2 →S3: 你喜欢什么颜色？

S3: 我喜欢黄色。

…continues around the room
