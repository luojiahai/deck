Activity 2 · 小摊 · 课本 p.112 Ex.4

GROUPS OF 3–4 · 12 MIN

西红柿 ¥3.00/斤　·　菜花儿 ¥2.50/斤　·　苹果 ¥10.00/五个　·　比萨饼 ¥60.00/个

Half the groups keep the stall. The other half go shopping — then swap after five minutes.

At each stall: ask two prices using both question forms, buy at least two things, ask 一共多少钱？

The stallholder works the total out out loud.

Extension · 加油题

You have 50 块 and must leave having spent between 45 and 50. Say aloud what you are not buying and why. Then report your total and your change — in Chinese.

我想买比萨饼，可是六十块，太贵了。我买两斤西红柿和一斤菜花儿。
