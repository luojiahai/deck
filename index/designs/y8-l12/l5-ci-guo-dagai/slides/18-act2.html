Activity 2 · 问一问 · Find Someone Who

MOVING · 10 MIN

A different name for each question. Ask in Chinese, write the answer in Chinese.

① 你一星期吃几次快餐？　② 你一个月看几次电影？

③ 你一星期做几次体育运动？　④ 你一个月吃几次中餐？

⑤ 你一星期看几次电视？　⑥ 你每个月大概花多少钱买衣服？

Eight minutes moving, two minutes reporting. Three students report one classmate each.

Extension · 加油题

Report on four classmates as a connected paragraph — compare them with 比, group them with 都. Deliver it standing, with nothing written in front of you.

美文和大生都一星期吃三次快餐，可是冬冬比他们吃得少，他一个月只吃一次。
