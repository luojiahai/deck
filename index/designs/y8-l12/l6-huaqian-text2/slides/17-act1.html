Activity 1 · 开店 · 课本 p.120 Ex.16

GROUPS OF 3–4 · 10 MIN

Each group opens either a fresh market or a clothing shop.

菜市场　黄瓜 ¥1.20/斤 · 生菜 ¥1.00/斤 · 西红柿 ¥2.00/斤

　　　　苹果 ¥10.00/四个 · 西瓜 ¥1.20/斤 · 橘子 ¥10.00/五个

服装店　牛仔裤 ¥260.00/条 · 衬衫 ¥150.00/件 · 毛衣 ¥220.00/件 · 外套 ¥200.00/件

At each stall: ask two prices, buy at least two things, ask 一共多少钱？, and finish with a verdict —— 贵 or 不算太贵.

Extension · 加油题

You have 500 块 and must come back with a full outfit and a family dinner, visiting both a shop and a market. Compare two stalls out loud with 比, justify each buy with 因为……所以, then report: what you bought, 一共花了多少钱, and what you left behind and why.
