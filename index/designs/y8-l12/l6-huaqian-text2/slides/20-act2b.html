Activity 2 · 写五句 · A real meal out

BOOKS · 8 MIN

Five sentences about a meal you really had —— when, where, what you ate, what you drank, what it cost and whether it was worth it.

Every sentence must carry something from this lesson: a 了, an 等等, a price, 一共, 不算太贵.

Three students read theirs out at the end.

Extension · 加油题

Write it as the 练习册 p.137 Ex.22 essay instead —— your favourite restaurant: how often you go （次）, what food you like, what you normally eat （一般）, what one meal costs （大概……左右）, and one sentence on why, using 得. Six to eight sentences, nothing on screen.
