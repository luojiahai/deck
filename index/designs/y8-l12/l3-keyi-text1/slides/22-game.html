Game · 写字接力 · Writing Relay

TEAMS · 5 MIN

Teams line up. Teacher calls a character by its English meaning —— “household!”

First student runs up, writes it, runs back. Next student.

Wrong stroke order can be challenged by the other team for a bonus point.

Extension · 加油题

Call by pinyin only, and the runner must write a word containing the character, not the bare character.

户 → 门户　·　革 → 皮革　·　足 → 足球
