Activity 2 · 听一听，打勾 · 课本 p.114 Ex.6 · CD 58

SOLO, THEN PAIRS · 6 MIN

Six items, three options each. Played twice. Tick in the book, then check in pairs.

The items reach back across the whole year —— weather, illness, 除了……以外, food, phone language.

Don't pre-teach. Working around an unknown word is exactly what the test's listening asks for.

Extension · 加油题

Write the whole sentence you hear for items 3, 4 and 5 instead of ticking. Then rewrite item 5 about yourself.
