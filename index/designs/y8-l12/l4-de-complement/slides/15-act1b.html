Activity 1 · 这炒面怎么样？

PAIRS · 7 MIN

A：这炒面怎么样？　B：非常好吃。

Work through all eight dishes.

The rule: no rung of the ladder twice by the same student. That forces all seven degrees into play.

Three pairs read theirs out at the end.

Extension · 加油题

B justifies every answer with a second sentence using 因为 and something from an earlier lesson. Then A asks a follow-up —— and B answers without looking at the board.

不太好吃，因为我不喜欢吃青菜，我一般吃水果。
