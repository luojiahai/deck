Activity 2 · 用「得」造句 · 课本 p.117 Ex.10

BOOKS · 8 MIN

Six pictures. One sentence each, with 得 and a different degree word every time.

游泳　·　打网球　·　弹钢琴　·　穿衣服　·　画画儿　·　跳舞

Pinyin stays up for two minutes, then comes down.

Then 练习册 p.137 Ex.21 as a fast check —— match the six sentence halves.

Extension · 加油题

Write all six in both shapes, then a four-sentence paragraph about one real person in your family: what they do well, what they don't, what they cook. Every sentence with 得, joined with 可是 and 除了……以外. No word bank.
