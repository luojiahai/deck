Game · 拍价钱 · Slap the Board

TWO AT THE BOARD · 6 MIN

Nine amounts written large on the board, in characters.

五毛 · 一块 · 五块 · 十块 · 二十块 · 五十块 · 一百块 · 三块五 · 十二块八毛

Teacher calls an amount — first to slap it scores. Winner stays on.

Extension · 加油题

Call a sum instead of an amount （五块加五块 → slap 十块）, or call it in 元/角 so the student has to slap the 块/毛 version.
