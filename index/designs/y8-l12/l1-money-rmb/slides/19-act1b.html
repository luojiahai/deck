Activity 1 · 卡片对练 · Card pairs

PAIRS · 7 MIN

Shuffle your card set. One holds a card up, the other says it. 30 seconds each way.

Second round: put two or three cards down together — the partner says the total.

五十块 + 二十块 + 五毛 → 七十块五毛

Extension · 加油题

Say each total twice — once as 块/毛, once as 元/角 — then say what it could buy.

七十块五毛。七十元五角。这些钱可以吃七个汉堡包。
