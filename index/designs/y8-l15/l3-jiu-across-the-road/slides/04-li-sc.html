Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say exactly where a place is, and stress that it is close by.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say 马路、对面、火车站

能说「马路」「对面」「火车站」

2

I can use 就 to mean right there

会用「就」说「就在马路对面」

3

I can say how quickly I get somewhere with 走路……分钟就到了

会说「走路五分钟就到了」
