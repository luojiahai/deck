轻松学中文 2 · Unit 5 · Lesson 15

社区

Neighbourhood · Year 8

Six 50-minute lessons, 154 slides. Lesson 13 drew the house and Lesson 14 furnished it; this one steps outside the front door. Twenty new-word entries spread across the sequence — three or four per lesson, each pair taught as a three-slide cycle: the words, then the words used, then the students using them. Every lesson carries a CFU board straight after the new pattern, question-only: there is no answer slide anywhere in the series, the two radical boards in Lesson 6 included. Revision happens inside every lesson rather than in a review lesson at the end.

Grammar Focus · 本课语法点

L1

附近有 A、B、C 等

List what is near you — and the 、 that separates the items

L2

A 离 B 远／近　·　挺…的

离 is not a verb: it always needs 远 or 近 after it

L3

就

Right there, just across the road — and it goes before the verb

L4

先…，然后…

A journey with two legs, and 从 A 怎么去 B？

L5

要坐多长时间？　·　大约

How long it takes, answered approximately

L6

全部一起用

No new pattern — the whole set running at once, on a map

Lesson 1 of 6

我家附近有花店

Shops near my home

Lesson 14 furnished the house; this one opens the front door. Four shops, three of which are a character students already own plus 店 — and the list pattern that strings them together.

New words · 4附近 · 花店 · 文具店 · 家具店

Lesson 2 of 6

超市离我家不远

Far or near

One character does the whole job — 离 names the span between two places. It is not a verb, and the error it invites (a 离 sentence with nothing after it) is pre-empted on a slide of its own.

New words · 4超市 · 离 · 远 · 挺

Lesson 3 of 6

就在马路对面

Right across the road

The small character that was deliberately skipped in Lesson 2. 就 has no English word of its own and changes the whole feeling of a sentence — and it goes before the verb, which is the error this lesson exists to prevent.

New words · 4马路 · 对面 · 火车站 · 路

Lesson 4 of 6

先坐船，然后坐车

First… then…

A journey with two legs, and the question that asks for one. The practice block is three games end to end — the teacher picked game-dominated for this lesson, so the consolidation task is folded into the relay rather than sitting beside it.

New words · 3电影院 · 船 · 然后

Lesson 5 of 6

要坐多长时间？

How long does it take?

The obvious next question after Lesson 4, and the answer that admits it is an estimate. Text 2 closes here, and with it the model dialogue the whole unit test is built on.

New words · 4时间 · 大约 · 飞机 · 机场

Lesson 6 of 6

我们的社区

My whole neighbourhood

Four more shops, all of them a known character plus 店 — and then the real work: a map, five patterns running at once, a stranger at the information desk, and the unit’s one radical slot, run as the lesson’s game.

New words · 4水果店 · 书店 · 理发店 · 快餐店

Six lessons, not seven. The sequence ends on content, not revision — there is no 复习 lesson. Each lesson opens with a cumulative Review phase instead, and Lesson 6's plenary looks back across all six.

No answer slides. At the teacher's request, and enforced in the builder rather than left to care: the CFU helper takes no answers parameter, so an answer slide cannot be added by copying a sibling.

No pinyin exercises — but radicals are in, in one place. Textbook Ex. 7 (s/sh, CD 72) and Ex. 3 (the 36-character memorise-and-dictate grid) are out of the series entirely. Radicals were too, until the consequence was weighed: Unit 5 Test parts 3 and 4 ask for the radical of a character and the simple character inside a compound, so students would have met two of the eleven test parts cold. Lesson 6 now carries a five-minute slot, run as that lesson's game — p.147 Ex. 9's four simple characters (反 血 习 山), then the exact six characters of Test part 3 and the exact six compounds of part 4. It belongs in this deck rather than only in Lesson 14's: three of part 4's six compounds hide a character this sequence itself teaches. Every other part of the test — the floor plan, the furniture matching, measure words, 离／有／在／就／先…然后… sentence building, translation both ways, opposites, the reading comprehension and the essay — is covered across Lessons 13 to 15.

Pinyin stays. Excluding the pinyin exercises is not the same as hiding pinyin: it sits on every new-word card and above every example sentence, then comes off the board during practice, which is the Year 8 norm.
