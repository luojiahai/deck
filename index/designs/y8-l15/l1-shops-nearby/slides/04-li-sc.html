Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to name the shops near our home and list them in one sentence.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can name four shops in Chinese

能说出四个商店的名字

2

I can say what is near my home using 我家附近有……

能用「我家附近有……」说家附近有什么

3

I can list several things in one sentence with 、 and 等

会用「、」和「等」把几样东西放进一句话里
