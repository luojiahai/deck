答案 · Round 2 · 改错句

1

我进校门就看见图书馆。 →

我一进校门就看见图书馆。

Missing 一 — the pattern is always 一……就……, never 就 alone.

2

我一会儿就借给你。 →

我一会儿就还给你。

借给 (to lend) confused with 还给 (to return) — returning something you borrowed uses 还, not 借.

Bonus point awarded if the team also correctly named which word was missing or wrong.
