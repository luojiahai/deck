How to Play

1️⃣

Project the 8 结果补语 words, scattered on the screen (as shown left).

2️⃣

Two students come to the front — or use mini whiteboards for a seated version, writing the answer instead of touching the screen.

3️⃣

Teacher says an English meaning (e.g. "sold out"); students race to point to / write the matching word.

🏆

First correct answer wins the round; repeat with new pairs of students until most students have had a turn.

🖥️

Setup Needed

Projector only — or mini whiteboards per pair for the seated version

Scaffold

Allow students to keep the 4-category table visible in a corner of the screen

Extension

Winning students must use the word in a full sentence before the next round starts
