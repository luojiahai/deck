结果补语 · Complement of Result

这些词都是"动词 + 补语"。补语告诉我们这个动作的结果——不只是"做"，而是"做完了"或"做到了"。

CategoryPairsMeaning
Perceive with senses 看见 / 听见 see / hear (successfully perceived)
Achieve / attain 看到 / 找到 / 买到 see / find / buy (successfully)
Finish completely 吃光 / 喝光 / 卖光 eat / drink / sell (all gone)
Finish successfully 买好 / 做好 finish buying / finish doing

Modelled Examples · 例句

我在文具店里找到了一支铅笔。

I found a pencil in the stationery store — successfully.

蛋糕已经吃光了。

The cake is completely eaten — none left.

老师说的话，我一个字都没听见！

I didn't hear a single word the teacher said! (silly example)

Contrast · 看 vs 看见

我看铅笔 → 我看见铅笔

哪个说明我真的看到了？ 第二个 — 看见 confirms the seeing actually happened.
