活动1 · 配对造句 Pair Sentence-Making

活动 1 · Activity 1

👥 Pair Work

  • Teacher models one example per category first, same as in I Do
  • Partner A picks a category and says a sentence; Partner B identifies which category it belongs to — then swap roles
  • Repeat until every student has made sentences from all 4 categories
  • Cold call 3–4 pairs to share their best sentence with the class

Model Answer Format · 句型支架

我在书包里找到了橡皮。

4 Categories · 结果补语

看见 / 听见 看到 / 找到 / 买到 吃光 / 喝光 / 卖光 买好 / 做好
OTR Turn-and-talk requires every student to produce at least 2 sentences during the pair activity

Scaffold

Keep the 4-category table projected throughout the pair activity

Extension

Use a stationery item as the object in at least 2 sentences, e.g. 我在书包里找到了橡皮。
