L6 · SHOP ROLEPLAY Game 2 · Shop Relay · 32–38 min

Game 2

⏱ 6 min

Rotating Pairs

购物接力 · Shop Roleplay Relay

Perform the shop dialogue, then swap partners and repeat with a new item

DIALOGUE SKELETON

买家你好！我想买……

卖家好的，给你。

买家……多少钱？

卖家……块……毛。

🔁 swap partners after 90 sec

How to Play

1️⃣

In pairs, students perform a short shop dialogue (buyer asks for an item + price, seller answers), using any items from Lessons 1, 4, and today.

2️⃣

After 90 seconds, students swap partners (move one seat over) and repeat with a new item, alternating who plays buyer/seller.

3️⃣

Repeat for 2–3 rounds so students practise with multiple partners and multiple items.

🖥️

Setup Needed

None — projector displays the sentence pattern as a reminder throughout

Scaffold

Students may keep a card in front of them listing 5 items + made-up prices to draw from

Extension

Fluent pairs negotiate ("太贵了！便宜一点，行吗？") as an improvised extra line — bonus, not required

OTR

Every pair performs 2–3 rounds with different partners and items
