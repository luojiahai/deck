Game 1

⏱ 6 min

Team Competition

猜价钱 · Price Guessing

In teams, guess the price of the projected item using the X块Y毛 pattern

GUESS THE PRICE

钢笔

？块？毛

How to Play

1️⃣

Teacher projects a stationery item (e.g. 钢笔) without a price.

2️⃣

In teams, students discuss briefly and write a guessed price in Chinese on a mini whiteboard (X块Y毛), hold up on three.

3️⃣

Teacher reveals the "real" price (teacher-set for the game); closest team without going over wins the round.

🏆

Repeat for 4–5 items.

🖥️

Setup Needed

Projector only — mini whiteboards for teams

Scaffold

Provide a price range on the board (0–200 块) to narrow guesses

Extension

Winning teams must say their guess as a full question-answer pair: "一支钢笔多少钱？——二十五块。"

OTR

Every team writes and holds up a guess for all 4–5 rounds
