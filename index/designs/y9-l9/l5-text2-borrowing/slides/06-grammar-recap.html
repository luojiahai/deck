语法重点 · Key Grammar

借 vs 借给 — Borrow AND Lend

借 alone typically means "borrow" — 我借你的铅笔 = I borrow your pencil. 借给 means "lend to" — 借给我一支 = lend me one. Context determines direction.

借你的计算器 (I borrow)  ·  借给我一支 (you lend me)

还 (huán) vs 还 (hái) — Don't Confuse!

还 (huán, 2nd tone) = return something borrowed. Do not confuse with 还 (hái) = also/still — same character, same tone number, different meaning; context distinguishes them.

我一会儿就还给你。 = I'll return it to you soon.

别 + Verb — Negative Imperative

别 + verb tells someone not to do something. 别 is informal; 不要 is also acceptable and more neutral.

别忘记 = don't forget

行 vs 可以 — Informal vs Formal OK

行 = informal "OK/all right." 可以 = more formal "may/can." Both appear in the dialogue.

行吗？ / 可以吗？
