28–38 min · You Do (10 min)

活动二 · 写你自己的借东西对话

YOU DO · Write & Peer-Check

1

Teacher models one new example live, thinking aloud about word choice: "如果我想借一本练习本，我可以说……'借给我一本练习本，行吗？'"

2

Individual writing (6 min): write a 3–4 line borrowing dialogue between two classmates, choosing your own item to borrow.

3

Peer-check (2 min): swap books with a neighbour and check for 借/借给 and 还/别 accuracy.

4

Cold call 2–3 pairs to read their dialogue aloud.

OTR: all students write independently; peer-check requires every student to read and mark a partner's work.
