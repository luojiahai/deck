How to Play

1️⃣

Students draw a 3×3 grid on the back of a mini whiteboard and fill it with 9 of today's vocabulary items (汉字 only).

2️⃣

Teacher calls out English meanings one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字.

3️⃣

First student to cross off a full row, column, or diagonal calls "Bingo!" and reads their winning line aloud.

📣

Teacher calls English → students cross off 汉字

e.g. "calculator" → students cross off 计算器

Scaffold

Allow students to keep the vocabulary table projected for their first grid

Extension

Winning students must use their winning word in a full sentence from today's dialogue pattern

Setup

Mini whiteboards only — no photocopies needed
