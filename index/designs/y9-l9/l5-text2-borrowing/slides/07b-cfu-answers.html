CFU 2 · Cold Call

"'借给我一支铅笔' — who is lending, who is borrowing?"

Answer

The listener (you) is lending; the speaker (me) is borrowing — 借给 always points toward the person receiving the item.

CFU 3 · Error Find

What's wrong?
我一会儿就借给你。

Answer

He is returning something already borrowed, not lending — needs 还给 (huán gěi), not 借给.

✓ 我一会儿就还给你。
