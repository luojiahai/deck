L5 · TEXT 2 BORROWING We Do · Activity 1 · 20–28 min

20–28 min · We Do (8 min)

活动一 · 听写 + 课文二填空

WE DO · Dictation + Gap-Fill

1

Dictation round: teacher says the English meaning, students write the 汉字 for 5 items from today's vocabulary — hold up on three each time.

2

Gap-fill: project the Text 2 dialogue with 借, 还, 别, 行, 计算器 removed. Students copy and complete it in their exercise books.

3

Cold call to check answers — students read the completed sentence aloud.

OTR: every student writes and holds up a whiteboard for the dictation; every student completes the gap-fill independently.

Scaffold

Word bank of the 5 missing words projected alongside the gapped dialogue

Extension

After filling the gaps, underline which word is 借 (borrow) vs 借给 (lend) in the dialogue and label each

Gapped Dialogue · 5 Blanks

— 你有铅笔吗？

— 有。

— 我今天忘记带铅笔了。借给我一支用一下，吗？

— 。

— 你的用一下，可以吗？我一会儿就给你。

— 对不起，我正用呢。你跟的同学借吧。

— 好吧。
