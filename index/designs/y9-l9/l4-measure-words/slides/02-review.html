Project 6 quick-fire prompts mixing L1–L3 content — e.g. "尺子" → English; "一……就……" starter → complete it; "找_____" → complete with the correct complement. Students write on mini whiteboards, hold up on three: "一、二、三，举起来！" Cold call to confirm 2–3 items.

Part B · Grammar Recall

一……就…… (L2)

他一放学回家做作业。

结果补语 Complement of Result (L3)

我找了半天，可是找到。

Cold call 2 students to complete each sentence aloud; class echoes chorally. Target: 一放学就回家做作业  ·  可是没找到 (到 = accomplished; 没……到 = not yet accomplished).

Connection · 承上启下

你们已经会说很多文具的名字了。但是中文说"一个东西"的时候，需要用对的量词——不是所有东西都用"个"！今天我们专门学文具的量词。

You already know lots of stationery words. But in Chinese, saying "one [item]" needs the correct measure word — not everything uses 个! Today we focus on measure words for stationery.
