L4 · MEASURE WORDS Game · Telephone Relay · 35–43 min

Game

⏱ 8 min

Whole Class · Teams

传声筒 · Telephone Measure Word Relay

Whisper down the line, then say the "measure word + item" phrase aloud — accuracy wins the point

How to Play

1️⃣

Divide the class into 2–3 teams, each standing in a line.

2️⃣

Teacher whispers a "measure word + item" phrase (e.g. 一把尺子) to the first student in each line.

3️⃣

Each student whispers what they heard to the next student down the line. The last student says the phrase aloud.

4️⃣

Compare to the original — correct = 1 point for the team. Repeat with 4–5 phrases.

🧰

Setup: none beyond the classroom

Teacher needs a list of 5 target phrases ready before the game starts

一支铅笔 yì zhī qiānbǐ

一本练习本 yì běn liànxíběn

一把尺子 yì bǎ chǐzi

一本日记本 yì běn rìjìběn

一本课本 yì běn kèběn

Scaffold

Start with shorter, more familiar phrases (一支铅笔) before moving to less familiar ones (一本日记本)

Extension

For the final round, use a full sentence instead of just the phrase (e.g. 我借了一把尺子。) to raise the difficulty
