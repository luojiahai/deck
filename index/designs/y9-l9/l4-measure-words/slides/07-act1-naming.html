L4 · MEASURE WORDS We Do · Activity 1 · 20–27 min

Activity 1

⏱ 7 min

We Do · Picture Flash

活动一 · 看图说名字 (Act. 7, p.86)

1

Teacher projects each picture for 3 seconds — class chorally names the item.

2

After naming, the 汉字 fades in to confirm.

3

After all 14: display all at once. Cold call individual students to point and name.

4

Final round: teacher says the English name, class chorally says the Chinese — no visual support.

OTR: choral naming for every picture; final round is 100% response by design.

Scaffold

Keep pinyin visible on the first pass through all 14 pictures; remove for the second and third passes

Extension

After naming each item, fluent speakers immediately add the measure word: "铅笔——一支铅笔。"
