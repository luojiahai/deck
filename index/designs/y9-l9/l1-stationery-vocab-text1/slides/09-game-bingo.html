How to Play

1️⃣

Students draw a 3×3 grid on the back of a mini whiteboard and fill it with 9 of the 11 vocabulary items (汉字 only), in any order.

2️⃣

Teacher calls out English meanings one at a time; students cross off the matching 汉字.

🏆

First student to cross off a full row, column, or diagonal calls "Bingo!" and reads their winning line aloud in Chinese to confirm.

🗂️

Setup Needed

Mini whiteboards only — already on desks

Scaffold

Allow students to keep the vocabulary table projected for their first grid to draw from

Extension

Winning students must read their winning line as a full sentence: "尺子——我的尺子在文具盒里边。"
