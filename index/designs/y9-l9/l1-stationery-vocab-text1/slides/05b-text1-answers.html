答案 · Text 1 Comprehension

1

文具店里边有什么？

文具店里边有铅笔、尺子、橡皮等。

Inside the stationery store are pencils, rulers, erasers etc. — any 3 of the 7 listed items are acceptable.

2

文具店在哪儿？

文具店在五号教学楼一楼。

The stationery store is on the ground floor of Building No. 5 — visible right as you enter the school gate.

3

除了文具，文具店还卖什么？

文具店还卖小说、课本、卡片。

It also sells novels, textbooks, and cards.
