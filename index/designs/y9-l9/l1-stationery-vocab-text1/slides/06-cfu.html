19–20 min · Check For Understanding

检查理解 · CFU Checkpoints

1

Gesture Check

文具盒 = "Pencil Case"?

Teacher says: "文具盒的意思是'pencil case'。"

"对不对？大拇指向上表示对，向下表示错。" Every student shows a thumbs-up or thumbs-down.

2

Mini Whiteboards

Write "Ruler"

Teacher asks: "Write the Chinese for 'ruler'."

Students write 尺子 on their mini whiteboards and hold up on three: "一、二、三，举起来！"

3

Cold Call

Rapid Translation

Teacher asks individual students, in order:

"Alex，'exercise book' 用中文怎么说？"
"Priya，'novel' 呢？"

Teacher note: Aim for 100% participation on checks 1 and 2. For cold call, pre-identify 4–5 students who need a stretch — give them the harder items like 卷笔刀/日记本.
