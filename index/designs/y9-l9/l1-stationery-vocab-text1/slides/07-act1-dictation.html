Round 1 (pinyin allowed): teacher says the English meaning, students write pinyin or 汉字, hold up on three: "一、二、三，举起来！"

2

Teacher scans and confirms across the room.

3

Round 2 (汉字 only): repeat with all 11 items — 汉字 required, no pinyin credit.

4

After each round, reveal the correct answer on the projector so students self-correct.
