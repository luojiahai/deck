Activity 1

借东西 · Borrowing Roleplay

⏱ 7 min

We Do → You Do · Speaking

Based on Act. 10, p. 88

Instructions

  1. Teacher models one round with a volunteer: borrow → lend
  2. Teacher models a second round: borrow → politely decline + redirect
  3. In pairs, students work through all 7 items, swapping roles (borrower/lender) halfway
  4. Cold call 3–4 pairs to perform one exchange for the class

Sentence Frames

A: 借你的______用一下，行吗？/可以吗？

B: 行。/可以。 or 对不起，我正用呢。你跟别的同学借吧。

7 Items to Borrow

1铅笔pencil
2尺子ruler
3橡皮eraser
4卷笔刀sharpener
5计算器calculator
6课本textbook
7练习本exercise book

Scaffold

Sentence frames projected throughout for both roles (A: borrower, B: lender)

Extension

Vary the decline reason beyond "我正用呢" for at least one item, e.g. 对不起，这是新的，我不想借。
