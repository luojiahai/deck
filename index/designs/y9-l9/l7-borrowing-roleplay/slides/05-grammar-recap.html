语法回顾 · 行 vs 可以

Informal

行

xíng

"OK / all right" — used in casual requests among classmates.

借给我一支用一下，行吗？——行。

More Formal

可以

kěyǐ

"May / can" — slightly more polite, works in any context.

可以借一下吗？——可以，给你。

💡

Both are interchangeable in most casual requests, but 可以 sounds more polite/formal.

Use either with classmates; prefer 可以 when speaking to teachers or people you don't know well.
