Project the full Text 2 dialogue with these 5 words blanked out. Cold call students to fill each one in turn; class echoes the full sentence chorally.

Part B · Mini-Whiteboard Check

Translate: "May I borrow this?"

All students write their translation, hold up on three. Accept: 可以借一下吗？ or 借一下，行吗？ — also recap L6's price-asking pattern (多少钱？) as a quick chorus.

这节课我们要更深入地练习"借东西"——不只是问，还要练习答应或者拒绝，还要想一想：什么东西你会借给同学，什么不会？
