Survey + Mingling

你会借给同学什么？

⏱ 8 min

Find Someone Who · Speaking

Based on Act. 11 & 12, p. 88

Instructions

  1. Stand up and interview 3–4 classmates, noting one item + reason from each in your exercise book
  2. Reconvene as a class; cold call 4–5 students to share what they found, e.g. "Sam说他会借铅笔，但是不会借他的日记本，因为……"
  3. Wrap up with 2–3 short partner borrowing dialogues, performed on the spot by volunteer pairs

Question Frame
