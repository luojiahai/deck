今日目标 · Today's Goal

Learning Intention

We are learning to ask to borrow and lend items politely, and to translate sentences using 行 and 可以.

1

I can ask to borrow an item and respond by lending it or politely declining 能礼貌地借东西、回答借东西的请求

2

I can translate sentences using 行 and 可以 能翻译用"行"和"可以"的句子

3

I can explain what I would and wouldn't lend, and why 能说出我会借什么、不会借什么，为什么
