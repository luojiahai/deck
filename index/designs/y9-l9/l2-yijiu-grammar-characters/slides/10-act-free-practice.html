YOU DO · Independent Writing

Write 2 more 一……就…… sentences about your own daily life (e.g. getting to school, arriving home) in your exercise book — without a word bank.

OTR · all students write independently; cold call 2–3 to share aloud

Scaffold

Sentence frame still projected for students who need it: 我一______就______。

Extension

One sentence must use a verb not modelled today (e.g. 一放学就……).

Ideas to Get Started

上学 — getting to school

到家 — arriving home

放学 — school finishing

起床 — waking up
