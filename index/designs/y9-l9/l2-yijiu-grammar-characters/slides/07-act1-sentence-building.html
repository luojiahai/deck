L2 · 一…就… GRAMMAR We Do · Activity 1 · 20–27 min

20–27 min · We Do · Based on Act. 2, p.82

活动一 · 一……就…… 造句

WE DO · Sentence Building

1

Teacher projects 图书馆 first and models aloud, thinking through the two actions: "图书馆——一进图书馆就要安静。"

2

For pictures 2–4 (客厅、礼堂、厨房), students write their own 一……就…… sentence on mini whiteboards.

3

Students hold up their whiteboard on three; teacher scans and confirms.

OTR · every student writes and holds up a sentence for every picture

Scaffold

Sentence frame projected: 一______就______。 with a word bank next to each picture (安静, 看电视, 吃饭, 睡觉, 做饭)

Extension

Use a different pair of actions from the word bank each time and add one extra detail — e.g. 我一进客厅就打开电视看新闻。
