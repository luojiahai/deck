GAME 2 33–38 min Pairs

一……就…… 句子重组 · Sentence Jumble

How to Play

1

Teacher projects a jumbled sentence, word by word out of order.

2

In pairs, students write the correctly ordered sentence on a mini whiteboard.

3

First pair to hold up a fully correct sentence wins the round. Repeat with 2 more jumbled sentences.

就

一

你

进

Setup Needed

Projector only.

Round 1 Answer

你一进校门就能看见图书馆。

Scaffold

Give the first word (你) as a starting point for the first jumble.

Extension

For the third jumble, remove one word entirely and have students supply it from memory before reordering.
