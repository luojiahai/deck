汉字大挑战 · Character Recall Relay

How to Play — Act. 3 Style

1

Recap (15 sec): briefly re-project the 12-character grid.

2

Hide the grid. Divide the class into two teams — left half vs right half.

3

Teacher calls out an English meaning (e.g. "knife"); the next student in rotation from alternating teams writes the matching 汉字 on their mini whiteboard and holds it up.

4

Correct = 1 point for the team; incorrect = other team may steal.

Scaffold

If a student hesitates, teacher may give the radical/component as a hint (e.g. "目 looks like an eye turned sideways").

Extension

Winning students must also give the pinyin, not just the character.
