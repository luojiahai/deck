L7 · CONSOLIDATION Activity 2 · You Do
Activity 2
Mingle Speaking
⏱ 8 min
You Do · Act.10-style

Instructions

  1. Circulate the room and ask 8 classmates the questions on the right
  2. Note down a one-word answer for each on your question sheet
  3. Extension: ask one unscripted follow-up question after each scripted one

Questions · 词库 8 Questions

1你昨天晚上八点在干什么呢？What were you doing at 8pm last night?
2你星期六几点打篮球？What time do you play basketball on Saturday?
3你今天早上七点在做什么呢？What were you doing at 7am today?
4你什么时候开始学跳舞？When did you start learning dance?
5你现在在做作业呢吗？Are you doing homework right now?
6你星期天几点起床？What time do you get up on Sunday?
7除了跳舞以外，你还喜欢什么运动？Besides dance, what other sport do you like?
8你朋友什么时候一起去游泳？When are you and your friend going swimming together?
Scaffold
All 8 questions pre-printed with pinyin; sentence-frame answers modelled on the board
Extension
Students ask one unscripted follow-up question of their own choosing after each scripted one
OTR
Every student asks and answers 8 times each — high-volume oral practice
