L7 · CONSOLIDATION Activity 1 · We Do
Activity 1
Grammar Relay
⏱ 8 min
We Do · Act.8-style

Instructions

  1. Teams line up at the board. Teacher reveals one prompt card at a time (10 total).
  2. The first student in each team runs up and writes a correct sentence using the required pattern.
  3. Team scores a point if correct — teacher or class checks.
  4. Next student in line goes for the next prompt.

Relay Prompts · 10 Items

1besides basketball…除了……以外
2besides sports, another hobby…除了……以外
3starting from age 5…从……开始
4starting from this year…从……开始
5what are you doing right now?在……呢
6say you're cooking right now在……呢
7invite a friend to swim吧
8invite a friend to watch a movie吧
9ask when someone's birthday is什么时候
10ask what time practice starts几点
Scaffold
The 5-pattern recap chart stays projected throughout; struggling teams may consult it before writing
Extension
Once a team's sentence is correct, a second member adds a follow-up sentence using a different pattern before the next prompt
OTR
Whole-team engagement — every student gets at least 2 turns across the 10 items
