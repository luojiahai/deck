关键句型 · 吧 + 什么时候 vs 几点
How to make a friendly suggestion — and ask "when" the right way
我们一起去
……吧！
→
"Let's go … together!"
我们一起去游泳吧！
Let's go swimming together!
我们一起去打网球吧！
Let's go play tennis together!
我们一起去打篮球。no 吧 — flat statement
Without 吧 it just states a fact — not an invitation.
Comparison · 什么时候 vs 几点
什么时候 — any time frame
你什么时候去打篮球？
Can be answered "this afternoon" or "Saturday" — a day, a part of day, a season.
几点 — clock time only
你几点去打篮球？
Must be answered with an hour, e.g. 四点 — never a day or season.
