How to Play

1️⃣
Teams share a set of phrase cards spread face-up on the desk.
2️⃣
Teacher calls out an English meaning or reads a phrase aloud.
3️⃣
First student to grab (or point to and copy) the correct card scores a point for their team.
📣
Teacher calls English/phrase → students grab the matching card
e.g. "when" / shénme shíhòu → students grab 什么时候
Scaffold
Today's word list stays visible on the board so students can double-check before grabbing
Extension
Students take turns as the caller, choosing English or phrase prompts for their team
Setup
One set of phrase cards per team, spread face-up on the desk
