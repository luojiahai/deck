1
What does 吧 do to a sentence?
吧 turns a statement into a suggestion
It softens the sentence into a friendly invitation, not a command or a flat statement.
2
Which question asks for the exact hour?
几点。
jǐ diǎn — 什么时候 can be answered with any time frame; 几点 must be a clock time.
3
Error check: 我们一起去打篮球呢！ What's wrong?
呢 marks continuation, not a suggestion — should be 我们一起去打篮球吧！
吧 is the suggestion particle; 呢 only signals an ongoing action or a follow-up question.
