L6 · 吧 + 什么时候/几点 Activity 1 · We Do
Activity 1
什么时候 / 几点 Completion Worksheet
⏱ 8 min
We Do
Fill each blank with 什么时候 or 几点 based on the context clue in the answer. Items 1–2 modeled together as a class, then continue independently on the worksheet.
1
你上课？
——下午两点。 → 几点
2
你去打球？
——星期六。 → 什么时候
3
你做作业？
——晚上七点。
4
你去游泳？
——星期天。
5
你起床？
——早上七点半。
6
你去打篮球？
——下午。
7
你睡觉？
——晚上十点。
8
你去逛街？
——星期五。
Scaffold
Items 1–2 solved together on the projector before students continue items 3–8 independently
Extension
Students write 2 of their own question–answer pairs, one for each word, and swap with a partner to solve
OTR
Whole-class simultaneous writing; teacher circulates and cold-calls to check answers
