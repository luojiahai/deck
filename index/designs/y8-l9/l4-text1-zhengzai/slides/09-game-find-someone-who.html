Game
⏱ 7 min
Mingle
Find Someone Who · 你正在做什么？
Each student secretly holds an activity card — circulate and quiz classmates to find a match

How to Play

1️⃣
Each student is secretly assigned an activity card (跳舞, 拉小提琴, 打篮球, 做作业, etc.)
2️⃣
Teacher calls a clue — "Find someone who is dancing" — students circulate asking 你正在做什么？ to check
3️⃣
Match found? Pair up and raise hands — teacher calls the next clue

你正在做什么？

Setup Needed
One activity card per student, clue list for the teacher to call out
OTR
Every student asks and answers repeatedly during the mingle — full-class response rate by design
