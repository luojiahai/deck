常见错误 · Common Errors
⚠️
从五岁开始跳舞从四岁开始跳舞
A common mixup from workbook exercises — 欢欢 started dancing at age 4, not 5. Double-check the number every time.
⚠️
什么时候 vs 几点
什么时候 = when (any time frame — day, age, season…). 几点 = what time (the hour only). Don't swap them.
⚠️
吧 vs 呢
吧 = suggestion ("let's…", "how about…"). 呢 = continuation ("[subject] is still …-ing"). Don't confuse the two particles.
⚠️
拉小提琴 vs 弹钢琴
Instrument verbs are not interchangeable — 拉 (pull/draw a bow) for 小提琴, 弹 (pluck/press keys) for 钢琴.
