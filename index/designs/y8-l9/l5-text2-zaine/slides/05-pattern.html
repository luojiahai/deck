关键句型 · 你在干什么呢？
How to ask and answer "what are you doing right now"
你在
干什么
呢？
→
"What are you doing [right now]?"
Compare · 正在 vs 在……呢
正在 · plain "in progress"
我正在做作业。
I am doing homework (in progress).
在……呢 · stronger, right now
我在做作业呢。
I'm doing homework right this second — and it continues.
我在玩电脑游戏呢。
I'm playing a computer game right now.
我在打篮球呢。
I'm playing basketball right now.
你在干什么呢？我在跟猫说话呢！silly example
What are you doing? I'm talking to my cat!
