Recap
除了X以外，我还喜欢Y"Besides X, I also like Y"
除了音乐以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。
Besides music, I also like dance.
除了游泳以外，她还喜欢打网球。
Besides swimming, she also likes tennis.
New
有的人在……有的人在……"some people are …, some are …"
有的人在跳舞，有的人在唱歌。
Some people are dancing, some are singing.
Re-uses 正在/在 (action in progress) from Lesson 7/8 — but with 有的人 (some people) as the subject.
