Activity 2
Park-Scene Picture Writing
⏱ 9 min
You Do

Instructions

  1. In pairs, look at the printed park-scene picture
  2. Write 4–5 sentences describing what different people are doing, using 有的人在……有的人在……
  3. Extension: combine two activities per person using 一边……一边…… from Lesson 7 — e.g. 有的人在一边跑步一边听音乐
  4. Cold call: be ready to read one sentence aloud
