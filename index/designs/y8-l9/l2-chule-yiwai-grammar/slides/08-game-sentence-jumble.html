How to Play

1️⃣
Pre-cut sentence-piece sets (word-by-word) for 4–5 sentences — a mix of 除了……以外 and 有的人在…… — are distributed to pairs/small groups.
2️⃣
Race to arrange the pieces into a correct sentence and write it out on your whiteboard.
3️⃣
First group with the correct order raises hands — teacher checks and calls the next sentence.
✂️
Setup needed: pre-cut word-strip envelopes
One set per pair/group — 4–5 sentences, mixing both patterns
Scaffold
Simpler, shorter sentences (fewer pieces) given to pairs needing more support
Extension
Fast finishers get an extra 6th sentence combining both patterns in one go
