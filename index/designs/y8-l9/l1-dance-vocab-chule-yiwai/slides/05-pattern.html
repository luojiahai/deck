How to add an extra hobby on top of one you already have
除了
X
以外，我还喜欢
Y
"Besides X, I also like Y."
除了音乐以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。
Besides music, I also like dance.
除了游泳以外，我还喜欢打网球。
Besides swimming, I also like playing tennis.
除了吃饭以外，我还喜欢吃饭！silly example
Besides eating, I also like eating!
除了 attaches to the first hobby; 以外 appears only once, right after it.
