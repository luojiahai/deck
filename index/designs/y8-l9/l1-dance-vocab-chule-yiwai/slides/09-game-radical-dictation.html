How to Play

1️⃣
Project the textbook's "Learn the Radicals" grid (p.81, 25 radicals) for 1 minute — students memorise the positions.
2️⃣
Hide the grid. Teacher dictates radicals one at a time by pinyin or meaning.
3️⃣
In teams, students write each radical into its correct grid square from memory on the blank template.
🏆
Most correct squares after 15 radicals wins.
🗂️
Setup Needed
Textbook radical grid (p.81) on the projector + blank 5×5 grid template per team
Radicals drawn from the textbook's own "Learn the Radicals" box, p.81 — teacher displays it briefly before dictating.
