Activity 2
除了……以外 Sentence-Building with Word Cards
⏱ 8 min
We Do
→ You Do

Instructions

  1. In pairs, arrange the word cards into a correct sentence
  2. Copy 3 correct sentences into your books, swapping in different hobby pairs each time
  3. Extension: write one sentence using a hobby not on the cards (from L7/L8), and one about a family member — "我爸爸除了……以外，还喜欢……"
  4. Cold call: be ready to read one sentence aloud

Word Cards · 词卡

除了
[hobby A]
以外
我还喜欢
[hobby B]
除了音乐以外，我还喜欢舞蹈。
Frame colour = gold · Hobby colour = teal, so the slots are visually obvious
Scaffold
Word cards colour-coded by slot (除了…以外 = one colour, hobby words = another) so the frame is visually obvious
Extension
One sentence with a hobby not on the cards; one sentence about a family member ("我爸爸除了……以外，还喜欢……")
OTR
Cold call 4–5 pairs to read one sentence aloud before the game
