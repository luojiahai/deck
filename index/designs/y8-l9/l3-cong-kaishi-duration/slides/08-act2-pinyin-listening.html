Activity 2
Pinyin Listening · ou / uo
⏱ 7 min
You Do

Instructions

  1. Teacher reads a series of words/syllables aloud — a mix of ou and uo sounds, drawing on Unit 3 vocabulary
  2. Write the pinyin with correct tone marks on the worksheet
  3. Extension: also write the character and English meaning for each syllable you hear, not just the pinyin

Worked Example

舞 → wǔ
说 → shuō
都 → dōu
走 → zǒu
Scaffold
Word bank of possible syllables provided at the bottom of the worksheet
Extension
Students also write the character and English meaning for each syllable they hear
OTR
Listen-and-write — every student responds to every item; teacher reveals answers on the projector for self-marking
