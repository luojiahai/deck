Sentence Strips Around the Room

📌
我从四岁开始学跳舞。
📌
我从六岁开始拉小提琴。
📌
我每天拉四十五分钟小提琴。

How to Play

1️⃣
Sentence strips using 从……开始 are taped up around the classroom / corridor
2️⃣
In pairs, one student runs to read a sentence, memorises it, runs back, and dictates it to their partner, who writes it down
3️⃣
Partners swap roles each round. First pair to correctly transcribe all sentences wins!
🏃
Setup needed
Sentence strips taped up around the classroom/corridor, pens and paper for each pair
Scaffold
Shorter, simpler strips (fewer characters) posted closer to the classroom door for pairs who need it
Extension
Fast-finishing pairs add a bonus duration clause of their own to an extra blank strip
OTR
Every pair runs and writes simultaneously — teacher circulates to check transcriptions as pairs finish
