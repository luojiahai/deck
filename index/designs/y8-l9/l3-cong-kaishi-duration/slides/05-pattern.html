L3 · 从……开始 + DURATION I Do · Key Pattern
10–20 min · I Do
关键句型 · Key Pattern
Say when you started an activity — then add how long you do it for
我从X岁开始
+
学Y
→
"I started learning Y from age X"
我从四岁开始学跳舞。
I started dancing from age 4.
我从六岁开始拉小提琴。
I started playing violin from age 6.
我每天拉四十五分钟小提琴。
I play violin for 45 minutes every day. (+ duration)
我从一岁开始拉小提琴！silly example
I started playing violin from age 1!
从……开始 works with any activity — dancing, an instrument, a sport, anything.
