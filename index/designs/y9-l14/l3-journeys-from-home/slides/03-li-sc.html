Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to interview someone about a real journey from their home, and report what they said to the class.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can ask how long a journey takes

能问：走路要多长时间？坐车要多长时间？

2

I can answer with a real journey from my own home

能说我自己家的真实路线，不是编的

3

I can report a classmate's answer in the third person

能用第三人称报告：他每天骑自行车上学，大概要十五分钟
