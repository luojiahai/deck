8–10 MIN 学习目标 · Learning Intention 3 / 21

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what is inside a bag, a wallet or a pocket, with the right measure words.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can name what I carry

能说我带什么：手提包、钱包、钥匙、手机、现金

2

I can list three or more things in one sentence

能一句话说三样东西：……里有……、……和……

3

I can add something extra with 除了……以外，还有……

能用「除了……以外，还有……」再加一样
