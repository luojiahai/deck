Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say whether an action reached its result — found it or didn't, finished it or didn't.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can attach a result to a verb

能给动词加结果：找到、吃完、借走、听见

2

I can say what I could not do

能说做不成：我没有找到 ／ 我找不到

3

I can report something missing at a service desk

能在服务台说丢了什么，回答里边有什么
