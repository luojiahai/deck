Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what we eat for breakfast and to add one thing to another in Chinese.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can name six breakfast foods in Chinese

能说出「谷类早餐」「牛奶」「面包」「煎蛋」「香肠」

2

I can use 加 to combine two things — 谷类早餐加牛奶

能用「加」把两样东西放在一起说

3

I can answer 你早饭吃什么？ with two or three sentences

能用中文回答「你早饭吃什么？」
