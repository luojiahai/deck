Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what is good or bad for us, and to talk about things we have liked since we were small.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say 牙齿, 从小, 总是 and 正餐 in Chinese

能说出「牙齿」「从小」「总是」「正餐」

2

I can say something is good or bad for something else — A 对 B 好／不好

能用「对……好／不好」说一句

3

I can say what I have liked since childhood — 我从小就……

能用「我从小就……」介绍自己
