Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what we eat for lunch and dinner, and to interview four classmates and report back what they said.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can name four lunch and dinner foods in Chinese

能说出「沙拉」「酸奶」「杂菜汤」「碗」

2

I can use 有时 = 有时候 and the right measure word for a dish

能用「有时」，也会说「一碗汤」「一杯牛奶」

3

I can interview a classmate and report their answer to the class

能问同学，然后向全班报告：他早饭一般吃……
