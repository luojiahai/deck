Activity 3 / Game

日常饮食习惯采访 · Partner Interview

⏱ 7 min

Speaking · Interview

Based on Textbook Act. 13, p. 99 · No setup needed — projected question list as prompt

How to Play

  1. In pairs, interview each other using the 8 textbook prompts, writing brief notes on your partner's answers
  2. 2–3 pairs report one interesting fact about their partner to the class ("Report to the class")
  3. Practises all of today's patterns in a communicative, personalised context — question forms, 有时候/通常/但是, food vocabulary

8 Interview Prompts

1

Wake time & breakfast

2

School commute

3

Morning/afternoon periods

4

Lunch time & food

5

School canteen habits

6

Dismissal & journey home

7

Dinner time & food

8

Evening activities & bedtime

Scaffold

Provide the 8 questions fully written out (not just prompts) for students who need support

Extension

Write up your partner's answers as a short paragraph instead of notes, using 但是 to note any exception to their routine
