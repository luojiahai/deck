Model Dialogue — Watch Shopping

买家你想看哪块手表？

卖家这块，一千五百块的。

Teacher models once at the front, then pairs pick 3 of the 8 item prompts and perform each dialogue for their partner, then swap. Cold call 1–2 pairs to perform for the class.

8 Item Prompts — Pick 3

金鱼goldfish

西瓜watermelon

计算器calculators

短裤shorts

项链/耳环necklace/earrings

书books

凉鞋sandals/shoes

皮带/领带belt/tie

Each item uses the verbs 买/试穿/看 and a price.

Scaffold

Dialogue frame with blanks: "你想___哪___？" / "这___，___块的。"

Extension

Add a 比 comparison or 不算太 comment, e.g. 这个比那个便宜，不算太贵。

OTR

Every pair performs at least 3 exchanges; cold call 2 pairs to share with the class.
