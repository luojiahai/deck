YOU DO · Written Retell

Sentence Frame

上个星期我跟一起去菜市场买菜了。我们买了、、，一共花了。

1

Students write their own version in exercise books, changing the items and prices, following the same structure (5 min).

2

Cold call 2–3 students to read their retell aloud.

Scaffold
