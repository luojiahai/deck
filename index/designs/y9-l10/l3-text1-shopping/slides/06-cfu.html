附近 = "Nearby"?

Teacher says: "附近的意思是'nearby'。"

"对不对？大拇指向上表示对，向下表示错。" Every student shows a thumbs-up or thumbs-down.

2

Quick Translate

"All Kinds Of"

Teacher asks: "How do you say 'all kinds of' in Chinese?"

Students respond chorally or write 各种 on mini whiteboards.

3

Cold Call

Price Comparison

Teacher asks:

"Priya，菜市场里的苹果比超市便宜还是贵？"

Teacher note: Aim for 100% participation on checks 1 and 2. For check 3, pre-identify 2–3 students who need a stretch and follow up with "为什么？" for a full sentence.
