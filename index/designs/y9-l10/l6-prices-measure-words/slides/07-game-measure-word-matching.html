量词配对比赛 · Measure-Word Matching Competition

The textbook's own Group Game (Act. 9, p.97) — race to match nouns to the correct measure word

How to Play

1️⃣

Split the class into small groups. Each group gets a list of 30 nouns and a bank of measure words.

2️⃣

Groups race to match each noun to its correct measure word, writing the pairs in their exercise books.

3️⃣

First group with the most correct matches after 6 minutes wins. Teacher reveals the answer key on the projector for whole-class checking.

🖥️

Setup Needed

Printed noun lists per group, or projected list with groups copying into exercise books
