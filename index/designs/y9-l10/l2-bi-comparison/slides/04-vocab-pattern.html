📖

NOTE · 比 is used to compare two nouns

e.g. 我比弟弟高。/ 我的头发比妹妹的长。

Modelled Examples · 例子 (teacher reads aloud, class repeats)

Example 1

牛肉比猪肉好吃。

Beef tastes better than pork.

Example 2

我的书包比铅笔盒便宜。

My schoolbag is cheaper than my pencil case.

Example 3 — stacked 比……还……

那里的菜比超市里的新鲜，还便宜。

The vegetables there are fresher than the supermarket's, and even cheaper.
