Game · 有时候接龙 · 38–43 min

Game

⏱ 5 min

You Do

有时候接龙 · Chain Response

Each student repeats the growing chain, then adds one more lunch item

1

学生1：我有时候带三明治。

↓

2

学生2：我有时候带三明治，有时候买盒饭。

↓

3

学生3：我有时候带三明治，有时候买盒饭，有时候……

How to Play

1️⃣

Students sit in a circle or row. First student starts "我有时候……" and completes it with a lunch-related activity.

2️⃣

Next student repeats the full pattern and adds their own item.

3️⃣

Continue around the group — if a student makes an error or forgets, they are gently corrected and the chain continues.

🎯

Practises: 有时候……有时候…… pattern, memory, listening

Scaffold

Allow students to use the vocabulary list on the projector for ideas.

Extension

Fluent speakers must add a price or opinion, e.g. 有时候买盒饭，十九块，不算太贵。

Setup

None needed.
