26–38 min · You Do (12 min) · Workbook Ex. 16

活动二 · 句子重组 Sentence Reordering

YOU DO · Reorder & Peer-Check

5 Scrambled Sentences · Food & Meals

1

Independent work (8 min): rearrange each set of scrambled words/phrases into a correct sentence about food or meals; write the final sentence in exercise books.

2

Pair-check with a neighbour.

3

Whole-class reveal with 2–3 cold-called readings.

OTR · every student reorders all 5 sentences independently before pair-check

Scaffold

Provide the English translation next to each scrambled set.

Extension

After correctly reordering, write one additional original sentence using 有时候……有时候…… or 不算太……

Sample Item (Illustrative)

买

我

盒饭

通常

↓ reorder ↓

我通常买盒饭。

✏️

Students rearrange words, not translate

All target vocabulary and grammar words come from today's and prior lessons' patterns.
