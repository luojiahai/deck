核心句型 · Saying a Price

物品 (item)

+

价钱 (price)

+

一斤/一公斤

"X costs Y per jin / kilogram"

1

猪肉十五块一斤。

Pork is $15 per jin.

2

牛肉比猪肉贵，二十块一斤。

Beef is more expensive than pork, $20 per jin.

3

豆腐一百块一公斤！太贵了！

Silly example — wait for reaction: tofu $100/kg! Too expensive!
