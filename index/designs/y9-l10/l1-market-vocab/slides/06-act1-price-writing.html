We Do · Guided Price-Writing

活动一 · 引导写价钱句

___十___一斤。

1

Project 6 market pictures from Textbook Act. 1 (猪肉, 牛肉, 鸡肉, 活鱼, 活虾, 豆腐), each with a price tag.

2

Teacher models the first 2 as full written sentences on the board, e.g. 猪肉十五块一斤。

3

Students copy the pattern and write the remaining 4 price sentences into their exercise books.

4

Circulate and cold call 2 students to read their sentences aloud for the class to check.

Scaffold

Keep pinyin visible under each picture; provide the sentence frame ___十___一斤。

Extension

Add a comparison using 比 (previewing next lesson), e.g. 牛肉比猪肉贵。

OTR

Every student writes all 4 sentences; whole-class check via cold call.
