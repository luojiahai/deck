How to Play

1️⃣

Use the 12 flash characters from Textbook Act. 2 (金匕亡尸丁入户井弓方习皿). Split the class into 4 teams, each lined up facing the board.

2️⃣

Teacher calls out one character's English/pinyin meaning; the first student in each team runs up and writes the correct 汉字 on the board, then tags the next teammate.

🏆

First team to correctly write all 12 characters wins.

🗂️

Setup Needed

Board space for 4 teams; no printed materials needed

Scaffold

Keep the 12 characters projected in a corner for teams that fall behind.

Extension

Winning team must use one of the characters in a real word from today, e.g. 户 → 户口 (accept any sensible attempt).
