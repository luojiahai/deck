YOU DO · Individual + Pair Check

Workbook Ex. 1 · Match

Match 8 pictures of meat/seafood/vegetables to the word bank: 猪肉, 牛肉, 羊肉, 鸡肉, 活鱼, 活虾, 豆腐, 蔬菜.

Workbook Ex. 4 · Write

Write the Chinese for 15 pictured food items — lettuce is given as an example, then tomato, broccoli, orange, potato, banana, apple, watermelon, pear, pizza, hamburger, baguette, steak, drink.

1

Students complete both exercises individually in exercise books.

2

Pair-check answers with a neighbour before teacher reveals the answers on the projector.

Scaffold

For Ex. 4, provide a partial word bank on the board for items not yet taught explicitly.

Extension

Fluent speakers write one full sentence (not just the word) for 3 Ex. 4 items, e.g. 我喜欢吃西瓜。

Fast-Finisher · Workbook Ex. 2

Students who finish Ex. 1 and Ex. 4 early move on to Ex. 2: circle 10 vocabulary phrases hidden in a 5×5 character grid and write their meanings.
