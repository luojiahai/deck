You Do · Activity 2b: Writing Task · 33–43 min

33–43 min · Based on Workbook Ex. 23

写作任务 · Describe Your Own Eating Habits

80–100 字 · Must Include

·

Breakfast / lunch / dinner — food & drinks

·

Favourite food / snacks

·

Your own height and build

Required Patterns — at least one each

比

有时候……有时候……

但是

早饭我吃，午饭，晚饭。我最喜欢吃。我比……

Scaffold

Paragraph frame with sentence starters for each section (breakfast/lunch/dinner; favourite food; height/build) + the 3 required patterns pre-written with blanks.

Extension

Extend to 120+ words and add a comparison between two meals, e.g. 午饭比早饭好吃。

OTR

Every student completes independently. Cold call 2–3 volunteers to read their writing aloud. Collect books for marking.
