Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what someone did to something using the 把 sentence — and see how 把 and 被 tell the same story from opposite ends.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can build a 把 sentence in the right order

能按顺序说：谁 + 把 + 什么 + 动词 + 了

2

I can say someone woke me up

能说：弟弟的哭声把我吵醒了

3

I can use 吵, 醒 and 声 in a sentence

能用上「吵」「醒」「声」

4

I can turn a 被 sentence into a 把 sentence and back

能把「被」字句改成「把」字句，也能改回来
