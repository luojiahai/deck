8–10 MIN 学习目标 · Learning Intention 3 / 21

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say that we have recently moved house, and to describe what our neighbours are like.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say we moved recently

能说：我们家最近搬家了

2

I can name the rooms of a house

能说房间：书房 阳台 浴室 卧室 厨房 客厅 餐厅

3

I can ask and answer 最近……有没有……？

能问也能答：你最近有没有……？

4

I can say whether my neighbours are annoying

能说邻居怎么样：楼上的邻居很烦人
