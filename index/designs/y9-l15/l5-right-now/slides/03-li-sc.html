Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what people are doing right now, thank people and accept apologies properly, and ask a neighbour to give something back.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say what someone is doing at this moment

能说现在：他们正在看电视呢

2

I can use the right phrase for the moment

能用对说法：谢谢你提醒我／不用客气／没关系

3

I can join sentences with 因为……所以……, 可是, 平时, 经常

能用连词把句子连起来

4

I can ask for something back politely

能有礼貌地要回来：能不能把我的……还给我？
