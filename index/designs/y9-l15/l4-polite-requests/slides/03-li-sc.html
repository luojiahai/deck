Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to ask a neighbour politely to change something, and answer a complaint — either by agreeing to be careful, or by saying there is nothing we can do.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can make a polite request with 能不能……？

能有礼貌地请求：能不能小点儿声？

2

I can ask for more or less with 一点儿

能说：请说慢一点儿／把声音开得小一点儿

3

I can promise to be careful

能回答：好，我以后注意

4

I can say there's nothing I can do

能回答：对不起，我没有办法
