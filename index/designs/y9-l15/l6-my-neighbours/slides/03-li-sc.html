Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to describe everyone who lives in a block of flats, suggest what to do about a difficult neighbour, and write a paragraph introducing our own neighbours.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say who lives on which floor

能说谁住哪儿：三零一住着一家人／楼上是……

2

I can suggest a solution

能出主意：我会想办法／如果……，我会……

3

I can write a paragraph about my neighbours

能写一段「我的邻居」——几口人、哪国人、周末做什么、我觉得

4

I can use 把, 被 and 得 inside that paragraph

那一段里，「把」「被」「得」各要用一次
