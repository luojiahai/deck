8–10 MIN 学习目标 3 / 20

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to name types of food and ask someone to choose, using 还是.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say and read six food words in Chinese

中餐、西餐、快餐、热狗、汉堡包、比萨饼

2

I can ask a choice question with 还是

你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？

3

I can answer without repeating 还是

我喜欢吃中餐。／我两种都喜欢吃。
