例句 · See them used

Wǒ xǐhuan chī zhōngcān.

我喜欢吃中餐。

I like eating Chinese food.

Wǒ bú tài xǐhuan chī xīcān.

我不太喜欢吃西餐。

I don't much like eating western food.

Zhōngcān, xīcān, wǒ dōu xǐhuan chī.

中餐、西餐，我都喜欢吃。

Chinese food, western food — I like eating both.
