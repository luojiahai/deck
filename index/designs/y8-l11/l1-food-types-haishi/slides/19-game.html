Game · 拍字 · Slap the Character

TWO TEAMS · 7 MIN

Two students at the board. Six A3 cards spread out.

Teacher says a word in Chinese — first to slap it scores.

Rotate every three calls.

Extension · 加油题

The teacher gives a clue sentence instead of the word, and students slap what it describes. Fluent students take the caller's chair and invent the clue themselves, in Chinese.
