Activity 1 · 三个问题 · Three questions

PAIRS · 8 MIN

Write three 还是 questions that between them use all six words.

你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？

你想吃热狗还是汉堡包？

你喜欢吃快餐还是比萨饼？

1

Write your three questions.

2

Stand up. Ask three different classmates.

3

Report two answers back to the class.

Extension · 加油题

Each question uses a different verb (吃／想吃／喜欢吃). Report back in the third person with a 可是 clause — and no notes.

小明喜欢吃汉堡包，可是他妈妈说他不应该每天吃快餐。
