复习 · Bingo

WHOLE CLASS · 6 MIN

Draw a 3×3 grid. Fill it with any nine of these ten words — in characters.

中餐 · 西餐 · 快餐 · 热狗 · 汉堡包 · 比萨饼 · 喝 · 饮料 · 可乐 · 汽水

Teacher calls each word in Chinese only — no English, no pinyin.

First to a line calls 我赢了！ Play on to two winners.

Extension · 加油题

Then one whiteboard question — everyone writes their own answer and holds it up.

你喜欢吃中餐还是西餐？
