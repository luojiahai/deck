27–36 MIN · YOU DO Activity 2 · 采访 18 / 20

采访 · Interview your partner

PAIRS · 4 MIN

Ask about all three meals. Note the answers.

你早饭一般吃什么？　午饭呢？　晚饭呢？

Then report two of them back to the class — in the third person.

Extension · 加油题

A three-turn version where you disagree, resolved in Chinese without switching to English. Use 可是 to object and 应该 to argue, then report the outcome in the third person.

可是我们昨天吃了西餐，今天应该吃中餐。
