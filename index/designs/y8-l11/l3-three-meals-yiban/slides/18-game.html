Game · 猜一猜 · 20 Questions

WHOLE CLASS · 7 MIN

One student holds a food or drink card from Lessons 1–3.

The class asks yes/no or 还是 questions — Chinese only.

是饮料吗？　是中餐还是西餐？　早餐吃吗？

Up to twenty questions, then guess. Beanbag passes to whoever asks next.

Extension · 加油题

The holder may only answer in full sentences — never a bare 是 or 不是. Fluent students take the card first, so they carry the answering load.

这不是饮料，这是快餐。
