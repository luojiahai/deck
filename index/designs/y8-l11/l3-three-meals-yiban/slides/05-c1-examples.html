例句 · See them used

Wǒ zǎocān chī shuǐguǒ.

我早餐吃水果。

I eat fruit for breakfast.

Wǒ wǔfàn chī kuàicān.

我午饭吃快餐。

I eat fast food for lunch.

Wǒ zǎocān chī shuǐguǒ, wǔfàn chī kuàicān.

我早餐吃水果，午饭吃快餐。

Fruit for breakfast, fast food for lunch.

两个说法 · Two words, one meal

The book writes 早餐 in Text 2 and 早饭 in the workbook. Same meal.

餐 and 饭 swap freely here — which is why 午饭 and 晚饭 take 饭.
