20–26 MIN · WE DO Activity 1 · 周末计划 15 / 19

Activity 1 · 这个周末 · Your family's weekend plan

SOLO · 6 MIN

Four sentences. Every one uses 会, and at least one uses 或者.

这个周末我们全家人会去饭店吃饭。

我们会吃面条或者炒面。

我会喝可乐。

星期天我们会去水果店。

Extension · 加油题

Six sentences across two days and all three meals, with two of them negative using 不会. Then a partner asks you two 还是 questions about the plan, answered live without your paper.

星期六我们不会在家吃饭。
