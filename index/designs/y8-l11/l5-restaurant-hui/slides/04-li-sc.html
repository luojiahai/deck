Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to talk about eating out, and to say something is likely to happen using 会.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say 饭店 and 吃饭, and build other 店 words

快餐店、水果店

2

I can use 会 to say something is likely

这个周末我们会去饭店吃饭。

3

I can read Text 2 aloud and answer in Chinese

他们下个周末会去哪儿？
