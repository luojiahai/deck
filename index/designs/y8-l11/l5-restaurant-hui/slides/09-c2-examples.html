例句 · See them used

Wǒ xǐhuan qù kuàicāndiàn chīfàn.

我喜欢去快餐店吃饭。

I like eating at a fast-food shop.

Shuǐguǒdiàn yǒu hěn duō shuǐguǒ.

水果店有很多水果。

The fruit shop has a lot of fruit.

Wǒ xǐhuan qù kuàicāndiàn, wǒ māma xǐhuan qù shuǐguǒdiàn.

我喜欢去快餐店，我妈妈喜欢去水果店。

I like the fast-food shop, my mother likes the fruit shop.

店 · A shop, on the end

Both words are built from things you already know — 快餐 + 店, 水果 + 店.
