Game · 接龙 · Category Chain

WHOLE CLASS STANDING · 4 MIN

课本 p.109 Ex.16. Teacher names a category and one item — 中餐：炒面.

Students around the room add one item each.

Repeat an item, or fail to add one in three seconds — you sit down.

Two rounds: 中餐 · 饮料.

Extension · 加油题

Your addition must be a full sentence using 或者 that links your item to the previous student's. Fluent students start each round, so they set the bar.

他说炒面，我早餐一般吃包子或者粥。
