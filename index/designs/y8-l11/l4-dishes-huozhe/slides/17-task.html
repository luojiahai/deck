Activity 1 · 一天的菜单 · A one-day menu

PAIRS · 7 MIN

Three rows — 早餐 / 午饭 / 晚饭. One sentence per row, every sentence using 或者.

Between the three rows you must use all five dishes plus 粥 from last lesson. Nothing repeated.

早餐：我们一般吃粥或者包子。

Extension · 加油题

Add a fourth row — 周末 — written as a contrast across two clauses. Then another pair asks you three 还是 questions about your menu, which you answer without looking at the sheet.

周末我们一般不吃米饭，我们吃炒面或者面条，可是我妈妈一般吃粥。
