写一句 · Write your own

我早餐一般吃 ______ 或者 ______ 。

60 seconds. Both blanks must be real food.

Extension · 加油题

Write three 或者 sentences, one per meal — then turn one of them into a 还是 question and write both side by side. That pair is what the test asks for.

我早餐一般吃粥或者包子。　／　你早餐吃粥还是包子？
