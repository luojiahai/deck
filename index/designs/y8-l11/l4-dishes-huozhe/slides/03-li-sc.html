8–10 MIN 学习目标 3 / 22

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to name Chinese dishes and use 或者 to say “or” in a statement.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say and read five Chinese dishes

面条、炒面、米饭、炒菜、包子

2

I can use 或者 in a statement

我午饭一般吃面条或者炒面。

3

I can choose between 还是 and 或者

还是 asks　·　或者 tells
