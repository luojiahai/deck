例句 · See them used

Wǒ wǔfàn yìbān chī miàntiáo.

我午饭一般吃面条。

I normally eat noodles for lunch.

Wǒ hěn xǐhuan chī chǎomiàn.

我很喜欢吃炒面。

I really like stir-fried noodles.

Miàntiáo hé chǎomiàn wǒ dōu xǐhuan chī.

面条和炒面我都喜欢吃。

I like both noodles and stir-fried noodles.

部首 · Radical clue

炒 has 火 (fire) on the left — 炒面 and 炒菜 are both cooked in a hot wok.
