写一句 · Write your own

我喜欢喝 ______ 。

我不太喜欢喝 ______ 。

60 seconds. Mini whiteboards.

Extension · 加油题

Write it, then add a 可是 clause of your own so it runs as two joined sentences.

妈妈说我应该多喝水，不应该喝太多饮料。

部首 · Radical clue

喝 has 口 (mouth) on the left — drinking happens at the mouth. 汽水 has 氵 (water) twice.
