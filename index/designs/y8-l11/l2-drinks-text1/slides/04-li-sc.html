8–10 MIN 学习目标 4 / 20

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what we drink, and sort food and drink into four categories.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can say and read four drink words

喝、饮料、可乐、汽水

2

I can ask and answer about drinks

你喜欢喝什么饮料？

3

I can sort a food into a category and say why

比萨饼是快餐。
