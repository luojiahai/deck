轻松学中文 2 · Unit 4 · Lesson 11

一日三餐

Three Meals a Day · Year 8

Five 50-minute lessons. Twenty-six new words spread across the sequence — two to six per lesson, each pair taught as a three-slide cycle: the words, then the words used, then the students using them. Revision happens inside every lesson rather than in a review lesson at the end. The two words for “or” are taught three lessons apart, then deliberately collided in Lesson 4.

Grammar Focus · 本课语法点

Five lessons, not six. The sequence ends on content, not revision — there is no 复习 lesson, so the unit's Revision and Test pages get prepared inside the five. Every lesson opens with a cumulative Review phase, the workbook's test formats are used as the ordinary practice activities, and Lesson 5's plenary looks back across the whole sequence.
