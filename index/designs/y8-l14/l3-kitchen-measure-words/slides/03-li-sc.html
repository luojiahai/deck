Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what is in a kitchen, and choose the right measure word for anything in the house.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can name three kitchen appliances

能说出三样厨房电器

2

I can pick the right measure word from 个 间 张 辆 台

会选量词，不都用「个」

3

I can tell 有 and 在 apart and use each correctly

分得清「有」和「在」，两个都会用
