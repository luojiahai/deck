8–10 MIN 学习目标 · Learning Intention 4 / 24

Learning Intention · 学习目标

We are learning to say what appliances are inside a room, and describe its size without saying just 大 or 小.

Success Criteria · 我能……

1

I can name an air-conditioner and a washing machine

能说出空调和洗衣机

2

I can say what is inside something using 里面有……

能用「里面有……」

3

I can describe a room's size with 不算大 or 不大也不小

能说「不算大」或者「不大也不小」
