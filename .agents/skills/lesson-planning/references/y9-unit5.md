### Grammar Notes

- **往 vs 向** — interchangeable before 左/右 in this lesson (往右拐 = 向右拐).
  拐 and 转 are likewise interchangeable here.
- **第 + number + 个 + noun** — ordinal. 第一个路口, 第二个路口.
- **就** — "then / right there", carrying both "就到了" (and then you're there) and
  "就在……" (it's right at…). Already taught; reused heavily.
- **离** — "distance from"; A 离 B + 远/近. Already taught in Y9 L8 context.
- **Direction complement 到** — 看到, 走到, and 就到了.

### Key Sentence Patterns (L14)

- 或者…，或者… — "either … or …" (statements only)
- 坐几路公共汽车？· 要坐几站？· 在哪站下车？
- 过了…以后 / 看见… — sequencing on a route
- 一座庙／桥 — measure word 座

## Cross-Cutting Notes

**The unit's shape.** L13 gives the *places* and *static position* language, plus a
first pass at directions. L14 takes directions further into *journeys* (buses,
routes, how many stops) and adds 被 + complement of result. L15 turns inward to
*people* — neighbours, complaints, 把 and degree complements. All three feed one
test.

**L13 Text 2 overlaps L14.** Text 2 of L13 is already asking-the-way. That is
deliberate — L13 teaches the walking-directions core (过马路, 一直往前走, 红绿灯,
拐/转, 第…个路口, 右手边) and L14 layers public transport on top (坐几路车, 坐几站,
在哪站下车, 或者…或者…). When planning L13, teach the walking core properly and
leave buses to L14.

**Position words are recycled, not new.** 左边 右边 对面 旁边 隔壁 附近 楼上 楼下
were taught in Book 2 Unit 5 and Book 3 earlier units. Only 前面 / 后面 / 面 are
formally new here. That means the Review phases of Y9 L13 can lean on them
confidently, and example sentences can use them freely without breaking the
"only two new words" rule.

**Facilities vocabulary far exceeds the New Words box.** The textbook's own
activities assume 超级市场/超市, 学校, 小学, 医院, 饭店, 体育馆, 体育中心,
公共图书馆, 公共汽车站, 地铁站, 电影院, 菜市场, 文具店, 茶馆, 飞机场, 停车场,
火车站, 服装店, 家具店, 书店 — all taught earlier. Treat them as review stock.

**Common student errors to pre-empt.**
- 离 vs 从 — 我家离学校很远 (distance) vs 从我家去学校 (journey origin).
- Word order in position sentences: *邮局在对面超市* → 邮局在超市对面.
- Dropping 的: *邮局左边是诊所* is acceptable spoken but the book writes 邮局的左边.
- 往/向 must precede the direction, not follow: *拐右* → 往右拐.
- 第 + number needs 个: *第一路口* → 第一个路口.
- 到了 vs 到 — 走五分钟就到了 (完成) not *走五分钟就到.
