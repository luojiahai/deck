# 轻松学中文 Book Index — all 45 lessons

Complete map of the six PDFs in `.resources/`. Use this to find the pages for any
lesson before extracting content.

**Year ↔ book:** Year 7 = Book 1 · Year 8 = Book 2 · Year 9 = Book 3.
Every book has 5 units × 3 lessons = 15 lessons.

## 🎯 Units in scope

Teaching is focused on five units. Everything else in this index is background —
useful for Review phases and cross-year context, but not what gets planned.

**Two pairings worth knowing.** Book 2 and Book 3 run the same page grid, and the
two years land on adjacent food and community topics at different levels — Y8 L12
and Y9 L12 are both *Eating Out 外出就餐*, and Y8 L15 and Y9 L13 are both
*Neighbourhood 社区*. Y8's treatment is the simpler one; Y9 revisits it with heavier
grammar. Don't reuse material across the two years without levelling it.
