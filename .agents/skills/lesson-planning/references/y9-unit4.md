### Key Sentence Patterns

1. **A 比 B + adj.** — comparison. 那里的菜比超市里的新鲜。/ 牛肉比猪肉贵。
   Book NOTE (p.93): 比 is used to compare two nouns, e.g. 我比弟弟高。/ 我的头发比妹妹的长。
2. **Price + measure word**: 一公斤苹果八块钱。/ 大白菜两块五毛一斤。/ 一卷糖果五块八毛。
   Measure words in play: 斤, 公斤, 条 (fish, trousers, belt), 块 (tofu), 个, 本, 双, 副, 盒, 卷.
3. **这家…店卖各种…** — 这家水果店卖各种新鲜水果。
4. **平时 / 通常 / 一般 / 有时候 / 经常 … ，但是 …** — habitual vs exception (Act. 12).
5. **多少钱一 + MW？** — 多少钱一盒？→ 十九块。
6. **不算太 + adj.** — 不算太贵。

### Grammar Notes

- 比 takes no 很 before the adjective: 牛肉比猪肉贵 (not 很贵).
- 还 = "also/what's more" linking two virtues: 比超市里的新鲜，还便宜。
- 各种 = "all kinds of", used before nouns: 各种新鲜蔬菜 / 各种饮料.
- 活 before an animal noun = live/fresh: 活鱼、活虾.

### Radicals / Character components

Textbook Act. 2 (p.92) — memorize within 5 minutes:
金 · 匕 · 亡 · 尸 · 丁 · 入 · 户 · 井 · 弓 · 方 · 习 · 皿 (12 components)

Useful clues to flag: 鲜 = 鱼 + 羊 · 猪/鸡 use 犭/鸟 family forms · 腐 contains 广 + 付 + 肉 · 薯 has 艹 (plant).

### Pinyin Focus
No "circle the correct pinyin" exercise in L10 textbook. Tone pairs worth drilling: shìchǎng / càishìchǎng; zhūròu vs zhūpái; xiā (1st) vs xià; piányi (neutral 2nd syllable); dòufu (neutral fu).

### Listening script — CD 40 (p.97, Act. 10)
> A: 你们学校有几家餐厅？ B: 有一家。
> A: 餐厅卖些什么？ B: 卖小吃、午饭、各种饮料、水果等。

## Cross-Cutting Notes

- **Prior knowledge from Unit 3 (L7–9)**: 里边/隔壁 location words, 文具 vocabulary, 从…开始, 因为…所以, prices with 块/毛 (L9 stationery prices). The L10 Review phase should open on 文具店 prices and 比较 of school facilities.
- **Common student errors**: inserting 很 after 比 (✗ 牛肉比猪肉很贵); dropping the measure word in a price (✗ 猪肉十块钱); confusing 斤 (½ kg) with 公斤 (kg); writing 便宜 with the wrong 宜; reading 便 as biàn in 便宜.
- **斤 vs 公斤**: the textbook prices are almost all 元/斤; 一公斤 appears only in Text 1's supermarket comparison. Teach 斤 as the market unit and 公斤 as the formal/metric one.
- **Y9 language of instruction**: aim ~70% Chinese; pinyin only for brand-new words.
