### Key Texts

**Text 1 (CD T65, p.104)**
> 这是我爸爸。他喜欢穿衬衫和牛仔裤。
> 这是我妈妈。她喜欢穿裙子。

**Text 2 (CD T67, p.108)**
> A: 你喜欢穿什么衣服？
> B: 我喜欢穿汗衫和短裤。
> A: 你上学穿校服吗？
> B: 穿。我穿衬衫、毛衣、外套和长裤。

### Key Sentence Patterns
- 他喜欢**穿** + 衣服。 — 他喜欢穿衬衫和牛仔裤。
- 你喜欢穿**什么**衣服？ → 我喜欢穿汗衫和短裤。
- 你上学穿校服**吗**？ → **穿。**／**不穿。** (verb-repeat answer, not 是)
- 颜色 + **的** + 衣服 — 天蓝色的衬衫、红色的衬衫、粉红色的裙子
- 他穿A和B。 — 他穿白色的汗衫和橙色的短裤。
- Listing with **、** inside a sentence: 我穿衬衫、毛衣、外套和长裤。(顿号 between items, 和 before the last)

### Grammar Notes
- **穿 vs 戴** — the book only teaches 穿 in this lesson; 帽子/手套/围巾 appear as Extra
  Words with no verb attached. Don't introduce 戴 unless the teacher asks.
- **Answering a 吗 question with the verb**: 穿。 not 是。 This is the first place Book 1
  drills it with a non-是 verb, and it's on the Unit Test (p.171 Q4).
- **的 is obligatory** between a colour and a garment: 红色的衬衫, never ✗红色衬衫.
- 长 is **cháng** (long) here; in L15 the same character is **zhǎng** (grow). Flag it.

## Unit 5 Revision (workbook pp.170–171)

Six word-list blocks plus a Q&A block:

1. **Colours** — 颜色：黑色 白色 黄色 蓝色 红色 粉红色 橙色 棕色 绿色 灰色
2. **Clothes** — 衣服：衬衫 牛仔裤 裙子 汗衫 短裤 毛衣 外套 长裤 校服
3. **Parts of the body** — 眼睛 鼻子 嘴巴 头 手 腿 脚 头发
4. **Adjectives** — 高 矮 长 短
5. **Verbs** — 喜欢 穿 长(得)
6. **Radicals** — 疒火爫弓力礻 ／ 冂牛贝冫户忄 ／ 厂车立革止虫

**7. Questions and answers** (p.171) — the seven the test expects:
> 1. 你喜欢什么颜色？→ 红色、橙色和蓝色。
> 2. 你喜欢黑色吗？→ 不喜欢。
> 3. 你喜欢穿什么衣服？→ 汗衫和牛仔裤。
> 4. 你上学穿校服吗？→ 穿。
> 5. 你穿什么校服？→ 白色的衬衫和蓝色的长裤。
> 6. 你喜欢你的校服吗？→ 不喜欢。
> 7. 你哥哥长什么样？→ 他长得高高的。他有大眼睛和黑色的短发。

Note Q5 and Q6 — **你穿什么校服？** and **你喜欢你的校服吗？** are on the revision list
but appear nowhere in the textbook lesson. They have to be taught inside the six.

## Unit 5 Test (workbook pp.172–175) — 11 parts

| Part | Format |
|---|---|
| 1 | Colour 20 words by category: vehicles 红色 / clothes 绿色 / adjectives 蓝色 / daily routine 橙色 / parts of the body 黄色 |
| 2 | Find the radical in 12 characters and write its meaning (弹 爱 病 祝 周 冷 房 忙 厅 辆 鞋 虾) |
| 3 | Write a character for each of 8 radicals (火 夂 穴 止 矢 士 又 礻) |
| 4 | Rearrange words into a sentence ×5 (incl. 上学/我/校服/穿) |
| 5 | Fill in the question word ×6 from 几 多大 怎么 什么 哪 哪儿 |
| 6 | Find the opposite ×6 (大→小, 长→短, 上→下, 高→矮, 黑→白, 早→晚) |
| 7 | Translate Chinese → English ×4 (incl. 爸爸每天穿衬衫和长裤上班。) |
| 8 | Write the correct sentence — word-order repair ×5 |
| 9 | Match question to answer ×4 (incl. 你每天穿校服上学吗？→ 穿。) |
| 10 | Translate English → Chinese ×5 (incl. "My mother wears a shirt and skirt to work every day." / "I do not like my school uniform.") |
| 11 | Choose two of three pictured people and describe them in Chinese |

**What this means for lesson design.** The test formats to rehearse during L14 are:
category sorting (Part 1), radical → meaning and radical → character (Parts 2–3),
word-order repair (Parts 4 and 8), translation both ways (Parts 7 and 10), and
picture description (Part 11). Use them as the *practice activities*, not as a
test-prep lesson.

## Cross-Cutting Notes

### Skills progression through the unit
L13 gives the colour words and the 颜色 + **的** + noun frame. L14 plugs clothing into
that same frame and adds the verb 穿. L15 swaps the frame for 有 + reduplicated
adjective + 的 + body part. By the end a student can describe a whole person —
which is exactly Test Part 11.

### Review source
Unit 4 (`y7-unit4.md`, L10–12: Time, Daily Routine, Transport) is the unit immediately
before this one and is heavily recycled: the L13 and L14 listening exercises mix
vehicles in with clothing, and the p.112 interview asks 你每天怎么上学？

### Common student errors
- **Dropping 的** — ✗红色衬衫 for 红色的衬衫. The workbook colouring exercises all print
  the 的, so use them as the model.
- **Answering 穿…吗？with 是** — the correct answer repeats the verb: 穿。/ 不穿。
- **长 cháng vs zhǎng** — same character, two readings, both in this unit.
- **牛 vs 午** — workbook L14 Ex.19 uses exactly this confusion (午仔裤) as its example.
- **仔 zǎi** — students read it zǐ by analogy with 子.
- **裤/裙/衬/衫 all take 衤, not 礻** — the extra dot is the single most common writing
  error in this lesson, and 礻 was taught one lesson earlier (L13 radical 6, 视).
